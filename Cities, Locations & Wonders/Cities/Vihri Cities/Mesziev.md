<p>Mesziev is a holy city of the Gethric faith in the southern Vihri Expanse, and the southern exclave of <a href="Iztaw" class="internal-link">Holy Iztaw</a>, surrounded on all sides by the Vadcy of <a href="Selbia" class="internal-link">Selbia</a>. It keeps the Band of Fire, the ring of the Prophet, in the Chapel of Fire on the Grey Hill, and the mausoleum of Saint <a href="Tytus" class="internal-link">Tytus</a> stands in the city. The Bishop of Selbia governs it in the name of the High-Bisop, and the Var of Selbia has long wanted it for his own.</p>  
<h2>Geography</h2>  
<p>Mesziev lies in the open country of the southern Expanse, north-west of Itarsky and east of Ibozka, the capital of Selbia. The city is built around the Grey Hill, a bare rise of grey stone that is the only height for miles around, and the Chapel of Fire on its summit can be seen from the Ibor. The lands of the city are small, a ring of farms and pilgrim inns around the walls, and beyond them the country belongs to Selbia.</p>  
<h2>History</h2>  
<p>Mesziev was given to the Church by the early kings of the House of Zav, in the years when they planted cities across the Expanse as instruments of the crown and of the faith, and it has been Church land ever since. How the Band of Fire came to the Grey Hill is not recorded in the Church's own histories, and the clergy of the Chapel do not discuss it.</p>  
<p>The city's fame grew with the saints who came to it. In 538PA Tytus, then a minister of the Church, received on the Grey Hill the visions that sent him north against the lich Mendizaba, and when his sister killed him in 549PA he was buried at Mesziev, where his mausoleum stands below the hill. In the last days of October of the same year the royal procession of <a href="Katerina I" class="internal-link">Katerina I</a> was attacked on its way to the city, and both of the Gras's children were killed in what became known as the Culling of 549. Half a century later Saint <a href="Kasimir" class="internal-link">Kasimir</a> came to the Grey Hill and meditated there in hunger for a week before his last journey. He called the hill "the closest a man could feel to the Flame eternal", and his words are carved over the door of the Chapel.</p>  
<p>When the Council of 870 carved the Vadcy of <a href="Torry" class="internal-link">Torry</a> out of the northern lands of Iztaw, Mesziev was left untouched, and it is today the largest holding of the Church outside the north. The precedent has not been lost on Selbia.</p>  
<h2>Government and Politics</h2>  
<p>Mesziev is ruled by the Bishop of Selbia, who sits in the city and not in Ibozka, and who governs it in the name of the High-Bisop of Iztaw. The office, held at present by Bishop Urzmyr V, is among the most respected in the Church, for its holder is the keeper of the Band. The whole of the city is Church ground, so the <a href="Candleguard" class="internal-link">Candleguard</a> keeps its walls and gates, and a guard of <a href="Waxborn" class="internal-link">Waxborn</a> stands watch over the Chapel of Fire, one of the few places outside Iztaw where they are still seen.</p>  
<p>Var Provim of Selbia wants Mesziev. The pilgrims who come to it spend their money in a city that pays nothing to Selbia, and an armed Church garrison sits in the middle of his Vadcy. If the crown and the Assembly could take Torry from Iztaw in 870PA, his house argues, they could give Mesziev to Selbia, and the question returns whenever the Church is weak at court.</p>  
<h2>Society and Culture</h2>  
<p>Mesziev is a city of pilgrims, clergy and the trades that serve them. Pilgrims come to see the Band, which is shown to the faithful on the great feasts of the Church, to pray at the mausoleum of Tytus, and to climb the Grey Hill in the steps of Kasimir. Soldiers in particular make the journey, for Tytus is venerated by soldiers, seers and those who mourn lost things. The people of the city are Tloky and Sarloni, like the people of Selbia around it, and devout.</p>  
<h2>Economy</h2>  
<p>The city lives on its pilgrims and on wax. The chandlers of Mesziev make the candles that burn in the Chapel of Fire, and the wax they work, known as Mesziev wax, is prized across the Gethric lands. The oath candles of the <a href="Candle Oath" class="internal-link">Candle Oath</a> are cast here to the measure called a span, which burns for about an hour, and a span of Mesziev wax is the measure by which oaths are judged across the Gethric world. It is said to be the wax used in the making of the Waxborn that guard the holiest cities of the faith, though the Church neither confirms nor denies it.</p>  
<h2>Places of Note</h2>  
<p>The Chapel of Fire stands on the summit of the Grey Hill and keeps the Band of Fire. The Mausoleum of Tytus stands at the foot of the hill, and the stone on the hillside where Kasimir sat for his week of hunger is marked and visited. The Bishop's Palace and the chandlers' quarter lie below the hill on the side of the city that faces the Ibor.</p>
