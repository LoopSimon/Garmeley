<p>Zavlech is the capital of Vihr and the seat of the Ras, a city of some 56,000 people on the Tryd River, where the river opens into Lake Zav. It stands in the heart of the crownlands, the part of the realm that belongs to no Vadcy, and it has been the political centre of the Vihri since the kingdom was proclaimed in 241PA.</p>
<h2>Geography</h2>
<p>Zavlech lies on the Tryd, the biggest commercial waterway in the country. The river comes up from Breza in the south-east, past the town of Vzsal, flows into Lake Zav at the city and leaves it again to the north. West of the river the land turns to the moors of the Higher Vihri, and behind the moors rise the Oathhills of <a href="Rovgi" class="internal-link">Rovgi</a>, with the Trollback Mountains and the silver town of Silverslach to the south-west. North along the river lie the town of Myrg on its western bank, the woods around Arzov and, farther on, Ahmsk. East of the city lies the town of Szulbod. Lake Zav is also as far south as the abyssal host of the Wermian Incursions ever came.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Vihrmen tribes settled on the shore of Lake Zav in 80PA, in the same years as at Werm and Trazlov. In the days of the <a href="Vihrmen Federation" class="internal-link">Vihrmen Federation</a> the Horn Lords met at Tlokyemy and swore their great pacts at <a href="Trydlew" class="internal-link">Trydlew</a>, and nobody would have called the lake town a capital. That changed with <a href="Viago I" class="internal-link">Viago of the Zav</a>, who proclaimed, who proclaimed the kingdom in 241PA and, when the Unification Wars ended at Glancz in 251PA, returned to his newfounded capital, built around the lake where his people had gathered since before memory. From then on Zavlech displaced Trydlew as the political centre of the Vihri, and the House of Zav ruled from it until the senior line died out in 501PA.</p>
<p>The city has seen the great crises of the realm at first hand. In 377PA the Candleguard stood in the capital and Ras <a href="Zervind II" class="internal-link">Zervind II</a> was ruled in all but name from Iztaw. The eight great lords marched on Zavlech, the Candleguard stood down, and the settlement that followed made the lordships hereditary Vadcy and led to the <a href="Honoured Assembly" class="internal-link">Honoured Assembly</a> of 384PA. Zervind II died in the city in 388PA. The palace has since housed the Szinmo of Szievek, the House of Worocek, and from 692PA the House of Ralpia, chosen by the Assembly and the Gethric Bishops. Vormil III was born in Zavlech in 869PA, and the many roads for which he is remembered all lead toward it.</p>
<h2>Ruling House and Politics</h2>
<p>Zavlech and the crownlands around it belong to the crown and answer to no Var. Ras <a href="Vormil III" class="internal-link">Vormil III</a> of House Ralpia is old now and spends his days on the greater business of the realm, so the rule of the city itself has been left to his son, the Kisras <a href="Waśko Ralpia" class="internal-link">Waśko zi Ralpia</a>, who governs it from the palace on the lake. The arrangement suits the heir, who wants political and academic reform and a narrower role for the Church in governing the realm, and in the capital he has the means to try. His own seat is the castle of Lovno, a day's ride north of the city. The Honoured Assembly meets in the city, as does the Sekretar of Vihr, <a href="Tadeuz Pilsud" class="internal-link">Tadeuz Pilsud</a>, whose <a href="Vaergriff" class="internal-link">Vaergriff</a> keeps its offices in the palace precinct and is said to have ears in every quarter.</p>
<p>The Church in Zavlech is led by the Bishop of Zavlech, at present Wolod III. He is not the Archbishop of Vihr, for by long tradition that office belongs to the Bishop of Wogrov Cathedral in Rovgi. A detachment of the <a href="Candleguard" class="internal-link">Candleguard</a> keeps the cathedral, on church ground only, as the settlement of 377PA demands.</p>
<h2>Society and Culture</h2>
<p>The people of Zavlech are Lower Vihri of the river basin, and Gethric, with a good many Higher Vihri who came down from the moors for work and stayed. It is a crowded, loud city, old at its centre and new at its edges, and each of its quarters has a character of its own. The Court Quarter lies around the palace on the lakeshore, and the Red Hill is the old citadel quarter whose hill gives the city its arms. The Academy Quarter is full of students, scribes and printers. Along the Tryd the Quays belong to bargemen, porters and customs clerks, and by the western gate the Moor Quarter holds the families of the Higher Vihri.</p>
<p>Zavlech Academy is smaller than the University of Szievek, but it is still among the important schools of the realm, and the clerks and secretaries of the court are trained there. Beside the palace stand the Towers of Open Skies, two tall towers in which the Kar-Zavr, the court wizard, resides. The office of the Kar-Zavr conducts the Royal Aptitude Census every second year, a custom begun by <a href="Zovac Harlini" class="internal-link">Zovac Harlini</a>.</p>
<h2>Economy</h2>
<p>The Tryd makes Zavlech a trading city before it is a court. Grain and timber come down to the lake from the Expanse, wool and peat from the western moors, and silver from Silverslach. The barges that carry them are the busiest on any river in Vihr, and the crown keeps its customs house at the Quays. The wharves and warehouses are held by guilds, most of them tied to the <a href="Trazlov Consortium" class="internal-link">Consortium</a> as everywhere in Vihr, and the roads built under Vormil III bring the carts of the countryside to the city's markets.</p>
<h2>Places of Note</h2>
<p>The Royal Palace stands on the lakeshore, with the Towers of Open Skies beside it. The cathedral of the Bishop of Zavlech stands on the Red Hill, and the Quays and the great bridge over the Tryd are the commercial heart of the city. Round about lie Myrg, Szulbod and Vzsal, the river towns that feed it.</p>
