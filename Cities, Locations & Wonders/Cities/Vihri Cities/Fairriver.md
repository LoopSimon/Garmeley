<p>Fairriver is an abbey town on the Pilv at the southern tip of <a href="Selbia" class="internal-link">Selbia</a>, the last town of Vihr before the Grevi Mountains and <a href="Grevenna" class="internal-link">Grevenna</a>. It grew around the abbey that Saint <a href="Lyandra" class="internal-link">Lyandra</a> founded after the <a href="Eight Years War" class="internal-link">Eight Years War</a> as a haven for those lost and displaced by war, and it remains one today. The town is held in name by the Var of Selbia, but it is the head of the abbey, at present the Abbess Riora Datky, who rules it, and in the affairs of the realm it is practically neutral. Its abbey keeps the relics of Lyandra, the most popular saint of Grevenna, and draws pilgrims of all three great churches of the faith.</p>  
<h2>Geography</h2>  
<p>Fairriver lies in the valley of the Pilv, south of Itarsky, where the river leaves the hills of southern Selbia and runs on south toward Grevenna. To the east and south rise the Grevi Mountains, which are ogre country and dangerous to cross, and east of the town, north of the mountains, stands Edessa Tower, which both Vihr and Grevenna claim and neither holds. Downriver, just beyond the border, lies Miograd, the northernmost of the great cities of Grevenna. The Fairriver Valley is the western road from Vihr into Grevenna, and the twin spires of the abbey church can be seen from far up and down the river.</p>  
<h2>History</h2>  
<p>Before the abbey there was only a small ferry town on the Pilv, a crossing on the road between the Expanse and the lands of <a href="Rean" class="internal-link">Rean</a>. Vihr took the town and the valley from Rean at the end of the Rean war in 306PA, and they have been part of the realm ever since.</p>  
<p>The town became what it is through Lyandra. A Knight of the <a href="Order of the Silver Wheel" class="internal-link">Silver Wheel</a>, she spoke for peace through the Eight Years War, which Vihr fought against <a href="Garmeley" class="internal-link">Garmeley</a> from 644PA to 652PA, and in 653PA, the year after the war ended, she founded an abbey at the ferry town as a haven for the orphans, widows, deserters and refugees it had left behind. The abbey grew into the town, and the town grew around the abbey. Lyandra died in 690PA and was canonised the following year at the Conclave of 691PA. Her body was burned, her ashes were laid in a shrine in the mountains near Fairriver, and her relics were kept in the treasury of her abbey.</p>  
<p>In the <a href="Wars of Elbae Aggression" class="internal-link">Wars of Elbae Aggression</a> the Fairriver Valley was the western way into Grevenna, and Vihri armies and mercenaries marched through it to fight beside the Grevenni against the Sultanate. Grevenna has always counted the valley as part of Pilvia, as it counts everything west of the Opuzna River, but it has not pressed the claim for centuries, and the bond the two realms forged in the Elbae wars has left it a claim in name only. In 890PA, in the reign of Jakub II "the Good", Vihr and Grevenna concluded the Treaty of Fairriver. Under it pilvian traders who carry a seal of pass may cross into Vihr through the valley without paying customs, and the knights of Selbia and the warriors of Miograd fight together against the ogre clans of the Grevi Mountains.</p>  
<p>In the winter of 922PA the dead rose in the Pilv Valley. Abbot Hoster, old, sick and afraid of death, had forsaken his calling and made a pact with a travelling witch, Simila the Sweet, giving her the Shawl of Respite, the holiest of Lyandra's relics, in exchange for a talisman that would let him return from the dead. When he died he returned, and the dead of the valley rose and massed around him and terrorised the farmers. The adventurers known as <a href="Piero & Friends" class="internal-link">Piero & Friends</a>, who were in the valley at the time, killed the witch, returned the Shawl to the abbey and banished the undead abbot, and the affair has been known since as the Fairriver Incident.</p>  
<p>When the <a href="Garmish Succession Crisis" class="internal-link">Garmish Succession Crisis</a> broke out in 923PA, Garmish refugees came over the Fellpass into Vihr, and many of them found their way to Fairriver. Since Prince <a href="Jakub Ralpia" class="internal-link">Jakub</a> and his unofficial entourage of knights and sellswords entered the war, the pass has been closed to all but shipments arranged by the armies, and it lies firmly in the hands of the Green Banner League. In early 924PA the adventurers Marianna Kaminski and Perzuk Uz-Kram came to Fairriver after their adventures in Niedrest and stayed for a while. During their stay the Tintongue Clan, an ogre clan of the northern Grevi Mountains, took Perzuk in the mountains, together with pilgrims and travellers from the road to the abbey and Gianna Vulma, the young apprentice of the Archmage <a href="Rigoran" class="internal-link">Rigoran</a> of Edessa Tower. Marianna went after them and freed them. Rigoran showed his gratitude by welcoming them at Edessa Tower and later helping Marianna, and in the summer of 925PA he travelled to Fairriver and raised defensive wards near the town, saying that he owed Marianna a debt.</p>  
<h2>Government and Politics</h2>  
<p>Fairriver belongs to the Vadcy of Selbia, and the Var of Selbia is its lord in law, but the town is governed by the head of the abbey, and the Var leaves it to them. The head of the abbey is elected by its brothers and sisters from among their own number and confirmed by the Bishop of Selbia at <a href="Mesziev" class="internal-link">Mesziev</a>. Every Abbot of the <a href="Church of Iztaw" class="internal-link">Church of Iztaw</a> is at least an ordained Minister, a rank no woman may hold, but Fairriver has a long history of electing women as well as men, and for the sake of Lyandra the Church has tolerated it and grants an Abbess of Fairriver the standing of a Minister. The wealth of the abbey, its pilgrims and its hold on the town make its head more powerful than any Deacon or Vicar of the province. The present Abbess is Riora Datky, who was elected after the death of Hoster in 922PA and has worked since to restore the good name of the abbey. She is a friend of <a href="Jana Rikolore" class="internal-link">Jana Rikolore</a>, the cleric who helped to end the Fairriver Incident.</p>  
<p>The town takes no side in the quarrels of the realm, and both Selbia and Grevenna find it useful that it does not. The Silver Wheel once kept a strong chapterhouse at Fairriver in honour of Lyandra, but like the order itself it has dwindled, and only a few knights of the Wheel still live there. They keep watch over the shrine of Lyandra in the mountains. Marianna Kaminski, a devout follower of Lyandra, was sent to Fairriver at the age of fifteen and trained there under Sir Zanislaw of Morset, a Paladin of the Silver Wheel, and she has kept a close bond with the town ever since.</p>  
<h2>Society and Culture</h2>  
<p>The people of Fairriver are Sarloni, like most of the people of Selbia, with a large minority of Pilvians from across the border, and the town is Gethric and devout. It is a town of refugees as much as of townsmen. The abbey takes in orphans and the displaced of wars all over Ilrakhan, and many of them stay. Pilgrims come to Fairriver from every church of the faith, Trinicist, Kethric and Gethric alike, to pray at the abbey and to climb to the shrine of Lyandra. She is loved above all by the common folk and by the people of Grevenna, for whom she is the most beloved figure of the faith after the Prophet himself.</p>  
<h2>Economy</h2>  
<p>Fairriver lives on its pilgrims and on the road. The Fairriver Valley is the western trade road into Grevenna, and since the Treaty of Fairriver the pilvian traders who pass through it with their seals of pass have brought more trade to the town than ever. The <a href="Trazlov Consortium" class="internal-link">Consortium</a> keeps an outpost at Fairriver. The farms and mines of the Pilv Valley around it are rich, and the abbey owns a good part of them.</p>  
<h2>Places of Note</h2>  
<p>The Abbey of Saint Lyandra stands at the heart of the town, with its twin-spired church, its hospice and its orphanage. Its treasury keeps the relics of the saint, the Shawl of Respite, the White Blade and the Cuirass of Mercy. The shrine where her ashes are kept lies in the mountains near the town. The ferry over the Pilv still crosses below the abbey, and the old chapterhouse of the Silver Wheel stands half empty beside it. The wards that Rigoran raised in 925PA stand on the approaches to the town from the mountains.</p>
