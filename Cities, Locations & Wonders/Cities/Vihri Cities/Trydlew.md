<p>Trydlew is the capital of the Vadcy of Breza, a river city on the Tryd, and the oldest seat of the Vihri. It is called the Old Capital, or simply old Tryd, because it was the sacred and political heart of the Vihrmen long before Zavlech was a capital at all. Today it is a quieter city than its younger rival, but it is proud of its past, and it is famous across Vihr for the Knights of the Unicorn and for the great horse tournaments held beneath its walls.</p>
<h2>Geography</h2>
<p>Trydlew stands on the Tryd in the central river country of the Expanse, upstream of <a href="Zavlech" class="internal-link">Zavlech</a> and south-east of it. The plains around it are flat and rich, good for grain and better for horses. The town of Pinopki lies close by, and to the south the land runs on to Atej, and farther still to Ibozka in <a href="Selbia" class="internal-link">Selbia</a>. The Fortress of Madzar, headquarters of the Knights of the Unicorn, stands on the outskirts.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Trydlew was a holy place before it was a city. The Ullkustone, the monument of Ollku, the god of river and sea, stood here, and pilgrims came to it from across the Vihri world. When the great tribes joined in the <a href="Vihrmen Federation" class="internal-link">Vihrmen Federation</a> in about 130PA, the most important pacts of the Horn Lords were sworn at the stone, though the Lords themselves met at Tlokyemy. Trydlew was the Federation's capital in everything but name.</p>
<p>That ended with the Kingdom. Viago of the Zav proclaimed it in 241PA, and after the Unification Wars his seat at Zavlech took over the business of the realm. The Church was given every support, and the old sacred sites were reconsecrated or abandoned, but the Ullkustone was left standing, a restraint that later centuries have found hard to explain. Trydlew remained a great city of the Expanse, and in 377PA the lords of Breza were among the eight who marched on Zavlech. In 529PA the Sarloni Uprising of the Expanse rose against the lords of Breza, Opuzna and Selbia, and was put down. <a href="Tytus" class="internal-link">Saint Tytus</a> was born at Pinopki nearby in 514PA.</p>
<p>Trydlew's greatest son is <a href="Andre" class="internal-link">Andre</a>, Gri of Trydlew, born in 820PA. In the War of Horn and Fang he led the armies of Vihr, and he killed the Meadi sorceress Muriel von Gloss at Gadly Bridge in 865PA. In 869PA he founded the Knights of the Unicorn at Madzar. He died in 874PA, perhaps poisoned, and was canonised in 876PA. Trinic Bishops reject him as a symbol of the corruption of Iztaw, but in Trydlew he is simply the Unicorn Knight.</p>
<h2>Ruling House and Politics</h2>
<p>Trydlew is the seat of the Varina of Breza, at present Milva of House Dibrevi. As the lord of one of the eight ancient lordships, the Varina sits in the <a href="Honoured Assembly" class="internal-link">Honoured Assembly</a>, and she is one of only three women among the nine Var. The title Gri of Trydlew, which Andre made famous, was not renewed after his death, and his line now holds the county of Atej to the south. The Knights of the Unicorn, strictly devoted to the Throne by name, keep their own counsel at Madzar.</p>
<h2>Society and Culture</h2>
<p>The people of Trydlew are Sarloni and Gethric, and they remember that they were Vihri before there was a Vihr. The Ullkustone is still respected, oddly so in so devout a country. Gethric pilgrims and old river families both leave offerings at its foot, and a small Gethric chapel stands beside it. What the Church thinks of this it has mostly kept to itself.</p>
<p>Horses are the city's passion. Its tournaments are the largest in the realm: riders and knights come from across the Expanse and from beyond Vihr, and the Knights of the Unicorn, mounted on their great unicorns, open the lists. The lists are a matter of honour for the lords of Breza, and a victory at Trydlew is remembered for a lifetime.</p>
<h2>Economy</h2>
<p>The Tryd carries the grain and timber of Breza down to Zavlech, and the barges that return bring the goods of the capital and of the <a href="Trazlov Consortium" class="internal-link">Consortium</a>. The city breeds and sells warhorses, and the tournaments bring in visitors, smiths, saddlers and sellers of every kind. The Order spends its substantial finances at Madzar and in the city around it.</p>
<h2>Places of Note</h2>
<p>The Ullkustone stands on the river bank, with its chapel beside it. The Fortress of Madzar, on the outskirts, is the headquarters of the Knights of the Unicorn and a paramilitary base of its own. The Tourney Fields lie outside the walls, and the old bridge over the Tryd joins the two halves of the city. Pinopki, the birthplace of Saint Tytus, is the neighbouring town, and Atej, the seat of Andre's line, lies to the south.</p>
