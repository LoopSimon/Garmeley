<div class="wiki-infobox"> <h4 class="wiki-header">Dolm</h4> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Country</strong> <span><a href="Garmeley" class="internal-link">Duchy of Garmeley</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Government</strong> <span>Mining city, surrendered to the Garmish Alliance</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Primary Religion</strong> <span><a href="Kindly Lords" class="internal-link">Kindly Lords</a>, Gods of Forge and Fire</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Primary Culture</strong> <span><a href="Urien" class="internal-link">Garmish</a> <a href="Human" class="internal-link">Human</a>, Dwarf</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Official Language</strong> <span>Urien</span> </div> </div> <p>Dolm is the mining city of western Garmeley, built on and into Dolm Mountain at the foot of the Lock. Its silver has made the Duchy rich for more than three centuries, and a large dwarven community works its mines. It is a mountain town of tunnels and fortified shafts, and in the Succession Crisis it held out against the southern armies for almost three years.</p> <h2>Geography</h2> <p>Dolm stands in the hills of the Lock, in the west of the Duchy, with the Trinmo and the Kerlaen Wilderness to the west and south. Dolm Mountain rises above the town, honeycombed with shafts, and the lands around it are poor and stony, good for little but sheep. The road from Redflow comes in from the east through the hills, and the road to Tringlen and Forrington runs south along the river. The Montegru Estate lies near the town.</p> <h2>History</h2> <p>Dolm is best known for the Dolm Catastrophe of 609PA. Beneath the mountain lay a vast complex of settlements, housing over a hundred thousand morlocks, a peaceful folk who had adapted to the dark. When Garmish excavators found the great silver under the mountain, they redirected the nearby river into the morlock shafts, under the guise of a hostile attack by the natives, and drowned them. The mines of Dolm are the result, and the silver has fed the Garmsmark ever since. The lower shafts have been closed ever since.</p> <p>In the ages that followed, Dolm grew rich, and the town became a place of some independence of mind, with a proud dwarven community, a handful of noble families and a mountain that made it hard to take. The Montegru, a red-haired noble family, were the best known of them.</p> <p>When the Succession Crisis broke out in 923PA, Dolm declared for the Green Banner League, largely in an effort to gain independence from Forrington and Tringlen. The siege of Dolm began in the early autumn of that year. The city had impressive mountain fortifications and a maze of tunnels, and the southern forces that sat around it were never able to take it by storm. Coster did not march to relieve the city, for the odds were against him, and the defenders held on alone. Dolm surrendered in late 926PA, after almost three years. The Trinvale cities were now connected by land to Hainach and the south of the Duchy, and the Alliance marched north to Redflow. The nobles of the town were taken into custody.</p> <h2>Society and Culture</h2> <p>Dolm is the city of the dwarves of Garmeley. They keep the mines and the smithies, and they honour the Gods of Forge and Fire and respect the Kindly Lords of the country they live in. The Garmish of the town follow the Kindly Lords. The Montegru were the first family of the town, and their daughters did not always behave as the town expected: Elena Montegru, born in 897PA as the third daughter and fifth child of her house, was a duellist who left Dolm to travel with Piero and Friends and came back to it in 923PA, shortly before the siege began.</p> <h2>Economy</h2> <p>Silver is the town's wealth. The ore is smelted in the town and carried east and south to the mints of the Duchy, to be struck into the Mark. The dwarves also work iron, and the smiths of Dolm make the finest weapons in the Duchy. The siege ruined the mines for a time, and the silver trade of the west is only now starting again under the Alliance.</p> <h2>Places of Note</h2> <p>Dolm Mountain and its mines are the heart of the town, and its upper gallery is a fortress of its own. The Montegru Estate lies near the town. The lower shafts, which were once the morlock settlements, are closed.</p> <h2>People of Interest</h2> <p><a href="Elena Montegru" class="internal-link">Elena Montegru</a>, duellist of House Montegru</p>
