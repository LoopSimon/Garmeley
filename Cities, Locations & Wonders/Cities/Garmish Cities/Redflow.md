<div class="wiki-infobox"> <h4 class="wiki-header">Redflow</h4> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Country</strong> <span><a href="Garmeley" class="internal-link">Duchy of Garmeley</a>, Capital</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Government</strong> <span>Ducal seat, besieged</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Population</strong> <span>ca. 30000</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Primary Religion</strong> <a href="Kindly Lords" class="internal-link">Kindly Lords</a> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Primary Culture</strong> <span><a href="Urien" class="internal-link">Garmish</a> <a href="Human" class="internal-link">Human</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Official Language</strong> <span>Urien</span> </div> </div> <p>Redflow is the capital of Garmeley and the seat of the Riverthrone, a city of about 30,000 people on the Melm in the middle of the Duchy. It is the oldest court of the Garmish, and since the last days of 926PA it has also been a besieged one. The armies of the Garmish Alliance lie round its walls, and the Keep that holds the Riverthrone is held against them for the Green Banner League.</p> <h2>Geography</h2> <p>Redflow stands on the Melm half-way between Jorrenstead in the north and Lake Forr in the south, with the hills of the Lock rising in the west and the poor heath of the Barrens to the south-east. In the spring floods the river carries down the red clay of the Lock, and it runs the colour of rust past the quays, which is how the city is said to have got its name. A watchtower at Idrik's Point guards the road to the north-east, and to the south-west lies Karolrest, named for the Karolian, the spirit who walks the burrows of the middle country and watches over the dead who lie in them.</p> <h2>History</h2> <p>The Riverkings of Garm held their court at Redflow, and the Riverthrone that Coster of Melmbirth claims by ancient custom is the throne of the Keep. When King Ahlef II fell at Greyfields in 648PA and the line of Alfin ended, Tyla of Aldale did not take the crown but proclaimed herself Duchess, and her house took the name of the city. The Dukes of House Redflow ruled from the same Keep for nearly three centuries, and the last of them was Agnes I.</p> <p>The city has also been a place of learning. Redflow College is the great school of the Duchy, and the historian <a href="Meynar of Jorrenstead" class="internal-link">Meynar of Jorrenstead</a> studied there as a young man. In the same streets a street child named Meyzar Rugior was taken in by the sorceress Tessia Anler, and he became the Archmage of Patwin Tower.</p> <p>The Succession Crisis came to Redflow late. Coster's league held the capital through the early years of the war, because Kylian, the castellan of the Keep, had declared for the Green Banner. Kylian died at Jestrick Fields in 925PA, and his son Almos took the castellany. In the last days of 926PA, with Dolm fallen, the Alliance marched north and laid siege to the Keep. In February 927PA its leader Hynek of Forrington was killed by an assassin in his camp south of the siege works, and the siege has continued without him.</p> <h2>Society and Culture</h2> <p>Redflow is the most mixed city of the Duchy. Most of its people are Garmish and follow the Kindly Lords, and a minority are Gethric. A good many of the gnomes and elves of the Duchy live in the city, and its streets are full of refugees from the burned countryside. The people of Redflow have always thought of themselves as the keepers of the Riverthrone, whoever sits on it, and they are in no hurry to choose between the north and the south.</p> <h2>Places of Note</h2> <p>Redflow Keep stands above the river and holds the Riverthrone. Redflow College is the great school of the Duchy, and the bridge over the Melm is the heart of the city's trade. Beyond the walls lie Idrik's Point and Karolrest.</p> <h2>People of Interest</h2> <p>Almos Brezina, Castellan of Redflow Keep<br><a href="Agnes I" class="internal-link">Agnes I of House Redflow</a>, late Duchess of Garmeley<br><a href="Hynek of Forrington" class="internal-link">Hynek of House Forr</a>, killed outside the city in 927PA<br><a href="Rugior" class="internal-link">Meyzar Rugior</a>, born in Redflow, late Archmage of Patwin Tower</p>
