<div class="wiki-infobox"> <h4 class="wiki-header">Forrington</h4> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Country</strong> <span><a href="Garmeley" class="internal-link">Duchy of Garmeley</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Government</strong> <span>County, held by House Forr</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Population</strong> <span>ca. 44000</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Primary Religion</strong> <a href="Kindly Lords" class="internal-link">Kindly Lords</a> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Primary Culture</strong> <span><a href="Urien" class="internal-link">Garmish</a> <a href="Human" class="internal-link">Human</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Official Language</strong> <span>Urien</span> </div> </div> <p>Forrington is the largest city of Garmeley, a trading town of some 44,000 people on the southern shore of Lake Forr, where the Melm runs into the lake. It is the seat of the Counts of Forrington, the richest of the Garmish lords, and the centre of the southern cause in the Succession Crisis. It stands in the oldest part of the country, for it was around Lake Forr that the Tribes of Garm lived before the Rakhians came.</p> <h2>Geography</h2> <p>The lake is the making of the city. The Melm brings down the grain and silver of the interior from Redflow and the north, and the Trinmo passes the lake on its way to the sea, so that the barge traffic of the Duchy meets at Forrington. The town stands on the flat shore, and the keep of the Counts stands on the lake shore east of the town. South of the city lie the Garmian Flatlands, with the towns of Sedlik and Gredec, and in the west the road runs to Tringlen.</p> <h2>History</h2> <p>The Counts of Forrington have been among the great lords of the Duchy since its founding as a duchy, and the Count sits on the Council of Lords. Count Deydrik of Forrington took the field in the Second War of Elbae Aggression, and the half-elven archmage Avero Kelitz, who later led Patwin Tower, was born in the city in 621PA.</p> <p>The city's great moment came with Hynek of Forr, who was one of the richest nobles of the Duchy. When the Duchess Agnes died in 922PA he was her heir by blood, and he gathered the southern lords around him as the Garmish Alliance. In 923PA his younger sister Regia was taken from the Vormy Estate by the Green Banner League, and he declared open war. Forrington became the war capital of the south. In 924PA Baron Roderick of Aldale gathered the southern army here before marching on Sunminster, and the plunder of the sacked church was shipped to the city afterwards, a fact that the north has not forgotten.</p> <p>Hynek was killed outside Redflow in February 927PA. His young son Milos is now Count of Forrington, and Regia is still held by the League.</p> <h2>Society and Culture</h2> <p>Forrington is the great city of the Kindly Lords. Its people honour the Farrman, the water spirit of Lake Forr, and the Farrman is worshipped here more than anywhere else in the Duchy. Offerings are thrown into the lake at the start of the fishing season, and the boatmen would sooner lose a day's catch than a custom. The priests and druids of the Farrman have taken up arms for the south and fight beside the Count's levies, which is unusual among the followers of the Kindly Lords. The city has a Gethric minority, and the Gethric merchants of Trinvale who trade here have always been made welcome.</p> <p>Forrington is a rich and confident city, and the fact that it is richer than Redflow is not forgotten in the capital. Many of its people think of the Count as the true head of the Duchy, whatever the law says.</p> <h2>Economy</h2> <p>The lake fisheries feed the town, and the barges carry the Duchy's grain and silver down the Trinmo to the sea and up it to Niedrest. The trade with Trinvale, which the city has carried on for generations, is one reason the Trinvale cities have taken the southern side. Craftsmen and boatbuilders fill the quarters along the shore, and the moneychangers of the harbour deal in the Garmsmark and, increasingly, in foreign coin.</p> <h2>Places of Note</h2> <p>The Keep of the Counts stands on the lake shore, and the harbour and its warehouses make up the commercial heart of the city. The shrine of the Farrman stands on the lake side of the town. Round about lie Sedlik, Gredec and Tringlen.</p> <h2>People of Interest</h2> <p><a href="Hynek of Forrington" class="internal-link">Hynek of House Forr</a>, late Count of Forrington, leader of the Garmish Alliance<br>Milos of House Forr, Count of Forrington<br>Regia of House Forr, sister of Hynek, held by the Green Banner League<br><a href="Avero" class="internal-link">Avero Kelitz</a>, born in Forrington, late Archmage of Patwin Tower</p>
