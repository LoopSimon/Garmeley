<div class="wiki-infobox"> <h4 class="wiki-header">Sunminster</h4> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Country</strong> <span><a href="Garmeley" class="internal-link">Duchy of Garmeley</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Government</strong> <span>Holy city of the Leafpriests</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Primary Religion</strong> <span><a href="Kindly Lords" class="internal-link">Kindly Lords</a>, <a href="Gethricism" class="internal-link">Gethricism</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Primary Culture</strong> <span><a href="Urien" class="internal-link">Garmish</a> <a href="Human" class="internal-link">Human</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Official Language</strong> <span>Urien</span> </div> </div> <p>Sunminster is the holy city of the Kindly Lords in Garmeley, a church town in the middle of the Duchy, south of the Barrens. It is the most revered seat of the Leafpriests, the diviners and oracles of the Kindly Lords, and the chief place where the Karolian is worshipped, and it also holds one of the oldest Gethric bishoprics in the country. For most of its history it has stood outside the quarrels of the lords. In 924PA, in the Succession Crisis, that neutrality failed, and the city was sacked.</p> <h2>Geography</h2> <p>Sunminster stands among old groves on the open ground south of the Barrens, between the Melm and the Kol River. The city is small, and its streets climb round a red-spired church that can be seen from a long way off. The roads of the middle country meet here, and pilgrims from the whole Duchy come to its festivals.</p> <h2>History</h2> <p>Sunminster was a sacred place long before it was a city. The groves around it were places of worship in the days of the druids, and in the Silver Age, when the Gethric faith came to Garmeley and was resisted by most of the people, a bishopric was established here, among the Garmish who would hear it. The see is now one of the oldest in the country. The Leafpriests and the Bishop lived side by side for centuries, and the city came to count itself as a place that belonged to both of them. The Leafpriests of the city are asked to read the omens when a Duke is chosen, and the Old Pact of the Riverkings is remembered in their rites.</p> <p>In 924PA, a year after the war began, the southern army of Baron Roderick of Aldale marched on the city. Roderick, who had secured Koltree, denounced the northern lords as Gethric barbarians and villains, and the token force of the city yielded after a minor engagement. Bishop Pityr was taken as a hostage, and the cathedral was looted of its relics and gold, which were shipped south to Forrington. The Leafpriests' own sanctuary was left untouched. The north says that this shows what the Alliance is, and the south says that it spared what was holy, and in the rest of the continent the sack was taken as the point at which a quarrel of lords became a war of faiths. Eldmar and Urbridge pledged themselves to the north in 926PA.</p> <h2>Society and Culture</h2> <p>Sunminster is a quiet place by the standards of the Duchy, with more priests than soldiers. The Leafpriests are not a church. They are oracles, soothsayers, augurs and druids who speak between the spirits and the common people, and they have no ranks to speak of. They carry a sprig of leaves as the sign of their calling. Most of the Garmish in the countryside around the city are followers of the Kindly Lords, with a Gethric minority, and the town has been one of the few places in the country where the two faiths have lived without trouble.</p> <p>The Karolian, the hooded spirit who guards the dead in the burrows, is worshipped at Sunminster more than anywhere else, for the burrows of the middle country lie just to the north. Families come to ask the Leafpriests to carry a word to the spirit about their dead.</p> <p>The best known Leafpriest of Sunminster, Mirosh Gocar, has not yet declared for either side, and the Leafpriests are as divided over the war as the rest of the faith.</p> <h2>Places of Note</h2> <p>The great church with the red spire is the cathedral of the bishopric, now stripped of its treasures. The groves and the sanctuary of the Leafpriests lie behind it, and the shrine of the Karolian stands among the trees. Round about lie Koltree, Egerlin and the Barrens.</p> <h2>People of Interest</h2> <p>Mirosh Gocar, Leafpriest of Sunminster, undeclared<br>Pityr, Bishop of Sunminster, taken hostage in 924PA<br>Roderick of House Jiric, Baron of Aldale, who took the city</p>>
