<p>Izhevk is a Grachy city on the bend of the Jaerflow River, sitting beneath the Jaerfall Waterfall and opposite a small cluster of hills and mountains known as the Irdknuckles, it is a major hub of commerce within the more rural east and north of Grachy. The small villages of Arville & Flassheym to the north of the city are officially part of the cities sphere of influence and under its jurisdiction, serving as important hubs of the massive amount of farmlands and fields north of the walls.</p>
<p>The City is home to the Church of Remembrance, also known as St <a href="Igor" class="internal-link">Igor's </a> Church but usually not called this due to the larger and more important church of the same name in the city of Modva, Izhevk Castle, and the Grey Mausoleum. The Jaerfall makes shipping from further up the river and the areas around Trozk impossible without coming through the land route into the city, similarly the many farmlands around Izhevk supply its northern neighbour Kharnov with valuable produce so the land routes coming in and out of Izhevk have made it a very popular destination for merchant caravans. The City profits from the political influence of its elected Leader, Gram Ruda, who's popular standing within the Traditionalist Faction of the Grand Ushkyn at Halgrach is very helpful in regards to expansionist charters granted by the central authority of the Republic. This has led to an extremely aggressive policy of deforestation and settlement along the northern Jaerflow, as Izhevks Boyars have begun to compete with the entrenched and rich Landowners of the West.</p>

<h2>Demographics</h2>
<p>The city is a majority grachy human settlement although humans from vihr and various bykmali traders make up small minorities. The largest minority however are Mistspire dwarves who enjoy great privilege in the city and are even given an honorary seat at the Ushykn of the Region for their contributions to Izhevks growth and prosperity, many of them stem from the communities around <a href="Tizin-Kol " class="internal-link">Tizin-Kol </a> originally but are now a few generations removed from initial migrants.</p>
<h2>Government and Politics</h2>
<p>Like all regional Capitals in the Republic, Izhevk is governed by a Gram elected by the Ushkyn, currently a position held by Eril zi Ruda, an influential and powerful politician on the national stage, proud traditionalist and ambitious leader, aiming to build out the traditionally poorer backwater of eastern Grachy. The Ushkyn of the city convenes in the Orsrag Hall where zi Ruda holds court as well. </p>
<h2>Economy</h2>
<p>The lifeblood of Izhevks economy is entirely dictated by its geography, most notably the roar of the Jaerfall. Because the massive waterfall  urther upstream makes continuous upriver shipping impossible, any merchant, miner, or trapper traveling down the Jaerflow must dock at Izhevk, offload their cargo, and portage it through the city to continue westward along the Zarsroad. This geographical chokepoint has transformed Izhevk into a remarkably wealthy toll and trading hub.</p>
<p>The city exports three primary resources: timber, stone, and grain. Gram Rudas aggressive expansionist policies have led to massive logging camps far north of the cities immediate outskirts and farmlands, feeding a growing timber industry that floats lumber down to the shipyards of the west. To the south, the Lavek and Bonemill mines in the Irdknuckles provide a steady stream of iron and raw stone, overseen largely by the city's privileged dwarven minority. Finally, the vast, fertile farmlands extending toward Arville and Flassheym produce a massive agricultural surplus, making Izhevk the primary breadbasket for the eastern Republic. The wealth generated from this trade is aggressively managed in the Oldmarket at the Kalny Exchange, where the local burghers and Boyars constantly vie for control over the tariffs.</p>
<h2>Izhevk Casle & Riverview</h2>
<p>The Jaerstone Keep sits on the southern side of the Jaerflow across the Iron bridge, it was the residence of the zi Beriz Lines for houndreds of years until their Exile following the Boyars Rebellion of 571PA. It has been the Seat of the Gram in the days of the Grachy Republic and is currently the residence of the House of zi Ruda.</p>
<p>Riverview Tower, often called simply just Riverview is the cities prison and regarded as one of the safest dungeons in all of eastern Grachy, its Tower complex reaches many corridors high but also stretches underground and is well guarded by the City Watch. Many criminals from all over the Jaerflow meet their final days in Riverview.</p>
<h2>Church of Remembrance</h2>
<p>Officially "Saint Igors Church" but almost never referred to as such due to aforementioned reasons, the Church of Remembrance is nevertheless the largest religious building east of Halgrach and a main attraction for regional pilgrimages. Saint Igor, the Patron of Grachy, is worshipped here more than any other gethric Saint and the Church is said to hold many relics associated with Igor in its sacristy. Notably the Church complex is also home to the Nightingale Library, one of the largest archives of knowledge in the entirety of Nostroya, well kept by the Candleflame Guard. The Church is overseen by Bisop Dreywin, a younger member of the clergy that has risen within its ranks due to his sense for people and a critical sense for politics. The clergy of Izhevk keeps a good relationship with the Gram and supports the zi Rudas politics of local expansionism.</p>
<h2>Grey Mausoleum</h2>
<p>The Grey Mausoleum of Izhevk is a burial complex initially build in 469PA  by Ras Ivan II of Jerinov for members of his dynasty, but was later also used by members of the of Stir Dynasty that succeeded the of Jerinovs. The Grey Mausoleum lies south-west of the City walls in the Jaerhills north of the River. </p>
<h2>Dusttown & Igorsfield</h2>
<p>The areas south of the River are pressed between the rising mountains of the Jaerhills and the riverfront itself. Between Izhevk Castle and Riverview Prison lies Dusttown, a sprawling community making use of the space and largely concerned with the day to day mining operations near the city itsel, as the Jaerhills are home to a few mines like Lavek Mine and Bonemill Mine relatively close to the city.</p>
<p>The Igorsfield is an open space east of Dusttown surrounding the other side of Riverview, its north a small wood near the river and its central and larger part and open field used for military exercise, tournament, games and other attractions, as well as ceremonies and parades of worldly and religious nature.</p>
<p>The Three main bridges of Izhevk connect the southern parts to the central city, the oldest bridge is the Iron Bridge spanning from Izhevk Castle to the small island of Irwig and then the southern part of Izhevks Oldtown. Jaerbridge in the centre of the city is the largest Bridge and build connecting Dusttown and Riverview with the city itself, when Izhevk kept prospering. Newbridge, aptly named, is the northernmost bridge and has only finished construction recently, mainly sponsored by various guilds and local businesses that aim to connect the remaining available space south of the river to the centre of the city.</p>
<h2>Oldmarket and Zarsroad</h2>
<p>The Zarsroad cuts through the entire City and is the central avenue for all traffic within Izhevk, flowing from the Springsgate in the west to the Tergate in the east. In the centre of Zarsroad, connecting it northwards to the Greengate and southwards to the Jaerbridge and Dusttown lies the Oldmarket. Oldmarket is a large square within Izhevk and home to the daily market of goods from out and inside the city, as well as various important commercial and administrative buildings such as the Orsrag Hall where the Ushkyn meets, the famous Lekeib & Zerek House of Commerce and the Kalny Exchange.</p>
<p>The Zarsroad leaves the city westwards and continues on to Melesk and later the Capital City Halgrach going further alongside the Halgior River flowing through southern Grachy by the cities of Bryansk, Modva and Ustikiv finally ending in the Port City of Voblag itself. It is the most important commercial lane of land travel in the entire Republic.</p>
<h2>West Izhevk</h2>
<p>Considered the secure, commercial anchor of the city, West Izhevks streets  are properly paved with cobblestone, and the architecture shifts from standard Grachian timber frames to stout, imposing stone masonry. It is the administrative heart of the city's wealth, housing the heavily fortified City Treasury and a collection of highly reputable, expensive inns catering to traveling magnates, rich boyars and foreign dignitaries.</p>
<p>West Izhevk is also uniquely cosmopolitan, acting as the primary enclave for the citys  dwarven population. Unlike in other Grachian cities where non-humans might be marginalized, dwarves here are celebrated masons, engineers and wealthy merchants. The skyline of the district reflects this: rising alongside the traditional Grachian onion domes of the Everwarm Church, a vibrant parish where the hearths are kept perpetually burnin, is the stark, geometric stone architecture of a sanctioned <a href="Terem-Zal " class="internal-link">Terem-Zal </a> temple, a massive political concession granted by the Ushkyn to keep the dwarven wealth flowing into the city.</p>

<h2>Northside</h2>
<p>The wealthiest area of the City, Northside is home to the Villas, Estates and Cottages of various Boyars and the like, as well as the tallest building in the city, Borni Tower. Constructed by the Astronomer Gregory Borni in the 9th century, the Tower is home to an impressive Observatorium and a mark of pride a city this far east, a region considered uneducated and poor. The mysterious occultist and sorcerer <a href="Antonin Slowoska" class="internal-link">Antonin Slowoska</a> currently resides within Borni Tower but is rarely seen outside of it. </p>
<h2>The Rags</h2>
<p>Pressed precariously against the southeastern riverbank is a sprawling, suffocating maze of leaning wooden hovels, muddy alleyways, and rotting docks. It is the poorest district within the city walls, perpetually smelling of river silt, gutted fish, and cheap, fermented birch beer. During the spring thaw, the lower streets routinely flood, turning the district into a miserable swamp.</p>
<p>Because it borders the upper Jaerflow just before the waterfall, the Rags are the arrival point for the rugged trappers, loggers, and river merchants coming from the eastern wilds. At the southernmost tip of the district sits the Jaerkontor, a massive and fortified trading house. Heavily guarded by private mercenaries and the City Watch, the Kontor operates like an island of untouchable wealth amidst an ocean of crushing poverty. Everything outside the Kontors gates is fiercely ruled by local gangs, smugglers, and river-rats, making The Rags an incredibly dangerous place for anyone wearing a heavy coin purse after dark.</p>
<h2>Greengate Slums & The House of Fortune</h2>
<p>As Izhevks wealth grew, so too did its population, eventually spilling out beyond the protection of the northern stone walls. The Greengate Slums formed as a shantytown along the dirt roads leading toward the northern farmlands. It is a chaotic, unplanned sprawl of muddy, rutted streets, thatched-roof shacks, and livestock pens. Populated largely by impoverished agricultural laborers, transient farmhands, and desperate refugees looking for work in the Grams expanding logging camps, the slums are entirely ignored by the citys ruling class. The City Watch rarely patrols past the Greengate, viewing the world outside the walls as lawless territory. When the harsh Nostroyan winter sets in, the slums become a brutal, freezing battleground for survival, entirely disconnected from the warmth and prosperity of the city just a few hundred yards away.</p>
<p>Deep within the unpatrolled labyrinth of the Greengate Slums sits a building that commands more power than many Boyars estate: The House of Fortune, ruthlessly operated by <a href="Livia Bistovka" class="internal-link">Livia Bistovka</a>. Originally a massive, sprawling grain barn, it has been heavily reinforced and converted into the premier illicit casino and vice den of the region. Lanterns glow warmly through its shuttered windows at all hours of the night, bleeding the sounds of clinking coins, roaring pit-fights, and lively string music into the muddy streets.</p>
