<p>Halgrach is the current Capital of the <a href="Grachy" class="internal-link">Holy Ushkyn Republic of Grachy</a> and the former capital of the <a href="Halgrach" class="internal-link">Rascy of Halgrach</a> whose name stems from the city itself, the City predates both entities and was founded during the so called "Age of Dispersion" following the Istrov Pact. Halgrach is the second largest city in the Republic and <a href="Nostroya" class="internal-link">Nostroya</a> in general after Voblag and sits on the Halgior River, along the Zarsrod.</p>
<p>As the Capital of the Republic and home of the Grand Ushkyn, Halgrach and its surroundings are theoretically governed by the Bagram of the Republic himself, currently <a href="Ilya Zemov" class="internal-link">Ilya Zemov</a>, although in practice have been largely ruled by the Rasazky Family of powerful Boyars since before the Fall of the Monarchy. The Gram of Halgrach, the smallest geographical Gram in all of the Republic, is officially ruled by Gram Oleg Zemov, Ilyas younger brother.</p>
