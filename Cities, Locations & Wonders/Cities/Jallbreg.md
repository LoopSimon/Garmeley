<p>The City of Jallbreg lies on the Hagshorn Peninsula, south of the Blind Heights mountains on the shores of the Torn Sea and is officially a part of the Kingdom of Baeling, although has become increasingly more autonomous in recent years. Jallbreg was founded in 411PA as a free trading settlement and became part of the great <a href="Baeling Empire" class="internal-link">Baeling Empire</a> of <a href="Valund Baeling" class="internal-link">Valund Baeling</a> in the mid 5th century. It is one of more important trading hubs in the Torn Sea and many vessels dock on its harbour crossing between Trykkerog, Nostroya and Urland.</p>
<p>Jallbreg is traditionally ruled by a Warder appointed by the Baeling King, currently the olbellinge noble Ingmar av Dalgån, and the position has been increasingly likened to a foreign oppressor and tax collector rather than a local lord who bleeds and fights for the City and her people.</p>
<p>While historically close to the Bykmåli of Trykkerog and politically under the umbrella of the Baeling, Jallbreg has always retained proud and unique local tradition and strived to as much autonomy and freedom as possible. This has recently resulted in a local movement for full independence from the Baeling Crown, the town is essentially a diverse mix of Bykmål, Vihri, Wermian and Urien peoples without any clear majority and its industry and strategic value as a central trade port in the Torn Sea allow it to project an air of self-reliance and autarky. This secession movement is supported en large by the Free City of Uhl to its west and the Kingdom of Juelfluk in the north-east. </p>
<p>The Queens and Kings of Jueli are the oldest rivals to the Baeling and the loss of the city and the hags horn territories would strike a significant blow towards Baeling military and economic power. The City of Uhl on the other hand aims to break the hold of powerful Kingdoms over free trade and shipping in the Torn Sea seeing as it itself is the largest port on the coasts, a free Jallbreg would also require new "protection" in the form of powerful friends and it is likely the Council of Uhl would enjoy replacing the Baeling Kings themselves.</p>
