<p>The City of Jallbreg lies on the Hagshorn Peninsular, south of the Blind Heights mountains on the shores of the Torn Sea and is officially a part of the Kingdom of Baeling, although has become increasingly more autonomous in recent years. Jallbreg was founded in 411PA as a free trading settlement and became part of the great <a href="Baeling Empire" class="internal-link">Baeling Empire</a> of <a href="Valund Baeling" class="internal-link">Valund Baeling</a> in the mid 5th century. It is one of more important trading hubs in the Torn Sea and many vessels dock on its harbour crossing between Trykkerog, Nostroya and Urland.</p>
