<p>Ikara, sometimes called "the City of Fate" is a beheran settlement sitting near the Toumis River, on the intersection between the Roads from Erun to Larash and Emiz to the Border of Dor Beher. It is part of the Elbae Sultnate and administered by the Pasha of Erun and the local Sanjak, Ilman Gorak. Ikara is one of the older still prominent towns in the eastern Asef, younger than the ancient cities of Mereskis and Erun perhaps but centuries older than many other settlements in the region, having been founded in the early 8th century BA. Its most famous for the so called "Falcons Pit", a massive Arena where grand games, tournament and duels are held for religious ceremony and public entertainment.</p>
<p>The Name "Ikaras" stems from the old Asefi tongue roughly translating for "Garden at the River" but few still speak ancient Asefi as the predominant languages in the region are high elven and the modern beheran dialects.</p>
<p>Besides the Falcons Pit, the City of Ikara is home to a other popular and impressive sights like the Magisters Bazaar, the Tower of Goal and the Brass Dome. It is a hub for Caravans and attracts various merchants and farmers from the eastern communities, as well as Beduins from the west and south.</p>
