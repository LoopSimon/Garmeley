<p>The City of Kulum, now more commonly known as the Cursed City of Kulum or simply Cursed Kulum, sits in the delta of the Harva River south of the Yevan moors and is currently abandoned and safeguarded sternly by the <a href="Kherovan Inquisition" class="internal-link">Tribunal of the Onyx Cult of the Kherovan Inquisition</a>, no creature entering or leaving its ground without their ever watchful eye.</p>
<p>However, Kulums history precedes the tyranny, cruelty and demise of <a href="Ahrmiz Saceren" class="internal-link">Ahrmiz "the Conjurer" Saceren</a> who would plunge it into darkness in the 9th century, and goes back to humbler days. In 510PA the term "Kulum" is first used to describe the growing trading and village communities of east pilvian humans. Its geography being of stellar nature, both sitting at the southern end of the Harva River that came through eastern Pilvia leading up the lands of the Sarloni, as well as the Bay of Kulum itself that offered trade opportunities and a natural harbour for orcish, elven, beheri and nirydian traders.</p>
<p>During the 7th century, Kulum became the primary reason for the outbreak of the <a href="Yellow War" class="internal-link">Yellow War</a>, a large scaled military conflict between he <a href="Grevenna" class="internal-link">Lordship of Grevenna</a> and the <a href="Kherova" class="internal-link">Khatrym of Kherova</a> from 660PA to 668PA.</p>
<p>The City of New Kulum, founded in 867PA following the Fall of Kulum on the other side of the eponymous Bay of Kulum, separated from its older parent only by the Yellow Moors and the still waters of the Bay itself, is in many ways the material and spiritual child of the Cursed City.A home to industry, opportunity, squalor and greed, a breeding ground for mercantile innovation and ruthless abuse. </p>
