<div class="wiki-infobox"> <h4 class="wiki-header">City of Korsfall</h4> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Country</strong> <span><a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Kingdom of Meadrath</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Government</strong> <span>Queen <a href="Gisela IV" class="internal-link">Gisela IV</a>,<br>Count Louis du Beurin, Lord of Korsfall</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Population</strong> <span>ca. 78000</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Heraldic Symbol</strong> <span>Golden Tree on Green</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Primary Religion</strong> <a href="Trinicism" class="internal-link">Trinicism</a> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Primary Culture</strong> <span><a href="Meadi" class="internal-link">Rhilmer</a> <a href="Human" class="internal-link">Human</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Official Language</strong> <span>High Meadi</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>First mentioned:</strong> <span>late 2nd century PA</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Flags/Korsfall_City_Flag.png" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;"> </div> <p>Korsfall is the capital of the Kingdom of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a> and the seat of its Queen, a city of about 78000 people on the upper <a href="Trinmo" class="internal-link">Trinmo</a>, near Lake Rog in the heart of the country. It is one of the largest cities in the world, the home of the Royal Court, the <a href="Royal Congress" class="internal-link">Royal Congress</a> and <a href="Korsfall College" class="internal-link">Korsfall College</a>, and the administrative and economic heart of Meadrath and of all <a href="Meadland" class="internal-link">Meadland</a>. It is a crowded, loud city of narrow streets, tall close-set houses and busy markets, and its arms are the Golden Tree of the old von Weide Dynasty on a green field.</p> <h2>The City</h2> <p>Korsfall grew from three settlements. Weidhill was at first no more than a tower on a hill above the Trinmo, which grew into a keep with a village and a town around it, and it later merged with its neighbours Kor and Hiln. The name Korsfall appears around the late 2nd century PA, and the three old quarters still give their names to the districts of the city. Kor lies along the river and is the quarter of the markets, the wharves and the guild halls, Weidhill is the old hill of the Keep, and Hiln is the hill of the College.</p> <p>On Weidhill stands Korsfall Keep, the oldest fortress of the city and the seat of the Lords of Korsfall. When <a href="Medlan" class="internal-link">Diedrik of Medlan</a> took <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a> in 589PA, the children of King Tibor II fled first to Weadland, where the Warder of Schipwig turned them away, and found refuge only at the Keep.</p> <p>The royal quarters lie on the side of the city that faces Lake Rog. There stands the Moonlight Palace, where the monarchs of Meadrath are crowned, and beside it the Korsfall Court, in which the Royal Congress meets and the Royal Diplomatic Corps keeps its offices. Hiln belongs to <a href="Korsfall College" class="internal-link">Korsfall College</a>, founded in 556PA, with the <a href="Wulfrick" class="internal-link">Wulfrick</a> Observatory rising above it, and the students and scholars of the College are part of the daily life of the city.</p> <p>The largest church of the city is Brightstone Cathedral, which the citizens call the Brightstone Dome for the great dome that rises above the roofs. It is dedicated to all the saints and to the Prophet <a href="Gethric" class="internal-link">Gethric</a> equally. It is not as large as the cathedral of Bellhome, but it comes close. Brightstone is the seat of the Bishop of Korsfall, the bishop of the royal court, whose only canonization so far is that of Saint <a href="Yahrwick" class="internal-link">Yahrwick</a> of Zeeport, although the informal primacy among the bishops of Meadland belongs to Bellhome.</p> <p>On the right bank of the Trinmo, between Korsfall and Anweide, lies the Korsvale, the region that bears the name of the capital.</p> <h2>Rule and Trade</h2> <p>Korsfall is a city of the Crown. The Queen holds it directly, and it is governed in her name by the Lord of Korsfall, an office that the monarch bestows and that is kept apart from the royal family. At present the Lord of Korsfall is Count Louis du Beurin. The office is among the oldest of the realm, and its tradition goes back to the days before the 4th century and to <a href="Torvald I" class="internal-link">Torvald the Tall</a>, the first Lord of Korsfall. Until the Union the title was borne by the rulers of Korsfall themselves, and since then it has been given by the Crown to a trusted noble, who governs the city from Korsfall Keep.</p> <p>The daily affairs of the city, its markets, guilds and taxes, are in the hands of the Town Hall, where the burghers elect a Mayor of Korsfall from among the guilds. The Mayor is one of the mayors of the great cities of the realm who give voice to the commons in the <a href="Royal Congress" class="internal-link">Royal Congress</a>, which meets at the Korsfall Court and speaks for burghers from the whole kingdom and not only from the capital. The Town Hall also gave its name to the Meadi Town Hall Rent, the government bond on Korsfall that the Congress created after the financial crises of the late 9th century.</p> <p>Korsfall is not only the administrative but also the economic heart of Meadrath and of greater Meadland, and everything flows together here: the trade of <a href="Giselstatt" class="internal-link">Giselstatt</a> and the east, of <a href="Jera" class="internal-link">Jera</a> and the south and west, of <a href="Meadhavn" class="internal-link">Meadhavn</a> and <a href="The New World" class="internal-link">the New World</a>, of Fulmer and the merchant ports and farms of the <a href="Meadi" class="internal-link">Weadi</a>, and of Bellhome with the southern <a href="Cevoli" class="internal-link">Cevoli</a> and <a href="Ott" class="internal-link">Ottian</a> trade. Timber and wool come down the Trinmo from the Whisperhills, the College makes a trade of its own in books and instruments, and even the coffee of the <a href="Qarzi" class="internal-link">Qarzi</a> is drunk at its tables.</p> <h2>Festivals</h2> <p>The great festival of Korsfall is Union Day, which remembers the Union of Meadrath, proclaimed on the eve of Fall in 664PA when Liara I of Gisvale married Gunther III of Korsfall. It is kept throughout the realm, although in this age no law makes it a holiday for all and every town and village keeps it in its own way. In Korsfall the Court and the city celebrate it with the greatest pomp, with the banners of the two old realms flown together.</p> <p>The Green Week around midsummer is kept as well, though in the capital, as in the centre and east of the country, in a quieter and more courtly form than in the west, where the old ways are stronger.</p> <h2>History</h2> <p>After the fall of the Auguri Dominion in 260PA, <a href="Torvald I" class="internal-link">Torvald I</a>, the first Lord of Korsfall, took power in 262PA, and the von Weide ruled the realm of Korsfall from the city until 469PA, when Siegmar V "the last Tree" died and the crown passed to Gernot I von Kor, whose house had served them as castellans. In the 3rd and 4th centuries Korsfall rose over its neighbours: in 285PA, after the Battle of the Voiceless, the king of <a href="Morelle" class="internal-link">Morelle</a> bent the knee, and in 381PA the Feast of Dragons decided the war against Rulais, which became <a href="Dirneux" class="internal-link">Dirneux</a>. The kings of Korsfall are listed in the <a href="Rulers of the Meadi" class="internal-link">Rulers of the Meadi</a>.</p> <p>The <a href="Gethricism" class="internal-link">Gethric</a> teachings reached Meadland in 263PA, and although Korsfall never officially converted, by around 390PA most of its people were Gethric. In 556PA King Gunther I founded Korsfall College, and after the death of Wulfrick the Elder in 661PA the Observatory that bears his name was raised on Hiln.</p> <p>In 664PA, at the end of the War of Trout and Drake, Liara I of Gisvale married Gunther III of Korsfall and the Union of Meadrath was proclaimed. Korsfall became the capital of the united realm, and in 674PA their son Gildher I was crowned its first King. <a href="Gisela II" class="internal-link">Gisela II</a>, who was born and died in the city, ruled from the Court for over sixty-eight years, survived the Praum Affair, centralized the realm around Korsfall and expanded the College.</p> <p>In 898PA Gisela III founded the Royal Congress at the Korsfall Court and named <a href="Odrick von Fulmer" class="internal-link">Odrick von Fulmer</a>, the head of the Royal Diplomatic Corps of Korsfall, its first High-Chancellor, and the web of dependencies known as the Fulmer-System has been run from the city ever since. In 913PA the young Queen <a href="Gisela IV" class="internal-link">Gisela IV</a>, who was born in Korsfall in 897PA, took the government into her own hands, and <a href="Jean du Jera" class="internal-link">Jean du Jera</a> left the capital for Jera.</p>
