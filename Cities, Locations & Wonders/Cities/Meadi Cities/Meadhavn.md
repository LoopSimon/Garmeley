<div class="wiki-infobox"> <h4 class="wiki-header">City of Meadhavn</h4> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Country</strong> <span><a href="Mear" class="internal-link">Duchy of Mear</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Government</strong> <span>Mayor Jeanne Orterre,<br>the Ports Round</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Population</strong> <span>ca. 25000</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Heraldic Symbol</strong> <span>Blue Anchor and White Stork</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Primary Religion</strong> <a href="Trinicism" class="internal-link">Trinicism</a> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Primary Culture</strong> <span><a href="Meadi" class="internal-link">Low Meadmer</a> <a href="Human" class="internal-link">Human</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Official Language</strong> <span>High Meadi</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>First mentioned:</strong> <span>399PA</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Flags/Meadhavn_Flag.png" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;"> </div> <p>Meadhavn is the capital of the Duchy of <a href="Mear" class="internal-link">Mear</a>, a city of about 25000 people and one of the most important ports of the Kingdom of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a>. It lies on the point where the Mead River flows into the Maidensea, which gave the city its name. A great deal of commerce finds its way to and from Meadhavn, above all trade with <a href="Sertova" class="internal-link">Sertova</a>, <a href="Othirdon" class="internal-link">Othirdon</a>, the new world of <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a> and even southern Cevolia, for merchants who prefer the faster and more efficient naval routes to the slower and more dangerous land passes.</p> <h2>The Port</h2> <p>Meadhavn is the home of the <a href="Gesellschaft für Westmeadischen Handel" class="internal-link">K.G.W.</a>, the chartered company of western trade, which has its headquarters here, but also of many other Meadi traders and companies, such as Whisperhill Shipping, and of various guilds and shipbuilders. The land passes that the sea route spares the merchants, such as the Arvale Pass into Othirdon and the Ottian Pass towards Cevolia, are slower and more dangerous.</p> <p>Meadhavn is the largest base of the royal navy, which also keeps forces in the other ports, and the title of High Mariner of the Kingdom falls by tradition to the Duke of Mear. The K.G.W. keeps a fleet of its own that is arguably larger, but it is currently stationed mainly at <a href="Dueren" class="internal-link">Dueren</a>.</p> <h2>Rule of the City</h2> <p>The city is run by the so called Ports Round, a council of guilders and traders as well as influential members of the city, which advises the Mayor of Meadhavn, at present Jeanne Orterre. The Duke of Mear is technically everyone's liege, but he does not live in the city. His estate lies further south at Castle Krenous, a larger estate with a castle, but with only a small village around it.</p> <h2>History</h2> <p>Meadhavn was founded in 399PA by the then Archdukes of <a href="Morelle" class="internal-link">Morelle</a>, and it was fortified against northern raids from the start. The Archdukes wanted to protect the shores of Morelle against the <a href="Bykmåli" class="internal-link">Bykmåli</a> of <a href="Trykkerog" class="internal-link">Trykkerog</a>. In the late 5th century the mage Jargeux helped to raise the seawall that still stands outside the city, thick walls out in the waters. For more than four centuries it was the northern port of Morelle. After the Fiseux Uprising Morelle lost Meadhavn together with its northern lands in 833PA, and the port went to Count von Firsberg as the first Duke of Mear, and it became the capital of the new duchy. In 900PA the old guild offices of Meadhavn, <a href="Zeeport" class="internal-link">Zeeport</a> and Dirdale were united with the Département du Col as the K.G.W.</p>
