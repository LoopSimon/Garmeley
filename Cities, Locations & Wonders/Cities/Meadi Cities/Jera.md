It is one of the larger cities of the Kingdom of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a>, known for its University, the oldest in western Ilrakhan, for the statue on Riverun Plaza and for the hanging district of Baldonne. Its arms are three silver trumpets on a red field.</p> <h2>The City</h2> <p>The name of Jera is older than the city. It belonged first to Jera Hill, the rise in the centre of the city where the Lind meets the Mead, on which a tower was built and later a keep, which grew into Jera Castle. The house that entrenched itself there became the du Jera, and the city that formed around the castle took the name of the fortification. The du Jera were the old holders of Jera Castle and servants of the royal line of Rulais, the House du Argon, and are one of the oldest lines of human nobility in the world still ongoing, older than the <a href="Rulers of the Meadi" class="internal-link">von Kor</a> and a rival in age to the line of <a href="Gismar" class="internal-link">Gismar</a> that has since merged with the von Kor. When Rulais fell to Korsfall in 381PA, most of the House du Argon was destroyed in the wars, although a cadet branch, the House of Argon-Rux, kept Ruxeres, and Thierry du Jera was declared the first Duke of Dirneux. The du Jera had stayed loyal to the kings of Rulais until the royal line was dead and did not come off as traitors, they were seen as the most important landholders, and many of the mages and nobles of the defeated side respected them, so that their appointment was the most graceful outcome for both sides.</p> 
<p>The Archduke rules the city directly, but below him sits the City Council under the Lord Castellan of Castle Jera, which has usually governed the more mundane tasks of the city. The present Lord Castellan is Gerville Hautin, a loyal servant of the Archduke, who has also mentored the Archduke's son Renard in many respects, as his father spent years at court. For the law and the more complicated matters the Archduke or the Lord Castellan calls on a <a href="Deacon" class="internal-link">Deacon</a>, a noble of the quill who is often found among the scholars of the University's school of law.</p>

<p>On Riverun Plaza stands one of the famous sights of Jera, the Triumph of the Three. The statue shows the three original heroes of the Gethric faith, the Prophet <a href="Gethric" class="internal-link">Gethric</a> and the saints <a href="Volboris" class="internal-link">Volboris</a> and <a href="Razmyr" class="internal-link">Razmyr</a>, the Iztaw Three of the <a href="The Incursion" class="internal-link">Incursion</a>, warding off a horde of gargoyles that represent the <a href="Daemon" class="internal-link">daemons</a> they fought. It was finished around 638PA, more than a century before the Great Schism.</p> <p>Baldonne is a hanging district of pavilions and gardens on the cliffs of the Mead, where the river cascades down the rock. It is raised above the rest of the city and lies in a dreamlike mist from the falling water.</p> <h2>University and Archdukes</h2> <p>The University of Jera is the oldest in western Ilrakhan and is renowned above all for its schools of law and history. In 894PA the theologian <a href="Perrin Diar" class="internal-link">Perrin Diar</a> addressed its scholars and gave birth to <a href="Diarism" class="internal-link">Diarism</a>, although the city of Ballina in western Othirdon has since become the unofficial centre of the movement. The Bishop of Jera, Berac Letrouse, an old friend of Perrin Diar and counted among the great living theologians of the <a href="Trinicism" class="internal-link">Trinicist</a> church, is surprisingly open to its ideals, and the Archduke, a Trinicist with little love for the movement, has never moved against the university.</p> <p><a href="Jean du Jera" class="internal-link">Jean du Jera</a> was born in the city in 875PA. When his daughter <a href="Gisela IV" class="internal-link">Gisela IV</a> took the government into her own hands in 913PA and he left the court at Korsfall, he returned to Jera, and rules Dirneux from there today. It is rumoured that part of the profits of the free port of <a href="Ulbreek" class="internal-link">Ulbreek</a> find their way back to the city, as an insurance for the old guard of the nobility. Silk production came to Jera in the late 9th century, together with Bellhome and Praum, and let the Meadi join a thriving market.</p>
