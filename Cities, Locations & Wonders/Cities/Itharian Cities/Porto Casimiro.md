<p>The City of Porto Casimiro, or simply Casimiro, is a small fortified outposts on the western shores of the island of <a href="Itharia" class="internal-link">Itharia</a>, founded by cevoli settlers in a daring mission by the Crown of <a href="Battvia" class="internal-link">Battvia</a> to facilitate trade between the ports of the Smoking Sea and the rich ports of southern Behera.</p>
