<p>Biadarkyr is an ancient city within the <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae Sultanate</a> situated on the Za-Jira River at the confluence of the Ajit River from the south in central Behera, lying between the great cities of Meterzand to the northwest and <a href="Jiravid" class="internal-link">Jiravid</a> to the east. It is among the oldest continuously inhabited sites in the known world, its founding dated by elven records to approximately 2370BA, and its significance has never been just architectural or commercial, but always spiritual. Biadarkyr was the first of the <a href="Elven Paragonworship" class="internal-link">Paragon Tomb Cities</a> of Behera: built simultaneously in surface and underland form by both <a href="Elf" class="internal-link">High Elf</a> and <a href="Drow" class="internal-link">Drow</a> communities at a moment when the two branches of elven civilization chose to unite together, it established the tradition of joint sacred stewardship that the <a href="Golden Dusk" class="internal-link">Golden Dusk</a> would eventually formalize nearly two thousand years later. The Great Tomb of Paret, the oldest and most revered of the Paragon burial complexes, lies here, and with it the permanent residence of the Grand Hierophant, the spiritual leader of all Beheran elves, currently the Drow Ramira Ued al-Raban.</p> 
<p>A place that has housed the supreme religious authority of the longest lived elven civilization for over four millennia is not merely a city in the ordinary sense of the word, and Biadarkyr does not present itself as one, its streets are quieter than its age suggests they should be, its most important institutions exist as much below the ground as above it, and the weight of what is kept here makes itself felt in ways that visitors from younger civilizations tend to find difficult to put into words.</p>
