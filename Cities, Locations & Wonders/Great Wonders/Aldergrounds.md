<p>The Aldergrounds are the remnants of the ancient <a href="Alder" class="internal-link">Alder</a> civilisation known as <a href="Aldermur" class="internal-link">Aldermur</a>, splattered few and far between the modern day world of Aisligos, especially following the destruction of the Alder heartlands of Naltor that have now sunken into the seas, they are sometimes avoided as dangerous grounds, worshipped as near religious sites, treasured as fonts of power and knowledge or simply absorbed and utilised as grounds for settled communities that have once again grown around them.</p>
<p>The most well known and important feature of the Aldergrounds are the so called "Alderbridges" with only a minuscule amount of them still functioning they act as a largely stable gateway system, connecting locations hundreds of miles away from each other to a single network, and allowing the daring travel through its ways under certain conditions. As of now  than a dozen of these Alderbridges have been discovered and deemed functional by the roaming tribes of the <a href="Narzoi" class="internal-link">Narzoi</a> peoples. During the devastating slaughter and enslavement of many Narzoi of the southern lands, certain leaders made the decision to collapse part of the Alderbridge system to trap a large part of the encroaching and insurmountable forces of the <a href="Hiqae" class="internal-link">Hiqae</a> and stop their attempts to conquer the entire continent.</p>
