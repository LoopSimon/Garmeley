 <p>The Brass Spire is the prison of <a href="Gelmsburg" class="internal-link">Gelmsburg</a> and one of the most infamous prisons in the western world. It is a tower built to hold mages, even the most gifted of them, and no mage has ever broken out of it. It takes its name from the bands of brass that sheathe it, which carry the wards that hold its prisoners in.</p>  <p>The builders of the Spire are not recorded, and it is older than the kingdom. In the tales told of it, it has held devious sorcerers of the past such as the necromancer <a href="Morfeld" class="internal-link">Morfeld</a> and Uthir. The wards on its walls are the work of the mages of the <a href="Gelmsburg Circle" class="internal-link">Gelmsburg Circle</a>. The Crown keeps the Spire, and the knights of the <a href="Order of the Silver Wheel" class="internal-link">Order of the Silver Wheel</a>, who have a chapter house in the city, stand among its wardens.</p>  <p>The best-known prisoner of the Spire in the present age is Vinfried <a href="Kabernos" class="internal-link">Kabernos</a>, the former Courtmage of Gisvale, who was arrested in Gelmsburg in 913PA by the Silver Wheel with the approval of Klovis I, on the charge of treason against the <a href="Wermian Accords" class="internal-link">Wermian Accords</a>. His trial was set for 915PA and was delayed indefinitely, and what has become of his body and mind in the Spire is not known to the public. Gisvale and the lords of western Meadrath demand his release, and the Spire has become a symbol in the rivalry between Gelmsburg and Gisvale.</p>
