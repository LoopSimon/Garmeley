<p>The Great Organius of Aviv is a vast shaft in the city of Aviv, the capital of <a href="Melgard" class="internal-link">Melgard</a>, that descends for kilometres beneath the city into the <a href="Underlands" class="internal-link">Underlands</a>. It is the centre of the Pagri church of <a href="Zuhurism" class="internal-link">Zuhurism</a>, which counts it as a fifth holy site beside the four of the classic tradition, and it is often numbered among the wonders of the world.</p>  

<p>On the walls of the Organius hangs the inner city of Aviv, a second city within the city that is held to be holy and is forbidden to outsiders. Its streets and temples descend along the walls into the depths, and the deeper reaches of the shaft are known to few even among the faithful. At the bottom of the Organius lies Avivium, a rare and precious ore found nowhere else in such quantity.</p>  
<h2>Origins</h2>  
<p>No one knows who made the Organius. The Pagri regard it as a work of Pen Zuhur, a gate cut into the earth so that his light might reach into the deep places of the world. Some scholars hold instead that it was dug by Nirydian <a href="Dwarf" class="internal-link">dwarves</a> who abandoned it for unknown reasons at least two thousand years ago, and that at its centre lies one of the <a href="Terem-Zal" class="internal-link">Terem-Zal</a>, the stone gods of the dwarves, a theory the Pagri church rejects.</p>
