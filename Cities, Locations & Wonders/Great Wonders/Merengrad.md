<p>Merengrad the forbidden, is a mysterious structure guarded by the Grell, a group of monastic warriors who have sworn to take the life of all who come near the Merengrad and believe it to be a supernatural fulcrum of evil. Merengrad lies somewhere in the no mans land of western Ur, long abandoned by mortal realms in times before hobgoblin invaded, yet untouched even by their daring hands.</p>
