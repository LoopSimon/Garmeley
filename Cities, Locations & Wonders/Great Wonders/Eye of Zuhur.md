<p>The Eye of Zuhur, also called the Eye of Gavivag, is a great dome on <a href="Gavivag Isle" class="internal-link">Gavivag Isle</a>, the island south of Trilom that gives the Gavivag Bay its name. Raised in 466PA as an offering to Pen Zuhur, it gathers the light of the sun and casts it out as a vast ray of destruction across the surrounding waters, and whoever holds it is able to threaten much of the trade that passes through the bay. It is regarded by the faithful of <a href="Zuhurism" class="internal-link">Zuhurism</a> as a sacred artifact and by the realms around the bay as one of the most dangerous weapons in Nirydia.</p>   
<h2>Creation</h2>   
<p>The Eye was conceived by Gav, an arcanist remembered as a madman, who meant to use it to destroy the entire ocean in honour of Pen Zuhur. It was made by his seventeen sons, who invoked their powers together in a single great working and offered it to the Sun God. The casting took weeks to complete, and all of the brothers gave their lives in it except the youngest, Miodas, the seventeenth son of Gav, who alone survived to see the Eye open. The Eye has never fulfilled its maker's purpose, but its ray remains the most feared power on the waters of the bay.</p>   
<h2>History</h2>   
<p>At the height of its power the <a href="Zepria" class="internal-link">Zeprian Empire</a> held the island together with its city of Trilom on the northern shore of the bay. In 667PA, a century before the fall of the Empire, both were lost to <a href="Cirinzel" class="internal-link">Cirinzel</a>, which made Trilom its capital and held the Eye for two centuries.</p>   
<p>From 872PA to 875PA the Kingdom of <a href="Plašato" class="internal-link">Plašato</a> fought the Gavivag War against Cirinzel over the island, and in a surprise manoeuvre landed a great force on Gavivag and took it. Plašato held the island at the end of the war, and with it the Eye, giving the Gorati kingdom a weapon on Cirinzel's very doorstep and a hold over the shipping of the bay that its rivals have not forgotten. Plašato points to the reconquest of the Eye as proof of its claim to be the true heir of Zepria.</p>
