<p>The Eye of Zuhur, also called the Eye of Gavivag, is a great dome on <a href="Gavivag Isle" class="internal-link">Gavivag Isle</a>, the island south of Trilom that gives the Gavivag Bay its name. Raised in the 460s PA as an offering to Pen Zuhur, it gathers the light of the sun and casts it out as a vast ray of destruction across the surrounding waters, and whoever holds it is able to threaten much of the trade that passes through the bay. It is regarded by the faithful of <a href="Zuhurism" class="internal-link">Zuhurism</a> as a sacred artifact and by the realms around the bay as one of the most dangerous weapons in Nirydia.</p>  
<h2>Creation</h2>  
<p>According to tradition the Eye was made by the Seventeen Sons of Gav, who invoked their powers together in a single great working and offered it to the Sun God. The casting took weeks to complete, and all of the brothers gave their lives in it except the youngest, Miodas, the seventeenth son of Gav, who alone survived to see the Eye open.</p>  
<h2>The Gavivag War</h2>  
<p>For most of its history the Eye lay within the sphere of <a href="Cirinzel" class="internal-link">Cirinzel</a>, whose capital Trilom stands just to the north. In the 870s the Kingdom of <a href="Plašato" class="internal-link">Plašato</a> went to war with Cirinzel over the island, and in a surprise manoeuvre landed a great force on Gavivag and took it. Plašato held the island at the end of the war, and with it the Eye, giving the Gorati kingdom a weapon on Cirinzel's very doorstep and a hold over the shipping of the bay that its rivals have not forgotten.</p>
