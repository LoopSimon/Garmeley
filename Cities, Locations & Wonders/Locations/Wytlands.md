<p>The Wytlands are the old hill and moor country of the Wyt in the south-east of <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Vihr</a>, between the Grachhyll, the southern Mistspires and the <a href="Ravenleaf Forest" class="internal-link">Ravenleaf Forest</a>. The name is older than any border and covers the whole country of the Wyt north of the forest. Its western parts, in eastern Opuzna and Levazci, are held and governed like the rest of the realm. Its eastern parts are claimed by both Vadcy but ruled by neither, and there the Wyt live under bandit lords and robber barons known as the Wytlords. The greatest of them is <a href="Goldenrose" class="internal-link">Goldenrose</a>, the master of Dag Valley. In recent years the eastern Wytlands have become the subject of a plan at court, so far unofficial, to grant noble titles to any adventurer who can pacify them.</p>  
<h2>Geography</h2>  
<p>The Wytlands are a country of rolling hills, high moors, stony valleys and birch woods. In the north they climb toward the Grachhyll, and in the east toward the southern Mistspires and the dwarven hold of <a href="Daban-Kol" class="internal-link">Daban-Kol</a>. To the south they run down to the northern edge of the Ravenleaf, and to the west they open onto the grasslands of the Expanse around Opuzna. The Wytra River rises in the eastern hills, runs through Lake Dag in the far east and then south and west toward the forest. Dag Valley, the country around the lake, is the richest land in the eastern Wytlands and the seat of Goldenrose.</p>  
<p>The western Wytlands, in eastern Opuzna and Levazci, are farmed and settled, with Wyt villages, the keeps of the local Gri and the roads that carry timber and herds to Opuzna and Pretko. The eastern Wytlands, the unruled part, have few roads and fewer towns. There the settlements are hill forts, walled hamlets and lone towers, and the roads are watched by whoever holds the nearest ford or bridge.</p>  
<h2>History</h2>  
<p>The Wytlands take their name from Wyt Hill, where in 32PA the Horn Lords of the <a href="Vihrmen Federation" class="internal-link">Vihrmen Federation</a> beat the Ogrelords and drove them back into the moors and the mountains. The Wyt were the people of these hills long before there was a Vihr. When Viago I proclaimed the kingdom, the Wyt and Sarloni lords of the south were the last to hold out against him, and they were broken at the Battle of Glancz near Opuzna in 251PA. Many of the Wyt lords who would not kneel withdrew east into the hills, and the eastern Wytlands have never since been brought fully under any lord.</p>  
<p>In 377PA both Levazci and Opuzna became hereditary Vadcy, and both have claimed the eastern Wytlands ever since. Neither has ever been able to enforce that claim. The Wyt nonetheless gave the realm one of its kings: <a href="Zawis I" class="internal-link">Zawis I</a>, the first Ras of the House of Worocek, was a Wyt Gri of Nediv in the southern marches. His castle has since fallen into ruin, and today it is held by one of the Wytlords.</p>  
<p>The Gethric missions of the 3rd century came hard to the Wytlands. Wyt and Sarloni who would not convert were driven from their homes, some of them naked into the Ravenleaf, and the old faith went quiet rather than away. In the late 910s a bandit chief in golden armour, known as Goldenrose, took Dag Valley and made himself the most feared man in the eastern Wytlands.</p>  
<h2>The Wytlords</h2>  
<p>"Wytlord" is not a title. It is the name the lowlanders give to any armed man or woman who holds a tower, a ford, a bridge or a hill fort in the eastern Wytlands and lives off the roads and the herds. Their number rises and falls with the seasons and the feuds between them, from a dozen holdings in a good year to half that after a hard winter. Each of them commands a tower and a few dozen riders at most, with the exception of Goldenrose, whose following is far greater. Most of them send gifts to Orzebachi and Opuzna on the great feasts, so that in law they remain the loyal servants of the Varina of Levazci and the Var of Opuzna, and so they keep just enough in step with the Vadcy and the crown not to provoke a greater response. Some of them descend from the Wyt lords who withdrew after Glancz. Others are deserters, outlaws and knights who failed elsewhere.</p>  
<p>Besides Goldenrose, the best known Wytlords of the present day are Wilk Haladyn, the Bridge Lord, who holds the only stone bridge over the Wytra and takes his toll from every drover bound for Pretko; Old Jaroslawa of Wyt Hill, who holds the barrow fort on the old battlefield, claims descent from a Horn Lord and is the nearest thing the Wytlands have to a judge; Brother Ostap, a defrocked Gethric priest whose band preaches and robs in the same breath, and whom the Church would like to see hanged; Tomira Krasz, a deserter from the Selbia Brigade who drills her riders like Brigade soldiers; Radim Bezuch, called the Bear of Nediv, who holds the ruined castle of Zawis I and claims that the blood of the Worocek runs in his veins; and Hedva of the Passes, who taxes the smugglers' paths through the Grachhyll and trades with the Hyllmen.</p>  
<h2>The Charters</h2>  
<p>In the last few years a plan has taken shape at court to bring the eastern Wytlands under the crown by granting noble titles to any adventurer who can pacify them, or at least a part of them. The plan began with Kisras <a href="Waśko Ralpia" class="internal-link">Waśko</a>, who pursued it on his own at first. It has since won the support of the Secretariat of the <a href="Vaergriff" class="internal-link">Vaergriff</a> and of various local Gri in Levazci and Opuzna, and, tentatively, of the Varina of Levazci and the Var of Opuzna. No official call has yet gone out, and the plan remains loose, but word of it has spread through the taverns of the realm, and adventurers have begun to drift east in hope of it.</p>  
<p>Those who back the plan do not want the same things from it. The two Var hope to see their old claims made good at last by other people's swords. Waśko would not mind a new and greater territory in the realm, held by someone indebted to him and to him alone, who might one day unite the Wyt people. With a confrontation between the Kisras and his younger brother <a href="Jakub Ralpia" class="internal-link">Jakub</a> seeming more inevitable every year, such a lord on the eastern frontier would be worth a great deal to him.</p>  
<h2>Peoples and Faith</h2>  
<p>The people of the Wytlands are Wyt, the same people who live in eastern Opuzna and Levazci, known for their love of dance and for the Bolov, their stringed instrument. They are Gethric, and the faith is strong among them, but the old faith of the land is kept as well, and in the Wytlands the two have married into each other. The Saints of the Church are worshipped and talked about like the old Turvik demigods, the children of the gods of the land, and the old stones on the hilltops are still visited at night.</p>  
<p>The Wytlands have neighbours of their own. The dwarves of Daban-Kol and <a href="Tizin-Kol" class="internal-link">Tizin-Kol</a> cross the southern Mistspires into the Wytlands on their way west, and pay Goldenrose for the privilege. The Grachhyll to the north is home to its own native tribes, the Hyllmen, who live in the mountain valleys and in caverns within the hills, with goblins below them in the deeper caves. Grachi traders from towns such as Miirgrad and Narav in the south-east of <a href="Grachy" class="internal-link">Grachy</a> come over the smaller passes of the Grachhyll into the Wytlands to avoid the heavily taxed and watched main pass at Tuskwatch. In the south, the Wytlands border the Ravenleaf, the home of the Ravenmen and the Ogres.</p>  
<h2>Places of Note</h2>  
<p>Dag Valley and Lake Dag in the far east are the domain of Goldenrose. Wyt Hill, the battlefield of 32PA, is crowned by the barrow fort of Old Jaroslawa. Nediv, the castle of Zawis I in the southern marches, stands in ruins, a once proud seat of kings now held by the Bear of Nediv. The stone bridge over the Wytra is held by the Bridge Lord. The barrows of the Horn Lords of the Federation lie scattered on the moors and draw treasure hunters, and old Turvik stones still stand on the hilltops, kept by the Wyt in secret.</p>
