<p>The Red Scars are a vast network of canyons, fault ridges and sheer-walled crags occupying the interior of mainland <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a>, running roughly between the <a href="Narzoi" class="internal-link">Narzoi</a> riverlands and flatlands of the east and the territories historically held by the <a href="Pharang" class="internal-link">Pharang</a> to the west. The canyon walls are composed of iron-rich stone that has oxidised to a deep red and ochre across the exposed faces, giving the formation its common name. The Scars are widely understood to be a direct consequence of the same tectonic catastrophe that sank the Ruins of Naltor into the sea during the <a href="Uraz" class="internal-link">Uraz</a> Period during which the central landmass subsided entirely, the interior of the mainland fractured along the same fault lines, splitting rather than sinking, and the result was a canyon system of sufficient scale and depth to be effectively impassable to any people without the means to traverse vertical terrain.</p>
 <p>The Red Scars function in practice as the unofficial border between the eastern Narzoi sphere, dominated by the <a href="Adate" class="internal-link">Adate Firmament</a>, and the western territories that were once united under the Pharang empire of <a href="Riarang" class="internal-link">Riarang</a> and remain fragmented among its successors. No treaty has ever formally designated the Scars as a boundary, and no power on either side has successfully established a permanent military presence within them. The difficulty of movement through the canyons has rendered conventional military projection across them largely impractical, and the great powers have adapted to this reality over centuries, treating the Scars as a fact of the political landscape rather than a problem to be solved. Traders, diplomats and raiders seeking passage rely almost entirely on <a href="Geipra" class="internal-link">Geipra</a> guides, whose intimate knowledge of the canyon routes and whose political neutrality have made them indispensable to both sides.</p> 
 <p>The interior of the Red Scars is home to the scattered settlements of the Geipra people, whose migration into the canyon system during the Uraz Period transformed what might otherwise have been empty terrain into a populated and politically consequential neutral zone. The largest of these settlements is the "City of Winds", a Geipra trade hub built across a cluster of canyon spires on an elaborate network of bridges and elevated walkways, which has grown into the primary point of exchange for goods passing between the Narzoi east and the Pharang west. The Scars are not uninhabited wilderness so much as a third space, governed by no single power and yet essential to the functioning of those that surround it, its red walls serving simultaneously as barrier, borderland and the foundation of an entire people's way of life.</p>
