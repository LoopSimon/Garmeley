<p>The Crown of the World, often called "Kalvels Peak", is a legendary location within nostroyan folkore, the tales of the ancient <a href="Aeldcult" class="internal-link">Aeldcult</a> and greater northern mythology.</p>
<p>According to Myth, it was here that Nissa ,the roymer hero of ancient days, travelled to rid herself of the influence of the Feylord <a href="Lempo" class="internal-link">Lempo, the Father of Moths</a>, who had made her his unwilling servant, leading to the events of the lesser known northern Saga, the "Dance of Nissa" during which she saw half-truths and deceit, blinded by pride and Greed, and caused the fall of her clan and family, as well as the death of the wise Giant Kalvel. Kalvel, even in death a mentor to Nissa, showed her the path to the Crown and it was here that she is said to have bested Lempo.</p>
