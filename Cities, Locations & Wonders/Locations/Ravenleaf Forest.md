<p>The Ravenleaf Forest is the largest forest in Vihr, a vast and ancient wood in the south-east of the realm. Most of it is claimed by the Vadcy of <a href="Opuzna" class="internal-link">Opuzna</a>, but little of it is truly held by anyone. It is a country of old trees, unbroken tribesmen and wild monsters, and the memory of two dark figures, the priest Kortyr and the Ogre Mage Yagund, who both made their lairs on its edges.</p>
<h2>Geography</h2>
<p>The forest fills much of the south and east of the Vadcy of Opuzna. The city of Opuzna stands on its north-western edge, on the Opuzna River, one of the three great rivers of the Expanse, which runs on from there to the south-east. To the north lie the Wytlands, the old country of the Wyt, and the open grasslands of the Expanse where the unicorns graze. To the east and south lies <a href="Dalniz" class="internal-link">Dalniz</a>, the land of the old Sarloni realm of Leok, and beyond it the Yellow Moor, a country of swamps on the borders of Kherova and Grevenna. The trees grow thickest in the middle of the forest, where few roads run and fewer people go.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>The forest was feared long before there was a Vihr. When the Ogrelords raided the settlements of the Expanse in the centuries before the Federation, the forest was held to be a different and less comprehensible kind of threat, and even the Ogrelords avoided it. They were beaten at Wyt Hill in 32PA and driven back to the moors and the mountains, but the forest stayed as it was. From its margins the half-elven priest <a href="Kortyr" class="internal-link">Kortyr Paleblood</a>, born about 77PA, preached his blood cult and hunted the sacred unicorns of the Expanse, until he was, so the tale goes, betrayed and buried alive in 190PA.</p>
<p>The second terror came in the 5th century. In 419PA the first of the <a href="Zuzka" class="internal-link">Zuzka</a>, the Beast of Errel, appeared on the southern plains near the forest and destroyed the village of Errel. The beasts were the work of <a href="Yagund" class="internal-link">Yagund</a>, an Ogre Mage of the Ravenleaf Ogres, who brought them forth in his Breeding Ground at Horomur and used them to rule over stretches of the forest, the moors and the southern hills. For seventy years they were the terror of the south. In 489PA the hero <a href="Darmil zi Perliav" class="internal-link">Darmil of Perliav</a> led an expedition of heroes against the Ogre, and killed him at Horomur. Ten years later the Ogrelords who had been held together by their fear of Yagund fell apart.</p>
<p>The forest has other monsters, and other heroes. <a href="Fredric" class="internal-link">Fredric</a>, later the Hero of Danipol and a Saint, is remembered in the 740s as the slayer of the Hydra of Ravenleaf,.</p>
<h2>Peoples and Creatures</h2>
<p>Those who live at the forest's edge are the Wyt, a people of Opuzna and the Wytlands who keep to their old customs and are known for their dance and their stringed instrument, the Bolov. In the depths live the unbroken tribesmen, who have never been brought under any lord and who do not welcome visitors. Ogres still live in the forest, and the wild creatures of the south share it with them. The vilest of the Zuzka are also said to be still here: unnatural mixtures of insect and magic that did not die with their maker and that, it is whispered, do not age.</p>
<h2>Economy</h2>
<p>Little of the forest is worked. Along its northern edge and the banks of the Opuzna River the Wyt cut timber and make charcoal, hunt game and gather what the forest gives, and the river carries both to the capital. The depths are left alone. Hunters and adventuring parties go in for bounties on monsters, and for the relics and curiosities of Kortyr and Yagund, and bounties on the worst of the forest's beasts are posted at Opuzna.</p>
<h2>Places of Note</h2>
<p>The city of Opuzna on the north-western edge is the gateway to the forest. The Crossing of Darmil, near the forest, is marked by the statue of the hero in his memory. Errel, the village lost to the first Zuzka, lies in the southern plains near the forest and is still remembered by name. Horomur, the Breeding Ground of Yagund, was destroyed with the Ogre and his secrets. The dens and nests of the Zuzka lie deep in the trees.</p>
