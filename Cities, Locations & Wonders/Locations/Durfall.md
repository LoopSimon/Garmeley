<p>The Durfall is a region in southern <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a> on the coast of the Stone Sea, beset by a massive, unmoving Cloud of Smoke. The Dur, what the local Narzoi call the substance hanging in the air here, numbs the senses of eye and ear almost completely and makes traversing the Region nigh impossible. </p>
