<p>The Immersong is a massive Storm centred around a gigantic vortex of water, located in the centre of the Tirean Ocean, north east of the shores of <a href="Eressa" class="internal-link">Eressa</a>. Its exact make up and origin are unknown as traversal is essentially impossible, although it has appeared in folklore for over 4000 years as a religious symbol. The Immersong is worshiped as a deity and part of various religious traditions in Eressa and Pagia, as well as the central piece in the <a href="Crustlords" class="internal-link">Krestasi Myth of Doom</a>.</p>
