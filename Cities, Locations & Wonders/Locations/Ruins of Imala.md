%% [[Peadyr]] [[Aldergrounds]] [[Alder]] [[Aldermur]] [[Alderbridge]] [[Uraz]] [[Senso]] [[Narzoi]] [[Finger of Life]] %% <div class="wiki-infobox" style="overflow: hidden; display: flex; flex-direction: column;"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Imala.webp" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;"> <small><i>Senso explorers in the Ruins of Imala</i></small> </div> <p>The Ruins of Imala are the remains of an <a href="Aldergrounds" class="internal-link">Aldergrounds</a> of the <a href="Alder" class="internal-link">Alder</a>, hewn into a crag in the south-west of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a>, south of the Spiritwall, the mountain range that marks the west of the island. The structure was built like a great cylinder sunk into the rock, level upon level, and it lies between the heartland of the <a href="Senso" class="internal-link">Senso</a> and the Ghostlands, though not on the shortest way between them. It was evidently a city of <a href="Aldermur" class="internal-link">Aldermur</a> rather than a single monument, and it held at least one <a href="Alderbridge" class="internal-link">Alderbridge</a>. What it was built for is not known, but those who have seen its lowest levels speak of sealed chambers and heavy doors that seem made to keep something in rather than out.</p> <p>After the Catarmon, when the Alder fell, Imala was abandoned and sealed, and without the Alder to tend it, it remained closed and protected for more than two thousand years, beyond the reach of the <a href="Narzoi" class="internal-link">Narzoi</a> who inherited the land. It was the eruption of the <a href="Uraz" class="internal-link">Uraz</a> in 283BA that broke open the layers and seals at the top of the city. For the first time since the Catarmon the Narzoi could venture deep within it, now far removed from any contact with the Alder who built it. But strange vapours and gases rose from the ruins, bringing dizziness and sickness to those who went down, and the further down they went the worse it grew, so that Imala was largely left untouched.</p> <p>In 379PA the Laso, a fanatic tribe of western Peadyr related to the Senso, marched on Imala, seeing in it a sign of evil to be destroyed. They hurled great boulders into the ruins from the heights above, breaking deeper into the city, and the explosion and collapse that followed destroyed the Alderbridge within. It is now thought that the boulders themselves did not cause this, but that they dislodged the gases trapped in the depths of Imala, and that it was their abrupt release that brought much of the city down. The Laso were later destroyed and absorbed by the Senso, but Imala has remained largely inaccessible and ruined ever since.</p> <p>Daring Senso cave divers have gone down into the ruins since. The upper levels are largely stone, hewn with great artistry, but the deeper one ventures, the more things of the indestructible metal the Narzoi call the substance are found, the same metal as the <a href="Finger of Life" class="internal-link">Finger of Life</a>. Some divers are said to have returned with strange objects pried from the lower levels, which the Senso keep a close secret. The settlers of the coast have heard tales of Imala, but it lies far from their present interests, and most of them take every story of the Alder for fiction.</p>
