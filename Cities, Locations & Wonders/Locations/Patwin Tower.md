<div class="wiki-infobox"> <h4 class="wiki-header">Patwin Tower</h4> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Location</strong> <span><a href="Garmeley" class="internal-link">Garmeley</a>, on the Trinmo near the Stormy Sea</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Type</strong> <span>College of magic</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Head</strong> <span>Archmage Vaelon Divis</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Treaties</strong> <span><a href="Wermian Accords" class="internal-link">Wermian Accords</a>, <a href="Orenburg Pact" class="internal-link">Orenburg Pact</a></span> </div> </div> <p>Patwin Tower is the great college of magic of Garmeley, a tall tower of pale stone that stands alone at the edge of the Gladewood, close to the mouth of the Trinmo. It regulates the magical affairs of the Duchy and holds to the Wermian Accords, and it has been one of the world renowned institutions of the arcane for well over two centuries. Its Archmage is a signatory of the Orenburg Pact. In the Succession Crisis it is the only magical institution that has taken a clear side.</p> <h2>The Tower and the College</h2> <p>The Tower is both a building and a school. The tower itself is the seat of the Archmage and the library, and the college around its foot teaches the arts of the arcane to students from Garmeley, Hainach, Reanport and the lands beyond. It is not the largest school of the continent, but it has a good name for its theory. Valvasor, the Grevenni scholar of Realmatic studies, was one of them. The Tower stands on the Trinmo, a short way from the sea, where it can be seen by ships on the river.</p> <p>The Tower has long had a peculiar position among the schools of the continent, for it stands in the country of the Kindly Lords. Where the mages of the Gethric lands look at the druids and shamans of the Garmish with suspicion, the Tower has always kept up good relations with them. The great Archmage Avero Kelitz, called of the Eightfolds, worked for that purpose throughout his life. He was a half-elf born in Forrington in 621PA, and he died at the Tower in 700PA. His heretical theories made him enemies among the Gethric, and his invitations to lecture at Karajal were held against him. The Council of Patwin Tower of 731PA bears its name.</p> <h2>Archmages</h2> <p>The most famous heads of the Tower are Avero Kelitz and Meyzar Rugior. Rugior was a street child of Redflow, taken in by the sorceress Tessia Anler, and he was Archmage between 811PA and 835PA. He was a planar theorist, credited with the creation of Fundamentalist Theory, and in 828PA he equipped and advised the party of adventurers that went into the cursed city of Kulum to destroy the necromancer Ahrmiz the Conjurer. He died in the Tower, choking on an apple. The present Archmage is Vaelon Divis.</p> <h2>The Tower in the Succession Crisis</h2> <p>When the Duchess Agnes died, the Tower declared for the Garmish Alliance. The Count of Forrington had always kept good relations with its mages, and the Tower saw in the Green Banner League an attack on the old ways of the country, and on the Kindly Lords whom the Tower had always protected. Archmage Vaelon Divis took the Alliance's side, and the Tower's decision was a heavy one in the world of the schools, for it was seen as a step toward the use of the arcane in war, which the Orenburg Pact forbids to its members. Until now the Tower has kept its mages off the field, and its enemies say that this will not last.</p> <p>The Tower's stand has divided its own people. Gharel Talnod, a Reani half-elf and a leading figure of the scholars who wish to be rid of the old restrictions of the Wermian Accords, left the college in opposition to its interventionism and now lives at Karajal. Janez Vajkard Valvasor, who had studied here, spoke for the southern cause at Reanport and called on the scholars of the Tower to hold to it, but he later vanished from public view. The Tower itself holds that the war threatens the Gladewood and the old ways of the Duchy, and that the Pact was not written for a case like this one.</p> <h2>Places of Note</h2> <p>The Tower stands on the bank of the Trinmo, at the edge of the Gladewood.
