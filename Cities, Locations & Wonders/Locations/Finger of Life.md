%% [[Chianism]] [[Peadyr]] [[Odise]] [[Kavi]] [[Senso]] [[Narzoi]] [[Alder]] [[Aldergrounds]] [[Almersport]] %% <div class="wiki-infobox" style="overflow: hidden; display: flex; flex-direction: column;"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/FingerofLife.jpg" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;"> <small><i>The Finger of Life above the Green Valley</i></small> </div> <p>The Finger of Life is a great pillar of metal rising roughly one hundred metres from a cliff above the Green Valley, in the interior of the east-centre of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a>. It is one of the holiest sites of <a href="Chianism" class="internal-link">Chianism</a>, held in the highest regard by the <a href="Odise" class="internal-link">Odise</a>, and it is worshipped by the <a href="Kavi" class="internal-link">Kavi</a> and <a href="Senso" class="internal-link">Senso</a> as well.</p> <p>The pillar is made of an unknowable and indestructible metal, which the <a href="Narzoi" class="internal-link">Narzoi</a> call simply the substance. The Narzoi know of the <a href="Alder" class="internal-link">Alder</a> only that they came before, and they do not believe the Finger to be related to them. They see it instead as a representation of the spirits and souls of their ancestors. Old World accounts class it with the <a href="Aldergrounds" class="internal-link">Aldergrounds</a>, as a work of the Alder.</p> <p>In Chianism the Finger gives life to the land through its rivers, but it is also said to be the end of all living energy in the world, where it is given back to nature in a great cycle.</p> <p>Pilgrims come to the Finger from across Peadyr, most of them Odise, to seek contact with their ancestors, and some report seeing visions of them. The pilgrim road runs through the northern Odise Riverlands, and Senso pilgrims rarely make the journey. The Laso, who destroyed the Aldergrounds of Imala in 379PA, never came so far east.</p> <p>Old World exploring parties have since reached the Finger, though so far no one has tried to do anything there. To the Odise, who hold it in the highest regard, their presence alone was seen as an incredible threat, and the expeditions that <a href="Almersport" class="internal-link">Almersport</a> sent up the rivers along the pilgrim road helped to bring the colony and the federation to war.</p>
