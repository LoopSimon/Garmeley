<p>THe Strait of Horad connects the Stormy Sea to the Pilvian Gulf and the Summersea and is and artifical strait created as a direct result of the so called "Invocation of Al-Biad", a spell of the infamous elf <a href="Jurahim al-Biad" class="internal-link">Jurahim al-Biad</a>. The Invocation was conjured in the 814PA marking the end of the 3rd and final War of Elbae Aggression and before it the region was known as the Isthmus of Horad and the Stormy Sea an inland body of water.</p>
<p>Its name derives of the Horad, the smallest of the three northwestern beheran regions, the Hessef, Asef and Horad and Strait is currently contested by both the Lordship of <a href="Grevenna" class="internal-link">Grevenna</a> and the <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae</a> Sultanate, although traffic and military presence are heavily regulated by the mutually agreed upon <a href="Treaty of Danipol" class="internal-link">Treaty of Danipol</a>. As the primary connection of many central and west ilrakhani nations to goods of the Niryd and purple sea through the sultanates mercantile empire, it is one of the most important and commercially viable waterways on the planet.  </p>
<p>The Fortress City of Danipol in the north and the Tower of Dor Horad in the south are the military fortifications of the two neighbouring nations closest to the Strait, but as of right now neither has a permanent naval presence directly within its waterway. Aside from its political and economical importance, the Strait also marks the geographical border between <a href="Behera" class="internal-link">Behera</a> and <a href="Ilrakhan" class="internal-link">Ilrakhan</a>.</p>
