<p>Manymirror, the Crystal Tower, Mirigal, this mysterious spire of the asefi desert has many names and holds many more mysteries although its location is a well guarded secret on its own. It is said none who are brought to the Manymirror may leave to speak of it yet a handful of lucky souls have found their way out of the deserts prison and lived to tell the tale, they dread the idea of ever returning.</p>
