<p>The Heavens Furrow is a massive canyon, stretching from the feet of the northern Mistspires into Drakes Mouth Bay, the Birsag River flows through the crags of the Furrow before leading into the Bay as well. The Furrow marks the border between  <a href="Nostroya" class="internal-link">Nostroya</a>, sitting not far north from the dense tree line of the Aeldveil itself, and the lands of Waelajokull and is associated with the  <a href="Giant" class="internal-link">Giants of the north</a> who are said to have removed themselves from the world of mortals in older days by traversing the Furrow.</p>
