

*Important Calendars of the known world*  

**The Common Calendar**
  
The Common Solar Calendar is accepted and used in the Subcontinent of Ilrakhan and dates its Year 0 to the Eruption of Al-Akka during the End times of the Rakhian Empire. It counts a Year as 400 Days, with 10 Months of 40 days every Year.

  
**The Rakhian Calendar**

The [[Rakh]]ian Calendar is no longer in wide use anywhere but remains relevant primarily in Academic Circles as it dates the year as 3104 Solar Days, that being the time between 2 Confluxes. It dates its Year 0 to the founding of the Great City of Rakh and most of our knowledge regarding it stems from the Alwazattar Tablets, created by Phiodales of Mizk in the 1st Century BA.


**The Elven Calendar**

Also known as the Elbae Calendar, it is a fixed Calendar Dividing the Year into 400 days, consisting of 4 seasons of 90 days and an intercalary month of 40 days that are treated as outside the year proper. Current understanding of the earliest development of the [[Elbae]] Calendar is unclear 22but likely relates to discoveries regarding the heliacal rising of the star [[Malham]] during the early first Jirahid Dynasty.
It is Commonly used in lands on the Beheran Subcontinent.

**The Alder Calendar**
Usually called the Alder Calendar, this is a lunar calendar that bases each month on a full cycle of the Moon's phases, lunation, without regard for the solar year. Its current year 0 is dated to approximately 819PA. Notably the Calendar is "reset" during times of great importance by the Narzoi, while other cultures such as the pharang tend to keep to the "true calendar", that dates the year 0 to approximately 4720BA, making it the oldest kept calendar in the known world.
The Alder Calendar observes Luna Major that completes a lunar orbit roughly every 39.5 days, as such the inaran calendars that date a month to exactly 40 days tend to drift away from the Alder Calendar.
