[[Meadland]]
%%
<p>The Meadi peoples, together with their closely related Weadi cousins on the Torn Sea Coast, are the inhabitants of Meadland, named after the River Mead, simply meaning "Those who come from the west" or just "west" in the tongue of the ancient tribes during the Age of the <a href="Auguri Dominion" class="internal-link">Auguri</a>, Weadi simply referring to "the North" in the same way. The Meadi make up one of the largest and culturally influential groupings of humans on Inara and are largely united under the Crown of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a> in the modern day. </p>
<h2>The Meadi</h2>
<p>The Meadi can be roughly divided into the Higher & Lower Meadi or Meadmer, situated in the south and north of western Meadland alongside the eponymous Mead river itself respectively; the Rhilmer of central Meadland who make up the largest culture group within the entire Region; the Gismer of the <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a>, named after the legendary Hero Gismar who slew the <a href="Dragon" class="internal-link">Dragon Heximar</a> in elder days; The peaceful but largely isolationist Hundi living around the Whisperhills where the Trinmo finds its spring and lastly the Greenfolk who are the wild and ferocious inhabitants of the Trinwood Hills still beholden to the ancient ways of the land.</p>
<h2>The Weadi</h2>
<p>The Weadi, or simply the Weadmer are often seen as their separate cultural group, largely due to their different albeit related language, their political independence from Meadrath and their more liberal and autonomous culture, beyond obvious historic differences and challenges of course. The Weadi have seen themselves sometimes as the Four Tribes of Wead, the Lotmer, Frijmer and Osmer, named after the three largest river dividing the flatlands of Weadland, although they are largely united within the loose political entity of the <a href="Blijgrat" class="internal-link">League of Blijgrat</a> in modern times, as well as the Kranmer of the Moors currently ruled by <a href="Lesmere" class="internal-link">Lesmere</a> as a Duchy under Meadrath.</p>
