<p>The Pehki are the oldest human inhabitants of the lands of <a href="Nostroya" class="internal-link">Nostroya</a>, traditionally associated with the fierce fighters of the forest of modern day Ukiolla, they still hold faith with the ancient traditions of the <a href="Aeldcult" class="internal-link">Aeld</a> where almost all of their old neighbours have embraced the Candle Flame of <a href="Gethricism" class="internal-link">Gethric</a>. </p>
<p>The three most well known divisions within the Pehki people are the Roymer, the Ukiollan and the Sehvki tribes, all with their distinct and problematic histories, often coloured by their relationship with the invaders from the west, the Grachi.</p>
<p>To day, many Roymer have assimilated within <a href="Ukiolla" class="internal-link">Ukiolla</a> or <a href="Grachy" class="internal-link">Grachy</a> itself, following the total collapse of <a href="Roygviil" class="internal-link">Roygviil</a> leading to the contemporary habit of simply calling all natives within the Principality of Ukiolla the "Pehki" and not differentiating between the two groups. The Sehvki, men of the mountains, make up most of the remaining population. Their relationship with the pehki is amicable but clearly defined, as they live mostly isolated and in small communities, usually at the slopes of the Mistspires or within the reaches of the Aeldveil. There are some northern Sehvki tribes that roam the Waelajokull, but contact with them is rare and it would be hard to describe them as citizens of the Principality. The Roymer that originally inhabited southern Nostroya where almost entirely eradicated, subsumed or displaced by the Grachian Expansionist policies, today few of them are still alive, many of them living in smaller communities within the <a href=" The Jättilkivi" class="internal-link">Jättilkivi</a> or having fled north towards the Principality.</p>
