<p>The Ganoan are the human peoples of the <a href="Ganoa Valley" class="internal-link">Ganoa Valley</a> and its neighbouring lands in southern Nirydia, living around Lake Ganoa, in the Ganoan Mountains, in the Citrine Swamp and on the Az Peninsula. Together with the Ganoan <a href="Bihra" class="internal-link">Bihra</a>, with whom they have shared the lake for centuries, they are commonly called the Ganoans, and within the valley itself the humans make up the larger part of the population. The Ganoan served the Khans of the <a href="Luchun Khaganate" class="internal-link">Luchun Khaganate</a> for over a century after the fall of <a href="Ekkam" class="internal-link">Ekkam</a> in 551PA, and since the end of the Luchun in 671PA most of them have lived in the independent cities of <a href="Jink" class="internal-link">Jink</a> and <a href="Majir" class="internal-link">Majir</a> or in the <a href="Kareli Hierarchy" class="internal-link">Kareli Hierarchy</a>, of whose people they make up a sizeable part, while a smaller minority lives in <a href="Roxeria" class="internal-link">Roxeria</a> and has taken up the faith of the Javalim. Outside of Roxeria itself, and unlike most human peoples of the region they follow the <a href="Vas Ira" class="internal-link">Vas Ira</a>, and the sect of the <a href="Karaluk" class="internal-link">Karaluk</a> that reveres the One of the <a href="Javalim" class="internal-link">Javalim</a> as a sixth Great draws most of its followers from among them.</p>  
  
<p>The Lake Ganoans along the shores of Lake Ganoa are the largest group and the most closely bound to the Ganoan Bihra, sharing with them the guilds, ferries and markets of Jink and Majir, where many have worked in the Parabol Domes since the 820s. The Mountain Ganoans of the highland villages in the Ganoan Mountains are the most isolated and have mixed least with the Bihra, while the Swamp Ganoans of the Citrine Swamp form, together with the people of the valley, the heartland of the sect of the One. The Ganoans of Az live on the Az Peninsula, in the countryside once ruled by the <a href="Az" class="internal-link">Omna of Az</a> and in the free port of Aznyr, where they make up much of the human population of the Hierarchy. Small clans of <a href="Ral" class="internal-link">Ral</a> shifters also live among the Ganoans, although here as elsewhere in the south they are widely feared.</p>
