<p>The Vihri peoples are descendants of the old "Men of the Horn", the literal translation of Vihrmen and likely in reference to the Unicorns of the expanse. While all three groups share distinct lingual roots, they are usually broadly divided into the so called "Three old Families" of the Vihri, the Grachi and the Wermi peoples.</p>
<h2>The Vihri</h2>
<p>The largest of the Families, the Vihri or often called Vihrmen are those humans living in the rich lands east of the Trollback mountains and are currently all ruled within the opulent and powerful <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Rascy zi Vihr</a>. The Vihri can be roughly divided into the so called "Higher" Vihri, of the elevated oath hills and the the moors to the west of the Tryd River, primarily administered in <a href="Rovgi" class="internal-link">Rovgi</a> and parts of <a href="Torry" class="internal-link">Torry</a>; the "Lower" Vihri of the basin and farmland to the central regions of the lower Tryd and Lehva River where the realms Capital of Zavlech, <a href="Iztaw" class="internal-link">Iztaw</a> and <a href="Trazlova" class="internal-link">Trazlova</a> rule; The Tlok, or <a href="Tloky" class="internal-link">Tloky</a>, that reside in the Tlogy Valley in the east of the Nation; the Wytmen who inhabit the Ravenleaf Forest and the aptly named Wytlands to its north as well as the Sarloni of the Expanse. While the Sarloni and Wytmen technically occupy the largest territory out of all Vihri they make up the two smallest groupings in sheer number and are often seen as old fashioned, backwater and simpleminded people, some rumoured to even still hold true to the old gods of Turv. In the late 6th century a violent and blood revolt of the people of the Expanse against their northern overlords  in <a href="Breza" class="internal-link">Breza</a>, <a href="Opuzna" class="internal-link">Opuzna</a> and <a href="Selbia" class="internal-link">Selbia</a> was put down, now known as the Sarloni Uprising. </p>
<p></p>
<h2>The Grachi</h2>
<p>The Grachi are descendants of those vihri settlers that followed Fidor of Trazlov when he sailed the Iron Claw and steeped foot in the land of <a href="Nostroya" class="internal-link">Nostroya</a> in 124PA. The Grachi can easily be divided between those who found in the cities and towns of the nostroyan lowlands, organized in the <a href="Halgrach" class="internal-link">Rascy zi Halgrach</a> and later the <a href="Grachy" class="internal-link">Grachy Republic</a> and opposed to them the so called "Free people of the Hyll" or simply the Hyllmen, that reside in the passes and smaller communities of the Grachhyll. Albeit over centuries of urbanization and cultural development, the people of the lowlands have themselves grown somewhat apart between the eastern Grachi of the rural, wild and harsh east and the southern "Grachior" of the lands south of the Halgior River and the citizens of the Coastal ports and communities of the so called "old land".</p>
<h2>The Wermi</h2>
<p>The Wermi, a term derived from the legendary hero Wermaz, are the branch of the vihri with the oldest but also the most tragic documented history out of the three families. The <a href="Wermia" class="internal-link">Kingdom of Wermia</a>, before its fall and the two Incursions, was one of the oldest feudal realms in all of Ilrakhan, competing with the <a href="Auguri Dominion" class="internal-link">Auguri</a> and standing in exchange with <a href="Urien" class="internal-link">urien</a> and Dwarvish communities for centuries before the Vihrmen of the Expanse ever began to unite as tribal federations. In the modern day the Old Wermi are group of humans living under the administration of <a href="Nuv Werm" class="internal-link">Nuv Werm</a> and the <a href="Order of the Silver Wheel" class="internal-link">Order of the Silver Wheel</a> around the Neverrest Heights and east of the Weirwood. The Taarmen are often differentiated from these "Old" Wermi due to their live away from the organised administration of the eastern parts of Wermia, living primarily in and around the Wermian Moor and the Hagshorn. In recent times many of them have re-organized, largely due to investments from the City of <a href="Uhl" class="internal-link">Uhl</a>, in the Realm of <a href="Taar" class="internal-link">Taar</a> officially recognized by Ras Orian II of Vihr in 879PA. </p>
