<p>The Beheri humans are amongst, if not the oldest cultures of humans, they reside in the western Beheran Desert, western Behera is known as the birthplace of agriculture and in some cases human civilisation itself although the latter is debatable. The ancient Civilization of the Hessir peoples is recorded as far back as 4800BA, nearly 6000 years removed from the present day.</p>
<h2>The Asefi, Horadi, Hessefi, Haeni & Almadi</h2>
<p>The Asefi are the ancient inhabitants of the northernmost parts of Behera, beyond the River Muhusni centred around the ancient Cities of Erun, Mereskis and Koron, once united by the ancient Marival of <a href="Samyr" class="internal-link">Samyr</a> but since having bent their knees before reani and elven conquerors, they value their cultural heritage and religious traditions immensely and are largely left alone in this regard by their elbae overlords. The Horadi are a seafaring peoples living around the Hoard, once an isthmus now a peninsular. The Hessefi are by far the largest group of the Beheran, they populate the cities in the heartland of the Sultanate, between the Muhusni and Za-Jira Rivers, and have almost entirely been absorbed by elven culture through their shared history. Their history dates back to the ancient Isphing Civilization that worshipped the immortal Sphinxes of the Desert, now long lost to the sands of time.</p>
<p>The Haeni and Almadi are newer cultures, compared to the aforementioned at least, emerging from elven expansionism and colonization, the Haeni in the western coast of <a href="Il Haena" class="internal-link">Il-Haena</a>, before during and after the even conquest of Qa, and the almadi the newest group of elven, human and gnomish peoples that live in the coastal hills of Iz-Almadus, protected by the Ilkiztis.</p>
<h2>The Zipivi, Yorgosi & Vendi</h2>
<p>The Zipivi are the people of <a href="Old Tusul" class="internal-link">Old Tusul</a>, now conquered during the White Wars and largely under the rule of the new Emirate of <a href="Tusul" class="internal-link">Tusul</a>, yet still largely rebellious against elbae rule. The Yorgosi and the Vendi live free of elven administration even further south, in the vale of <a href="Yorgos" class="internal-link">Yor</a>, the Salt Plains of the White Abyss and the expanse of the Ud-Vendili dunes. These are fiercely independent and largely different tribes to the northerners, having never known foreign rule. The ancient high culture of <a href="High Vendil" class="internal-link">High Vendil</a> achieved technological progress and great wonders in times where men, elf and gnome were still smelting bronze but collapsed long before records were kept. </p>
<h2>The Arevi, Inori & Migoni </h2>
<p>The Inori & Mignoi are pastorally nomadic tribes, that have inhabitaed the central Beheran desert for thousands of years, they trade with the Sultanate, the Yorgosi, the Safiri and the Palmuni all the same, and the secrets of the deepest deserts are theirs to keep, their kingdom without crown but their realm greater than any other. The <a href="Arevi Yerkir" class="internal-link">Arevi</a> are their northern cousins,  inhabiting, the foothills of the Cloudscratchers. Known as masterful horsemen and fierce protectors of their sacred lands, they have remained in a state of perpetual conflict with the <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae Sultanate</a> for generations. </p>
