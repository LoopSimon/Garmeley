<p>The Inive are the <a href="Azbihra" class="internal-link">Azbihra</a> and humans of the Forbidden Lands, the green south-western reaches of the <a href="Niryd Steppe" class="internal-link">Niryd Steppe</a> between the Kherovan frontier and the <a href="Blodrise Dark" class="internal-link">Blodrise Dark</a>. They are known for their red skin, which the gases that rise from beneath their land have given them, and their name derives from the <a href="Kheri" class="internal-link">Kheri</a> word iniv, meaning "red". The Inive have never formed a realm of their own. They live in scattered communities that often pay tribute to the most powerful Khans of the <a href="Steppe Bihra" class="internal-link">Steppe Bihra</a> of the time, and in the modern day many of them pay tribute to the <a href="Duol Khaganate" class="internal-link">Duol Khaganate</a>.</p>  
<h2>Geography</h2>  
<p>The Forbidden Lands are sometimes counted as part of the steppe and sometimes not. They lie east of the lands of the Kurini, the frontier people of <a href="Kherova" class="internal-link">Kherova</a>, and west of the Blodrise Dark, with the open grasslands of the Duol to the north. Unlike the heart of the steppe, where the Aspira grows high enough to cut flesh to pieces, the grass of the Forbidden Lands is milder and the land rich and green. Gases rise from beneath the ground in many parts of it, and it is these that have turned the skin of its people red over the generations.</p>  
<p>The Kheri have tried to settle the Forbidden Lands for as long as they have fought the Khans of the steppe, but every Kheri city and fort ever raised there has fallen to the bands and Khaganates of the Steppe Bihra, and the Kheri know the land by its name for that reason.</p>  
<h2>Society</h2>  
<p>The Inive are made up of Bihra and humans who live side by side. Most of the Bihra among them are Azbihra, and though they share the land with the Steppe Bihra who raid and rule it, the Steppe Bihra count them among the Zibihra, the lesser Bihra of the tributary lands. Both Bihra and humans among the Inive bear the red skin of the Forbidden Lands, and it marks them as Inive wherever they travel.</p>  
<p>Having no strength to hold the Forbidden Lands against the Khans, the Inive communities pay tribute to whichever Khan is strongest in the south of the steppe, and their loyalty has shifted with the rise and fall of the Khaganates. In the time of the <a href="Great Khaganates of the Steppe" class="internal-link">Great Khaganates</a> they paid tribute to the Great Khans, and since the rise of the Duol in 851PA most of their tribute has gone to Duol Rabad.</p>  
<h2>Notable Inive</h2>  
<p>The monk Cinderstone Mikor, known in <a href="Ikara" class="internal-link">Ikara</a> as the <a href="Red Monk" class="internal-link">Red Monk</a>, was born among the Inive in 865PA and became an elder member of the <a href="Cinderstone Temple" class="internal-link">Cinderstone Temple</a> of Tazil.</p>
