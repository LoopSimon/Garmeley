<p>The Bykmåli are the inhabitants of <a href="Trykkerog" class="internal-link">Trykkerog</a>, the large island north of the Torn Sea, they can be roughly divided into the Olbellinge, Jueli, Hevri and Skori peoples. Bykmåli literally means "inheritors of the land" in the shared tongue of the many Bykmål Dialects. </p>
<p>The natives of Trykkerog are often viewed as harsh and brutish men, primarily due to their long history of raiding the southern coasts, a practice largely replaced by the greater empire building practices of the Baeling Dynasty and phased out following the gethric conversions of Melmers Voyages. The Bykmåli are culturally closely tied to the <a href="Pehki" class="internal-link">Pehki peoples</a> of <a href="Nostroya" class="internal-link">Nostroya</a>, sharing many cultural and religious traditions with them.</p>
<p>The Skori, primarily found within the Realm of <a href="Vignasoya" class="internal-link">Vignasoya</a> are the eastmost cultural group on the Island and hold truth to the old ideals of pre gethric conversions. The Olbelline of central Trykkerog are the largest single grouping of humans on Trykkerog, centred around Melmers Bay and to date united largely under the Banner of <a href="Baeling" class="internal-link">Baeling</a>, always opposed to their eastern rivals, the jueli peoples of the Malksbay, found under the Crown of <a href="Juelfluk" class="internal-link">Juelfluk</a>, the jueli are also found within northern Nirydia on the shores of <a href="Nilborg" class="internal-link">Nilborg</a>. Finally the Hevri people are the smallest grouping of Bykmåli and live in largely isolated communities in the northern wastes of the islands, as well as on top of the heights of Fagnirs Rest. </p>
