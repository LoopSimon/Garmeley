<p>One of the races native to Ilrakhan, the short statured halflings are primarily found in the western parts of the continent within Meadland, Cevolia, Othirdon and Sertova. Usually rising to a humble height of 3-3`6 feet, their ears are slightly pointed but proportionately not much larger than those of a human. While often brought into context with the Dwarves or Gnomes of the mountains, there is no evidence of a shared biological or rather evolutionary history and the Halfling seems to be largely related to the Human.</p>
