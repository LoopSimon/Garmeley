<p>The Narzoi of are a race of blue-skinned, tall and slender humanoids native to the lands of Aisligos, they embody many human features but lack external ears while their noses are broad and flat. The Narzoi of Bulug, often simply called the Bulugi, are usually considered distinct from the "free" Narzoi of Peadyr, Verzi and Aisligos as they have lived for houndreds of years under the rule of the Hiqae of Latazec, being genetically altered through their breeding and biomancy programs. </p>
<p>The Narzoi were the first sentient beings to encounter both the Hiqae and Humans of Eressa and Ilrakhan when those species first set foot on the lands of Aisligos, in a period commonly referred to as the Death of the World in Narzoi culture.</p>
