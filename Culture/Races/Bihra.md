Known to the humans of Kher as the Kobolds, generally smaller sized, Bihra are bipedal, scaled reptilian humanoids that tend to weigh about 120kg, standing at around 2 metres. They are native to the continent of Nirydia and show variance in phenotype based on their environment: There are reports for significant differing in size, head- and tail shape based on regional origin, as well as the striking change in the tint of their scale colour.
A sub group of Bihra, living in the hostile [[Niryd Steppe]], are abnormally prone to mutation and worship stronger and more exotic variants among them religiously and culturally, this can take on extremes such as some Bihra standing at almost 9 metres tall and possessing 2 or even 3 heads and other abnormal characteristics.
Although they like claim kinship to [[Dragon]]s, there is no evidence outside of the mythological that would agree with the idea the two races are related. 
Many Bihra of the Steppes have evolved to possess extremely durable protective hides covering their entire body, to endure the dangerous Aspira Bladegrass.

In opposition to these more "monstrous" Bihra, more known to the western ilrakhani, the eastern Bihra are the other part of the same coin, their mutations much tamer and many of the visually and physically similar to each other.
The eastern Bihra are smaller but still bipedal reptilian humanoids, most standing between 1 metre and 1 metres 60 tall, having powerful jaws for creatures of their size and noticeable claws on hands and feet. Many eastern Bihra have varying exotic colours and stronger scales than their western cousins, but as mentioned before they are significantly less prone to great physical mutation.
