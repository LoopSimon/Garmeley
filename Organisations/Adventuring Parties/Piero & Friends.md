<p>Piero & Friends, was a now disbanded party of adventurers operating in the once united Duchy of Garmeley in 922PA. They are named after the Bard Piero whose illusions of grandeur were usually indulged by the remaining members of the party. Infamously this group of heroes was present during the Assassination of Duchess Agnes of Garmeley and were the ones who hunted down her killer, the rogue mage Jonek Two-Tongues. Months later they would find themselves at the Monastery town of Fairriver and save its populace from an undead evil threatening to take over the entire popultion. Piero & Friends consisted of:</p>

<p><a href="Elena Montegru" class="internal-link">Elena Montegru</a>, a <a href="Urien" class="internal-link">Garmish</a> Duellist from Dolm,</p>
<p><a href="Mikael Bivaldi" class="internal-link">Mikael Bivaldi</a>, a reani Knight, known as the "Sword of the Sea", the last of the <a href="Knights of the Spring" class="internal-link">Knights of Spring</a>, and a former member of the <a href="The Merry Three" class="internal-link">Merry Three</a>,</p>
<p><a href="Piero Digolo" class="internal-link">Piero Digolo</a>, a <a href="Cevoli" class="internal-link">levi human</a> Bard from the city of Battvia,</p>
<p><a href="Jana Rikolore" class="internal-link">Jana Rikolore</a> a <a href="Urien" class="internal-link">Gim</a> Cleric and devout follower of  <a href="Dorian" class="internal-link"> Saint Dorian</a>.</p>
