<p>Piero & Friends, was a now disbanded party of adventurers operating in the once united Duchy of Garmeley in 922PA. They are named after the Bard Piero whose illusions of grandeur were usually indulged by the remaining members of the party. Infamously this group of heroes was present during the Assassination of Duchess Agnes of Garmeley and were the ones who hunted down her killer, the rogue mage Jonek Two-Tongues. Months later they would find themselves at the abbey town of <a href="Fairriver" class="internal-link">Fairriver</a> in the Pilv Valley, where the dead Abbot Hoster had returned from the grave through a pact with the witch Simila the Sweet. They killed the witch, returned the stolen Shawl of Respite to the abbey and banished the undead abbot, saving the town from an undead evil threatening to take over its entire population. Piero & Friends consisted of:</p>

<p><a href="Elena Montegru" class="internal-link">Elena Montegru</a>, a <a href="Urien" class="internal-link">Garmish</a> Duellist from Dolm,</p>
<p><a href="Mikael Bivaldi" class="internal-link">Mikael Bivaldi</a>, a reani Knight, known as the "Sword of the Sea", the last of the <a href="Knights of the Spring" class="internal-link">Knights of Spring</a>, and a former member of the <a href="The Merry Three" class="internal-link">Merry Three</a>,</p>
<p><a href="Piero Digolo" class="internal-link">Piero Digolo</a>, a <a href="Cevoli" class="internal-link">levi human</a> Bard from the city of Battvia,</p>
<p><a href="Jana Rikolore" class="internal-link">Jana Rikolore</a> a <a href="Urien" class="internal-link">Gim</a> Cleric and devout follower of  <a href="Dorian" class="internal-link"> Saint Dorian</a>.</p>
