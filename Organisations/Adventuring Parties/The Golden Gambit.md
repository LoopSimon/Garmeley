<p>The Golden Gambit is a group of like minded adventurers that first gained notoriety in the summer of 927PA while travelling through eastern <a href="Hainach" class="internal-link">Hainch</a> and the western <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Sultanate</a>. They consist of:</p>
<p>Perzuk Uz-Kram, a <a href="Goblinoid" class="internal-link">goblin</a> Daggermaster of unknown origin,</p>
<p>Marianna Kaminska, a <a href="Vihri" class="internal-link">wermian</a> Knight of the <a href="Gethricism" class="internal-link">Gethric Faith</a>, devoted to <a href="Lyandra" class="internal-link">Lady Lyandra</a>,</p>
<p>Yara, a <a href="Mogyr" class="internal-link">mogyri</a> spellcaster and former  <a href="Rash Gopal" class="internal-link">Rash Gopal Operative</a>,</p>
<p>Zinris Nava, a <a href="Vihri" class="internal-link">low vihri</a> <a href="Alchemy" class="internal-link">Alchemist</a> with a paranoid complexion.</p>
<p>During Ikaras <a href="The 927 Falcons Flight" class="internal-link">Falcons Flight Tournament of 927PA</a> they would first appear in public under the Golden Gambit moniker, representing the rich Dye Magnate <a href="Kessim Miximur" class="internal-link">Kessim Mixmiur</a>.</p>
