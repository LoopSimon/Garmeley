[[The Poem of the Bad Omen]]

<p>The Bad Omen was a group of like minded adventurers that ventured into the unknown, following the <a href="The Golden Ring" class="internal-link">Golden Ring</a> in the late Fall of 904PA. The party was assembled by the elven twins Jorji and Maiwynn and sought to find out what happened to the four famous adventurers, that had disappeared in the turvik ruins known as the Howling Henge, south of the Wormwoods near Ansley. They consist of: </p>
<p><a href="Sturla" class="internal-link">Sturla Omenborn</a>, a <a href="Bykmåli" class="internal-link">Hevri</a>  Ranger from the Stormlands of Trykkerog,</p>
<p>Harold, a <a href="Urien" class="internal-link">gim</a> Swashbuckler and follower of the <a href="Gethricism" class="internal-link">Gethric Faith</a>,</p>
<p>Orlatz, a <a href="Vihri" class="internal-link">low vihri</a> Axethrower and mercenary,</p>
<p>Jorj Ainé Ael, a sertovi <a href="Half-Elf" class="internal-link">Half-Elf</a> Knight and devout <a href="Panrity" class="internal-link">follower of the Creator</a>, ever masked.</p>
<p>Maiwynn Armelle Ainé Ael, a sertovi <a href="Half-Elf" class="internal-link">Half-Elf</a> Wizard of pale complexion, always at her brothers side.</p>
