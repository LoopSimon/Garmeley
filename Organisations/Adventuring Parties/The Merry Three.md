<p>The Merry Three were an adventuring party that slaid many a beast and saved a great number of innocents in their exploits, they were active around the Stormy Sea and in southern Vihr around the years of 892 until 901PA. The Party consisted of:</p>
<p><a href="Adral Fidrakis" class="internal-link">Adral Fidrakis</a>, a west Reani Investigator from <a href="Hainach" class="internal-link">Hainach</a>,</p>
<p><a href="Mikael Bivaldi" class="internal-link">Mikael Bivaldi</a>, a east reani Knight, known as the "Sword of the Sea", the last of the <a href="Knights of the Spring" class="internal-link">Knights of Spring</a>, and a future member of the <a href="Piero & Friends" class="internal-link">Piero & Friends</a>,</p>
<p><a href="Xara Ilig" class="internal-link">Xara Ilig</a>, a <a href="Dwarf" class="internal-link">sarloni Dwarven</a> Bounty Hunter from Dalniz.</p>
