<p>The Scarlet Feather Band was formed by Engelbert Gerber, also known as the Gallant Crimson Knight, in the mid 8th century PA in southern <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a>. He was a seasoned adventurer, known for his gallantry, chivalry but also unfaltering will, wearing at the height of his prominence a red armour and scabbard. His most famed act is the vanquishing of the flaming red and infamous cockatrice known as the Scarlet Menace of Gisvale. The <a href="Cockatrice" class="internal-link">Cockatrice</a> and its flock terrorised many villages in Gisvale until they were put down by Engelbert and his party in 905 when he was 28 years old. He lost his right arm in the final battle with the beast, which dampened his will to fight but not to continue to have an impact in the world. He took every scarlet feather from the Cockatrice and preserved them in resin. Thus his Scarlet Feather Band was born, while it was officially a mercenary band it was more akin to a noble order of chivalry. He wanted to promote a cheerful behaviour and chivalric deeds while also giving mercenaries a place to test their mettle and prove their worth.</p> <p>While the chivalric orders of Meadrath were mostly kept for nobles and limited in their members, the Scarlet Feather Band was only bound by your own worth. If you were found worthy by Engelbert or proved yourself before him you were eligible to join. You were also not forced to stay with the company, which only almost never engaged in fights between humans but specialised more on hunting those that would threaten humanity and its allies. The company was particularly favoured by <a href="Halfling" class="internal-link">Halflings</a> that loved the cheerful attitude and chivalric codex, they were largely active in southern and eastern Meadrath, Battvia, and all parts of Weadland.</p>
