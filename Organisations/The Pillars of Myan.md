<p>The Pillars of Myan, named after the <a href="Myan-Bond" class="internal-link">Myan Bond</a> between the will of the world and the <a href="Elemental" class="internal-link">Elementals</a>  of Thandars Circle, are a group of Elementalists originating from the students of <a href="Szuan" class="internal-link">Szuan</a> himself. Elementalists, sometimes conflated entirely with the broader term "Channelers" are a group mages that harness the power of the Elements and enter bonds with Myan, who seek to learn and become more sentient by accompanying these Scholars. The Four Pillars of the inner Circle, Fire, Wind, Earth & Water, where held in high regard and their study was more popular than the six transitory Elements.</p>

<p>Among these Elementalists, there are 10 schools known as the Pillars, that specialize in one of the 10 Elemental states of the old Circle of <a href="Thandar" class="internal-link">Thandar</a>. The Pillars were once a royal institution within the later Empire of <a href="Naigan" class="internal-link">Naigan</a>  but many of them fell into dishonour or full disrepair during the <a href="Period of the Bleeding Heron" class="internal-link">Period of the Bleeding Heron</a> . Essentially all magical research and practice of the Yppori of Tal was centralized within these Circles, as the ultimate expression of what Thandar had discovered and Szuan perfected.</p>

<p>In the current day, little is known about the whereabout or fate of many of the Pillars, however the Pillar of Fire and three of its adjacent Elemental Pillars, survived the Era of the Heron and adopted religious iconography, gaining the protection of the Ashcult of Sochor. The Great <a href="Hanshi" class="internal-link">Hanshi</a> outlawed the Pillars in the late 9th century under the often called "Divine Edict" and instead founded the "School of Chorol" that unified Elemental studies and Magical research under a royal institution on the islands of the purple Sea. </p>

<p>There are rumours that some of the Pillars that are largely lost in their old homeland, survived not within Tal but by fleeing outside of it, towards the Sultante of the Elves in north and east and the Safiri islands of the Moons gate to the west.</p>
