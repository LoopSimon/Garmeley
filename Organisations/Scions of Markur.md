<p>The Scions of Markur, sometimes simply called the Scions, are a relatively obscure international organisation that operates throughout <a href="Ilrakhan" class="internal-link">Ilrakhan</a> and even in parts of <a href="Nirydia" class="internal-link">Nirydia</a>. They were founded in the 7th century by Saint <a href="Markur" class="internal-link">Markur of the Many Eyes</a> and work under principles of secrecy, using cloak and dagger tactics. <a href="Avero" class="internal-link">Avero of the Eightfolds</a>, the friend and student of Markur, is usually credited as the first of the Scions.</p>
<h2>Beliefs</h2>
<p>The Scions see themselves as the guardians of the world as it is known and as the wardens of the fundamental balance of magic. Markur, a powerful wizard and one of the most gifted oracles of all time, is said to have founded the Scions on the basis of his later divinations and prophecies, which make up the most closely guarded secrets of the order. Their tenets hold that even to understand the making of the cosmos without disturbing it can change it, that to see it is to change it, and that only a precious membrane of divinity hangs between the world and apocalypse. In practice they enforce aspects of the <a href="Wermian Accords" class="internal-link">Wermian Accords</a> in their own ways and watch over research into the deeper and more occult theorems of the cosmos.</p>
<h2>Organisation</h2>
<p>The Scions have members in the highest stations of society, be it the clergy, academia or the nobility, and they also infiltrate other organisations such as knightly orders and commercial groups. Almost nothing is known about their internal structure, ways of communicating or leadership. In the Order of <a href="Urbridge" class="internal-link">Urbridge</a> they operate in a variant of their own, the Sunwardens, and control the politics of the order.</p>
<p>Unlike other paramilitary or academic organisations, the Scions enjoy large support from old and traditional institutions and are entrenched within the Gethric and Trinicist clergy and the Church itself, which makes them much harder to track down, at least without drawing attention to oneself.</p>
<h2>Killings and Rumours</h2>
<p>The Scions do not shy away from brutal tactics or outright murder. The Paladin Gimmael, all but proven to have been a member of the organisation, infamously killed High Weaver <a href="Krindor" class="internal-link">Modan Krindor</a> of Sallesand in 821PA to put an end to his research.</p>
<p>Beyond such cases, many deaths and killings are attributed to the Scions, often at random. One theory holds that they were behind the arrest of <a href="Kabernos" class="internal-link">Vinfried Kabernos</a> in Gelmsburg in 913PA, and even behind the<a href="Niedrest" class="internal-link">Niedrest Incident</a> of 923PA and the death of Valeri Temzin, although many dismiss the second claim as a rumour and believe the killings at Niedrest to have been an inside job of the <a href="Kherovan Inquisition" class="internal-link">Kherovan Inquisition</a> itself. Others hold the Scions responsible for the death of Saint <a href="Andre" class="internal-link">Andre of Trydlew</a> in 874PA and the disappearance of <a href="Zovac Harlini" class="internal-link">Harlini</a>, and some believe that they work for the elven Sultanate.</p>
