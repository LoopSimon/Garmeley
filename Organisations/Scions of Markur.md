<p>The Scions of Markur, sometimes just called the "Scions" are a relatively obscure international organisation operating in all of Ilrakhan and even parts of Nirydia, they were founded by the Saint <a href="Markur" class="internal-link">Markur of the Many Eyes</a> during the 7th century and operate under principles of secrecy, utilising cloak and dagger tactics. <a href="Avero" class="internal-link">Avero of the Eightfolds</a>, Markurs friend and student, is usually credited to be the first of the Scions.</p>

<p>The Scions have members in the highest stations of society, be it the clergy, academia or the nobility, but also infiltrate other organisations such as Knightly Orders of Commercial Groups. Almost nothing is known about their internal structure, ways of communicating or leadership roles. </p>
 <p>Their main view of themselves seems to be that of Guardians of the world itself as we know it and wardens of the fundamental balance of magic. Markur, himself a powerful Wizard but also one of the most gifted Oracles of all time, is said to have founded the Scions on the basis of his later Divinations and Prophecies, who make up the most well guarded secrets of the Order. Their tenets are that to understand but not disturb the making of the cosmos, can change it in itself, that to see it to change and that only a precious membrane of divinity hangs between the world and apocalypse. </p>
 
 <p>The Scions do not shy away from brutal tactics or all out murder, the Paladin Gimmael, essentially proven to have been a member of the organisation, infamously killed High Weaver <a href="Krindor" class="internal-link">Modan Krindor</a> in 821PA to put an end to his research. Unlike other paramilitary or more academia sourced organisations, the Scions actually enjoy large support from old and traditional institutions and are entrenched within the gethric and trinicist clergy and church itself, making them much harder to track down, at least not before raising attention to oneself.</p>
