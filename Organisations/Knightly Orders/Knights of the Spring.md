<p>The Knights of the Spring were a mythical order of <a href="Rean" class="internal-link">Reani</a> and <a href="Garmeley" class="internal-link">Garmish</a> knights who are said to have slain foul beasts in the name of the Gethric candleflame, mainly in the lands around the Stormy Sea. The order was founded around the 630s PA and was seemingly destroyed during the second <a href="Wars of Elbae Aggression" class="internal-link">Great Elven War</a>. Its knights are associated with Saints such as <a href="Berengar" class="internal-link">Berengar</a>, <a href="Yelena" class="internal-link">Yelena</a> and <a href="Dorian" class="internal-link">Dorian</a>, figures who set out on legendary quests not only to protect the innocent but also to vanquish the foul.</p>
<p>Although the knights are essentially extinct and remembered as heroes of myth, they continue to be emulated and referenced by adventurers and other groups in the present day. <a href="Mikael Bivaldi" class="internal-link">Sir Mikael Bivaldi</a>, the Sword of the Sea, was raised in their traditions by an unknown mentor and is often called the "last Knight of the Spring", and the mysterious <a href="Flower Knights" class="internal-link">Flower Knights</a> seem to emulate the image of the order, at least in their appearance.</p>
