 <p>The Gelmsburg Circle is the old circle of mages of the kingdom of <a href="Gelmsburg" class="internal-link">Gelmsburg</a>, the local centre for the magically gifted of the country and one of the ancient houses of learning in Ur. It sits at the royal court, and its mages have long been spoken of together with it as the Circle and Court of Gelmsburg.</p>  <p>The roots of the Circle lie in the sage practice of the old <a href="Turvik Paganism" class="internal-link">Turvik</a> peoples and in the Auguric tradition, and over the centuries it was established as the place where the gifted of the region were taught, kept and watched. It has always worked in the support of, and under the eye of, the Dukes of Gelm and later the Kings of Gelmsburg, and its fortunes have followed theirs.</p>  <p>The most famous work done at the Circle is the Thesis of Tronos of the Veil, put forward at Gelmsburg in 354PA by the Urien arcanist who would be canonised as <a href="Tronos" class="internal-link">Saint Tronos</a>. His inquiries into spirits and the soul made him a controversial saint, and his methods were a main reason for the restrictions of the <a href="Wermian Accords" class="internal-link">Wermian Accords</a> that the Circle observes today.</p>  <p>In 787PA the student Falia Venwick, a friend of <a href="Jevana" class="internal-link">Jevana</a> of Leston, put on a ring left behind by <a href="Morfeld" class="internal-link">Morfeld</a>, thinking it a relic of the old Urlander magi, and fell under the necromancer's power. Jevana herself was a mage of the Circle, ranking Second of it, until King Gandry II of Gelmsburg courted her again and again and she refused, and her parting from the Circle is often held to be the result. She went to Greenstone Tower. <a href="Horatio Tuliper" class="internal-link">Horatio Tuliper</a>, of the Tuliper clan of Calmbrook, studied at the Circle too, where he met <a href="Filip of Leston" class="internal-link">Sir Filip of Leston</a>, and there are rumours of his experiments and of the haste of his leaving.</p>  <p>The Circle is a member of the <a href="Orenburg Pact" class="internal-link">Orenburg Pact</a>, and the King of Gelmsburg signs the Wermian Accords. The wards on the walls of the <a href="Brass Spire" class="internal-link">Brass Spire</a>, the prison of the capital, are its work.</p>
