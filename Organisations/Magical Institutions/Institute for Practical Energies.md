<p>The Institute for Practical Energies of Almadus, often shortened as IPEA, is the premier institution for <a href="" class="internal-link"></a> and alchemy in northern Behera, Kaito and the larger Niryd Sea, only exceeded in its prestige on the continent by the Toprosum of <a href="Ott" class="internal-link">Ott</a>. Its headquarters are located in <a href="Iz-Almadus" class="internal-link">Iz-Almadus</a> but it has outposts in various corners of Behera and Nirydia, as an institution of learning it requires a letter of recommendation of varying passing marks at higher places of learning. The Institute was founded in 769PA by Alzheman al Bil during the earlier years of the colonies autonomy and has since steadily grown as the 2nd axis of arcane learning in the Sultanate alongside the <a href="Karajal School of Insight" class="internal-link">Karajal School of Insight at Alparaz</a> . The head of the IPEA at this moment in time is <a href="Charatov Podlim" class="internal-link">Charatov Podlim</a>, a Gnome of interntational renown.</p>
