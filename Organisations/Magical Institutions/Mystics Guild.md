<p>The Mystics Guild is an organisation of practitioners of the arcane in southern Nirydia, with its headquarters in the city of Shilmak. One of the Nine Great Guilds of the Grand Principle, it acts as a formal authority over the practice of magick in some places and as an informal one in others, and maintains Chambers in most cities of the <a href="Kareli Hierarchy" class="internal-link">Kareli Hierarchy</a> and far beyond its borders. It teaches the cosmology of <a href="The Anak" class="internal-link">the Anak</a>, the system that is accepted across most of Nirydian scholarship as the true account of the world and its magicks.</p>  
<h4>History</h4>  
<p>The Guild traces its origins to Kareli <a href="Bihra" class="internal-link">Bihra</a> who turned to the forces of the arcane to resist the <a href="Luchun Khaganate" class="internal-link">Luchun Khaganate</a>, and it was founded during the protectorate of <a href="Gaden" class="internal-link">Gaden</a> in the decades after the Khaganate's fall. Before the spread of the Anak the practice of magick in the south was poorly understood and split between many traditions, and the early Guild was less a body of scholars and teachers than an assortment of powerful individuals, valued for the effects and results they could achieve. After the breakaway of the protectorate the Mystics Guild was one of the nine guilds that put their names to the Grand Principle, the founding declaration of the Kareli Hierarchy, and together they have since been known as the Nine Great Guilds of the Grand Principle. As the teachings of the Anak spread through Nirydia in the following century, the Guild adopted them as its doctrine and grew into the school of learned magicians it is today.</p>  
<h4>Organisation</h4>  
<p>The Mystics Guild is organised in the manner of the classical merchant and craft guilds, with a Chamber in each city where it is present, led by a local Guildmaster. Its Chambers can be found in most cities of southern Nirydia, in some of the Gorati countries of the Limsul such as <a href="Ruginë" class="internal-link">Ruginë</a> and Amlija, in Qahor, the capital of the <a href="Qahori" class="internal-link">Qahori Federation</a>, and even in some cities of <a href="Roxeria" class="internal-link">Roxeria</a>.</p>  
<h4>Doctrine and Rivals</h4>  
<p>The Mystics of the Guild consider themselves elegant magicians and hold little respect for the shamanism of old, although most Bihra remain averse to arcane and other non spiritual magicks and still prefer to rely on the long standing and largely oral traditions of <a href="Bihra Shamanism" class="internal-link">Bihra Shamanism</a>. The Guild has every political incentive to protect its monopoly on the mystical and occult arts, and works to erode the power of the "barbarian shamans" and the "degenerate Artificers" wherever it can. The latter have grown into its greatest rival in recent years, as a new wave of arcane scholarship led by the Storogast of Aznyr <a href="Ghiraspir" class="internal-link">Anir Ghiraspir</a> and his school of applied and experimental energies, drawing on the modern <a href="Artificery" class="internal-link">Artificery</a> of the Elbae Sultanate, challenges both the Guild's authority and its teaching of the Anak.</p>
