<p>The Pastelliora is a group of financiers and merchants independent from any specific nation, operating mostly in <a href="Cevolia" class="internal-link">Cevolia</a>, Ur & <a href="Sertova" class="internal-link">Sertova</a>. Their primary concerns are the flow of trade and wealth across south-western Ilrakhan and the investment opportunities overseas discoveries have brought with them, as such not only do they employ a variety of mercenary bands but also have directly sponsored many voyages and explorers attempting to find new and safer naval routes. A relatively new association, they have yet to come into conflict with any major political power and rely almost entirely on soft power and mercantile influence. </p>
<p>The Pastelliora has been in active cooperation with the <a href="Excavators Guild of Butera" class="internal-link">Excavators Guild</a> and has even considered the re settlement of the <a href="Rakh" class="internal-link">rakhi</a> coast as a potential investment opportunity.</p>
