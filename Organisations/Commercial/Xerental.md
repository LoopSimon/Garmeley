<p>The Xerental are the commercial arm of the Hold of <a href="Xer-Kol" class="internal-link">Xer-Kol</a>. Initially a private cartel within the hold that controlled a large portion of the export business with Vihr and Urland, the Xerental eventually grew so large and influential that it was subsumed by the official hold administration, and it now functions essentially as the hold's ministry for trade and finance. The symbol of the Xerental is the Basalt Sun of <a href="Terem-Zal" class="internal-link">Xer-Higrid</a>, a white sun with six flares emitting from it.</p>
<p>The business is commonly associated with Xer-Koli engineering, the hold's most famous international export, which on the continent is exceeded only by the wonders of <a href="Ott" class="internal-link">Ott</a>, although the Xerental itself does not necessarily take a significant part in the design and production of the goods it sells. The hold is widely considered an innovator in firearms and mechanized mining equipment.</p>
<p>The Xerental is one of the largest competitors of the <a href="Trazlov Consortium" class="internal-link">Trazlov Consortium</a>, although the two have at times been known to cooperate in ventures. The organisation's significant advantages include its control over the Xer-Kol tunnel, a major connection through the <a href="Trollbacks" class="internal-link">Trollback</a> mountains, as well as the hold's metalworking expertise.</p>

<h2>Black Powder and Firearms</h2>
<p>Xer-Kol has long been both partner and rival to Ott, and much of the metalwork and construction behind the first Ottian firearms was carried out in collaboration with Xer-Koli engineers. Through this collaboration the secret of <a href="Black Powder" class="internal-link">black powder</a>, which the Ottian Nabat had developed since the 8th century, proved impossible to keep from the hold, and Xer-Kol began refining and manufacturing powder of its own in the mid 910s.</p>
<p>Xer-Koli powder and firearms differ from their Ottian counterparts in method. Where Ottian weapons rely on artefact igniters and alchemical refinement, the engineers of Xer-Kol do their best to avoid both, in part because many dwarves are allergic to magic. Their weapons are therefore free of the artefact components that tie Ottian firearms to <a href="The Ottian Syndicate" class="internal-link">The Ottian Syndicate</a>, and the Xerental has become one of the most important sellers of firearms outside of Ott, although guns and cannons remain extremely rare in the surface world.</p>
<p>The largest customer of Xer-Koli firearms is the Kingdom of <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Vihr</a>. The Xer-Kol tunnel gives the hold a direct route into Vihri lands, the Vihri settlements bordering the hold have long hosted a sizeable dwarven population, and the recent proclamation of the <a href="Church of Iztaw" class="internal-link">Church of Iztaw</a> that the use of firearms in the cause of the Candleflame is just and right has removed much of the religious hesitation that might otherwise have slowed their adoption. While Ottian firearms reach the west chiefly through Meadrath, Vihr has thus come to draw its supply from Xer-Kol.</p>
