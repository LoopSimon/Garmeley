<p>The Nine Great Guilds of the Grand Principle are the nine guilds that signed the Grand Principle, the founding declaration of the <a href="Kareli Hierarchy" class="internal-link">Kareli Hierarchy</a>, when the southern ports broke away in the years after the death of <a href="Gaden" class="internal-link">Gaden</a> in 743PA. The Great Guilds remain among the most powerful institutions of the Hierarchy and many of them reach far beyond its borders.</p>  
<p>The nine are the <a href="Mystics Guild" class="internal-link">Mystics Guild</a>, which holds sway over the practice of the arcane, the <a href="Velvet Caravan" class="internal-link">Velvet Caravan</a>, which carries the trade of the Velvet Road towards the Jade Sea and the ports of Ilrakhan, and the guilds of the Dyers, the Lacemakers, the Spicers, the Jewellers and Pearl Divers, the Slavers, the Mariners and the Moneychangers. The Dyers, Lacemakers and Spicers command the luxury crafts and markets of the Az Peninsula, the Jewellers and Pearl Divers the precious stones and pearls of the Hierarchy's mines and seas, and the Slavers the great slave markets that supply the cotton and rice fields of the south. The Mariners hold the ports and fleets of the Kareli coast, while the Moneychangers handle the coin and credit on which the markets of Aznyr depend.</p>
