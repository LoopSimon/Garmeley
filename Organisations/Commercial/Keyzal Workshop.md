<p>The Keyzal Workshop, named after its founder Iris Keyzal, is an important private organisation within the underland Hold of <a href="Tizin-Kol" class="internal-link">Tizin-Kol</a>, working within the weapons manufacturing sector. They collaborate in many aspects with the distribution network of the <a href="Xerental" class="internal-link">Xerental</a> but are following Tizin-Kol mandates. </p>
<p>Originally a envisioned as the research and development part of the larger Inos Mining Cartel, they made a fortune when selling the now famous Keyzal Visor as military equipment to paying mercenaries of the Mistspire Underlands, and have since the mid 800s begun to branch out into the realm of alternative weapon design. One of their most famous lines of arms is the Keyzal Osmiumthrower, utilizing the revolutionary technology of Mamus Rolim to Currenbend Metal and allow for the devastating propulsion of refined Osmium, the incredibly dense metal holy to the industrious dwarves of Tizin-Kol </p>
