<p>The Third Moon Fleet is a lose Alliance of seafairers, traders, merchants and primarily settlers that have come together to organize a great voyage south to the shores of the New World: the Continent of <a href="Sirenna" class="internal-link">Sirenna</a>. It is primarily based out of the ports of Paqor, New Kulum, <a href="Mereskis" class="internal-link">Mereskis</a> and Kahtis and plans to set sail in 928PA.</p>
<p>It is often seen as a orcish endeavour of the Qahori Qalmyr, an effort of the largely pacifist groupings that do not sail under the flag of the federation to find a new home, instead of follow dreams of a free Qa that have long fallen into the dust of history. Although that would be forgoing the large amounts of not only human, but also Bihra Sailors and settlers from southern Nirydia and Behera that make up large parts of the Fleet as well.</p>
