<p>The Third Moon Fleet is a lose Alliance of seafairers, traders, merchants and primarily settlers that have come together to organize a great voyage south to the shores of the New World: the Continent of <a href="Sirenna" class="internal-link">Sirenna</a>. It is primarily based out of the ports of Paqor, New Kulum, <a href="Mereskis" class="internal-link">Mereskis</a> and Kahtis and plans to set sail in 928PA.</p>
