<p>The Third Moon Fleet is a loose Alliance of seafarers, traders, merchants and primarily settlers that have come together to organize a great voyage south to the shores of the New World: the Continent of <a href="Sirenna" class="internal-link">Sirenna</a>. It is primarily based out of the ports of Paqor, New Kulum, <a href="Mereskis" class="internal-link">Mereskis</a> and Kahtis and plans to set sail in 928PA.</p>
<p>At its core are the followers of the <a href="Path of the Third Moon" class="internal-link">Path of the Third Moon</a>, the denomination of <a href="Qalmyr" class="internal-link">Qalmyr</a> that holds Qa to be a paradise one must make for oneself. For some of them this is an inner peace, but for the Fleet it means a new home for their community, and the news of the New World in the south has drawn many Third Path adherents together into a single endeavour. The Fleet is often seen as an Orcish endeavour of the Qahori, but it does not sail under the flag of the Federation, and it looks to the future rather than to the dreams of a free Qa that have long fallen into the dust of history. Neither is it only Orcish: large numbers of human sailors and settlers, as well as Bihra from southern Nirydia and Behera, make up a large part of the Fleet as well.</p>
<p>The Fleet is not pacifist, whatever its reputation. The Third Path does not hold to pacifism as an absolute tenet and asks its students for force when force is needed to quell greater force and suffering, but it keeps an absolute taboo on killing. The ships of the Fleet are armed and its Orcish crews are armoured in the bone-plate of the Qari, but they sail to settle, not to conquer.</p>
<p>The Fleet's most important sponsor is the <a href="Kareli Hierarchy" class="internal-link">Kareli Hierarchy</a>, and the veteran Captain <a href="Sirmon Rezan" class="internal-link">Sirmon Rezan</a> of the Albatross sails with it. Its course is south, beyond the reach of <a href="Malham" class="internal-link">Malham</a>, and its Qari expect to steer by <a href="Tholmir" class="internal-link">Tholmir</a>, the Warden. The continent was claimed for the Pearl Throne of Hanshi in 924PA, however, and how the Fleet's settlers are to find land on it remains unresolved.</p>
