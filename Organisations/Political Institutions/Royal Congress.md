<p>The Royal Congress is the council of dignitaries that advises the Crown of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a> on matters of economic, national and international policy, and in practice the body through which the Kingdom and its dependencies are administered. It meets at the Korsfall Court and was founded in 898PA by Queen <a href="Rulers of the Meadi" class="internal-link">Gisela III</a>, who named <a href="Odrick von Fulmer" class="internal-link">Odrick von Fulmer</a>, the head of the Royal Diplomatic Corps, the first High-Chancellor of the Realm to lead it.</p>
<h2>Composition</h2>
<p>The Congress sits for the three estates of the realm: the Church, the nobility and the commons. The second estate, the nobility, counts among its members not only the landed houses but also the learned, the nobles of the quill, whom the Meadi hold in high regard, and with them the universities, the greater academic establishment of the realm and, to a degree, the wider magical establishment around <a href="Korsfall College" class="internal-link">Korsfall College</a>. The commons are represented by the burghers and the chartered guilds of the cities.</p>
<p>The premier nobles of the Crown sit in the First Chamber with a permanent chair, and every great vassal realm is represented in the Congress: the Archduchies of <a href="Dirneux" class="internal-link">Dirneux</a> and <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a>, the Duchies of <a href="Lesmere" class="internal-link">Lesmere</a>, <a href="Mear" class="internal-link">Mear</a>, <a href="Morelle" class="internal-link">Morelle</a>, <a href="Risfalt" class="internal-link">Risfalt</a> and <a href="Wargel" class="internal-link">Wargel</a>, the Landgraviate of <a href="Weisch" class="internal-link">Weisch</a>, the Republic of the <a href="Freelands" class="internal-link">Freelands</a> and the city of <a href="Zmalig" class="internal-link">Zmalig</a>. All of them enjoy varying degrees of local autonomy at home.</p>
<h2>Role and History</h2>
<p>The Congress took over the business of the old High Council of the great vassals, in which the archdukes had once sat, and left them with a seat of prestige and little else. On paper it is a merely internal body of administration and efficiency, but in essence it enforces Meadi influence upon the neighbours of the Crown through soft power, and it is largely responsible for the centralization of political power in Korsfall and for the web of dependencies and alliances known as the Fulmer-System, after the High-Chancellor who built it. One of its first tasks was the creation of a government bond system on Korsfall, the "Meadi Town Hall Rent", after the financial crises that had repeatedly struck the royal household in the late 9th century.</p>
<p>The Congress stood at the heart of the rivalry that dominated the last years of the reign of Gisela III, between the universalists around von Fulmer, who wished to concentrate power in Korsfall, and the old guard around the Archduke <a href="Jean du Jera" class="internal-link">Jean du Jera</a>. After the death of the queen in 910PA du Jera tried to disband it, but in 913PA the young Queen <a href="Gisela IV" class="internal-link">Gisela IV</a> took the government into her own hands and restored von Fulmer to his office, and the Congress has remained the heart of the Meadi court ever since.</p>
