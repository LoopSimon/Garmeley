<p>The Rash Gopal was an organisation of diplomates, spies, clerical observers and holy warriors created by the late Mycomother Chorioactis Lingpa Arjanuva that acted both within Drizan and its clan structure as well as acting as foreign escorts and diplomates in neighbouring countries. The Rash Gopal is functionally defunct since the current Mycomother of <a href="Yl-Lhatsi" class="internal-link">the Temple</a>, <a href="Suolang" class="internal-link">Porpolomopsis Lingpa Suolang</a>, disbanded the organisation shortly after her ascendence in the year 907PA.</p>
<p>Chorioactis, hailed by as many <a href="mogyr" class="internal-link">mogyri</a> as one of the greatest Mycomothers in history as well as by as many as one of the worst, created the Rash Gopal as both a way for her to project internal power and authority over the virtually autonomous <a href="Mogyri Clans" class="internal-link">Clans</a>, as well as to satisfy personal curiosity and ambitions in regards to foreign politics. While revisionist attitudes of Suolang may have destroyed many of her policies, the larger cultural shift instituted by Geaster and monitored and maintained by the Rash Gopal is one there is no coming back from.</p>
<p>The Treaty of Nzhun signed in 888PA, is the hallmark achievement of the Rash Gopal and a fundamental symbol for Arjanuvas progressive foreign policy and active attempts to centralize international relations with the mogyri people as a somewhat united entity, as it pertains to the rights of the peninsulars people and free trade within the larger sphere of the Niryd Sea and the 1. <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Sultanates</a> ports.</p>
