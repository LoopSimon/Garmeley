<p>The Penitent Ward is a body of highly effective agents of the Nan of Rites of the <a href="Twintail Imperium" class="internal-link">Twintail Imperium</a>, operating throughout the Imperium. Its best known duty is the discovery and keeping of the <a href="Rodor" class="internal-link">Rodor</a>, Shirin whose magical gifts have taken on a personality of their own, but it also oversees the <a href="Liandar" class="internal-link">Liandar</a> on many of its missions and keeps a close watch on all clerical and arcane research conducted within the Imperium. The Ward was founded by Imperator Yoroy III in 675PA, after the jurisdictional conflicts surrounding the Rodor Tonzuen and his killings in Rezizer, and is led by the Genhai, currently Weidang Lin.</p>  
  
<h2>History</h2>  
<p>Before the foundation of the Ward no single institution of the Imperium held authority over the Rodor. The Nans of the Honanoh, the Office of the Imperial Secretary and the Liandar each claimed a part in the care, study or control of magically gifted Shirin, and their claims frequently overlapped. The weakness of this arrangement was exposed in 674PA, when the Rodor Tonzuen broke out and went on a killing spree in the great city of Rezizer. The inquiries that followed found that the competing interventions of several government institutions, each acting on its own authority and interfering with the others, had brought about his outbreak. In the first months after the killings the Liandar was blamed for the destruction, and its alchemists were persecuted by agents of the Imperial Secretary, until the investigations revealed the part the other offices had played.</p>  
<p>To end these disputes Yoroy III founded the Penitent Ward in 675PA and placed it under the Nan of Rites, giving it sole jurisdiction over the Rodor and the duty of overseeing magical and clerical research throughout the Imperium. The foundation was one of the few lasting reforms of a reign otherwise remembered for its quiet and competent administration.</p>  
  
<h2>Leadership</h2>  
<p>The Ward is led by the Genhai. The Imperator may appoint a Genhai personally, but the office is usually filled by election from among the leading members of the Ward, conducted by the Nan of Rites itself. The current Genhai is Weidang Lin.</p>  
  
<h2>The Keeping of the Rodor</h2>  
<p>When a Rodor is found, whether in a village of the river plain, a temple school or a noble household, the Ward takes them into its care. Rodor are "protected" in enclosed houses maintained by the Ward, where they are looked after, studied and observed by scholars for the rest of their lives. The Nan of Rites presents this as the safeguarding of a gift of <a href="Lakalism" class="internal-link">Lakal</a> and of the people around its bearer, since most Rodor eventually go mad. Because the condition can surface at any stage of life, the Ward's watch never ends with childhood, and its agents keep track of every Shirin known to show magical talent.</p>  
<p>The reign of Yoroy XV "the Mad", a Rodor whose condition had been hidden by the Imperial Palace since his youth, showed the limits of the Ward's reach, since the palace and the imperial family stand outside the authority of the Nan of Rites. Since his death in 926PA the question of whether the Ward should have a say in the palace, and in the examination of imperial heirs, has become one of the quieter but more bitter disputes between the Nan of Rites and the Grand Vizier.</p>  
  
<h2>Oversight of Research</h2>  
<p>Beyond the Rodor, the Ward is charged with keeping a tight eye on clerical and arcane research of every kind conducted within the Imperium. Its agents inspect temple schools, private workshops and the households of mages, and they accompany the Liandar on various missions, a duty that brings the Nan of Rites into the affairs of an institution that otherwise answers only to the Imperial Secretary. The Liandar tolerates this oversight as the price of its privileges, but the memory of the persecutions after Rezizer has never quite faded, and the relationship between the alchemists and the agents who watch them has never been an easy one.</p>  
  
<h2>Limits of Jurisdiction</h2>  
<p>The authority of the Ward ends at the borders of the Imperium. The Ward does not pursue or seize anyone outside the Twintail, and any case beyond the borders that could interfere with its work is referred to one of two other offices: the Tishan, the information gathering office of spies within the Office of the Imperial Secretary, or the Office of Imperial Security Defense, the foreign agents of the Nan of War. Shirin of magical talent who leave the Imperium for the Shirin communities of <a href="Gorat" class="internal-link">Gorat</a> or the ports of the Gavivag Bay therefore pass out of the Ward's reach, although the Tishan or the Office of Imperial Security Defense may continue to watch them on its behalf.</p>
