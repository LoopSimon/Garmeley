<p>The Liandar describes both those who conduct ancient practice of Lian, the shirin word for <a href="Alchemy" class="internal-link">Alchemy</a>, as well as the organization within the <a href="Twintail Imperium" class="internal-link">Twintail Imperium</a> that practices the Art in central nirydia. It is seen as the highest academic calling in Shirin Culture above the practice of magecraft or the runic teachings of artificery.</p>
<p>The Lian can be divided into three practices, the Wudan, Pindan and Taidan. The Wudan describes the creation or infusion of matter interacting with the world, the Pindan opposed to it describes the alteration of organic matter interacting with the self. The Taidan is seen as the crown jewel and highest of the Arts, being the marriage of Wudan and Pindan and only practiced by the foremost teachers of the Lian, the secret crown jewel of the Taidan is the creation of the Runur, the gelfir word for <a href="Homunculus" class="internal-link">Homunculus</a>, the Runur are used as an elite force of loyal guards to protect the Palace of the the Imperator. </p>
<p>As an organization the Liandar stands firmly under the control of the Imperial Secretary within the Imperium, it is headed by the Zuanzaz, or "The Two Minds", a dualist position held by the two highest-ranking members of the Institution.</p>
