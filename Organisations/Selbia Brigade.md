<p>The Selbia Brigade is a standing regiment of <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Vihr</a>, quartered in the Vadcy of <a href="Selbia" class="internal-link">Selbia</a> and commanded by the Hetman of the Brigade. Up to five thousand strong, it is among the most famous bodies of warriors on the continent and is feared far beyond the borders of the Rascy. It was raised after the Sarloni Uprising of 529PA to hold the south of the realm, and in the centuries since it has answered every call of the Ras and fought in every war of the crown. To the Vihri it is a byword for loyalty and for the strength of the realm, while many of the Sarloni of <a href="Breza" class="internal-link">Breza</a> and <a href="Opuzna" class="internal-link">Opuzna</a> see in its red shields the sign of Vihri dominance and of their own oppression.</p>  
<h2>History</h2>  
<p>In 529PA the people of the Expanse, Sarloni and Wyt alike, rose against their northern overlords in Breza, Opuzna and Selbia. The Sarloni Uprising was put down, with Selbia playing an instrumental part, but it had shown the crown how thinly the south was held. In the years that followed, under the regency of <a href="Katerina I" class="internal-link">Katerina</a>, a royal charter raised a standing force in Selbia to hold the south, the first in the realm to stand under arms in peace as well as in war. The charter gave the Brigade its command, its pay and its colour: the pure red shield, which no one but a soldier of the Brigade may bear.</p>  
<p>From the beginning the command went to the Hetman of the Brigade, and from the beginning the Hetmans came from Breza. The office has been held almost exclusively by members of House Dibrevi, the lords of Breza since the Boris Uprising, and rarely by the Var of Selbia himself. The Brigade has fought in all the wars and conflicts of the Ras since, from the wars with <a href="Leok" class="internal-link">Leok</a> and Garmeley to the Wars of Elbae Aggression, and in the <a href="Third War of Elbae Aggression" class="internal-link">Third War of Elbae Aggression</a> it served under Var <a href="Rezvyr zi Sribo" class="internal-link">Rezvyr zi Sribo</a>, the supreme commander of the coalition. In the <a href="Brothers War" class="internal-link">Brothers War</a> its Hetman was Rezvyr's younger son <a href="Idrov zi Sribo" class="internal-link">Idrov zi Sribo</a>, the Var of Selbia, called Quick Idrov, who took command of all the Vihri armies after Ras Vormil II was wounded at the Red Cloud and led the Brigade to the last great Vihri victory of the war at Kanyak Keep in 831PA.</p> 
<p>Its most famous action came at the end of the <a href="War of Horn and Fang" class="internal-link">War of Horn and Fang</a>. At the 2nd Battle of Tears in 866PA, Var Feliks zi Sribo, one of the few Hetmans of the Brigade who were not of House Dibrevi, led it wide around the battle while the Meadi army held the field after days of fighting. The Brigade fell on the Meadi supply convoys, destroyed the knight escort and burned the wagons, and Meadrath had won the field and could do nothing with it. Peace talks began before the winter. Since the Treaty of Fairriver of 890PA the Brigade has also fought beside the warriors of Miograd against the ogre clans of the Grevi Mountains.</p>  
<h2>Organisation</h2>  
<p>The Brigade is commanded by the Hetman of the Brigade, who is usually not the Var of Selbia but a member of the higher nobility of Breza, and for most of its history a Dibrevi. Its companies are known as Banners, each under its own captain, and at full strength the Brigade numbers up to five thousand. Most of them are mounted, and the Brigade is as much at home riding down a convoy as holding a line on foot.</p>  
<p>Only born Vihrmen may serve in the Brigade. The rule sets it apart from the other proud fighting institutions of the Rascy, such as the Cloudcatchers, a renowned company of sellswords, knights and heroes in Breza and <a href="Levazci" class="internal-link">Levazci</a> that counts many foreigners from Grevenna, Garmeley, Xer-Kol and Dalniz in its ranks. Within that rule the Brigade takes whoever can meet its standards: Tloky farm sons, landless Sarloni petty nobles with a name, a horse and a sword, and women as well as men. Its recruits are trained harder than any other soldiers in Vihr, and those who serve out their years retire on generous pensions, which are a charge on the Vadcy of Selbia and which it bears gladly. A veteran of the Brigade is a respected man or woman in any village of the realm.</p>  
<h2>Arms and Colours</h2>  
<p>The Brigade is best known for its red plumes and its red shields, and its soldiers are called the Reds across Vihr. By its founding charter the pure red shield, the traditional Selbian red, may be borne by the Brigade alone, and to bear it without the right is a crime. Officers of higher rank may quarter their personal heraldry in a small corner of the shield, but the field stays red. Its armour is often painted, and it flies the Crimson Banner. Ahead of its cavalry it carries, mounted upon a lance, the personal gauntlet of <a href="Idrov zi Sribo" class="internal-link">Idrov zi Sribo</a>, a symbol of victory and pride known as the Gauntlet of Quick Idrov, which is said to possess magical abilities. Its motto, "Faithful though the sky should fall", speaks of the loyalty to the Ras for which it is famous, however dire the cause may look.</p>  
<h2>Dinazk Keep</h2>  
<p>Most of the Brigade is quartered at Dinazk Keep, a fortress complex in western Selbia. It stands south of Cranerise Lake, the great lake through which the border between Selbia and <a href="Tloky" class="internal-link">Tloky</a> runs, on the Selbian side of that border. The Keep holds the barracks, stables, training grounds and armouries of the Brigade, and its Banners ride out from it to wherever the crown needs them.</p>  
<h2>Reputation</h2>  
<p>The Selbia Brigade is known as a vicious, elite, expertly trained and above all loyal fighting force. It is not a company where one will find the noble knights of grandest blood and tale, and it has never pretended to be. Its soldiers are cold-blooded and efficient killers, warriors and soldiers, not the knights of fairytales, and the Vihri say so with pride. The Brigade has never gone against the command of the Ras, and unlike the Knights of the Unicorn it has stayed out of the Garmish Civil War, which the throne has not called it to.</p>  
<p>Among the Sarloni the Brigade stands for both sides of their history with the north. Many in Breza and Opuzna see it as the sign of Vihri dominance and oppression, raised to keep them down after the Uprising of 529PA. Yet it recruits among the Sarloni too, and a Sarloni veteran of the Reds is a figure of pride in his own village, so that the Brigade divides still further a people who were never one to begin with. Its deserters are rare and not forgiven, and the best known of them, Tomira Krasz, now holds a band of riders in the <a href="Wytlands" class="internal-link">Wytlands</a> and drills them like Brigade soldiers.</p>
