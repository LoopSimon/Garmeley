<p>The Opal Ring is a guild of assassins based in the city state of <a href="Bruša" class="internal-link">Bruša</a> on Sgar Isle, and the most feared organisation of its kind in <a href="Gorat" class="internal-link">Gorat</a> and on the eastern seas. It is one of the institutions for which the City of Masks is most famous, and it is closely connected with the Grandmaster, the ruler of Bruša whose identity is unknown to outsiders. Whether the Grandmaster commands the Ring, is drawn from its ranks or merely makes use of it is a matter of endless speculation in Gorat, and the people of Bruša, when asked, tend to answer that it makes no difference.</p>  
<h2>History</h2>  
<p>The origins of the Ring are obscure. In Bruša it is said to be as old as the city itself, and to have been founded among the anti-imperial dissidents who settled Sgar in the 6th century PA, some of whom had killed officials of the <a href="Zepria" class="internal-link">Zeprian Empire</a> before they fled. The guild first became known throughout Gorat during the Bruša Emancipation, when five Duždi of <a href="Dišela" class="internal-link">Dišela</a> were assassinated within a single year in the period known as the Bleeding of the Shark. The killers were never named, but an opal was found on the body of three of the five Duždi, and few in Diprije doubt where they came from. Since the independence of Bruša in 885PA the Ring has worked more openly, and its services are now sought by rulers, merchant houses and families across the region.</p>  
<h2>Organisation</h2>  
<p>Little is known with certainty of the inner workings of the Ring. Its members are said to be organised in small cells known as settings, each of which knows only its own master and receives its contracts through masked brokers in Bruša, so that no member can betray more than a handful of others. In the city of masks this secrecy comes easily, as members of the Ring wear masks among themselves as well as in public, and many are believed never to have seen the faces of those they serve with. Recruits are said to be drawn from the orphans and foundlings of the city, from the younger children of the families of the Parrots Council and, according to rumour, from among the Bihra of the island.</p>  
<h2>Methods</h2>  
<p>The Ring is known above all for its patience. Its agents are said to spend months or even years in the households of their targets as servants, clerks, oarsmen or musicians before they strike, and it is a common saying in Gorat that the Ring kills its mark long before the mark dies. Its killings are often made to appear as illness, accident or drowning, and its knowledge of poisons is held to be unmatched in Gorat, owing much, it is said, to the plants and venomous creatures of the interior of Sgar and to the lore of the <a href="Bihra" class="internal-link">Bihra</a> tribes who live there. When the Ring wishes a killing to be known as its own, it leaves a single opal on the body, a sign known as the Opal Kiss.</p>  
<p>The Ring accepts contracts from outsiders, which are offered and paid for through its brokers in Bruša. According to its own reputation it never withdraws from a contract once it has been accepted, and it never accepts a contract that would harm the interests of Bruša.</p>  
<h2>Allegiances</h2>  
<p>The Ring is presumed to serve Bruša and the Grandmaster first, and in Diprije it is regarded as an arm of the Bruša government. Dišela counts it among its greatest enemies, and the secret Quiet Council of the Republic is said to devote much of its effort to hunting its agents. The Ring is widely believed to have acted on behalf of <a href="Amlija" class="internal-link">Amlija</a> during the Emancipation, which the crown of Amlija has always denied. In recent years it has been rumoured to take contracts from <a href="Kamerzin" class="internal-link">Kamerzin</a> and other Durenni powers, in keeping with the rumoured agreement between these powers and Bruša. The priests of <a href="Zuhurism" class="internal-link">Pen Zuhur</a> condemn the Ring, though it is often noted that they rarely do so by name.</p>  
<h2>Areas of Operation</h2>  
<p>The Ring operates throughout the Gorati Sea, above all in Dišela, in the courts and cities of Amlija and in the ports of the Gavivag Bay. The Free City of <a href="Sredit" class="internal-link">Sredit</a>, where the hirelings of Jateš and of the Twintail Imperium already skirmish in the Greenlands, is regarded as one of its most profitable fields, and both sides are rumoured to have made use of it. It is also said to be active in the ports of northern Durennis, on the islands of the Vanpur Ocean and along the spice routes that pass through Bruša.</p>  
<h2>Rites and Traditions</h2>  
<p>A recruit of the Ring is said to receive a raw and unpolished opal at the start of their training. Only after the recruit has fulfilled their first contract is the stone cut and set into a ring, a rite known as the Setting, after which the recruit is counted a full member. The ring is never worn openly and is carried on a cord or beneath a glove, and to be found with an opal ring in Dišela is said to be enough to be hanged. When a member dies, their opal is taken back by the guild and cast into the sea off the harbour of Bruša.</p>  
<p>Members of the Ring are believed to wear masks of a pale and shimmering white, the colour of the opal, when they act in the name of the guild. Although the people of Bruša wear masks of every kind and colour, such opal-white masks are avoided by its citizens, and to wear one at the festivals of the city is considered either a grave insult or a very poor joke.</p>
