<p>The Cinderstone Temple is a monastic group of warriors in service to the divine Tears located on the island of Tazil in the niryid Sea, its members travel the world in personal missions of great spiritual importance and are known as the Cinderstones, the temple is primarily made up of human, bihra and orcish members, but there are reports of mogyri followers as well.</p>
<p>Within the greater <a href="Javalim" class="internal-link">Javalim</a> belief system, the Divine Tears are often viewed as the holiest artifact in existence, and the only way one could be made aware of the "One Mind". Pilgrimage to Cinderstone Temple and reception at the Oracle of the Divine Tear is a seldom granted privilege. Cinderstone is inofficially part of <a href="Roxeria" class="internal-link">Chosen Realm of Roxeria</a></p>
