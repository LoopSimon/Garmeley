<p>The Broken Fingers are a monastic Order, ordained to Gethricism and specifically the Saint Razmyrs teachings, they were founded in the 5th century in southern Grevenna on the island of Ardol where their headquarters, the Fortress of Ardol, is located to this day. Later on they would become one of the strongholds and centres of what is now known as <a href="Razmyrism" class="internal-link">Razmyrism</a>.</p>
