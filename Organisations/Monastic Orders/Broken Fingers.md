<p>The Broken Fingers are a monastic order devoted to Gethricism and specifically to the teachings of Saint <a href="Razmyr" class="internal-link">Razmyr</a> the Soul Shepherd. They were founded in the 5th century on the island of Ardol in southern <a href="Grevenna" class="internal-link">Grevenna</a>, where their headquarters, the Fortress of Ardol, stands to this day. Later on the order became one of the strongholds and centres of what is now known as <a href="Razmyrism" class="internal-link">Razmyrism</a>, and its stalwart monks are the best known institution of the movement.</p>
<h2>Name</h2>
<p>Although the ideas of Razmyrism likely date back to the founding days of the Church, the movement only gained wider popularity in the early 8th century. In 713PA the Var Gizimor zi Selbia had all practising Razmyrists in his lands rounded up, peaceful as they were, and his knights mutilated their hands by trampling them under the hooves of their horses. The monks, clerics, priests, scholars and warriors of the order later adopted the name of the Broken Fingers as a mark of pride.</p>
<h2>Relations with the Church</h2>
<p>Razmyrism reveres Razmyr as the one true Prophet and questions the role of Gethric in the <a href="The Incursion" class="internal-link">Incursion</a>, and its small and isolated communities in Grevenna, Dalniz, Il-Haena, Hainach and southern Ur have been repeatedly persecuted. The <a href="Church of Iztaw" class="internal-link">Church of Iztaw</a>, however, largely tolerates Ardol, as the order has never acted against the Church in any observable way. Razmyrists are a minority in most places, but one that observes almost all of the Gethric rites properly, and they are far more closely aligned with High Gethricism than with Trinicism, and far smaller.</p>
<p>Much of the work of the order takes place in heathen lands, in Il-Haena, on the Niryd Sea, in the Sultanate, in Hainach and even in southern Tal, and as missionaries its monks have done much to spread the faith.</p>
