<p>Meadland is the region of northern <a href="Ilrakhan" class="internal-link">Ilrakhan</a> that lies north of the <a href="Kortopis" class="internal-link">Kortopis</a>, stretching from the Lowsteps in the west to the Bend of the <a href="Trinmo" class="internal-link">Trinmo</a> in the east, and from the Maidensea and the Bloody Strait in the north to the foot of the mountains in the south. It is the home of the ancient <a href="Meadi" class="internal-link">Meadi and Weadi</a> peoples, and today most of it lies within the sphere of the <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Kingdom of Meadrath</a>, the so called "Meadi Empire".</p>

<h2>Land and Water</h2>
<p>Most of Meadland is a plain of broad river valleys and gentle hills, closed in by mountains on three sides. In the south the Kortopi Mountains fall onto the Meadi Highlands, and in the north-west the Urdfall Peaks rise above <a href="Sulbra-Kol" class="internal-link">Sulbra-Kol</a> and <a href="Cimfon" class="internal-link">Cimfon</a>. From them the Lowsteps run south, a long chain of foothills that shuts the valley of the Mead off from <a href="Othirdon" class="internal-link">Othirdon</a> and gives the <a href="Freelands" class="internal-link">Halfling Freelands</a> their home. Within this frame lie the Whisperhills in the north, where the Trinmo rises, and the Trinwood in the east, a wide belt of forested hills that divides the Meadi heartland from the wetlands of the Weadi.</p>
<p>Two great rivers drain the land. The Mead flows north from the uplands of Dirneux past Jera, Morelle, Roiberg and Gloss to the Maidensea, where Meadhavn stands at its mouth, and it is joined at Jera by the Lind, which comes down from the lake of Drakesbleed past Lindfalls. The Trinmo is the greater of the two. It rises in the Whisperhills, runs south-east past Korsfall and Wormill, flows by Giselstatt and turns south at the Bend, where Trinfurt, Barbridge and Turv, the Bendburgs, stand on its banks, before it leaves Meadland for Ur and the far south. East of the Trinwood the Frij, the Lot and the Ostor run through the Weadi lowlands to the coast, and in the north the Weisch and the Bela flow to the Bloody Strait. The larger lakes are Lake Rog near Korsfall, Lake Eisig in the north and Lake Tran near the Bend.</p>
<p>The northern shore looks out on the Maidensea in the west and the Bloody Strait further east, the channel between Meadland and <a href="Trykkerog" class="internal-link">Trykkerog</a>, and the north-eastern coast on the Gullsong Sea. The Meadi count the Gullsong Sea as a sea of its own, the western counterpart of the Torn Sea beyond the Bay of Uhl, although some mariners still name the whole water between Trykkerog and the mainland the Torn Sea.</p>

<p>Meadlanders name their country by its lands rather than its realms. The Meadi Lowlands are the broad plains between the Mead and Korsfall, and Huners Fields the farmland between them and the Whisperhills. North of the Whisperhills the Silverwoods and the Dale lead down to the coast at Zmalig, a country the old Meadi call the Drakstal. The Westerlands are the lands on the left bank of the Mead below the Lowsteps, and the Meadi Highlands are the hill country north of the Kortopis between Praum and Wormill, where the Wundore of the Allwund crater is mined at the town of Wundplaz. Along the Trinmo between Rahn and Wormill lies Melaras Gift, the stretch of river that the old cults hold to be a gift of the river goddess <a href="Melara" class="internal-link">Melara</a>. East of the Trinwood the land flattens into the Frijlands and the Weadi Wetlands, and in the south-east the moor of Gismoor spreads along the Trinmo west of Giselstatt, the Kranmoor lies east of it towards the sea, and the Silentree Forest covers the western bank at the Bend.</p>
<h2>The Realms of Meadland</h2>
<p>The Crown's own lands lie around Korsfall in the heart of the country, and the great vassal realms ring them. In the west the Archduchy of <a href="Dirneux" class="internal-link">Dirneux</a> holds the upper valley of the Mead around Jera and the Arvale Pass at Arkeep, and borders the gnomes of <a href="Ott" class="internal-link">Ott</a> in the Kortopis. Downstream lie the Duchy of <a href="Morelle" class="internal-link">Morelle</a> and, in the delta around Meadhavn, the Duchy of <a href="Mear" class="internal-link">Mear</a>, while <a href="Zmalig" class="internal-link">Zmalig</a> keeps the northern coast as a theocratic city-state and the Landgraviate of <a href="Weisch" class="internal-link">Weisch</a> holds the shore of the Bloody Strait to the east. In the Highlands the Duchies of <a href="Risfalt" class="internal-link">Risfalt</a> around Praum and <a href="Wargel" class="internal-link">Wargel</a> around Wormill divide the old lands of Medlan between them. The Archduchy of <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a> holds the Trinmo from Giselstatt to the Bend, and the Duchy of <a href="Lesmere" class="internal-link">Lesmere</a> the Kranmoor to its north. Between Meadland and Othirdon, the Halfling Freelands keep their peasant republics in the Lowsteps.</p>
<p>In the north-east, the Weadi cities of the <a href="Blijgrat" class="internal-link">League of Blijgrat</a> stand apart from the Crown. Zeeport on the Bloody Strait is a Free City, claimed by Meadrath as part of Weisch and often counted among the league cities, Weadi in culture and Meadi in politics.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Before the kingdoms, most of Meadland was held by the <a href="Auguri Dominion" class="internal-link">Auguri Dominion</a> from 295BA to 260PA. When the Dominion fell, the petty kingdoms of Korsfall, Morelle and Rulais rose in the Meadi lands and the Kingdom of Gisvale along the Trinmo, and Korsfall slowly swallowed Morelle and Rulais, the later Dirneux. The Weadi lands of <a href="Weadland" class="internal-link">Weadland</a> in the east and north came under the grip of the <a href="Baeling Empire" class="internal-link">Baeling Empire</a> in the 6th century, a grip that was finally broken on the northern coast at Zeeport in 737PA. The long War of Trout and Drake ended in 664PA with the union of Korsfall and Gisvale as the Kingdom of Meadrath, whose Crown has since drawn most of the region into its orbit. For the history of the realm see <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a>, and for the peoples of the land see <a href="Meadi" class="internal-link">Meadi</a>.</p>
