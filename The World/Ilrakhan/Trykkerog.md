<p>Trykkerog is a large island north of the mainland of <a href="Ilrakhan" class="internal-link">Ilrakhan</a>, beyond the Torn Sea. It is linked to the mainland only by the island chain of the Giants Steps and is generally counted as part of the Ilrakhani continent. In the far north its ice runs into Jeturlund, the lands of ice eternal, which are so remote and inhospitable that most outsiders ignore them or count them as part of Trykkerog.</p>
<p>Trykkerog is one of the largest islands in the world and the home of the <a href="Bykmåli" class="internal-link">Bykmåli</a> peoples. It is politically dominated by the kingdoms of <a href="Baeling" class="internal-link">Baeling</a>, <a href="Juelfluk" class="internal-link">Juelfluk</a> and <a href="Vignasoya" class="internal-link">Vignasoya</a>.</p>
