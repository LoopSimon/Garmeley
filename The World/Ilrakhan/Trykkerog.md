<p>Trykkerog is a large island in the north of the world, lying beyond the Torn Sea from the mainland of <a href="Ilrakhan" class="internal-link">Ilrakhan</a>. It is linked to the mainland only by the island chain of the Giants Steps and is generally counted as part of the Ilrakhani continent. It is the home of the <a href="Bykmåli" class="internal-link">Bykmåli</a> peoples, and most of it is divided between the kingdoms of <a href="Baeling" class="internal-link">Baeling</a>, <a href="Juelfluk" class="internal-link">Juelfluk</a> and <a href="Vignasoya" class="internal-link">Vignasoya</a>, beside the free port of <a href="Sallesand" class="internal-link">Sallesand</a>, the city of <a href="Levangr" class="internal-link">Levangr</a>, the Archbishop's city of Tversby, the jarldom of <a href="Rensköldsvik" class="internal-link">Rensköldsvik</a>, the valley of Hvirfill and the <a href="Zren" class="internal-link">Zren</a> of the Vannsi.</p>

<h2>Land and Water</h2>
<p>The spine of the island is Fagnirs Rest, a broad range of high mountains named after the wyrm Fagnir of the <a href="Fjorisjom Saga" class="internal-link">Fjorisjom Saga</a>. It runs from the Stormlands in the west to the Furkenheights in the east and divides the settled south from the frozen north. Beneath its highest peak lies the valley of Hvirfill, where the ancient tower of the Nárauðna stands, and below the valley the great forest of the Uvarumr reaches down towards the coast.</p>
<p>The west is a land of fjords, forests and hills. The Gotjebay cuts deep into its northern shore, and the Onje River runs from Braki past Onvell to Skeppbirk. Between the Tergasywoods, the Swamps of Arel and the Stormlands lies wild country that no crown truly rules. The Wolfsbay, with the isle of Nyllheim in its mouth, opens onto the Gullsong Sea between Vignasoya and Levangr, while the Rensvik Bay lies in the far west on the Bloody Strait, the channel between Trykkerog and Meadland.</p>
<p>The centre of the island is the coast of Melmers Bay, the largest bay of Trykkerog, which looks south across the Torn Sea to the Hagshorn and is named after Saint <a href="Melmer" class="internal-link">Melmer</a>. Its shores hold the oldest and richest towns of the island, among them Alsebyn, Rillberg, Kastock and Troese, and the long peninsula to its west ends at Sallesand, whose waters carry the trade between the Gullsong Sea and the Torn Sea. North of the bay the farmland of Briland rises towards the mountains.</p>
<p>The east is divided by the Malksbay. To its west lie Malksland, the heart of Juelfluk, and the contested farmland of Seldrland on the Egillsbay. To its east a rugged peninsula rises into the Furkenheights and the Horn Valley and ends in the south at Ansvang and in the north at Norgud Point. Beyond the Synbay the Giants Steps, a chain of rocky isles, lead east towards the Waelajokull and <a href="Nostroya" class="internal-link">Nostroya</a>, and the isle of <a href="Mørbrig" class="internal-link">Mørbrig</a>, seat of the oldest circle of mages in the north, lies among them.</p>
<p>North of the mountains lie wide plains of ice. Most of them cover frozen sea, and only in the east does solid land run north past the Kalmes Garden towards the Aelf realm of <a href="Skemfing" class="internal-link">Skemfing</a> in Jeturlund, the lands of ice eternal. Jeturlund is so remote and inhospitable that most outsiders ignore it or count it as part of Trykkerog. In the north-east, below the deep inlet of the Kaldverjod, volcanic heat keeps the rivers, hot springs and marshes of the Zreni Wetlands free of ice.</p>
<h2>Climate and Seas</h2>
<p>Trykkerog has long, cold winters and short summers. Westerly storms and cold blasts from the north sweep through the gaps in its mountains, and in hard winters Melmers Bay, the waters around Juelfluk and the sea towards the Giants Steps freeze over, while the bays of the southern Torn Sea stay open. The Giants Steps close with ice from the Frozen Sea every winter, and Henmars Passage, the sea road east to <a href="Nilborg" class="internal-link">Nilborg</a>, can be sailed for only about three months of the year. Almost every town of note lies on the coast, and the sea has always been the island's main road. The cold seas are also home to the <a href="Unðimaðr" class="internal-link">Unðimaðr</a>, the most feared creature of Bykmåli sea lore.</p>
<h2>Peoples</h2>
<p>Most of the people of Trykkerog are <a href="Bykmåli" class="internal-link">Bykmåli</a>, a name that means "inheritors of the land". They fall into four groups. The Olbellinge of Melmers Bay are the largest and form the core of Baeling. The Jueli live around the Malksbay and the Giants Steps and form the core of Juelfluk. The Skori of the west are the people of Vignasoya and Rensköldsvik, and the Olbellinge of the bay have long called them "wolfsmen", a name for a wild and unruly people. The Hevri, the smallest group, live in isolated communities in the northern wastes and high in Fagnirs Rest. All of them speak one tongue, Bykmål, whose Upper, Lower and Jueli forms are regional dialects, and the Hevri speak whichever dialect is spoken around them.</p>
<p>The Vannsi of the <a href="Zren" class="internal-link">Zren</a> are the largest and most settled Vannsi people of the northern seas, and Hevri and Jueli families have lived among them for centuries. The <a href="Firbolg" class="internal-link">Firbolg</a> live with the Hevri in the valley of Hvirfill. The mountains belong to older and less friendly peoples: <a href="Ogre" class="internal-link">ogres</a>, the giantkin who have begun to gather again in Fagnirs Rest, and their ancient enemies the <a href="Troll" class="internal-link">trolls</a> and <a href="Trog" class="internal-link">trogs</a>. The caverns beneath the mountains are said to hold great numbers of the vättari, as the Bykmåli call the goblins of the north. In the great ports, above all Sallesand, live merchants and sailors from all around the Torn Sea, among them orcs and Qalmyr.</p>
<h2>Realms</h2>
<p>The Kingdom of <a href="Baeling" class="internal-link">Baeling</a> holds almost all of Melmers Bay from its capital at Alsebyn. Its kings claim the name and inheritance of the old <a href="Baeling Empire" class="internal-link">Baeling Empire</a>, and its realm reaches across the bay to the large city of Troese and across the Torn Sea to <a href="Jallbreg" class="internal-link">Jallbreg</a> on the Hagshorn, though Jallbreg now strains for its independence.</p>
<p>The Kingdom of <a href="Juelfluk" class="internal-link">Juelfluk</a>, ruled by House Udry from Juelrup, holds Malksland, the eastern peninsula and the Giants Steps, and its castle at Jedir faces Troese across the contested fields of Seldrland. Its sailors hold the trade with the Zren, with Skemfing and with its own colony of <a href="Nilborg" class="internal-link">Nilborg</a> in northern Nirydia. Baeling and Juelfluk have fought nine <a href="Wars of Baeling Succession" class="internal-link">Wars of Baeling Succession</a> since 684PA.</p>
<p>The Korringe of <a href="Vignasoya" class="internal-link">Vignasoya</a>, the oldest of the three crowns, rules the Wolfsbay and the Onje from Mannelik. Once a strong realm ruled by sword and blood, it is today more of an idea than a state, and its jarls largely go their own way.</p>
<p>Beside the three crowns stand four smaller powers. <a href="Sallesand" class="internal-link">Sallesand</a>, the largest city of the island and one of the three great ports of the Torn Sea, is nominally Baeling but independent in practice and deep in the debt and influence of the <a href="Trazlov Consortium" class="internal-link">Trazlov Consortium</a>. <a href="Levangr" class="internal-link">Levangr</a> on the Wolfsbay broke away from Baeling in 829PA with the help of the <a href="Blijgrat" class="internal-link">Blijgrat League</a>. <a href="Rensköldsvik" class="internal-link">Rensköldsvik</a> is a great jarldom of traders and mercenaries on the Rensvik Bay that has long resisted the pull of Vignasoya. Tversby, the city founded by Saint Melmer on the Egillsbay, is ruled by the Archbishop of Trykkerog as an independent city-state, a status it won as a result of the 6th War of Baeling Succession.</p>
<p>The north is held by peoples who take little part in the quarrels of the south. The valley of Hvirfill has never been conquered, though few have tried and none have succeeded, and its Hevri and Firbolg guard the Nárauðna and the True Stones. The <a href="Zren" class="internal-link">Zren</a> is governed by the Inamid, a council of Vannsi sages, and is known in the southern kingdoms mostly from sailors' tales.</p>
<h2>Faith</h2>
<p>Trykkerog is overwhelmingly Gethric. The conversion of the island began with the six voyages of Saint <a href="Melmer" class="internal-link">Melmer</a> from 372PA, spread slowly from the halls of jarls and kings into the countryside, and was completed under the Baeling Empire. Melmer is the most venerated saint of the Bykmåli, and his trident is kept in Wavewrought Cathedral in Tversby, the city he founded and the seat of the Archbishop of Trykkerog, who also rules it as its temporal lord. The see has long been caught up in the rivalry of Baeling and Juelfluk, and Baeling has more than once proclaimed the Bishop of Kastock head of the Gethric church of Trykkerog in its place.</p>
<p>The Gethric faith is strongest in the coastal towns from Levangr to Juelfluk. In the west and the far north, in the swamps, hills, dales and mountain forests, rural communities still keep the old faith of <a href="Bykmali Paganism" class="internal-link">Bykmali Paganism</a>, which is hard to tell apart from the <a href="Aeldcult" class="internal-link">Aeldcult</a> of Nostroya and venerates gods such as <a href="Otso" class="internal-link">Otso</a>, <a href="Yaalwe" class="internal-link">Yaalwe</a> and <a href="Ingele" class="internal-link">Ingele</a>. Rensköldsvik and some of the towns of Vignasoya are divided between the old faith, the Gethric church and a <a href="Trinicism" class="internal-link">Trinicist</a> minority brought by traders from Blijgrat and Meadrath, and Sallesand also has a small Trinicist community. In Hvirfill the Hevri and Firbolg worship the True Stones, rune stones said to have been made by the Giants in their primordial war with the trolls.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>In the age of the <a href="Giantsrul" class="internal-link">Giantsrul</a> the Giants of the North ruled Trykkerog and Nostroya, and the Nárauðna of Hvirfill is held to be one of their works. They withdrew beyond the Waelajokull and the Heavens Furrow in the 2nd century PA. The <a href="Fjorisjom Saga" class="internal-link">Fjorisjom Saga</a>, set around 201PA, tells of the age that followed, an age of jarls and heroes such as Ylva Feathercall and Toski Ironhair, from whom the royal houses of Baeling and Juelfluk claim descent, and the Skori champion Egill Foambeard.</p>
<p>For much of their early history the Bykmåli were known on the southern coasts above all as raiders, and in 399PA the Archdukes of Morelle founded Meadhavn to guard the mouth of the Mead against them. Melmer's voyages between 372PA and 386PA began the slow conversion of the island, and in 404PA the first Korre of Vignasoya was declared in the west. In 465PA Valund Baeling, Lord of Alsebyn, united the lords of Melmers Bay, and in 517PA his successors proclaimed the <a href="Baeling Empire" class="internal-link">Baeling Empire</a>, a sea empire that came to rule most of Trykkerog, northern Urland, Weadland, Droil and many coastal cities. It conquered much of the coast of Vignasoya, but the Skori jarls resisted from Skeppbirk and never gave up their old gods.</p>
<p>The Empire fell in 590PA. Rensköldsvik made itself independent in the decades that followed, the Skori of Vignasoya held Zeeport and Droil and in 688PA reached the islands off Aisligos, and in 617PA Rossgi I of House Udry founded the Kingdom of Juelfluk on the Malksbay. In the 7th century the short-lived Great Kingdom of Skorland rose in the west and for a time held Rensköldsvik. In 684PA Sivdil the Tall, captain of the Runeblade Mercenary Company, claimed the Baeling name and founded the modern Kingdom of Baeling, which began the long Wars of Baeling Succession with Juelfluk.</p>
<p>The centuries since have seen Baeling lose ground. Zeeport was freed from the Skori in 737PA and Droil in 772PA, the Treeplague broke out on the island in 772PA, and Levangr won its independence in 829PA. Juelfluk chartered its colony of Nilborg in 845PA and has grown rich on the northern passages. The 6th War of Baeling Succession left Tversby an independent city under its Archbishop, and the 9th War of Baeling Succession was fought over Troese from 911PA to 916PA. In 927PA King <a href="Varald I" class="internal-link">Varald I</a> of Baeling faces the secession of Jallbreg, the drift of the free cities and the rising of the ogres of Fagnirs Rest, while Queen <a href="Madeley IV" class="internal-link">Madeley IV</a> of Juelfluk looks to profit from his troubles.</p>
