 <p>Ur is the name of the lands between the <a href="Kortopis" class="internal-link">Kortopi</a> and <a href="Trollbacks" class="internal-link">Trollback</a> mountains, a region of roughly 3.2 million square kilometres, dominated by the rivers Trinmo and Melm and stretching from the Bay of Uhl in the north to the Lands of Rean and the Stormy Sea in the south. It is often called Urland as well, after the great Silver Age kingdom that ruled most of it under a single banner, and its people are the <a href="Urien" class="internal-link">Urien</a>, whose name comes from the Tribe of the Urna, the river people of the west. The lands are hilly, rich and old, and they have been divided and united many times. In 927PA Ur is split between eight nations and deeply occupied with the <a href="Garmish Succession Crisis" class="internal-link">Garmish Succession Crisis</a>.</p> <div class="wiki-infobox" style="overflow: hidden; display: flex; flex-direction: column;"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Maps/UR.png" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;"> <small><i>Ur, 927PA</i></small> </div> <h2>The Peoples of Ur</h2> <p>When the Rakhi first came to Ur they found five tribal groups. The Garm lived in the south around Lake Forr, and the Ares to the west and north of them around the hills of the Lock. The Gim held the lands of modern Gelmsburg and north of the Eld Valley, the Urna the country west of the Trinmo, and the Turv the northern coast of the Torn Sea and the Bend of the Trinmo. The Turv gave their name to <a href="Turvik Paganism" class="internal-link">Turvik Paganism</a>, the old faith that could be found in many shapes across eastern Meadland, Vihr and Ur.</p> <p>Their descendants are the <a href="Urien" class="internal-link">Urien</a> of today. The Garmish of <a href="Garmeley" class="internal-link">Garmeley</a> are the most distinct of them, the Ares make up most of <a href="Urbridge" class="internal-link">Urbridge</a> and <a href="Eldmar" class="internal-link">Eldmar</a>, and the Gim are found in <a href="Gelmsburg" class="internal-link">Gelmsburg</a>. The Turvi live around the Bay of Uhl and along the Bend. The Urna have lost their country to the hobgoblins of <a href="Ak-Saray" class="internal-link">Ak-Saray</a>. The Kerleans of the south, who once had a kingdom of their own, were absorbed over the centuries by the Ares, Urna and Garmish, and the Niedmen of <a href="Trinvale" class="internal-link">Trinvale</a> have taken their place in the Nied Valley. True Kerlean traditions are kept only in the Kerlaen Wilderness. Urien culture is known for its chivalry, its arts and a strong sense of independence, and the stereotypes between its peoples are strong, from the backwater Ares and Kerleans to the humourless Turvi and the free-spirited, godless Garmish.</p> <h2>Geography</h2> <p>Two mountain ranges frame Ur. The <a href="Kortopis" class="internal-link">Kortopis</a> in the west are the mountains the hobgoblins came from, and their high valleys are still troubled by ogres. The <a href="Trollbacks" class="internal-link">Trollbacks</a> in the east stand between Ur and the Kingdom of <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Vihr</a>, with the dwarven hold of <a href="Xer-Kol" class="internal-link">Xer-Kol</a> within them. The Trinmo, the largest river in the known world, comes down from the west past Niedrest and Calmbrook and crosses the south by Lake Forr on its way to the Stormy Sea, and it is the great trade road of the country. The Melm comes down from the northern highlands to Lake Forr. Between the rivers lie the Frogleaf Forest in the north, the Eld Valley and the Ares Highlands in the middle, the hills of the Lock, the rich Garmian Flatlands and the great Gladewood in the south. South of Lake Eresh the swamps and hills of the Kerlaen Wilderness, which no nation holds, run into the Windy Rocks and the <a href="Wasteland of Rakh" class="internal-link">Wasteland of Rakh</a>.</p> <p>On the Bend of the Trinmo in the north, <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a>, an archduchy of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a>, holds the Bendburgs, among them Turv, which it won from Gelmsburg in the <a href="War of 896" class="internal-link">War of 896</a>. In the south the flatlands run down to the Vale of Rean and the Princedom of <a href="Hainach" class="internal-link">Hainach</a>.</p> <h2>History</h2> <p>The <a href="Rakh" class="internal-link">Rakhi Empire</a> held the tribes of Garm and Ares from 310BA, and for more than three centuries its roads and officials made the south a core province of the empire. When the empire collapsed the surviving Rakhi gathered at Gibaresh, today's Calmbrook, as New Rakh, and they were gone from Ur by 28PA. The Urien raised their own realms in their place: <a href="Kerlaes" class="internal-link">Kerlaes</a> in 27PA, <a href="Urland" class="internal-link">Urland</a> and <a href="Arester" class="internal-link">Arester</a> in 29PA, and Garmeley under its Riverkings of Garm in 51PA. They were the four great realms of the Silver Age. <a href="Gethricism" class="internal-link">Gethricism</a> came from Vihr, became the state religion of Urland in 306PA and slowly pushed out the old Turvik faith, though Kerlaes held to it until the end and the Garmish never took the Gethric faith as a state church.</p> <p>Kerlaes was destroyed in 327PA, betrayed by its ally Arester and conquered by Urland, and its lands became the Duchy of Nied. Arester fell to Urland in turn in the Unification Wars of 454PA. The Golden Age of Urland followed under the Dunling kings, and after the last of them died in 494PA the Rassel kings reigned from Dursan until the end. For more than two centuries Urland held the north and centre of Ur, and the Archbishop of Ur sat at Dursan. Only Garmeley stayed outside it, first as a kingdom of the Riverkings and from 648PA as a duchy.</p> <p>Urland ended in six years. In the winter of 690PA the hobgoblins of the Kortopis came out of the mountains under their warlord Tirbo and swept over the west, and by the time the Battle of Gobsfall Keep stopped them at the Trinmo in 696PA everything west of the river was lost, with the capital Dursan, which the conquerors renamed Hobgiliath, the royal line and the Archbishop's seat. The <a href="Hobgoblin Invasion" class="internal-link">Hobgoblin Invasion</a> left the federation of Ak-Saray in the west, and in 703PA the east broke into successor states. Jorwyle of Gelm crowned himself King in Gelmsburg and claimed the crown of Urland, which nobody acknowledged, and the lords of the Eld Valley refused him and crowned Theara I of Hornsley in Eldmar. The two kingdoms fought the <a href="90 Days War" class="internal-link">90 Days War</a> in 713PA, and the quarrel between them has run through Ur ever since. The Order of Urbridge was refounded from the old Knights of the Gryphon in 736PA, Remillion made the Trifflands free in 766PA, and the cities of Nied founded the Trinvale Republics in 775PA. Uhl, which accepted Vihr as its overlord in 735PA, won its freedom back in 866PA.</p> <p>Most of what followed was fought among the heirs of Urland. In 841PA the <a href="League of the Gryphon" class="internal-link">League of the Gryphon</a> was sworn at Pearfawl to take back the west, and in the two <a href="Wars of the Gryphon" class="internal-link">Wars of the Gryphon</a>, from 841 to 843PA and from 883 to 888PA, it won back the east bank of the Trinmo and a wedge of the river country as far as Merbridge, but it never crossed the river in force. The Dance of Husks struck central and southern Ur hard in 854PA, and in 896PA Gelmsburg lost Turv to Gisvale.</p> <h2>The Nations of Ur</h2> <p>Eight nations hold Ur in 927PA.</p> <table> <thead> <tr><th>Nation</th><th>Government</th><th>Head of State</th><th>Capital</th><th>Founded</th></tr> </thead> <tbody> <tr><td><a href="Gelmsburg" class="internal-link">Kingdom of Gelmsburg</a></td><td>Feudal Monarchy</td><td>King Klovis I</td><td>Gelmsburg</td><td>703PA</td></tr> <tr><td><a href="Eldmar" class="internal-link">Kingdom of Eldmar</a></td><td>Feudal Monarchy</td><td>Queen Moira III</td><td>Eldmar</td><td>703PA</td></tr> <tr><td><a href="Urbridge" class="internal-link">Order of Urbridge</a></td><td>Hereditary Stratocracy</td><td>Lord-Protector Gorlan V</td><td>Urbridge</td><td>736PA</td></tr> <tr><td><a href="Trinvale" class="internal-link">Trinvale Republics</a></td><td>Oligarchic Republic</td><td>Citykeeper Rimond von Junkel</td><td>Niedrest</td><td>775PA</td></tr> <tr><td><a href="Trifflands" class="internal-link">Free Lands of Triff</a></td><td>Stratocratic Council</td><td>Council of Triff</td><td>Ralt</td><td>766PA</td></tr> <tr><td><a href="Ak-Saray" class="internal-link">Federation of Ak-Saray</a></td><td>Stratocratic Federation</td><td>Boss Tenzea (no Great Boss)</td><td>Hobgiliath (unofficial)</td><td>696PA</td></tr> <tr><td><a href="Uhl" class="internal-link">Free City of Uhl</a></td><td>Merchant Republic</td><td>Count Benedict Beywill</td><td>Uhl</td><td>866PA (free city)</td></tr> <tr><td><a href="Garmeley" class="internal-link">Duchy of Garmeley</a></td><td>Feudal Duchy</td><td>Vacant, see the <a href="Garmish Succession Crisis" class="internal-link">Crisis</a></td><td>Redflow</td><td>51PA (duchy since 648PA)</td></tr> </tbody> </table> <p>Gelmsburg, Eldmar and Urbridge are the three great Urien successor states of the east and centre. The Archbishop of Ur, whose seat was at Dursan until the Invasion, sits in Gelmsburg under the High-Bisop of <a href="Iztaw" class="internal-link">Iztaw</a>, and the present Archbishop is Gadwin V. <a href="Trinicism" class="internal-link">Trinicism</a>, which split from the Gethric Church in the Schism of 764PA, has a following in Trinvale and on the Bend. The Turvik gods survive only as a rare rural cult. Only in central and southern Garmeley do the <a href="Kindly Lords" class="internal-link">Kindly Lords</a>, the spirits of river, glade and hill, still hold the people, and the divide between the Gethric north and the spirit-worshipping south lies at the root of the Succession Crisis.</p> <h2>Ur in 927PA</h2> <p>Ur in 927PA is occupied by the Garmish Succession Crisis. Duchess <a href="Agnes I" class="internal-link">Agnes I</a> of Garmeley died childless in 922PA, and the Gethric north behind <a href="Coster of Melmbirth" class="internal-link">Coster of Melmbirth</a> and the spirit-worshipping south behind <a href="Hynek of Forrington" class="internal-link">Hynek of Forrington</a>, who was killed outside Redflow in May of 927PA, have gone to war. Eldmar and Urbridge back the north, and so does Prince Jakub of Vihr with his order of knights, while Hainach and the Trinvale Republics back the south. Gelmsburg is largely undecided, watching Eldmar and the old quarrel over Turv. Uhl and the Free Lands of Triff have taken no side.</p> <p>In the west the League of the Gryphon is quiet. Gorlan V of Urbridge has twice called for a Third War against Ak-Saray, and both calls failed against the rivalry of Gelmsburg and Eldmar. Ak-Saray has no Great Boss, and the League's planners still have no answer for <a href="Zurgush" class="internal-link">Zurgush</a>, the Wyrdspeaker who stopped the Second War, while the Crisis draws the League's members south.</p>
