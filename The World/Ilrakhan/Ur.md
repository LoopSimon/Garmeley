<p>Ur is the most commonly name for the lands between the Kortopi and Trollback mountains, dominated by the rivers Trinmo and Melm and stretching all the way from the Bay of Uhl in the north to the Lands of Rean and even the Stormy Sea in the south. It is often also simply referred to as "Urland" in reference to the large silver age kingdom that used rule almost all of Ur under a single banner. The ancient most history of Ur goes back to contacts with rakhi traders and later conquerors who found the region dominated by five distinct tribal cultural groups, the Tribes of Garm in the south around Lake Forr, the Tribe of Ares to the west and north of the Garm around the Lock hills, the Tribe of Gim in modern day Gelmsburg and north of the Eld Valley, the Tribe of Urna west of the Trinmo in the lands now lost to the Hobgoblins of Ak-Saray and the Tribe of Turv at the northern coast of the Torn Sea and the Trinmos Bend, after whom the broad pagan structures of Turvik Paganism are named, that could be found in various shapes and forms around easter Meadland, Vihr and Ur. </p>
<p>The hilly, rich and vast lands of Ur have been separated and united many times in their history, beginning with the slow formation of various early feudal structures like <a href="Arester" class="internal-link">Arester</a>, <a href="Urland" class="internal-link">Urland</a> and <a href="Kerlaes" class="internal-link">Kerlaes</a> in the first centuries following the collapse of the Rakhi Empire, that had controlled southern Ur for hundreds of years and dominated but also organized and advanced the local tribes; to the slow unification of Urland through betrayal and conquest and the century long reign over the lands of Ur, to its eventual downfall in the 7th century when Hobgoblin warriors spilled out of the Kortopis and swept over western Ur like a swarm of locusts; until we arrive at the precarious political situation of the day where powerful nations like <a href="Uhl" class="internal-link">Uhl</a>, <a href="Gelmsburg" class="internal-link">Gelmsburg</a>, <a href="Ak-Saray" class="internal-link">Ak-Saray</a>, <a href="Trinvale" class="internal-link">Trinvale</a>, <a href="Eldmar" class="internal-link">Eldmar</a> and <a href="Garmeley" class="internal-link">Garmeley</a> all contend to become the strongest player in the region. As of 927PA, Ur is deeply occupied with the <a href="Garmish Succession Crisis" class="internal-link">garmish civil war</a>, where the largest of the urien nations had broken apart in infighting and many of its neighbours picked a side to tear open old scars and profit of the slaughter of brothers.</p>
