<p>Ilrakhan is one of the three major continents of the <a href="Inara" class="internal-link">Inaran</a> continental area. It borders Nirydia at the <a href="Mistspires" class="internal-link">Mistspires</a> in the east and Behera at the <a href="Cloudscratchers" class="internal-link">Cloudscratchers</a> in the south.</p>
<p>The name Ilrakhan, literally "of Rakh", stems from the ancient <a href="Rakh" class="internal-link">Rakhian</a> civilisation that dominated western Ilrakhan in antiquity.</p>
<p>The large island of <a href="Trykkerog" class="internal-link">Trykkerog</a> is traditionally counted as part of Ilrakhan.</p>
<p>The largest mountain range of Inara is the <a href="Kortopis" class="internal-link">Kortopis</a>, and its largest river is the Trinmo.</p>
