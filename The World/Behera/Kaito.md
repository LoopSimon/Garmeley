<p>The Subcontinent of Kaito is located on <a href="Behera" class="internal-link">Behera</a>, south of the Beheran Desert itself and east of the Smaragd Sea. It is home to the massive Kaito Jungle and the Xermesis mountains. A  large amount of the <a href="Safir" class="internal-link">Safiri population</a> lives in western Kaito where they have build cities and empires for thousands of years, while the yppori Humans call the eastern and southern parts of Kaito their home. The Islands of the Purple Sea are usually considered part of Kaito as well. Eastern Kaito, home to the <a href="Yppori" class="internal-link">Yppori</a>, is usually referred to as <a href="Tal" class="internal-link">Tal</a>, in the language its inhabitants, the Talang, it simply means "Our Ground".</p>
<p>Alongside the various Safiri races, Kaito is home to houndreds of unique species, among them the mysterious, godlike beings known as the <a href="Yari" class="internal-link">Yari</a>, the <a href="Hatara" class="internal-link">Hatara</a> of the Xermexis and the <a href="Yppo" class="internal-link">Yppo</a>, as well as the <a href="Elik" class="internal-link">Elik</a> of the deep jungle and the <a href="Mipaz" class="internal-link">Mipaz</a> of the Purple Sea reefs.</p>
