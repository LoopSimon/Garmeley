<p>The large Island between <a href="Behera" class="internal-link">Behera</a> and <a href="Nirydia" class="internal-link">Nirydia</a>, is traditionally counted as part of the Beheran Subcontinent due to its centuries-long cultural and political control by the elves of the <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae</a> Sultanate. Il-Haena is an elvish name, translating roughly to "Land of the Ibex", due to the common occurrence of the species of goat on the island. Prior to the elves settling the island, its primary residents were the <a href="Orc" class="internal-link">Orcs</a>, or Qari, that called the Island simply "Qa", or "home".</p>
<p>The history of Il Haena is in many ways the history of the orcs and elves, as the natives were forced to adapt to the stronger and dominant elves settling, conquering and dominating the peace and sea loving orcish natives. Some like the <a href="Qarzi" class="internal-link">Qarzi</a> adapted and cooperated with their new overlords, many others fled, causing the great orcish diaspora.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Elven settlement of the eastern shores began in 278BA and was, for most of the following seven centuries, a slow process of demographic and institutional pressure rather than war. It ended in the <a href="Wars of Qahori Subjugation" class="internal-link">Wars of Qahori Subjugation</a> of 413 - 457PA, which the Orcs call the Destruction of Qa and which left the whole island part of the Sultanate. Those who did not flee were the Qarzi, who were granted autonomous rule in the south-east, and the many Orcs who had submitted and stayed on their land.</p>
<h2>Geography</h2>
<p>At the heart of the island are the Maqari Mountains, which the Orcs hold to be the closest place on earth to Qastor himself. The holy city of Ensihr, the closest settlement to their sacred summit, was left standing but never rebuilt after its fall. Great Qatir, once the largest Qahori city ever built, lies in ruins on the north coast, and the old cities of Timoq, Mahraq and Zeqer are scattered across the north and the east. The Qarzi hold the south-east, with their capital of Benwaral and the coastal trade hub of Kahtis. North of the island lies the Qahori Archipelago.</p>
<h2>People</h2>
<p>Il-Haena is home to about 70% Orcs, 20% Elves, 5% humans and 5% Bihra. The Sultanate's Dawn Army is stationed on the island and the Elves hold its administration, but the great majority of the people are Orcs. Most live pleasant lives under the Sultanate's rule, and the conquest happened hundreds of years ago, so there is simply no desire for religious revolt. The Orcs of the island who are not Qarzi follow the First Path of <a href="Qalmyr" class="internal-link">Qalmyr</a> as the Quiet, and many of them the <a href="Path of the Third Moon" class="internal-link">Path of the Third Moon</a>.</p>
<p>Especially among the youth there are insurgent and terrorist groups, who dream of the return of Qa and are largely unsuccessful. They are in conflict among themselves, and their own people largely do not support them: embracing violence against a non-violent opponent is against all that the Qalmyr stands for.</p>
<h2>Fauna</h2>
<p>The island is the home of the ibex that gave it its name, and of the <a href="Igulan" class="internal-link">Igulan</a> and the <a href="Ossur" class="internal-link">Ossur</a>, two animals native to Il-Haena whose bones are carved, built with and worn by the Orcs. See <a href="Orc" class="internal-link">Bone-Craft</a>. The small <a href="Krelling" class="internal-link">Krelling</a> is also believed to have originated on the island.</p>
