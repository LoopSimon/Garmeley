[[Behera]]
%%
<p>The large Island between <a href="Behera" class="internal-link">Behera</a> and <a href="Nirydia" class="internal-link">Nirydia</a>, is traditionally counted as part of the Beheran Subcontinent due to its century long cultural and political control by the elves of the <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae</a> Sultanate. Il-Haena is an elvish name, translating roughly to "Land of the Ibex", due to the common occurrence of the species of goat on the island. Prior to the elves settling the island, its primary residents were the <a href="Orc" class="internal-link">Orcs</a>, or Qalmyr, that called the Island simply "Qa", or "home".</p>
<p>The history of Il Haena is in many ways the history of the orcs and elves, as the natives were forced to adapt to the stronger and dominant elves settling, conquering and dominating the peace and sea loving orcish natives, some like the <a href="Qarzi" class="internal-link">Qarzi</a> adapted and cooperated with their new overlords, many others flew causing the great orcish diaspora.</p>
