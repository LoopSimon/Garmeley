<p>Tal is the eastern part of the subcontinent of <a href="Kaito" class="internal-link">Kaito</a>, the land between the eastern slopes of the Xermesis mountains and the shores of the Purple Sea, together with the islands of that sea. In Talang, the language of the people who raised its first great cities and gave the country its name, the name simply means "Our Ground". Tal is the home of the <a href="Yppori" class="internal-link">Yppori</a>, the dark skinned humans who take their name from the <a href="Yppo" class="internal-link">Yppo</a>, and the heartland of the faith of <a href="Sochor" class="internal-link">Sochor</a>, the Ashen God of Judgement and Chance. Foreigners often speak of Tal and the Purple Sea as one place, and the Yppori seldom correct them, for the sea has always been the road that joins their lands.</p> <div class="grid-item" style="overflow: hidden; display: flex; flex-direction: column;"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Maps/Tal.jpg" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;"> </div> <small><i>A map of Tal and the Purple Sea, dated 927PA</i></small>  <p>In the west of Tal the Xermesis rise out of the jungle, a long wall of peaks whose heights are the home of the <a href="Hatara" class="internal-link">Hatara</a>. East of them the land falls away into the vast jungle of Kaito, a country of rivers, warm rains and forested hills that reaches the coast in a series of bays and low peninsulas. In the north the Kaitan Gulf cuts deep between the mainland and the lands of the <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae Sultanate</a>, and in the south the Sheregal Sea washes the jungle coast below Byamon. Beyond the mainland lies the Purple Sea, a scatter of islands and straits. The Isles of Naigan hold the old imperial city of Kiamar, the mountainous island of <a href="Hanshi" class="internal-link">Hanshi</a> lies in the east across the Ashen Strait, and the lonely Aoke Islands far to the south are said to be the home of the seven headed Kuruchi, a <a href="Dragon" class="internal-link">dragon</a> of the waters.</p> <p>Elephants roam the jungles and river valleys of the mainland, and the deep forest hides the <a href="Elik" class="internal-link">Elik</a>, a winged ambusher of the canopy. The great running <a href="Yppo" class="internal-link">Yppo</a> is found from the jungle floor to the steppes, and the reefs of the Purple Sea are the home of the <a href="Mipaz" class="internal-link">Mipaz</a>, a magical creature that the Rytoku revere and that the <a href="Krestasi" class="internal-link">Krestasi</a> call the Red Death, for the waters around Tal are closed to many of them. The Boneshell, a shellfish of the Purple Sea whose white shell is the emblem of Chezin, yields a purple dye that is prized in Tal and far beyond it, and it is from this dye that the sea takes its name.</p> <h2>Peoples and Faith</h2> <p>The great majority of the people of Tal are Yppori, a family of peoples of which the Talang of the south east, the Al Ypp of the western jungles, the Anpon of the north and the Rytoku of the islands are the greatest, and almost all of them follow Sochor. The great temple of the faith, the <a href="Great Kiln" class="internal-link">Great Kiln</a>, stands in the jungle of <a href="Byamon" class="internal-link">Byamon</a>, upstream of Fiyang on the river Fohang, and from there the Acolytes and wandering Judges of the <a href="Ashcult" class="internal-link">Ashcult</a> travel the world. Its head is the Osafar of Sochor, today <a href="Hien-Ro" class="internal-link">Hien-Ro</a>, who is held to be reborn and who has declared the New World of <a href="Sirenna" class="internal-link">Sirenna</a> the destined paradise of the children of Sochor. Older than Sochor is the faith of the Sunrider, <a href="Chenism" class="internal-link">Chenism</a>, whose god the priests of Sochor took into their own myth. Only one great city of Tal does not share the faith. <a href="Koshtir" class="internal-link">Koshtir</a>, on the Kaitan Gulf, is a city of Mirani <a href="Safir" class="internal-link">Safir</a> who follow the <a href="Singer Worship" class="internal-link">Faith of the Singer</a> under the Great Sage <a href="Hilcarax" class="internal-link">Hilcarax</a>.</p> <p>West of the Xermesis lie the Safiri cities and realms of the Smaragd Sea, above all the great Empire of <a href="Pravazik" class="internal-link">Pravazik</a>. The empire of Naigan once conquered the Safiri cities of the western Antulun Bay, and the memory of that conquest has not faded on either side. Many Al Ypp live in the jungles beyond the Xermesis under the rule of the Safiri states. Beneath the jungles of Tal and the floor of the Purple Sea lie the Fire Cities of <a href="Tur Filash" class="internal-link">Tur Filash</a>, a realm of drow who first made contact with the Yppori in 514PA and who keep a wary eye on the Krestasi of the deep ocean.</p> <h2>Learning and Magic</h2> <p>The Yppori have studied the <a href="Elemental" class="internal-link">elements</a> like no other people, and above all the moments in which matter passes from one state into another, a fascination that in time gave rise to the faith of Sochor. In the 2nd century PA <a href="Thandar" class="internal-link">Ung Thandar</a>, the Athaw, set out the Circle of Thandar and founded the Arts of Change, and centuries later <a href="Szuan" class="internal-link">Tihir Szuan</a> added the <a href="Myan-Bond" class="internal-link">Myan-Bond</a> to it. Together they still form the core of the arcane canon taught across the Purple Sea, though the scholars of the north have long dismissed it.</p> <h2>The States of Tal</h2> <p>Six states inherited the lands of the Purple Empire of <a href="Naigan" class="internal-link">Naigan</a>, and most of them look back to it as the age of Yppori greatness. <a href="Byamon" class="internal-link">Byamon</a> is an absolute monarchy under King Thuriya Min, with its capital at Hytzen on the southern coast, in the jungle homeland of the Al Ypp, a land of Yppo Riders and of the <a href="Great Kiln" class="internal-link">Great Kiln</a>, whose rulers hold the Ivory Crown of the old Dynasties. <a href="Soren Vin" class="internal-link">Soren Vin</a> is an absolute monarchy of the Talang in the south east, ruled from Natein by King Molopgar V, and its banner shows the Golden Elephant. Between the two kingdoms, on the river Fohang, lies Fiyang, once the capital of the Golden Empire and now a faded great city under the rule of Soren Vin. Byamon and Soren Vin were allies in the Pact of Water and are rivals today.</p> <p><a href="Rikarma" class="internal-link">Rikarma</a> is a feudal kingdom of the Anpon in the north eastern highlands, ruled from Pienun by King Quang Hùng, and carries the Head of Sochor as its emblem. <a href="Chezin" class="internal-link">Chezin</a> is a merchant republic of the Anpon on the north eastern coast, governed by a council of trading houses under the First Merchant, Datu Mallari, with the Boneshell on its arms. Chezin and Rikarma quarrel over tolls but have stood together against Hanshi. Koshtir is the Safiri city described above, a theocracy under the Great Sage Hilcarax. <a href="Hanshi" class="internal-link">Hanshi</a> is the Divine Queendom of the great island of Ryto and the islands around it, a land of the Rytoku ruled by the Divine Queen Teruna from Gyedo and the Pearl Throne, and the leading power of the Purple Sea. Its wealth rests on the trade in Astafar, and its ships are the farthest travelled in Tal.</p> <h2>Trade</h2> <p>Tal lies at the meeting of three trading worlds. To the north the Elbae Sultanate reaches the Kaitan Gulf by sea, and its <a href="Grand Ziflis Company" class="internal-link">Grand Ziflis Company</a> trades at Koshtir and the other ports of the Purple Sea. To the west the Safiri realms of the Smaragd Sea trade with Tal by the roads around the Xermesis and by the coasts, and to the south the New World of Sirenna has begun to send imports of its own, which the Company finds very valuable.</p> <p>The goods of Tal are known across the world, among them the purple dye of the Boneshell, cinnamon from the islands, nutmeg from the plantations of Myrnifer in the north of the Purple Sea, cloves from the coasts of Kaito, teak and lacquerware. Most of them travel the routes described under <a href="Trade" class="internal-link">Trade</a>. The most notorious export is <a href="Astafar" class="internal-link">Astafar</a>, a drug made from the dried latex of the Asta poppies, which grow only on the Fitta Islands near Hanshi. It is extremely common in the Sultanate and in Kaito, and its income is a substantial reason for the dominance of Hanshi in the region.</p> <h2>History</h2> <p>The earliest records of Tal are those of the <a href="Dynasties of Tal" class="internal-link">Dynasties of Tal</a>. In 1099BA <a href="Chagor" class="internal-link">Chagor</a> claimed the Ivory Crown of Tal at the city of Char and founded the Chagor Dynasty. Some five centuries later, in 623BA, <a href="Melop" class="internal-link">Melop</a> rose in the south of Tal and claimed the Ivory Crown of the Chagor, but his dynasty lasted scarcely more than a century. In 503BA <a href="Muyra" class="internal-link">Muyra</a> defeated the forces of the Melop and was raised as Lord of Lords, and the Muyra Dynasty that he founded ruled until its fall in 229BA, when the Ivory Crown was sealed in a great vault. The <a href="Era of Thunders" class="internal-link">Era of Thunders</a> followed, centuries of warlords and small kings in a splintered Tal, and out of the strife rose the <a href="Kingdom of Rin" class="internal-link">Kingdom of Rin</a>, a relatively strong power of the age.</p> <p>The faith that ended the strife was born in it. According to its own tradition Sochor was born as a man in 253PA and ascended as a god in 275PA, whereupon his followers began the Holy Work. In 281PA the <a href="Ashcult" class="internal-link">Ashcult</a> recognised the three year old Chusfun as the reincarnation of Sochor, declared him Ngalas and raised him to the office of Osafar, and in 283PA <a href="Laizin" class="internal-link">Laizin</a>, King of Rin, converted and was proclaimed Guardian of the Ngalas, which founded the <a href="Golden Empire" class="internal-link">Golden Empire</a>. In 294PA its armies defeated the warlord Tien Jijichan and ended the Era of Thunders.</p> <p>The Golden Empire ruled Tal from Fiyang on the southern coast until 532PA. Its provinces had already begun to go their own ways, and in 492PA Lord <a href="Aung Shant" class="internal-link">Aung Shant</a> of Naigan, who no longer recognised the Golden Rule, declared himself King. The power of Naigan lay in the might of its channelers, who commanded elemental forces and even bound elementals as tireless forces of war, and in 532PA its armies took Fiyang and the last Golden Emperor was killed. In the same war the ancient city of Char was razed on purpose, so that the power of Tal would pass to the imperial island and its capital, Kiamar. Naigan became the greatest Yppori realm in recorded history and conquered the entire Purple Sea and eastern Kaito, and from 658PA, when <a href="Bayratha" class="internal-link">Bayratha</a> was declared Divine Emperor of the Purple Sea, it was also called the Empire of the Four, after the four peoples of Tal that it united, the Talang, the Al Ypp, the Anpon and the Rytoku. It conquered the Safiri cities of the western Antulun Bay, and in 681PA it took the city of Gyedo, sacked it and killed the Channeler Tihir Szuan. Only Koshtir was never taken: when Naigan tried to conquer it the Great Sage bent the mist itself against the attackers, and the Sage agreed in the end only to hail Naigan as the rulers of the Purple Sea.</p> <p>Plague and strife followed that conquest, and the imperial city of Kiamar sealed itself away for a decade. In that time the warlords whom the Emperor himself had raised as peacekeepers turned on him, and in 771PA <a href="Piri Cao" class="internal-link">Piri Cao</a>, "the Hungering Wave", sacked the Imperial Palace and forced the last Emperor, <a href="Belen Yun" class="internal-link">Belen Yun</a>, to abdicate. Kiamar has been looted many times since, and the island of Naigan, still often called the Imperial City, is now the home of a sorcerer known as <a href="Jinùk" class="internal-link">Jinùk</a>, who bows to no land. The <a href="Period of the Bleeding Heron" class="internal-link">Period of the Bleeding Heron</a> that followed broke Tal into the states of today. In the south the wars were won by the Pact of Water, an alliance of the warlord <a href="Molopgar I" class="internal-link">Molopgar I</a> of Soren Vin and the earlier rulers of Byamon, though the two kingdoms are rivals now, and the Period is held to have ended with the Peace of Chezin in 849PA, when Koshtir, Chezin and Rikarma made peace with their enemy Hanshi. The war had been fought largely over the Ariho, the stretch of land between Bangoy and Chezin. The royal army of Hanshi was beaten there and thrown into the sea, and Chezin, which kept the Ariho, gave up all its claims to the islands east of Chezin Island.</p> <p>The latest chapter was written by the sea. In 923PA Captain Armida Zheni sailed the Cylla from Gyedo, and in 924PA she found the continent of Sirenna, which the Pearl Throne of Hanshi proclaimed royal territory (see <a href="The Discovery of Sirenna" class="internal-link">The Discovery of Sirenna</a>). The Grand Ziflis Company has found its trade with the Yppori more profitable since, but also more tense, as the colonial ambitions of the Sultanate in the New World gather pace.</p>
