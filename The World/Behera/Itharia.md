<p>Itharia is the large Island on the north-western coast of Behera, south of the smoking Sea and infamous home to the now fallen Cyclopi Civilisation of Cyraclion that mastered the sciences and unravelled the mysteries of the world in antiquity. Many consider the shores and lands of Itharia cursed due their fearsome inhabitants, mysterious ruins and the treacherous Strait of Ithar itself, running between the island and the continent, with its signature crimson cliffs. </p>
<p>Although dangerous, the sea route through the Strait is commercially extremely viable and has been the subject to many attempts by various seafarers, first successfully completed by the Orc Komukha Naman, Captain of the Lyras Kiss, in 806PA. Trade between the ports of Cevolia and Sertova in Ilrakhan and the unliving inhabitants of Bilsworth in Dandry or the warlike and proud vahri of the Bay of Geol is extremely profitable and lucrative, but the real golden promise is the direct connection via sea lane to the ports of the Smaragd sea and Kaito in southern Behera, circumnavigating the continent and avoiding the elven ports thats have controlled the trade from the south with Ilrakhan for millenia.</p>
<p>Since Namans Voyage, many have attempted and completed the perilous journey along the western coast of Itharia, some even successfully venturing through the Strait of Ithar itself. In 839PA, a heavily armed contingent of settlers and sailors set out and constructed the fortified port of Casimiro, named after Saint Kasimir that had perished on a mission to the godless island centuries earlier. Casimiro would become an important port in facilitating further travel and trade between western Ilrakhan and Behera in the future.</p>
