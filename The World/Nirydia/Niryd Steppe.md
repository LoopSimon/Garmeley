<p>The Niryd Steppe, Laham in the old Lihbare tongue of the <a href="Bihra" class="internal-link">Bihra</a>, also called the "Kobold Steppe" or the "Bihra Steppe", is a vast grassland in the south-west of <a href="Nirydia" class="internal-link">Nirydia</a>, lying mostly within a temperate climate. It stretches from the lands of Lake Kher and the frontier of <a href="Kherova" class="internal-link">Kherova</a> in the west to the Gorati Highlands, the Kolrog Highlands and the Limsul Peaks in the east, and from the <a href="Red Iron Towers" class="internal-link">Red Iron Towers</a> of the <a href="Twintail Imperium" class="internal-link">Twintail Imperium</a> in the north to the Forbidden Lands, the <a href="Blodrise Dark" class="internal-link">Blodrise Dark</a> and the Roxerian border at Fort Mun in the south. It is the ancestral homeland of the Steppe Bihra and of many native species, among them the Gnarlskin Salamanders and the Gleeflies, which have all adapted to its harsh flora.</p>  
<p>For most of recorded history the steppe has been ruled by the Khaganates of the Steppe Bihra, and at the height of the <a href="Great Khaganates of the Steppe" class="internal-link">Great Khaganates</a> their Khans held not only the whole steppe but vast lands beyond it, directly or through tributaries. Since the fall of the last Great Khan at Adsal in 794PA it has been divided between rival Khaganates, of which four are counted as great in the modern day.</p>  
<h2>Geography</h2>  
<p>The grass of the steppe is dominated by the Aspira, a name roughly translated as "Bladegrass". Aspira grows to a height of roughly 3 metres and its blades carry edges sharp enough to cut flesh to pieces. Coupled with the strong winds of the steppe this makes the central regions essentially uninhabitable for any species that has not naturally adapted to it, and outsiders rarely cross the heart of the steppe without Bihra guides or the backs of their salamanders.</p>  
<p>The steppe is crossed by the Agav River and its tributaries, which gather the waters of the central plains and carry them south. Along the Agav lie the river cities, large farming communities that grow rice on the flooded banks and pay tribute to the Khans. In the northern steppe lie the Shavai Lakes, the largest lakes of the grassland, whose shores hold some of the greatest homesteads of the steppe. In the north-east, near the line of the Red Iron Towers, lie the Amethyst Caverns, a complex of mineral caves on the steppe margin, and in the north-west the grass rises towards Mount Ithel and the foothills of the Mistspires, where the dwarves of <a href="Tizin-Kol" class="internal-link">Tizin-Kol</a> have dammed the waters at Zian Station.</p>  
<p>The south-western reaches are known to the <a href="Kheri" class="internal-link">Kheri</a> as the Forbidden Lands and are sometimes counted as part of the steppe and sometimes not. Their grass is milder and the land rich and green, but every Kheri city and fort ever raised there has fallen to the bands and Khaganates of the Steppe Bihra. Gases rise from beneath the ground in many parts of the Forbidden Lands, and the people who live there have taken their name from the colour these have given their skin.</p>  
<p>In the west, between the Kurini Mountains and the open steppe, lie the wastes of Thamiras Rest, named after the wyrm Thamira of the Fated Journey of Khariel. They hold the salted ruins of Alahmid, the fortress of the sorcerer <a href="Merzigal" class="internal-link">Merzigal</a>, destroyed by the <a href="Kherovan Inquisition" class="internal-link">Kherovan Inquisition</a> in 587PA and guarded by its agents ever since. Thamiras Rest is claimed by no one and is a common battleground for the Khans, Kherovan bands of warriors and many lesser factions.</p>  
<h2>Flora and fauna</h2>  
<p>The Gnarlskin Salamanders are the best known creatures of the steppe and the mounts of the Khaganates. They are great reptiles with long, serpentine bodies and powerful forelegs, covered in rough, plated hides, and the young among them are able to breathe fire. A salamander can live for up to 220 years and keeps growing as it ages, and after around a century its ability to spew fire fades while its teeth continue to grow. The oldest and largest of them carry entire communities on their backs. Scholars believe that the common tales of fire-breathing <a href="Dragon" class="internal-link">dragons</a> stem from sightings of these creatures, since true dragons, to which they are unrelated, are so rare that they are barely documented. In <a href="Bihra Shamanism" class="internal-link">Bihra Shamanism</a> a rider and their salamander are believed to share a soul.</p>  
<p>The steppe is home to the largest herds of <a href="Auroch" class="internal-link">Aurochs</a> in the world, which the Steppe Bihra follow and keep, and to the Gleeflies, which like most native species have adapted to life among the Aspira.</p>  
<h2>Peoples</h2>  
<p>The Steppe Bihra are the dominant people of the grassland. They stand up to 2 metres tall and often weigh far more than other Bihra, and their hides are tough enough to endure the Aspira. They are unusually prone to mutation and honour their most mutated as leaders and shamans. Most of them are nomadic, migrating with their herds between the sacred sites of their shamans and the lands of their tributaries. Their largest communities are the walking cities, whole camps carried across the steppe on the backs of the oldest salamanders. Few Steppe Bihra live beyond 70 years, though the span of a life depends greatly on the nature of their mutations.</p>  
<p>The river cities of the Agav and the other tributary communities are largely populated by Bihra who are not of the steppe, whom the Steppe Bihra call Zibihra, together with smaller numbers of humans and goblins. Many of them are slaves, and their rice harvests feed the Khaganates. In the Forbidden Lands live the Inive, Bihra and humans whose skin has been turned red by the gases of the land, their name deriving from the Kheri word iniv, meaning "red". They often pay tribute to the most powerful Khans of the time. The monk Cinderstone Mikor, known as the <a href="Red Monk" class="internal-link">Red Monk</a>, was born among them. Goblin caravans from <a href="Pintrixaz" class="internal-link">Pintrixaz</a> cross the north-western steppe, and in the west small communities beyond the Kherovan forts keep their own defences, serving as havens for Kheri outriders or paying tribute to the Khans.</p>  
<h2>Politics</h2>  
<p>The steppe is divided between four great Khaganates, each of which holds tributaries of its own and claims descent from the blood of the Jigallor, the last of the Great Khans. The <a href="Duol Khaganate" class="internal-link">Duol Khaganate</a> is the largest and most powerful of them and is ruled from the great encampment of Duol Rabad on the Cliffs of Rabad. When it rose in 851PA its Khan was accepted by the other great Bihra as their overlord, but they have since refused him service, and although the Duol Khan still styles himself the Khan of Khans, all four are rivals in the modern day.</p>  
<p>The <a href="Anatai Khaganate" class="internal-link">Anatai</a> are farmer and slaver lords more than anything else and hold many of the great homesteads around the Shavai Lakes. The <a href="Yadu Khaganate" class="internal-link">Yadu</a> are a warlike Khaganate of shamans, famed for their witches and casters, and are the eternal enemies of the Twintail Imperium. The <a href="Gökol Khaganate" class="internal-link">Gökol</a> of the north-west are the most mercantile of the four, long allied with the goblin caravans of Pintrixaz and the hobgoblins of the mountain slopes as far as <a href="Lugosch" class="internal-link">Lugosch</a>, and are at war with Tizin-Kol.</p>  
<p>A Great Khan was traditionally raised by a mixture of conquest, the strength and mutation of their body and the acclamation of the shamans. Single combat between rival Khans, such as the Duel of the Great Khans of 513PA, once decided who ruled the whole steppe, but the custom has slowly faded over the last century.</p>  
<h2>History</h2>  
<p>The Kheri have fought the Khans of the steppe since before the days of the Genbela and Odovo Khaganates, which rose in 298PA and 415PA and ended together at the Duel of the Great Khans in 513PA. In the north the Xungin Khaganate ruled the northern and north-western steppeuntil it was broken by the Twintail Imperator Natan X Irontail from 338PA onwards, whose conquests south of the Mondi River began the line that would become the Red Iron Towers.</p>  
<p>The <a href="Luchun Khaganate" class="internal-link">Luchun Khaganate</a>, the largest ever assembled, rose in 536PA and subjugated the Bihra realms of <a href="Ekkam" class="internal-link">Ekkam</a> and <a href="Az" class="internal-link">Az</a> in the south before Gaden of Drizan slew the Great Khan Luchun at Verivale in 671PA. The <a href="Jigallor Khaganate" class="internal-link">Jigallor Khaganate</a> followed in 685PA under a line of Khans who all bore the name Jigallor, and was the last to truly rule the whole steppe and the lands beyond it. In 782PA it struck Kherova during the 2nd War of Elbae Aggression, destroying the fortress cities of Adsal and Iblis and besieging Erbil, and it ended when the last Jigallor fell at the Battle of Adsal in 794PA.</p>  
<p>After Adsal the heirs of the Jigallor divided the horde between them, and the steppe has never again been united. The Duol rose to primacy in 851PA, but the other Khaganates broke away from its overlordship in the following decades. In 912PA the Elek, vassals of the Yadu, took the mountains and hills between the steppe and the Gorati Highlands with large hosts, keeping to the high ground to avoid the Shirin legions and spilling into the lands of <a href="Jateš" class="internal-link">Jateš</a> and the Mustari. Two years later the Tuchewa, another Yadu vassal, were destroyed by <a href="Kubil no Vang" class="internal-link">Kubil no Vang</a> at the Battle of the Amethyst Caverns in 914PA. In the north-west the Gökol remain at war with Tizin-Kol over the surface lands around Zian Station, while in the south the Duol have once again begun raiding the <a href="Kareli Hierarchy" class="internal-link">Kareli Hierarchy</a>.</p>
