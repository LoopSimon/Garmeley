<p>Gorat is a region of eastern Nirydia lying between the Limsul Peaks and the Gavivag Bay, south of the <a href="Twintail Imperium" class="internal-link">Twintail Imperium</a> and north of the <a href="Velvet Caravan" class="internal-link">Velvet Road</a>. It is the homeland of the <a href="Gorati" class="internal-link">Gorati</a>, a tall and deeply religious human people, and is today divided between the Kingdom of <a href="Plašato" class="internal-link">Plašato</a>, the Kingdom of <a href="Amlija" class="internal-link">Amlija</a>, the Republic of <a href="Dišela" class="internal-link">Dišela</a>, the Duchy of <a href="Jateš" class="internal-link">Jateš</a>, the Principality of <a href="Ruginë" class="internal-link">Ruginë</a> and the city states of <a href="Sredit" class="internal-link">Sredit</a> and <a href="Bruša" class="internal-link">Bruša</a>. From 491PA to 767PA almost all of Gorat was united under the <a href="Zepria" class="internal-link">Zeprian Empire</a>, whose collapse gave rise to the modern Gorati nations. The region is one of the heartlands of <a href="Zuhurism" class="internal-link">Zuhurism</a> and home to Hastermon Temple, one of the four great holy sites of the faith, and the <a href="Rian" class="internal-link">Rian</a>, first minted by the Zeprian Empire, is the most widely used currency of central Nirydia.</p>  
<h2>Geography</h2>  
<p>Gorat stretches from the Gorati Highlands in the north-west, on the edge of the <a href="Niryd Steppe" class="internal-link">Niryd Steppe</a>, down the eastern slopes of the Limsul Peaks to the coasts of the Gavivag Bay and the Gorati Sea. The Mustar River divides the north of the region into the Gorati Highlands above it and the Mustar Peaks to its south, and flows east past the Free City of Sredit into the Gavivag Bay. South of the Mustar lies the Kragu Valley, the heart of Jateš, and beyond it the long coast of Plašato with its capital Rivno and the old imperial capital of Plavigrad, which is supplied with water by a great aqueduct from Lake Višine. The marble, gold and silver mines of the Plašati mountains are among the richest in Nirydia.</p>  
<p>In the south the Buzin River runs from the Limsul Peaks through the Velvet Valleys and the vineyards of the Buzin Vale into the ports of Amlija and the Gorati Sea. Amlija holds the south-eastern coast with its capital Rashice, the largest city of Gorat, and the hill of Hastermon Temple and the Sunrise Mountains, near Meveti, lie in its north. Offshore, the Strait of Zelarvo separates the mainland from the island of Zelarvo, seat of Dišela and its capital Diprije, while Sgar Isle, with Bruša on its northern shore, lies further south-east at the meeting of the Gorati Sea and the Vanpur Ocean. In the west the high valleys of the southern Limsul Peaks are held by Ruginë, whose capital is Proske, while the Sunset Mountains lie south of Amlija and the Velvet Road. Beyond the peaks lie the Bihra realm of <a href="Rha-Gis" class="internal-link">Rha-Gis</a>, the Soki Valley and the Kolrog Highlands, and to the south the road leads on to the <a href="Kareli Hierarchy" class="internal-link">Kareli Hierarchy</a>.</p>  
<h2>History</h2>  
<p>The first records of Gorati cities date to around 615BA, shortly after <a href="Zuhurism" class="internal-link">Zuhurism</a> was first practised by the Pagri nomads across the Gavivag Bay. According to tradition the prophet Hastermon reached communion with Pen Zuhur on the hill that now bears his temple in the 4th century BA, and the faith has been bound to the region ever since. For much of its early history Gorat was divided between petty princes and lords. Jateš was founded in 287PA, and the united Principality of Ruginë is first mentioned in 325PA, making it older than any other Gorati realm that survives today.</p>  
<p>In 491PA <a href="Suzana Dragović" class="internal-link">Suzana Dragović</a>, Queen of the small Kingdom of Zepria, proclaimed the <a href="Zepria" class="internal-link">Zeprian Empire</a> after a series of conquests that brought most of the Gorati, as well as Bihra Khans and Shirin generals, under her rule. At its height under Suzana and her immediate heirs the Empire stretched from the northern Drizan Peninsula to the borders of the <a href="Twintail Imperium" class="internal-link">Twintail Imperium</a>, and its Rian became the common coin of the region. Ruginë was never truly conquered, and Jateš passed between Zeprian and Shirin rule several times. Later historians call the period up to 588PA the Zeprian Golden Age, which ended with the accession of Emperor Jožek and the Nighmilk Plague of 591PA to 594PA. In the 650s the Empire fought the Pagri Campaigns as its part in the Second Holy War against the heresy of <a href="Umo" class="internal-link">Umo</a>, and in 657PA it suffered a heavy defeat against the undead of the witch king Zologos at the Battle of Brina. Over the following century the Empire slowly lost control of its provinces, beginning with the Buzinci Delegation Incident of 724PA, and by 758PA the Opmani and the Buzinci had all but abandoned imperial rule. It was dissolved in 767PA, after its last Emperor, Radov the Silver, turned himself and nearly three dozen members of his family and court into pure silver in a failed invocation of great magic in the palace of Plavigrad. From its ruins rose the kingdoms of Plašato and Amlija, the Republic of Dišela and an independent Jateš.</p>  
<p>None of the successor states took an active part in the Fifth <a href="Zuhuri Holy Wars" class="internal-link">Zuhuri Holy War</a>. In 846PA the plague known as the Dance of Husks ravaged the Gorati and their Pagri neighbours. In 880PA the patricians of Sredit renounced the overlordship of Jateš with the backing of the Twintail Imperium, and in 885PA Bruša broke away from Dišela, beginning that republic's decline. In the early 10th century <a href="Black Powder" class="internal-link">black powder</a> weapons have begun to spread among the Gorati realms from the north.</p>  
<h2>Peoples</h2>  
<p>Almost all of the people of Gorat are <a href="Gorati" class="internal-link">Gorati</a>, who are divided into six peoples. The Zepri live mostly in Plašato, the Buzinci in Amlija, Dišela and Bruša, the Kraguvci in Jateš, and the Mustari in northern Jateš, Sredit and the southern lands of the Twintail Imperium. The Opmani inhabit Ruginë, the Velvet Valleys and the Sunset Mountains, while the Soki, the wildest and least like the others, live in the Soki Valley and the Kolrog Highlands beyond the Limsul Peaks. Most Gorati states use Yezepri as their official language, while Ruginë uses Firhë. The Gorati have little contact with the Bihra of the Limsul Peaks, and small clans of <a href="Ral" class="internal-link">Ral</a> shifters, who came into the region from the north long ago, live in its highlands.</p>  
<h2>Economy</h2>  
<p>Gorat is one of the wealthiest regions of Nirydia. Plašato draws its wealth from its marble, gold and silver mines and its great roads, while the Buzinci of the south are known for their trade and for the wines of the Buzin Vale, and Dišela is among the most commercially dominant powers of the continent. Jateš is known for its metalwork and for spirits distilled from wine, and the carpets and rugs woven along the Mustar are sold throughout Nirydia. Ruginë and the Opmani profit from their hold over the Velvet Road, the caravan route to the Kareli Hierarchy.</p>  
<p>Slavery is common throughout Gorat. Rashice is known for its galley slaves and Diprije for its pleasure houses, and the Gorati realms carry on a lively exchange of slaves with the Kareli Hierarchy beyond the Velvet Road. The horns of the Zecorog, a large horned hare native to Gorat, are prized in alchemy for potions of vigour and desire.</p>  
<h2>Religion</h2>  
<p>Religion is central to life in Gorat, and the Gorati are among the most fervent followers of <a href="Zuhurism" class="internal-link">Zuhurism</a>. Hastermon Temple in Amlija draws pilgrims from all over the Zuhuri world, and its priests and monks guard the site fiercely, its highest-ranking members wearing sun masks of brass and gold. Unlike some of the Pagri churches to the north, the Gorati rarely practise ritual blinding. The Nyads of the Gorati Sea and the Gavivag Bay are held to be the daughters of the Sun Lord's dreams, and to harm one is among the gravest taboos of the region. Magic is regarded as a gift of Pen Zuhur, and its highest form, the <a href="Veziva" class="internal-link">Veziva</a>, is passed on in circles of masters, the most renowned of which are the Five Towers of Rivno and the Hall of Sunrise in Meveti in northern Amlija.</p>  
<h2>Politics</h2>  
<p>Plašato, the largest and most populous of the Gorati nations, regards itself as the rightful ruler of all Gorat and has been at odds with the Pagri realm of <a href="Cirinzel" class="internal-link">Cirinzel</a> since the Gavivag War, but it is outshone in wealth by Amlija, its chief rival for dominance in the region. In the north both Plašato and the Twintail Imperium seek to extend their influence over Jateš and Sredit, a contest that feeds the old rivalry of the Kraguvci and the Mustari. The desire of Jateš to regain Sredit, which is held by a Twintail fleet and garrison under Admiral <a href="Tucao Chuwen" class="internal-link">Tucao Chuwen</a>, is the greatest conflict of the region, and hirelings of both sides already skirmish in the Sredit Greenlands. Dišela has been in decline since the loss of Bruša, while Ruginë remains apart in its mountains, and the Opmani of the south are bitter enemies of their Buzinci neighbours.</p>
