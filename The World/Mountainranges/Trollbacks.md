<p>The Trollback mountains, sometimes just called the Trollbacks, are one of the largest Mountain ranges on all of <a href="Inara" class="internal-link">Inara</a> and the second largest on in the subcontinent of <a href="Ilrakhan" class="internal-link">Ilrakhan</a>. They divide central and eastern Ilrakhan and the lands of Ur and Vihr its northern slops falling into the lands of Wermia and Uhl and the Gladewood hugging its south. The highest Peak of the Trollbacks is Mount Thenmore, located in the north of the mountain range.</p>
