<p>The Mistspires are the largest Mountain ranges in the subcontinent of <a href="Nirydia" class="internal-link">Nirydia</a> and all of <a href="Inara" class="internal-link">Inara</a>. They are located between Nirydia and <a href="Ilrakhan" class="internal-link">Ilrakhan</a>, dividing the two continental plates, the icy lands to their north are often referred to as the Waelajokull and the lands of the kheri peoples lie to their south. The Grachyll hills and mountains stretch out in their central west and are sometimes counted as part of the Mistspires as well. The highest Peak of the Mistspires is Mount Laram, located in the south of the central Mistspires.</p>
