<p>The Kortopis are one of the largest Mountain ranges on all of <a href="Inara" class="internal-link">Inara</a> and the largest one on the subcontinent of <a href="Ilrakhan" class="internal-link">Ilrakhan</a>. The Kortopis are often divided into the northern and southern Kortopi mountains, the southern Kortopis making up the border between the regions of Ur and Cevolia while the northern Kortopis divide Cevolia from Meadland. In the far north they form the southern end of the Ardvale opposite of the Urdfall Peaks. The highest peak of the Kortopis is the Henrak, located in the north-west of the mountain range.</p>
<p>The name "Kortopis" derives from the gnomish "Kortop" roughly translating to "breaking the Wind" or "Windbreakers". While the gnomes are technically not natives to the Kortopis in the truest sense of the word, the term has stuck in wider culture and is almost always used by any surface dwellers when referring to the range.</p>
