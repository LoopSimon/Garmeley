<p>The Cloudscratchers are one of the largest Mountain ranges on all of <a href="Inara" class="internal-link">Inara</a> and the largest one on the subcontinent of <a href="Behera" class="internal-link">Behera</a>. The Cloudscratchers lie on the border between the continental plates of <a href="Ilrakhan" class="internal-link">Ilrakhan</a> and Behera, dividing the two landmasses. In the west their peaks fall into hills and the craggy Lameidus, while in the east they curve south around the lands of Arevi Yerkir. The highest Peak of the Cloudscratchers is Marvol Peak, located in the central Cloudscratchers.</p>
