[[Aisligos]]
%%
<p>Bulug is one of the largest Islands on the continental group of Aisiligos, sitting across the Whisperway south of the Island of Verzi. Bulug is home to varying Hives of the Pharang, as well as most notably  the Hiqae realm of Lazatec and their enslaved and altered Narzoi servants.</p>
<p>The centre of the island is defined by the Omaci Peaks, a mountain range that divides Bulug into its north eastern more habitable riverlands and its south western wild and less friendly jungles, called the Sharem.</p>
