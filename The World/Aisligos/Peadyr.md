%% [[The New World]] [[Aisligos]] [[Narzoi]] [[Uraz]] [[Alder]] [[Aldergrounds]] [[Chianism]] [[The Colonization of Peadyr]] [[Finger of Life]]%% <p>Peadyr is a large island in the continental sphere of <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a>, commonly referred to simply as <a href="The New World" class="internal-link">"The New World"</a>, and was the first larger landmass of the region to be explored in depth by sailors from the east in the so called <a href="Inara" class="internal-link">"Old World"</a>. It is geologically and demographically very distinct from greater Aisligos, to which it was once joined through the lands of Naltor until the eruption of the <a href="Uraz" class="internal-link">Uraz</a>, and it was here that sailors from Inara first made lasting contact with the mysterious <a href="Narzoi" class="internal-link">Narzoi</a>.</p> <p>The northern coast of Peadyr curves around the Gulf of Peadyr, and in the north-east the land rises into the rocky Pea Highlands. The south and south-east are given over to the broad Odise Riverlands, while the south-west holds the <a href="Ruins of Imala" class="internal-link">Ruins of Imala</a> and the Ghostlands, said to be the haunt of terrible creatures and the spirits of death, among them the <a href="Triarog" class="internal-link">Triarog</a>, which hunt there in great number. A long arm of the island reaches north-west towards the Ruins of Naltor, the broken peaks that mark where the land bridge once ran, and in the interior of the east-centre rises the <a href="Finger of Life" class="internal-link">Finger of Life</a>, a site of Chianist pilgrimage, particularly among the Odise. Much of the island is covered by rich forest, home to the colossal Toltrees native to Peadyr, and its climate is far gentler than that of neighbouring <a href="Bethun" class="internal-link">Bethun</a>, which lies beyond the Heroes Grave Sea to the south and was often counted as part of Peadyr by the explorers of the Old World.</p> <p>Several of the waters and islands around Peadyr carry the names of those who first charted them. The Eyks Channel divides the north of the island from Jurland, Balansbay opens on the north-eastern shore, and the Viamali Sea bears the name of the explorer who first made lasting contact with the Narzoi. Off the north-eastern tip lies Reydsland, an island only at high tide and a peninsula at ebb, while the Pear Isles lie off the east coast. Among the lands of the Pea the Ghostlands are held to be forbidden ground, said to be the haunt of terrible creatures and the spirits of death, and little beyond these tales is known of them to outsiders.</p> <p>Until the eruption of the Uraz in 283BA, Peadyr was no island. Through the lowlands of Naltor, the heartland of the <a href="Alder" class="internal-link">Alder</a>, it was joined to mainland Aisligos, and when the earthquakes that heralded the eruption sank Naltor beneath the sea in the course of days, the land bridge went with it and Peadyr was severed from the continent. The drought and famine that followed across Aisligos in the Uraz Period largely spared the island, whose forests and coasts remained fertile, though not all of its life endured, and the Peadi <a href="Pangomor" class="internal-link">Pangomor</a> is known to have gone extinct in these centuries.</p> <p>The Alder had left their mark on Peadyr as they had on the mainland. In the south-west stood the <a href="Aldergrounds" class="internal-link">Aldergrounds</a> of Imala, and within them an <a href="Alderbridge" class="internal-link">Alderbridge</a>, one of the gateways by which the Alder once crossed great distances in a single step. In 379PA the Laso, a fanatic tribe of western Peadyr related to the <a href="Senso" class="internal-link">Senso</a>, destroyed the Aldergrounds of Imala and the Alderbridge within, believing them to be the work of evil spirits, and the Ruins of Imala are all that remain of them. No Alderbridge functions on Peadyr today. With its gateway lost, the island lay beyond the network through which the <a href="Hiqae" class="internal-link">Hiqae</a> of Lazatec later threatened the lands of the mainland, and no Hiqae force ever reached its shores.</p> <p>For more than a thousand years the island lay effectively cut off from Aisligos. The boats of the mainland Narzoi were unequal to the crossing, and the one exception was the <a href="Thun" class="internal-link">Thuni</a> Narzoi of Bethun, whose better ships began crossing the Heroes Grave Sea in 497PA and who made first contact with the Senso of the southern coast. Otherwise the Pea, the principal inhabitants of the island, were left to themselves, and for nearly every explorer and settler of the Old World they remained the first and often only encounter with the Narzoi as a whole. They developed in ways their cousins on the mainland did not. The Pea learned to work bronze, with smelting established by 400PA, but never discovered iron, and their speech drifted so far from the dialects of the mainland that the Pea Narzoi dialect is now close to a language of its own.</p> <p>The Pea are most widely known through the three federations into which their tribes gradually drew together, the <a href="Kavi" class="internal-link">Kavi</a> of the northern coast and the Pea Highlands, the <a href="Odise" class="internal-link">Odise</a> of the south and south-east, and the <a href="Senso" class="internal-link">Senso</a> of the south-west. Not every people of the island belongs to one of them, for example the <a href="Vudi" class="internal-link">Vudi</a>, fisherfolk of the eastern coast, stand apart from all three. Their formation appears to have followed no single founding event, and by the seventh and eighth centuries PA all three were well established, while the older tribal identities beneath them have faded in more recent generations. Old World writers, who first met them as tribes, still often call them so. Alongside these federations the Pea have historically been distinguished by geography and way of life, among them the Plains Pea, who adopted hunting and a nomadic culture, and the Woodland and Swampy Pea, who relied on hunting, fishing and gathering in the rich forests. Over the same centuries <a href="Chianism" class="internal-link">Chianism</a> took shape across the island, a faith of Water, Land and Forest whose reverences speak of a people whose home had been spared when the mainland was not.</p> <p>The isolation of Peadyr ended in the eighth century PA with the arrival of ships from the Old World. <a href="Balan Reyd" class="internal-link">Balan Reyd</a> landed on the north-eastern shore in 785PA and founded Camp Reyd, where he and many of his men soon died of disease, <a href="Jurim Eykszoon" class="internal-link">Jurim Eykszoon</a> charted the shores of the island and its northern isles in 834PA, and in 840PA <a href="Marino Viamali" class="internal-link">Marino Viamali</a> made the first sustained contact with the Narzoi, in a peaceful meeting with a people most accounts name as the Kavi. The Pea came to call the newcomers the Dari. Colonial settlements followed along the coasts, among them New Rav, founded in 842PA and destroyed by the Narzoi in 847PA, <a href="Livoa" class="internal-link">Livoa</a> in the Pear Isles in 858PA, <a href="Faarhovn" class="internal-link">Faarhovn</a> on Jurland in 878PA, <a href="New Fryd" class="internal-link">New Fryd</a> on Reydsland in 880PA, <a href="Almersport" class="internal-link">Almersport</a> on the south-eastern coast in 895PA, <a href="Alinanora" class="internal-link">Alinanora</a> on the eastern coast in 900PA and <a href="Dueren" class="internal-link">Dueren</a> on Giselland in 921PA. Their history is told in <a href="The Colonization of Peadyr" class="internal-link">The Colonization of Peadyr</a>.</p> <p>In the present day the colonial presence remains confined to the coasts and offshore islands, and the interior of Peadyr is still the domain of the three federations. Relations between the settlements and the Narzoi vary widely. Faarhovn, by arrangement with the Kavi, is the only port permitted to trade on the mainland of Peadyr, Alinanora has kept to amicable terms, and Dueren, which has no direct contact with the Narzoi, deals with the island only through its partnership with Faarhovn. Almersport has pursued expansion with open aggression, spreading onto Odise land against the wishes of the federation and threatening the northern Riverlands through which the Odise travel on pilgrimage to the Finger of Life, and the two are now in a state of declared war.</p>
