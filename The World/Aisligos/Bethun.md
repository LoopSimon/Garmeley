<p>Often counted as part of Peadyr by the explorers of the Old World, the island of Bethun lies beyond the Heroes Grave Sea south of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a> and is home to the <a href="Thun" class="internal-link">Thuni</a> Narzoi, arguably the most prolific seafarers of all Narzoi. Its climate and biomes are a lot less forgiving than those of Peadyr, and the northern parts are home to dry flats and craggy red wastes, with only Thun Valley, after which the Thuni take their name, in the south-east being largely habitable.<p/>
