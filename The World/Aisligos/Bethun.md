<p>Often counted as part of Peadyr by the explorers of the old world, the Island of bethun les beyond the Heroes Grave Sea south of Peadyr and is home to the <a href="Thun" class="internal-link">Thuni</a> Narzoi, arguably the most prolific seafairers of all Narzoi. Its climate and biomes are alot less forgiving than peadyr however, and the northern parts are home to dry flats and craggy red wastes, with only Thun Valley, after wich the thun take their name, in the south-east being largely habitable.</p>
