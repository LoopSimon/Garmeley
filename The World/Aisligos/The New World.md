<p>Aisligos is both the overall name for the "new world in the west" encompassing the massive landmasses and islands across the Tirean Ocean, as well as the largest single landmass of them, usually referred to as a continent in itself. This article will discuss the greater continental Sphere of Aisligos, for the largest of the primary islands  see <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a>. 
It stretches from icy wastes in the north down to the warm waters of the Silken Sea and is connected to Verzi along a small landb ridge, making Verzi in itself part of the continent of Aisligos.</p>
<p>Colloquially when referring to Aisligos as a continental Sphere, the islands of Aisligos Proper, <a href="Bulug" class="internal-link">Bulug</a>, <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a> and <a href="Verzi" class="internal-link">Verzi</a> are counted, as well as the various larger and smaller island chains in the Gulf of Nyllena, the Stoney Sea, the Ruins of Naltor, the Lisari Sea, the Silken Sea, the Heroes Grave and any other archipelagos adjacent to these major landmasses.</p>
<p>Aisligos has survived numerous eras of complicated histories, geological catastrophe and political conflict of grand scale. It was first discovered by the <a href="Hiqae" class="internal-link">Hiqae</a> of <a href="Pagia" class="internal-link">Pagia</a>, travelling in their so called Eternal Arks, in 561PA and by <a href="Bykmåli" class="internal-link">skori</a> sailors from <a href="Ilrakhan" class="internal-link">Ilrakhan</a> in 688PA ( see <a href="The Discovery of Aisligos" class="internal-link">he Discovery of Aisligos</a>.</p>
