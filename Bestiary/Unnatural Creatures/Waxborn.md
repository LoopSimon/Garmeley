<p>The Waxborn are a type of <a href="Golem" class="internal-link">Golem</a>, created under specific rites and ordained tradition by powerful followers of the <a href="Gethricism" class="internal-link">Gethric Faith</a>, the techniques required to create such a creature are secret and passed only within small circles within the Faith, although it is unclear if they are, on a secular and material level, merely a variant of the classical Principle of a Golem, or are truly made of a divine spark.</p>
<p>According to the gethric canon, the first Waxborn were created by the Scholar and later Saint, <a href="Palus" class="internal-link">Palus Aregestes</a> around 572PA. A classical Waxborn is often clad in the garbs of a Gethric clergyman in the style of Argestes, albeit they themselves have as constructs of course no need for clothing or such frivolities.</p>
<p>The Waxborn are classically associated with protection and reverence duties, only few ever visible to the public, aside from parades and excursions of the highest of clerical staff. While there are rumours about the practice in the west, it seems like in the modern day and age the few who have attained the ability to create such a creature practice it within the <a href="Church of Iztaw " class="internal-link">Church of Iztaw </a> and the High-Gethric denomination. Many of them are members of the <a href="Candleguard" class="internal-link">Candleguard</a>, such as the Censors of <a href="Iztaw" class="internal-link">Iztaw</a>, who command a guard of Waxborn that patrol the streets of the holy city. Outside of Iztaw itself, it has become incredibly rare to see a Waxborn accompanying their master in the modern age.</p>
