<p>A Golem is a broader classification for beings created to serve, from materials of the earth and infused with energies by their creator, usually in the shape of a scroll or object embedded within. They are associated with Artisanry and  the earth, materials such as mud, clay, rock and marble have been famously used to create Golems in various shapes and forms throughout history, such as the "Silent Wardens" of <a href="Cearic" class="internal-link">Cearic</a>. The art to create a Golem is an extremely well guarded secret, sometimes associated with alchemy, sometimes with Artificery, but usually its own unique discipline of the supernatural philosophy. The first Golem was supposedly created by Thuad of Im, a legendary figure in beheran folklore that is usually depicted as having created the creature to serve and protect him from persecution, sometimes as a criminal sorcerer, more often heroically as a wrongfully admonished member of an infringed community.</p>
<p>Whiel many associate these rare creations with purely physical and earthy elements, examples like <a href="Palus" class="internal-link">Palus Argestes</a> great work, the so called <a href="Waxborn" class="internal-link">Waxborn</a>, are prevalent as well. The Scholar Unther of the Willows created an exhaustive tome over the span of nearly two decades detailing various cultural arcane and religious practices  and legends that can be brought in line with the greater art of Golem Creation.</p>
