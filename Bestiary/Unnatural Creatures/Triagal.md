%% [[Triarog]] [[The New World]] [[Aisligos]] [[Alder]] [[Narzoi]] [[Chianism]] [[Peadyr]] [[Bulug]] [[Verzi]] %% <div class="wiki-infobox" style="overflow: hidden; display: flex; flex-direction: column;"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Triagal.jpg" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;"> <small><i>A Triagal</i></small> </div> <p>The Triagal are the humanoid and sentient members of the <a href="Triarog" class="internal-link">Triarog</a> race, descended from an ancient rite of post-Catarmon <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a>. They are far more humanoid in appearance than their feral kin, and they possess incredible physical prowess. A Triagal is so fast that it is often faster than the eye can follow, and it is able to tap into primal magic to control light and darkness.</p> <p>The rite that made them belongs to the age after the Catarmon, when the <a href="Narzoi" class="internal-link">Narzoi</a> had inherited the lands of the fallen <a href="Alder" class="internal-link">Alder</a>. Young babes were given to the Gods of Death and Night, deities associated with the Triarog, in rituals in which shamans transformed them into hybrids. These gods precede <a href="Chianism" class="internal-link">Chianism</a> and go back thousands of years. Theirs was one of the primordial faiths of Aisligos from the times before, whose worshippers revered what were likely relics of the things that ruled and roamed the land during the Age of Roil, tens of thousands of years ago. Very little is known of them, they are held by most to be mythical, and they are associated with fear and darkness.</p> <p>The Triagal can only mate with each other, and there are extremely few of them left. Many search their whole lives for a mate of their own kind. There is no record of a Triagal dying of old age or showing any sign of physical deterioration, and rumours of ancient "elders" of the race persist, but it must be assumed that any sentient creature condemned to such an existence, unable to die, would long since have gone insane. A Triagal usually surrounds itself with a brood of Triarog servants, whom it controls and dominates.</p> <p>The Narzoi regard the Triagal with absolute fear, and where one is met there is no contact, only fight and flight. On <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a> they are so rare as to be nearly mythical, and are associated with the evil spirits of Chianism, but on Aisligos, <a href="Bulug" class="internal-link">Bulug</a> and <a href="Verzi" class="internal-link">Verzi</a> they are a real and material threat. It is unlikely that anyone still practises the rite, though the Gods of Death and Night are worshipped in the far north of Aisligos, and it may survive there.</p>
