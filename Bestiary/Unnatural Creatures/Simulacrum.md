[[Homunculus]]

<p>A general term for attempts at creating a magical version of the self through summoning and duplicating once essence not to be confused with a <a href="Homunculus" class="internal-link">Homunculus</a>. Where a simulacrum is fundamentally a copy or imitation of an existing person or thing, the homunculus is an artificially created miniature human or humanoid being through the pracitce of <a href="Alchemy" class="internal-link">Alchemy</a>. </p>
