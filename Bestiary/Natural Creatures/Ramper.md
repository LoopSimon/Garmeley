<p>Rampers are inhabitants of the Deep Sea encountered within the Beheran Ocean, the Niryd Sea and the Deep Waters of eastern Nirydia. They possess vaguely humanoid characteristics and appear in great variety, able to survive extended periods on land. Their most recognizable feature are their unique head shape, and Fin-Arms that act as a 3rd set of limbs attached to their upper arms. The largest ever measured adult Ramper weighed in at over three and a half tons, but its age was impossible to attain, they are among the fastest known swimmers among all species on the globe.</p>
<p> There are no recorded instances of domesticated members of the species, or even individuals held successfully within captivity outside of the <a href="Krestasi" class="internal-link">Krestasi</a> civilizations such as the <a href="Golomid" class="internal-link">Golomid</a>, where Rampers have been seen as mount - like creatures in service to various Warriors during the Elbae-Golomid War.</p>
