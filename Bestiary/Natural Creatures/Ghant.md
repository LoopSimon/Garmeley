<p>A Ghant is a hulking humanoid that not only survives but thrives in the moors and swamps of the World, giant like in appearance they enter extreme periods of hibernation and only wake when something enters their territories, devouring their prey without mercy. Ghant have been spotten everywhere from Sertova in western Ilrakhan to Gaal in eastern Nirydia and are sometimes associated with Ogres, Firbolg and other Tallfolk due to their statue and sheer mass alone, although there is no other reason to believe them related. The Bones of a Ghant are a rare item of luxury and often used in expert craftsmanship around the world, although only few undertake the perilous challenge to set out and purposefully bait and hunt a Ghant. </p>
<p>Ghants have been reportedly sighted in many different sizes and shapes, indicating likely at dietary differences or biological influence of their respective habitats, some ranging from 3 metres to over 11 metres tall, there are no specific gender discerning markers discovered on the bodies of the Ghant and they likely do not reproduce through sexual partnerships. This is further underlined by the fact that Ghants are decidedly isolated creatures and innately extremely territorial. Some of them have become folklore like deities to smaller communities, and are said to have lived for hundreds of years, although nothing about their age or other characteristics can truly be confirmed outside of near mythological tellings, as no Ghant has ever been properly observed for a long time by a scholar.</p>
<p>The famous "Father Kran" of the Kranmoor in <a href="Lesmere" class="internal-link">Lesmere</a> is supposedly an ancient Ghant, hidden away in the deepest morass of the swamps, as is the so called "Monster of Roha" a creature preying on unsuspecting travellers in southern <a href="Grevenna" class="internal-link">Grevenna</a>. The kheri Inquisitor <a href="Darvid Maerlon" class="internal-link">Darvid Maerlon</a> reportedly slew a gigantic Ghant in the northern Yellow Swamps in the mid 910s. </p>
