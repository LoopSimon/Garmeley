<p>The cockatrice is a monstrous animal that can be found in the northern hemisphere of Inara, particularly in western Nirydia, <a href="Nostroya" class="internal-link">Nostroya</a>, <a href="Sertova" class="internal-link">Sertova</a> and <a href="Othirdon" class="internal-link">Othirdon</a>. The creature is of the avian variety and has the body of a gigantic emaciated rooster, the wings of a bat and a long, scaly tail, its fierce bite is to be feared as it is rumoured to possess the ability to cause organic matter to calcify and harden, eventually fossilizing its prey into stone. Cockatrices are hunted as dangerous predators in the regions they appear and were almost completely wiped out within Vihr, Ur and Meadland during the Age of Silver. </p>
<p>The urien author <a href="Hubert of Tanverley" class="internal-link">Hubert of Tanverley</a> infamously held a Cockatrice named "Ginger" as a pet for nearly a year and documented much of what we now know about the animal in the second volume of his lifelong work. The cockatrice is the national symbol of the sertovan <a href="Erlmondy" class="internal-link">Kingdom of Erlmondy</a> and proudly displayed on its Coat of Arms.</p>
<p>As avians, the cockatrice too lays eggs which take an unusually long time to hatch and are sometimes seen as rare and luxurious prices sought out by hunters, collectors, alchemists and wizards for various practical and non practical reasons. The female cockatrice grows almost three timers as big as her male counter part, and only one in five eggs hatch females, the female cockatrice tends to their spawn and becomes considerably more dangerous while caring for her young, but will usually abandon them after a year of care. Most cockatrices only lay eggs three for four times before death and continue to grow more and more hostile towards other members of their species. </p>
<p>The ability of the cockatrice, to "petrify" its enemies upon repeated bites is mysterious and has become the subject of many fairy tales and varying academic interest, although it should be noted that the animals ability seems to not work on fellow members of their species. While their diet consists primarily of seeds and petrified insects (which conveniently double in the creature’s gizzard as both gastroliths and nutrition as they grind away), cockatrices fiercely defend their territories from anything they deem a threat, and the wanderings of rogue males seeking new spots to build dens sometimes bring them into unintentional contact with humanoids, with devastating results.</p>
