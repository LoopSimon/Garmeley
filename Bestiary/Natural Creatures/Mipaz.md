<div class="wiki-infobox" style="overflow: hidden; display: flex; flex-direction: column;"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Mipaz.png" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;"> <small><i>A Mipaz of the Purple Sea</i></small> </div> <p>The Mipaz is a magical creature of the reefs of the Purple Sea, found above all in the shallows of the Kaitan Gulf and among the Isles of Naigan. It is long and slender, with a narrow, tapering snout and a body that ends in a curling tail, and it is said to reach some three metres in length. Its fins are broad and red and flutter like silk when it hangs motionless in the water, and two long, dark blue filaments trail from its snout. Foreign sailors call it the red reef dragon, though it is no kin of dragons.</p> <p>The most striking feature of the Mipaz is the branching red growth that covers its head and back. It is living coral, and the Mipaz carries it as a gardener would, giving the coral a safe place to grow while the coral hides the animal among the reef. A Mipaz at rest in a bed of red coral can be passed within arm's reach without being seen. It feeds on small fish and crustaceans, which it draws into its snout with a sudden suck, and it is shy.</p> <p>The Mipaz is a creature of innate magic. It can project a current of emotional power into the water around it, not unlike the planar energy that the <a href="Krelling" class="internal-link">Krelling</a> conducts through its tail, and the long filaments at its snout are thought to carry the current as the tail tip does in the Krelling. A human diver feels almost nothing of it, at most a passing unease or a strange calm that fades when he leaves the reef. For the <a href="Krestasi" class="internal-link">Krestasi</a> it is deadly. A Krestasi who enters the waters of a Mipaz is driven mad within moments, loses control of its body and soon dies, and the effect seems to bear on Krestasi biology in particular. It is the main reason why so many Krestasi cannot live on the floor of the Purple Sea or in the ocean around Tal at all, and to the Krestasi the Mipaz is known as the Red Death.</p> <p>The Yppori, and above all the Rytoku seafarers of the Purple Sea, revere and honour the Mipaz as a guardian of their waters. Rytoku fishers will not net or spear one, and leave offerings on the reefs where one is known to live. Some scholars of the Arts of Change compare its red crown to the rarest state in the Circle of <a href="Thandar" class="internal-link">Thandar</a>, fire burning within water, though no elemental is known to be involved. To cut the coral from a living Mipaz, as divers have sometimes done to sell it as jewellery, is held a grave offence in the harbours of the islands, and the coral grows back only slowly.</p>
