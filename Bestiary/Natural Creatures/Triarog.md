%% [[The New World]]  [[Triagal]] [[Kavi]] [[Senso]] [[Chianism]] [[Dueren]] %% <div class="wiki-infobox" style="overflow: hidden; display: flex; flex-direction: column;"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Triarog.jpg" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;"> <small><i>A feral Triarog</i></small> </div> <p>The Triarog are a family of massive, bat-like predators native to the new world. They are found across <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a>, <a href="Bulug" class="internal-link">Bulug</a>, <a href="Verzi" class="internal-link">Verzi</a> and <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a> and on essentially all of the smaller islands around them, though none are known on the Pear Isles. There are two kinds of Triarog, the standard feral Triarog described here and the sentient <a href="Triagal" class="internal-link">Triagal</a>, who belong to the same race and usually surround themselves with broods of Triarog servants.</p> <p>A Triarog measures between one and 2.8 metres in head-and-body length, with a wingspan of three to five metres. It is covered in dark fur, with large ears and wings of leathery membrane whose tips end in great hooked claws. Triarogs are predators that hunt in the dark, and they gather in great numbers where the hunting is good.</p> <p>The best known haunt of the Triarog is the Ghostlands of Peadyr. They hunt and roam there in great number, and to enter the Ghostlands is to meet essentially certain death. The <a href="Senso" class="internal-link">Senso</a>, who believe the Ghostlands to be where evildoers wander after death and who sometimes exile criminals into them, thereby send them to the Triarog, and the creatures may go some way to explaining the tales of terrible beasts that the Pea tell of the region.</p> <p>The Rele, a tribe of the <a href="Kavi" class="internal-link">Kavi</a> Federation, were known for hunting in the dark in imitation of the Triarog, but they only imitate the creature. The settlers of the Old World have met it as well. <a href="Dueren" class="internal-link">Dueren</a>, the colony of the Meadi on Giselland, has already had to fight off a swarm of Triarog from the western part of the island.</p>
