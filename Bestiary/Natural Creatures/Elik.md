<div class="wiki-infobox" style="overflow: hidden; display: flex; flex-direction: column;"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Elik.png" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;"> <small><i>An Elik</i></small> </div> <p>The Elik is a winged reptile of the deep jungles of Kaito, a predator that lives in the canopy and the broken forests of the Xermesis foothills. It is about the size of a large hound, with a wingspan of some four metres, and it stands low on powerful hind legs, folding its wings along its flanks. Its body is covered in broad, leaf shaped plates of mottled green and yellow veining, so that an Elik crouching on a mossy branch is easily taken for a clump of foliage. Its tall crest is banded in yellow and black and marked with a dark ring like an eye, which it raises to startle rivals and attackers, and its jaws are lined with dense rows of short red teeth.</p> <p>The Elik hunts from ambush, dropping onto its prey from above or striking from the leaf litter. It can fly, though clumsily and only for short distances, and it more often glides from tree to tree. It feeds on birds, gliding mammals and the young of the <a href="Yppo" class="internal-link">Yppo</a>, which makes it the most feared of the few predators of the great running birds, and the Al Ypp breeders of Byamon kill any Elik that strays near their stock.</p> <p>Foreigners sometimes mistake the Elik for a young dragon, but it is only an animal, if a dangerous one. The Yppori tell that the Elik is the jungle itself grown hungry, a thing that looks like a tree and bites like a trap.</p>
