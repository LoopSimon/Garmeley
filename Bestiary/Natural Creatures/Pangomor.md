<p>The Pangomor is a massive scaled mammal native to mainland <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a> and the adjacent lands of <a href="Verzi" class="internal-link">Verzi</a>, representing an evolutionary lineage derived from the common pangolin but grown to a scale that places it among the largest land animals of the known world, dwarfing a standing human many times over. The Pangomors body is entirely covered in broad overlapping plates of dense keratin that have proven resistant to virtually all conventional weapons and to the claws and teeth of every predator in Aisligos save the bravest <a href="Spinemarder" class="internal-link">Spinemarders</a>. The Pangomor moves on four heavy limbs, is slow to anger, and in its natural state grazes on vegetation and insects, producing milk sufficient to sustain young for extended periods and exhibiting a notable capacity to form lasting bonds with individual humans who handle it consistently over time.</p>

 <p>The domestication of the Pangomor by the <a href="Narzoi" class="internal-link">Narzoi</a> peoples of Verzi and mainland Aisligos represents one of the defining developments in the history of the continent, providing a single animal capable of fulfilling the roles of horse, ox and elephant simultaneously, none of which are native to Aisligos and signifying the difference in the more tribal narzoi societies of Peadyr and the organised realms of Aisligos and Verzi. Pangomor are used for heavy transport, agricultural labour and as mounts, are milked regularly, and are occasionally slaughtered for meat though this is uncommon given the value of a living animal. The bond that forms between a Pangomor and its keeper is widely attested and has been described in Ai accounts across cultures as something closer to partnership than ownership, with individual animals recognising their handlers across years of separation.</p> 
 <p>Two principal subspecies have emerged through geography and selective breeding. The Elazi Pangomor, native to the central regions of mainland Aisligos, is smaller and faster, with a more aggressive disposition that has made it the preferred mount in warfare and rapid overland travel. The Jali Pangomor of the southern interior of Aisligos and Verzi grows to considerably greater size, is more docile, and forms the backbone of the famous Jali Caravans that have connected the interior settlements of <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a> for centuries. Within <a href="Adate" class="internal-link">Adate</a> culture the Pangomor has achieved a prominence that extends well beyond utility: the Pangomor Knights of Adate are among the most formidable and recognisable martial institutions in the continent, and the image of an armoured rider atop a plated Elazi Pangomor has become one of the defining symbols of Adate power and identity.</p>
