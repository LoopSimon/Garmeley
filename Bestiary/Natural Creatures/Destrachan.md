<p>The Destrachan is a species of underland beast that can be found in all parts of Inara, usually dwelling at significant levels underground avoiding sunlight at all costs. They are bipedal reptilians with a unique largely featureless head with the exception of a massive circular mouth and large ear structures, from head to tail tip an adult male Destrachan is about 3 metres long weighing approximately 1800 kilogram. The Destrachan makes an appearance in urien author <a href="Hubert of Tanverley" class="internal-link">Hubert of Tanverleys</a> fifth part of his Bestiary Series, describing them ultimately as "a beast most foul with the uncompromising beauty only found by a predator of the dark". </p>
<p>Destrachan usually resemble simple minded animals but use surprisingly crafty and almost cruel hunting tactics, regularly playing with their food and keeping it alive to various stages. The unique biology of a Destrachan allows it to survive on decaying and rotting flesh without serious impact on their health and ability to hunt further.</p>
<p>The striking feature of the beast is its reliant on sound, their complex, three-part ears can supposedly adjust to be more or less sensitive to different soundwaves, allowing them partial protection from sonic attacks while also letting them navigate by a sense of hearing more precise than almost any surface creatures eyesight. The natural blindness of the destrachan also affords them various protections as they are essentially immune to many attacks based on light that would usually work effectively in beasts of the underlands like the Diremole.</p>
