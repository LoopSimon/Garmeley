<p>The Frugg is a small primate found throughout the forests, canopies and deep jungle undergrowth of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a>, southern <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a> and the islands of Bulug and Verzi, making it one of the most widely distributed mammals in greater Aisligos. It is lean and fast moving, with bare pink red skin on the face and extremities, coarse grey brown fur across the body, and an open, prominent dentition that lends the animal an aggressive appearance consistent with its temperament. Its most remarkable feature is a dense crest of the Velena Plant growing from the skin of its back and shoulders, a symbiotic organism that has become so thoroughly integrated into the Frugg's anatomy over thousands of years of co-evolution that the boundary between host and plant is no longer easily defined. The Velena crest is believed to have originally allowed the Frugg's cave-dwelling ancestors to process an atmospheric environment too thin for unassisted survival on the surface, enabling the species to colonise the trees and forest floors it now dominates.</p> <p>Whether the Frugg and the Velena Plant retain a dependency on one another or have each become capable of independent survival is a question naturalists have not resolved, the symbiosis having proceeded long enough that neither organism presents as it would have before the relationship began. The Frugg is an opportunistic omnivore and can be aggressive when cornered or when defending territory, but it is the Velena crest rather than the animal's behaviour that has attracted most cultural attention. Within <a href="Chianism" class="internal-link">Chianism</a> as practised on Peadyr, the Frugg is sometimes associated with nature spirits, the living plant growing from a living creature read as evidence of the world's interior vitality made visible, and sightings of large Frugg in old growth forest are occasionally treated as significant by local Chianist communities.</p>
