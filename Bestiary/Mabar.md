<p>The Mabar of <a href="Qalzan" class="internal-link">Qalzan</a> are a species unique to the southern continent of Qalzan, where they have highly adapted to life around the icy wastes. They are spherically shaped "ball-like" creatures whose mouth makes up most of their body, their extremely sturdy skin contains an intricate network of cellular membrane, supported by thick fat sheets, that acts as the Mabars brain.</p>
