<p>The Pukluwuk is a hunchbacked creature, vaguely resembling the statue of a goblinoid with characteristically large and venomous quills upon its back, similar to a hedgehog. They dwell in caves and mountainous, elevated areas and possess naturally manifesting magical abilities often classified as necromantic, being able to infuse the bodies of recently passed away creatures with purpose and semi autonomous life, often by the help of the quills. Many Pukluwuk have been spotted in Ilrakhan, but reports of similar creatures in Behera are substantiated as well.</p>
<p>They seem of middling intellect and avoid forming relationships, not much if anything is known about their lifecycle but there are reports of a single pukluwuk existing as a threat or folktale around a community over many generations, indicating a long natural lifecycle. They are elusive, isolationist yet sedentary creatures and often hide away from larger groups of humans while menacing smaller communities in the night, your typical tales of stealing newborns and killing livestock are found everywhere where the Pukluwuk is mentioned.</p>
<p>A unique aspect of the Pukluwuk is its natural proclivity to dead organic matter and necromantic magicks, they often keep company with undead animals, forming cult like protective circles in their lairs made up of various undead beasts. The quills of a Pukluwuk are said to be charged with a venom, able to infest and pass on its necromantic capabilities, are are the main magical outlet of the monster, usually worth a fortune in various academic and especially alchemical circuits. </p>
<p>The Zoologist <a href="Hubert of Tanverley" class="internal-link">Hubert of Tanverley</a>, upon tracking down and killing a Pukluwuk in the Trollback mountains with the help of a group of adventurers, dissected the beast and interviewed the Vihri Alchemist Nessa Arevi, a prominent member of the <a href="The Alchemists Guild" class="internal-link">alchemists Guild</a>, who confirmed that the process with which the Pukluwuk infused its victims with necromantic energy was indeed naturally occurring <a href="Alchemy" class="internal-link">Kynosis</a>, fundamentally not dissimilar from drinking an arcane potion.</p>
