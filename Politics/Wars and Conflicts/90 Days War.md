<div class="wiki-infobox"> <h4 class="wiki-header">90 Days War</h4> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Date</strong> <span>713PA</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Location</strong> <span>The Eld Valley, ending at Silverfields</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Result</strong> <span>Inconclusive.<br>Truce after ninety days.<br>No change of borders.</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row" style="display: block;"> <strong>Belligerents</strong><br> <em>Gelmsburg:</em><br> Kingdom of Gelmsburg<br> <br> <em>Eldmar:</em><br> Kingdom of Eldmar<br> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row" style="display: block;"> <strong>Notable Casualties</strong><br> <a href="George the Brave" class="internal-link">George the Brave</a> </div> </div> <p>The 90 Days War was the attempt of the young kingdom of <a href="Gelmsburg" class="internal-link">Gelmsburg</a> to bring the Counter Queen of <a href="Eldmar" class="internal-link">Eldmar</a> to heel. It was fought in the Eld Valley, and it ended inconclusively after the Battle of Silverfields and the death of the mage knight <a href="George the Brave" class="internal-link">George the Brave</a>.</p> <h2>Background</h2> <p>Ten years after the Splintering of <a href="Urland" class="internal-link">Urland</a>, neither the crown of Gelmsburg nor the crown of Eldmar had given way. Jorwyle of Gelm had been crowned King in Gelmsburg in 703PA by George the Brave, who hoped for a quick reunion of all Urland, and he had claimed the title of King of Urland, which no one acknowledged. The lords of the Eld Valley had refused his call, beaten his northern knights with an army raised in haste, and set Theara of Hornsley on a throne of their own under the Charter of the Vale. For Gelmsburg and for George the Eldmari crown was the plain obstacle to a united Urland, and in 713PA Gelmsburg went to war to remove it.</p> <h2>The War</h2> <p>The Gelmish host came south across the Redwood into the Eld Valley. Gelmsburg sent its heavy cavalry, and George rode with it at the head of the mage knights. Eldmar answered with the levy of the valley, called by the Council of the Vale, and with its bowmen. The war was a short one, ninety days from the crossing of the Redwood to the truce that gave it its name, and neither side was able to take and hold ground from the other.</p>  <p>The one great battle of the war was fought at Silverfields, the plain in the northern valley below the Redwood, where the host of Eldmar met the Gelmish advance. George the Brave, a mage knight of around fifty years, fell there leading the mage knights, and the Gelmish host lost heart with him. Both hosts withdrew from the field, and neither could claim to have won it.</p> <h2>Aftermath</h2> <p>A truce was made at the end of the ninety days, with no change of borders. Gelmsburg never recognised Theara I or any of the rulers of Eldmar after her, and the Gelmish kings still hold the title of King of Urland, dusted off whenever Eldmar gives offence. With George dead, the hope of a quick reunion of Urland died with him. The valley kept its independence and its Charter, and the battle was remembered in Eldmar as proof that the Counter Queen could stand against the Lords of Gelm.</p> <p>The rivalry outlived the war. When the two kingdoms joined the <a href="League of the Gryphon" class="internal-link">League of the Gryphon</a> in 841PA against the hobgoblins of the west, they marched together, but the old enmity went with them.</p>
