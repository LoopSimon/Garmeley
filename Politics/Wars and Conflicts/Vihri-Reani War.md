<p>The Vihri-Reani War, also called the Pilv Valley War or simply the Rean War, was fought from 291PA to 306PA between the young kingdom of <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Vihr</a> and the Kingdom of <a href="Rean" class="internal-link">Rean</a>. It was the first great war that Vihr fought against a foreign kingdom. It cost Vihr its Ras, Viago III, who fell at the Battle of the Pilv in 299PA, but under his brother <a href="Jasper II" class="internal-link">Jasper II</a> Vihr won it, and the Peace of the Pilv in 306PA gave it the Pilv Valley.</p>  
<h2>Background</h2>  
<p>Rean was an old and wealthy kingdom of the Stormy Sea, ruled from Reanport over a ring of princely vassals, and its people kept the faith of the <a href="Kindly Lords" class="internal-link">Kindly Lords</a>. For centuries it had dealt with the southern Vihri as a trading partner, an occasional enemy and a rival, always from a position of strength. The unification of the Vihri under the House of Zav changed that. King Salkon I du Rean saw a Gethric kingdom rising on his northern border, still digesting its conquests, and in 291PA he declared war on it before it could grow any stronger.</p>  
<h2>Course of the War</h2>  
<p>For its first eight years the war went Rean's way. The Rean host advanced up the Pilv into the hills of the southern Expanse, and the lords of the south bore the brunt of it while the crown gathered the strength of the realm. In 299PA Viago III led the Vihri host south to hold the river line and was killed at the Battle of the Pilv. His younger brother Jasper, who commanded the reserve, rallied the army, held the crossings, and was acclaimed Ras in the field.</p>  
<p>Jasper II changed the war. He refused to meet the Rean host in open battle and fought it instead on the river, in the hills and along its supply lines, while the lords of the south raided across the border. Over seven years the war turned slowly, and as Rean's princes began to count the cost of a war they had not wanted, Salkon I lost the will to continue it.</p>  
<h2>Peace of the Pilv</h2>  
<p>The war ended in 306PA with the Peace of the Pilv. Rean ceded to Vihr the Pilv Valley between the Grevi and the Trollback Mountains, a corridor of rolling hills, rich mines and good arable land, together with the ferry town on the Pilv that would later become <a href="Fairriver" class="internal-link">Fairriver</a>. The valley has been part of Vihr ever since, and it now forms the south of <a href="Selbia" class="internal-link">Selbia</a>.</p>  
<h2>Aftermath</h2>  
<p>The war proved that the new kingdom could survive the loss of its king, and it gave the House of Zav a long peace. Jasper II settled the valley with Vihri farmers and miners and gave its churches and lands generously to the Church of Iztaw, and he rewarded those who had served him, among them Belia zi Syd, whose family he made Gri of Sydrow. For Rean the war was the first sign of a long decline that ended with the fall of the kingdom in 622PA. Its successors, Grevenna above all, have counted the valley as part of Pilvia ever since, though the claim has not been pressed for centuries.</p>
