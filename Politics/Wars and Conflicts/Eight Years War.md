<div class="wiki-infobox"> <h4 class="wiki-header">Eight Years War</h4> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Date</strong> <span>644PA - 652PA</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Location</strong> <span>Trollback Mountains, Garmeley</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Result</strong> <span>Garmish Victory.<br>End of the line of Alfin.<br>The Kingdom of Garmeley becomes a Duchy.</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row" style="display: block;"> <strong>Belligerents</strong><br> <em>Vihr:</em><br> Kingdom of Vihr (House of Worocek)<br> <br> <em>Garmeley:</em><br> Kingdom of Garmeley (House of Garm)<br> Duchy of Garmeley (House of Redflow) </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row" style="display: block;"> <strong>Commanders</strong><br> <em>Vihr:</em><br> Boris II "Hornsword" (died 648PA)<br> Valena I<br> <br> <em>Garmeley:</em><br> Ahlef II † (648PA)<br> Tyla of Aldale </div> </div> <p>The Eight Years War was fought between the Kingdom of <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Vihr</a> and the Kingdom of <a href="Garmeley" class="internal-link">Garmeley</a> from 644PA until 652PA, in and around the <a href="Trollbacks" class="internal-link">Trollback Mountains</a> that divide the two. It ended in a Garmish victory, but it cost Garmeley its king and brought to an end the line of Alfin, which had held the Riverthrone since the first Riverking in 51PA.</p> <h2>Background</h2> <p>The passes of the Trollbacks carry the shortest road between the Vihri crownlands and the river Trinmo, and in 644PA they lay in Garmish hands. Garmeley was old and rich, and at the time ruled by King Ahlef II of House Garm, the last of the Riverkings, while the Rascy zi Vihr was ruled by Ras Boris II "Hornsword" of the House of Worocek, who sent his host across the mountains in that year and began the war.</p> <h2>The War</h2> <p>For four years the Vihri held the initiative. They crossed the Trollbacks by the pass that Fellpass Castle watches, and the fighting moved through the foothills and the valleys beneath them, where the Garmish levies and knights stood on their own ground but had to be raised lord by lord.</p> <p>In 648PA the two armies met at Greyfields where King Ahlef II fell in the battle, and the line of Alfin ended with him. Ras Boris II died in the same year, and his successor Valena I took the throne of Zavlech and the war with it.</p> <p>Garmeley was left without a king and without an heir of the old line. The next in succession, Tyla of Aldale, refused the crown and proclaimed herself Duchess of Garmeley, so that no rival claim could split the realm while the Vihri were still inside its borders. Her house took the name of Redflow after the Capital City and seat of power of the former Riverkings.</p> <p>The last four years were a mountain war. Duchess Tyla did not seek another great battle, and the Garmish held the passes and the castles at their mouths while the Vihri, with a new ruler and a long road behind them, could not break them. In 652PA the Vihri withdrew across the Trollbacks and the war was over.</p> <h2>Aftermath</h2> <p>Garmeley kept its lands but not its crown. The Riverthrone at Redflow passed to the Dukes of House Redflow, and the realm that had been a kingdom for almost six centuries became a duchy, largely a nominative change at first. The war ended in the same year in which Theris I of Pikris united the Princedom of <a href="Hainach" class="internal-link">Hainach</a>, whose princes had spent the war years fighting each other and took no part in the quarrel between Vihr and Garmeley when otherwise they may have supported their northern cousins.</p> <p>The war is remembered by two saints of the Gethric canon. <a href="Lyandra" class="internal-link">Lyandra of Fairriver</a>, a young Vihri Knight of the Silver Wheel, was an advocate for peace in the war, and the abbey town she later founded at Fairriver remained a haven for those displaced by war. <a href="Dorian" class="internal-link">Dorian the Kind</a>, a Garmish commoner born in 641PA, was three years old when the war began, and met Lyandra in the 660s, he spoke in life of the events that occurred in his childhood home as being deeply touching and formative in his later endeavours for peace in Ur.</p> <p>More than two and a half centuries later, in the <a href="Garmish Succession Crisis" class="internal-link">Garmish Succession Crisis</a>, knights of Vihr crossed the Trollbacks into eastern Garmeley again, among them <a href="Jakub Ralpia" class="internal-link">Jakub zi Ralpia</a>, Captain of the <a href="Knights of the Unicorn" class="internal-link">Knights of the Unicorn</a>, this time on the side of the Green Banner League.</p>
