<p>The Revolution of Rox was a religious revolt of the Kheri of the eastern Bay of Azuv against their overlords in the <a href="Azuvke" class="internal-link">Khatrym of Azuvke</a>, fought from 560PA until 564PA. Led by the siblings <a href="Rox" class="internal-link">Rox</a> and <a href="Fenhir" class="internal-link">Fenhir</a> of the Sincali Moors, it ended Azuvken rule over the eastern shore, which the Khatrym had held since its Golden Age, and founded the Principality of <a href="Roxeria" class="internal-link">Roxeria</a>. The rebels renounced the <a href="Kethricism" class="internal-link">kethric</a> faith for the <a href="Javalim" class="internal-link">Javalim</a>, and the revolution is remembered in Roxeria as the moment its people became the chosen of the One.</p>  
<h2>Background</h2>  
<p>Azuvke had ruled the eastern shore of the bay since the 2nd century PA, when it conquered the lands of modern Roxeria and destroyed the Bihra <a href="Vol" class="internal-link">Empire of Vol</a>. By the 6th century its hold had weakened. The Red Pestilence, first seen in Azuvke in 488PA, had emptied many of its eastern towns, and the Khatrym had lost the <a href="Ganoa Valley" class="internal-link">Ganoa Valley</a> to the <a href="Ekkam" class="internal-link">Omna of Ekkam</a> as early as 433PA The rise of the <a href="Luchun Khaganate" class="internal-link">Luchun Khaganate</a> in the east made matters worse. After the Khaganate subjugated Ekkam in 551PA, Azuvke drew men and coin from its eastern cities to guard the frontier, and refugees, many of them <a href="Ganoan" class="internal-link">Ganoans</a>, crowded into the towns around Mount Javal.</p>  
<p>In the hills around the mountain an older faith had survived the centuries of Kheri rule. Its shamans and druids revered the One, kept the Pillar on Mount Javal and the Tears on Tazil, and were persecuted by the kethric priesthood as heathens. It was in this world that Fenhir, a seer of the Sincali Moors, began to preach. In 558PA he was proclaimed the Prophet of the Javalim in the eastern cities, and his teaching, that the One had foreseen a chosen people on the eastern shore and would give them a hero from his own blood, spread quickly among Kheri townsfolk and the keepers of the old faith alike.</p>  
<h2>The Revolution</h2>  
<p>The revolution began in Bakalas in 560PA, when the kethric authorities seized Fenhir and condemned him to burn as a heretic. His younger sister Rox led the crowd that broke up the pyre and pulled him from the flames, and the city rose with her. Roxeria remembers the burning as the cinder that set the revolution on fire. Within the year the Azuvken garrisons had been driven from Bakalas and the surrounding country, and the kethric temples of the eastern shore had been torn down or given over to the One.</p>  
<p>The decisive struggle was the siege of Furel, the chief Azuvken stronghold of the east, which held out from 561PA to 562PA. When it fell, Rox had the spears of its defenders planted along the city walls, and Furel has been known as the City of Spears ever since. Throughout the war Rox fought at the head of her people with Livar, a massive sword forged from a single piece of meteorite, whose weight she could change at will, and her enemies came to call her the Annihilator and the blade the Nullblade. In 564PA the Khat Tyrvan II sent an army by sea to retake the coast, led by Nitol the Good, a kethric paladin and the greatest fighter of the Azuvken side. Rox defeated it on the shore near Alkon and killed Nitol with Livar in the battle. With its eastern garrisons gone and the Luchun threat still in the east, Azuvke made peace in the same year and gave up its hold on the eastern bay.</p>  
<h2>Aftermath</h2>  
<p>The lands freed in the revolution became the Principality of Roxeria, with Rox as its first ruler and Fenhir as Great Seer of the Javalim. The Roxanae, once counted among the Kurini, renounced the kethric faith, and over the following centuries grew into a people distinct from their cousins across the bay. Those Kheri who held to the kethric faith left the new realm in the years after 564PA, many of them for the shores of Lake Ganoa, where their descendants form the kethric community of the <a href="Ganoa Valley" class="internal-link">Ganoa Valley</a>. Many Ganoans who had fought beside the Roxanae took up the Javalim and stayed, and are the ancestors of Roxeria's Ganoan minority.</p>  
<p>After Rox stepped into the Pillar of the One in 600PA, Fenhir proclaimed the principality the Chosen Realm of Roxeria, ruled by her heirs as the house chosen by the One. In the kethric west the revolution was remembered as the great apostasy of the age, and some historians count it among the causes that led to the founding of the <a href="Kherovan Inquisition" class="internal-link">Kherovan Inquisition</a> in 585PA. Livar now rests in the Siblings Tomb near Bakalas, whose great stone murals tell the story of the revolution. Azuvke and Roxeria have traded and quarrelled across the bay ever since, but the wound between the cousins of differing faiths has never fully closed.</p>
