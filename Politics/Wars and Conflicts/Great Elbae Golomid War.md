<p>The Great Elbae Golomid War, fought from 871PA to 893PA, was the war between the <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae Sultanate</a> and the <a href="Golomid" class="internal-link">Golomid Empire</a> of the Krestasi, fought around Il-Haena and across the waters of the Summer Sea. It ended inconclusively in the Peace at Algolom, and the agreements made at its end left the Golomid on amicable terms with both the Sultanate and the Qahori Federation.</p>
<h2>Origins</h2>
<p>The <a href="Risen Reef" class="internal-link">Risen Reef</a> completed its ascension to the surface south of Il-Haena in 857PA, the first large scale contact between the Krestasi and the surface dwellers, and in 860PA tensions between the Reef and the traders of the Niryd Sea began to escalate. In 864PA Qarzi diplomats successfully established an embassy on the Reef. In 869PA relations between the Qarzi and the Golomid worsened following a string of communication-based incidents, and in 870PA Elbae traders stranded on the Reef and were never heard from again. In 871PA the diplomacy between the Qarzi and the Reef broke down fully, and the war began.</p>
<h2>The War</h2>
<p>The war lasted twenty-two years and was fought across Il-Haena and the sea around it. It ended in 893PA with the Peace at Algolom, without a decisive victory for either power.</p>
<h2>The Orcs and the War</h2>
<p>It was the first time since the Destruction of Qa that the Orcs of Il-Haena and the Archipelago were caught between two great powers. Neither the endurance of the First Path nor the embrace of Zolra seemed to offer an answer, and it was in the 870s, in the shadow of the war, that the <a href="Path of the Third Moon" class="internal-link">Path of the Third Moon</a> took shape as a distinct denomination.</p>
