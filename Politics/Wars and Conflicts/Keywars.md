<p>The Wars over the Keys of Heaven, describing the great conflict embroiling most of Nirydia for centuries concerning the pieces of the mythical Keystar, found by the legendary figure Genevi north of the Ronra mountains in the 7th century BA.</p>
<p>While the conflict and the greater struggle to obtain the Keys has been going on for over a millenia, the term Keywars usually specifically refers to the ongoing series of conflicts between the powers of eastern Nirydia, dominated by the Minotaur Lords of the great <a href="Gaal" class="internal-link">Gaal</a>.</p>
