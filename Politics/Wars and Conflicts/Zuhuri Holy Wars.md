<p>The Zuhuri Holy Wars were a series of five religious wars fought in eastern Nirydia between 620PA and 792PA by the realms of <a href="Zuhurism" class="internal-link">Zuhurism</a> against heretics and unbelievers. Fought in the name of the security of pilgrims and the holy sites, and above all in the Templi Heights around the Spring of Light, they began as wars against witches and heresy and ended as the first clash between the two great powers of Nirydia, the <a href="Twintail Imperium" class="internal-link">Twintail Imperium</a> and <a href="Gaal" class="internal-link">Gaal</a>.</p>  
<h2>First Holy War</h2>  
<p>The First Holy War, begun in 620PA, was fought against the Peruxae witches of <a href="Sihilixar" class="internal-link">Sihilixar</a>. In its course the southern forest of Sihilixar was burned down, leaving the land now known as the Ashlands, and Volun's Ward was raised, a magical barrier that brings nightmares and curses upon any who enter the forest. The construction of the <a href="Vimgalab Monsis" class="internal-link">Vimgalab Monsis</a> in Nessos began in 630PA in the aftermath of the war. In this and the following war the <a href="Zepria" class="internal-link">Zeprian Empire</a> was inarguably the main force of the faithful, its armies crossing the Gavivag Bay from its city of Trilom.</p>
<h2>Second and Third Holy Wars</h2>  
<p>The Second and Third Holy Wars, begun in 645PA and 701PA, were fought for a single cause, the heresy of <a href="Umo" class="internal-link">Umo</a>. In its later years Umo had fallen under the witch king Zologos, who commanded hordes of the undead and crowned himself the reborn Pen Zuhur. In the Second War the <a href="Zepria" class="internal-link">Zeprian Empire</a> fought alongside the Pagri realms in what it called the Pagri Campaigns, but quarrels between the allies over the conduct of the war weakened the alliance, and in 657PA the Zeprian army suffered a heavy defeat against Zologos at the Battle of Brina. Zologos fell at the end of the Third Holy War, and the lands of Umo were divided between the new realms of <a href="Uriston" class="internal-link">Uriston</a> and <a href="Nessos" class="internal-link">Nessos</a>.</p>  
<h2>Fourth Holy War</h2>  
<p>The Fourth Holy War was declared in 751PA by the <a href="League of Aviv" class="internal-link">League of Aviv</a>, an alliance of <a href="Melgard" class="internal-link">Melgard</a>, Nessos and <a href="Cirinzel" class="internal-link">Cirinzel</a> joined by Uriston, against the young realm of <a href="Labella" class="internal-link">Labella</a>, which threatened to lay claim to the Spring of Light in the Templi Heights. The Gorati of <a href="Zepria" class="internal-link">Zepria</a> fought alongside the Pagri realms, the last of the Holy Wars in which a Gorati realm took part. The Spring of Light remains today under the guardianship of <a href="Mirel" class="internal-link">Mirel</a>.</p>  
<h2>Fifth Holy War</h2>  
<p>The Fifth Holy War, begun in 777PA, grew out of a dispute over the rights of pilgrims in southern Labella and the petitions of wealthy pilgrims and traders to force open the road west, and soon became an effort to convert Labella by force. When Labella appealed to the Twintail Imperium for help, Gaal in turn sent help to the League, turning a regional war of faith into a great war between the two awakened powers of Nirydia. It ended in 792PA with the defeat of the League by the Twintail legions at the Battle of Minea, after which the League of Aviv was effectively disbanded.</p>
