<p>The Wars of Baeling Succession are a series of armed conflicts between the Kingdom of <a href="Baeling" class="internal-link">Baeling</a>, which claims the legacy of the fallen <a href="Baeling Empire" class="internal-link">Baeling Empire</a>, and the Kingdom of <a href="Juelfluk" class="internal-link">Juelfluk</a>. They began in 684PA and have been fought on and off ever since across <a href="Trykkerog" class="internal-link">Trykkerog</a> and the Torn Sea.</p>
<h2>Background</h2>
<p>After the fall of the Baeling Empire in 590PA, House Udry under Rossgi I declared its hegemony over the lands and peoples of the Malksbay in 617PA. For a long time the legendary House of Baeling was thought extinct, until Sivdil the Tall, the leader of the Runeblade Mercenary Company, laid claim to the Baeling name in 684PA.</p>
<p>After uniting the lands of Sallesand and Alsebyn under his newly declared Baeling Kingdom, Sivdil announced his claim to all the former lands of the fallen Empire, including the entirety of Trykkerog. The claim led to a series of wars between his new realm and the other powers of the region, first and foremost the Kings and Queens of Juelfluk.</p>
<h2>The 9th War</h2>
<p>The 9th War of Baeling Succession is the most recent of the conflicts. It broke out in 911PA and ended only in 916PA, and it was fought in a period of Baeling decline over the city of Troese across Melmers Bay. Although he lacked the support of his eastern lords because of internal strife, King <a href="Varald I" class="internal-link">Varald I</a> of Baeling held Troese through its siege against the forces of Queen <a href="Madeley IV" class="internal-link">Madeley IV</a> of Juelfluk.</p>
