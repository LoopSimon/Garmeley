<div class="wiki-infobox"> <h4 class="wiki-header">War of 896</h4> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Date</strong> <span>896PA</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Location</strong> <span>Turv, on the Bend of the Trinmo</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Result</strong> <span>Gisvale victory.<br>Turv passes to Gisvale.<br>Gelmsburg does not recognise the loss.</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row" style="display: block;"> <strong>Belligerents</strong><br> <em>Gisvale:</em><br> Archduchy of Gisvale<br> Rebels of Turv<br> <br> <em>Gelmsburg:</em><br> Kingdom of Gelmsburg<br> </div> </div> <p>The War of 896 was a short conflict between the Archduchy of <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a> and the Kingdom of <a href="Gelmsburg" class="internal-link">Gelmsburg</a> over the city of Turv, the last of the three Bendburgs on the Bend of the Trinmo. It was decided by a coup in the city and a march of the Archduke's knights, and it left Turv in the hands of Gisvale and Gelmsburg with a grievance that has not been forgotten.</p>  <p>Turv is the southernmost of the Bendburgs, a town of the Turvi, the people who gave their name to the old Turvik faith and who still live on the lower Bend beside the Gismer. It was reconquered in 843PA at the end of the First War of the Gryphon, and Gelmsburg held it for the half century that followed, the chief prize of that war. A Gelmish city among the Bendburgs of the Trinmo was never an easy neighbour for Gisvale.</p> <h2>The War</h2> <p>In 896PA the city rose against Gelmish rule. The coup was a city wide affair, and  supported by Gisvale. When the old order of the city had been overthrown, the warriors and knights of the Archduke marched in, announcing that they had come to save Turv. They took the city under the protection of the Archduke and did not leave. The whole affair was short. Greater Meadrath took no part in the war.</p>  <p>Turv is held by Gisvale today. Gelmsburg has never accepted the loss and still lists the city among the lands of the Crown, and relations between the two realms have been abysmal ever since. The arrest of the Courtmage <a href="Kabernos" class="internal-link">Kabernos</a> at Gelmsburg in 913PA deepened them, and each side regards the other as the one that has wronged it. Talk of retaking Turv has never died in Gelmsburg, and an attempt to reconquer it is a possibility that Gisvale watches for.</p>
