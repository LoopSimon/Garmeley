<p>The League is a great alliance between the Kingdoms of <a href="Gelmsburg" class="internal-link">Gelmsburg</a> and <a href="Eldmar" class="internal-link">Eldmar</a>, as well as the Order of <a href="Urbridge" class="internal-link">Urbridge</a> named in honour of the Gryphon of the fallen Kingdom of <a href="Urland" class="internal-link">urland</a>. Its primary goal is the destruction of the "Hobgoblin Infestation" and the Federation of <a href="Ak-Saray" class="internal-link">Ak-Saray</a>, in western <a href="Ur" class="internal-link">Ur</a>, as well as the restoration of its western lands under <a href="Urien" class="internal-link">urien</a> rule. During the <a href="Wars of the Gryphon" class="internal-link">Wars of the Gryphon</a>, the League managed to reconquer significant territories from the hobgoblins but was both times unable to press any advantages into deeper territories west of the Trinmo. Beyond its common goal, the League has suffered from varying rivalries and contested interests between its members, specifically Eldmar and Gelmsburg, in recent decades attempts by individual leaders within the realms of the League to begin a third conquest into hobgoblin territory have failed due to political issues.</p>
