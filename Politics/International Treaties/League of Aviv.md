<p>The League of Aviv was a lose military alliance in eastern Nirydia during the 8th century that led the united Zuhuri forces during the 4th Holy War against the realm of Labella. Initially it only consisted of Melgard, Nessos and Cirinzel, however as the Shirin of the Twintail joined to support Labella the mighty giant of the far east, the Empire of Gaal, joined the Leagues forces in turn.</p>
<p>The League of Aviv was effectively disbanded with the end of the 4th Holy War at the Battle of Minea in 760PA with the defeat of the zuhuri forces at the hands of the Twintail legions. It is primarily remembered as a shift in nirydian politics towards a contest of superpowers between Gaal and the Twintail from the religious holy wars of the last few centuries.</p>
