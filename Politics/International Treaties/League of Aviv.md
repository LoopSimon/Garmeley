<p>The League of Aviv was a lose military alliance in eastern <a href="Nirydia" class="internal-link">Nirydia</a> during the 7th century that led the united <a href="Zuhurism" class="internal-link">zuhuri</a> forces during the 3rd and 4th Holy War against the <a href="" class="internal-link"></a> of <a href="Sihilixar" class="internal-link">Sihilixar</a> and the realm of <a href="Labella" class="internal-link">Labella</a>. Initially it only consisted of <a href="Melgard" class="internal-link">Melgard</a>, <a href="Nessos" class="internal-link"></a> and <a href="Cirinzel" class="internal-link">Cirinzel</a>, however as the Shirin of the <a href="Twintail Imperium" class="internal-link">Twintail</a> joined to support Labella the mighty giant of the far east, the <a href="Gaal" class="internal-link">Empire of Gaal</a>, joined the Leagues forces in turn.</p>
<p>The League of Aviv was effectively disbanded with the end of the 4th Holy War at the Battle of Minea in 692PA with the defeat of the zuhuri forces at the hands of the Twintail legions. It is primarily remembered as a shift in nirydian politics towards a contest of superpowers between Gaal and the Twintail from the religious holy wars of the last few centuries.</p>
