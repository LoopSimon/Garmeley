<p>The League of Aviv was a lose military alliance in eastern <a href="Nirydia" class="internal-link">Nirydia</a> during the 8th century that led the united <a href="Zuhurism" class="internal-link">zuhuri</a> forces during the Fourth and Fifth Holy Wars against the realm of <a href="Labella" class="internal-link">Labella</a>. Initially it only consisted of <a href="Melgard" class="internal-link">Melgard</a>, <a href="Nessos" class="internal-link">Nessos</a> and <a href="Cirinzel" class="internal-link">Cirinzel</a>, however as the Shirin of the <a href="Twintail Imperium" class="internal-link">Twintail</a> joined to support Labella the mighty giant of the far east, the <a href="Gaal" class="internal-link">Empire of Gaal</a>, joined the Leagues forces in turn.</p>
<p>The League of Aviv was effectively disbanded The Fifth <a href="Zuhuri Holy Wars" class="internal-link">Zuhuri Holy War</a>, which began in 777PA with the defeat of the zuhuri forces at the hands of the Twintail legions. It is primarily remembered as a shift in nirydian politics towards a contest of superpowers between Gaal and the Twintail from the religious holy wars of the last few centuries.</p>
