<div class="wiki-infobox"> <h4 class="wiki-header">The Paper Cities</h4> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Government</strong> <span>Federal Union of City-States</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Capital</strong> <span>Albesan, Ligoggia, Butera<br>(rotating Grand Mayoral Council)</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Primary Religion</strong> <a href="Trinicism" class="internal-link">Trinicism</a> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Primary Culture</strong> <span>Cevoli <a href="Human" class="internal-link">Human</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Official Language</strong> <span>Cevoli</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Preceded by</strong> <span><a href="Battvia" class="internal-link">Battvia</a>,<br><a href="Cevolis" class="internal-link">Kingdom of Cevolis</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Formed</strong> <span>800PA</span> </div> </div> <p>The Paper Cities are a federal union of three city-states situated on the Bay of Butera in eastern Cevolia, <a href="Albesan" class="internal-link">Albesan</a>, <a href="Butera" class="internal-link">Butera</a> and <a href="Ligoggia" class="internal-link">Ligoggia</a>, whose independence from the <a href="Battvia" class="internal-link">Land of Battvia</a> was secured at the end of the Battvian Civil War of 792-800PA. The name was coined for Albesan's enormous printing industry, which made paper itself one of the most significant exports of the Bay. What unites the three cities is not shared governance and their internal politics could not be more different, but a common rejection of Battvian authority and a shared commercial interest in the free flow of trade through the Bay of Butera, one of the wealthiest maritime approaches in western Ilrakhan. The Paper Cities are formally governed through the Grand Mayoral Council, a body whose sessions are as contentious as the cities themselves, and whose decisions have historically reflected the balance of power between Albesan's printing oligarchs, Butera's Excavators Guild and Ligoggia's rather different kind of power structure. Two older city-states: <a href="Mindano" class="internal-link">Mindano</a> to the south and <a href="Bassira" class="internal-link">Bassira</a> to the west, are not members of the Paper Cities but maintain close economic and diplomatic ties, having supported the league militarily in both the Civil War and the Vorellan Wars without being bound by its charter.</p> 
<h2>History</h2> 
<p>The cities of the Bay of Butera had been commercially significant long before their independence. Under the <a href="Cevolis" class="internal-link">Kingdom of Cevolis</a> and later the <a href="Battvia" class="internal-link">Land of Battvia</a>, they contributed enormous tax revenues to the crown in exchange for growing degrees of internal autonomy. The influx of trade generated by <a href="Mario Benvolo" class="internal-link">Mario Benvolo's</a> Great Expedition into the ruins of <a href="Rakh" class="internal-link">Rakh</a> in 580PA transformed the Bay's commercial character entirely as the <a href="Excavators Guild of Butera" class="internal-link">Excavators Guild of Butera</a> monopolized the expedition routes, Albesan's printing houses began producing the academic and cartographic literature that the expeditions generated, and wealth flowed into the Bay at a rate that made the cities increasingly difficult for Battvia to govern without their consent. Prince Anselmo I's Great Privilege, the charter granting the cities significant autonomy and political influence over national affairs in exchange for increased taxation, formalized this arrangement, but also created the mechanism by which the cities would eventually claim their independence. When Prince Guido II "the Lame" refused to honor the Grand Mayoral Council's rights over his marriage arrangements and chose a local noble over the northern candidate they had approved, the western cities rose in revolt. The war lasted eight years. At its end in 800PA, the royalist party signed for peace with the newly liberated Paper Cities.</p> <p>The Vorellan Wars of approximately 910-919PA tested the League's survival when the city of Vorella attempted to secede from Battvia and join the Paper Cities, drawing Battvia and <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a> into a combined military response against the Leagues forces. The war saw the first deployment of gunpowder weapons on Cevolian battlefields: gnomish mortars and the first flintlock rifles in the hands of well-equipped mercenaries and ended with Battvia victorious, Vorella subjugated, and Battvia's economy nearly ruined. The Paper Cities, whose naval position protected them from the worst of the fighting, emerged comparatively intact, though the political relationship with Battvia remains defined by these conflicts.</p> <h2>The Three Cities</h2> <p>For individual articles on the member cities and their governance, see <a href="Albesan" class="internal-link">Albesan</a>, <a href="Butera" class="internal-link">Butera</a> and <a href="Ligoggia" class="internal-link">Ligoggia</a>. The Paper Cities are three distinct political entities that share a flag, a council and a common enemy in Battvian expansionism  but beyond that they share very little. Albesan is governed by its printing oligarchs, Butera by the Excavators Guild, and Ligoggia by whoever currently controls its criminal networks. The Grand Mayoral Councils ability to function depends almost entirely on whether the interests of these three power structures are temporarily aligned. When they are not, the council does not meet.</p>
