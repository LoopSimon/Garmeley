<p>Signed on the 16th of May 900PA in the city of Lotsprings, on the border of the two realms, the Treaty of Lotsprings regulatescustoms and trade between Blijgrat and the Realms of the Meadi Crown, trade relations of the G.U.V. and K.G.W. on the continent and in Sertova & Othirdon, unified charters in international ports for ships of both companies and the colonization of lands west of the Tirean Ocean. Furthermore the treaty essentially guarantees mutual military defence of Company holdings, within international waters, on foreign shores and reaffirms the Continental Borders. </p>
<p>The growing rivalry following the so called "Opening of the West" in the 800s of Meadi and Weadi traders and sailors, as well as common interests and mutual enemies in Sertovi, Othiri, Cevoli and Trykkerogi commercial Unions and traders brought the two dominant entities of the Meadi Empire, encompassing the Kingdom itself as well as all its vassals, and the Blijgrat League, including the incredibly industrious and successful Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel. The meadi Gesellschaft für Westmeadischen Handel was created as a somewhat mirroring entity and unified Company to the G.U.V and for lack of better words, the Ports and Lands of Othirdon, Sertova and all of Aisligos were essentially divided between the two. This alliance not only guaranteed a dominant military union in the wake of potential conflicts for both countries, most importantly the weaker League still worrying about northern invasions from the past, but also an incredibly tight grip on all western Trade that would inform most international policy for both countries in the centuries to come. The Free City of <a href="Zeeport" class="internal-link">Zeeport</a>, tied to both sides, was instrumental in arranging the treaty as a semi neutral party and signed it as well. </p>
<p>The division of the new world itself followed a diagonal line drawn across the old maps from south-west to north-east, assigning Jurland and the lands south of it to the League and the G.U.V., and the lands north of it to the Meadi Crown and the Gesellschaft. Drawn when the new world was barely charted, the line proved a surprisingly even split once the coasts were surveyed, though to the settlers on the ground it leaves the Meadi with no claim to anything near Peadyr.</p>
<p>Following the establishment of settlements and greater colonies in the <a href="The New World" class="internal-link">New World</a>, the Treaty has acted as the foundation for cooperation between the colonial endeavours of <a href="Faarhovn" class="internal-link">Faarhovn</a> and <a href="Dueren" class="internal-link">Dueren</a>. Faarhovn, which deals directly with the <a href="Narzoi" class="internal-link">Narzoi</a> of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a>, has elected to take a more considerate and careful approach to those contacts than its Cevoli and Othiri rivals, while Dueren, on its island north of the treaty line, has had no direct contact with them and works with Faarhovn where Peadyr is concerned.</p>
