<p>The Colonization of Peadyr is the settlement of the coasts and offshore islands of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a> by the powers of the Old World, a process that began with the landing of <a href="Balan Reyd" class="internal-link">Balan Reyd</a> in 785PA and continues to the present day. Although Peadyr was the first larger landmass of the region to be explored in depth, its settlement proved slow, violent in places and bitterly contested between rival powers, and in 927PA every colonial settlement on and around the island still clings to its shores or to the islands beside it, while the interior remains the undisputed domain of the <a href="Narzoi" class="internal-link">Narzoi</a> federations of the <a href="Kavi" class="internal-link">Kavi</a>, <a href="Odise" class="internal-link">Odise</a> and <a href="Senso" class="internal-link">Senso</a>.</p> <p>The wider discovery of the continental sphere is told in <a href="The Discovery of Aisligos" class="internal-link">The Discovery of Aisligos</a>. The first Old World colony in the region, <a href="Grynborg" class="internal-link">Grynborg</a> in the Gerjodi Islands, was founded in 719PA, but the lands beyond remained largely unvisited until Weadi traders opened the Kelzen Route sealane in 795PA, linking the Torn Sea to the Gerjodi Islands and the northern shores of Aisligos. The so called Opening of the West that followed in the 800s drew Meadi and Weadi traders and sailors, and with them the interest of kings and queens who had long been shut out of the rich Niryd and south Beheran trade by the mercantile hegemony of the eastern sultanate and saw in the new lands a way to break it. In the decades that followed, the realms of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a> and <a href="Blijgrat" class="internal-link">Blijgrat</a>, Lissvalley, the Cevoli powers and a multitude of Sertovi princes all sought a foothold on Peadyr.</p> <p>The first landings were individual and mostly unlucky. Balan Reyd, a Ravlander explorer, came ashore on the north-eastern coast in 785PA and founded Camp Reyd, but he and many of his men died of disease within a week, and only the survivors returned to the Old World with a map. <a href="Jurim Eykszoon" class="internal-link">Jurim Eykszoon</a>, a Frijmer in the service of Blijgrat and the Archmage <a href="Teriador" class="internal-link">Teriador</a>, charted the shores of the island and its northern isles in 834PA and landed on Jurland, but was forbidden by his sponsor to set foot on Peadyr itself, Teriador fearing plague, a fear that cannot have been eased by the sickness that had swept Grynborg fourteen years before. In 840PA <a href="Marino Viamali" class="internal-link">Marino Viamali</a> of Martella made the first sustained contact with the Narzoi of Peadyr, in a peaceful meeting with a people whom most accounts name as the Kavi, and two years later the Turpadori explorer <a href="Jaime di Dilin" class="internal-link">Jaime di Dilin</a> negotiated with the <a href="Vudi" class="internal-link">Vudi</a>, a people of fisherfolk on the eastern coast, his name later given to the river at whose mouth <a href="Alinanora" class="internal-link">Alinanora</a> would rise. Each power would afterwards claim its own explorer as the true discoverer of Peadyr, the Sertovi and Othiri favouring Reyd, the Cevoli Viamali and the Weadi Eykszoon, a rivalry that has never been settled.</p> <p>The first attempt at permanent settlement followed in 842PA, when Sertovi settlers of Ravland founded New Rav on the north-eastern tip of the island, on the ground where Camp Reyd had once stood. The colony lasted only five years. In 847PA it was destroyed by warriors of the Rele tribe of the Kavi, and the catastrophe discouraged Ravland from further colonial investment for over three decades.</p> <p>It was the Cevoli who established the first lasting foothold. In 858PA the Grand Duchy of <a href="Martella" class="internal-link">Martella</a>, whose expeditionary spirit had sent Viamali across the Tirean Ocean eighteen years before, founded <a href="Livoa" class="internal-link">Livoa</a> on Pearsmount, the highest of the Pear Isles off the eastern coast of Peadyr. Its island position kept Livoa clear of the struggles over the mainland, and it grew into the principal first port of call for ships arriving by the central Tirean Route. In 861PA the <a href="Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel" class="internal-link">Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel</a>, the G.U.V., was formed in Blijgrat, and it would soon become the most commercially significant power in the new world.</p> <p>The G.U.V. established its own foothold in 878PA. Its expedition included the Diviner <a href="Adreliz" class="internal-link">Adreliz</a>, a mage employed by the company not only to help with the steering of the ships and the identification of any ruins that might be found, but also to communicate with the natives through magical means. Her telepathy allowed the expedition to speak with the Kavi directly, and her methods were controversial by the standards of the <a href="Wermian Accords" class="internal-link">Wermian Accords</a>, which forbid any spell that influences or invades another's mind, though the Accords were by then considered outdated in the western lands and were of no concern to the commercial interests of the merchants who employed her. Through her, the Kavi Shaman spoke of the divinity of the land of Peadyr, and told the newcomers that the Kavi considered the island the Weadi call Jurland to be cursed, because of what had befallen the people who were once said to live there. The Kavi agreed to welcome Weadi traders into their communities on conditions, chief among them religious respect in the form of sacrifice, with Weadi merchants bringing food to be offered before they may enter into trade, and they raised no qualms about the Weadi settling Jurland, an island whose earlier inhabitants had been lost in the 710s PA for reasons that remain unknown. The settlement of <a href="Faarhovn" class="internal-link">Faarhovn</a> on Jurland, across the Eyks Channel from the northern coast of Peadyr, was the result, and it has remained the only colonial port permitted to trade on the mainland of the island.</p> <p>Two years later Ravland returned. In 880PA a new expedition, led this time by returning military veterans rather than traders, founded <a href="New Fryd" class="internal-link">New Fryd</a> on Reydsland, a tidal island joined to the north-eastern tip of Peadyr at ebb, on the same ground as New Rav. Settled in deliberate defiance of the Narzoi's continued opposition, and without the diplomatic cover that Faarhovn enjoys, New Fryd has remained a martial and comparatively modest Kalbinist outpost on the shores of Balansbay.</p> <p>The most aggressive venture was <a href="Almersport" class="internal-link">Almersport</a>, founded in 895PA on the south-eastern coast of Peadyr by <a href="Lissvalley" class="internal-link">Lissvalley</a> and <a href="Erlmondy" class="internal-link">Erlmondy</a> as a commercial port and holding of the Visian League powers. Where its neighbours sought accommodation with the Narzoi, Almersport recklessly sent military expeditions of armed men inland and up the rivers, and these soon began to disturb the Odise pilgrims who travel through the northern Odise Riverlands to the <a href="Finger of Life" class="internal-link">Finger of Life</a>, a site of Chianist pilgrimage.</p> <p>In 900PA the <a href="Treaty of Lotsprings" class="internal-link">Treaty of Lotsprings</a>, signed on the 16th of May by Meadrath, Blijgrat and the two great trading companies, divided the new world between its signatories along a diagonal line drawn from the old maps. Jurland and the lands south of it, Peadyr among them, were assigned to the Blijgrat League and the G.U.V., and the lands north of it to the Meadi Crown and the <a href="Gesellschaft für Westmeadischen Handel" class="internal-link">Gesellschaft für Westmeadischen Handel</a>, with each side pledging to defend the holdings of the other. To the settlers, who knew little of the lands beyond their own shores, this left the Meadi with no claim to anything near Peadyr, and it is a division that the Cevoli, Sertovi and Othiri powers, who were not party to it, have never recognised.</p> <p>The same year the Duchy of Turpador founded Alinanora at the mouth of the Dilin delta on the eastern coast, and has since pursued influence there through trade and presence rather than confrontation. In close coordination with Livoa, and with the backing of the Pastelliora financiers, Alinanora forms part of a growing Cevoli axis that hopes to secure an exclusive commercial presence in the new world and see its more aggressive rivals displaced without the need for open war. In 921PA the Meadi followed, founding <a href="Dueren" class="internal-link">Dueren</a> on Giselland, north of the treaty line, whose company has no direct contact with the Narzoi and cooperates with Faarhovn where Peadyr is concerned.</p> <p>The strain on the southern shore broke into open war in the decade before the present. In 917PA a young stormcaller and warleader of the Odise named <a href="Rylla" class="internal-link">Rylla</a>, of the Flaia, gathered many warriors around her, not only from her own people but from other tribes of the Odise, and raided a number of Almersport's camps, after which relations between the colony and the federation only worsened. Rylla was captured in 925PA and taken back to the Old World, where she was displayed like an animal before being burned alive in Karsord, the capital of Erlmondy. The affair caused widespread uproar among colonists and Narzoi alike. Many settlers saw her raids as a danger to every colonist on the island, others saw an opportunity to weaken Almersport, among them Alinanora, and a very small minority condemned Almersport's treatment of her as inhumane. Among the Odise the effect was to unite the federation, which took up arms as a whole and has fought Almersport in full force ever since.</p>
<p>As the colonies grew, so did their hunger for labour, and from the early years of the century a trade in the Narzoi themselves took shape. It began with the kidnapping of the <a href="Vudi" class="internal-link">Vudi</a> around Alinanora and spread to the smaller tribes of the islands and, since the war, to captured Odise warriors and members of the federation's larger tribes. The Cevoli axis of Livoa and Alinanora and the financiers of the Pastelliora stand at the centre of what has become the <a href="Narzoi Slavetrade" class="internal-link">Narzoi Slavetrade</a>, and the Sertovi and Othiri settlers take part with less ceremony. Faarhovn and Dueren have kept clear of it, though a faction within the G.U.V. would see its accord with the Kavi ended.</p>
 
<p>The settlers brought their faiths with them. Trinic and Gethric missionaries were among the earliest ships to follow the explorers, and the ports of Peadyr have since become a stronghold of Trinicism, though Kalbinist communities remain strong among the Sertovi, Othiri and Locni who came in search of a new life, and the newer school of Diarism, which seeks a marriage between Kalbinist Creator worship and Gethric belief, is growing in popularity, as described in <a href="Gethricism in the New World" class="internal-link">Gethricism in the New World</a>. These remain largely the faiths of the settlements, while the peoples of the interior hold to <a href="Chianism" class="internal-link">Chianism</a>. Beyond the charters of the great companies, a more lawless class of Old World settlers has also made the waters of the island its own, among them pirates such as <a href="Kylian Juz" class="internal-link">Kylian Juz</a>, the Windcat, captain of the Fast Duchess, who is wanted by nearly every major power.</p> <p>In 927PA the colonization of Peadyr remains unfinished and divided in several ways at once. The accord between Faarhovn and the Kavi has endured for nearly half a century, though Almersport's conduct has strained it, the Cevoli axis of Livoa and Alinanora pursues its patient designs, the Narzoi Slavetrade grows year by year, and the war between the Odise and Almersport has become the gravest conflict on the island, fought over land and over the road to the Finger of Life. The Senso of the south-west, whose lands lie furthest from the settled coasts, have had the fewest dealings with the newcomers, whom the Pea call the Dari, and the interior remains in the hands of the three federations.</p>
