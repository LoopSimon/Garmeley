<p>Marino Viamali (813PA to 869PA) was a Cevoli explorer of the Grand Duchy of <a href="Martella" class="internal-link">Martella</a> and the first man of the Old World to make sustained contact with the <a href="Narzoi" class="internal-link">Narzoi</a> of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a>. He set out across the Tirean Ocean in 840PA at the age of twenty-seven, financed by sponsors to whom he was heavily in debt, and the Viamali Sea off the shores of Peadyr is named after him.</p>
<p>The contact he made was peaceful. The natives he met, whom most accounts name as the Kavi but whom the sources do not clearly identify, were greatly impressed by the visitor, and above all by his massive beard. The Narzoi are unable to grow facial hair, and a beard of such size was taken as a sign of power and dignity akin to that of the spirits.</p>
<p>Viamali returned from his voyage a success, repaid his debt to his sponsors and became a heavy investor in the settlement of <a href="Livoa" class="internal-link">Livoa</a>, founded by Martella on Pearsmount in 858PA. The port of the city is graced by a large marble statue of him, gazing west across the ocean he crossed, and a similar statue stands at his home in Daria, the capital of Martella. He died in 869PA at the age of fifty-six, eleven years after the founding of Livoa. The Cevoli honour him as the true discoverer of Peadyr, a claim contested by the Sertovi and Othiri, who favour <a href="Balan Reyd" class="internal-link">Balan Reyd</a>, and by the Weadi, who favour <a href="Jurim Eykszoon" class="internal-link">Jurim Eykszoon</a>.</p>
