<p>Jurim Eykszoon (784PA to 853PA) was a Weadi explorer from Hevn, one of the great metropoli of <a href="Blijgrat" class="internal-link">Blijgrat</a> and the home of the Weadi peoples, and the first to chart the shores of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a>. Before he took to exploration he was a soldier and mercenary captain, and it was in the wars against the Skori raiders that he lost his right hand. He replaced it with a classical steel hook, which became as much a part of his legend as his charts.</p>
<p>In 834PA, at the age of fifty, Eykszoon sailed west in the service of Blijgrat and of the mage <a href="Teriador" class="internal-link">Teriador</a>, who sponsored the voyage. He charted the shores of Peadyr and its northern isles and landed on the island that the Weadi would name Jurland in his honour, finding it empty, its former inhabitants having been lost more than a century before for reasons that remain unknown. He never set foot on Peadyr itself. Teriador, fearing plague, forbade the landing, a fear that cannot have been eased by the sickness that had swept Grynborg fourteen years earlier. The channel that divides Jurland from the northern coast of Peadyr, the Eyks Channel, also bears his name.</p>
<p>Eykszoon returned to Hevn a celebrated man and died there in 853PA at the age of sixty-nine. His charts remained in Weadi hands and guided the <a href="Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel" class="internal-link">Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel</a> when it founded <a href="Faarhovn" class="internal-link">Faarhovn</a> on Jurland in 878PA, twenty-five years after his death. The Weadi and the company have long held him up as the true discoverer of Peadyr, a claim contested by the Sertovi and Othiri, who favour <a href="Balan Reyd" class="internal-link">Balan Reyd</a>, and by the Cevoli, who favour <a href="Marino Viamali" class="internal-link">Marino Viamali</a>.</p>
