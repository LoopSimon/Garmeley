 <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Fiago.jpg" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;"> </div> <p>Polgo Fiago is the Baron of <a href="Grivil" class="internal-link">Grivil</a> and the head of its Syndicate Council. He made his name as the leader of the Bearded Singers, the band that quelled the racial unrest against the hobgoblins of the city and brought peace to Grivil, and he is greatly respected by the hobgoblins who live there.</p> <h2>The Peace of Grivil</h2> <p>Grivil has taken in a surprisingly large number of <a href="Hobgoblin" class="internal-link">hobgoblins</a>, and for a time the unrest against them threatened to tear the city apart. Fiago and his band, the Bearded Singers, put an end to it and brought peace to Grivil. The peace made his name among the syndicates and communities of the city, and it carried him to the head of the council. The hobgoblins of Grivil have not forgotten who gave it to them.</p> <h2>The Baron and Hynek of Forrington</h2> <p><a href="Hynek of Forrington" class="internal-link">Hynek of Forrington</a> met Fiago several times in his role as the protector of the south in <a href="Garmeley" class="internal-link">Garmeley</a> and <a href="Forrington" class="internal-link">Forrington</a>. Grivil is culturally close to the rich southerners, yet it stands in conflict with them more often than not, and the friendship that grew between the Count and the Baron was the more remarkable for it. Fiago kept Grivil out of the <a href="Garmish Succession Crisis" class="internal-link">Garmish Succession Crisis</a> and did not join the Garmish Alliance, but he sympathised with Hynek's cause.</p> <p>Hynek was assassinated in February 927PA, and rumour says that his assassins found refuge in Grivil or were sent from it. For a baron who counted the dead Count a friend this is an awkward matter, and in a city that is home to so many different factions and peoples it is not one that can easily be answered.</p>
