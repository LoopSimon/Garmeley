<p>Eril Zarivic Ruda is the current Gram of the Jaerflow Region of the Holy Ushkyn Republic of Grachy and Lord of the City of Izhevk, he is son to the late Zariv Ruda and one of the Leaders of the Traditionalist Faction within the Republics Grand Ushkyn. His policies of industrialism and expansionism as well as a cutthroat approach to political rivals have made him one of the richest men in eastern Grachy and seen his city grow in influence and wealth during his rule. He became Gram in 914PA at the age of 25, when his father who was Gram before him, could no longer perform his duties to an ailing disposition.</p>
