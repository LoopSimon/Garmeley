<p>Kur Pharabal is the ninth Karnak of the Great Gaal, also known as the Gaal Empire and the former Leader of the Great Army. Due to his exploits during the Crisis of 738PA he is known as the Thorn of Vezan, having vanquished the <a href="Daemon" class="internal-link">Great Other</a> in personal combat. Pharabal became the Karnak of the Gaal in 882PA following the death of Kur Urasal, the eight Karnak.</p>
<p>His policies have shifted from those of his predecessor, antagonising and openly challenging various political opponents and diplomatic liaisons eventually leading to a grand purge of the higher Caste and exile to Argis, in the far east of Ynkal. The Karnak is currently overseeing the war effort in the military conflict with Renessis that broke out in the summer of 920PA.</p>
