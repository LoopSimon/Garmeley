<p>Sar Damok is a blue-scaled <a href="Ganoan" class="internal-link">Ganoan</a> <a href="Bihra" class="internal-link">Bihra</a> of <a href="Majir" class="internal-link">Majir</a> and the present Second Consul of the free city. He is the leading voice of the radicals of Majir, a growing movement that rejects the compromises of the truce of 895PA and is ready to risk a second war with <a href="Jink" class="internal-link">Jink</a>, and has steadily gained ground on the moderates of the First Consul, <a href="Serilla Yan" class="internal-link">Serilla Yan</a>.</p>
<h2>Early Life</h2>
<p>Sar Damok was born in 898PA in the Lungs, the Dome belt of Majir, three years after the truce, to a mother who worked the Domes. Like most children of the Lungs he went into the Domes young, and worked the shifts of the Wheelhouse, the Dome in which the uprising of 893PA had begun, until he became one of its foremen. He made his name in the Dome yards as a speaker, and his speeches against Jink, its lenders and the Mystics Guild drew ever larger crowds of young dome workers.</p>
<h2>Political Career</h2>
<p>Carried by the dome workers of the Lungs and by the Masks, the youngest and most violent of the gangs of Majir, Damok was elected Second Consul while still a young man. He speaks for those who feel the truce has given Majir freedom in name only, while its debts, its food and its medicine remain in the hands of the northern shore. He has called for the treaty of Consul Aatal to be torn up and for Majir once again to shelter the slaves who flee Jink, for the <a href="Mystics Guild" class="internal-link">Mystics Guild</a> to be driven from the city and its Domes, and for the Silverpit and the other mines seized in the war never to be returned. In Jink he is regarded as the greatest danger to the peace of the valley, while in Majir many of the young see in him the spirit of the Turning of the Wheel.</p>
