<p>Serilla Yan is a <a href="Ganoan" class="internal-link">Ganoan</a> politician of <a href="Majir" class="internal-link">Majir</a> and the present First Consul of the free city. She is the leading figure of the moderate establishment that has governed Majir for most of the time since the truce of 895PA, and stands for the preservation of the truce with <a href="Jink" class="internal-link">Jink</a> and the steady running of the <a href="Parabol Domes" class="internal-link">Parabol Domes</a>. Her position has weakened in recent years with the rise of the radicals under the Second Consul, <a href="Sar Damok" class="internal-link">Sar Damok</a>.</p>
<h2>Early Life</h2>
<p>Serilla Yan was born in Majir in 885PA, the daughter of a dome worker. Her father was among the workers who shut the valves of the Wheelhouse in 893PA and was killed there in the first months of the uprising, when she was eight years old. She grew up in the years of the war and the truce, and found work as a clerk in the Tower of Relativity, where she learned the accounts of the Domes and the debts of the city better than most of the Consuls she served.</p>
<h2>Political Career</h2>
<p>Yan rose through the offices of the Tower with the backing of the Valvers, the oldest of the gangs of Majir and long the strongest supporters of the moderates, and with their votes was elected First Consul. As First Consul she has kept to the course of her predecessors, holding to the truce, working with the Mystics Guild to keep the Domes stable and bargaining each year with Jink over the price of the flowers and the medicine that Majir needs against the fumes of its Domes. Her supporters call her careful and cool-headed, while the radicals accuse her of being too close to Jink and its lenders. She holds the final say in matters of war, and should she die in office the Second Consul would take her place.</p>
