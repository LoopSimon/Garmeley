<p>Tadeuz Pilsud is the current Sekretar of Vihr, one of the chief advisors to his majesty King Vormil III and the Head of the Vaergriff, the Vihri Intelligence & Security Service. A physically unimpressive man little is known about Tadeuz past and although rumours about him coming from lesser nobility have existed since he entered the political stage it is an open secret he is a commoner. An exceptionally clever man, having studied at Szivek University and survived a career amongst men of higher birth who showed him no love, he is one of the most influential figures of the Realm. His loyalty lies with the Crown and the Crown only, with him having frequently come in conflict with the various Vadcy of the country, being feared by many of them and elevating the Vaergriff to unknown heights. He holds a Seat in the Honorary Assembly as the only member not of noble blood and acts as the representative of the Throne within the Institution.</p>
