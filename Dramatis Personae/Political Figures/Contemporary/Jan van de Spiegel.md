<div class="wiki-infobox"> <h4 class="wiki-header">Jan van de Spiegel</h4> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Epithet</strong> <span>Bluecap</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Occupation</strong> <span>Stadtholder of Schipwig and of the Blijgrat League</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Culture</strong> <span><a href="Meadi" class="internal-link">Osmer</a> <a href="Human" class="internal-link">Human</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Born</strong> <span>868PA, Schipwig</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Died</strong> <span>alive</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Age</strong> <span>59</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Association</strong> <span><a href="Blijgrat" class="internal-link">Blijgrat League</a></span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Spiegel.jpg" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;"> </div> <p>Jan van de Spiegel is the Stadtholder of Schipwig and, since 909PA, the Stadtholder of the <a href="Blijgrat" class="internal-link">Blijgrat League</a>, where he is known as the Leader-Elect. He was born in Schipwig in 868PA into the van de Spiegel, a famous patrician clan of the city, and is fiftynine years old as of 927PA. He is an extremely cutthroat man, in league with various organisations that span continents,  known to keep contacts as far east as Grachy, many of them high in government and society</p> <p>Van de Spiegel is known for his relatively modest and simple clothing. He dresses himself in fine dyed cloth, but of jewellery he wears nothing more than the golden ring on his finger that marks his position, and his signature blue hat has given him his relatively innocent moniker, Bluecap.</p>  <p>The van de Spiegel are a famous patrician clan, and over the last fifty years the family has made great money from the growing sugar trade. Jan's uncle Gustav took part in the Faarhovn Expedition of 878PA, in which the <a href="Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel" class="internal-link">G.U.V.</a> founded <a href="Faarhovn" class="internal-link">Faarhovn</a> on Jurland, and the family is rumoured to have connections to members of the <a href="Pastelliora" class="internal-link">Pastelliora</a>. Jan has no issue of his own by blood, but his sister has sons.</p> <p>He became Stadtholder of Schipwig in 899PA. Ten years later he rose to the head of the League, and he did it by removing the man who held it. Stadtholder Willem van Barnevelt, who had signed the <a href="Treaty of Lotsprings" class="internal-link">Treaty of Lotsprings</a> for the League in 900PA, was a conservative career politician more than a merchant, and he argued against the expansion of the G.U.V. in the <a href="The New World" class="internal-link">new world</a>. The vote of the Council of Cities that ran him out of office in 909PA was orchestrated by van de Spiegel and a cabal of his trusted men. It was unanimous and blindsided Barnevelt, who until then had been well respected. As custom has it for an able Stadtholder, the Council re-elected van de Spiegel in 921PA, and his term now runs until 933PA.</p> <h2>Methods and Aims</h2> <p>In the 920s van de Spiegel made deals with various privateers of <a href="Zeeport" class="internal-link">Zeeport</a> to attack Cevoli, Othiri and Sertovi traders coming back across the north Tirean Ocean from the new world, and he is rumoured to have been in contact with <a href="Kylian Juz" class="internal-link">the Windcat</a> himself. One of his political opponents at home, the mage Vera Truug, found herself on her way to a Cevoli slave colony with her tongue removed and her fingers broken, after she had opposed him publicly. That van de Spiegel was behind it is an open secret in the League.</p> <p>Centralisation is one of his many desires. The cities of the League may be small in extent, but they are extremely powerful in wealth, and van de Spiegel seeks to channel the momentum of the present day into a real empire of trade, overseas and at home. He is the patron and ally of <a href="Pieter van Dielen" class="internal-link">Pieter van Dielen</a>, the Kamerspreker of the G.U.V., who is in effect his groomed successor as Stadtholder. Abroad he is in contact with <a href="Ivanova Orin" class="internal-link">Ivanova Orin</a>, the Gram of Wangrad in Grachy, with whom he deals mainly in the alcohol trade, in spirits imported from Grachy.</p>
