<small><i>this article concerns the <a href="The Discovery of Aisligos" class="internal-link">Discovery</a> and <a href="The Colonization of Peadyr" class="internal-link">Colonization</a> of Peadyr, for overviews look here</i></small> <p>Marrin Andil is the Lord of <a href="Ulbreek" class="internal-link">Ulbreek</a>, the free port on the island of the same name off the southern coast of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a>, and the Count of Andil in title if no longer in fact. Born in 880PA at Andil in the Duchy of <a href="Morelle" class="internal-link">Morelle</a>, close to the borders of <a href="Dirneux" class="internal-link">Dirneux</a>, he was a man of the circle of Archduke <a href="Jean du Jera" class="internal-link">Jean du Jera</a>, and the fall of that circle in 913PA sent him across the Tirean Ocean with his fortune and a fleet of fourteen ships to found a lordship of his own. He is a cunning and opportunistic man, known rather more for his wits than for his courage, and in the new world he has made himself the one Meadi lord who answers to neither of the two great companies.</p>
<p>The county of Andil lay in Morelle, but Marrin attached himself to the household of the Archduke of Dirneux, the foremost voice of the old guard of the realm. He was no soldier. Andil served du Jera as his steward of coin and his fixer, a man who dealt in money and favours and knew who owed what to whom, and when the Archduke became head of the regency council of the young Queen <a href="Gisela IV" class="internal-link">Gisela IV</a> after the death of her mother in 910PA, Andil rose with his master.</p>

<p>When the queen took the government into her own hands in 913PA, dismissed much of the palace staff, recalled the exiled dignitaries and restored Count <a href="Odrick von Fulmer" class="internal-link">Odrick von Fulmer</a> to his office, the faction of the Archduke collapsed, and Andil, who feared persecution, saw no future for himself at home. He effectively sold his ancestral estate, the lands and fiefs of Andil passing to those who were tied to him by debt, and today the county is ruled by the Countess Treve, who threw in with the Fulmer faction. Andil keeps the title and nothing more. With his wealth he bought and hired no fewer than fourteen ships, and with the financial backing of the Houses di Vadeux and von Larm he sailed for the new world.</p>
<p>On the unclaimed island of Ulbreek, which had been named decades before by a Weadi explorer, he declared himself Lord and raised a port that serves any and all. He still proclaims himself a servant of the Crown of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a>, at least in name, and the K.G.W. leaves him alone for it, though neither company stands behind him. The Houses that financed the armada share in the government, and Andil rules less as a lord than as the first among oligarchs. Most of the city follows <a href="Diarism" class="internal-link">Diarism</a>, the faith born in the lands of his old patron, while Andil himself, a Trinicist by birth, takes no interest in the quarrels of the faiths so long as his port stays full. The clergy of Diarism are few and little established in Ulbreek, and its settlers have found it convenient to read the teachings of Perrin Diar on equality and worldly wealth as meant for themselves and their betters, and not for slaves. Ulbreek is a city of utilitarians, and its Diarists do not much mind the Narzoi auctions held in its harbour.</p> <p>For the last three years the daily rule of the city has been in the hands of his daughter and heir, Melina. Born in 905PA, she was eight years old when her father sailed, and now, at twenty-two, she has no ambition to return to Meadrath and reclaim her birthright, but stands to inherit the rule of Ulbreek instead. She has inherited her father's best and worst qualities, his cunning and his appetite, and unlike him she is said to have a spine. She makes her deals with pirate captains and traders of new money and has little use for the old noble families that came over with her father. Among her partners is the Battvian auctioneer <a href="Gabriella Mile" class="internal-link">Gabriella Mile</a>.</p> <p>It is rumoured that part of the profits of Ulbreek find their way home to Jera, as an insurance for the Archduke should the fortunes of the old guard turn again, and Count von Fulmer is said to suspect as much.</p>
