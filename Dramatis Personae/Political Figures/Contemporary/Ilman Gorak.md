<p>The acting Sanjak of <a href="Ikara" class="internal-link">Ikara</a>, Ilman Gorak is a beheran Elf and the current Head of the Gorak Dynasty, old elven nobility from the Summer Coast. He has been in charge of the cities governance since 910PA, following the death of Sanjak Tumur Herjalan and has moved into the Palace in the cities Temple Quarter with his daughter Ishir and his Son Benmehd.</p>
<p>Ilman is a great admirer and sponsor of the Games within the Falcons Pit, and has directed extensive funds and resources towards the Season of Falcons Flight, a period of many spectacles during the Summer. He sponsors the Black Sail, a group of athletes competing in the games that are among the favourites to take the price during the City Championships this year. Due to his unusual joviality for a man of his station, the Sanjak is well regarded amongst the cities population, although an apparent rivalry between him and the head of the Silver Garrison north of the city, the Bey Razom, has developed during the last years of governance.</p>
