<p>Drogo Barzin, better known as the Lord of Marrow, is a Sorcerer turned skeletal Revenant that resides in the labyrinthine halls of Laram, an ancient burial complex the southern highlands of modern day <a href="Erlmondy" class="internal-link">Erlmondy</a>. Various attempts to dislodge him from his hold and destroy him for good have been undertaken, all failing to overcome his ceaselessly resurrecting court of animated servants and warriors, skeletal and relentless, as such he has "ruled" from Laram for over one hundred years. </p>
<p>Barzin was born to the House Barzin, now known as the Warni following his infamy, to Leorod Barzin, the Lord of Mindahl and one of the most powerful man in all of Erlmondy. Developing an obsession with immortality and the dead, young Drogos experiments are said to have disturbed even the most degenerated observer and he was sent away to the Temple of Iarl, near a small town on the coast of the Visian Sea, to live out his live away from his sick desires. Alas it was here that Drogo amassed the knowledge he required, still commanding the purse of fantastically rich scion of greater nobility and now in the peace and quiet of the Temple. </p>
<p>Barzin faded from memory during his days at Iarl, never causing trouble that would leave the immediate vicinity of his surroundings and if so, quickly kept quiet by a watchful family. His younger brother superseded him when his father died and when he too grew old their family secret at Iarl began drifting into memory, when Barzin who was now in his 7th decade left for the Highlands with a small retinue of scribes and servants, no one thought of it more than an old eccentrics final journey to die. They were right in one way at least.</p>
<p>Barzin, now a skeletal lich himself, has commanded a court of the dead for the last century from Laram and still calls himself "the rightful Lord of Mindahl", citing his legal right to the Lordship due to the laws of succession, although apt observers have answered the somewhat ridiculous claim of the Revenant that he too, had died.</p>
