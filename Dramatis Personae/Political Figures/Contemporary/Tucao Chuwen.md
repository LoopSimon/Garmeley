<p>Tucao Chuwen is an admiral of the <a href="Twintail Imperium" class="internal-link">Twintail Imperium</a> and the commander of the Ting Fleet, currently stationed at the Free City of <a href="Sredit" class="internal-link">Sredit</a>. One of the most illustrious members of the Nan of War and a personal appointee and loyalist of <a href="Kubil no Vang" class="internal-link">Kubil no Vang</a>, the Commander of the Southern Legions, he made his name at the Battle of Segao in 924PA and has commanded at Sredit since late 926PA. As the commander of the Shirin forces in Sredit he is inarguably the most powerful person in the city.</p>  
<h2>The Battle of Segao</h2>  
<p>Tucao earned his reputation as a great commander at the Battle of Segao in 924PA, where the fleet of the Imperium inflicted a crushing defeat on the Manyfingers, a band of <a href="Xolor" class="internal-link">Xolori</a> buccaneers who had terrorised the Segao Islands and hampered the northern route of the <a href="Vanpur Pepper Trade" class="internal-link">pepper trade</a> into the Gavivag Bay. The victory made him one of the most popular figures in the Nan of War, and in late 926PA he was sent to Sredit.</p>  
<h2>At Sredit</h2>  
<p>Tucao now holds local command over the 2nd and 3rd Cohorts of the 5th Legion and the Ting Fleet at Sredit. The 5th Legion is divided along the Gavivag Bay and traditionally serves under the various fleets of the Imperium, and many within it are personally loyal to Tucao, who has a reputation for caring for his men more than the average Shirin admiral, who may value a ship above a thousand Shirin. He is welcomed in the city as a guest and spends most of his time aboard his flagship, the Sixth Heaven, yet his presence is the main reason the Duchy of <a href="Jateš" class="internal-link">Jateš</a> has not retaken the city, and with it he holds the balance of power in the region in his claws. It is rumoured that he has brought experimental weapons of war to Sredit, guarded by a contingent of alchemists of the <a href="Liandar" class="internal-link">Liandar</a> and possibly related to the Huling, the black powder of the Shirin.</p>
