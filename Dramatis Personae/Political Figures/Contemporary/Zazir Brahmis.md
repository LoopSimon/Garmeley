<p>Zazir Ibn Brahmis, sometimes just called "Brahmis", is the current Emir of <a href="Tusul" class="internal-link">Tusul</a> and the personification of Elbae tyranny and rule over the Zipivi. He was infamously responsible for brutally putting down the Uprising of 925PA and has been described as ruthless, cunning and utterly loyal to the Sultanat. Following the Death of Emir Javal ar Effes at the hands of zipivi revolutionaries led by <a href="Asper Inrates" class="internal-link">Asper Inrates</a>, better known as the White Hawk, the Sultan send Brahmis as "his stern hand of justice" to Tusul. While the new Emir undoubtably is fearsome and focused on the war against the rebells, his draconic practices have so far only helped to fan the fires of the resistance he is tasked to put down.</p>
