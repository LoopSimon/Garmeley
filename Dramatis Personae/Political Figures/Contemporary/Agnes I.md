She was the daughter of Duke Eadwin II and the granddaughter of Duke Albrecht V. Her elder brother Kazlav and her elder sister Natalia died before their father, and Agnes was the only one of his children to outlive him and take the Riverthrone. Under her father, her grandfather and herself the Duchy enjoyed a long peace, though the quarrels between the Gethric north and the spirit-worshipping south, which her house had always kept quiet, were never settled.</p> <p>She was killed at the Midsummer Festival of 922PA by the rogue sorcerer <a href="Jonek Two Tongues" class="internal-link">Jonek Two-Tongues</a>, who had escaped from prison. She was twenty-six years old, unmarried and without children. Her closest kin was her cousin <a href="Hynek of Forrington" class="internal-link">Hynek of Forrington</a>, the son of her father's sister Fiora, and the quarrel over her succession became the <a href="Garmish Succession Crisis" class="internal-link">Garmish Succession Crisis</a>. See also <a href="Rulers of Garmeley" class="internal-link">The Rulers of Garmeley</a>.</p>
