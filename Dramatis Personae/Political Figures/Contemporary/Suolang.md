<p>A member of the clerical caste of the Propolomopsis Clan in the north east of Drizan, Suolang is the current Mycomother of the Temple of Yl-Lhatsi and as such the spiritual and religious leader of the Mogyri people, following the death of her predecessor, Chorioactis Lingpa Arjanuva. She was born in Rangeen, the largest settlement of the Chorioactis in the year 798PA and ascended the High Chair of the Temple in 907PA, at the age of 109 years old.</p>
<p>Suolang is an extremely polarizing figure, much like Arjanuva before her, however for many different reasons: Where Arjanuva rallied around her progressive elements within the Clan structure and promoted foreign exchange and political union in the face of it, Suolang rejects almost all of Arjanuvas most radical policies but also actively distances herself from the hard line traditionalist elements in the Clans. This notably includes even the conservative leadership of the Chorioactis, leaving Suolang without a strong base of political allies to support her in the game of power constantly played in and around the Temple.</p>
<p>Her decision to both uphold and enshrine the Treaty of Nzhun, while simultaneously disbanding the <a href="Rash Gopal" class="internal-link">Rash Gopal</a> embodies this centralist reign very well, while at least somewhat commendable for her integrity one has to question this self inflicted knee capping of the few executive tools she was given by the previous Mycomother.</p>
