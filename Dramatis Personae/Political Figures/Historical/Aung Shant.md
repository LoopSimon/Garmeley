<p>Aung Shant was the Lord of <a href="Naigan" class="internal-link">Naigan</a>, a province of the <a href="Golden Empire" class="internal-link">Golden Empire</a>, who no longer recognised the Golden Rule and in 492PA declared himself King of Naigan. He founded the Aung Dynasty, which ruled Naigan as a kingdom and later as an empire, until a junior branch of his house, the Belen, took the throne some four decades before the fall of the Empire. Aung Shant died in 518PA, and the conquest of the Golden Empire in 532PA was the work of his successors. Aungs personal religious conviction is usually heavily debated, whether or not he truly believed in Sochor is unclear as under him the modern day practice of local shrines to Sochors many aspects in  the purple sea islands began.</p>
