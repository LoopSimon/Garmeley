<div class="wiki-infobox"> <h4 class="wiki-header">Marlina I</h4> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Epithet</strong> <span>the White Lady</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Occupation</strong> <span>Queen of Gisvale</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Predecessor</strong> <span>Tibor I</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Successor</strong> <span>Ardol VI</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Culture</strong> <span><a href="Meadi" class="internal-link">Gismer</a> <a href="Human" class="internal-link">Human</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Born</strong> <span>unknown</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Died</strong> <span>504PA</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Dynasty</strong> <span><a href="Gismar" class="internal-link">von Gismar</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Allegiance</strong> <span><a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a></span> </div> </div> <p>Marlina I was Queen of <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a> from 475PA until 504PA. She is remembered as the White Lady for her characteristic white garb, and as one of the many great queens of the House of Gismar. She was utterly beloved for her devotion to her people.</p> <p>Marlina took no husband. The Wizard Redemor, said to have been a scorned lover though that is likely fiction, had told her that her fruits would poison one in three and break the crown forever, and she never married. When she died the crown passed to the son of her younger sister, who reigned as Ardol VI. Later generations did not forget the warning when Tibor II lost the throne to Diedrik of <a href="Medlan" class="internal-link">Medlan</a> in 589PA, and when the crown of Gisvale was joined to that of Korsfall in 664PA.</p> <p>In 491PA the queen built Castle Gis in <a href="Giselstatt" class="internal-link">Giselstatt</a> to fortify the city, the most military and defensible of its three fortifications. She is recorded in the <a href="Rulers of the Meadi" class="internal-link">Rulers of the Meadi</a>.</p>
