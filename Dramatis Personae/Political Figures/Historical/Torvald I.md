<div class="wiki-infobox"> <h4 class="wiki-header">Torvald I</h4> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Epithet</strong> <span>the Tall</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Occupation</strong> <span>Lord of Korsfall</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Predecessor</strong> <span>N/A</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Successor</strong> <span>Siegmar I</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Culture</strong> <span><a href="Meadi" class="internal-link">Rhilmer</a> <a href="Human" class="internal-link">Human</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Born</strong> <span>unknown</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Died</strong> <span>288PA</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Dynasty</strong> <span>von Weide</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Allegiance</strong> <span>Korsfall</span> </div> </div> <p>Torvald the Tall was the first Lord of Korsfall and the founder of the House von Weide, which ruled the realm from 262PA until 469PA. Later tradition makes him a general of the <a href="Auguri Dominion" class="internal-link">Auguri Dominion</a> who took power after its fall, and he is the earliest known "Lord of all Meadi in Korsfall".</p> <p>The early accounts from around his own time are plainer. They describe a warlord, a Burgherr or castellan of Weidhill, in a long line of such men who protected the people around the hill as travelling rulers, one of the fortified warlords the Auguri had ordained as their chosen rulers of men. In his reign Korsfall went to war with Morelle in 282PA, and after the Battle of the Voiceless in 285PA the king of Morelle bent the knee. Most of the rest of the early history of his realm is the story of its wars with the Locni of the western valleys.</p> <p>He is called the Tall for his height, and the name has also been read as a play on the hill from which his line ruled and looked out over the land. The house took its name from Weidhill, upon which many willows grew. Torvald died in 288PA and was followed by Siegmar I. His house ruled Korsfall for eleven reigns, until Siegmar V, "the last Tree", died in 469PA and the crown passed to Gernot I von Kor.</p>
