<p>Chagor was the founder of the Chagor Dynasty, the first of the <a href="Dynasties of Tal" class="internal-link">Dynasties of Tal</a>. A Talang of the city of Char, he brought the early cities of the Talang under one crown and in 1099BA claimed the Ivory Crown of Tal at Char, from which his house ruled for close to five centuries. No likeness of Chagor survives, for the Muyra Dynasty erased every depiction of the earlier rulers of Tal.</p>
