<p>Molopgar I was a warlord of the south of <a href="Tal" class="internal-link">Tal</a> in the <a href="Period of the Bleeding Heron" class="internal-link">Period of the Bleeding Heron</a>. In league with the earlier rulers of <a href="Byamon" class="internal-link">Byamon</a> he formed the Pact of Water, the alliance that won the southern conflicts of the Period. He later founded the Kingdom of <a href="Soren Vin" class="internal-link">Soren Vin</a> and became its King, and he died in 858PA. He is said to have been as a shrewd a diplomat and strategist as he was a competent fighter in his own right, and lead his armies himself, usually considered to have been the most prestigious of the Warlords during the Period of the Bleeding Heron.</p>
