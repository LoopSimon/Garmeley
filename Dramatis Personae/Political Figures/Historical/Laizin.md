<p>Laizin the Strong was the last King of the Kingdom of Rin and the first Golden Emperor. In 283PA he converted to the <a href="Ashcult" class="internal-link">Ashcult</a> of <a href="Sochor" class="internal-link">Sochor</a> and was proclaimed Guardian of the Ngalas, which founded the <a href="Golden Empire" class="internal-link">Golden Empire</a>, and he ruled it from Fiyang. He is remembered as a giant of a man, broad and heavily muscled, always clad in his leopard pelt with a golden helm of red plumes and curved horns. Laizin wielded the Godbreaker, a golden mace enchanted with the divine ash of Sochor and said to have been tempered in the gods last flames himself, this relic weapon was later returned to the Ashcults Kiln and its current whereabouts are unknown.</p>
