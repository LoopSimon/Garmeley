<p>Piri Cao, known as the Hungering Wave, was a Rytoku warlord of the Sea lands who was raised as a peacekeeper in the last years of the Empire of <a href="Naigan" class="internal-link">Naigan</a> and turned on his Emperor. In 771PA he sacked the Imperial Palace at Kiamar and forced <a href="Belen Yun" class="internal-link">Belen Yun</a> to abdicate, which ended the Empire and began the <a href="Period of the Bleeding Heron" class="internal-link">Period of the Bleeding Heron</a>. He ruled as a warlord of the Sea lands for around eleven years, until he was murdered in 782PA, likely by one of his concubines on the order of one of his captains. 
He was known for his thundering laugh and extraordinary cruelty, as well as a legendary dagger that was said to have brought him supernatural luck, known as the Curved Blade of Horon.</p>
