<p>Remillion, also known as Remillion the Usurper,or Remillion the Truelance, was the legendary Ruler of the so called Free Lands, otherwise called the disputed lands of Triff. He famously renounced all gods and lords, and declared the <a href="Trifflands" class="internal-link">Trifflands</a> free of all chains and bounds in 766PA. When the Duke of Triff, Ingemar of Triff, led a host against his camp Remillion slaughtered the armies fielded against him and the Duke himself. The King of <a href="Gelmsburg" class="internal-link">Gelmsburg</a>, nominally overlord over the Duke of Triff, declared Remillion a Usurper and that any loyal man who could slay the fiend, would become the new Duke of Triff, much to the chagrin of the  exiled of Triffs. Following his passing, Remillion who had amassed a cult like following in his small realm, became the focal point of a cult like group known as the Circle of Triff, a group of like minded individuals that protected Remillions legacy and believed in a prophecy foretelling his eventual return, ironically eventually becoming a near religious sect in itself.</p>
