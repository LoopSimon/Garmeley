He famously renounced all gods and lords, and declared the <a href="Trifflands" class="internal-link">Trifflands</a> free of all chains and bounds in 766PA. Where he came from and what he was is not recorded, and he is presumed to have been human.</p> <h2>The Usurper</h2> <p>When the Duke of Triff, Ingemar of Triff, led a host against his camp in 767PA, Remillion slaughtered the armies fielded against him and the Duke himself. The King of <a href="Gelmsburg" class="internal-link">Gelmsburg</a>, nominally overlord over the Duke of Triff, declared Remillion a usurper and declared that any loyal man who could slay the fiend would become the new Duke of Triff, much to the chagrin of the exiled nobles of the House of Triff.</p>  <p>Remillion defeated the Sister of Storms in personal combat in 774PA, and Tristen the Ironguard in the same way in 785PA. He is also said to have slain a figure known as the Blue Mage, who enchanted <a href="Gryphon" class="internal-link">gryphons</a> and claimed the skies and hills for himself.</p>  <p>The Tower at Ralt is where Remillion rested and what he made his home. It is seen as a symbol of his legacy.</p>  <p>In 791PA Remillion was gone from the Trifflands, and the Council of Triff took over and administered his domain. He is presumed dead.</p>  <p>Remillion had amassed a cult like following in his small realm, and following his passing it became the focal point of a group known as the Circle of Triff, like minded individuals who protect Remillion's legacy and believe in a prophecy foretelling his eventual return. Ironically, the Circle has eventually become a near religious sect in itself. The prophecy runs:</p> <p><em>In days of lost and damned returning,<br> when long spun veil rips torn at last,<br> great Remillion will rise, his lance held high<br> to pierce the shades and bring back light.</em></p>
