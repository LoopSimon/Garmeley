Nursili I, called the Conqueror by later Rakhian tradition, was the Lord of Cities who rose to power in 610BA and presided over the first great expansion of Rakhian authority beyond its original city-state. Before Nursili, Rakh was one power among several in a contested landscape of Palmunian colonial successors; by the time his campaign of consolidation was complete, it was the dominant force across the region. Under Nursili and the dynasty of rulers who followed him, the smaller Palmunian colonies, city-states and trading posts that had coexisted uneasily along the Smoking Sea coast for generations, were absorbed one by one into the growing Rakhian domain. The process culminated in 560BA, when Rakhian armies conquered <a href="Palmunia" class="internal-link">Palmunis</a> itself and the ancient mother-civilisation from which Rakh had grown in an act so transformative that the ruler's title changed with it: the Lord of Cities became the Fireking, sovereign not merely of a federation of urban centres but of a true empire. Nursili did not live to see that final conquest, but it was the trajectory he set that made it possible, and his name has been attached to the expansionist ambition of his era ever since.</p>
