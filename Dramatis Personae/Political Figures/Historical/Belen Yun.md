<p>Belen Yun was the last Emperor of <a href="Naigan" class="internal-link">Naigan</a>, the third of the Belen Dynasty, which arose from a junior branch of the Aung and ruled for around four decades. He took the throne around 747PA at the age of 18 and ruled in the years of the plague and of the sealing of Kiamar. In 771PA <a href="Piri Cao" class="internal-link">Piri Cao</a> sacked the Imperial Palace and forced him to abdicate. After proclaiming his abdication with his imperial seal, Belen Yun was sealed in his throne room, and he and his court of concubines and servants all died within it. The room was opened only a full year later, by raiders and it is unknown what happened to the last emperors personal belongings. Yun is not remembered fondly by history, as are few of the Belen Dynasty during the latter days of Naigan.</p>
