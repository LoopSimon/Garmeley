<p>Farkiz II, sometimes simply referred to as "the Silent Monarch", was the fifth Ras of Dalniz and ruled the east Ilrakhani realm between 767-780PA. He was a prestigious scholar and adept mage that studied under the great Professor <a href="Joakim zi Szarbel"class="internal-link">Joakim zi Szarbel</a> at Szievek University and was never expected to inherit the Throne as the fourth son of his father, Ras Aldrig I. His reign is often associated with various plays and stories about a "Witch King" or "Sorcerer Lord" but was in truth rather uneventful and there is no evidence to the matter that Farkiz was a particularly gifted Channeller of Magicks himself, preferring the world of theory instead, a claim which his amicable and good relationship with the neighbouring Khatrym of <a href="Kherova"class="internal-link">Kherova</a>, exceptionally skeptical of the Arcane and Occult, substantiates.</p> 
<p>The Ras would continue his predecessors policy of neutrality during the ongoing hostilities to the south, later known as the <a href="Wars of Elbae Aggression"class="internal-link">Wars of Elbae Aggression</a>, although he allowed free passage to various vihiri armies and mercenaries that took the eastern route through Dalniz instead of the western way through the Fairriver Valley when venturing into the invaded lands of Grevenna.</p> 
<p>Farkiz is most well known for two things, his treatise on what he dubbed as <a href="Acceleration Theory" class="internal-link">"Acceleration Theory"</a> and the circumstances surrounding his later years and demise, all but confirmed to have been overcome by manic depressions that would later lead to death at his own hands. As he had no children of his own, his younger brothers eldest son Erzel Drageri, would succeed Farkiz as the Ras zi Dalniz in 780PA. </p>
