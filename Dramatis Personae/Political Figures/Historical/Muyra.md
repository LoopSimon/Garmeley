<p>Muyra defeated the forces of the Melop in 503BA and was raised as Lord of Lords in southern Tal, founding the Muyra Dynasty, the last of the <a href="Dynasties of Tal" class="internal-link">Dynasties of Tal</a>. He refused to wear the Ivory Crown and ruled instead as the first among the lords of Tal, a title that his house kept for the better part of three centuries. In the portraits of him he wears no crown but a tall headdress of gold with curved horns. His dynasty later erased every depiction of the earlier rulers of Tal, and no likeness of the Chagor or the Melop survives.</p>
