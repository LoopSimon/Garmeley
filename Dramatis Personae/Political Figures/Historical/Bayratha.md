<p>Bayratha was the ruler of <a href="Naigan" class="internal-link">Naigan</a> of the Aung Dynasty who in 658PA declared himself Emperor of the Purple Sea and was proclaimed the Divine Emperor and the envoy of <a href="Sochor" class="internal-link">Sochor</a> himself, which made the Kingdom of Naigan the Empire of Naigan. Under him the Empire of the Four, the four peoples of Tal, was united, and his name came to stand for the height of the Purple Empire. He was said to have been an exceptionally tall man of an unreadable expression, and said to never show fury or joy upon his visage.</p>
