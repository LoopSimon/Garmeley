<p>Melop rose in the south of Tal in 623BA and took the Ivory Crown from the Chagor, founding the Melop Dynasty, the second of the <a href="Dynasties of Tal" class="internal-link">Dynasties of Tal</a>. His house was a hard and warlike one and ruled for scarcely more than a century, until its forces were defeated by <a href="Muyra" class="internal-link">Muyra</a> in 503BA. No likeness of Melop survives, for the Muyra Dynasty erased every depiction of the earlier rulers of Tal.</p>
