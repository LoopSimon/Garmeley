</div> <p>Hantili III, later known as "the Great", who came to power as Fireking in 275BA, stands apart from every other ruler of the Rakhian Empire as the greatest conqueror in its history,  a figure whose campaigns in a single generation transformed the extent and character of the Rakhian domain more completely than any other individual in the empire's long existence. The context he inherited was one of unresolved conflict as three Great Rakhi-Addaci Wars had failed to end the great rival of the <a href="Addacia" class="internal-link">Addacian Empire</a>, which had endured for nearly six centuries and proved uniquely resistant to Rakhian arms. Hantili launched the Fourth immediately upon his accession. In 271BA his armies stormed Cothar, the Addacian capital, and conquered it ending a civilisation that had met Rakh as an equal for generations.</p>
<p> Between 266 and 258BA, the Cevoli Campaigns swept through the forests to the northwest, absorbing Cevolia into the empire. Hantili concluded this extraordinary sequence of conquests with what his court proclaimed the Peace of the Firelord in a deliberate generation of enforced calm in which the empire was reorganised, its administration rebuilt across the new territories, and rakhi settlers installed in the lands of the conquered. For the court scholars who composed in his reign, and for the historians who studied the tablets left by those scholars, Hantili was the measure against which every subsequent Fireking would be weighed.</p>
