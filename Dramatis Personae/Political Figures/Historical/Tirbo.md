<p>Tirbo was the infamous warlord who led the his Clan and many othhers out of the collapsing <a href="Mitimgar" class="internal-link">Mitimgar</a> cave network and into the western valleys of <a href="Urland" class="internal-link">Urland</a> in the winter of 690PA, initiating the <a href="Hobgoblin Invasion of Urland" class="internal-link">Hobgoblin Invasion</a> that destroyed the Kingdom of Urland and established what became the <a href="Ak-Saray" class="internal-link">Ak-Saray</a> confederation.</p>  
<p> The Shroomfever of 678PA and the subsequent collapse of Urgil Cave had severed his clan from all contact with the rest of the Mitimgar network; what emerged from the Kortopi Mountains at his command was an army of warriors, workers and children with nowhere else to go. They were led by a man in deep red enchanted plate, the legendary Thirsting Guard, whose magical properties were said to compound with every enemy it had slain and who had commanded them with only one direction in mind. He was thirty-nine years old at the beginning of the War when he led personally in every major engagement of the invasion: Elpfield, Foarley, Hadley, the long Siege of Dursan. He was advised throughout by the young Grand Priest Chiakal Ken-Har and served by his champion <a href="Korgaz" class="internal-link">Korgaz the Blade of Noon</a>, whose individual prowess delivered the wars most celebrated kills. Between the fall of Dursan in 695PA and his own death at Gobsfall beyond,  he had already begun organizing the clans occupation of the western territories into the governance structures that would become Ak-Saray, work left unfinished when <a href="Kifrir" class="internal-link">Kifrir the Mirror-Dwarf</a> killed him at Gobsfall Keep in 696PA and the advance was permanently halted at the Trinmo.</p> <p>The naming traditions of Ak-Saray reflect his status without requiring elaboration. Lake Tirbo formerly Hadley Lakem bears his name and the modern day Dirgae call themselves the Tirbi in his honour. Hobgiliath, formerly Dursan, the capital of the kingdom he destroyed is named for his clan and the confederation itself carries his dream forward as a political entity. The Thirsting Guard, was lost to the Dirgae at Gobsfall when he fell to Kifrir. In Ak-Sarays religious tradition Tirbo occupies a position that sits somewhere between a god in the Kaladi Mysticism sense and a mere ancestral hero in the ordinary sense. Tirbo died at forty-five, his people named a lake, a city and a nation after him but their continued survival are his greatest Legacy.</p>
