She was said to be the most beautiful woman in the lands of the <a href="Trinmo" class="internal-link">Trinmo</a> and the Mead, with hazel locks flowing down her shoulders, and it is for them that she is remembered as the Hazel Queen.</p> <p>She followed Kar II, the Seer King, on the throne. In the year before her death the <a href="Baeling Empire" class="internal-link">Baeling</a> conquered Weadland, and her successor Gunther I founded the <a href="Korsfall College" class="internal-link">Korsfall College</a> six years after her death. She is recorded in the <a href="Rulers of the Meadi" class="internal-link">Rulers of the Meadi</a>.</p>
