	<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Viago.jpg" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;">  </div> <p>Viago I zi Zav, called Firespear, was the first Ras of Vihr, ruling from the Proclamation of 241PA until his death in 258PA. He was a warlord of the Zav before he was a king. He commanded the High Vihri host through the Wermian Incursions, broke the lords who would not bow to him in the Unification Wars, and raised Zavlech on the lake where his people had always gathered. Every king of his house was measured against him, and most were found short, as a remark about <a href="Zervind II" class="internal-link">Zervind II</a> shows: a man who wore the crown of Viago Firespear but could not lift it.</p>  <p>Viago was born about 180PA among the Zav, a Horn Lord's line on the shore of Lake Zav in the days of the <a href="Vihrmen Federation" class="internal-link">Vihrmen Federation</a>. His world was not yet a feudal one. The lords of his generation were closer to the warlords of an early age than to the landed knights who came later: leaders of war bands who moved between halls and seasonal camps, held their people by gift and prowess and not by charter, and owed one another only what a pact sworn at Trydlew required. The Zav gathered at the lake as their fathers had, but there was no city there yet. He had a younger brother, Jasper, called the Tall, who did not live to see the end of the war.</p> <h3>The Incursions</h3> <p>The First Incursion, from 215 to 219PA, was mostly an affair of Wermia itself, and Viago was by then a lord of standing. The Second, from 226 to 240PA, was the war of his life. He commanded the High Vihri host and the largest allied contingent from the first year to the last, riding with <a href="Gethric" class="internal-link">Gethric</a> and the Vihri Union of Vanquishers, with dwarves among the men beneath his banner. Jasper the Tall fell in the fighting. It was in these years that he was first seen with the weapon that gave him his name: a long spear, its head tempered in the manner that Gethric's companions and the old Wermian smiths had learned, which he was said to be able to engulf in flames of red and black in battle. They were not the white fire of the Prophet, and his enemies later made what they could of that. The spear was in his hand when Gethric died in 240PA and the war ended.</p> <h3>The Proclamation and the Unification Wars</h3> <p>Viago came out of the war as the dominant soldier of the Vihri world, with a certain divine legitimacy. Within months he proclaimed the Kingdom of Vihr, in 241PA, and dissolved the Federation by asserting that a new order had replaced the old. He took <a href="Gethricism" class="internal-link">Gethricism</a> as the creed of the new realm, in the spirit of the words of <a href="Volboris" class="internal-link">Volboris</a> the Healer, and the Church of Iztaw had his support from the first. The lords of the south, who had kept away from the front, refused him. The Unification Wars lasted a decade. Viago rode to both of the great battles at the head of the High Vihri host, with a small force of knights on the great unicorns of the Expanse: Tlok Valley in 246PA, where he broke the lords of the centre and the east, and Glancz in 251PA, where he broke the Sarloni and Wyt lords of the south for good. Turvik practice was forbidden in the lands he held, though the Ullkustone at Trydlew was left standing.</p>  <p>After Glancz he went back to Lake Zav and built the capital there. Zavlech grew on the shore where the Zav had camped since before memory, and in the years that followed Szievek, Perliav and Pretko were founded or enlarged as instruments of the crown and of the Church. In 254PA the Doctrine of Human Exceptionalism was first spoken of in the teaching of the realm. Many humans of the time felt that they had been chosen, having suffered the Incursion and overcome it, though dwarves had fought beside them. Viago does not seem to have opposed the idea.</p>  <p>He grew very old for a man of his time. In his last years it was said that he was more active than men twenty years younger, and that he could still throw the Firespear a great distance. He had three sons. The eldest, Viago, was born during the Incursion, and Mirgo and Breczo came after the war, late in the king's life. His enemies spread the rumour that the eldest's line was tainted with Demonblood, since the boy was born in the years of the Abyssal advance, but the rumour did not keep Viago II from the throne. The name Jasper returns among the later kings of the house.</p> <h3>Death and Legacy</h3> <p>Viago I died in Zavlech in 258PA at about the age of seventy-eight. His eldest son reigned for only four years, and the House of Zav ruled from the capital until its senior line died out in 501PA. The Firespear passed down that line and was lost when it ended, and no one knows where it lies now. Several crowns have been made for the Rasi of Vihr over the centuries, and the one that the remark about Zervind II means is Viago's own, which his house wore for generations. He left the kingdom, its capital, and the alliance of crown and Church that has shaped Vihr ever since.</p>
