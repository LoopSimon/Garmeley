The kings of Gisvale fought the ogres of the <a href="Kortopis" class="internal-link">Kortopi</a> foothills for centuries, and it was Ardol III who conquered the eastern foothills near the Bend of the <a href="Trinmo" class="internal-link">Trinmo</a> from the ogres of the vale.</p> <p>The hundreds of valleys of those hills were never truly bound by human hands, and the ogres of the higher vales troubled Gisvale long after him, but the foothills were held and the name stuck. Ardol was a king of the House of Gismar, which claimed descent from the hero <a href="Gismar" class="internal-link">Gismar</a>, and he was followed by Gisling II. His reign is recorded in the <a href="Rulers of the Meadi" class="internal-link">Rulers of the Meadi</a>.</p>
