<p>Vlad V was King of Halgrach from 600PA until 628PA and the last Ruler of the of Jerinov Dynasty that had ruled the country from 448PA onwards, due to the tragic events surrounding his rule and death, he is colloquially known as "the Cursed", his body is currently buried in the Grey Mausoleum of <a href="Izhevk" class="internal-link">Izhevk</a>. Vlad ascended the Throne following the death of his father, the King Andrej I, in 600PA at the age of 22 as the only surviving child of the former king. Vlad married Irina of Berzi, later known as "The good Queen Irina" who was said to possess a great love for the commonfolk and reached near saintly status for her work to improve the life of orphans and victims of druid raids during the Roymer War. Irina would live to see the death of her husband and her two children as mentioned below, living until her 84th birthday under the protection of the Kings of Stir and the honoured late Monarch of the Realm.</p>
<p>Only a little more than a year following Vlads ascension to the Throne the Last and greatest of the grachi-roymer wars would break out again, when the Druids of the southern circles convened and attacked thousands of grachi settlements, households and even some castles with hordes of rampaging animals and beasts of nature, an event forever known as the Day of the Elk. Vlad would never see the end of the War and the fighting that would slowly envelop all of Nostroya again over the course of the next decades. Vlad himself was said to have been an excellent and mighty warrior on the battlefield, renowned for his personal physical strength and the large Axe he wielded in combat, known as the Iron Cleaver, said to melt away the armour, flesh and bones of his foes.</p>
<p>Vlad and his two sons died following a skirmish on the road south of Durilsk after being ambushed by a circle of southern Druids, the Prince of Roygviil later considered the group part of the Roymer war effort and claimed credit for the kill, with it being seen as a large reason for the greater mobilization of the Kingdom and the eventual fall of Roygviil decades later during the rule of the of Stir Kings.</p>
<p>In late May of 927PA he is rumoured to have been the victim of a disdainful necromancer that broke into the Grey Mausoleum and resurrected the last of Jerinov Monarch to slave away as his bound servant.</p>
