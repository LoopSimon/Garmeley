	<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Silverbeard.jpg" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;">  </div> <p>Zawis I zi Worocek, called Silverbeard, was Ras of Vihr from 562 to 580PA and the founder of the House of Worocek, whose line held the throne for 130 years. He was a minor lord of the south who won the Vihri War of Succession, nine years of civil war that ended the Szinmo dynasty and cost Szievek two thirds of its lands. He is remembered as the king who beat the faction of the unchecked mages, and who was crowned at last in a realm that had been bleeding for most of a decade.</p>  <p>Zawis was born about 507PA at Nediv, a keep and town in the southern marches, which have fallen into irrelevance since and are barely a name on a map today. He was a Gri of the south, a lord of the Wyt country near Opuzna, and until the age of forty-six he was nothing more. House Terjan, which later held Opuzna, is vaguely related to the Worocek. Nothing is recorded of his youth beyond his holding of the keep, and the south itself was a quiet place under the Szinmo kings.</p> <h3>The War of Succession</h3> <p>Katerina I zi Szinmo died in 553PA with no clear heir, and with her the Szinmo dynasty ended. A cousin, Dobrov Szinmo, the Var of <a href="Szievek" class="internal-link">Szievek</a>, claimed the crown with the backing of his Vadcy, the family's seat. The Honoured Assembly and the lords of the south acclaimed Zawis. The Church at Iztaw hesitated, and in 555PA it canonised Tytus of Pinopki, the slayer of the Lich Mendizaba, as a symbol of the Wermian Accords' necessity and of sacred continuity in a time of confusion. That choice was not a judgement between the claimants, but it fell on a country already split, and in the end Iztaw came down on the side of the winner.</p> <p>The war lasted nine years. In its later years the Szinmo faction grew steadily more hated. Dobrov filled his ranks with mages beyond any control and broke the <a href="Wermian Accords" class="internal-link">Wermian Accords</a>, and the lords who might have waited began to come over to Zawis. He was armed against them in a suit of enchanted armour of pure silver, said to turn vile magic against those who cast it. The war ended in 562PA. Dobrov and four of his five sons were executed. The youngest, a boy, was spared and kept as a ward and captive, and with him the Szinmo survived. The House still holds Szievek today, although the Vadcy lost two thirds of its lands to the crown and its neighbours as the price of the war. Zawis was crowned at Zavlech in the same year, the first king of the House of Worocek.</p>  <p>His iconic epithet is plain. His beard had gone silver by the time he was crowned, at fifty-five, and the army that followed him called him Silverbeard before the court did. The armour of pure silver supplied the rest of the name, and the legend, and in a war about mages it was a promise as much as a protection. His enemies said he wore it out of fear. His soldiers saw that he wore it in the front rank.</p>  <p>He ruled for eighteen years. His task was to knit a realm that had been split along the lines of a family quarrel. He kept the lords of the south close, spared the surviving Szinmo heir, and held the Church to its role as the keeper of the Accords while giving it no cause to doubt the crown. He died in Zavlech in 580PA at about seventy-three, and Miskomir I succeeded him.</p>  <p>His line was the third royal line of Vihr, and it outlasted him by 112 years. It ended with Zawis III in 692PA, after the Usily Plot at Opuzna and the death of the king at the jaws of his own pet Glinsar. The Silverslach Trade, which made the silver of the mountain towns a business of its own, began a little after his reign, and no source ties it to him. While Nediv remains, the silver armour has not been seen since the House of Worocek fell.</p>
