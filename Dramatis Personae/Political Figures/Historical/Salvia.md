<p>Salvia Releado, also known as Salvia of the Moons or Salvia the Witch, was the Tyrant of <a href="Firocco" class="internal-link">Firocco</a> from 709PA until her betrayal at the hands of her own apprentice, the later Tyrant Harvina. A Sorcereress and trueborn mage by birth, virtually nothing is known about her life prior to leading the coup of 709 with an alliance of local nobles, mercenaries and her own personal circle of magically gifted individuals. She declared Firocco would be a subject to northern Jeledo no more and from hereon out be the free "Firocco Hegemony of the Grizani Islands". Her rule, although vilified and denounced as a period of dark magicks and evil slavery in modern times, was actually a rather successful one and saw a period of economic upswing and a restoration of a local identity in the Isles. Salvia is said to have "laid with the Moons" at night and lived for a remarkably long time, being 132 Years of Age at the time of her Banishment, although even then her mortal body was never fully destroyed according to legends and she still appears in various cevoli tales of folklore as a dark presence seeking freedom and a return to rule. Essentially nothing is known about the exact circumstances of Salvias demise, although many tales agree she was imprisoned or banished into an object, in most tales a piece of jewelry.</p>
