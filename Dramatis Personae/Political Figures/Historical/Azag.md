<p> The drow Azag, a young girl born in the great undercity of <a href="Tur Vuron" class="internal-link">Tur Vuron</a>, was chosen by the Sect of Azag as the chosen Consort for their great god, history does not even remember her real name before her ascension and eventual demise. </p> 
 <p>The elevation and ritual worship of Azag as the bride to be of their very own god represented the culmination of a century long plan devised by the Priests of Vuron end would be finalised when, shortly after the young girl was crowned as Queen, she was made to drink the Elixir of the Consort and be buried within the Temple Complex of Azagon. The Elixir, infused by generations of shamans and priests with potent death magic through ritual suicide, would charge the girls spirt and soul with so much latent energy it would bind her to her remnant body for perpetuity, so she may always be the beautiful consort to Vuron. Upon drinking she immediately died and was then buried and her temple sealed, the merely twenty year old Consort was alone then, and would be for the next 2600 years.</p> 
 <p>When Tur Vuron itself fell, noone disturbed the peace of Azag, when the great Flood destroyed the later and former empire of the drow even later in 1090, noone disturbed her either, when Turvik faithfuls buried deep into the earth to bury their dead, stumbling near her ruins, they erected wards and walls and left the trapped Queen alone still.</p> 
 <p>It was a simple colony of spiders, around the beginning of the 9th century, that found their way finally into the broken cavern walls of Azags Mausoleum, her mind forced itself onto the beasts and she began her work: dig up the mummified bodies of her priests and defile them in vengeance, turning them into grotesque monstrosities, clear out the rubble of millenia and find passage until they met a lake too far too cross, erect pillars of spider husks to project the Queens mind and call out further, and breed and breed and breed to serve the Queen faithfully.</p> 
 <p>When <a href="Horatio Tuliper" class="internal-link">Horatio Tuliper</a>, a halfling member of the <a href="The Golden Ring" class="internal-link">Golden Ring</a>, stepped near the ruins above the turvik henge, above the dark lake and above the queens resting place, fate finally granted Azag a little respite. The Sorcerer carried within himself an Anchor to this plane, a failed experiment and dangerous font of powers, that had allowed him to summon magicks and become a hero, but also slowly eroded his mind, made it open for suggestions, suggestions such as the call of Azag. And so the Golden Ring, deceived by equal parts as worried for their friend, ventured into the Howling Henge and found their way towards the Queens Room. Destiny would however not grant Azag her wish, the <a href="The Bad Omen" class="internal-link">heroes of the bad Omen</a>, disrupted her efforts to return to the world of the living in a body reforged with Horatios power, but instead finally it granted Azag what she truly wanted: a final death.</p>
