<p>Harrusham Orazatar was a Rakhian loyalist who in 12PA, twelve years after the eruption of Al-Akka, declared himself Fireking and proclaimed the existence of New Rakh> in the city of Gibaresh, the rakhi settlement that would later become Calmbrook. His claim rested entirely on birth and lineage: the conviction that those Rakhi who had survived the catastrophe, whether by chance of geography or accident of absence from the heartland, were born and bred to rule and the rightful inheritors of an empire whose physical destruction did not extinguish its legitimacy. For fifteen years, Harrusham maintained the forms of that empire in exile: the titles, the court ceremonies, the administrative traditions of the old Rakhian order, supported by Kerlaeni mercenaries whose loyalty he had purchased rather than earned. The reliance on those mercenaries would end him. In 27PA, his own bodyguards  killed him. They sacrificed more than five thousand of the surviving Rakhi to <a href="Turvik Paganism" class="internal-link">Mornal</a>, the Turvik Goddess of Earth and Family, and crowned their warlord Arvald as the first Lord of <a href="Kerlaes" class="internal-link">Kerlaes</a>. New Rakh had lasted fifteen years. With Harrushams death, the last institutional claim to Rakhian imperial continuity was extinguished.</p>
