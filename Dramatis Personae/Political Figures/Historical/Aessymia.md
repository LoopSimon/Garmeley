<p>Aessymia was a Leadi Cyracli Mage and the Oracle of <a href="Urden" class="internal-link">Shoyal</a> during the mid to late 6th century BA. As the Oracle of Shoyal she was the effective spiritual Leader of the United League of Cyraclion and responsible with divining the future. She oversaw the large scale conflict with the emerging Empire of <a href="Rakh" class="internal-link">Rakh</a> and successfully lead her people to victory at the Battle of Milasam. Her eventual political decline followed a stern campaign of anti-kynotic restriction and accusations of many opponents the Oracle was attempting to crown herself as a tyrant of the Cities. In 489BA she committed suicide in the tradition of ancient Cyraclion by swallowing a poisonous herb known as the Tanlea.</p>
<p>She was known as the Palatea, the "Daughter of Decay", for her love of Disease and Plague related magicks and curses that she summoned upon the invading Rakhi Forces at Milasam, notable this nickname was seen as an honorific not a morbid moniker. Her popularity was later used against her, as well as her embracement of the reputation as a "Saviour of the Cities" as evidence she was trying to elevate herself over all other Cyclopi.</p>
