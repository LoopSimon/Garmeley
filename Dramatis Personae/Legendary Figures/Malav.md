<p>Almost nothing is known about the mysterious figure, known as Malav or sometimes "The Malav", who destroyed and absorbed the <a href="Yari" class="internal-link">Yari</a> <a href="Qezinas" class="internal-link">Qezinas</a> in the year 838PA, becoming Yarimanji.</p> 
<p>Based on the fractured surviving records of the Circle of Qezinas that have found their way to public eyes and ears, Malav is often described as an Outsider and a wanderer, strange and foreign, leading to the popular belief that he likely was no local to the northern Smaragd Coast or southern Behera, if not Behera in general, where he found and ended Qezinas at his hicui.</p> 
<p>The Malav has become a tale of horror and warning amongst the local Safir population, specifically the many vekti that worshipped Qezinas as one of their patron Yari, and the few surviving Ginru of Qezinas have sworn eternal vengeance on the figure. His horned face, singular eye and colourful cloak are common markers within the stories surrounding him and his appearance that Qezinas hicui almost 100 years ago, although it is unclear which parts of these are permanent fixtures of his appearance or simply works of magic conjured by the figure during its battle with the Yari, as well as whether or not Malav always looked like this or his image changed following Qezinas demise and his ascension.</p>
