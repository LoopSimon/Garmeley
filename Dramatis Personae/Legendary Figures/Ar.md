<p>Ar of the Hills was a mythical human hero from central <a href="Othirdon" class="internal-link">Othirdon</a>, said to have performed wondrous deeds with the Sword of the Stars, a powerful relic weapon. The Arvale, Arpass as well as various settlements and locations in Othirdon and the Westerlands are named after him, and both Meadi and Locni traditions incorporate him within their folklore. He was said to have slain the Wendigo of Dyra at the age of just 16 and later ventured into the Underworld itself to retrieve the soul of his beloved. </p><p>The Sword of Stars as a weapon has real historical evidence as a weapon of the same name was wielded by the members of the House of Por until its line was extinguished in the mid 6th century PA. It later resurfaced during the 10 century PA in the hands of the so called "Featherknight", a mysterious Warrior that accompanied the infamous author <a href="Hubert of Tanverley" class="internal-link">Hubert of Tanvelery</a> on his later travels.</p>
