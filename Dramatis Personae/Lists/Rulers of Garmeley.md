<p>The Riverkings and Dukes of Garmeley</p> <h3>The House of Garm</h3> <small>51PA - 648PA</small> <p><a href="Alfin I" class="internal-link">Alfin I</a> "Leafback", from 51PA</p> <p>Hyrek I</p>



<p>Ahlef II, until 648PA</p>

Ahlef II fell at the Battle of Greyfields in the Eight Years War, and with him the line of Alfin ended.

<h3>The Ducal House of Redflow</h3> <small>648PA - 922PA</small> <p>Tyla of Aldale, Duchess, from 648PA</p>

Tyla refused the crown and proclaimed herself Duchess of Garmeley to prevent a civil war. Her house took the name of the capital.


<p>Jakub III, reigning by 690PA</p>

Duke Jakub III sent Garmish knights to the last stand of Urland against the Hobgoblin Invasion in 696PA.

<p>Aras II, reigning in 805PA</p> <p>Albrecht V</p> <p>Eadwin II</p> <p><a href="Agnes I" class="internal-link">Agnes I</a>, until 922PA</p>

Agnes I died at the Midsummer Festival of 922PA, aged 26, unmarried and without issue.

<h3>The Disputed Throne</h3> <small>922PA - present</small>

The Riverthrone is claimed by two parties. Hynek of Forr, the nephew of Agnes I by her half-sister, was her heir by blood, and was killed in February 927PA. Coster of Melmbirth claims the throne by ancient custom. See the [[Garmish Succession Crisis]].
