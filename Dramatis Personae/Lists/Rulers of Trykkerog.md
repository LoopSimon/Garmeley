<p>The rulers of <a href="Trykkerog" class="internal-link">Trykkerog</a> since the first crowns rose among the <a href="Bykmåli" class="internal-link">Bykmåli</a> in the 5th century. No single ruler has ever held the whole island. The Korres of <a href="Vignasoya" class="internal-link">Vignasoya</a> have ruled the Skori of the west since 404PA, the House of Baeling ruled Melmers Bay and for a time most of the island from 465PA until the fall of the <a href="Baeling Empire" class="internal-link">Baeling Empire</a> in 590PA, and after almost a century of division the Kingdoms of <a href="Juelfluk" class="internal-link">Juelfluk</a> and <a href="Baeling" class="internal-link">Baeling</a> were founded in 617PA and 684PA. The smaller powers of the island, the free cities, jarldoms, the Archbishop of Tversby, Hvirfill and the <a href="Zren" class="internal-link">Zren</a>, are not listed here.</p>

<h3>Ancestral Figures</h3>
<small>Pre-Dynasty</small>

<p>Juel, called the Eel of the West, a legendary hero of the 2nd century BA who is said to have built the tower that became Juelrup Keep, and after whom the castle and the Kingdom of Juelfluk are named.</p>
<p><a href="Ylva" class="internal-link">Ylva</a> Feathercall, heroine of the <a href="Fjorisjom Saga" class="internal-link">Fjorisjom Saga</a> of 201PA, who sang the wyrm Fagnir to sleep. She is remembered as the Mother of the Seal Lords of Alsebyn, the forebears of the House of Baeling, and both the old and the new rulers of Baeling claim descent from her.</p>
<p><a href="Toski" class="internal-link">Toski</a> Golleki, called Ironhair and Trollbinder, hero of the Saga and captain of the Rauttafein. House Udry of Juelfluk claims descent from him, and through him from Juel.</p>
<p><a href="Egill" class="internal-link">Egill</a> Bylgjurek, called Foambeard, champion of Nyllena and the great hero of the Skori in the Saga. Both royal houses of Vignasoya have claimed him as an ancestor.</p>

<h3>Korres of Vignasoya</h3>
<small>404PA - present</small>

*The Korre is chosen at the Great Thing of the Skori from among the kin of the royal house, and the crown has passed between houses only when the Thing turned away from the old line.*

<h5>House Hjaldring</h5>
<small>404PA - 548PA</small>

<p>Jassvr I "the Elder", 404PA - 431PA</p>

*First Korre of Vignasoya, declared at Mannelik in 404PA.*

<p>Hjalmar I, 431PA - 452PA</p>
<p>Ketil I, 452PA - 470PA</p>
<p>Jassvr II, 470PA - 498PA</p>
<p>Ulfar I "Broadaxe", 498PA - 521PA</p>
<p>Hjalmar II, 521PA - 548PA</p>

*The Baeling Emperor Ragnvald I invaded the southern coast of Vignasoya. Hjalmar fell in the defence of Mannelik in 548PA, and Faersfur, Wogni and Forik were lost in the following years.*

<h5>House Roering</h5>
<small>548PA - 603PA</small>

<p>Eyvind I, 548PA - 566PA</p>

*Jarl of Skeppbirk, chosen Korre by the Great Thing after the fall of Mannelik. He bent the knee to the Emperor and paid tribute, but kept the old gods, and the seat of the Korre remained at Skeppbirk.*

<p>Orm I, 566PA - 581PA</p>

*The risings of the Skori lords of the Wolfsbay coast against Baeling rule began in his reign.*

<p>Ulfar II "the Wolf", 581PA - 603PA</p>

*Won back the lost coast before the fall of the Baeling Empire in 590PA and returned the seat of the Korre to Mannelik. The Skori took Zeeport, and in 594PA Vignasoya took over the governance of Droil. At his death the Great Thing returned the crown to the old line, and his descendants have held Skeppbirk as Jarls and Lord Reavers ever since.*

<h5>House Hjaldring (restored)</h5>
<small>603PA - present</small>

<p>Jassvr III, 603PA - 629PA</p>
<p>Haddr "the Great", 629PA - 667PA</p>

*Subdued the jarls of the west and Rensköldsvik and proclaimed himself Great King of Skorland in 641PA. The Great Kingdom of Skorland broke apart after his death in 667PA, and Rensköldsvik regained its independence.*

<p>Sigvat I, 667PA - 694PA</p>

*Skori sailors under Lars Gerjodi crossed the North Tirean Ocean and reached the Gerjodi Islands off Aisligos in 688PA.*

<p>Brandr I, 694PA - 719PA</p>

*Settlers from Vignasoya, Cimfon and Baeling founded Grynborg in 719PA.*

<p>Ketil II, 719PA - 741PA</p>

*Zeeport was lost to the Weadi rising of 737PA.*

<p>Asvid I, 741PA - 768PA</p>
<p>Ulfar III, 768PA - 792PA</p>

*Droil rose against its Skori rulers and freed itself from Vignasoya in 772PA.*

<p>Jassvr IV, 792PA - 823PA</p>
<p>Hjalmar III, 823PA - 850PA</p>
<p>Sigvat II, 850PA - 879PA</p>
<p>Orm II, 879PA - 906PA</p>
<p>Jassvr V, 906PA - present</p>

<h3>Kings of the Bay and Emperors of the Baeling</h3>
<small>465PA - 590PA</small>

<p><a href="Valund Baeling" class="internal-link">Valund "the Whale"</a>, 465PA - 499PA</p>

*Lord of Alsebyn, victor of the Battle of Assafurd over King Sweyn of Grelm, after which he united the lords of Melmers Bay and declared himself King of the Bay. Counted as the first Emperor of the Baeling.*

<p>Erik I, 499PA - 524PA</p>

*Eldest son of Valund. Proclaimed the Baeling Empire in 517PA, the year in which the conversion of Trykkerog outside Vignasoya was completed.*

<p>Håkan I, 524PA - 536PA</p>

*Younger son of Valund, succeeded his brother.*

<p>Ragnvald I "the Conqueror", 536PA - 557PA</p>

*Conquered the southern coast of Vignasoya, among it Faersfur, Wogni, Mannelik and Forik. Baeling Conquest of Weadland 549PA.*

<p>Håkan II, 557PA - 574PA</p>

*Baeling Conquest of Droil and northern Tintarol 563PA.*

<p>Ottar I, 574PA - 583PA</p>

*Failed Conquest of Halgrach. The risings of the Skori lords of the Wolfsbay coast began in his reign.*

<p>Erik II "the Last", 583PA - 590PA</p>

*Last of the direct line of Valund. The Skori had already thrown off Baeling rule on the Wolfsbay when he died without an heir in 590PA, and the Empire broke apart among its lords, warders and captains.*

<h3>Interregnum of the Runeblades</h3>
<small>590PA - 684PA</small>

*No ruler held Melmers Bay for almost a century. Its towns were ruled by petty lords, imperial warders and the captains of mercenary companies, and the raids on the southern coasts of the Torn Sea began again. In the east House Udry founded the Kingdom of Juelfluk in 617PA.*

<h3>Kings and Queens of Juelfluk</h3>
<small>617PA - present</small>

*By old Jueli custom the crown passes to the eldest child of the monarch, whether son or daughter, and every ruler of the kingdom has been of House Udry. Before the founding of the kingdom the Lords and Ladies of Juelrup Castle held the Malksbay, from 517PA as vassals of the Baeling Empire.*

<p>Rossgi I, 617PA - 644PA</p>

*Lord of Juelrup, declared the hegemony of House Udry over the lands and peoples of the Malksbay after the fall of the Baeling Empire and founded the Kingdom of Juelfluk in 617PA.*

<p>Madeley I, 644PA - 671PA</p>

*First of the ruling Queens of Juelfluk.*

<p>Munja I, 671PA - 690PA</p>

*Sivdil the Tall claimed the Baeling name and all the former lands of the Empire in 684PA. The Wars of Baeling Succession began in his reign.*

<p>Bjark I, 690PA - 718PA</p>
<p>Madeley II, 718PA - 749PA</p>
<p>Rossgi II, 749PA - 763PA</p>
<p>Signe I, 763PA - 795PA</p>
<p>Emil I, 795PA - 821PA</p>

*6th War of Baeling Succession 801PA - 806PA, after which Tversby became an independent city-state under the Archbishop of Trykkerog.*

<p>Madeley III, 821PA - 842PA</p>
<p>Munja II, 842PA - 866PA</p>

*Granted the colony of Nilborg its Charter of rights in 845PA, with the first Lord of Hremny as its leader.*

<p>Rossgi III, 866PA - 884PA</p>
<p>Bjark II, 884PA - 921PA</p>

*9th War of Baeling Succession 911PA - 916PA, in which his daughter and heir Madeley led the Jueli army against Troese.*

<p><a href="Madeley IV" class="internal-link">Madeley IV</a>, 921PA - present</p>

*Her children are Prince Emil and Princess Signe of Udry.*

<h3>Kings of Baeling</h3>
<small>684PA - present</small>

*The Kings of Baeling claim descent from Valund and count their regnal numbers on from the Emperors of the old House of Baeling, although the present house descends from Sivdil the Tall. Historians call it the Second House of Baeling.*

<p>Sivdil "the Tall", 684PA - 711PA</p>

*Leader of the Runeblade Mercenary Company, claimed descent from Valund and the Baeling name in 684PA, united Alsebyn and Sallesand and laid claim to all the former lands of the Empire. The Wars of Baeling Succession with Juelfluk began in his reign.*

<p>Håkan III, 711PA - 733PA</p>

*Founding of Grynborg off Aisligos with Baeling support 719PA. Reconquest of the Hagshorn and Jallbreg 720PA.*

<p>Erik III, 733PA - 752PA</p>
<p>Ingvald I, 752PA - 770PA</p>
<p>Håkan IV, 770PA - 794PA</p>

*Great outbreak of the Treeplague on Trykkerog 772PA.*

<p>Ottar II, 794PA - 815PA</p>

*6th War of Baeling Succession 801PA - 806PA, after which Tversby became an independent city-state under the Archbishop of Trykkerog.*

<p>Ingvald II, 815PA - 838PA</p>

*The plague quarantine of 820PA cut Grynborg off from Baeling. Levangr War of Independence 829PA, Levangr broke away in alliance with the Blijgrat League.*

<p>Erik IV, 838PA - 861PA</p>
<p>Sivdil II, 861PA - 887PA</p>
<p>Håkan V, 887PA - 913PA</p>

*The 9th War of Baeling Succession broke out over Troese in 911PA. Håkan died in 913PA in the middle of the war.*

<p><a href="Varald I" class="internal-link">Varald I</a>, 913PA - present</p>

*Held Troese against the Jueli army of the crown princess Madeley, the later Queen Madeley IV, until the end of the 9th War in 916PA. His heir is his son, Crown Prince Håkan.*
