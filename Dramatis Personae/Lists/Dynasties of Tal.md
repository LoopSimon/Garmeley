<p>The Dynasties of Tal are the three houses that ruled the earliest recorded realms of the <a href="Yppori" class="internal-link">Yppori</a>, the Chagor, the Melop and the Muyra, from the founding of the Chagor Dynasty in 1099BA to the fall of the Muyra Dynasty in 229BA. The Yppori speak of dynasties and not of empires, for the realm was reckoned by the house that ruled it, and each house held that it ruled by the favour of Chenza, the Sunrider (see <a href="Chenism" class="internal-link">Chenism</a>), and that a house that failed had lost it. The age that followed their fall is remembered as the <a href="Era of Thunders" class="internal-link">Era of Thunders</a>.</p>
<h2>The Ivory Crown</h2>
<p>The symbol of the dynasties was the Ivory Crown of Tal, carved from a single tusk, which the Yppori held to be a tusk of the Parakar, the elephant of the Sunrider. To wear it was to rule as the favoured of Chenza. Muyra refused to wear it and took the title Lord of Lords instead, and when the Muyra Dynasty fell the Crown was sealed in a great vault. It was later stolen, and it resurfaced on the mainland during the later days of the Empire of <a href="Naigan" class="internal-link">Naigan</a>. Today it is held by the rulers of <a href="Byamon" class="internal-link">Byamon</a>.</p>
<h2>The Chagor Dynasty</h2>
<p>The Chagor Dynasty was founded in 1099BA, when <a href="Chagor" class="internal-link">Chagor</a>, a Talang of the city of Char, brought the early cities of the Talang under one crown and claimed the Ivory Crown of Tal at Char. From Char his house ruled for close to five centuries, the longest reign of the three.</p>
<h2>The Melop Dynasty</h2>
<p>In 623BA <a href="Melop" class="internal-link">Melop</a> rose in the south of Tal, took the Ivory Crown from the Chagor and founded the Melop Dynasty. It was a hard and warlike house, and it lasted scarcely more than a century, until its forces were defeated by Muyra in 503BA.</p>
<h2>The Muyra Dynasty</h2>
<p>In 503BA <a href="Muyra" class="internal-link">Muyra</a> was raised as Lord of Lords in southern Tal, and the house that he founded ruled as a confederacy of lords under a Lord of Lords and not under a crowned king. It lasted for the better part of three centuries, until in 229BA the lords stopped obeying after a quarrel over the succession and the dynasty dissolved into warring houses. Its fall began the Era of Thunders. The Muyra Dynasty also erased every depiction of the earlier rulers of Tal from the face of the earth, and no likeness of the Chagor or the Melop survives.</p>
