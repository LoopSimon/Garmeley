<p>The Khats of the Khatrym of Kherova, rulers of the Kheri peoples since the unification of the lake shore city states at the Day of Brothers in 262PA. The title of Khat descends through the male line exclusively, with succession passing to the eldest son of the Erkhati, the first wife, or in her absence to the eldest son of the second or third wife. All Khats claim descent from the legendary hero Khariel, slayer of the Wyrm Thamira, whose lineage the Khari and Heriod dynasties both trace through different branches of the same ancient line.</p>

<h3>Ancestral Figure</h3>
<small>Pre-Dynasty</small>

<p>Tiblin I "the Strong", in 156PA is the victor of the Battle of the Yevan River, who united the Kheri city states in common cause against the Kingdom of Rean and laid the foundations from which the unified Khatrym would eventually emerge. While not formally a Khat, he is recognized by all subsequent rulers as the ancestor from whom the Khari line drew its legitimacy and whose name became the most honoured in the Kherovan royal tradition.</p>

<h3>Era of the Empty Seat</h3>
<small>593PA - 661PA</small>

*With the main Khari line extinct at Bagsar III's death, two noble houses put forward rival claimants each asserting nearest kinship to the Khari bloodline, House Zirad and House Aldani, both with strong cases and neither with sufficient military or political dominance to force the other's submission. The seat of the Khat sat contested for 68 years in a cold war of noble factions, proclamations and manoeuvring that stopped short of outright civil war but prevented any legitimate governance from the throne. Both original claimants died without resolution, their heirs inheriting the standoff. The Heriod line, tracing descent from Heriod, a brother of Bagsar III himself who had never sought the throne in his lifetime, emerged gradually as the de facto inheritor whose dynastic claim was powerful and whose political base had grown steadily across the decades of deadlock. Tiblin VII, of the Heriod line, ended the Era not through negotiation but through action,  launching the Yellow Wars against Grevenna in 660PA and using the unifying pressure of external conflict to consolidate the Kherovan nobility behind him before the rival factions could mount a coherent response.*

<h3>The Heriod Dynasty</h3>
<small>661PA - present</small>

<p>Tiblin VII "the Consolidator", 661PA - 693PA</p>

*Founder of the Heriod Dynasty and the Khat who ended the Era of the Empty Seat. Launched the Yellow Wars against Grevenna in 660PA as the unifying act of his consolidation, drawing the fractious Kherovan nobility behind a common cause before his claim to the throne could be effectively contested. Signed the peace at Kulum in 668PA with Grand Lord Stefan I of Grevenna. His long reign of 32 years established the Heriod line's legitimacy beyond further meaningful challenge.*
