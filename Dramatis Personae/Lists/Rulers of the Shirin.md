<p>Epithets of individual rulers are not included for all entries. For the Twintail Imperium, the regnal name is assumed upon ascension, all rulers of the Natani Dynasty bear the name Natan, the Lisil Dynasty the name Lisil, and the Yoroy Dynasty the name Yoroy.</p>

<p>The Silent War period (560PA–572PA) saw three successive Natani Imperators rule in rapid succession. Their reigns are noted accordingly.</p>

<p>This list accounts for the honorary year of mourning as the last year of the previous ruler's reign where applicable to the Chihim and Aatar periods.</p>
