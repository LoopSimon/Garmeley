<p>The Kings of the Great Kingdom of <a href="Urland" class="internal-link">Urland</a>, seated on the Gryphon Throne, from the kingdoms founding in 29PA to its fall in 696PA. Two dynasties ruled across 667 years: the Dunling Dynasty, which produced the kingdoms earliest consolidation and its greatest rulers, and the Rassel Dynasty, which inherited a mature kingdom, weathered through civil conflict but ultimately could not survive the <a href="Ak-Saray" class="internal-link">Hobgoblin Invasion</a> from the Kortopi Mountains.</p>

<h3>The Dunling Dynasty</h3>
<h5>The House of Dunling</h5>
<small>29PA - 494PA</small>

<p>Versil I, 29PA - 49PA</p>

*The founding king, whose name became the most honored in Urlander tradition, adopted by many subsequent monarchs who sought to ground their claim in the kingdoms origins. The circumstances of his rise from the Province of Garm following the collapse of the Rakhian Empire are imperfectly recorded, but the kingdom he established endured for nearly seven centuries.

<p>Jeoffrik I "the Red", 49PA - 66PA</p>
<p>Versil II, 66PA - 87PA</p>
<p>Readwulf I, 87PA - 99PA</p>
<p>Jeoffrik II, 99PA - 104PA</p>
<p>Ansil I, 104PA - 131PA</p>
<p>Osrik I, 131PA - 150PA</p>
<p>Versil III "the Well-Advised", 150PA - 172PA</p>
<p>Gergil I, 172PA - 180PA</p>
<p>Ansil II, 180PA - 191PA</p>
<p>Answulf I, 191PA - 212PA</p>
<p>Osrik II "the Good", 212PA - 236PA</p>
<p>Remir I, 236PA - 249PA</p>

*Last of the main Dunling line. His death without a suitable direct heir ended the primary branch after two and a half centuries of unbroken succession. The transition to the cadet branch of Chloris, Osrik II's cousin was managed without major civil crisis, though a brief interregnum of two years preceded Tergil Is coronation.*

<h5>The House of Chloris</h5>
<small>251PA - 494PA</small>

<p>Tergil I, 251PA - 278PA</p>

<p>Johran I, 278PA - 299PA</p>
<p>Edric I "the Zealous", 299PA - 329PA</p>

*The defining religious king of Urland's history. His thirty year reign coincided with and drove the Gethric conversion that transformed the kingdom's spiritual and institutional character.*

<p>John I, 330PA - 341PA</p>

<p>Wildur I, 341PA - 359PA</p>
<p>Edric II, 359PA - 368PA</p>
<p>Idward I "the Keen", 368PA - 399PA</p>

*The first of the two rulers under whom Urland was at or near the peak of its continental power. His 31-year reign, the longest of the Dunling line, saw Urland exercise a degree of political and military influence that made it the single strongest or co-strongest nation on Ilrakhan.*

<p>Wildur II, 399PA - 414PA</p>
<p>Idward II, 414PA - 432PA</p>
<p>Edric III, 432PA - 448PA</p>
<p>John II "the Gryphon King", 448PA - 469PA</p>

*The second of Urland's great rulers and the one whose name the kingdoms golden age is most closely associated with. Where Idward I had built the institutional foundations of Urlander dominance, John II inhabited them fully, his court becoming the cultural and political reference point for the known world.*

<p>Edric IV, 469PA - 484PA</p>
<p>Berengar I, 484PA - 490PA</p>
<p>Cargyll I, 490PA - 494PA</p>

*Died aged six, having reigned for four years under regency. His death without heir extinguished both the main Dunling line and the Chloris cadet branch simultaneously, ending 465 years of Dunling rule and opening the succession crisis that the House of Rassel resolved.*

<h3>Interregnum</h3>
<small>494PA - 498PA</small>

*With the Dunling line extinct, two houses pressed competing claims to the Gryphon Throne: House Rassel under Wilhelm, and House Lindholm under Jeoffrey. A single engagement resolved the contest as Wilhelm of Rassel defeated Jeoffrey of Lindholm in the field, taking Jeoffrey and his sons prisoner. He held them under honourable confinement until his coronation, retaining Jeoffrey' youngest son for a decade thereafter as political insurance against any revival of the Lindholm claim. Wilhelm was crowned in 498PA, renaming himself Versil IV to anchor the new dynasty in the founding kings memory rather than his own house's name.*

<h3>The Rassel Dynasty</h3>
<small>498PA - 696PA</small>

<p>Versil IV (born Wilhelm of Rassel), 498PA - 520PA</p>
<p>Uther I, 520PA - 540PA</p>
<p>Berengar II, 540PA - 554PA</p>
<p>Thelion I "the Cruel", 554PA - 563PA</p>

<p>Uther II, 563PA - 584PA</p>
<p>John III, 584PA - 599PA</p>
<p>Wilhelm I, 599PA - 619PA</p>
<p>Kylian I "the False Gryphon", 619PA - 626PA</p>

*Accusations of bastardy, that Kylian was not the legitimate son of Wilhelm I, emerged during his reign and could not be definitively refuted or confirmed. The result was a civil war that lasted seven years before his stepbrother deposed him. Both his epithet and that of his successor were coined by Uther III's rule and Kylian himself used no such title.*

<p>Uther III "the True Gryphon", 626PA - 650PA</p>

<p>John IV, 650PA - 667PA</p>
<p>Wilhelm II, 667PA - 682PA</p>
<p>Uther IV "the Last Gryphon", 682PA - 696PA</p>

*Reigned eight years before the <a href="Ak-Saray" class="internal-link">Hobgoblin Invasion</a> began in 690PA and six years fighting it before dying in the siege of the Capital in 696PA alongside Duke Oswald III of Turfield and the majority of the kingdoms entrenched nobility. His nickname was not given by his own regime but by the successor states that followed, looking back at the man who had held the Gryphon Throne when it was finally lost. The Kingdom of Urland ended with him.*
