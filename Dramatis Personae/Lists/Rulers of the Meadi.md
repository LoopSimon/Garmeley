<p><a href="Ardol III" class="internal-link">Ardol III "Ogre Hammer" von Gismar</a>, 345PA - 378PA</p>

<p><i>The House von Gismar, descended from the hero <a href="Gismar" class="internal-link">Gismar</a>, held the throne until Diedrik of Medlan usurped it in 589PA. The von Medlan ruled until Liara I restored the line in 660PA. Ardol V "the Blessed" was the first Gethric born King of Gisvale.</i></p>

<p><i>Gisling I "Silverwing" took his name from a flying banner or a winged helm, and there was no magic in it. Ardol III earned the name Ogre Hammer in the wars against the ogres of the eastern foothills of the Kortopis. Tibor I "Strongarm" was a warrior king. Gisdol II "Steelfinger" was known for an iron-fingered gauntlet or prosthetic. Marlina I "the White Lady" took no husband, was beloved for her devotion to her people and was known for her white garb, and her younger sister's son succeeded her as Ardol VI. Diedrik I and Ringil I were Lords of <a href="Medlan" class="internal-link">Medlan</a> who held the throne of Gisvale between 589PA and 660PA.</i></p>

<p>Gunther III von Kor, 641PA - 664PA</p>

<p><i>The von Weide ended with Siegmar V "the last Tree" in 469PA, and the von Kor line began with Gernot I.</i></p>

<p><i>Thorsten I "the Sailor" opened the Trinmo to river trade and fleets. The name von Weide recalls Weidhill, the hill of willows from which the dynasty rose, and Torvald I was called the Tall for his height and the hill he ruled from. Thorvald II "the Small" was a strong ruler, and the name refers to the young age at which he took the throne and above all to Torvald the Tall. Siegmar V was given the name "the last Tree" after his death, as the last ruler of the Willows. The von Kor were castellans of the von Weide and one of the emerging houses of the realm, with records going back to the middle of the 4th century. When Siegmar V died, his sons having died before him, Gernot I claimed that Siegmar had chosen him on his deathbed, a claim that is heavily debated and largely unverifiable. Kar II "The Seer King" was rumoured to be an oracle able to foretell the future to a limited degree, and later scholars believe it true. Ulrike I "The Hazel Queen" was said to be the most beautiful woman in the lands of Trinmo and Mead, with hazel locks flowing down her shoulders.</i></p>

<p>Margaret II von Kor, 820PA - 829PA</p>
<p>Giselher I von Kor, 829PA - 841PA</p>
<p>Arngil II von Kor, 841PA - 858PA</p>
<p>Gilmar I von Kor, 858PA - 883PA</p>
<p>Gisela III "the Kind" von Kor, 883PA - 910PA</p>
<p><a href="Gisela IV" class="internal-link">Gisela IV von Kor</a>, 910PA - current</p>

<p><i>The union of Korsfall and Gisvale was proclaimed in 664PA, and Gunther III and Liara I ruled the united realms together until their firstborn son Gildher I was crowned King of Meadrath in 674PA. The crown passes to the eldest child regardless of sex. Margaret II was followed in 829PA by her younger brother Giselher I, who had fought the Meadi Othiri War of 824PA to 827PA as Prince Giselher. The <a href="Royal Congress" class="internal-link">Royal Congress</a> was founded under Gisela III in 898PA.</i></p>
<p><i>Gildher I "Springbringer" was named for the spring that followed the long winter of wars. Arngil I "the Judge" was a strict lawgiver.</i></p>
