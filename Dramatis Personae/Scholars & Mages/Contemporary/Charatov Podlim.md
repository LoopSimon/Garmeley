<p>Charatov Aljolla Mu Podlim is the Maester Matris at the <a href="Institute for Practical Energies" class="internal-link">Institute for Practical Energies of Almadus</a>, an honoured member of the Council of Almadin and one of the most important voices in the beheran and nirydian academic establishment in regards to Artificery, he is well respected within the both beheran as the "western" canon and has held a lecture at Ott itself, an honour bestowed to no other foreign practitioner of Prosum. Podlim is best known for his study and refinement of Single Core Automata and treaties on <a href="Yeorite" class="internal-link">yeorite</a> Isolationism.</p>
<p>Born in 837PA, the ilkizti <a href="Gnome" class="internal-link">Gnome</a> was brought up within the gnomish elite of the city and joined the local Academia and later the Institute at a rather young age, studying under the renowned Maester Perele Gobi herself. Charatov has become especially popular due to his programs including elvish and half-elvish as well as human practitioners of the arts in the programs, widening the field dominated by the ilkizti gnomes. His campaign to popularise the "lesser ars magicka" as <a href="Artificery" class="internal-link">Artificery</a> was known within the beheran space, is largely responsible for the rise in importance of the IPEA and its current position to be mentioned in the same vein as Ott. In February of 909PA, Charatov was declared a Hero of the Sulanate for his contributions to Behera and her people, the highest civilian honour bestowed upon members of the raised nobility within the Sultanates Sphere.</p>
