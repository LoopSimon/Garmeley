<p>The Sorceress Kara Gos is a grachi woman that acts as the guardian and associate of the southern Border of the <a href="Grachy" class="internal-link">Holy Ushkyn Republic of Grachy</a> at the Garrison of Tuskwatch, watching over the Hyllpass to Vihr and the dangers of the wild mountains, and lending aid to the Guard-Captain of Tuskwatch in practice and arcane expertise.</p>
<p>Gos is a former apprentice of <a href="Rigoran" class="internal-link">Boris Rigoran</a>, the Archmage of Edessa Tower north of the Grevi Mountains, and rumoured to be part of his information network tracking various trends and phenomena in the known world. She was born in Halgrach and is a proud daughter of the Republic, yet has always showed interest in the older and ancient customs of the land, such as the myths of the Aeld. As a trueborn Sorcerer her powers manifest in a way unique to her own individual personality, and she has become an expert illusionist in her own right.</p>
