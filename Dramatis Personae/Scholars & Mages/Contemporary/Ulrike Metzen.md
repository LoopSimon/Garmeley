<p>Ulrike Ror von Metzen is the current Chair of Astronomy at <a href="Korsfall College" class="internal-link">Korsfall College</a> and one of, if not the, single greatest authority on Planar Conflux, known as the Creator of <a href="Conflux" class="internal-link">modern Conflux Theory</a>. Her work titled "Conflux: Tide of the Stars", published in 905PA was noted as revolutionary and admirable by scholars and researchers of the field, crossing the borders of not only international academia but also the membrane between beheran and ilrakhani study.</p>
<p>Metzen was born as the daughter and 3rd born child of the Count of Metzen in southern Morelle, Meadland and followed a relatively ordinary career in the natural sciences, always displaying a love for the stars and the movement of the heavenly bodies. </p>
<p>Metzen is currently preparing to join the so called "Royal Expedition" and make the perilous journey across the Tirean Ocean to the new world of Aisligos for as of yet unknown reasons.</p>
