<p>Vinfried Kabernos is the former Courtmage of the <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Archduchy of Gisvale</a>, currently held prisoner at <a href="Gelmsburg" class="internal-link">Gelmsburg</a> in the Brass Spire, the cities infamous prison capable of holding even the most magically gifted incarcerated. After a long and illustrious career as one of Ur and Meadlands most promising members of the new generation of academics, the outspoken member of the <a href="Luminists" class="internal-link">Luminist Association</a>, was infamous arrested on a diplomatic visit to Gelmburg in 913PA on charges of treason against the Wermian Accords and the Gethric Order of the world, by members of the <a href="Order of the Silver Wheel" class="internal-link">Knights of the Silver Wheel</a> who acted with approval of King Klovis I himself.</p>
<p>The reactions to this were mixed and cautious, initial demands of release and reparations by the Archduke of Gisvale were ignored and condemned by Gelmsburg, and later the Church of Iztaw itself. Trinicist establishment worldwide was in uproar seeing it as a direct attack upon its sovereignty, alas were not ready to fully support a member of such a radical group as the Luminist through matters of war, the old Accords being often seen as one of the few institutions that held the gethric world still together. Academic reactions were deceidely more negative to the movement, bringing many popular voices, such as <a href="Janez Vajkard Valvasor" class="internal-link">Janez Vajkard Valvasor</a> that had previously been hesitant to support Luminism per se, closer to the international association; It was known that while eccentric, Kabernos was a humble and polite man whos primary field of research concerned the study of beasts and animals who exhibited supernatural abilities. Many defenders argued that the experimental channelling of Enchantment Magicks banned against sentient creatures, was forbidden for his meddling with the mind and the will of a conscious being, not for its invocations per se, making his hypothetical spells against lower animals not grounds for such drastic measures. </p>
<p>Seeking support, internationally, many monarchs, rulers and thought leaders of the western world, including the powerful and influential <a href="Stefano Maldini" class="internal-link">Bishop Maldini</a> of Cevolia, warned that the planned execution and trial of Kabernos would be an act of open war against the trinicist world, his trial in 915 was delayed indefinitely and while still alive Kabernos situation and state of mind and body are not clear to the public at this day and time, movements in Gisvale and western Meadrath to put further pressure on the King of Gelmsburg have not yet given up, and this overall first rustling of sabres between the old Guard of Accord Puritans and Luminists and adjacent Progressives only spells worry for the years to come.</p>
