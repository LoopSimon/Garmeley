<p>Zurgush, called the Hand of Blight, is the Wyrdspeaker of <a href="Ak-Saray" class="internal-link">Ak-Saray</a>, the foremost magical practitioner of the Dirgae confederation and the primary strategic concern of the <a href="League of the Gryphon" class="internal-link">League of the Gryphon</a> during its attempts to organize a Third War. Born in 869PA in the western settlement of Mara, he trained under the Witch Sakramon and developed a specialization in plague, disease and their medical counterparts that sits vaguely between the healing arts and something considerably less benign. The Wyrdspeaker title within Ak-Sarays tradition combines spiritual advisorship with the practical application of channeling ability, a hybrid role shaped by the intersection of magical practice and Kaladi Mysticism whose holders are often neither purely warriors nor merely scholars. Zurgushs interpretation of the role leans decisively toward the esoteric and the offensive, a fact that commands respect and generates unease within Ak-Saray itself, the Men of the League fear him for his terrible might all the more.</p> <p>He was nineteen years old during the <a href="Wars of the Gryphon" class="internal-link">Second War of the Gryphon</a> in 888PA and had held the Wyrdspeaker title for less than two years. In the wars final campaign he deployed his plague magic against League supply lines and troops in a manner that forced the retreat which led to the eventual terms of the current day border, terms considerably less favourable to the League than its position earlier that year had suggested they would be. League commanders who remember 888 remember what he was capable of at nineteen. In 927PA he is fiftyeight years old and his research has continued as his power grew. Whatever he has become in the four decades since the Second War he has kept to himself, and the League's planning for a potential Third War has struggled to answer Zurgush, bound themselves by the <a href="Orenburg Pact" class="internal-link">Orenburg Pact</a>.</p>
