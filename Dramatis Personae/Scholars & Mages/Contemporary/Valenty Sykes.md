<p>Grandmaster Valenty Tytus Sykes is the Head of <a href="The Alchemists Guild" class="internal-link">the Alchemists Guild of Ilrakhan</a>, also known as the Men of Glass and the Gri of Treuka, a Castle in southern <a href="Opuzna" class="internal-link">Opuzna</a>. As the leader of one of the most important and successful commercial operations on the continent, one of the few that operates internationally, he is arguably one of the most powerful and influential people in <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Vihr</a>, seen through his repeated influence on policy making and council given at the royal Court.</p>
<p>Sykes was born as the third son to Marius Sykes, Gri of Radke, into the vihri nobility in the year 878PA. He underwent a formative education at Szievek University and later at the Guilds College in <a href="Trazlova" class="internal-link">Trazlov</a> itself showing a talent for Chemistry, Math and Alchemy. After a storied career as a Guild soiree in Uhl and later the advisor to the Var zi Trazlova himself, Sykes was chosen as the successor to the office of Grandmaster, enjoying a great reputation within the higher ranking circles of the Guild, he has held the office since 219PA for now 7 years.</p>
<p>The Grandmaster is a member of the Valeski School, which largely rejects the worship of the "old cyracli masters" and follows the example and teachings of Orwald Valeski who saw  as an art and expression of emotion and will, akin to many studies on divine channelling, rather than a base science to understand and harness natural forces of the world around us.</p>
<p>In recognition of services rendered to the crown and the people of Vihr, Skyes was granted the castle of Treuka in 925PA which he is rumoured to have never visited in the two years since. His personal obsession with the mythical concept of the "Perpetual Brew", a legend of the profession speaking of a fluid able to permanently reproduce itself without the need for any external energy, has been the only leisure Valety has granted himself over the course of his career.</p>
