<p>Hubert of Tanverley is a urien author and researcher from <a href="Eldmar" class="internal-link">Eldmar</a>, most famous for his widely popular "Beasts of Land, Skies & Seas, how to recognize and avoid them" series of books, colloquially often shortened to "Huberts Beastiary". Perhaps one of the most well travelled men of our time, Hubert has travelled most of Ilrakhan, with stints in Behera and even a visit to Nirydia during his life and published five entries to his series of Books. He has made it a habit to keep various, often rather dangerous, beasts as pets in his villa at Tanverley, much to the worry and annoyance of the citizens of the town and the eldmari nobility in general.</p>
<p>Of Tanverleys first work, discussed various Beasts in the lands of Vihr & Nostroya, a compilation more than anything of stories with only few examples and specific studies, his infamous entry on the common Cockatrice was based on a personal pet of the species he had called "Ginger" and kept by his side for a few months. A certain charm and committal to his studies accompanied the attempt by the author to not only compile an up to date documentation of various creatures, but also to bring it out of a purely academic context. This garnered him and his work great favour and popularity, causing various versions to be published for multiple runs and making the 1st entry in his ensuing series one of the best selling books since the invention and popularization of the Printing Press.</p>
<p>The Zoologist would travel to the northern lands of Trykkerog, Weadland, Gelm and Uhl in his 2nd entry, followed by a journey through Meadland and stays in the Kortopi halls of mountains and gnomes in his third book, a journey through Othirdon, party of Sertova and Cevolia in his fourth entry and a visit to Hainach, Garmeley, Grevenna and northern Behera in his fifth. He is reportedly working on a sixth entry at the moment concerning creatures from around the niryd Sea. Over the years Hubert has amassed a great following of readers from various walks of life and become a rather wealthy individual, as well as one of the most well travelled men on the continent. He employs the services of a variety of security, travel and diplomatic experts and has started to re-publish his popular series with popular sketches of his various exotic and exciting beats displayed.</p>
