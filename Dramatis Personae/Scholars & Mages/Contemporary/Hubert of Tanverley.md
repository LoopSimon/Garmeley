<p>Hubert of Tanverley is a urien author and researcher from <a href="Eldmar" class="internal-link">Eldmar</a>, most famous for his widely popular "Beasts of Land, Skies & Seas, how to recognize and avoid them" series of books, colloquially often shortened to "Huberts Beastiary". Perhaps one of the most well travelled men of our time, Hubert has travelled most of Ilrakhan, with stints in Behera and even a visit to Nirydia during his life and published five entries to his series of Books. He has made it a habit to keep various, often rather dangerous, beasts as pets in his villa at Tanverley, much to the worry and annoyance of the citizens of the town and the eldmari nobility in general.</p>
