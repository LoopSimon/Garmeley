<p>Ulesha Ez Garyal is the current Sapphire Eye of <a href="Karajal School of Insight" class="internal-link">Karajal</a>, a gifted protege of the Arcane and more importantly a savy political operator. As the Sapphire Eye she sits on the Dirhan of the Sultanate and fulfils the role of the Asafi of Suhur, making her one of the most influential voices in the entire Sultante. Her relative youth has earned her much doubt and ill will from her peers with many accusing Garyal to be a product of nepotism and a tool for various powerful figures to influence the School and the Dirhan.</p>
<p>Ulesha is a Shar of Behera and the current head of the Garyal Family, as a prominent Magister and politician she is following the example of many of her predecessors such as  Mons Garyal, known as "the Butterfly", the Citrine Eye of Karajal and elven Magister that infamously took part in the <a href="First War of Elbae Aggression" class="internal-link">First War of Elbae Aggression</a>. She studied under Zihar ab-Rassa who also taught the acting Citrine Eye, <a href="Carassa al-Kael" class="internal-link">Carassa al-Kael</a> and spends most of her days in the Capital of <a href="Jiravid" class="internal-link">Jiravid</a>.</p>
<p>Garyal is a renowned Telepath and Mentalist, proposing various solutions for diplomatic and military problems with the means of invasive magical procedures, as well as developing measures of protection from the very same. </p>
