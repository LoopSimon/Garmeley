<p>Jinùk is a sorcerer who makes his home in Kiamar, the former capital of the Empire of <a href="Naigan" class="internal-link">Naigan</a>, on the island of Naigan that is still often called the Imperial City. He was born in 881PA, more than a century after the fall of the Empire, and he bows to no land: no crown of <a href="Tal" class="internal-link">Tal</a> or the Purple Sea has his allegiance. Kiamar has been looted many times since the Empire fell.</p>
<p>He is a lean man with dark skin, a short pointed beard and wields a long staff crowned with a golden crescent. A golden dragon curled into a ring and biting its own tail is often associated with him and the sigil of power for his cult, usually referred to as the "Dragon of Jinùk". </p>
