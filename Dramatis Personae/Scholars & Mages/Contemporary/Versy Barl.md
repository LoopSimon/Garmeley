<p>This disgraced former lecturer at Szievek University is said to have fled back to his home in the <a href="Grachy" class="internal-link">Republic of Grachy</a> after authorities discovered his heinous experiments in the realm of Necromancy and Biomancy, breaking numerous laws and going against the <a href="Wermian Accords" class="internal-link">Accords of Wermia</a>.</p>
 <p>The Scholar kidnapped, imprisoned, tortured and killed victims, earning the alias "the Fleshmonger" until he is said to have moved on to more physically impressive specimen. He was finally discovered in 922PA as a strange beasts remains caught the attention of a servant at the University that noticed a strange behaviour of rats around a specific area in the abandoned parts of the Szievek Sewers. While Versys wrongdoings were revealed, the Fleshmonger himself could not be caught and rumours of his flight eastwards towards his fatherland is all his victims families have to cling to.</p>
 <p>On the evening of the 31st May of 927PA, a party of heroes tracked suspicious activites and a missing courier deep into the Grey Mausoleum of <a href="Izhevk" class="internal-link">Izhevk</a>, where Versy had made himself a productive home and constructed various monstrosities. After a long fight, the Necromancer fell to the Arrows of vihri Archer Danusz and was finally killed by the flames of his own <a href="Chimera" class="internal-link">Chimera</a>, a beast he had constructed out of the remains of Wyvern, Lion and Dire-Goat, and infused with life in a powerful ritual that took energy from his lesser servants. Versy was also responsible for the overall desecration of the Mausoleum and the reanimation of <a href="Vlad V" class="internal-link">Ras Vlad "the Cursed" of Jerinov</a> whom his killers put to final rest once more.</p>
