<p>Gharel Valiam Talnod is a renowned reani Mage and avid researcher in Planar Synallagma, as well as an infamous member of the rising Group of arcane academics known as the <a href="Luminists" class="internal-link">Luminists</a> in open scientific revolution to the harsh and archaic restrictions and laws set by the century old Wermian Accords. He famously developed the Turnometer and the <a href="Talnod-Swarn Gradient" class="internal-link">Talnod-Swarn Gradient</a> in cooperation with weadi scientist <a href="Jan van Swarn" class="internal-link">Jan van Swarn</a >. During the 870s and 80s he studied under the <a href="Carassa al-Kael" class="internal-link">Mirai</a> herself at  <a href="Karajal School of Insight" class="internal-link">Karajal</a > and has taught for three years at Reanport University as well, before settling down at Patwin Tower and engulf himself fully in his research.</p>
<p>Living and working most of his life in Urland and Garmeley, recent political changes forced Talnod out of the greater circle of Patwin Tower as he saw himself disenfranchised and in opposition to the Towers policies of interventionism regarding the Garmian Civil War. After a year of travel in Cevolia he accepted an invitation of academic asylum at Karajal, granted to various reani, garmish and urien Scholars at the behest of the elven institution, where he resides still today.</p>
