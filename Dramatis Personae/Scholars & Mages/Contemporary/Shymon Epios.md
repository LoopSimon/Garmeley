<p>Professor Shymon Epios is a meadi Daemonologist and Arcanist that has taken extensive interest into the <a href="The Abyss" class="internal-link">Abyss</a> and its Inhabitants, following in the footsteps of disgraced Mages like Renhard and elven scholars like Ulmator. He studied at <a href="Korsfall College" class="internal-link">Korsfall College</a> and Hevn University and even became a Professor and then a Seat at the College which he held for a record low amount of time of 3 months before being unanimously expelled after an Incident in 916PA. Epios most controversial work is the Grimoire Paralis that was never published by any printing House following the recommendation of various institutions, leading to only a handful of handmade copies circulating. The Grimoire Paralis is banned in a majority of Countries and its creation has lead to various agents of the <a href="Kherovan Inquisition" class="internal-link">Inquisition</a> taking an increased interest in Epios and his whereabouts.</p>
<p>Epios studied under Hannah Verick at Korsfall where he would also meet fellow Arcanist <a href="Pavo of Alban" class="internal-link">Pavo of Alban</a>, the two are said to have initially came long quite nicely, considering their more than unorthodox fields of study and methodology, however Alban quickly showed himself as the more cunning career focused of the two and would later reject any requests of Epios for help during his academic Exile. Following his leave of the College and the failed publishing of the Grimoire, Epios travelled through Meadland, Vihr and Grevenna for around 3 years before crossing the <a href="Strait of Horad" class="internal-link">Strait of Horad</a> and was last seen at the Capital of the Sultanate itself, the great city of <a href="Jiravid" class="internal-link">Jiravid</a>, in the year 925PA.</p>
<p>Due to the controversial nature of his research, even the revolutionary <a href="Luminists" class="internal-link">Luminists</a> have rejected many of Epios ideas and processes and an extensive amount of rumours has been spread around the meadi: Ranging from summoning and consorting with Daemons to being a reincarnated Wermian Sorcerer or even one of the many supposed "Elfborn Skinwalkers" that appear to have infected the western academic canon in the minds of certain conspiracy theorists. Given his effectively open break with the <a href="Wermian Accords" class="internal-link">wermian Accords</a> and much of the magical establishment it is unclear where life will lead Shymon in his future, if he is even still of sound mind and body and free to make his own decisions.</p>
