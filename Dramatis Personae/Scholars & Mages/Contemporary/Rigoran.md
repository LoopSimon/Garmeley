<p>Boris Rigoran, better known as just "Rigoran" or "One-Eye", is the current Archmage of Edessa Tower in the sarloni hinterlands north of the Grevi Mountains. He took the position following his masters, the legendary Planar Theorist  <a href="Vilkin" class="internal-link">Roizar Vilkin</a>, Departure to Reanport in 921PA.</p>

<p>Rigorans nickname is not a metaphorical one, the middle Aged Arcanist, usually dressed in nice but not extravagant clothing, has seemingly lost the function of his left eye, it being replaced by a permanent fixture of magical light, likely due to a complex enchantement. How this came to be, wether a birth defect, accident, experiment gone wrong or, as many common folk believe, "sacrifice to demonfolk in turn for forbidden knowledge", is unknown to most.</p>
<p>Rigoran is an eccentric figure at first glance, his interests often viewed as occult or doomsayerish, and his reputation an an Accelerationist has not done him any favours with many of the western established members of Academia. He is rumoured to be a member of the  <a href="Children of Dust" class="internal-link">Children of Dust</a>, although likely has never ventured into Rakh himself, and supports many of their endeavours if not coordinating them. His studies of basic <a href="Acceleration Theory" class="internal-link">Acceleration Theory</a>, the so called <a href="The Thinning" class="internal-link">"Thinning - Phenomenon"</a>, observations made by his regular oversight over parts of the Royal Aptitude Census in Vihr have caused him more and more concern over the stability of the Veil itself over the past years. He was repeatedly attempted to share his findings with the <a href="Mural Theory" class="internal-link">Mural Theory</a> of <a href="Korsfall College" class="internal-link">Korsfall College</a> to no avail, and it is likely he only stands in active contact with his former Master.</p>

<p>The Archmage guards the tower and continues his studies into the Veil there to this day, his young apprentice Gianna Vulma being groomed as his successor. In 926PA, the only eighteen year old Gianna was captured alongside pilgrims and travellers on her way from Fairriver Abbey, by the Tintongue Clan, an Ogre Clan of the northern Grevi Mountains that had grown especially restless. Alongside her the Goblin Perzuk Uz-Kram had found himself in a similar pickle, and this spelled luck for Gianna as soon his companion, Marianna Kaminski, came to the rescue and saved them both, travelling home to Edessa Tower as a Trio.</p>
