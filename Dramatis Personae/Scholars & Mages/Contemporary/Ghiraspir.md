<p>Anir Ghiraspir is the current Storogast of Aznyr and as such one of the leading scholars and voices in the realm of south-nirydian Artificery, even by his friends he is at best described as eccentric and strange, but the Bihra is by no means malicious or out to do harm. His fascination with the power of the Skies, the Lightning Storms that are so common in the Kareli lands between the Lim dul Peaks and the Nirydian Sea, have defined his life and career and led to him pleading with the leader of the Karaeli, Hierarch Lazai, to establish his own school of applied and experimental energies, an endeavour happily founded by the magnanimous and incredibly rich Hierarch.</p>
<p>Ghiraspir is an alumni of the <a href="Institute for Practical Energies" class="internal-link">Institute for Practical Energies</a>, where he studied under <a href="Charatov Podlim" class="internal-link">Charatov Podlim</a>.</p>
