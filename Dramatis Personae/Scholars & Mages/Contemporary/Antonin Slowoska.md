<p>Antonin Slowoska is a grachi practitioner of the Arcane currently working at the Borni Observatorium in the eastern town of <a href="Izhevk" class="internal-link">Izhevk</a>. He infamously was involved in the so called "Iorminsk Affair" of 919, during which Olena Nargu was found guilty of breaking the <a href="Wermian Accords" class="internal-link">Wermian Accords</a> and invade and control the minds of various powerful grachi politicians. Nargu as well as her other apprentice were tried and executed but Slowoska judged as innocent. Many suspect or theorize that Slowoska, who supposedly assisted authorities with the containment and investigation of his former master, was not innocent at all and merely tried to save his own skin, if not successfully use his own magics to manipulate the minds of investigators. Be it as it may, his reputation was tarnished and it was made clear to Antonin he would no longer be welcome at Iorminsk or anywhere near the Grachi centre of power any longer, an officially merely academic position at Borni became his way out of the hornets nest for now.</p>
<p>Slowoska seems to be involved in measures taken by the bykmali traveller <a href="Bersi Hurtfing" class="internal-link">Bersi Hurtfing</a> to investigate and potentially counteract the mysterious plague that had recently befallen the town of <a href="Kharnov" class="internal-link">Kharnov</a>.</p>
