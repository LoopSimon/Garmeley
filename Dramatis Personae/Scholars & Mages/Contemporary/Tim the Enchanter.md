<p>Tim the Enchanter or "Blue Tim", his real name is unknown, is a travelling Spellcrafter and Mage that has been sighted on various roads in the lands of Ilrakhan for many a year, almost becoming a part of the regional folklore. He is said to possess a mighty red beard and a colourful blue and white checkered garment and hat, giving him one of his many nicknames. </p>
