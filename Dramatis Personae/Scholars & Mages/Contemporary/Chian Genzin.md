<p>Chian Genzin is one of the last remaining Channellers of the Second <a href="The Pillars of Myan" class="internal-link">Pillar of Myan</a>, and thus a Student of the Elemental Pillar of Water itself. As many of his specific invocation, he is leading the life if a nomad within the greater Diaspora of the Yppori Channelers. He is currently leading a group of Channelers, many of them his acolytes, in the desert lands of the Sultanate in north-eastern Behera. It is unknown if Chian stands in contact to a great amount, few or any other Channelers of the Second Pillar, and he may well be the most senior practitioner of the arts remaining in the entirety of northern Behera.</p>

<p>Chian is currently travelling the Asef lands on a as of yet unknown mission, while teaching his two young disciples, the boy Galan and the girl Tyenna, both orphans that have joined Chians quest for meaning and make up a family of their own, while trying to embrace the teachings of the Second Pillar for themselves. This nomadic teacher-student relationship is not uncommon for the extremely splintered and spread out Second Pillar, and it has lead to various non Yppori, such as the beheri Tyenna or the Reani Galan, to be taught the ancient teachings of <a href="Tal" class="internal-link">Tal</a>. </p>
<p>Accompanying Chian is his oldest acquaintance and Bond Parnter, the Myan Elemental who has chosen Ilandin has a name while roaming the world of mortals.</p>
