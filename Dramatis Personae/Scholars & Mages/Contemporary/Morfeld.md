<p>Christiaan Morfeld, later often known as "the Returned", was a Necromancer of considerably prowess that haunted the lands of northern Ur, Weadland and the Gisvale in the 7th century PA, before being brought to justice by <a href="Lyandra" class="internal-link">Lyandra of Fairriver</a> before being tried and burned at the stake in the  <a href="Uhl" class="internal-link">City of Uhl</a> for his crimes in 666PA. Infamously he later returned to the realm of the living through unknown means, taking possession of the young sorceress Falia Venwick in 787PA, more than a century after his first demise.</p>
<p>Little is known about Morfelds youth, as he first surfaced in 657PA, haunting the Kranmoor and "purging" numerous villages and settlements with a coterie of <a href="Unliving" class="internal-link">undead</a> abominations to do his bidding. He would move across the Bow and establish himself in the wild hills of the Trinbend and the untamed woods of Silentree Forest as a King of the Undead for years, killing various adventurers, knights and even members of the Inquisition of Kherova, that sought his capture and end. He is said to have written various grimoires and tomes in this time, recording his findings and seemed to view himself more as a progressive academic than a criminal Magus who abused his powers to terrorize the common man. Following his defeat and capture at the hands of Lyandra, she undertook rigorous steps to destroy any of his writings and many of his artefacts, it seems he later on formulated many of his works again upon returning to this life, including a macabre "memoir" of sorts, copies of which have become an almost occult curiosity.</p>
<p>He was initially rumoured to have been an agent send by the forces of the loyalists and Korsfall during the <a href="War of Trout and Drake" class="internal-link">War of Trout and Drake</a> meant to disturb and terrorize the lands of Gisvale and the Moors beyond in the name of the western alliance, although these rumours were never proven and are largely unsubstantiated. Korsfall College itself vehemently rejects and rumours of association with the Necromancer for obvious reasons, while conspiracy theorists connect the fact that upon his return he remained almost suspiciously well hidden but connected to the outskirts of academia to him having blackmail on the College itself.</p>

<p>Falia Venwick, a young urien student at the Circle of Gelmsburg and friend to the later incredibly popular and revolutionary scholar <a href="Jevana" class="internal-link">Jevana of Leston</a>, fell to the corruptive magicks of a ring Morfeld had left behind, thinking it a relic of old Urlander Magi, Venwick who was specifically interested in magical chronology studied and later on wore the ring of the Necromancer, unaware of its full powers before it was too late. Contemporaries of Venwick describe her gradual change in personality before the 23 year old fully fell to the powers of the cursed artifact, some of her manuscripts regarding her object of study are all the writings we have to document her tragic fate. It seems Morfeld in his new form too changed somewhat to his old self, much more cautious and reserved, he only resurfaced a few times over the last 150 years and is almost certainly still roaming the lands of Ilrakhan. As mentioned above he even published autobiographical material, as well as various unorthodox treaties on the world of the living and the unliving, under differing pen names, who all eventually are dated back to him. </p>
<p>In recent years he published works on the similarities and differences between the nostroyan <a href="Graccum" class="internal-link">Graccum</a> & <a href="Brystone" class="internal-link">Brystone</a>, used by the necromancers of Bilsworth. He notes that while many of the conductive and amplifying powers are similar, Brystone offers much more stable and reliable values and is especially useful in regards to its use in continued necromantic magicks. Graccum, whose purified form could be used to resurrect the dead as husks, was considerably more tenable when incorporated in enchantment spells affecting the living, or organic material. Morfeld decries the rarity of Brystone outside of Danbry and the incredibly complicated and dangerous procedures to purify Graccum, differentiating between the original material, and its purified form further by describing the differences between "failed" purification and "stable" purification. </p>
