<p>Anak Montener was a Pagri arcanist and philosopher as well as a researcher into the fundamental building blocks of the cosmos, he is most well known for writing and compiling the <a href="The Anak" class="internal-link">Anak Codex</a>, and as such essentially the Founder of modern niryd Cosmology and Arcane Theory. His life and writings were defined by his religious convictions as a devout Zuhurist contrasted by an extremely progressive understanding and utilitarian view of the supernatural, that was elemental in his fundamental Thesis. </p>
 <p>This very academic and ratio based approach, only coloured in religious metaphors not hindered by liturgic dogmata, is what has allowed Anak and his philosophy, simply known as "The Anak" to become the multinational, continent wide basis for academic discourse in terms of abstract cosmology and arcane theory, on an international level he is often compared to important ilrakhani figures such as <a href="Wulfrick" class="internal-link">Wulfrick the Elder</a> if not the forefathers of magic itself in the palmunian, cyracli and elven traditions.</p>
 <p>His most famous student was undoubtably the gelfir <a href="Shirin" class="internal-link">Shirin</a> Skaks Rhal, who studied for two decades under him and helped compile his later writings, as well as introduce shirin culture to his teachings under a <a href="Lakalism" class="internal-link">Lakalic</a> umbrella.</p>
