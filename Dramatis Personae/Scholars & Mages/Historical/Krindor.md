<p>Modan Krindor, birth name unknown, was am <a href="Bykmåli" class="internal-link">Olbellinge</a> Arcanist and high ranking member of the <a href="Mørbrig" class="internal-link">Mørbrig Chapter of the Gifted & the Learned</a>, that practiced his arts in his birth place, the great city of Sallesand, in the late 8th and early 9th century PA. He was the mentor to the <a href="Half-Elf" class="internal-link">Half</a>-<a href="Aelf" class="internal-link">Aelfen</a> Oracle Lari Anner</p>
<p>As is tradition within the Chapter, he chose a new name upon completing his initiation, and very little is known about his life before surfacing as a Scholar and Researcher of Realmatic Theory. He acted as the High-Weaver of the Sallesand Council, on the behest of the Chapter and the King of <a href="Baeling" class="internal-link">Baeling</a> at Alsebyn, and garnered a reputation for exceedingly eccentric behaviour. Rumours of strange visitors to Krindors Hall, men and women turning to ravens and whispering dark secrets from far beyond, strange lights during the deepest hours of the night and the like, always surrounded the Arcanist.</p>
<p>Krindor is most well known for his thorough study, compilation and advancements of <a href="Realmatic Nativism" class="internal-link">Realmatic Nativism</a> and within it the so called "Four Principles". He wrote multiple manuscripts and treaties on the matter that almost all went unpublished during his life, such as his famous "Magar Sib", containing records of historical ventures into the spiritual realm from as far away as southern <a href="Nirydia" class="internal-link">Nirydia</a>, the <a href="Bihra" class="internal-link">bihra Shamans</a> of the <a href="Niryd Steppe" class="internal-link">Steppe</a> and the <a href="Mogyr" class="internal-link">Mogyr</a> of Drizan.</p>

<p>Krindor was slain by the gethric  Paladin Gimmael in 821PA, in the outskirts of Sallesand at a small Estate for largely unknown reasons.</p>
