<p>The yppori human Tihir Szuan, raised in the tradition of the Thandari Channelers on the island of Surma in the early 7th century, became one of the most groundbreaking and important scholars in the greater yppori tradition of the magical arts. His discovery of the so called <a href="Myan-Bond" class="internal-link">"Myan Bond"</a> is likely the greatest contribution to the original Circle of Thandar.</p>
<p>Szuan lived during the so called Golden Age of the Naigan, where the Empire of the Four became the uncontested power of the Purple Sea, but was himself employed by the King of Gyedo, one of the last holdouts against the influence of the Naigan. He actively supported the city against the approaching and besieging armies of the Naigan but refused to bring his significant personal powers to bear against another human in physical combat directly. When Gyedo fell in the Fall of 681PA, he was killed in the terrible sack of the city.</p>
<p>The great <a href="The Pillars of Myan" class="internal-link">"Pillars of Myan"</a> are named after Szuans theoretical Improvement upon Thandars work, and represent the greatest Age of Yppori arcane research into the elements. Today they have fallen into dishonor and disgrace, became religious or state institutions or have been hunted to extinction and forced into diaspora, leading inadvertently to a greater spread of Thandar and Szuans Teachings.</p>
