<p>The largely mysterious Mage by the name of Viagoris, undoubtably not his real name as it was an ancient honorific used by the Augurs of ancient Meadland, practiced the arts of magic during the first half of the 9th century and was infamously the opponent of Muriel von Gloss in their now famous duel during the War of Horn and Fang, in which he is said to have perished, albeit no eye witnesses other than von Gloss herself ever substantiated this claim. Viagoris is largely known as a vihri or wermian human by appearence and behaviour, as he served the armies of Vihr during the War.</p>
<p>Perna "the Mad" Ratz is said to have studied under Viagoris and has given various conflicting accounts of her time under his tutelage during her stay at Wangrad Asylum, although methods used during her therapy bordered or truly veered into torture and are to be taken with stern consideration even beyond her metal condition.</p>
