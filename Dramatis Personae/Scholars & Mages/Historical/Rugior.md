<p>Meyzar Rugior was the Archmage of Patwin tower between 811 and 835PA and a planar Theoriest who is largely credited with the creation of Fundamentalist Theory. His most well known contribution to the western Canon is likely the Hierarchical Model, building upon the work of the elven Arcanist <a href="Izrafur" class="internal-link">Izrafurs</a> Four-Axis Model. in 828PA, eight years after the Fall of Kulum, Rugior equipped and advised the heroic group of adventures that would  venture into the cursed city and slay the Necromancer <a href="Ahrmiz Saceren" class="internal-link">Ahrmiz the Conjurer</a>.</p>
<p>Meyzars upbringing is somewhat shrouded in mystery and he was seemingly adopted by the Sorceress Tessia Anler as a street rat in Redflow, a garmish practitioner of the arcane that saw potential in a young Rugior and took him under her wing. Studying at Gelmsburg, Rugior would come to know Falia Venwick and <a href="Jevana" class="internal-link">Jevana of Leston</a>, and witness the formers fall under the influence of the cursed dark wizard <a href="Morfeld" class="internal-link">Morfeld</a> who had returned from the dead and taken possession of Falia. He would be a lifelong friend to Jevana and even recommend her as the new Archmage of Greenstone Tower, following her problematic situation at the Circle and Court of Gelmsburg.</p>
