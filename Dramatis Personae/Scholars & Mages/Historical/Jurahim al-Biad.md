<p>One of the most infamous Wizards in modern history, Jurahim al-Biad was an elven practitioner of the arcane arts and for over 140 years the Ruby Eye of Karajal. He is without a doubt remembered by most for the so called "Invocation of Al-Biad", the apocalyptic Spell cast at the end of the <a href="Wars of Elbae Aggression" class="internal-link">3rd War of Elbae Aggression</a> that sunk the entire Horad Isthmus and connected the Stormy Sea with the Pilvian Gulf, to this date one of the single most destructive and at the same time powerful and technically complex feats of magic ever performed by a single mortal. It is said that Sultan Arbalas gave the command to abandon the invocation that took al-Biad weeks to prepare and perform as active engagements on the Isthmus were ongoing and other members of the General Staff had finally gotten through to the Sultan with their pleas to abandon the risky spell with unknowable consequences, infamous al-Biad is said to have acknowledged the command and promptly ignored it, fulfilling the invocation anyways and perishing along side thousands of soldiers.</p>
