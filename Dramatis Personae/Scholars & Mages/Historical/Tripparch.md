<p>Tripparch of Nyleikos was an ancient Tarpian Cyracli philosopher, polymath and astronomer that lived during the 7th century BA and considered the founder of trigonometry and one of most important scholars in the realm of mathematics and astronomy of antiquity. Tripparch is considered one of the, if not the single greatest astronomers of the ancient palmunian, rakhian and cyracli traditions by many and was the first whose quantitative and accurate models for the motion of the Moons and the Sun are enshrined within the religious traditions of cyracli Starworship and beyond.</p>
<p>Tripparchs development of trigonometry include the discovery and measurement of the Globes precession and the development of the Diolagma, the first known comprehensive star catalogue in history among many other treaties. For his contributions to the sciences he is often dubbed the "Father of Astronomy" by modern scholars such as <a href="Carassa al-Kael" class="internal-link">Carassa al-Kael</a>.</p>
