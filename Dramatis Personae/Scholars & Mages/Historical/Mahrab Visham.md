<p>Mahrab Ikiro Visham, commonly just referred to as Visham, was a hessefi philosopher, astronomer and author that is best known for his critical view on beings from beyond our world, commonly referred to as Daemons, Spirits or other culturally and historically relevant distinctions. He lived in the 6th century PA and his thoughts were largely ignored by many scholars within and outside of the Sultanate, where he lived and worked his entire life, until it received a controversial resurgence in modern times as part of various neo-academic movements.</p>
<p>Little of Vishams earlier life is known but it is often assumed he was born into considerable wealth and standing that allowed him to visit and study at the Karajal School of Insight, finding a sponsor in the Shar, an elven title of hereditary nobility, Jahraf Offir, and working for most of his life within the letters private estate on the island of Ziflis within the Summer Sea. He was an avid Daemonologist himself and studied the ancient grimoires, tablets and papyri of long lost civilisations, going back as far as <a href="Alparaz" class="internal-link">Alparaz</a> himself to compare the visual appearance and observable behaviour of various summoned and, in most cases successfully, bound creatures from beyond our own plane. </p>
