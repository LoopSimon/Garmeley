<p>Larkomas Mi Terix was a gnomish Physicist and Planar Theorist whos considerations on planar forces and their relativity lay the bedrock for the modern universal model of planar theory and cosmic forces such as <a href="Vilkin" class="internal-link">Roizar Vilkins</a> Universal Model or the even more cutting edge <a href="Modern Realmatic Theory" class="internal-link">Triquetra Model</a> of <a href="Carassa al-Kael" class="internal-link">Carassa al-Kael</a>. He is revered as an icon of gnomish ingenuity and a rare example of Ottian scholarship in constant exchange with the surface world. He was a lifelong admirer of <a href="Palus" class="internal-link">Saint Palus</a> whom he briefly met in the early stages of his own life.</p>
<p>He is credited with bridging the academic divide between the so called Arcanists and the Church, opening up an exchange in information between the incredible surplus of empirical proof, sheer amount of magical practitioners and longstanding tradition of the clergy with the advanced understanding and superior ratio of the arcanists. His perception as an unbiased observer not concerned with clerical liturgy of worldly politics afforded him a universal respect in both Church and School.</p>
