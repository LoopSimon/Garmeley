<p>Larkomas Mi Terix was a gnomish Physicist and Planar Theorist whos considerations on planar forces and their relativity lay the bedrock for the modern universal model of planar theory and cosmic forces such as <a href="Vilkin" class="internal-link">Roizar Vilkins</a> Universal Model or the even more cutting edge <a href="Modern Realmatic Theory" class="internal-link">Triquetra Model</a> of <a href="Carassa al-Kael" class="internal-link">Carassa al-Kael</a>. He is revered as an icon of gnomish ingenuity and a rare example of Ottian scholarship in constant exchange with the surface world. He was a lifelong admirer of <a href="Palus" class="internal-link">Saint Palus</a> albeit he never officially converted to the Gethric Faith himself.</p>
<p>He is credited with bridging the academic divide between the so called Arcanists and the Church, opening up an exchange in information between the incredible surplus of empirical proof, sheer amount of magical practitioners and longstanding tradition of the clergy with the advanced understanding and superior ratio of the arcanists. His perception as an unbiased observer not concerned with clerical liturgy of worldly politics afforded him a universal respect in both Church and School.</p>
<p>The existence of the Concrete and Ideal Plane was first theorised by Larkomas as a result of his expeditions within the Wastelands of Rakh and the Ruins of Tarsus and is part of his "Syntropic-Collapse Hypothesis" of the Eruption of Al-Akka in the year 0 AA. Larkmos would die in 691PA at the noble age of 121 Years of Age as a consequence of what would later on be dubbed "Nur-Disease" a weakness of flesh, bone and body due to overt exposure to the <a href="Nurlight" class="internal-link">Nurlight</a> at the centre of the Rakhian Wastes.</p>
