<p>Pixos, also known as Pixos Trygisos, was a <a href="Cyraclion" class="internal-link">cyracli</a> Scholar and Master of <a href="Alchemy" class="internal-link">Kynosis</a> during the 5th and 4th century BA, he is best known for his work in the field of magnetism and his work "Lithotroska" is often considered the first treaty on electromagnetic Theory. As a controversial and infamous figure living right at the dusk of the Cyracli Civilisation he is often considered as one of the causes for the so called "Fall of Cyraclion", usually mentioned in the same breath as his legendary kynotic design dubbed "The Heart of Pixos" which conception and activation is the most common theory for the destruction and change that befell the vast majority of Cyraclion and the <a href="Cyclopi" class="internal-link">cyclopi</a> population of <a href="Itharia" class="internal-link">Itharia</a> as a whole in 370BA. Myth speaks of Pixos experiencing an infinite moment of both euphoria and horror as he activated the device being in control and at the same time fully absorbed of forces fundamental to existence itself for a mere fraction of a second and thousands of lifetimes of perception both.</p>
<p>The Wave of energy and force unleashed dissolved all living matter in the High Cities of the Itharian Strait and caused a change in the structure of the inorganic, while no living being survived the events at the apex of the cyclopi city in their original form to tell the tale, the communities and settlements around the southern tip of Itharia, primarily those close to the City of <a href="Lorqis" class="internal-link">Lorqis</a>, survived to denote the events immediately following the Fall. States of life and unlife at the same time, creatures made of matter dissolving and reappearing, the great shaking of the earth at their feet, Waves a hundred metres high and floods cruel and unforgiving haunted Itharia. The once proud and beautiful cyclopi twisted as those cities in central Itharia that did not get immediately eradicated gave birth to the true horrors: Degraded and massive hordes of changed forms, animals and cylopi both moulded and twisted into monsters, some mindless horrors others cursed with intellect. The horses and goats walked on two legs, <a href="Satyr" class="internal-link">dancing and laughing forever around their cruel fires</a>, the great artists and scientists of Cyraclion became numb things of mud and rain, some turned into yet worse shaped by centuries to come. After the events of the Fall and the mysterious survival of Lorqis, the islands food could only support few and the city became the singular reminder of the high culture that had once inhabited Itharia, some survivors flew to the continent and the shadows of the great <a href="Thurimoi" class="internal-link">Thurimoi</a>, later founding the Tower Cities in cooperation with beheran nomads.</p>
