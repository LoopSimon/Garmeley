<p>Born as the firstborn son to the Count of Rilsa on the Isle of Butera, Mario Benvolo, full name Mario Piero Juzifer Benvolo, would become known as the legendary Leader of the First Expedition into the ancient ruins of <a href="Rakh" class="internal-link">Rakh</a> in the year 580PA, better known as the Great Expedition. He would make over 14 ventures into the ruins of Rakh and its sorroundings and is largely held responsible for the founding of the <a href="Excavators Guild of Butera" class="internal-link">Excavators Guild of Butera</a> together with both wealthy sponsors, the Duc of Butera himself and other successful adventurers that had joined him in his ventures.</p>
<p>At the age of 72, Mario would undertake his last voyage deep into the catacombs of the city of Rakh on this fifteenth expedition in the year 622PA. He would emerge petrified as a statue of stone, carried out by his faithful servants after having fallen victim to a trap in the ancient dungeon.</p>
<p>His legacy is kept alive in Cevolia and especially Butera, where he is hailed as a hero and one of the most important children of the Island. The Guilds modern day Flagship, the Benvolos Fury, is named after him among many other honours.</p>
