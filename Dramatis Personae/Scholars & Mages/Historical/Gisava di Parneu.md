<p>The Discoverer of the eponymous "<a href="Parneu Field" class="internal-link">Parneu Field</a>", although only in the context of contemporary western Academica one must add, Gisava Vitrina di Parneu was a powerful Arcanist operating in the lands of Cevolia during the 7th century. She not only gained notice for her extremely influential scientific treaties but also for her active role during the War of 672 between the Duchy of Jeledo and the Realm of Battvia, during which she took a more than active role in supporting her homeland of Jeledo, being often cited as the primary reason as to why early efforts of battvian Royalty to re-unite the falled Kingdom of Cevolis failed. She fought at the Battle of Zavix and was largely responsible for the Treaty of Ciravelli that would grant the Tower political independence from both countries following the end of the war.</p>
<p>Gisava was born as the daughter to the Conte di Parneu into the nobility of Jeledo in the year 609PA and would later go on to inherit the title. She was a fervent supporter of the national identity of the Duchy and its ownership over the ancient city state of <a href="Firocco" class="internal-link">Firocco</a>, ironically being cited as an inspiration for the 8th century Sorceress <a href="Salvia" class="internal-link">Salvia</a> that would proclaim herself the sole autonomous ruler over Firocco and the  Grizani Islands. 
<br>Di Parneu died during the Gallfever Epidemic in 692PA at the age of 83, she is seen as a national hero in Jeledo and an icon for cevoli arcane excellence and scholarship, even in modern day Battvia. Muriel von Gloss, the meadi Wizard and famous student of <a href="Jevana" class="internal-link">Jevana of Leston</a>, would describe di Parneu as a personal icon and a symbol of decisiveness during the discourse surrounding magical applications in war following her own controversial breaking of the <a href="Orenburg Pact" class="internal-link">Orenburg Pact</a>. </p>
