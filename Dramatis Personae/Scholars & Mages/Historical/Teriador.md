<p>Edmund Teriador was a Turvi archmage and alchemist of <a href="Uhl" class="internal-link">Uhl</a>, and an associate member of the <a href="The Alchemists Guild" class="internal-link">Alchemists Guild</a>. His seat was Swallow Tower, a large tower owned by a line of archmages and unofficially sponsored by the city in exchange for magical expertise and protection, an arrangement not uncommon in the larger cities. His fields of research were arcane diseases and magical cartography. After the plague that struck <a href="Grynborg" class="internal-link">Grynborg</a> in 820PA he grew more and more panicked, and spent the last part of his life obsessing over the idea of a magical plague from the new world.</p>
<p>In 834PA Teriador sponsored the voyage of <a href="Jurim Eykszoon" class="internal-link">Jurim Eykszoon</a> across the Tirean Ocean. He was looking for theories on his new diviner's map of the known world, and he also needed someone to test a hypothesis about a magical compass he was developing, which required testing thousands of miles from his home. Fearing plague, he forbade Eykszoon to land on <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a>. Teriador died in Uhl ten years later, having poisoned himself by accident. He believed he had created a preemptive antidote against all poisons, and in a sense it worked, but the potion, known in folklore as Teriador's Delight, was a vaccine-like draught of immense power that only a being of incredible supernatural constitution could have survived, and he was a 53-year-old scholar who had rarely left his tower. His student John of Gadly survived him and documented his final experiment.</p>
