<p>Xarelius Mi Hator, better known simply as Xarelius, was the Pirixim ur Ott between 565 and 609PA and is one of, if not the single most celebrated Arcanist in ottian history. His student <a href="Larkomas" class="internal-link">Larkomas Mi Terix</a>, sometimes referred to by outsiders as Larkomas of Ott, reached great fame as well and succeeded him as Pirixim, albeit only for  a short while. </p>
<p>Xarelius, a <a href="Gnome" class="internal-link">Gnome</a> of unusually tall statue, was born in the Hator Shaft of the eastern parts of <a href="Ott" class="internal-link">Ott</a> in 520PA and lived to be 96 Years old, dying from complications related to his diet in the winter of 609PA while still the acting Pirixim.</p>

<p>He is perhaps best known for his rediscovery and expanded work on what he dubbed the <a href="Cilenax Principle" class="internal-link">"Cilenax Principle"</a>, a concept lost since the days of old <a href="Cyraclion" class="internal-link">Cyraclion</a> that he transcribed from its elementary descriptions within the Codex <a href="Neyadastes" class="internal-link">Neyadastes</a> and spend the majority of his life contemplating, investigating and codifying. He, in modelling the efficiency of spells and artefacts, found that workings consistently cost less than their effects should demand, and that the saving grew with the precision of the prism without ever reaching certainty. Xarelius took up Neyadastes' term for what he had measured, and the Principle entered the classical teaching of <a href="Artificery" class="internal-link">Prosum</a>, where Ottian artificers still reckon an artefact's reliability by it. His work would later influence advanced Leyprism Theory as per <a href="Jevana" class="internal-link">Jevana of Leston</a> and effectively all future generations of physics based arcanists enormously. He is cited by <a href="Gharel Talnod" class="internal-link">Gharel Talnod</a> & <a href="Jan van Swarn" class="internal-link">Jan van Swarn</a >, who created the eponymous Turnometer and <a href="Talnod-Swarn Gradient" class="internal-link">Talnod-Swarn Gradient</a> in the 10th century, over 300 years after Xarelius death, as the single most influential and important Arcanist in post Rakhian history. </p>
<p>Within the Great Hall of the first Ott, the so called "Obsidian Wish" stands as a statue and memorial to the great Xarelius.</p>
<h2>Racism & Political Controversy</h2>
<p>Unfortunately the image of the great Xarelius has been sometimes overshadowed by his stern political opinions regarding the nature of "the Dwarf and other Vermin". He is credited to be largely responsible for the Crisis of Dihil Peak, that almost saw communications between Ott and <a href="Ogul-Vosko" class="internal-link">Ogul-Vosko</a> full break down and advocated a record 189 times in the Senate of Ott for the destruction of the Goblin Capital of <a href="Mitimgar" class="internal-link">Mitimgar</a>. One of his later propositions was ventures into the Useage of Kortopi Snails, organisms native to the underland caverns below the mountains that grew to great size, he is recorded to have "preferred to converse only with Snails for the rest of the year" if it meant he would never have to be escorted past the Great Tunnel to Mitimgar again, and later added: "perhaps one could look into turning these wretched breeders into a sustainable food source for the Goat herds during winter" Within Ott itself he is however largely respected for his "brave expression of patriotism".</p>
