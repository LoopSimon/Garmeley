<p>Muriel von Gloss was Countess of Gloss and a member of the meadi magical establishment during the 9th century, famously breaking with the <a href="Orenburg Pact" class="internal-link">Orenburg Pact</a> and fighting alongside the united meadi Army during the <a href="War of Horn and Fang" class="internal-link">War of Horn and Fang</a>. She was the acting Rector of Korsfall College from 843 until 859PA when she became subject of one of the most famous cases of the Arcane Judiciary and international magical law following her open disregard of the Orenburg Pact. She defeated the Sorcerer Viagoris in a magical duel in 861PA during the War and would later die herself during the Battle of Gadly Bridge a few years later.</p>
<p>Born at Gloss Castle in the northern Meadi Highlands, Muriel was send to study at the Capital from a very young age and quickly displayed an affinity for conditional casting and a general aptitude for magic, leading to her quickly becoming one of the Colleges rising prospects. After having come in contact with the urien Scholar during a guest lecture at Korsfall, von Gloss journeyed to central Ur and became a personal student to <a href="Jevana" class="internal-link">Jevana of Leston</a> at Greenstone Tower from 831 until 841PA .</p>
<p>Now a widely respected scholar and expert in the field of modern Prism Theory, von Gloss began campaigning within the organisation for internal change and a modernization initiative, leading to the eventual resignation of Rector Urgemar Bast and her election as Rector within the Institution. To this date she remains one of only people that have ever served in the role without prior stay as a Chair of the College. She reformed parts of the Ichori Choir and introduced a group of specialists, known as the Ichorhand that were trained especially for observation and acquisition of the greater population of the realm in watch for magical aptitude and danger.<br> During her stay as Rector she would also encourage a more open-minded approach with arcane research and practice in regards to traditional schools of thought, the Meadi establishment post Wulfrick and Galiatar had become extremely advanced for human standards and developed a great understanding, making progress in fields previously thought to be dominated solely by the beheran scholars, but this once modern way of thinking had become a stale act of performance and other schools of thought or arcane tradition were quickly disregarded. An avid historian and follower of the ancient <a href="Auguric Rite & Invocation" class="internal-link">auguric</a> rite herself, she encouraged the return to a pluralist approach when deciphering the planes and the ley, instead of quickly brushing off non-elven research.</p>
<p>Following the outbreak of the Great War with eastern Vihr, known as the War of Horn and Fang, Muriel became an outspoken supporter for the idea of academic support an magical resources in the war effort, having inherited the title of Countess herself she was a strong political ally to King <a href="Rulers of the Meadi" class="internal-link">Arngil II von Kor</a> and would eventually decide to personally join the united Army herself. This decision caused almost immediate criticism from local meadi circles, established arcanists and powerful institutions such as the Hevn Academy and Korsfall College herself.</p>
