<p>Miarat, known within the <a href="Liandar" class="internal-link">Liandar</a>'s internal tradition by the honorific the First Mind, was the senior Taidan master who served as co Zuanzaz of the Liandar alongside its founder Grand Vizier Chiangal, from the institutions formal establishment in 755PA. She is the highest ranking Shirin alchemist in the historical record and, by the account of the Liandar's own institutional histories, the individual most directly responsible for translating Chiangals political vision for the institution into a functional scholarly and operational reality. Beyond her role in the Liandar's founding, almost nothing about Miarat is known with certainty.</p>

<h3>The Runur and Miarat's Contested Reputation</h3>

<p>Miarat's association with the Runur program, the Liandar's production of alchemical constructs serving as the Imperial Palace's most sensitive guard force, is the dimension of her legacy that has generated the most controversy both within and outside the institution. The Liandars vague position is that the Runur represent a legitimate and technically demanding application of the Taidan whose development under Miarat's direction was a crowning achievement of Shirin alchemical knowledge. Critics within the broader scholarly tradition, particularly those associated with Zuhuri academic institutions in <a href="Vigzil" class="internal-link">Vigzil</a>, have argued that the creation of purpose built constructs designed to serve as an officially unacknowledged palace security force represents precisely the kind of power serving application of scholarship that distinguishes the Liandar's tradition unfavourably from more independent alchemical schools.</p>

<p>Miarat herself left no known written record of her views on these questions. The treatises attributed to her in the Liandar's internal curriculum are not publicly available, and external scholars who have requested access to them have consistently been declined. She remains, by design or accident, the most significant and least knowable figure in the history of Shirin alchemy: Both present at the foundation of the most powerful alchemical institution in Nirydia and almost entirely absent from the record that institution has chosen to share with the world.</p>
