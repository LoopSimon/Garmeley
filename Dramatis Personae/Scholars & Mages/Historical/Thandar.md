<p>Ung Thandar, better known simply as "The Athaw" (a traditional yppori honorific meaning roughly "the greatest teacher"), was a yppori Elementalist and Channeller , credited with founding the Arts of Change, the most popular school of the supernatural and magic within the lands of eastern Kaito.  </p>
<p>Among his greatest and most famous contributions to said Arts is without a doubt his masterwork, the "Circle of Thandar", within which  he describes the forces of the world as the ten elements. Thandars Circle holds four pure elements, Earth, Water, Wind and Fire, and six between them, each born where one element passes into another: magma between earth and water, storm between water and wind, lightning between wind and fire, frost between earth and wind, and two so rare that few have witnessed them, glass struck from stone by fire from the sky, and fire burning within water.</p> 
<p>His work can be traced back to the ancient elven Arcanist and scholar <a href="Ptamon" class="internal-link">Ptamon</a> and his eponymous "Ptamons Key. It would later be improved upon by <a href="Szuan" class="internal-link">Tihir Szuans</a> nearly half a millenia later but is still effectively held as truth and represents the fundaments of magical understanding within the lands of the Purple Sea. </p>
<p>The great <a href="The Pillars of Myan" class="internal-link">"Pillars of Myan"</a> are named after Szuans theoretical Improvement upon Thandars work, and represent the greatest Age of Yppori arcane research into the elements. Today they have fallen into dishonor and disgrace, became religious or state institutions or have been hunted to extinction and forced into diaspora, leading inadvertently to a greater spread of Thandar and Szuans Teachings.</p>
