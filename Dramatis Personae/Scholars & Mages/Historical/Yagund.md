<p>Yagund was a remarkably devious and troublesome Ogre Mage that wrought terror and pain on the peoples of southern Vihr, Grevenna and Kherova during the 5th century with his insectoid monsters, the <a href="Zuzka" class="internal-link">Zuzka</a>, wich he used to enforce his rule over stretches of the Ravenleaf Forest, the Yellow Moor, the southern mountains and the hills and dens of the Expanse. Yagund was slain by the Hero <a href="Darmil zi Perliav" class="internal-link">Darmil</a> in 489PA at his Lair, the Breeding Ground of Horomur where the Ogre was bringing forth his monsters into existence.</p>
<p>Yagund is notable as one of the last of the Ravenleaf Ogres to unite a considerably amount of their kind and attempt to establish a great realm of their own once again, following the many Ogre Wars of the 1st - 3rd century PA. With his demise, the Ogrelords roaming eastern Ilrakhan would end up splintering as little as a decade later, having only been kept united out of fear for Yagund and his powers. His hatred for the Vihri was exceptional and while he himself has died long ago, the vilest of his Zuzka are still out there, unnatural mixtures of Insect and Magic that haunt the Ravenleaf and far beyond.</p>
