<p>The Elf Khaba was an ancient Beheran mathematician and philosopher, who worked in the fields of geometry and logic, his ground breaking treaty "Zher Djemin" is widely considered to be the foundational work for the field of geometry and still dominates the field until today. Although scientifically extremely influential, very little is known about Khabas real life and it seems likely he went largely unrecognized for his work during his day. There is speculation that Khaba studied at Mereskis and helped establish the initial foundations for the Acaramensis Academy, introduced during the Alparid Dynasty, that would later become the foundation for the so called "Eastern Spring" and the establishment of a greater scientific canon within Behera, but we cannot say anything for certain.</p>
