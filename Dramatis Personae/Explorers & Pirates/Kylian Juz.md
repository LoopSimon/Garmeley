 <small><i>this article concerns the <a href="The Discovery of Aisligos" class="internal-link">Discovery</a> and <a href="The Colonization of Peadyr" class="internal-link">Colonization</a> of Peadyr, for overviews look here</i></small> <p>Kylian Juz, called the Windcat, is a Sertovi <a href="Halfling" class="internal-link">halfling</a> pirate and the captain of the Fast Duchess, and one of the best known outlaws of the <a href="The New World" class="internal-link">new world</a>, wanted by nearly every major power. He was born in 886PA in <a href="Revelle" class="internal-link">Revelle</a>, the halfling dominion of western <a href="Sertova" class="internal-link">Sertova</a>, and belongs to a disenfranchised and rebellious generation raised in a failing realm. He has no love left for his home.</p> <p>Juz came by his ship through mutiny. The Fast Duchess was a Sertovi brigantine bound for <a href="New Fryd" class="internal-link">New Fryd</a>, and when her captain refused to delay the voyage and insisted on running extremely dangerous currents to keep their quota, Juz led the crew against him. The captain did not survive the mutiny and was left to the sea, and the brigantine never reached New Fryd. The Sertovi crew, who could expect nothing but a swift execution if they returned, had little choice but to follow him, and the Fast Duchess has sailed as a pirate ever since.</p> <p>He is called the Windcat for the speed and efficiency with which he takes ships. He is said to pounce on his prey like a cat, and his reputation rests above all on taking over a ship, whether she surrenders or resists, before the heavier galleons of the nearest port can arrive, a dangerous and risky act. He preys mostly on the shipping of the Sertovi, Othiri and Cevoli crowns and is a plague on the rich colonies of New Fryd, <a href="Livoa" class="internal-link">Livoa</a>, <a href="Alinanora" class="internal-link">Alinanora</a> and <a href="Almersport" class="internal-link">Almersport</a>. Rumour places his haven in the independent ports of the new world, above all <a href="Vaggerport" class="internal-link">Vaggerport</a>, the lawless cavern port where he is far from the only pirate to dock.</p> <p>It is also rumoured that he keeps contact with Meadi merchants and agents in <a href="Dueren" class="internal-link">Dueren</a>, and his light touch with, if not outright ignorance of, the vessels of Dueren and <a href="Faarhovn" class="internal-link">Faarhovn</a> is often cited as evidence. The rumour is denied, and it is pointed out that those colonies lie in the north,while Juz works the south. A further rumour places him in contact with Stadtholder <a href="Jan van de Spiegel" class="internal-link">Jan van de Spiegel</a> of the Blijgrat League, who in the 920s made deals with privateers of Zeeport to prey on the same Cevoli, Othiri and Sertovi shipping.. His most recent action came in 926PA, when he attacked a heavily guarded Cevoli vessel half a day out of Alinanora against heavy odds, only to find her loaded with <a href="Narzoi" class="internal-link">Narzoi</a> slaves, among the first to be shipped by the <a href="Narzoi Slavetrade" class="internal-link">slave trade</a>. The loss was a blow to the profiteers of that trade, the<a href="Pastelliora" class="internal-link">Pastelliora</a> first among them. Rumours differ on what became of the captives, some holding that he set them ashore on the coast of Peadyr, others that they were sold on within the week.</p>
