<small><i>this article concerns the <a href="The Discovery of Aisligos" class="internal-link">Discovery</a> and <a href="The Colonization of Peadyr" class="internal-link">Colonization</a> of Peadyr, for overviews look here</i></small> <p>Eleanor Grig, Lady of Grig, was a Spurshey noblewoman and explorer of the <a href="Runsby" class="internal-link">Republic of Runsby</a>, and the leader of the expedition of 882PA that found the Loreli Islands, where the colony of <a href="Dalesire" class="internal-link">Dalesire</a> was later chartered. Born in 842PA at the Grig Estate in the west of Runsby, she was the eldest child of the last Lord Grig, and as in Runsby the eldest child inherits everything, without any bias towards sons or daughters, she inherited both the estate and the title of her father. Runsby is a republic, but its noble houses still hold great power in the two chambers of Parliament in Hark, and she married a man of the upper class, with whom she had a son, Archibald, who was to be the next of the house.</p> 
<p>Eleanor was a noble entrepreneur with a taste for adventure. She and Nale Dirrenger, who sailed across the Tirean Ocean four times between 874 and 878PA, were friendly rivals, and where he charted the seas and set up supply posts, she built a larger expedition of her own on his charts. Financed from her own fortune, with investments from other rich nobles and members of the upper class of Runsby, she sailed in 882PA aboard her personal ship, the <i>Winged Dream</i>, and mapped the tiny rocky islands of the Spring Sea.</p> 
<p>Among them she found the islands that now bear her name as Grigs Landing, where her party was met with hostility. They are home to a particularly wild breed of the <a href="Sarmi" class="internal-link">Sarmi</a>, little more than primitive and predatory animals, and to a small tribe of <a href="Narzoi" class="internal-link">Narzoi</a>. Beyond them lay the lush Loreli Islands, which Eleanor, a devout follower of Lorel and her <a href="Doggerism" class="internal-link">Doggerist</a> faith, named after the goddess.</p> 
<p>In the years that followed she kept up her own supply posts on the islands and petitioned Parliament in Hark to grant the growing communities a charter. In 901PA she died in a riding accident in the countryside of western Runsby, aged 59, four years before Parliament granted the charter she had asked for. Her son Archibald became the first Lord-Governour of Dalesire in 905PA and held the office until his death in 914PA.</p> 
<p>The family she left behind has only risen since. Through their continued investment in the colonies and their importance to them, the Grigs have become famous, prestigious and increasingly wealthy, and the present head of the house, her grandson Leicester Grig, leads a wing in Parliament and has set his sights on the Chancellorship. Of the islands that carry her name, Grigs Landing remains untamed, and Dalesire is now planning an invasion to pacify them.</p>
