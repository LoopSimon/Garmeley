<p>Balan Reyd  was a Ravlander explorer and the first sailor of the Old World known to have set foot on <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a>. He came ashore on the north-eastern coast of the island in 785PA and founded Camp Reyd, but the expedition was cut short almost at once. Within a week of landing, Reyd and many of his men had died of disease, and only the survivors returned across the Tirean Ocean, carrying with them a map of the coast he had reached.</p>
<p>Reyd was buried at the camp he had founded, and the ground has been settled twice since. In 842PA Sertovi settlers of Ravland raised New Rav on the site of Camp Reyd, and when that colony was destroyed in 847PA, <a href="New Fryd" class="internal-link">New Fryd</a> was founded on the same ground in 880PA. His grave is thought to lie beneath it.</p>
<p>His name survives in Balansbay, which bears his given name, and in Reydsland, the tidal island on which New Fryd stands. Because he died within days of landing and left no lasting settlement, the claim that he discovered Peadyr is disputed. Sertovi and Othiri tradition honours Reyd as its true discoverer, a claim contested by the Cevoli, who favour <a href="Marino Viamali" class="internal-link">Marino Viamali</a>, and by the Weadi, who favour <a href="Jurim Eykszoon" class="internal-link">Jurim Eykszoon</a>.</p>
