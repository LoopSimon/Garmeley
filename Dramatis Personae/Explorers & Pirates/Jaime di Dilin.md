<p>Jaime di Dilin (c. 806PA to 919PA) was a Turpadori Half-Elf explorer and diplomat, the first Conte di Dilin. In 842PA, at about thirty-six, he negotiated with the <a href="Vudi" class="internal-link">Vudi</a>, a tribe of fisherfolk living near the site of modern <a href="Alinanora" class="internal-link">Alinanora</a> on the eastern coast of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a>. The Vudi are not associated with any of the federations of the island, and they gave him great respect and enjoyed the trade he brought. The river at whose mouth Alinanora now stands was named after him.</p>
<p>For his endeavours Dilin was granted the title of Conte, and he was able to repay the massive debts he owed to the <a href="Pastelliora" class="internal-link">Pastelliora</a>. He never returned to the new world. Instead he made a good living as a member of high society, travelling through Cevolia and even Meadrath to entertain nobles and wealthy burghers with the stories of his journey, and he lived to the age of about one hundred and thirteen before dying in 919PA.</p>
<p>His family endures today as the Conte di Dilin, a minor rank of nobility created on one of the islands of the new world near Alinanora, though none of its members has ever visited it. Alinanora, founded by the Duchy of <a href="Turpador" class="internal-link">Turpador</a> in 900PA, draws its founding spirit from his journey. The Vudi, once held in great respect, are today treated increasingly worse.</p>
