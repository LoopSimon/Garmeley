<p>Elabar, born Glarraq, was the prophet who founded the <a href="Path of the Second Moon" class="internal-link">Path of the Second Moon</a> and the first Sandimar of <a href="Abaleq" class="internal-link">Abaleq</a>. In 788PA, on the Fazaar Isles, he rejected the name Glarraq, took the name Elabar and announced his truth to his followers: that Zolra had won. That night he and his followers hosted the feast remembered as the Night of Zolrai, during which various people were killed, largely by accident, and he is known to the Zolraii as the one who first spoke her name in the open.</p>  
<p>As Sandimar he led the Fazaar chiefdoms out of the Qahori Federation and established Abaleq as a sedentary orcish kingdom in the ancient style of Qa. He is far better known as Elabar than as Glarraq, a name he rejected, and of the man he was before that night little is recorded.</p>
