<p>Tezmer is the greatest living Shaman of the Chiani faith of Peadyr and a religious leader respected by the three major tribes of the island. He has time and time again advocated for peace and cooperation with the new men from the east, the Dari in the tongue of the Pea Narzoi, and his efforts allowed various settlers to establish communities on the shores of the island.</p>
