A Kavi by birth, he is the most powerful and famous of the shamans, said to be able to fly like a bird and to receive visions from his ancestors.</p> <p>He has time and again advocated peace and cooperation with the new men from the east, the Dari in the tongue of the Pea Narzoi, and his efforts allowed various settlers to establish communities on the shores of the island. He is credited with keeping in place the accord between the <a href="Kavi" class="internal-link">Kavi</a> and <a href="Faarhovn" class="internal-link">Faarhovn</a>, and with easing the founding of <a href="Alinanora" class="internal-link">Alinanora</a> in 900PA, though he has since grown uneasy about the settlement whose arrival he helped to smooth.</p> <p>When the Odise rose under <a href="Rylla" class="internal-link">Rylla</a> in 917PA, Tezmer sought to speak with <a href="Almersport" class="internal-link">Almersport</a>, but the conflict had already broken out. He had opposed her raids but honoured her after her death. Since the war began in earnest he has urged the Odise to stop, for he can see that they cannot win, and has argued instead that the Dari should be played against one another through diplomacy. Criticism of him has grown since the death of Rylla and the outbreak of the war.</p>
