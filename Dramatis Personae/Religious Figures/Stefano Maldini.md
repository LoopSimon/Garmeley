<p>Stefano di Volio Maldini is the Bishop of the Velasangra Cathedral, also known as the Archsanctuary of Jiseux, and the in official head of the Trinic Church in Cevolia, if not the entirety of the faith. He was chosen to succeed the previous Bishop of the Velasangra in 921PA at the age of 45 and is rumoured to be backed completely by the powerful <a href="Fellows of the Dancing Flame" class="internal-link">Fellows of the Dancing Flame</a>, a cevoli paramilitary organisation with considerably military, economic and political power. In the nearly six years since his ascendence he has begun a "holy cleanse" of unwanted elements within the entire cevoli clergy and is often considered part of a so called Neo-Trinicist movement, that aims to strengthen the clergy akin to a more gethric structure of hierarchies and political influence, while retaining trinic progressive and puritanical convictions. In this regard he has allied himself with the <a href="Luminists" class="internal-link">Luminist movement</a>, and officially backed many of their propositions, even going so far as to question the validity of the Wermian Accords that were after all, written and underlined by a clergy corrupt with gethric teachings. Many consider Maldini the most powerful man in all of Cevolia.</p>
<p>Maldin's elevation in 921PA did not occur in a vacuum. The <a href="Vorellan War" class="internal-link">Vorellan Wars</a> had ended only two years prior, leaving <a href="Battvia" class="internal-link">Battvia</a> economically ruined, deeply indebted and militarily dependent on <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath's</a> intervention for its survival, a position that made the heroic but battered Prince Rigaro IIIs court considerably more receptive to institutional reform than it might otherwise have been. Within six months of his elevation, Maldini called the Lona Council, convened at the central levo city of Lona, which formally restructured Battvia as a constitutional monarchy, the first such settlement in Cevoli history, negotiated under the shadow of a war the crown had barely survived and could not afford to repeat.</p> 
<p>Whether Maldini acted as a genuine reformer seeking to prevent future wars of dynastic ego, or as a political architect who understood that a constitutionally constrained Battvia was easier to influence than an absolute one, is a question that his critics and admirers have argued about since 921PA without reaching agreement. What is noted in certain circles, cetrainly never in public or printm is that <a href="Odrick von Fulmer" class="internal-link">Odrick von Fulmer</a>, whose alliance brought Meadrath into the Vorellan Wars at precisely the moment that tipped their outcome, has maintained a diplomatic and vague relationship with Maldiniever since. The Vorellan Wars left Battvia weakened, Meadrath ascendant and the Bishop the most politically consequential ecclesiastical figure in Cevolia. Maldini has declined to address the argument of his involvement with the war directly, which his supporters describe as appropriate episcopal dignity and his detractors describe as confirmation.</p>
