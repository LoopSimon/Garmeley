<p>Sasha "Gurna" Gurnakov, is a gethric Minister working at the Nightingale Library in the City of Izhevk, under the oversight of St. Igors Cathedral. Once on the fast track of a promising clerical career in the central regions, due to unknown and potentially scandalous circumstances he was removed from his office at Voblag and send away to Izhevk, not in charge of a Ministry but instead as lector of a library, albeit a large and old one at that. Although St. Igors is one of the larger and more imposing churches in Grachy, the position as lector is essentially a dead end and sentence to fall into dust and unknown for Gurna whose young life within the church may already be at a grueling standstill.</p>
