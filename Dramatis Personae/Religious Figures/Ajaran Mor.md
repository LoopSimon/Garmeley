<p>Ajaran Mor was a Durenni Bihra Philosopher and Teacher during the 3rd and 2nd century BA and one of the Yova, the "Great Teachers" of the Vas Ira Traditions. He is notable for transcribing many core principles within the greater umbrella of the Vas Ira belief and founding the unified denomination known as the <a href="Rayadan" class="internal-link">Rayadan</a>. Mor died in 169BA in his domicile at the Spire of Arag, within the outskirts of the great City of Balramur while meditating, he apparently had rejected all food as worldly sustenance to rid himself of earthly tethers.</p>
<p>As one of the founding figures within the Rayadan Faith, a denomination in the Vas Ira religious Umbrella, it was Ajaran Mor that first described the God known as Raym as the supreme being of the cosmos and the final inheritor of all collective experiences. He wandered the island of Durennis during the early period of the Omna of Japhal and amassed a great following during his lifetime, reaching near mythical status during his later years, amassing a great fortune and holding large lectures in the population centres of the Omna.</p>
<p>Many of his words were later recorded in a scholarly and religious effort to preserve them by transcribing them onto great stone tablets, based on the retellings of those that had studied under him and listened to his great lectures. While primarily known within Durennis and the followers of the Rayadan, many followers of the great four traditions within the Vas Ira honour Ajarans status as a Yova.</p>
