<p>Father Timothy Mevan is the leader of the gethric Congregation of <a href="Ikara" class="internal-link">Ikara</a> who leads his flock from Candlehouse Chapel, situated beyond Riverside in the east of the city. He has so far managed to stay under the radar of the authorities and the more criminal groups of the city, but recently was forced to face the tragic consequences of a growing drug trade in his community and the greater city beyond.</p>
