<p>Ued al-Raban is the current Grand Hierophant of the <a href="Golden Dusk" class="internal-link">Golden Dusk</a> and the de-facto spiritual leader of the greater Elven Paragon Cult of eastern Behera. She is a drow elf and was born in the undercity of Siskaral in 701PA, having held the office of Grand Hierophant since 891PA for over three decades. She famously rejected the appointment has High Priest of Jiravid, the most government facing role within the Golden Dusk that has a permanent seat on the Diran, the Sultans highest council and has spend overall extremly limited time in the public.</p>
<p>The Grand Hierophant is the current Head of the Shar al-Raban, one of the eldest drow lineages with significant influence and holdings in Siskaral itself. Her family has always been close with the spiritual engagements of the Sultanate and open critics of the so called Doctrine of Exceptionalism as instituted by Sultan <a href="Lizun I" class="internal-link">Lizun I</a>. She is rumoured to be a part of the mysterious and intangible Scions of Noval, a group that is supposedly working against the official perspective of the Sultante and considers non Elbae blood relatives for the prospect of Paragon Ascension. As the Grand Hierophant herself, these rumours are fully unsubstantiated however and al-Raban would never dignify them with a response. </p>
