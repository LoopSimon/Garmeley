<p>Nophoy "Ngalas" Hien-Ro is the acting Osafar of Sochor, the highest office of the Church of <a href="Sochor" class="internal-link">Sochorism</a> and the Voice of Ash for all his worshippers. He resides within the Great Kiln and is the spiritual leader of most inhabitants of <a href="Kaito" class="internal-link">Kaito</a> and the Purple Sea. Unlike his predecessor Pyon Meley, Hien-Ro is decidedly political and has declared the New World of <a href="Sirenna" class="internal-link">Sirenna</a> the destined paradise of the children of Sochor and called for all devout worshippers to support any undertaking that would douse the southern continent in Sochors holy ashes.</p>
<p>Hien-Roe has been deemed Ngalas, "born of Fire" in ancient yppori, and is the fourth Osafar to be declared part of the reincarnation cicle of the first Ngalas, the ancient Prophet of Sochor himself. This has manifested itself in unique abilities for the Osafar who seems to be unable to sustain physical injury and emit a strange, fiery glow from within as well as radiate immense heat as if burning alive.</p>
