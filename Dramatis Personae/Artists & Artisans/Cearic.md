<p>Reyner Cearic was a Stonemason and Artificer of great renown during 6th and 7th century, that worked primarily under the patronage of Pippin du Bregelle, the Duke of Morelle. While his artistry in the base studies of marble is without a doubt impressive, he is most famous for his famous "Silent Wardens", <a href="Golem" class="internal-link">Golems</a> he created for his patron the Duke, that lined the palace of Morelle and can still be found within the Duchy to this day, motionless but pristine. His work was particular in the sense that all his Wardens were lovers, made in pairs and immortalised in their bond.</p>
<p>Cearic died in the Whisperhills, said to have been torn apart and killed on the orders of Ingerma, the Dukes own daughter, upon his rejection to serve her in the same way he is said to have her father. Reyner and Pippin are rumoured to have been more than patron and artist, more than liege and servant and more then merely friends.</p>
