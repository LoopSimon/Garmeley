<p>Anito Cadarello is a turpadori Poet, infamous for his outspoken rejection of Trinicist and Gethricist Ideals, romantic view on old othiri and cevoli cults and tentative friend to many Luminists, although his extremely radical views on the Faith as a Whole have made him a pariah even within their ciricles. His works are very controversial but nonetheless popular in Cevolia, southern Sertova, Ur and Meadland.</p>
<p>"They paint the world in shadows, then tell us to hide by their lights."</p>
<p>Cadarello is sponsored by Duccessa Alina I Sevir of <a href="Turpador" class="internal-link">Turpador</a> herself and this powerful patron is likely the only reason he not only enjoys the ability to publicize his writings further, but also for his personal well being, as many have called for not only his imprisonment but even more archaic practices for cevoli standards such as to burn the heathen alive on the square of Viago. His connections to the mysterious <a href="Cult of Vibattu" class="internal-link">Cult of Vibattu</a>, a sect that has been growing more and more in the highlands and the Diebri countryside north of Calmona, but more and more within the elites of the cities of eastern <a href="Cevolia" class="internal-link">Cevolia</a> as well, are more obscure and less troubling to the public, although they likely cut way deeper than his perceived kinship with the <a href="Luminists" class="internal-link">Luminist</a> movement.</p>
