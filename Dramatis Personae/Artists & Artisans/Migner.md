<p>Andreas Migner is one of the most famous painters of the modern Age, active at the courts of Meadland, in the cities of the weadi, travelling through northern Ur and even in the capital of Vihr. He has created numerous portraits, but also produced religious art and satire, and his portraits are renowned in these days for their likeness.It is through his eyes that many famous figures of his day are pictured today and will likely be remembered for generations to come.</p>
<p>Migner was born in 888PA in Druell, one of the major towns in the <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Archduchy of Gisvale</a>, as the son of a successful Grain Merchant. At first, he painted murals and religious works before making international marks with his now famous Portrait of Johann Marpal, a famous trinicist theologian and philosopher.</p>
<p>His later career would culminate in the creation of various famous portraits made for members of the upper nobility and richer burgher class of northern ilrakhan, especially notable for his portrayals of various monarchs and princes.</p>
