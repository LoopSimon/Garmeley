<p>Tezir Dulakis was a reani author from hainach that published various works on the nature of war, its consequence and necessity as well as the art of diplomacy during the early 9th century, he famous wrote the work "Of Sand and Storms" detailing the nature of the political world following the <a href="Wars of Elbae Aggression" class="internal-link">three Wars of Elbae Aggression</a> and was a lifelong supporter of the sultanate and policies embracing its suzerainty over Hainach. He is a well read author and political philosopher in many courts and academies across Inara, although there is an unmistakable negativity towards him in the discourse of his homeland itself, where Dulkas is more than not referred to as a traitor to his fatherland and part of the very generations that cemented post war elven rule.</p>
