<p>Signa Nylldottr was a Jueli warrior from <a href="Trykkerog" class="internal-link">Trykkerog</a> and one of the three Heroes of Sydrow who killed the witch <a href="Black Tanya" class="internal-link">Black Tanya</a> around 400PA. She came south across the Torn Sea to free her brother, who had fallen captive to Tanya, and she is remembered as a hero among the Jueli peoples to this day.</p>  
<h2>Origins</h2>  
<p>Signa was born among the Jueli of Trykkerog, the seafaring people of the Malksbay and the Giants Steps, in the days when the Gethric faith was first taking hold of the island and most of its people still kept to the old <a href="Bykmali Paganism" class="internal-link">Bykmali</a> ways. She became a devout follower of <a href="Melmer" class="internal-link">Melmer</a>, the Wave-Kissed, the missionary whom the waters loved and obeyed, and in his honour she wielded a trident of pure water. Through it she could channel the divine powers of the Candleflame, but she never left behind the traditions of her own people, and the tales tell of her as both a Gethric hero and a daughter of the north.</p>  
<h2>The Heroes of Sydrow</h2>  
<p>Black Tanya invited seers and shamans from the north to her court on Sydrow, and many who answered her call never came home. Signa's brother was among the captives of the witch, and Signa sailed south to find him. On Sydrow she joined the elf <a href="Thiar" class="internal-link">Thiar</a> and the Vihri knight <a href="Espir" class="internal-link">Sir Espir of the Oathhills</a>, and the three of them broke into Syd Keep and killed Tanya around 400PA. In the keep Signa found her brother alive, but he had become a slave to Tanya's whims, a puppet still conscious inside his own body but no longer able to control his movements. Signa killed him with tears in her eyes and so brought him peace.</p>  
<h2>Legacy</h2>  
<p>In Vihr Signa is told as one of the Heroes of Sydrow, a Gethric warrior of the north with a trident of water, who fought beside a Vihri knight and a converted elf against a Vihri witch. Among the Jueli she is a hero in her own right and one of the early heroes of the Gethric faith on Trykkerog, and her voyage south to free her brother, and the mercy she gave him at its end, is still told in the songs of the Malksbay.</p>
