<p>Eremir Arfaal al Kiar is the Head of the al Kiar Family and the leader of their Caravans, that travel the Asef, Hessef and parts of <a href="Hainach" class="internal-link">Hainach</a> selling precious perfumes, he has been in charge of the Caravan for almost two decades now and survived in the tough and dangerous business while remaining a kind and open hearted man. A devout <a href="Ilonism" class="internal-link">Ilonist</a>, Eremir observes the many rites and days of worships of the Faith while respecting foreign sects and religions when he encounters them. His travels have brought him as far as <a href="Fogorix" class="internal-link">Fogorix</a>, New Kulum and Almadin and he speaks many tongues and has taken many lovers.</p>
<p>As of May 927 Eremir and his three children, Saram, Marat & Effira, have journeyd from Triloska in eastern Hainach back into the Sultanate, no their way to the old city of <a href="Ikara" class="internal-link">Ikara</a>. Here in the "City of Fate" they hope to sell their remaining wares during the spectacles of the arena that are to take place in a week. On the road he encountered the adventurers Marianna, Zinris, Yara and Perzuk, offering them hospitality and familiar treatment as is custom for his peoples.</p>
