<p>The Elf known to many as Ayerlan, although it is unlikely this was his real birth name, was a slave and drug dealer and general facilitator of illegal activities such as smuggling and criminal auctions in the lands of the Storm Sea during the early 10th century. Ayerlan served many masters over his lifespan, all of them unknown to this date, and was able to circumvent authorities through a mix of blackmail, bribery, murder and connections.</p>
<p>During the Festival of Flowers of May 927, he turned the Son of the prominent viner <a href="Zavro Firelle" class="internal-link">Zavro Firelle</a>, Loukas, into a hideous monstrosity by administering a strange potion under the guise it would help the child. Ayerlan appeared to have used the Firelle ship for smuggling activities that required him to go unnoticed by <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae</a> authorities specifically a few months back, likely related to his recent spree of kidnappings. When a party of strangers intervened and Loukas turned into a monster without thought, he flew and the ensuing combat would leave his long term enforcer and bodyguard Helia captured, and later executed by <a href="Adral Fidrakis" class="internal-link">Sherrif Fidrakis of Kontini</a>, and the Firelle Manor burned down to its foundations.</p>
<p>The party of heroes, with the help of Archeomancer <a href="Theresa Salbridge" class="internal-link">Theresa Salbridge</a>, was able to track down Ayerlan into the ancient beheran ruins of an ilonist temple, later in history sealed by the elves under the Meteri Dynasties religious opression of Ilonism. It appears Ayerlan had, if alone is questionable, made the Vaults below the Seal a base of operations and been part of terrible experiments on human, elven and mogyri victims in the ruins deep below the Sun Dial. He was forced into a corner and seeing no other way out drank one of the experimental potions created in this very lab. Alas his will and body would not stand up to the forces he toyed with and he convulsed in terrible distortion and mutation into a being of waxlike plasma, bone and flesh. Who knows how much of Ayerlan really remained in the last moments of hits life when the hero Marianna cleaved him in half. </p>
