<p>Kessim Miximur is an incredibly rich landholder and magnate in the northern Sultanates City of <a href="Ikara" class="internal-link">Ikara</a>, he is one of the most influential members of the cities merchant class and often connected to its criminal underworld as well. The embodiment of a self-made Goblin and the definition of new money, Miximur has recently build an extravagant Villa outside of the city in the sprawl near the Riverside districts, apparently employing the help of a personal magus within its construction. The Goblin runs various enterprises to, allegedly, hide and wash his illicit smuggling and other criminal enterprises, but is most famous for his ownership of the Cities massive Dye Halls, south east of Ikara. As the dye and textile industries are among the biggest in the area and the city itself, he has earned a fortune distributing and working with local merchants and caravans.</p>
<p>Kessim is the sponsor of the Velvet Jesters, a Banner under which various athletes and duellists compete in the Fights and contests of Ikaras Falcons Pit, striving to one day win one of the great prizes.</p>
