<p>The enigmatic Head of the Kirelli Cartel, Marosz Kirelli is one of the richest men in eastern Ilrakhan and one of the least scrupulous ones as well. He rose to power as the head of the Velvet Tears Mercenary Band in early 10th century central <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Vihr</a> where he garnered a reputation of brutal tactics when tasked with pacifying the region of Eskerel south of <a href="Opuzna" class="internal-link">Opuzna</a> by the local Gri. The Velvet Tears disbanded and were essentially turned into the foundation for what is now known as the Kirelli Cartel.</p>
<p>Officially speaking, the Kirelli Cartel is a mercantile Organisation in the business of distributing cargo safely from Nirydia into Vihr and greater Ilrakhan via land routes, primarily through the Hold of <a href="Tizin-Kol" class="internal-link">Tizin-Kol</a>, the cities of <a href="Kherova" class="internal-link">Kherova</a> & <a href="Azuvke" class="internal-link">Azuvke</a> and the ports of <a href="Grevenna" class="internal-link">Grevenna</a>.</p>
<p>In reality, although technically exclusively, the Kirelli Cartel is one of the largest international drug rings shipping illegal and dangerous addictive substances into Vihr and <a href="Grachy" class="internal-link">Grachy</a>, primarily and notoriously almost all the  <a href="Zondust" class="internal-link">Zondust</a> and <a href="Astafar" class="internal-link">Astafar</a> trade in the Rascy go directly or indirectly through the hands of the Cartel.</p>
<p>Marosz himself owns multiple estates and villas but currently primarily resides in Bryansk, central Grachy.</p>
