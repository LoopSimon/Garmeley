<p>Kamzi Mardil is the acting master of ceremonies of Ikara and the voice of the Falcons Pit, one of the most beloved celebrities of the city of Fate. He has been upholding this position for nearly four decades ever since he arrived from the southern lands of Iz-Almadus, a born entertainer Kamzi employs a mystical hookah that allows his voice to be projected in houndreds of voices to anyone who hears it, in their native tongues.</p>
<p>Mardil is a notorious fan of the art of performance and theatrics in the arena, but hates those who hide what occurs in shadows and magic, putting the spectators experience ahead of everyone else. He has a large sway over the crowd as well that is known to have effected the outcome of whole encounters.</p>
