<p>Meana Tofir Telamon is the current head of the Telamon Family of old asefi nobility, living within Telamon Keep just south of <a href="Ikara" class="internal-link">Ikara</a> itself, she is the richest citizen of the City of Fate, owns a large portion of the Toumis Farmland, sponsors the Skyrider Company of the Falcons Pit, the owner of various businesses within the city such as the armour outfitter and weaponsmith Pieros Blades, and the head of the Glimmercapes Band of Mercenaries. As a wealthy and important celebrity of the city, she is a figure of influence only rivalled by the highest elven officials and most powerful of Magisters.</p>
<p>Following the death of her father, Meana has taken charge of the family business under a decade ago but already gathered experience holding a seat on the cities council of commerce, a position she till holds and an institution she exerts immense influence upon. Her sponsorship of one of the most promising and the single most successful company of the City Games has put her at a jovial rivalry with, amongst other, the <a href="Ilman Gorak" class="internal-link">Sanjak Ilman </a>himself. Little of Meanas private life is known and she is only rarely seen within the city walls.</p>
