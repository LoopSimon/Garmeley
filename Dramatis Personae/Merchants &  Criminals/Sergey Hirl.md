<p>Sergey Hirl, known in his neighbourhood simply as "Salty Sergey" is the water-loving Proprietor and Owner of Sergeys Salty Spoils, located in the western Riverside of <a href="Izhevk" class="internal-link">Izhevk</a>. He sells, maintains and buys magical items, scrolls and potions, specifically those related to his eternal love, the Rivers and Seas of the world, and operates with the blessings and under the watchful eye of the City Watch, working as a liason for magical identifications during investigations, a much preferred alternative to the eccentric and often aloof inhabitants of Borni Tower. His relationship with authorities and general beloved reputation within in the city has allowed Sergey to prosper with a more liberal approach to the buying an selling of artefacts, his only real competition being the Peddlers of the Black Alley Shops and the generally very busy residents of the Alchemists Guild, who work almost entirely on contract in these days.</p> 
<p>While noone doubts Sergeys love for the Seas, his entire shop is themed after the maritime after all, the verity of his tales from before he arrived at Izhevk is another matter, wether or not he truly served in the Republican Navy, saw the spawn of the Black Kraken or heard the song of the briney Maids is at the very least up for debate, but spinning tales is perhaps the most seamen characteristic about Sergej.</p>
