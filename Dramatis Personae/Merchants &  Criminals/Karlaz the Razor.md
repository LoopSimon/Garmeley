<p>Karlaz "the Razor" is the Captain of the Seaworm, a massive Galleon crewed by a mix of <a href="Shirin" class="internal-link">Shirin</a>, Human,<a href="Orc" class="internal-link">Orc</a>,  <a href="Bihra" class="internal-link">bihra</a> and <a href="Mogyr" class="internal-link">mogyri</a> Pirates and one of the most infamous vessels haunting the Niryd Sea, he famously has a rivalry with fellow Pirate turned Pirate Hunter, <a href="Sirmon Rezan" class="internal-link">Sirmon "the Weasel" Rezan</a> the Captain of the Albatross.</p>
<p>His name is associated with cruelty and recklessness although Karlaz himself thinks himself more of a philosopher and bringer of freedom, living life on his own terms. Unfortunately for his victims, those terms include the sacking, kidnapping and murder of many innocents, both sailors and merchants on vessels of the Sea, as well as those few ports the Crew of the Seaworm waged itself strong enough to take on by themselves as well. A Bounty of 25000 Talents sits on Karlaz head alone.</p>
