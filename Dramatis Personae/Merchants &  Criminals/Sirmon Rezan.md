<p>Sirmon Rezan, commonly referred to as "the Weasel" is the infamous Captain of the Albatross, a Qahori Galleon sailing under the Banner of the <a href="Grand Ziflis Company" class="internal-link">G.Z.C.</a> as far as latest reports show. While a reputable Captain now, his past is a chequered vest of more or less sanctioned piracy, as he was one of the most successful Pirates during the three decades following the Sultans Peace after the <a href="Great Elbae Golomid War" class="internal-link">Golomid War</a> at the end of the 9th century. His knowledge of the Niryd Sea is unrivalled and the Weasel is said to have broken many blockades and fought of hundreds of vessels in his time, stepping foot on the shores of <a href="Mogyr" class="internal-link">Drizan</a> and Durenni, smoking with the <a href="Shirin" class="internal-link">Shirin</a> of the <a href="Twintail Imperium" class="internal-link">Twintail</a> and bartering with the <a href="Safir" class="internal-link">Safir</a> Lords of <a href="Pravazik" class="internal-link">Pravazik</a>. </p>
<p>Most of his youth and the circumstances of his birth remain unknown, but remnants of his past were discovered in the docks of Paqor, the city between Worlds. He has been sailing under the Banner of the Company for around 14 years, his valued connections at foreign ports, knack for diplomacy and experience navigating and battling on the Niryd Sea being qualities the Company values more than to make an example of the Orc. The Weasel had been caught and put in chains in the year 912 but released as an apparently free man only a year later instead of hanging from the walls of Fort Arag in Ziflis.</p>
