<p>The unknowable and mysterious Gabriella Mile is the proprietor of Pireggi & Mile, the largest auction house in <a href="Battvia" class="internal-link">Battvia</a>, and one of the leading members of the <a href="Pastelliora" class="internal-link">Pastelliora</a>. Her reputation spreads throughout <a href="Cevolia" class="internal-link">Cevolia</a>, <a href="Meadland" class="internal-link">Meadland</a> and Urland as do her agents and influence. Although an avid socialite and desired guest, essentially nothing is known about her past or road to power and wealth.</p> 
<p>The name of Pireggi, her old business partner who died under mysterious circumstances in the 900s, remains in the name of the house. She is rumoured to have been involved in that death, but the rumour has done nothing to dim the standing of the house, which has since grown into the largest in Battvia.</p> 
<p>At home, the only act of Mile that anyone sees is the sale of the odd curiosity from the new world through her auction house, sales that collectors talk of for weeks. Rumour speaks of more, of hidden auctions of truly powerful and rare artefacts from the new world, held behind closed doors.</p> 
<p>Behind the sales stands the Pastelliora, the association of independent financiers and merchants of Cevolia, Ur and Sertova, and Mile is one of its leading members. The Pastelliora have found their greatest fortune in the <a href="Narzoi Slavetrade" class="internal-link">Narzoi Slavetrade</a>, in whose routes they have entrenched themselves, and have begun to build farms and plantations in the new world that are worked by slaves. Mile has shared in that fortune from the start.</p> 
<p>In the new world she now plans something of her own. She is preparing to establish private plantations, held in her own name and not in that of the association, in the hinterland of <a href="Alinanora" class="internal-link">Alinanora</a>, with the blessing of Conte Rigardo di Nello, and like the others they will be worked by the slaves the trade provides. The crop is sugar. Sugar is rare in Ilrakhan, where it otherwise has to be brought from the far east of Nirydia, and while it has been grown in the south of the new world for around two hundred years, it still sells dear in the west of Ilrakhan, to which the planters of the new world export and in which they make their fortunes. Mile means to be one of them.</p> 
<p>In this she works with Melina, the daughter of <a href="Marrin Andil" class="internal-link">Marrin Andil</a>, who runs the free port of <a href="Ulbreek" class="internal-link">Ulbreek</a>. Her move has raised questions as to the future of the Pastelliora as a whole, an association so secretive that little more than speculation is possible.</p>
