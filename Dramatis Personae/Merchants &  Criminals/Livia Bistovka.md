<p>The Owner of Izhevks House of Fortune, known to the slums simply as "Grandmother Livia." To the untrained eye, she appears as a kindly, plump older woman draped in thick furs and traditional knitted shawls, quick to offer a patron a hot bowl of stew and a sympathetic ear. In reality, she is the undisputed matriarch of Izhevks outer wall criminal empire. She runs rigged dice games, orchestrates high stakes card matches for disgraced boyars, and handles smuggling operations that bypass the citys toll gates entirely. The City Watch knows exactly what happens in the House of Fortune, but Grandmothers bribes are so generous, and her enforcers so brutally efficient, that the law gladly turns a blind eye to her kingdom in the mud.</p>
