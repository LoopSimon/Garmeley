<p>Darmil zi Perliav, later on in his life the Gri of Perliav, was a Tlok noble and hero of his people that lived during the 5th century, he is perhaps most famous for leading the expedition of heroes to bring an end to the <a href="Zuzka" class="internal-link">Zuzka</a> menace and was the figure that would end up slaying their Creator, the devious Ogre Mage <a href="Yagund" class="internal-link">Yagund</a> in 489PA. Darmil was an outspoken supporter of the ruling house of Zav and is sometimes seen as an early national hero of Vihr. His later representations depict him as a devout Gethric who achieved his victories by the grace of divine providence, although personal accounts and histories paint a less religious picture of the man.</p>
<p>Darmil was born in 443PA as the heir to the Castle of Perliav, the modern day prosperous town of Perliav that had grown around it still a small hamlet at the feet of the Keep. He was a famed slayer of beasts and is said to have been a virtuous knight as well, gifted with a strong arm and a sense for killing. Only eight years after his greatest deed, he would pass from the Red Pestilence that was wandering its deadly way through Ilrakhan at the end of the 5th century, his son Musko zi Perliav would succeed him as Gri and his line still rules to day under Gri Moreslav zi Perliav. A great statue was built near Ravenleaf Forest at the Crossing of Darmil, and various statues of him stand in Perliav itself.</p>
