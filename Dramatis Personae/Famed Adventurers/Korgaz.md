<p>Korgaz, called the Blade of Noon, was the champion of the Tirbo Clan during the <a href="Hobgoblin Invasion" class="internal-link">Hobgoblin Invasion</a> and the greatest warrior the <a href="Ak-Saray" class="internal-link">Ak-Saray</a> tradition has ever known. Born in 636PA, he first won the champion's title in 669PA at the age of thirty-three through the Dirgae tradition of annual ritual combat and held it for thirty-one consecutive years, longer than any champion in recorded Dirgae history.</p> <p> The Relic Sword he carried throughout was deceptive in appearance: a plain, unornamented blade made entirely of <a href="Wyrdrock" class="internal-link">Wyrdrock</a>, a strange ore,  lighter and sharper than any forged steel, unbreakable, capable of parting air with an edge that never dulled. He was fifty four years old when the invasion began, at Elpfield in 691PA he killed Earl Lodvig the Fat, later at the great Battle of Hadley he killed the famed Grand Master Samson the Quickof the Knights of the Gryphon and fought through the engagement in a manner that survivors on both sides describe as beyond what a mortal warrior should be able to do. At the Siege of Dursan he killed the great Knight Oswald III, Duke of Turfield, in personal combat but the urien defender of the Dursans Walls dealt him wounds severe enough that Korgaz played no part in the wars final engagement at Gobsfall Keep, lying wounded in the city his own blade had helped open while his Leader <a href="Tirbo" class="internal-link">Tirbo</a> died at the river without him.</p> <p>Korgaz survived the invasion, recovered, and defended his title for four more years before a younger challenger took the Relic Sword from him in Hobgiliath in 700PA, dying aged sixty-four. The Relic Sword passed through the champion tradition and is believed to remain within Ak-Saray still, held by whoever currently bears the title, though it has not been seen by outside observers in decades. The great forest north of Ak-Saray that Urien cartographers call Silenttree has been known in Overgob as the Korgaz since his death. In Ak-Sarays martial culture he represents the fullest expression of what the champions tradition is meant to produce: a warrior who held the blade for longer than anyone before him, killed enemies of the highest caliber, and lost eventually to someone younger and faster in exactly the manner the tradition requires. There was no dishonour in his life and his back knew no scars.</p>
