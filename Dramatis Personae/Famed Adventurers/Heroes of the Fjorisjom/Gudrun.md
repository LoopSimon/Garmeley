<p>Princess Gudrun, often called Gudrun Darnoda, is one of the main characters within the Fjorisjom Saga and the focal point of the stories narrative. Her capture and theft at the banquet of her father the rich Jarl Millgi, sets off the oath of the four heroes the chase after her.</p>
