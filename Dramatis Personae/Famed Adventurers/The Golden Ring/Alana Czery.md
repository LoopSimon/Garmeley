<p>Alana Czery, sometimes going by her old alias "the Mole" is a retired agent of the <a href="Vaergriff" class="internal-link">Vaergriff</a> running with the Golden Ring, a band of adventurers. She is a rather shadowy character owed to her past but is said to possess a surprisingly jovial personality and amicable character. She joined the party later than the other three, during a convoluted attempt of hers to fake her own death on a mission to eliminate a prominent merchant in the city of <a href="Jallbreg" class="internal-link">Jallbreg</a>, her paths first crossed with the Golden Ring. After a few mutual exchanges of strikes and lies, the affair was resolved when Czery was essentially saved from the hangman noose by the party, repaying the favour later when breaking the three out of a tough situation involving an angry Gnomish Crimelord, she has been a member of the Ring ever since. Like the other three members of the Golden Ring, she has gone missing after venturing to the Howling Henge, old burial grounds in the Wormwoods south of the urien settlement of Ansley.</p>
