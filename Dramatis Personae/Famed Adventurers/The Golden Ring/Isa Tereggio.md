<p>Isa Tereggio is an alchemist and doctor medicae that has worked with the Golden Ring, a band of adventurers since the 890s. Obsessed with various cure-alls, medicines and alternative methods of care she is known for her somewhat aloof and uninviting personality, although does not seem to be entirely above helping the common man. She was born in Molina, central Battvia, and studied the medical arts the cevoli have pioneered at the University of Aredo. Rumours about her involvement with the mysterious <a href="Cult of Vibattu" class="internal-link">Cult of Vibattu</a> have woven around her for decades. She is rumoured to be romantically involved with <a href="Filip of Leston" class="internal-link">Filip of Leston</a>. Like the other three members of the Golden Ring, she has gone missing after venturing to the Howling Henge, old burial grounds in the Wormwoods south of the urien settlement of Ansley.</p>
