<p>Horatio Momery Tulpier III, is a member of the Tuliper Clan of Calmbrook, a family of influential and rich burghers. A gifted sorcerer, he discovered is disinterest in the family business of cloth and coin early on and was send to study at the Circle of Gelmsburg, where he would later meet his lifelong friend <a href="Filip of Leston" class="internal-link">Sir Filip of Leston</a>. The two founded the Golden Circle and became local celebrities in Ur and beyond, Horatio being the least flashy and well known of the four for various personal reasons. Rumours about his arcane experiments and unorthodox methods had never subsided, pertaining to his studies at Gelmsburg and the reason he was forced to make a quick exit as well. Like the other three members of the Golden Ring, she has gone missing after venturing to the Howling Henge, old burial grounds in the Wormwoods south of the urien settlement of Ansley.</p>
