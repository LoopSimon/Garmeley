<p>Gismar is the legendary hero of the <a href="Meadi" class="internal-link">Meadi</a> who slew the Black <a href="Dragon" class="internal-link">Dragon Heximar</a>, and the founder of the House of Gismar, whose kings ruled <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a> for centuries. He is remembered as a man of fair hair and great strength who bore a sword as his weapon. The tales say that he lured Heximar over a region of craggy hills, cut by gorges and waterfalls, which have been called the Drakesfall Hills ever since, and hid himself on a ledge of the gorge below the beast, and as it passed over him he drove his sword up into its belly and was drenched in its blood. The blood of the Dragon is said to have given Gismar, his line and his sword a mythical, magical quality, though the tales are careful never to say what it was. The Meadi chronicles place the deed in 81PA, in the days of the <a href="Auguri Dominion" class="internal-link">Auguri Dominion</a>. Ardol I, who founded the Kingdom of Gisvale in 273PA, was counted among his descendants, the Gismar line kept the throne until the usurpation of Diedrik of Medlan, and the Black Dragon on green ground is still the banner of the Kingdom of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a>. For the kings of his house see <a href="Rulers of the Meadi" class="internal-link">the Rulers of the Meadi</a>.</p>
