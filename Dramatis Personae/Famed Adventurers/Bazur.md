<p>The Dwarf Bazur is the greatest servant of Ran, the Dwarven God of Death and lost member of the <a href="Terem-Zal" class="internal-link">Terem-Zal</a>, in the current age. He carries the Great Seal of Ran with him under the earth and above it, guided by the whispers of his predecessors on the eternal pilgrimage. Few know Bazur by name and fewer still call him a friend, but his presence is not always a marker for danger or ill omen as the devout dwarf may bring absolution and respite for those worthy of Rans blessings and wrath and demise for those that incur the Gods ire. </p>
