	<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Hrened.jpg" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;"> </div> <p>Hrened was a hobgoblin of the <a href="The Malagul" class="internal-link">Malagul</a>, the sect that follows the teachings of Elamak, the Silence in the Storm, and the only Malagul remembered for taking up arms in the <a href="Wars of the Gryphon" class="internal-link">Wars of the Gryphon</a>. His sect had refused to join the united force of the tribes. He joined the First War anyway, and not for the tribes.</p>  <p>When the <a href="League of the Gryphon" class="internal-link">League of the Gryphon</a> marched in 841PA, the hobgoblin holdings along the east bank of the Trinmo were overrun. They were farmsteads and steadings that had stood for a century and a half, and the cruelties done to the people in them, who were by no means all warriors, passed counting. Hrened came down from the northern forests against the teaching of his sect, because he still counted the dispossessed as his own people, and he gathered the survivors behind him.</p>  <p>His host was not part of the army of the Great Boss Shattal. It was a gathering of refugees, families and the warriors that were left, who fought the League on their own account. He was at Rinstree in 843PA, where the tribes were broken, and after that the histories lose sight of him.</p>  <p>The Malagul do not speak of him as one of their own, yet they do not speak against him either. Many among them hold that all creatures are leaves drifting to the ground, and that each is carried where its breeze takes it. Among the Tirbi of the old river country he is remembered with honour and some unease, as a man of peace who went to war for the weak, spoken in reverence but also almost as if too far removed from regular hobgoblin culture.</p>
