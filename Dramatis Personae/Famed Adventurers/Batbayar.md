<p>Batbayar of the Udan is an Aznyar born <a href="Bihra" class="internal-link">Bihra</a> and martial artist of considerable renown born in the year 880PA. He travelled the dangerous path through the Steppe at a young age and became a fervent adherent to the jorvi practice of <a href="Bigronism" class="internal-link">Bigronism</a>, taking well to their ideals of introspective development and changing ones environment by changing ones mind first. After over a decade of pilgrimage in the central Niryd lands of the Idols, Batbayar returned to the <a href="Kareli Hierarchy" class="internal-link">Hierarchy of the Kareli</a> in southern Nirydia, now spending his life as a wandering martial artist and philosopher. </p>
<p>Despite being born in Aznyar and still living within the Hierarchy, Batbayar renounces the ideals of Slavery so commonplace within its ports, even known to be in personal contact with rebellious internal elements, such as the group known as "the Chainbreakers" that made it their expressed goal to free those put in chains and sold at the Markets of Aznyr, Shilmak or Relloril.</p>
<p>As dictated by the Ilog-Kar, the legendary Archon of Force from ancient Jorvistan, Batbayar personally rejects the notion to kill another sentient life with his gifts and skills, seeing it as beneath him. To the Followers of Ilog-Kar within the perhaps most well known School of Bigronism, their body is not a weapon but an instrument and a total expression of their spirit, to kill is to sully and degrade not only this instrument but their entire persona. </p>
