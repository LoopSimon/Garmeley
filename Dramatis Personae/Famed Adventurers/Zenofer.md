<p>Zenofer of the Iskrel Blood, is a Ginru of Ihisa and a Paladin within her Cult as well as one of the advisors to the current, 15th, Tazamosh of Yokosch. He is the only living Ginru of Ihisa and often hailed as her greatest servant on the quest to fully awaken her from the eternal slumber she resides in. Zenofer led his people against the Dwarves of the north and successfully defended Yokosch from their invasion. Zenofer is also the unofficially leader of the Cult of Ihisa itself as an institution within northern Vinjira and has served as its High-Priest and Envoy on multiple occasions throughout his life.</p>
<p>Iskrel Zenofer wields the Mucus Blade, the holy relic weapon of Ihisa herself and is an experienced combatant and capable strategist. Following his heroics during the Dwarven Invasion of Yokosch, a rift has emerged between him and the 15th Tazamosh, with some voices heralding or warning of Zenofers coming as the promised saviour of the city and true ruler of the "free Irudi of the West". It is unclear whether Zenofer agrees or supports movements against the ruler in any such way himself, although he has notably not made any efforts to actively discourage his supporters.</p>
