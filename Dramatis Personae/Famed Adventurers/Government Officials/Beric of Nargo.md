<p>Sir Beric Nargo is a Knight of the City of Uhl and one of the Adjudicators of the City, hunting down criminals that threaten to interfere with the official minting of the Gryphon according to the Treaty of Uhl and the laws of the City. He is currently operating in the eastern Torn Sea and has been spotted in the port cities of Nostroya. As an official envoy of the Vrieman Bank and Adjudicator of the City, he enjoys certain privilieges in many foreign cities as almost all rely on the gryphon as a currency, the banks business and the various trading agreements with the city of Uhl itself.</p>
<p>As a sign of his office he wields the Scepter of the City, a heavy Mace and brutal instrument that both shows his authority and threatens his judgement. Beric is a scion of the house of Nargo, a minor urien house of nobility, and proudly displays their traditional armour painted in red with the iconic of Nargo maned helmet. He is reported to have been a great admirer of the hero Filip of Leston, a member of the Golden Ring, having seen him win the great Tourney at Uhl in his youth.</p>
