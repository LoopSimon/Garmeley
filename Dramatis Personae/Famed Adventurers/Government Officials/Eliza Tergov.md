<p>Eliza Tergov is one of the two Guard-Captains of Izhevk, responsible for matters of Daylight, her office is at the Kilosky Guardhouse. She is a Izhevk born warrior that joined the City Guard at the age of 18, a tough reputation and a tough personality preceding her. Her command of various difficult situations in the dangerous city of Izhevk have earned her unilateral respect from Boyars, Burghers, Commoners and even Gram <a href="Eril Ruda" class="internal-link">Ruda</a> himself, she has a distrusting attitude towards the local clergy and the mages of Borni Tower however and choses to rely on independent contractors such as <a href="Sergey Hirl" class="internal-link">Sergey Hirl</a> when in need to arcane expertise.</p>
<p>During the summer of 924PA she was one of the few survivors of the raid on Cargell Hall, a rogue band of adventurers had turned drug traders and invaded the mansion and estate of Boyar Cargell, going undetected for months before her investigation led to the capture and killing of the Band. Her matter of fact attitude and personal accolades have allowed her a great deal of freedom and she is largely able to enforce the law in her own way. The recent troubles with the so called Green Plague have however confronted her with a more dangerous, less tangible foe, than any before.</p>
