<small><i>this article was written by Tombo and concerns a Player Character played by Tombo</i></small>

<p>A hunter from northern <a href="Trykkerog" class="internal-link">Trykkerog</a> looking to make something of his life and overcome the poor fate he had been bestowed. He was born in a small village of the Stormlands of northern <a href="Vignasoya" class="internal-link">Vignasoya</a> in 883 PA to a single mother Guorun Jonsdotter. His misfortune would start at an early age with his mother claiming him to be a cursed child born of a fell omen using a birthmark on his back as evidence and that he should be sacrificed upon coming of age in accordance with cult like traditions of the worship of <a href="Ingele" class="internal-link">Ingele</a> who is the most prevalent deity in the region. With his mother unwilling to put much effort into raising him Sturla would end up being for the most part raised by a local hunter who would regale him of tales of the snow elf's in the far north and other fantastical beings he had supposedly encountered when he was exploring the far north of trykkerog in his younger years. as he approached the date of sacrifice Sturla would confess to his mentor that he to wanted to live a life and witness these mythical beasts for himself one day and asked his mentor to run away with him to be free of his burden. his mentor would reluctantly explain that Ingele's will was not something that could be defied and in the end convinced Sturla to except his fate for the good of everyone including himself. Upon the day of the ritual as the sacrificial knife was being passed toward Sturla with each villager drawing their own blood on the knife to gain the blessing of Ingele before the sacrifice Sturla would realize that he was not ready to die overcome by fear he would manage to break out of his restraints and make a bolt for the woods. Running through the snow and trees Sturla would be able to reach the small hunting lodge he had lived in, retrieving his bow and some arrows, he would however be pursued by a dozen villagers with pitchforks and torches including his mentor who had been sent to track him down.</p>
<p>An inexperienced Sturla was unable to cover his tracks well enough leading to a confrontation between him and his mentor where they would exchange shots firing arrows back and forth at each other in the snow. to Sturla's disbelief none of the arrows from his mentor found their mark after managing to land a lucky shot on his mentors side he managed to shake him of his track. he however found himself surrounded by villagers in the mean time with no where to escape Sturla was forced to hide among the bushes hoping he could wait them out. It was at this point perhaps drawn by the commotion and torchlight in the snow a great hulking beast would appear a mass of scale muscle and scale its visage would strike fear into Sturla for a life time the horrifying shrike. appearing out of nowhere it would in an instant dispose of three villagers in one swift motion leaping from body to body seamlessly, frozen in fear Sturla could do nothing but watch. some of the villagers would attempt to fight back in desperation but it was quickly evident the their pitchforks and rudimentary equipment was far outclassed by this beast, but their desperate attempt made opportunity for Starla's mentor to loose a volley of arrows into its blind spot unlike the attempts from the villagers these arrows found their mark for a moment it seamed as though the beast could be slayed this however was short lived as with the beast sharp senses it could smell the blood from its injured assailant, quickly pinpointing the hunter the beast was able to move like lightning the the wood and snow and sink its large claws into the flesh of it opponent it would be able to almost instantly overwhelm him lifting his body and impaling it into a nearby tree. the beast seeming satisfied with overcoming such a pray would return into the snow where it came from leaving bloodshed and carnage along with an unforgettable image in Sturla's mind.</p>
<p>From here Sturla would make his way aimlessly through the woods with no objective but to distance himself from the events that just conspired. he would find himself at the top of Yaalpeak where he would find a monument to <a href="Yaalwe" class="internal-link">Yaalwe</a> after considering his situation he would make an oath to Yaalwe who once inspired him in the tales he heard as a child. an oath to hunt down the vile shrike and prove that he had a worth beyond that of a sacrifice, that his death wasn't the only way for him to have meaning, that his decision to try and live no matter what wasn't a mistake. with his newfound resolve he would travel the land looking for clues on the whereabouts and any method for hunting the shrike, this would lead him to cross the sea hearing rumours of sightings in <a href="Ur" class="internal-link">Ur</a>. Upon arrival in northern Ur, he would spend the most part of 3 years looking for clues honing his bow while working for the scholar and mage <a href="Jan Van Swarn" class="internal-link">Jan Van Swarn</a> collecting materials and completing odd jobs. it was here that he would hear of an adventuring party named the golden ring who had survived an encounter with the foul shrike unfortunately by the time this rumour reached Sturla the golden ring had already gone missing. a couple days later Sturla would come across and advertisement for adventurers willing to track down the golden ring in exchange for some coin. Sturla would meet with the recruiters of these adventurers and form a party known as the bad omen setting out to find the members of the golden ring in the late fall of 904PA this would be the last anyone outside of that party saw of Sturla.</p>
