<p>Xara Ilig is a dwarven bounty hunter and ranger that has been active for the better part of six decades in a rather dangerous business. She was born in the sarloni town of Yevarin in Dalniz, on the border to kherova south of the Mistspire mountains to parents that would leave her to fend for herself on the surface, as is the fate of many unfortunate dwarven children born outside of the mountains to the most conservative and loveless parents.</p>
<p>Ilig was part of the group that took down the Red Ahool of the Vale, killed the nefarious Druid Hegarid of the Glades and exposed Baron Midil during the Grevenni Crisis of 901 with his original group, the <a href="The Merry Three" class="internal-link">Merry Three</a>, together with the reani investigator and fighter <a href="Adral Fidrakis" class="internal-link">Adral Fidrakis</a>, with whom she was rumoured to have struck up an affair with, and the human knight <a href="Mikael Bivaldi" class="internal-link">Mikael Bivaldi</a>, whom she and Fidrakis mentored and would later become known as "the Sword of the Sea".</p>
<p>Xara is currently still active although she has started to become grumpier and more careful with age, she is rumoured to be working for parts of the Dalniz nobility and was responsible for taking down the drug ring surrounding <a href="Provost Mihun" class="internal-link">Provost zi Mihun</a>, Count of Retsky, in 918PA.</p>
