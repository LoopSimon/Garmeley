<small><i>this article concerns a Player Character played by Tom during Mini Games 1 & 2</i></small>
<p>Jana Rikolore is a travelling priest of <a href="Dorian" class="internal-link">Saint Dorian</a> and an avid collector of Flowers. She is a formidable fighter and rejects the notions of employment, money or possessions for various intellectual reasons, a fact that does not stop her from drinking on her friends purses whenever the need arises. Jana was born in 994PA in the bustling port city of <a href="Trazlova" class="internal-link">Trazlov</a> in northern <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Vihr</a> and has travelled the roads of northern ilrakhan all her life. </p>

<p>Alongside other heroes such as the Lady <a href="Elena Montegru" class="internal-link">Elena</a>, the stoic Knight <a href="Mikael Bivaldi" class="internal-link">Mikael</a> as well as the quick mouthed Bard <a href="Piero Digolo" class="internal-link">Piero</a> who assembled in a different party calling themselves <a href="Piero & Friends" class="internal-link">"Piero & Friends"</a> Jana brought down the murderous Sorcerer Jonek Two-Tongues in the Summer of 922PA and averted a necromantic curse from taking over the Abbey-Town of Fairriver in the Winter of the same year, before the group largely dissolved as Elena and Mikael had to return to their homes with the garmish Civil war threatening to erupt as a consequence of Joneks actions.</p>
<p>Piero and Jana meanwhile continued their travels together, however where exactly their adventure took them in the following years is as of yet unknown.</p>
