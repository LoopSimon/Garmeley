<small><i>this article concerns a Player Character played by David during Mini Games 1 & 2</i></small>
<p>Piero Maria Digolo is charming Troubadour from the capital City of <a href="Battvia" class="internal-link">Battvia</a> in the western lands of <a href="Cevolia" class="internal-link">Cevolia</a>. Fleeing various angered noblemen and women, he is ever in search of his next story and place of adventure, although Piero prefers to rely on his many friends to get his hands dirty with the more "base" tasks of adventuring. His mouth has brought him much coin and favour but as much and more trouble as well so he usually does not stay too long in the same place if he can avoid it.</p>

<p>Alongside other heroes such as the Lady <a href="Elena Montegru" class="internal-link">Elena</a>, the stoic Knight <a href="Mikael Bivaldi" class="internal-link">Mikael</a> as well as the devout Cleric <a href="Jana Rikolore" class="internal-link">Jana</a> who assembled in a different party calling themselves <a href="Piero & Friends" class="internal-link">"Piero & Friends"</a>, Piero brought down the murderous Sorcerer Jonek Two-Tongues in the Summer of 922PA and averted a necromantic curse from taking over the Abbey-Town of Fairriver in the Winter of the same year, before the group largely dissolved as Elena and Mikael had to return to their homes with the garmish Civil war threatening to erupt as a consequence of Joneks actions.</p>
<p>Jana and Piero meanwhile continued their travels together, however where exactly their adventure took them in the following years is as of yet unknown.</p>
