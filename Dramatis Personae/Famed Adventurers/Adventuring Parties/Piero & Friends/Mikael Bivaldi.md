<small><i>this article concerns a Player Character played by Giulia during Mini Games 1 & 2</i></small>
<p>The Sword of the Sea, is a living legend in the Rean region, having bested foul beasts, ended vile sorcerers, revealed rogue-ish plots and put down too many robber outfits to count. Bivaldi was raised in the traditions of the <a href="Knights of the Spring" class="internal-link">Knights of the Spring</a>, by an unknown mentor and is often considered to be the last of their order. His first known exploit was the duel at Tirbrige, where an only 17 year old Bivaldi is said to have valiantly put down a devious <a href="Troll" class="internal-link">Troll</a> and rid the lands of his influence and terror; this would be noly the first notch in a belt of heroics and legends surrounding the Knight.</p>
<p>Bivaldi was part of the group that took down the Red Ahool of the Vale, killed the nefarious Druid Hegarid of the Glades and exposed Baron Midil during the Grevenni Crisis of 901 with his original group, the <a href="The Merry Three" class="internal-link">Merry Three</a>, together with the reani investigator and fighter <a href="Adral Fidrakis" class="internal-link">Adral Fidrakis</a> and dwarven bounty hunter <a href="Xara Ilig" class="internal-link">Xara Ilig</a> who both took on somewhat of parental roles for the younger Bivaldi back in the day.</p>

<p>He ended the live of Jonek Two-Tongues after he had killed the Duchess of Garmeley alongside other heroes such as the Lady <a href="Elena Montegru" class="internal-link">Elena</a>, the devout Cleric <a href="Jana Rikolore" class="internal-link">Jana</a> as well as the quick mouthed Bard <a href="Piero Digolo" class="internal-link">Piero</a> who assembled in a different party calling themselves <a href="Piero & Friends" class="internal-link">"Piero & Friends"</a>. Notably Bivaldis later retirement was largely caused by him blaming himself for her death, friends often reported him regretting the choice to pursue Jonek instead of saving the Duchess, considering the eventual bloody developments of the <a href="Garmish Succession Crisis" class="internal-link">Garmish Civil War</a>.</p>
<p>Following the groups heroic saving of the monastery town of Fairriver in what would later be known as the "Fairriver Incident" in December of 922PA, Mikael bid his farewell and the group split apart, he retired with considerably wealth to <a href="Reanport" class="internal-link">Reanport</a> where his family resides.</p>

<p>Mikael still travels the Lands of the Stormy Sea to visit old friends but avoids the lands touched by the war, he spends more and more time in Reanport where his two children reside and have started a printing business. He had been contacted by Sofia Firelle, the daughter of esteemed wine trader <a href="Zavro Firelle" class="internal-link">Zavro Firelle</a>, in regards to troubles at their Estate close to Kontini, in turn he made sure to recommend to the young Firelle to write to a younger adventurer by the name of Marianna, a wermian warrior that at that time travelled the lands of Rean with a goblin compatriot.</p>
