<p>Onar the Voice was the mythical God of the Pry <a href="Krestasi" class="internal-link">Krestasi</a> that ascended to divinity over 3000 Years prior to the current age. He was the founder of the <a href="Onari Empire" class="internal-link">Onari Empire</a>, the largest Krestasi Realm in known history, and would rule his people through his divine bloodline until his murder in 413PA, upon which his Empire collapsed. The <a href="Onar Despotate" class="internal-link">Onar Despotate</a>, a successor state without direct connection to their now dead God, still worships him in similar rite.</p>
