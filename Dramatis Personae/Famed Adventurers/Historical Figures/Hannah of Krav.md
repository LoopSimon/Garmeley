<p>Lady Hannah of Krav, better known as "Thawblade" was the ruler of Krav by birth and a famed pilvian warrior and duellist during the 7th century. She is known for her deeds during the <a href="Yellow War" class="internal-link">Yellow War</a>, the conflict between the <a href="Kherova" class="internal-link">Khatrym of Kherova</a> and <a href="Grevenna" class="internal-link">Grevenna</a> in the 660s, as well has her string if remarkable victories in the duelling championships of Pilvia in the following decades. Her epitaph stems from her glowing, ice cold Sword that is said to have either possessed remarkable magical properties itself or been enhanced by Hannahs own supernatural capabilities. </p>
<p>Born as the only child to the Lord of Krav, a castle in south-western Grevenna, she was raised within the martial culture of the pilivian aristocracy and as a life long admirer of <a href="Yelena" class="internal-link">Saint Yelena</a>, a fellow and much celebrated warrior that had been born in Grevenna before being uplifted to sainthood by the Gethric Clergy, mere decades earlier. Hannah died in the Winter of 698PA in Kisnau for unknown reasons at the age of 63, leaving stewardship of Krav Castle to her son and fellow member of the Pilvian order of Knights, Mirako of Krav. Her line still rules in Krav to day.</p>
<h3>The Yellow War</h3> <p>Hannah was twenty five years old when the <a href="Yellow War" class="internal-link">Yellow War</a> broke out in 660PA and rode east with the Grevenni host under Lord Ahrim of Sera. At the Battle of Tark Fields,  the war's opening engagement and its clearest pilvian victory, she distinguished herself in a manner that established her name across both sides of the Yevan River. Her personal combat record through the moor campaign was formidable: several notable Kheri knights fell to her blade during the early conquests, among them Lord Darvit of Yevstatt, whose death at her hands became one of the conflicts most retold encounters. The Thawblade name she carried for the rest of her life was earned here, in the moor's early fighting, before the war had hardened into the long siege and the river stalemate that followed. She was present at Yazbridge when the Kherovan Spellbreakers destroyed Ahrim's Magi and ended Grevenni offensive capacity, and she survived the war entire, one of the few senior figures on either side to do so without wound or capture. She returned to Krav in 668PA with a reputation that the duelling championships of the following decades would only deepen.</p>
