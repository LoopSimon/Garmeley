<p>Timut Zolor is a hessefi Occultist and expert on all things arcane currently in the services of <a href="Kessim Miximur" class="internal-link">Kessim Miximur</a>, the Dye Magnate of <a href="Ikara" class="internal-link">Ikara</a>. Unbeknownst to many, he is also part of the mysterious group calling themselves the <a href="Children of Dust" class="internal-link">"Children of Dust"</a> and a researcher and historian into the lost times, and has himself undertaken a venture into the eastern Wasteland of <a href="Rakh" class="internal-link">Rakh</a>.</p>

<p>The exact nature of Miximurs and Zolors arrangement is unknown, however it seems to go deeper than simply financial. Miximurs influence has also allowed his Occultist to remain largely undisturbed by authorities even though the latter is not part of beheras <a href="Magisters Ring" class="internal-link">Magisters Ring</a> and would normally remain under great scrutiny as far as his arcane proclivities are concerned.</p>
