<p>Moraq, known in the villages of <a href="Il Haena" class="internal-link">Il-Haena</a> as "the Breaker", is an Orcish insurgent turned brigand, a zealot of the violent strain of the <a href="Path of the Second Moon" class="internal-link">Path of the Second Moon</a> and one of the most wanted criminals on the island. He is a lone swordsman of terrible strength who walks into the rural communities of the Il-Haena mainland unannounced, challenges whom he pleases, takes what he wants, and is said to fall into endless rages. He has no band, no banner and no patron, and the Sultanate has never held him.</p>
<p>Moraq is a massive, heavily scarred Orc with a heavy brow, small tusks, rings through his ears and a mane of long black hair swept back from his head. He carries a great curved blade over his shoulder and wears a harness of leather, iron rings and rivets, never the bone of the Qari. To the Qari the blade and the iron are the first sign of what he is, since the bone-craft is the craft of a people who would rather endure a blow than strike one, and Moraq goes armed and armoured in the manner of those who took Qa. His shoulders and arms are covered in raised scars, some taken in his fights and, it is said, some cut by his own hand, one for every follower of the First Path he has made raise a hand against him.</p>
<h2>The Breaker's Creed</h2>
<p>The Zolraii of <a href="Abaleq" class="internal-link">Abaleq</a> embrace the tempers and appetites of Zolra, the Night, and are no bloodthirsty killers. Moraq's strain embraces only one of them. It holds, as the Second Path does, that Zolra won her struggle with Qastor when the elves took Qa, and it draws the conclusion that the endurance of the First Path and the patience of the Quiet are nothing but surrender to a god who has lost. To make a Qari raise his hand in anger is, to Moraq, to turn him from Qastor toward Zolra, and every vow to endure, and above all the absolute taboo on killing of the <a href="Path of the Third Moon" class="internal-link">Third Path</a>, is a vow he has made it his work to break.</p>
<p>His method is always the same. He walks into a village and names a champion, and if the elders refuse, as the First Path counsels, he begins on the village, its stores, its beasts and in time its people, until someone raises a hand against him. Such a person he holds broken and won for the Night, and will sometimes spare for it. He does not always, and he spares as often as he pleases. Those who go on enduring he does not spare at all. The elders of the Quiet and the investigators of the Sultanate agree on one thing, that the creed is a pretext. Moraq enjoys causing harm and he enjoys a fight, and the faith is the reason he gives and the only part of it he has kept. The Zolraii of Abaleq have never spoken for him, and the devout of the Federation point to him as proof of everything they say of the Second Path.</p>
<h2>Brigand</h2>
<p>The first villages Moraq challenged were Orcish, but for years now he has walked into elven, human and Bihra villages as well, where there is no vow to break and no creed to serve, and simply takes what he wants: food, drink, coin, beasts and whatever else catches his eye, and he hurts whoever stands in his way. He travels alone, on foot and never for long in one place, and no one knows where he sleeps.</p>
<p>Few who have fought him have lived to speak of it. Tales of his strength are many and have likely grown in the telling: that he has broken the axle of a loaded cart across his knee, that he has walked out of a skirmish with a Dawn Army patrol with arrows in his back. The villagers who have met him agree on the rages. Moraq falls into fits that last through a night and into the next day, in which he feels no wound and knows no one, and in the villages they say that the Night has him.</p>
<h2>Background</h2>
<p>Little of his youth is certain. He was born in 891PA, in the last years of the <a href="Great Elbae Golomid War" class="internal-link">Great Elbae Golomid War</a>, and is most often said to have been raised among the Quiet of the interior as a child of the First Path. In his youth he joined one of the insurgent cells who dream of the return of Qa and was cast out for violence that even they could not stomach. How he came to the Second Path is told in different ways, most commonly through a Zolraii preacher he met on the coast. He first appears in the records of the Sultanate in the 910s and has been a brigand since the early 920s.</p>
<h2>The Hunt for Moraq</h2>
<p>The Elbae administration of Il-Haena lists Moraq among the most wanted men on the island, and the Dawn Army has sent patrols into the interior to find him. Bounty hunters have followed, and he has outlasted them. The Quiet find themselves in an uneasy position. Their faith forbids them to meet him with force, and the villages of the interior, which would rather not have the Sultanate's soldiers in their streets, have more and more come to ask for them. The insurgent cells of the island disown him as well, a gift to every Elbae account of what the dream of Qa breeds.</p>
