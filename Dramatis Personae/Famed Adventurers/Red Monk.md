<p>The Red Monk, as he is known in Ikara, also going by Cinderstone Mikor or just Mikor, is an elder member of the <a href="Cinderstone Temple" class="internal-link">Cinderstone Temple</a> of Tazil that has spend the last few months holed up in the Ebony Grin, an establishment in the Riverside of <a href="Ikara" class="internal-link">Ikara</a> after reportedly escaping captivity of the mysterious <a href="Manymirror" class="internal-link">Manymirror</a>. Not much is known of the monks past or heritage, but he seems to be from lands far away, as far as the local population is concerned, in truth Mikor was born in the lands of Gorat beyond the Velvet Road, within the borders of <a href="Amlija" class="internal-link">Amlija</a>.</p>
<p>The Monk is currently avoiding capture after fighting another ikaran at the Ebony Grin, a less reputable Inn in Riverside, reports are that his hands reached beneath the very skin of his opponent and his skeleton burst into a thousand pieces. Wether true or not, he fled north into the Izker Moor soon after and hasnt been seen since.</p>
