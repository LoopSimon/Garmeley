<p>Rylla, called Clouds Fury, was a stormcaller and warleader of the Flaia tribe of the <a href="Odise" class="internal-link">Odise Federation</a> and the leader of the first great rising of the Odise against <a href="Almersport" class="internal-link">Almersport</a>. Her capture and death in the Old World turned a series of raids into a war of the whole federation, a war that has not ended.</p> <p>She was born in the east of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a> two years after Almersport was founded on the coast, and she grew up in the shadow of the colony. Rylla was gifted with the power to call rain and storm, an ability that was innate in her but that her people largely saw as a gift of the gods, and it earned her the name Clouds Fury. In 917PA, at the age of twenty, she gathered many warriors around her, not only from her own tribe, which had no clear leader beyond its elders, but from other tribes of the Odise, and began to raid the camps of Almersport. In the early years of the war the colony fared badly.</p> 
<p>Her end came in 925PA. Her enemies set a massive fire to the grove in which her camp lay, and the ash and smoke were poisonous. With the help of her magic, summoning rain and clean water, Rylla brought her entire company out of the grove as one body, but in doing so drew the attention of the force that was waiting for them. The Almersport troops, led by Lord Eric Zallon, a Ravlander noble known for his cruelty and efficiency, fell upon the exhausted warriors, slaughtering many and capturing the rest, Rylla among them.</p> <p>She was taken back to the Old World, where she was displayed like an animal before being burned alive in Karsord, the capital of <a href="Erlmondy" class="internal-link">Erlmondy</a>, at the age of twenty-eight. The affair caused widespread uproar among colonists and Narzoi alike, and among the Odise it united the federation, which took up arms as a whole. The Flaia have since joined the greater Odise tribe.</p> <p>The shaman <a href="Tezmer" class="internal-link">Tezmer</a> had opposed her raids but honoured her after her death. Criticism of him grew in the wake of her execution and the outbreak of the war.</p>
