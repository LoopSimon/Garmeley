	<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/George.jpg" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;">  </div> <p>George the Brave was a mage knight of <a href="Gelmsburg" class="internal-link">Gelmsburg</a>, a warrior who fought with both sword and spell, and the man who crowned the first king of Gelmsburg. In the Gelmish tradition he is remembered as the knight who made the kingdom.</p>  <p>George was already a famous man in 703PA, known as the slayer of the ogre Babkar and as the man who broke the Lindgawker Brotherhood, a band of former paladins and knights who had turned robber barons. His name was known in the last days of the Kingdom of <a href="Urland" class="internal-link">Urland</a>, and he is remembered as George the Brave for deeds of that kind.</p>  <p>After the fall of Urland and the Splintering that followed the <a href="Hobgoblin Invasion" class="internal-link">Hobgoblin Invasion</a>, George crowned Jorwyle of Gelm King in Gelmsburg in 703PA and supported him afterwards, in the hope of a quick reunion of all Urland under the new crown. The reunion never came. The lords of the Eld Valley refused the Gelmish call and raised a crown of their own, and no other lord of Urland acknowledged the new king's claim to the old crown.</p>  <p>George was a man of around fifty years and still in his strength when, in 713PA, the Gelmish host attacked <a href="Eldmar" class="internal-link">Eldmar</a> in the <a href="90 Days War" class="internal-link">90 Days War</a>, and George rode with it at the head of the mage knights. He fell at the Battle of Silverfields, and the war ended inconclusively soon after. With him died the hope of a quick reunion of all Urland. The Gelmish still tell of how the knight who set the first crown on a king's head did not live to see the kingdom whole.</p>
