<p>Anna of Harsy, better known by her moniker "Anna the Moth", is an infamous warrior turned brigand who gained notoriety during the <a href="Garmish Succession Crisis" class="internal-link">Garmish Succession Crisis</a>, during the early years of 927PA she founded the Crowntaker Band and began taking more or less reputable jobs in the vale of Rean and northern Hainach, before leaving for the cities of the Asef desert in the northern Sultanate.</p>
<p>Anna wears a strange looking armour resembling the aesthetics of a Moth and is said to be able to evoke mysterious powers throughout her armament as well, fearsome as she is almost no one has ever seen the Warrior without her helmet.</p>
<p>During the <a href="The 927 Falcons Flight" class="internal-link">927 Falcons Flight</a> Tournament in <a href="Ikara" class="internal-link">Ikara</a>, she and her group managed to qualify for the Cities Championship.</p>
