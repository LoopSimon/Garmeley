<p>Mara Bolo, sometime going by "Marbolo", is an Orcish Magus from the Islands of the Qahori Federation, north of Il-Haena, most famous for her adventures on the sides of the <a href="Wispmer" class="internal-link">Wispmer</a> <a href="Lario" class="internal-link">Lario of Tah</a> with whom she would later join in the current and most infamous incarnation of the Black Sails group of Atheletes, sponsored by <a href="Ilman Gorak" class="internal-link">Ilman Gorak</a>, the ruling Sanjak of Ikara.</p> 

<p>Bolo is an adept student of the arcane, a less common pursuit for the peoples of the Qahori and an avid student of history. She largely indulges Lario, her long time off and on partner in love and war, when it comes to the more competitive nature of the Wispmer but is considerably less invested in the tournament winnings or result, seeing it more as an opportunity to finance her other research. Mara is a member of the <a href="Magisters Ring " class="internal-link">Magisters Ring </a>of Behera and the only one of the institution to participate in the Falcons Flight.</p>
