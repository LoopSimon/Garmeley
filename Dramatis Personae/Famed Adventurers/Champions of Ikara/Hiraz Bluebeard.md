<p>Hiraz Narel, better known as "Bluebeard" is a famous mercenary and pit fighter working in northern Behera. Most recently he has become a permanent stay in the Skyrider Company of the city of <a href="Ikara" class="internal-link">Ikara</a> under the sponsorship of <a href="Meana Telamon" class="internal-link">the lady Telamon</a>. He is a jovial and energetic fighter that is not above the odd trick and fanciful movement and refuses to wear armour. His flashy style and boastful demeanour, coupled with his striking appearance, have made Hiraz a crowd favourite in the nearly three years he has been a part of the tournaments. He is currently participating in the 927PA Falcons Flight Tournament for the Skyriders under the leadership of <a href="Cường Đức Lý" class="internal-link">Cường Đức Lý</a>.</p>
