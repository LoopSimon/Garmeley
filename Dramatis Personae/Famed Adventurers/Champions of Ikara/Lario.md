<p>Lario of Tah, better known as "The Final Chapter", is a <a href="Wispmer" class="internal-link">Wispmer</a> from the city of <a href="Tah" class="internal-link">Tah</a> that is the current reigning Duelling Champion in the Falcons Pit, the infamous Hippodrome of <a href="Ikara" class="internal-link">Ikara</a>. He is sponsored by <a href="Ilman Gorak" class="internal-link">Ilman Gorak</a>, the ruling Sanjak of Ikara, and is as such part of his Black Sails Group of Athletes. Aside from the individual Duelling Tournaments and events, Lario also leads the Black Sails in the City Championships where groups of fighters square off against each other.</p> 
<p>His love for the fight has not restricted itself to just the Pit and he infamously came under trouble for slaying the rich Merchant Herzafer in the Year 925PA, on grounds of an honour Duel, a practice not as common in Behera as it may be in northern Ilrakhan. His killing of Herzafer did not significantly impact his standing in the city as role as a champion as he is ostensibly under the Protection of the Sanjak himself.</p>
