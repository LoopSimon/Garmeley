<p>Cường Đức Lý is the leader of the Skyriders, Champions of the Falcon Flight in <a href="Ikara" class="internal-link">Ikara</a> and a winner of the Archery contest in said spectacle.</p> 
<p>Very little is known about his past; he came to the city as wanderer from <a href="Kaito" class="internal-link">Kaito</a> but soon got the attention of the crowd when he won the archery contest with ease and was granted their their support with his foreign demeanour. With his rise in fame and his natural draw to debate people, he quickly made friends and not long after founded the Skyriders. They were soon contracted by the <a href="Meana Telamon" class="internal-link">Telamon</a> family to fight in their honor. It is not uncommon for him to put you in the dirt just to raise you up with a smile and debate you on various topics, his favourites being justice and the deity he revers, <a href="Sochor" class="internal-link">Sochor</a>.</p>
<p> Since his name does not spark familiarity in these parts of the world, Cường soon adapted the pet name that was given to him by the crowd and now all just call him Duke. The Duke favours a bow in conjunction with his martial arts studies but sees Sochor holy commandment of justice even on the battlefield and thus refuses to shoot only a single target. For him these Tournaments are akin to ritualistic fightings.</p>
