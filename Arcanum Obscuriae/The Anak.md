<p>The Anak is a system of scientific and philosophical understanding of the world in its supernatural forms made popular by the Pagri Arcanist <a href="Anak" class="internal-link">Anak Montener</a> in the 7th century PA. It is sometimes viewed as the nirydian answer to contemporary ilrakhani <a href="Basic Magical Theory" class="internal-link">Theoretica Arcana</a>, although this is a rather simplified view of things. While it holds many elements combining the natural and supernatural sciences with various elements of zuhuri theology, it is nonetheless the essential work on the Magicks of the Cosmos accepted in greater Nirydian Scholarship. The Core work of the Anak is the Anak Codex, compiled by Montener himself, but it has since transcended his original writings and build upon them to become an international project of discourse, largely removed from its originally stern religious roots and studied and accepted in wides part of the easter Continent, from Ynkal to Direnna and from the Twintail Imperium to the Jungles of Drizan.</p>
<p>The Anak categorizes the universe into a giant tapestry of "Essence" within the Lands of Time and those Beyond; imagining ourselves as an evermoving apparatus on a cosmic scale that leaves behind shadows or afterimages of itself in its wake, without the sun of time (the sun imagery here is a clear reference to original zuhuri roots and many of Anaks metaphors should not be taken literal).</p>
<p>Essence is the matter that makes up all things, given weight and reference by the so called "absolutes", points of either no or infinite essence within the cosmos that Anaks initial religious perception declares as the "Rays of Pen-Zuhur". Only our own perceived world, sometimes called a dimension or simply the reality, possesses the infinitely small absolute of zero, or null, the point towards everything moves, giving it the unique characteristic of time. Time as such is the continuous progression of existence from the past through the present to the future, serving as the metric for sequencing events and measuring duration.  Philosophically within the Anak, it is defined as the the order in which essence changes and events can occur, allowing for causality where one event precedes another.</p>
<p>The manipulation of essence, so the varying depth to wich one can impact the building blocks of the cosmos itself are what is known as magicks, but viewed by Anak in a surprisingly progressive attitude for his time, as nothing more than tools to a mean, "Rudders on the grand ocean of the world, that reach deeper than most but still barely scrap the Essence of Truth", and can only occur in our dimension, where time has allowed essence to be naturally affected. </p>
 <p>The other Dimensions or worlds, are often viewed in two categories: the Afterimages of our reality drifting through cosmos, or subworlds and torn realms on a spectrum of twilight, pulled by the Gravity of our Absolute null point of time to our own world, known as the Lands Beyond. When a wizard or magi manipulates Essence to create or destroy, manipulate or revert, he changes Essence by inviting either the afterimages of the past (often referred to as "instinctual or spiritual magic") or modelling untouched and unchanged essence to his whim by connecting to the lands beyond, whose inhabitants yearn for nothing more than the finite pull to reality.</p>
