The Ideal Plane lies on the so called "Formative Axis" opposite the [[The Concrete Plane]].
The existence of the Concrete and Ideal Plane was first theorized by the Gnomish Mage <a href="Larkomas" class="internal-link">Larkomas</a> as a result of his expeditions within the <a href="Wasteland of Rakh" class="internal-link">Wasteland of Rakh</a> and the Ruins of Tarsus and is part of his "Syntropical Model" of the Eruption of Al-Akka in the year 0 AA.
The ideal Plane exists as the absolute and true nature of an objects form on the material and spiritual realm.
