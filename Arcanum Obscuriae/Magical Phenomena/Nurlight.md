[[Rakh]]
%%
<p>The Nurlight, also called "The Nur", "the Red Sun", "the Rakhlight" or "Ziuvas Wrath", is a mysterious visual phenomenon above the crater of Al-Akka, the volcano sitting in the centre of the Wasteland of Rakh. It is observed as a sphere of crimson red light radiating a strange glow and can be seen for many miles, unaffected by day or night is has been observed since the end of the so called "Burning Decades" for over 900 years and is associated with the Doom of the Rakhi civilization.</p>
<p>The many ventures and expeditions into the Wastes following the so called "Great Expedition" in the 6th century PA, have usually stirred far away from the Nurlight and kept to the outer ruins, but over the centuries more and more reports of expeditions venturing closer to the light have surfaced, reporting catastrophic effects on members exhibiting among others strange mutations, a rapid weakness of the bones, hallucinations, blindness, skin burns, hair loss, cataracts, nausea, vomiting, failure of the body, skin change and deathly sickness. These gruesome effects seam to increase with continued exposure to the Nur and the proximity to it one finds themselves within, the Scholar and Explorer Derdryk Trahava self documented the progression and effects of the Nur on his party and himself in his treaty "Boons of the Red Sun", published posthumously, in wich he also decribes a lifelong series of symptoms within other explorers that had not ventured as far and even risks of hereditary mutations in the children of the affected. </p>
<p>The Nurlight is worshipped as a living God by the fringe "Cult of the Red Sun", with reports of "final pilgrimages" of members venturing as far to their deity as their body permits.</p>
