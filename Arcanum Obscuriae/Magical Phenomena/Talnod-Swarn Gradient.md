<p>The Talnod-Swarn Gradient was created by the Reani and Weadi Scholars <a href="Gharel Talnod" class="internal-link">Gharel Talnod</a> and <a href="Jan van Swarn" class="internal-link">Jan van Sworn</a> during the 10th century and published within their cooperative Treatise "Turnotic Variance: A consideration on measurement and classification of outer planer energies" in the year 920PA.</p>
<p>The Gradient describes the relation of varying outer-planar energies within a region, and attempts to classify different types of energies and planar residue. Unlike the various abstract and occult classifications this Gradient accounts for measurably differences in residue quality after the opening of a Fluxbridge by noting the reaction of an exposed <a href="Brystone" class="internal-link">Brystone</a>. Talnod and Swarn created an Instrument to measure the reaction and classify the results on their scale, calling it the Turnometer. </p>
