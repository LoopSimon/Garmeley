<p>A Parneu Field describes an array of interlinked prisms, usually in the form of physical artifacts that creates a conjoined conditional Fluxbridge, constantly activating and re activating prisms within, thereby creating an essentially permanent Effect with a fraction of the planar energy required to usually uphold any Invocation for an equivalent amount of time. The Parneu Field is named after <a href="Gisava di Parneu" class="internal-link">Gisava di Parneu</a>, a 7th century Arcanist that described and related the effect to contemporary ley theory. The underlying mechanism had been used for over a millenia before Gisavas work and it is likely the "Sar Ilmentis" of the Elves describes the same phenomenon. Nevertheless modern day casters almost all refer to the concept as Parneu Field. It has been adapted to surprising efficiency in the ship building industry when constructing specialized Parneu-Keels for the largest modern Galleons, tasked with traversing the vast and wild oceans of the World</p>
