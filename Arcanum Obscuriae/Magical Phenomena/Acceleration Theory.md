<p>Acceleration Theory broadly describes the idea that as time progresses, the veil between the planes diminishes. This is supposedly noticeably and proven by the increasing amount of individuals capable to open Fluxbridges/channel magic and the growing amount of more and more powerful magical feats performed across recorded magical history. Although this could simply be due to the lack of chronology and global, or even international awareness and the lack of information of historic mages as many critics point out, Acceleration Theory has not been entirely dismissed as unscientific due to the capabilities of modern scholars to actually measure planar variables via tools such as the <a href="Talnod-Swarn Gradient" class="internal-link">Talnod-Swarn Gradient</a>.</p>
<p>According to Accelerationists, a so called "Veil" between the planes exists, only held together by the fabric of matter itself, that is corroded with ever Fluxbridge opened on our plane. Allowing an easier access to the energies from beyond, an increase in trueborn casters and a resulting exponential further corrosion of the Veil, until at one point it will cease to exist entirely.</p>
<p>Hardline Acceleration Theory often drifts into apocalyptic doomsaying, prophesizing a supposed "Great Convergence" and end to all life or all magic, being often conflated with <a href="Falt" class="internal-link">Falt Truthers</a> or believers in a coming so called "<a href="The Incursion" class="internal-link">Third Incursion</a>". </p>
