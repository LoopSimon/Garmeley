<p>Sometimes simply called the "Settled Ledger" or the "Ninth Law", the Law of the Settled Ledger goes as follows: Every working balances out. Nothing is made, only moved, and a residue is left behind. It is one of the Laws of the <a href="Artificery" class="internal-link">Artificery</a> and developed by Artificers of <a href="Ott" class="internal-link">Ott</a> within the <a href="Muxul" class="internal-link">Muxul</a>, the principle of the Settled Ledger is an actually rather new one and its first followers have openly pledged themselves to the truth of the ninth law only in the 10th century PA.</p>
<p>The Followers of the Settled Ledgers within the Muxul believe that the planes are reservoirs being drained, and predict magic’s “cold end” once this process is completed. Within Ott they craft great engines that harvest energy from falling weights and chemical change to charge artefacts. A new Ottian grade for residue, read with the Turnometer, based on this is known as the Plark.</p>
<p>This opposes those scholars that believe in the ancient <a href="Cyraclion" class="internal-link">cyracli</a> school of thought: Magic in itself creates no energy; it spends what settling releases.</p>
