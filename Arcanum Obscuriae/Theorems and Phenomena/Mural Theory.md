<p>Mural Theory is a very modern development within western arcane academia that relies on extensive subject to subject experimentation and documentation of organisms, it concerns itself with the nature of the mysterious <a href="The Veilcurse" class="internal-link">Veilcurse</a>  and <a href="The Veil" class="internal-link">the Veil</a> itself, as well as the biological or mental differences within a sentient organisms that classify them as a Trueborn Mage. The current Rector of <a href=" Korsfall College" class="internal-link"> Korsfall College</a>  itself, Thomar von Whisperhill, is a stern adherent to Mural Theory and has published various treaties and documentations of the Colleges Experiments.</p>
<p>Derived from studying Veilcurse patients, they conclude the veil isn’t between planes but a wall inside each persons own mind.</p>
<p>The most die hard believers in this theory are called the "Wallwardens" usually in charge of treating patients at the Colleges own Sanatorium, the House of Chimes. They treat the cursed with itual routine and silence. An, officially,  banned practice called “breaching” thins a patient’s wall on purpose to make a trueborn. The Ichori Choir is rumoured to supply the Wallwardens with a regular and steady supply of to be examined and studied patients.</p>
