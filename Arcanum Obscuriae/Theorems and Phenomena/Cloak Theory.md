<p>Exile Heresy, as it is usually described within the greater world of the safir, or" Cloak Theory", by its own practitioners, describes the study of the <a href="Tageyo" class="internal-link">Tageyo Field of southern Behera</a> and posits its true nature as a deliberate barrier laid upon the lands the <a href="Yari" class="internal-link">Yari</a> rule.</p>
<p>Due to the effects of the Tageyo Field, it is nigh impossible to summon extra-planar objects or organisms, invoke planar magicks or rituals or even divine or contact its inhabitants through "regular" means of magic. The Cloak Theory considers why a race as mysterious and controlling as the Yari may erect such a Field, and comes to the conclusion that they themselves are refugees, exiled or criminals of sorts that hide under their Field like common bandits. </p>
 <p>What speaks to this idea is the naturally averse and individualistic nature of the known Yari, only few of them group together, often stand in conflict even. Yet only  <a href="Oleithec" class="internal-link">Oleithec, the Hallowed One</a> ever questioned or attempted to destroy the mysterious Tageyo . According to a subsect of Cloak Theory, the school of Invasion, the Yari are not even creatures of our own world, but more like parasites from afar, dominating the true natives of these lands while living in hiding and fear under their cloak from what they ran.</p>
