<p>The unborn Hypothesis, developed and build upon the works of the meadi Demonologist <a href="Shymon Epios" class="internal-link">Shymon Epios</a>, claims that Daemons aren’t called from elsewhere but rather that they’re made in the moment of summoning.</p>
<p>The Unborn Hypothesis posits the idea of "Calm Summoning", a discipline of calling without fear and stillness of the mind, creating tamer entities. This Hypothesis further describes the practice of "unbirth" that the life and sentience of a Daemon begins and ends within the mind of its summoner.</p>
<p>The entire concept of a safe and secure summoning ritual goes against the warnings of the Wermian Accords, those suspected or known to be active practitioners of the Calm Summon are persecuted across national border. The idea has found, as many formerly illegal and banned Theorems of the Arcane have, many followers within the <a href="Luminists" class="internal-link">Luminist Movement</a>, but is divisive even within that. The theory and considerations should not be barred from study and pondering according to many Luminists, but even for them the act of Summoning goes often a step too far.</p>
