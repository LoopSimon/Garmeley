<p>The Thinning describes the smaller and smaller amount of <a href="Djinn" class="internal-link">Nurdjinn-Entities</a> observable within the <a href="Wasteland of Rakh" class="internal-link">wastelands of Rakh</a>. The Djinnbinder cultures that arose around the wastes, particular in Kerlaen and the Lameidus, have begun a race to capture and utilise the fewer and fewer Nurdjinn of the Wastes, upon whose power they depend for station and survival. </p>
<p>The <a href="Children of Dust" class="internal-link">Children of Dust</a> , some among them Djinbinders themselves are the only academic group seriously studying this phenomenon but have noticed an alarming rate of depreciation amongst Nurdjinn Entities that is bound to reach a true end point in the not too distant future. Elwan of the Rains, a garmish occultist currently said to have been conducting various experiments related to the wastes from the Bandit City of Grivil, is one of the only scholars seriously investigating the phenomenon and has noticed a worrying trend connected to the Census of Vihrs bi-yearly numbers, that have exponentially increased as the Thinning progresses.</p>
<p>Some among the Children of Dust claim the <a href="Nurlight" class="internal-link">Nurlight</a> itself has grown dimmer in step with the Thinning.</p>
