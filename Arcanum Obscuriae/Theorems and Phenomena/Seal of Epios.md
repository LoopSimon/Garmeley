<p>The Seal of Epios is a relatively obscure and unknown <a href="Sigil" class="internal-link">Sigil</a>, that aims to not only ward of but imprison sentient planar energy, rogue spell like elements or in the classical but most occult case: an extraplanar entity, within it. It was developed by and named for the meadi Demonologist <a href="Shymon Epios" class="internal-link">Shymon Epios</a> and described within his famous initial treatise "The Chains of Grahul, to bind and to borrow", and later on referred to and defined in detail in his magnum opus, the forbidden "Grimoire Paralis".</p>
