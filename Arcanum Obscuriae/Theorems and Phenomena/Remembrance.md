<p>Remembrance is a religious concept, derived from the old <a href="Gethricism" class="internal-link">Gethricism</a> Rite of Remembrance, that dates back to some of the earliest records of <a href="Razmyr" class="internal-link">Razmyrs</a> White Book and musings of his that while attributed to him are not part of the greater gethric Canon. As an institution and magical Thesis it is widely accepted within <a href="Razmyrism" class="internal-link">Razmyrism</a> but less popular in other clerical or academic communities.</p>
<p>The Remembrancers keep the names of the dead and read them aloud without end, believing this shelters souls and in turn burn a name-roll as the order’s harshest sentence. Necromancers or aspiring ones at least, have repeatedly attempted to steal the name rolls of Remembrancers that guard them like holy treasures.</p>
<p>Some Remembrancers claim the oldest halls of names have begun to open onto the spiritual realm as the urien tombs once did, a claim their order has been careful not to repeat.</p>
