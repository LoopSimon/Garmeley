<p>The Invocation of the Open Heart, known to the <a href="Auguri Dominion" class="internal-link">Auguri</a> as the Heresy of the Open Heart, is a practice of summoning dating back to the days of the followers of <a href="Augurism" class="internal-link">Augur Ralfantis</a> within the Dominion. As best we understand it today, in contrast to the classical <a href="Auguric Rite & Autonomous Spells" class="internal-link">Auguric Invocation</a>  and their doctrine demanding clean restraint, these casters instead worked through raw longing and dread, achieving or attempting to, a greater level of energies and powers summoned. </p>
<p>The Followers of the Open Heart were persecuted during the days of the Dominion and seem to have been mainly active in the lands of the Urdfall and the region of Othirdon, where most of what we know to day is transcribed upon the Great Runestone Circle known as the Nangalith. From what we know, the school of thought was banned after a summoning soaked in dread awoke something terrible. Its survivors cultivate grief as a craft.</p>
<p>In the modern day it is actively expressed by the circles of <a href="Othirdon" class="internal-link">Othirdon</a>, specifically within the locni tradition that bases much of its arcane scholarship on the records inscribed on the Nangalith, <a href="Cernela nu Bas" class="internal-link">Cernela of the Ink</a>, the Court Magus of Afgillion, is an avid proponent of the Open Heart.</p>
