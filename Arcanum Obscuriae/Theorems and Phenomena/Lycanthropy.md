<p>Lycanthropy is an affliction that turns its victims, against their will, into wolves or wolf-like beasts,and is most feared in the <a href="Blodrise Dark" class="internal-link">Blodrise Dark</a> of southern Nirydia, whose werewolves are told of across the region. It is passed by the bite or the blood of one already afflicted, and the wolves of the forest are said to carry it as surely as its people do. Physicians who have studied the afflicted compare it to the madness that some bitten beasts carry, which turns a gentle dog into one that bites in turn, for those who suffer it are driven to spread it.</p>  
<p>Unlike the shifters it is so often blamed on, the afflicted choose nothing. They are drawn into the beast against every effort to hold their shape and cannot return from it by any craft of their own; the folk of the Blodrise hold that the turning comes on the nights when Luna Major is full. The forest's werewolves are larger and heavier than any wolf, and those who have survived them speak of them as monstrous in a way no natural beast is. Similar afflictions are told of elsewhere, each taking the shape of a different beast in a different land, though none is so widely known as the wolves of the Blodrise.</p>  
<h2>Origins</h2>  
<p>In the south, lycanthropy is nearly always blamed on the <a href="Ral" class="internal-link">Ral</a>. The common telling holds that a Ral clan, hunted down in the Blodrise Dark, laid a curse upon its killers by bleeding into their wounds as it fell, and that the curse has spread through the forest ever since. The Ral deny it, and <a href="Rivid Tepor" class="internal-link">Rivid Tepor</a>, who travelled among them for two decades, noted that no account of the curse he could find was older than the hatred it is used to justify; he argued that the werewolves of the Blodrise are not Ral at all, and was killed by one of them in 768PA while trying to prove it. <a href="Sadizka Ovoletru" class="internal-link">Sadizka Ovoletru</a> of Rivno reads the affliction through the Anak as the afterimage of a beast loosed into the blood of another, with no vessel to return to. Whether it began with a curse, an accident or something else entirely, the tale has made the Ral outcasts across the south, and few there would draw any distinction between a Ral and the beasts of the Blodrise.</p>
