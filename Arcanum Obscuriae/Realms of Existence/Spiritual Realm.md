<p>Within Planar Theory the Spiritual Realm represents the immaterial spiritual World, all <a href="Greater Spirit" class="internal-link">Spirits</a> reside here.
Research into the Spiritual Realm and its nature is extremely limited, compared to Planar Theory, and is largely based on contact with the <a href="Fey" class="internal-link">Fey</a> who are creatures that exist in both the material and the spiritual realm, as well as certain magical practices such as <a href="Kobold Shamanism" class="internal-link">Kobold Spirit travel</a> or <a href="Aeldcult" class="internal-link">aeld</a> Druidism that allow sentient beings of the <a href="Material Realm" class="internal-link">material Realm</a>to cross over into the spiritual.<p>
