<p>Within Planar Theory the material Realm represents the physical World, as perceived by us, existence in contrast to sub-existence within the spiritual and absence of existence in the astral realm.
It is important to recognize the natural bias of research into realmatic theory when it comes to the differences between the Material, <a href="Spiritual Realm" class="internal-link">Spiritual </a> and the <a href="Astral Realm" class="internal-link">Astral</a> Realms, as all mortal life and corresponding research originates from the material.<p>
