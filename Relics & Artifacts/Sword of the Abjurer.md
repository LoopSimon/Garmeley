<p>The Sword of the Abjurer is a relic blade of incredible abjuring power, able to cut through woven spells as if they were made matter. It resembles a large longsword, its blade painted by dancing runes of a dozen colours and its hilt made of solid shining bronze. Its first mentions are dated back to the time of the old meadi Augur, powerful and primal magi that forged power into reality and sought means to fight back against the forces of the great Empire of Rakh, this blade was the result of many of their sages coming together to forge a weapon to best the Rakhi Thaumaturges.</p>
<p>It famously was wielded by the vihri Magus <a href="Viagoris" class="internal-link">Viagoris</a> during the <a href="War of Horn and Fang" class="internal-link">War of Horn and Fang</a> and taken by <a href="Muriel von Gloss" class="internal-link">Muriel von Gloss</a> after she bested him in combat. Once she was killed in turn by the later canonised Saint, <a href="Andre" class="internal-link">Andre of Trydlew</a> the sword fell into unknown hands for years. During the last years of the 9th century, the heroic Knight <a href="Filip of Leston" class="internal-link">Filip of Leston</a>, a member of the <a href="The Golden Ring" class="internal-link">Golden Ring</a> became the owner of the Blade.</p>
