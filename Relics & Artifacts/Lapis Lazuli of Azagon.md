<p>The Lapis Lazuli of Azagon, are a set of precious stones, enchanted with powerful magicks across generations and molded into the shape of a set of earrings, fit for the Chosen Consort <a href="Azag" class="internal-link">Azag</a> herself. The Stones lay dormant with Azag for millenia in the collapsed and abandoned ruins of the Temple Complex of Azagon, until the bound spirit of the drow, awake and perceiving for all these years, was finally put to rest during the events described in the <a href="The Poem of the Bad Omen" class="internal-link">Poem of the Bad Omen</a>, the precious stones reclaimed by the Axe throwing warrior <a href="Orlatz" class="internal-link">Orlatz</a> before the ruins caved in. In a way these stones are all that remains of Azags legacy and the story of her fallen tomb.   </p>
