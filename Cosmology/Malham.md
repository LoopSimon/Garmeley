<p>Malham is the brightest star of the Princess-Constellation, and is known across the world as the North Star. It sits more or less directly above the world's northern pole, along its rotational axis, and because of this it traces only a very small circle over a full rotation and always stays in roughly the same place in the sky. It neither rises nor sets, and is never lost in the glare of the Sun. For sailors and travellers it is therefore a reliable way to find north. In the far south of the Beheran Ocean it sinks low over the horizon, and beyond that it is lost entirely, which is when sailors turn to <a href="Tholmir" class="internal-link">Tholmir</a>.</p>
<h2>Names</h2>
<p>The Elves of the Beheran Subcontinent call it Ennar, "the Still", and the Urien sailors Polwacht, "Polewatch", a name that mirrors their Kielwacht for the southern star. In High Meadi it is Nordhal.</p>
<h2>In Qalmyr</h2>
<p>To the Qari of the <a href="Qalmyr" class="internal-link">Qalmyr</a> it is Qisvahl, "the Child of Destiny", one of the <a href="Qinari" class="internal-link">Qinari</a>, and is associated with hope and innocence. Because it never moves from its place, it is also the Qinari that no one has ever lost sight of, and the Qari say it is the one hope that is handed to every child at birth. The <a href="Path of the Third Moon" class="internal-link">Path of the Third Moon</a> teaches that this is only the beginning, and that the Third Moon Fleet, when it sails south and watches Qisvahl sink behind it, must make its own hope under the southern sky.</p>
<h2>In Starworship</h2>
<p>Within the Cyclopi <a href="Starworship" class="internal-link">Starworship</a>, Malham is the inner God of Order, Governance and civilisation, known as the Xanring, and one of the two strongest forces in their cosmology. The other is <a href="Urden" class="internal-link">Urden</a>.</p>
