<div class="wiki-infobox"> <h4 class="wiki-header">Luna Minor</h4> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Orbits</strong> <span><a href="Luna Major" class="internal-link">Luna Major</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Distance</strong> <span>About 20,000 km from Luna Major</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Orbital Period</strong> <span>About 3 days</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Diameter</strong> <span>About 10 km</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Age</strong> <span>About 120,000 years</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Appearance</strong> <span>A bright star at the moon's side</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Qari</strong> <span><a href="Zolra" class="internal-link">Zolra</a><br>"the Ruinous Mother"</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Elves</strong> <span>Hidja</span> </div> </div> <p>Luna Minor is the small moon of the world, a rock only about 10 km across that circles <a href="Luna Major" class="internal-link">Luna Major</a> rather than the <a href="Globus" class="internal-link">Globus</a> itself. It is a young moon, formed about 120,000 years ago from the wreckage of a great impact on Luna Major, and it is the only moon in the sky that can be seen moving around another.</p> <h2>In the Sky</h2> <p>To the naked eye Luna Minor shows no disc and looks like a very bright star, among the brightest in the night sky, although it never outshines <a href="Sarhen" class="internal-link">Sarhen</a>. Its surface is fresh and has not yet darkened, so it reflects about twice as much light as the old rock of Luna Major.</p> <p>It never strays far from the great moon. It circles it in about three days, swinging out to about two degrees from it, some five or six moon-widths, and then back to pass in front of Luna Major or behind it before swinging out on the far side. The movement is easy to follow, and in a single night the small light can be seen to shift against the face of the moon. Anyone who watches the sky will know that the moon has a companion that circles it, and so most peoples of the world count Luna Minor as a second moon, usually the small moon, although no eye can make out more than a point of light.</p> <p>It is brightest at the full moon, but then it is lost in the glare, and it is easiest to find around the half moon, when it stands out beside the pale half disc. Its stretched orbit sometimes carries it clear of the glare even at the full. Being an irregular tumbling rock, it also brightens and dims a little as it turns. When it passes behind Luna Major or into its shadow it vanishes for a while, and returns on the other side.</p> <h2>Orbit</h2> <p>The orbit of Luna Minor is still stretched and tilted, because 120,000 years is too short for tides to round it out. The same tides slowly drag the small moon inward, for Luna Major turns more slowly than Luna Minor circles it. It is spiralling down, and will one day strike Luna Major, but not for ages far beyond the span of any civilisation. Heavier bodies would have sunk much sooner, and Luna Minor is about as large as such a moon can be and survive.</p> <h2>Origin</h2> <p>About 120,000 years ago a large asteroid struck the near side of Luna Major. The blast threw up a cloud of debris, and the fragments collided and gathered into Luna Minor, so that it is made of the moon's own missing piece. The crater is the scar on the face of Luna Major that never turns away.</p> <p>Some of the debris fell on the Globus as meteorites, and the heavy meteor showers that followed are the likely cause of the Era of Ash and Ice that began in the same age (see the <a href="Complete Timeline" class="internal-link">Complete Timeline</a>). The impact changed nothing in the orbit of Luna Major or the tides, for Luna Minor holds only a tiny fraction of its mass. The age of the impact is far beyond the reach of any tradition, and no people remember it.</p> <h2>In Qalmyr</h2> <p>The Orcs of the <a href="Qalmyr" class="internal-link">Qalmyr</a> faith know the small moon as <a href="Zolra" class="internal-link">Zolra</a>, the Ruinous Mother, and the elves call her Hidja. To the Qari she is everything that Qastor cast away in the <a href="Ikval" class="internal-link">Ikval</a>, and her bright light, forever circling him and never able to leave his side, is read as the sign of what was torn from him. The First Path takes her small size for a sign of her lesser power. The Zolraii of the <a href="Path of the Second Moon" class="internal-link">Path of the Second Moon</a> keep their feasts by her round of three days.</p>
