# Wispmer (Pathfinder 1e race rules)

The <a href="Wispmer" class="internal-link">Wispmer</a>, sometimes called Lyrspawn or Bleakers, are ooze-like beings that take on a fixed form by mimicking another living creature, a process called alignment. The name usually refers only to those who have aligned as a sentient creature. A Wispmer aligns only once, and in doing so replicates much of the cellular structure of its model, down to the brain, so that its lifespan, height and weight closely follow those of the creature it copied. Wispmer keep some of their native nature: a pale, milky complexion, a resistance to great heat and cold, and an unusual conduciveness to electricity. At their centre sits the core, a small twig-like membrane, and any matter that is cut off from it decays to an ash-like dust within minutes. Wispmer are found on many continents, usually near lakes or in swamps, and their largest community is the city of Tah in the jungles of Vinjira, known as the "City of Wax".

A Wispmer character uses the traits below and chooses an aligned form.

## Wispmer racial traits

- **Ability Score Modifiers:** +2 Constitution, +2 Intelligence, −2 Charisma. Wispmer are resilient and quick to learn, but their waxy pallor and copied manner unsettle most other peoples.
- **Type:** Wispmer are humanoids with the wispmer subtype.
- **Aligned Form:** Choose one Small or Medium humanoid race as the Wispmer's aligned form, such as human, Shirin or Bihra, with the GM's approval. The Wispmer uses that race's size, base speed, vision (low-light vision or darkvision, if any) and age, height and weight table, and counts as both a Wispmer and a member of that race for any effect related to race. They do not gain the race's ability score modifiers or other racial traits.
- **Native Resilience:** Wispmer have cold resistance 5 and fire resistance 5.
- **Conductive:** Electricity runs easily through a Wispmer's body. They take a −2 penalty on saving throws against electricity effects. Once per day, after taking electricity damage, a Wispmer can release the stored charge as a melee touch attack on their next turn, dealing electricity damage equal to the damage they took, up to a maximum of 1d6 per character level. The charge fades if not used by the end of their next turn.
- **Ashen Remains:** Any blood, hair or flesh separated from a Wispmer's core turns to dust within minutes. Effects that need a piece of the Wispmer's body, such as the bonus a scrying spell gains from a body part, cannot use these remains, and the Wispmer gains a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against scrying.
- **Fixed Form:** Having aligned once, a Wispmer's body holds its shape stubbornly. They gain a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against polymorph effects, poison and paralysis.
- **Languages:** Wispmer begin play speaking the language of the people of their aligned form, or Vindi if they come from Tah. Wispmer with high Intelligence scores can choose any bonus languages of the player's choice.

## Age, height and weight

A Wispmer uses the age, height and weight table of their aligned form.

## Alternate racial traits

- **Trueborn:** The Wispmer was born of two Wispmer parents and carries their genetic memory. They gain a +2 racial bonus on two Knowledge skills of their choice, and those skills are always class skills for them. This trait replaces Fixed Form.
- **Child of Tah:** The Wispmer grew up in the jungles of Vinjira, which few other peoples survive. They gain a +2 racial bonus on Survival checks and on Fortitude saves against disease, and can move through jungle undergrowth at normal speed. This trait replaces Ashen Remains.
