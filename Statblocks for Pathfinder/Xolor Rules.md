# Xolor (Pathfinder 1e race rules)

The <a href="Xolor" class="internal-link">Xolor</a> are a people of far-eastern Nirydia, distantly related to the Qari, and the main inhabitants of the Terenni Isles. Like the Orcs, they have a long seafaring tradition. Their bodies produce a unique protein that lays down a protective layer of fat around their muscles, and that can also store memories. When the flesh is eaten, the memories can be understood and absorbed by another Xolor. This gave rise to a culture of ritual cannibalism in which food holds a revered place, and it is the main reason other peoples shun them. Xolor who eat too many memories become Chimera, mutated beings who can only live on the flesh of thinking creatures. The Xolor were once numerous on the Nirydian mainland, but the Zuhuri Purges of the 8th and 9th centuries killed a great many of them. Today their largest community is the Divine Commune of Pervantes on Durennis, the last remnant of the old Empire of Vanpur.

## Xolor racial traits

- **Ability Score Modifiers:** +2 Constitution, +2 Wisdom, −2 Charisma. Xolor are hardy and carry the knowledge of those they have eaten, but most peoples find them unsettling and treat them as outcasts.
- **Type:** Xolor are humanoids with the xolor subtype.
- **Size:** Xolor are Medium creatures and have no bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Xolor have a base speed of 30 feet.
- **Darkvision:** Xolor can see in the dark up to 60 feet.
- **Protective Fat:** A layer of fat cushions the Xolor's muscles and organs. They gain a +4 racial bonus on all Fortitude saves and a +2 racial bonus to CMD.
- **Islander:** Xolor gain a +2 racial bonus on Profession (sailor) and Swim checks, and Swim is always a class skill for them.
- **Memory Eating:** A Xolor can absorb the memories of a creature with an Intelligence of 3 or higher by eating at least a pound of its flesh within one day of its death. The meal takes 10 minutes and leaves the Xolor sated for a day. A Xolor can only hold the memories of one creature at a time, and a new meal replaces the old one. For the next 24 hours, the Xolor gains the following benefits.
  - **Memories:** The Xolor can ask the GM up to three questions about things the creature knew, such as faces, places, passwords or plans. The GM answers truthfully as far as the creature knew, in the form of half-remembered images and feelings.
  - **Skills:** Choose up to two skills in which the creature had ranks. The Xolor uses the creature's ranks in those skills in place of their own, up to a maximum of their own character level, and treats them as class skills.
  - **Instinct:** The Xolor gains one feat the creature had, ignoring all prerequisites other than ability scores.
  - **Tongue:** The Xolor can speak and understand one language the creature knew.
  - **Overwhelming Memories:** If the creature had more Hit Dice than the Xolor, the Xolor must succeed at a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 the creature's Hit Dice + its Charisma modifier) when the meal ends. On a failure, the creature's memories fight their own and the Xolor is staggered for 1d4 hours, and the GM may give them a lasting habit or fear that belonged to the creature.
  - Most peoples see this practice as a horror, and a Xolor seen doing it can expect to be treated as a monster.
- **Languages:** Xolor begin play speaking Xolor. Xolor with high Intelligence scores can choose any bonus languages of the player's choice.

## Feat

**Flesh Remembers (General)**
What you eat becomes part of you, a first step on the road towards the Chimera.
Prerequisites: Con 13, Xolor, Memory Eating racial trait, character level 5th.
Benefit: When you use Memory Eating on a creature that had darkvision, low-light vision, scent, a climb speed, a swim speed or a natural attack, you can also gain that one ability for 24 hours. A gained natural attack deals damage as normal for its kind for a Medium creature and is a secondary attack when used alongside manufactured weapons. A speed gained this way cannot be higher than your base speed.
Special: Each time you use this feat, the GM may ask for a DC 15 Will save. On a failure, some small physical trace of the creature stays with you permanently, such as a patch of scales or a change in your voice or eyes, with no game effect.

## Alternate racial traits

- **Abstainer:** The Xolor refuses to eat the flesh of thinking creatures, whether from conviction, fear of becoming a Chimera or a wish to live among other peoples. They gain a +2 racial bonus on Diplomacy checks with non-Xolor and on saving throws against effects that cause hunger, nausea or compulsion. This trait replaces Memory Eating.
- **Pervanti Feaster:** The Xolor was raised in the meat cult of Pervantes, which joins the Vas Ira with the memory feast. While holding a creature's memories through Memory Eating, they also gain a +1 morale bonus on Will saves and cast divine spells at +1 caster level. This trait replaces Darkvision.
- **Starwatcher:** The Xolor keeps the old star worship of Vanpur and steers by the night sky. They gain a +2 racial bonus on Knowledge (geography) and Survival checks, and can always find true north on a night when the stars are visible. This trait replaces Islander.
