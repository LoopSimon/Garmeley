# Mogyri (Pathfinder 1e race rules)

The <a href="Mogyr" class="internal-link">Mogyri</a> are a humanoid people of the jungles of the Drizan Peninsula in southern Nirydia, most of them living under the Temple of Yl-Lhatsi and organised in clans. Every Mogyr carries a bonded mushroom on their back, received within hours of birth from a parent or guardian who gives up part of their own. The shroom lives in symbiosis with its carrier and, under extreme stress, pours out a store of energy, hormones and nutrients known as shroom-boosting. Mogyri grow up to 7 or 8 feet tall, but most adults spend their lives on all fours, exposing their shroom-covered backs to the sky. They are known for being xenophobic and isolationist, and practise Tsurigism, a faith built around the bond between Mogyr and shroom. A Mogyr without a shroom is shunned by almost all of Mogyri society.

## Mogyri racial traits

- **Ability Score Modifiers:** +2 Constitution, +2 Wisdom, −2 Charisma. Mogyri are enduring and patient, but distrustful and hard to read for outsiders.
- **Type:** Mogyri are humanoids with the mogyr subtype.
- **Size:** Mogyri are Medium creatures and have no bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Mogyri have a base speed of 30 feet.
- **Low-Light Vision:** Mogyri can see twice as far as humans in conditions of dim light.
- **All Fours:** When a Mogyr has both hands free, they can drop to all fours as a move action. While on all fours their base speed is 40 feet and they gain a +4 racial bonus to CMD against bull rush and trip attempts, but they cannot use their hands to wield weapons or manipulate objects. Standing up again is a free action.
- **Jungle Born:** Mogyri gain a +2 racial bonus on Survival checks and can move through jungle undergrowth at normal speed.
- **Fungal Symbiosis:** A Mogyr's bonded shroom filters what enters their body. They gain a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against poison and disease.
- **Bonded Shroom:** Every Mogyr carries a bonded shroom, chosen from the list below. The shroom passes down from parent or guardian, so it usually marks the character's bloodline and social standing.
- **Shroom-Boost:** Once per day as a swift action, a Mogyr can trigger their shroom. For a number of rounds equal to 1 + their Constitution modifier, they gain a +2 racial bonus to Strength and the effect of their shroom type below. The rush is hallucinogenic, and while it lasts the Mogyr takes a −2 penalty on Perception checks and on saving throws against illusions. A Mogyr can use Shroom-Boost one additional time per day at 10th and 20th level. If the Mogyr is below half their hit points, they can trigger it as an immediate action instead.
- **Languages:** Mogyri begin play speaking Mogyri. Mogyri with high Intelligence scores can choose any bonus languages of the player's choice.

### Clans and bonded shrooms

A Mogyr's shroom passes from parent to child, so each type of shroom marks one of the great clans of Drizan. Choose one.

- **Armillaria:** A traditionalist clan that keeps close to the beasts of the jungle. During a Shroom-Boost, the Mogyr gains two claw attacks that deal 1d4 points of damage each and can communicate with animals as if under the effect of speak with animals.
- **Chorioactis:** When the Shroom-Boost begins, the shroom bursts with spores. Every other creature adjacent to the Mogyr must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 the Mogyr's level + their Constitution modifier) or be sickened for 1 round.
- **Ganoderma:** The clan of merchants and sailors, and the most outward-looking of the Mogyri. Profession (merchant) and Profession (sailor) are always class skills for them. During a Shroom-Boost, the Mogyr gains a +4 bonus on Acrobatics, Climb and Swim checks and is immune to the nauseated condition.
- **Hericium:** The shroom of the bloodline of the Great Gaden, prized for the clarity it brings. During a Shroom-Boost, the Mogyr gains a +2 bonus on Intelligence-based and Wisdom-based checks and concentration checks, and is immune to confusion.
- **Hydnellum:** The shroom weeps a red sap that the Mogyr's body carries into their blows. During a Shroom-Boost, the Mogyr's melee attacks deal 1 point of bleed damage.
- **Lactarius:** A clan of artisans and silkworkers whose shrooms bleed a thick, sticky milk. Once during each Shroom-Boost, the Mogyr can spray this milk as a standard action, making a ranged touch attack against a creature within 20 feet. On a hit, the target is entangled for 1d4 rounds.
- **Lingpa:** The shroom of the high houses of Yl-Lhatsi, said to carry visions of the Greater Will. During a Shroom-Boost, the Mogyr gains a +2 insight bonus to AC and on Will saves, and ignores the penalty against illusions. This is the rarest lineage of shroom, and can only be taken with GM discretion.
- **Porpolomopsis:** When the Shroom-Boost begins, the Mogyr gains temporary hit points equal to their character level, which last until the boost ends.
- **Sparassis:** A clan of farmers and wanderers. During a Shroom-Boost, the Mogyr's base speed increases by 10 feet, they ignore difficult terrain, and they do not suffer the effects of the fatigued condition.
- **Trametes:** A clan of artisans and singers. During a Shroom-Boost, the Mogyr's song carries the rush to their companions, and allies within 30 feet who can hear them gain a +1 morale bonus on attack rolls and on saving throws against fear.
- **Trichoderma:** An isolationist, monk-like clan whose shrooms are toxic. During a Shroom-Boost, the first creature the Mogyr hits with a melee or unarmed attack each round must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 the Mogyr's level + their Constitution modifier) or take 1d4 points of poison damage. This lineage is among the rarest of shrooms.

### Losing a shroom

A Mogyr can lose their shroom as punishment for grave crimes or through magic that ends the bond. A shroomless Mogyr loses Fungal Symbiosis, Bonded Shroom and Shroom-Boost, and is shunned by almost all other Mogyri. A new shroom can only be bonded if someone is willing to give up part of their own.

## Alternate racial traits

- **Dres-Ma:** The Mogyr comes from one of the many small clans gathered under the name Dres-Ma, whose shrooms follow no great bloodline. Their Shroom-Boost lasts 2 additional rounds, and they gain a +2 racial bonus on one skill of their choice, which is always a class skill for them. This trait replaces the clan effect of Bonded Shroom.
- **Mthar Dzin:** The Mogyr belongs to the Mthar Dzin, a brotherhood of religious fanatics drawn from many clans. They gain a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against fear and enchantment effects, and during a Shroom-Boost they gain a +1 morale bonus on attack rolls against creatures that are not Mogyri. This trait replaces Jungle Born and the clan effect of Bonded Shroom.
- **Ancient Tsurigist:** The Mogyr follows the older Tsurigism of the wild corners of Drizan, which seeks enlightenment through the shared journey of Mogyr and shroom. Once per day, after resting for 8 hours with their shroom exposed to sun or rain, they can regain one use of Shroom-Boost or remove the fatigued, sickened or shaken condition. This trait replaces the effects of a specific Shroom during a Shroomboost, ancient Tsurigist typically are not part of a great Clan.
- **Elder Shroom:** The Mogyr's shroom is ancient and has been passed down through many generations. Shroom-Boost lasts 2 additional rounds, and once per day the Mogyr can spend a use of Shroom-Boost to cast lesser restoration on themselves as a spell-like ability. This trait replaces All Fours. This is the rarest type of shroom, and can only be taken with GM discretion.
