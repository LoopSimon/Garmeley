# Safir (Pathfinder 1e race rules)

The <a href="Safir" class="internal-link">Safir</a> are a broad family of reptiloid humanoids native to the jungles of southern Behera, on the subcontinents of Kaito and Vinjira, and the most numerous and politically significant non-human species in the south of the continent. Beheran humans call them the Naga. They descend from the fully serpentine Astafir, and their oldest traditions say they were changed by the Ichor of Atul, the first of the Yari to share his divine blood. The Safir are divided into four colour groups, the Pravi, Vekti, Irud and Zoroi, each an ancient adaptation to a different environment. Separately from colour, Safir bodies range from fully bipedal to nearly serpentine, depending on how much Yari Ichor a community has received over the generations. Colour and body form are entirely independent of each other.

A Safir character uses the shared traits below, then chooses one colour group and one body form.

## Shared Safir traits

- **Type:** Safir are humanoids with the reptilian subtype.
- **Size:** Safir are Medium creatures and have no bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Low-Light Vision:** Safir can see twice as far as humans in conditions of dim light.
- **Forked Tongue:** Safir taste the air as they move. They gain a +2 racial bonus on Perception checks and on Survival checks to follow tracks.
- **Inheritor of Behera:** Safir are the old masters of the Beheran jungles, and their ancestral senses keep them alert to danger. A Safir can never be surprised and always acts in a surprise round, and gains a +2 racial bonus on initiative checks.
- **Languages:** Safir begin play speaking Safiri. Safir with high Intelligence scores can choose any bonus languages of the player's choice.

## Colour groups

### Pravi

The Pravi are the most numerous and politically dominant colour group, warm-scaled in browns, yellows and reds. They hold the fertile interior heartlands of Kaito and Vinjira and are above all builders of cities, laws and institutions, most of all the Empire of Pravazik. Their elaborate scale patterns record their ancestry, and other Pravi can read them as easily as a written genealogy.

- **Ability Score Modifiers:** +2 Intelligence, +2 Charisma, −2 Dexterity.
- **Builders of Empire:** Pravi gain a +2 racial bonus on Knowledge (engineering) and Knowledge (nobility) checks.
- **Patterned Lineage:** Pravi are raised to read heritage, rank and intent from the smallest details of a body, whether the scale patterns of another Safir or the bearing and features of other peoples. They gain a +2 racial bonus on Diplomacy and Sense Motive checks.
- **Broad Tolerance:** Unlike most Safir, Pravi are at home outside the jungle. They gain a +2 racial bonus on Fortitude saves against hot and cold environments.

### Vekti

The Vekti are the great wanderers of the Safir world, dark-scaled in greys, dark blues and blacks. They are adapted to cave systems and deep shadow, and are the only colour group truly at home in the dry heat of the desert. Their nomadic caravans and diaspora communities are found across Behera and beyond, but within the Pravi realms they usually occupy the lowest tiers of society. The Oro are their greatest independent tribe, and many Vekti follow the Faith of the Singer.

- **Ability Score Modifiers:** +2 Dexterity, +2 Constitution, −2 Charisma.
- **Darkvision:** Vekti can see in the dark up to 60 feet. This replaces Low-Light Vision.
- **Desert Wanderer:** Vekti gain a +4 racial bonus on Fortitude saves against hot weather, thirst and forced marches.
- **Caravan Born:** Vekti gain a +2 racial bonus on Survival checks and on Bluff checks, and Survival is always a class skill for them.

### Irud

The Irud are the deep jungle Safir, green-scaled and camouflaged in the densest undergrowth. They live in communities built vertically around and within the God Trees, from the roots to the canopy, and are the most fanatically devoted to the Yari of all the colour groups. The Irud are the least known of the Safir to outsiders.

- **Ability Score Modifiers:** +2 Dexterity, +2 Wisdom, −2 Intelligence.
- **Canopy Dweller:** Irud have a climb speed of 20 feet, gaining a +8 racial bonus on Climb checks.
- **Jungle Camouflage:** Irud gain a +4 racial bonus on Stealth checks in forest and jungle terrain.
- **Yari Devoted:** Irud gain a +1 racial bonus on Will saves and a +2 racial bonus on Knowledge (religion) checks.
- **Jungle Bound:** Irud suffer in open, dry heat. They take a −2 penalty on Fortitude saves against hot weather.

### Zoroi

The Zoroi are the sea people of the Safir, blue-scaled and amphibious. They live along the coasts, reefs, rivers and islands of the Smaragd Sea and the Beheran Ocean, and are the finest navigators and seafarers the Safir produce. They are the most outward-facing colour group, and foreign sailors usually meet Zoroi intermediaries long before they meet Pravi officials.

- **Ability Score Modifiers:** +2 Strength, +2 Intelligence, −2 Wisdom.
- **Amphibious:** Zoroi can breathe water and air, and have a swim speed of 30 feet, gaining a +8 racial bonus on Swim checks.
- **Navigator:** Zoroi gain a +2 racial bonus on Profession (sailor) and Knowledge (geography) checks, and can always find true north when at sea.
- **Sea Bound:** Zoroi suffer in open, dry heat. They take a −2 penalty on Fortitude saves against hot weather.

### Mixed colour

Safir interbreed freely across colour lines, and mixed individuals show blended or patterned scales. A mixed-colour Safir takes the ability score modifiers of one parent's colour group, and may swap one other trait of that group for a trait of the other parent's group, with the GM's approval.

## Body forms

Choose one body form. Bipedal forms are most common in lineages with a long history of Yari Ichor, and the most serpentine forms in those with little. The Zoroi most often have the Tail form.

- **Bipedal:** The Safir walks upright on two legs. Their base speed is 30 feet, and they gain a +2 racial bonus on Acrobatics checks.
- **Serpentine:** The Safir's lower half is a long serpent body. Their base speed is 30 feet and they have a climb speed of 15 feet. They gain a +4 racial bonus to CMD against trip attempts and a +2 racial bonus on Stealth checks, but cannot wear boots or other items worn on the feet.
- **Tail:** The Safir has arms and a massive tail in place of legs. Their base speed is 20 feet, but they gain a swim speed of 30 feet (or increase an existing swim speed by 10 feet). They gain a +4 racial bonus to CMD against trip and bull rush attempts, but cannot wear boots or other items worn on the feet.

## Feats

**Yari Ichor (General)**
You have drunk the Ichor of a Yari, and the divine blood that first changed your people still works within you.
Prerequisites: Safir, character level 3rd.
Benefit: You gain a +2 sacred bonus on saving throws against poison and disease. Once per day, when you fail a saving throw, you can reroll it with a +2 sacred bonus and must take the second result.
Special: If you have the Ichor-Fed racial trait, you can use Ichor-Fed one additional time per day, and its duration increases to 1 round per character level.

## Alternate racial traits

- **Kash:** The Safir is a Kash, a pure albino with no colour group's markings, whose birth is seen as an omen of change and doom. The Yari Arishi has demanded that every Kash be killed at birth, and any Kash alive today was hidden, protected or born beyond the Empire's reach. A Kash gains +2 to one ability score of the player's choice instead of a colour group's modifiers, and gains a +2 racial bonus on Intimidate checks. Once per day, when they roll a natural 1 on any d20 roll, they can reroll it and must take the second result. They have light sensitivity and are dazzled in areas of bright light. This replaces the colour group and should only be taken with the GM's permission.
- **Ichor-Fed:** The Safir's community has a living Yari patron and drinks of its Ichor. Once per day as a swift action, the Safir can gain a +2 sacred bonus on attack rolls and saving throws for 1 round per two character levels (minimum 1). This trait replaces Forked Tongue.
- **Serpent Venom:** The Safir has venom glands behind their fangs. Once per day as a swift action, the Safir can envenom a bladed or piercing weapon they wield, either by biting along its edge or by coating it from their mouth. The venom stays potent until the end of their next turn or until the weapon hits. Safir venom: injury; save Fortitude DC 10 + 1/2 the Safir's character level + the Safir's Constitution modifier; frequency 1/round for 6 rounds; effect 1d2 Dexterity damage; cure 1 save. The Safir is immune to their own venom. The number of uses per day increases by one at 5th level and every five levels after that. This trait replaces Inheritor of Behera.
- **Shed Skin:** The Safir can tear free of their outer skin far faster than normal. Once per day as a move action, they can shed their skin to automatically escape a grapple or pin, free themselves from the entangled condition, and end any ongoing acid or fire damage affecting them. The shed skin is left behind as a complete husk. This trait replaces Low-Light Vision, or Darkvision for a Vekti.
- **Hypnotic Gaze:** Once per day as a standard action, the Safir can attempt to fascinate one creature within 30 feet with their gaze. The target must succeed on a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 the Safir's character level + the Safir's Charisma modifier) or be fascinated for 1 round. This is a mind-affecting gaze effect. This trait replaces Inheritor of Behera.
