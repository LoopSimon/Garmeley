# Bihra (Pathfinder 1e race rules)

The <a href="Bihra" class="internal-link">Bihra</a>, known to the humans of Kher and much of Ilrakhan as kobolds, are bipedal, scaled reptilian humanoids native to Nirydia and the largest racial group on the continent. They fall into two broad groups. The Steppe Bihra of the Niryd Steppe are large, warlike and unusually prone to mutation, and see themselves as the only true Bihra. They call all other Bihra the Zibihra, or "failed Bihra". The Zibihra are smaller and far less prone to mutation, and are divided here into the Mainland Bihra of eastern and southern Nirydia and the much smaller Durenni Bihra of the island of Durennis. A Bihra character uses the shared traits below and then takes one lineage.

## Shared Bihra traits

All Bihra have the following racial traits, whatever their lineage.

- **Type:** Bihra are humanoids with the reptilian subtype.
- **Darkvision:** Bihra can see in the dark up to 60 feet.
- **Scales:** Bihra gain a +1 natural armor bonus.
- **Two Souls:** Bihra believe that every person holds a base soul bound to the body and one or more higher souls that can leave it. Bihra gain a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against possession, death effects and negative energy effects. Once per day, after failing a Will save against a mind-affecting effect, a Bihra can reroll the save and must take the second result.
- **Spirit Tradition:** Most Bihra hold to the oral shamanic traditions of their people and distrust arcane magic. Bihra gain a +1 racial bonus on Knowledge (religion), Spellcraft and Sense Motive checks.

## Steppe Bihra

The Steppe Bihra live among the larger and lesser Khaganates and tribes of the Niryd Steppe. They stand up to 2 metres tall and are heavily built, often weighing 150 kilograms or more. Their tough hides let them live among the razor-edged Aspira bladegrass that makes the central steppe uninhabitable for most other peoples. Their culture rests on the triumph of the physical, the belief that spiritual worth is proven through strength, mutation and victory, and their most mutated members are revered as leaders and shamans.

- **Ability Score Modifiers:** +2 Strength, +2 Wisdom, −2 Intelligence. Steppe Bihra are powerful, but their warrior culture values strength over learning.
- **Size:** Steppe Bihra are Medium creatures and have no bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Steppe Bihra have a base speed of 30 feet.
- **Bladegrass Hide:** Steppe Bihra take no damage from bladegrass, thorns, briars and other cutting plants, and can move through such undergrowth at normal speed. They also gain a +4 racial bonus on saving throws against bleed effects.
- **Triumph of the Physical:** Steppe Bihra gain a +2 racial bonus on Intimidate checks. Other Steppe Bihra treat a character's mutations as a sign of status, and a Steppe Bihra with two or more mutations gains a +2 bonus on Diplomacy checks with them.
- **Mutation:** Every Steppe Bihra is born with one mutation from the Steppe mutation list below. The GM may have the player roll for it rather than choose.
- **Languages:** Steppe Bihra begin play speaking Lihbare. Steppe Bihra with high Intelligence scores can choose any bonus languages of the player's choice.

### Steppe mutations

1. **Horned Crest:** The Bihra has a natural gore attack that deals 1d6 points of damage. It is a secondary natural attack when used alongside manufactured weapons.
2. **Heavy Tail:** The Bihra has a natural tail slap attack that deals 1d6 points of damage. It is a secondary natural attack when used alongside manufactured weapons. The Bihra also gains a +2 racial bonus to CMD against trip attempts.
3. **Third Eye:** The Bihra gains a +2 racial bonus on Perception checks and on saving throws against illusions.
4. **Vestigial Arm:** The Bihra has a small third arm. It can hold a light object such as a torch, wand or potion, retrieve a stowed item as a swift action, and counts as a free hand for the somatic components of spells. It cannot wield a weapon or shield, make attacks, or help grip a two-handed weapon.
5. **Spined Hide:** Bony spines grow through the Bihra's scales. A creature that grapples the Bihra or is grappled by them takes 1d4 points of piercing damage at the start of each of its turns.
6. **Fire Gullet:** Once per day, the Bihra can breathe a 15-foot cone of fire as a standard action. It deals 1d6 points of fire damage per two character levels (minimum 1d6), and a successful Reflex save (DC 10 + 1/2 the Bihra's character level + the Bihra's Constitution modifier) halves the damage.
7. **Shaman's Mark:** The Bihra has extra eyes, a second partial head or another anomaly that Bihra see as the sign of a shaman. They gain a +2 racial bonus on Will saves against possession and spells and effects that would expel or harm their soul, and the save DCs of their spells from the shaman class increase by 1. A Bihra with a full second head should be agreed with the GM.
8. **Armoured Plates:** Thick bony plates grow over the Bihra's scales. They gain DR 1/-, which increases to DR 2/- at 10th level.
9. **Heat Pits:** Pits along the Bihra's snout sense warmth. They gain blindsense with a range of 15 feet, which only detects living creatures that are warmer than their surroundings.
10. **Gliding Membranes:** Folds of skin stretch between the Bihra's arms and flanks. They take no damage from falling, as if under a constant feather fall effect, and while falling can move 5 feet horizontally for every 5 feet they fall.
11. **Venomous Bite:** The Bihra has a natural bite attack that deals 1d4 points of damage. It is a secondary natural attack when used alongside manufactured weapons. Once per day, when the Bihra hits with the bite, the target must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC 10 + 1/2 the Bihra's character level + the Bihra's Constitution modifier) or take 1d2 points of Dexterity damage.
12. **Frilled Crest:** A wide frill of skin rings the Bihra's head. Once per combat, the Bihra can flare it to demoralize an opponent with Intimidate as a move action instead of a standard action.
13. **Grass-Coloured Scales:** The Bihra's scales shift slowly to match their surroundings. They gain a +4 racial bonus on Stealth checks in grassland, forest and other natural terrain, as long as they move no more than half their speed.
14. **Runner's Legs:** The Bihra has long, digitigrade legs built for the open steppe. Their base speed increases by 10 feet.
15. **Second Heart:** The Bihra has a second heart that keeps them alive through terrible wounds. They automatically stabilize when below 0 hit points, and gain a +4 racial bonus on Fortitude saves against death from massive damage, this can only be used once every 40 days.

Steppe Bihra can take the Further Mutation feat to gain more mutations.

**Further Mutation (General)**
Your body continues to change, as your people believe it should.
Prerequisites: Steppe Bihra, character level 3rd.
Benefit: You gain one additional mutation from the Steppe mutation list.
Special: You can take this feat more than once, but no more than once for every five character levels you have. Each time, choose a different mutation.

## Mainland Bihra (Zibihra)

The Mainland Bihra live across eastern and southern Nirydia and make up the majority of all Bihra in the world. Most stand between 1.4 metres and 1.8 metres tall, with powerful jaws for creatures of their size, claws on their hands and feet, and scales that are tougher than those of their western cousins and come in a wide range of striking colours. They are far less prone to great mutation than the Steppe Bihra. Mainland Bihra are a communal people with a long love of trade, and Bihra families run ferries, markets and merchant houses across the south. Many follow the Vas Ira and its denominations, such as Iratism and the Karaluk, while others have turned to Pen-Zuhur.

- **Ability Score Modifiers:** +2 Constitution, +2 Charisma, −2 Dexterity. Mainland Bihra are hardy, sociable and born traders, but stocky and deliberate in their movements.
- **Size:** Mainland Bihra are Medium creatures and have no bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Mainland Bihra have a base speed of 30 feet.
- **Hard Scales:** The natural armor bonus from a Mainland Bihra's scales increases to +2.
- **Colour-Scaled:** A Mainland Bihra's scale colour is tied to an element. Choose a colour and gain resistance 5 to the matching energy type: red, orange or gold (fire), blue or violet (electricity), green or yellow (acid), white or silver (cold).
- **Powerful Jaws:** Mainland Bihra have a natural bite attack that deals 1d4 points of damage. It is a secondary natural attack when used alongside manufactured weapons.
- **Languages:** Mainland Bihra begin play speaking Lihbare. Mainland Bihra with high Intelligence scores can choose any bonus languages of the player's choice.

## Durenni Bihra (Zibihra)

The Durenni Bihra live on the island of Durennis, where the Omnas of Lisava, Zilkand and Raximan succeeded the old Bihra realm of Japhal. They are much smaller than their mainland kin, and most live in the monsoon jungles of northern Durennis and the Pepper Islands, where they grow the spices of the Vanpur Pepper Trade. The Durenni are known as traders, spice growers and perfumers, and the city of Prankabur in Lisava is said to hold the most advanced school of medicine in the world.

- **Ability Score Modifiers:** +2 Dexterity, +2 Wisdom, −2 Strength. Durenni Bihra are quick and studied philosophers, but small and feeble in stature.
- **Size:** Durenni Bihra are Small creatures and gain a +1 size bonus to their AC, a +1 size bonus on attack rolls, a −1 penalty to their CMB and CMD, and a +4 size bonus on Stealth checks.
- **Speed:** Durenni Bihra have a base speed of 30 feet.
- **Monsoon Born:** Durenni Bihra grow up among jungle rivers and seasonal floods. They have a swim speed of 20 feet, gaining a +8 racial bonus on Swim checks, and can move through jungle undergrowth, mud and shallow bog at normal speed. They take no penalties on Perception checks from rain, and gain a +2 racial bonus on Fortitude saves against disease.
- **Durenni Learning:** Durenni Bihra gain a +2 racial bonus on Craft (alchemy), Heal and Perception checks.
- **Languages:** Durenni Bihra begin play speaking Durenni. Durenni Bihra with high Intelligence scores can choose any bonus languages of the player's choice.

## Racial feats

**Jaws and Steel (Combat)**
Bihra fighters are trained from youth to bite and claw between the strokes of their weapons.
Prerequisites: Bihra, base attack bonus +3, a natural bite, claw, gore or tail slap attack.
Benefit: When you make secondary natural attacks alongside manufactured weapons, you take a −3 penalty on them instead of −5.

**Tail Sweep (Combat)**
A heavy Bihra tail can take a foe's legs out from under them in the middle of a brawl.
Prerequisites: Bihra, Str 13, Improved Unarmed Strike or a natural attack.
Benefit: Once per round, when you hit a creature with an unarmed strike or a natural attack, you can attempt to trip that creature as a free action at a −4 penalty. This does not provoke an attack of opportunity. If the trip fails by 10 or more, you are not knocked prone in return.

**Flesh Ascendant (General)**
Your mutations grow stronger with age and battle, as the Khans say a true Bihra's should.
Prerequisites: Steppe Bihra, at least two mutations, character level 5th.
Benefit: Choose one mutation you have. If it grants a natural attack, the attack's damage increases by one step (1d4 to 1d6, 1d6 to 1d8). If it can be used a limited number of times per day or per combat, you can use it one additional time in that period. If it grants a racial bonus, that bonus increases by 2.
Special: You can take this feat more than once. Each time, choose a different mutation.

**Coloured Breath (General)**
The element of your scales burns in your throat as it does in the old stories of the dragons.
Prerequisites: Mainland Bihra, Colour-Scaled racial trait, character level 3rd.
Benefit: Once per day as a standard action, you can breathe a 15-foot cone of the energy type your Colour-Scaled trait protects you against. It deals 1d6 points of damage of that type per two character levels (minimum 1d6), and a successful Reflex save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Constitution modifier) halves the damage. At 10th level, you can use this ability twice per day.

**Mesmeric Gaze (General)**
The stories told of Bihra eyes are true in your case.
Prerequisites: Bihra, Cha 13, Hypnotic Eyes racial trait.
Benefit: You can use Hypnotic Eyes three times per day. A creature fascinated by your gaze also takes a −2 penalty on Will saves against your enchantment spells and on Sense Motive checks against you until the end of your next turn after the fascination ends.

**Prankabur Physician (General)**
You trained in the medical traditions of Lisava, the most advanced in the world.
Prerequisites: Durenni Bihra, Heal 3 ranks.
Benefit: You can treat deadly wounds on the same creature twice per day, and creatures under your long-term care recover 2 additional hit points per character level each day. You gain a +4 bonus on Heal checks to treat poison and disease.

## Alternate racial traits

- **Salamander Bond (Steppe):** The Bihra shares a soul with a Gnarlskin Salamander, in the western shamanic tradition. They gain a +4 racial bonus on Handle Animal and Ride checks with salamanders, and if they gain an animal companion or mount from a class feature, it can be a salamander with the statistics of a monitor lizard. This trait replaces Triumph of the Physical.
- **Tame Blood (Mainland or Durenni):** A few Zibihra are born with a true mutation, often treated as a sign of a future shaman. The Bihra gains one mutation from the Steppe mutation list. This trait replaces Colour-Scaled for Mainland Bihra or Durenni Learning for Durenni Bihra.
- **Hypnotic Eyes:** Some Bihra are said to hold the eyes of the old dragons, and this Bihra is one of them. Once per day as a standard action, they can fix their gaze on one creature within 30 feet that can see them. The target must succeed on a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 the Bihra's character level + the Bihra's Charisma modifier) or be fascinated for 1d4 rounds. A creature that is in combat or threatened gains a +4 bonus on the save. While the target is fascinated, the Bihra gains a +2 racial bonus on Bluff and Diplomacy checks against it. This is a mind-affecting gaze effect. This trait replaces Spirit Tradition.
- **Khan's Wrestler (Steppe):** The Steppe Bihra was raised on the wrestling bouts that settle quarrels in the camps of the Khaganates. They gain a +2 racial bonus on combat maneuver checks to grapple and to their CMD against grapple attempts, and can deal lethal damage with unarmed strikes without taking a penalty on the attack roll. This trait replaces Bladegrass Hide.
- **Banded Scales (Mainland):** The Bihra's scales are banded or patched in two colours, which Mainland Bihra see as a mark of good fortune in trade. They choose two colours from the Colour-Scaled list and gain resistance 5 against both matching energy types, but the natural armor bonus from their scales stays at +1. This trait replaces Hard Scales.
- **Raking Claws (Mainland or Durenni):** The Bihra fights with the claws of their hands rather than their jaws. They have two claw attacks that are primary natural attacks and deal 1d4 points of damage (1d3 for Durenni). This trait replaces Powerful Jaws for Mainland Bihra or Monsoon Born for Durenni Bihra.
- **Weather-Wise:** The Bihra was raised in the weather rites at the heart of Bihra shamanism. They gain a +2 racial bonus on Survival checks and on saving throws against wind and weather effects, and can predict the weather for the next 24 hours as a free action without a check. This trait replaces Spirit Tradition.
- **Cold-Blooded Stamina:** The Bihra's slow reptilian body endures where others falter. They can hold their breath for a number of rounds equal to four times their Constitution score, and gain a +4 racial bonus on Fortitude saves against starvation, thirst and hot environments. This trait replaces Darkvision.
