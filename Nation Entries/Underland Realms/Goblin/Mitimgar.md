Once so great and rich it could rival <a href="Pintrixaz" class="internal-link">Pintrixaz</a>, it is ruled by an elected autocrat. It follows <a href="Goblin Mysticism" class="internal-link">Goblin Mysticism</a> and is known for its storyteller cultures and for the smiths who work <a href="Wyrdrock" class="internal-link">Wyrdrock</a>, a strange ore whose secret it guards.</p> <h2>History</h2> <p>The first Mitimgar was built near Timgar Lake in 2580BA by the Timgari goblins. In 2245BA the Vuron zealots, exiled from the civil wars of <a href="Tur Enzal" class="internal-link">Tur Enzal</a>, besieged and burned it in the Day of Pain and went on to found <a href="Tur Vuron" class="internal-link">Tur Vuron</a>. The goblins resettled the lake in 1400BA and founded the second Mitimgar in 1315BA, only for the dwarves of <a href="Ogul-Vosko" class="internal-link">Ogul-Vosko</a> to conquer it in 1201BA and enslave its people for centuries, the beginning of the Granite Rivalry. The goblins were freed in 965BA under the Hinmak Sect of Warriors, the beginning of the Timgar Silver Age, and in 662BA the warlord Opronik led the Vengeance of Mitimgar and annihilated the last cities of Tur Vuron. The Silver Age ended with the Death of the Sects in 445BA, and after the Birth of the End earthquake of about 240BA Mitimgar lost contact with the world for hundreds of years.</p> <h2>The Cave Network</h2> <p>Hobgoblin clans lived beside the goblins within the vast Mitimgar cave network, and <a href="Tirbo" class="internal-link">Tirbo</a> and <a href="Korgaz" class="internal-link">Korgaz</a> were both born in the Dirgae Quarters of Mitimgar. When the <a href="Hobgoblin" class="internal-link">hobgoblins</a> cornered the food supply and threatened to hold the more numerous but weaker goblins hostage, its ruler Kerez-Ik collapsed the tunnel to Urgil Cave, where they were encamped. The Tirbo Clan, cut off from the network, emerged in 690PA in the <a href="Hobgoblin Invasion" class="internal-link">Hobgoblin Invasion</a> of Urland. Cut off from the network, the hobgoblins of what became <a href="Ak-Saray" class="internal-link">Ak-Saray</a> also lost easy access to the ore of Wyrdrock, and their few masters of the craft have been dwindling ever since. In recent years small tunnel paths have been opened between Mitimgar and Ak-Saray, relations between the goblins and the hobgoblins are surprisingly friendly, and the tribes are working to bring help to the city in the Granite Crusade, although the primary cavern connection at Urgil remains collapsed.</p> <h2>The Craft of Wyrdrock</h2> <p>Mitimgar is the home of the craft of <a href="Wyrdrock" class="internal-link">Wyrdrock</a>, the strange ore that becomes a metal of extraordinary toughness in the hands of those who know its secret. The craft comes from the ancient sects of the city and from the legendary Rock Keepers, a tradition of smiths and artists that has endured the Death of the Sects and the many centuries since. Only a select few lines of masters and students know how to bring the ore to its precise heat and quench it to perfection, and a piece quenched the proper way can never be reforged. The tradition has become an oral one and is losing its grip on the modern day.</p> <p>The hobgoblins who lived beside the goblins in the caves produced few such artisans themselves, but some of their great heroes were awarded weapons of Wyrdrock in exchange for their service and for their protection of the goblins and the Rock Keepers. The dwarves of <a href="Ogul-Vosko" class="internal-link">Ogul-Vosko</a> and the gnomes of <a href="Ott" class="internal-link">Ott</a> have long desired the secret, and neither has learned it.</p> <h2>The Granite Crusade</h2> <p>In 862PA Ogul-Vosko marched on Mitimgar in the <a href="Granite Crusade" class="internal-link">Granite Crusade</a>, after a prophecy of victory by the High Priest of Ogul-Makar. The war became a struggle of attrition through the caves around the Modim Cavern, and although Ogul-Vosko won the Battle of Modim Cave in 916PA it lost almost its entire 2nd Army, and the war goes on. Supplied by its undercity settlements across the great lake to its north, Mitimgar is cut off from almost all outside communication and has come to rely on questionable rituals and desperate experiments. The goblins also have Wyrdrock, which they use to great effect against the dwarves, above all in the Dridil, the "invincibles", an elite guard clad in full Wyrdrock armour and armed with Wyrdrock glaives, who are among the most feared and infamous warriors of the Underlands. For Ogul-Vosko the ore is yet another reason to take the city. The enmity between Drow and goblins that began with the Day of Pain still shapes the politics of the Underlands in 927PA.</p>
