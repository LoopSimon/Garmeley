<p>The Kingdom of Naigan, later the Empire of Naigan, the Empire of the Purple Sea or often "the Purple Empire", was a yppori realm that first rose to power during the end of the Golden Rule in the late 5th century. Naigan, then a provincial administration under a Lord within the Golden Empire, followed suit with other regional rulers and exploited the weakness caused by internal stability and external pressures within the Golden Empire and in 492PA, Lord Aung Shant declared himself the King of Naigan. </p>
<p>Over the course of its rule Naigan would become to be the greatest Yppori realm in recorded history, conquering the entire Purple Sea and eastern Kaito. By 658PA, Bayratha of Naigan was declared the Divine Emperor of the Purple Sea and the envoy of Sochor himself.</p>
<p>Following centuries of strife and plague after the Naigan conquered the safiri cities of the western Antulun Bay, the Imperial City of Kiamar entered a decade long sealing, effectively quarantining itself on the imperial island. During this Period, various powerful warlords arose in the Empire and it gradually began falling apart, many of them initially raised as peacekeepers by the Emperor himself who then later turned on him. In 771PA, Warlord Piri Cao, known as "the Hungering Wave" sacked the Imperial Palace at Kiamar and forced the last Emperor to abdicate, giving effectively leading the lands of the Purple Sea into the Period of the Bleeding Heron.</p>
