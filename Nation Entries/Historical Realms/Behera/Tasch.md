<p>The Ezilon of Tasch was one of the oldest greater human civilisations, like formed during the 10th century BA in Merek, east of the Ilkiztis. The history of ancient Tasch is largely shrouded in mystery, as is their civilisations collapse in the 1st to 2nd century BA. In the current day the <a href="Iz-Almadus" class="internal-link">Emirate of Iz-Almadus</a> has rebuild cities in many of the ruins of old Tasch, including the rebuild Great Palace of Tasch whose construction can be dated back to the 6th century BA. It seems like the Fall of Tasch occurred before the great Ilkizti Emergence during which the ancestors of the modern day Gnomes of the Merek left the mountain range. Interestingly it seems like the people of the ancient culture did not stand in contact with other high cultures such as the Vendili or the yorgosi or zipivi peoples from across the mountains, nor did they worship the great Cult of the Wheel, otherwise known as Ilonism, but instead followed traditions around the worship of a mysterious Blood Deity that included ritual human sacrifice.</p>
