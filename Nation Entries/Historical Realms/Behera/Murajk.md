<p>The Kingdom of Murajk was one of the earliest drow realms in recorded history, and arose around the ancient undercity of Anazirt in the mid 3500s BA. It is notable for its well maintained history in the later Drow state of <a href="Tur Ajk" class="internal-link">Tur Ajk</a>, and for being one of few drow realms that dominated vast surface areas as well. A rigorous slave state, the Murajk kingdom likely only lasted around 200 years before its cities were abandoned and Murajk fell during the so called Age of Thirst, a stark dry period on Behera that the ever growing cities above and below Behera were unable to survive. Alongside great Anazirt, Murajk controlled the greater cities of Elajal and Inalar, were religious celebrations were held to the precursor of what would later become the <a href="Onosiam" class="internal-link">Onosiam</a> Faith.</p>
<p>While Murajk essentially dominated most of the eastern beheran underlands, its surface presence was largely restricted towards the lands of modern day Yorgos, Tusul and some notable cities along the western Za-Jira River. </p>
