<p>The Golden Empire was the realm that ruled <a href="Tal" class="internal-link">Tal</a> from 283PA to 532PA, the first great realm of the <a href="Ashcult" class="internal-link">Ashcult</a> of <a href="Sochor" class="internal-link">Sochor</a> and the age that the Yppori remember as the Golden Rule. It rose out of the Kingdom of Rin, whose king <a href="Laizin" class="internal-link">Laizin</a> converted to the Ashcult in 283PA and was proclaimed Guardian of the Ngalas, and it ruled from the southern city of Fiyang on the river Fohang.</p>
<h2>The Kingdom of Rin</h2>
<p>The Kingdom of Rin was a relatively strong power of the <a href="Era of Thunders" class="internal-link">Era of Thunders</a>, a realm of the southern coast around the city of Fiyang on the river Fohang. While warlords and small kings rose and fell around it, Rin held together long enough to become the heir of the old order. Its last king, <a href="Laizin" class="internal-link">Laizin</a>, converted to the Ashcult in 283PA and was proclaimed Guardian of the Ngalas. With that the kingdom became the Golden Empire, which kept Fiyang as its capital.</p>
<h2>The Golden Rule</h2>
<p>The Golden Empire was an absolute monarchy in which throne and church were bound together. The Emperor was the Guardian of the Ngalas, the protector of the Osafar in the <a href="Great Kiln" class="internal-link">Great Kiln</a> upriver from the capital, and the Ashcult gave his rule the blessing of the god. The Holy Work that the followers of Sochor had begun in 275PA was in time known as the Golden Rule. Its language was Talang and its faith Sochorism, and its provinces were governed by Lords. In 294PA its armies defeated the army of the warlord Tien Jijichan, which ended the <a href="Era of Thunders" class="internal-link">Era of Thunders</a> and brought Tal under one rule once more.</p>
<h2>Decline and Fall</h2>
<p>In time the provinces began to go their own ways. Unrest within them and pressure from the <a href="Safir" class="internal-link">Safir</a> realms in the west weakened the throne, and in 492PA Lord <a href="Aung Shant" class="internal-link">Aung Shant</a> of <a href="Naigan" class="internal-link">Naigan</a> no longer recognised the Golden Rule and declared himself King. Forty years later the channelers of Naigan took Fiyang, the last Golden Emperor was killed, and in 532PA the Golden Empire came to an end.</p>
