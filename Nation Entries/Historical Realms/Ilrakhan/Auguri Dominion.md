<p>The Auguri Dominion was a union of tribes and settlements under the control of a shamanistic and magical elite of arcane scholars and warlords. It rose in 295BA, controlled vast stretches of <a href="Meadland" class="internal-link">Meadland</a> and parts of <a href="Othirdon" class="internal-link">Othirdon</a> and <a href="Urland" class="internal-link">Urland</a>, and fell in 260PA. It is the direct predecessor of the old Meadi kingdoms and so the ancient root of modern <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a>.</p>
<h2>The Rule of the Sages</h2>
<p>The Dominion was ruled from Praum and the Darkwater Falls by the Sages of Ralfantis, the religious and spiritual successors of the godlike Augur Ralfantis, whose teachings on the marriage of the arcane and the divine are known as <a href="Augurism" class="internal-link">Augurism</a>. Their power rested on an incredible monopoly on magic and on carefully built up structures of reliance among the tribal warlords, whom they played against each other with impressive diplomacy and cunning, and over the centuries these structures grew into a system of magocratic dominance. Around 69PA the Stone of Burd was raised, which records the classic invocation of the Augurs, still taught at Korsfall as the <a href="Auguric Rite & Autonomous Spells" class="internal-link">Auguric Rite</a>.</p>
<p>The Sages ruled over many peoples, and most of them kept their own gods. The tribes of the west and north worshipped various constellations of natural deities and spirit gods, commonly referred to as the "Green Family", while the south and east held to the <a href="Turvik Paganism" class="internal-link">Turvik gods</a>. The philosophical and intellectually advanced teachings of Ralfantis were practised only in a minority of communities around Praum and the Darkwater Falls. In the hills of what would later become Gisvale there lived a culture of sedentary hill-burg builders, already old and still thriving in the later ages of the Dominion, whose close ties to northern Urland and the Turvik gods set them apart from their Meadi and Weadi cousins.</p>
<h2>War and Fall</h2>
<p>The Auguri are notable for having resisted the unprecedented conquests of the <a href="Rakh" class="internal-link">Rakhian Empire</a>. The Sages united the warring tribes of the Meadi and Weadi under a single banner, and at the Battle of Gisfurt in 88BA the Army of Zunirel was destroyed, the worst defeat Rakh had suffered in centuries. The <a href="Sword of the Abjurer" class="internal-link">Sword of the Abjurer</a> is said to be a work of the Sages from the struggle against Rakh. Later, between 159PA and 185PA, the Dominion fought the Auguri-Thirdoni Wars against <a href="Thirdonis" class="internal-link">Thirdonis</a>, beyond the Urdfall Peaks.</p>
<p>The Dominion did not fall to a foreign army. The arrival of Gethricism played a large part in its fall, and with it the monopoly on magic that the rule of the Sages rested on wore away. In 260PA the Circle of the Sages was broken, an event the Meadi remember as the Breaking of the Circle, and the petty kingdoms of Korsfall, Morelle and Rulais rose in its place, followed by Gisvale in 273PA.</p>
<h2>Legacy</h2>
<p>Torvald the Tall, an Auguri general, became the first Lord of Korsfall, and Korsfall kept the reverence for medicae, scholars and mages that is still a mark of the Meadi, from the Auguric studies at <a href="Korsfall College" class="internal-link">Korsfall College</a> onward. Augurism itself survives in small communities in the north and west of Meadrath and in <a href="Cimfon" class="internal-link">Cimfon</a>. <a href="Dirneux" class="internal-link">Dirneux</a>, <a href="Morelle" class="internal-link">Morelle</a>, <a href="Mear" class="internal-link">Mear</a>, <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a>, <a href="Lesmere" class="internal-link">Lesmere</a>, <a href="Weisch" class="internal-link">Weisch</a>, <a href="Zmalig" class="internal-link">Zmalig</a>, the <a href="Freelands" class="internal-link">Freelands</a> and <a href="Ott" class="internal-link">Ott</a> all name the Dominion as their predecessor.</p>
