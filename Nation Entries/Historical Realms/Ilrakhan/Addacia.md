<p>Addacia, usually divided into the periods of the old, middle and later Empire, refers to the great consolidation of human city states around the Ancient Iza River during the Age of Iron. Addacia is in many ways the precursor of the realms of <a href="Rean" class="internal-link">Rean</a> and <a href="Hainach" class="internal-link">Hainach</a> and represented the connective tissue for the two powerful birthplaces of civilisations on the eastern and western coasts of northern Behera. The peoples of the time are often referred to as Proto-Reani in a historical context but records shows they were likely calling themselves Izian or Izani, named after the River Iza. </p>
<p>Addacia fought a series of Great wars spanning multiple centuries with the dominant Empire of <a href="Rakh" class="internal-link">Rakh</a> to whom it would ultimately fall during the 3rd century BA.</p>
