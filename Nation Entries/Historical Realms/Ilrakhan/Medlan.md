It was founded in 339PA by Toskar, who declared himself Lord of the Highlands, and it was ruled from Wormill on the <a href="Trinmo" class="internal-link">Trinmo</a> by the Lords of Medlan until the Crown of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a> broke it up in 682PA. For most of its history it was a country not unlike Korsfall, <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a> or <a href="Weadland" class="internal-link">Weadland</a>, with its own lords, its own faith and its own quarrels, and it is remembered above all for Diedrik, the Lord of Wormill who took the throne of Gisvale in 589PA. Its lands are now the Duchies of <a href="Risfalt" class="internal-link">Risfalt</a> and <a href="Wargel" class="internal-link">Wargel</a>.</p> <h2>The Lords of the Highlands</h2> <p>Toskar was a warlord of the Highland marches, and the realm he formed took its seat at Wormill. Its people were the Rhilmer of the hills, and they were never particularly Auguric. The Gethric faith had come to Meadland in the years after the fall of the <a href="Auguri Dominion" class="internal-link">Auguri Dominion</a>, and Medlan was very early to take it. The Lords of Medlan between Toskar and Diedrik are not named in the records that survive, but for some two and a half centuries they quarrelled with the Kings of Gisvale over the valley of the Trinmo.</p> <h2>Diedrik and Gisvale</h2> <p>In 589PA Diedrik, the Lord of Wormill, supplanted Tibor II "the Feeble" and took the throne of Gisvale at Giselstatt. Many histories paint him as an archvillain, for many reasons, and the victors write history, but the simpler reason that Gisvale fell to him is that it was rich and weak. Its king was bound perpetually to his chambers, its realm was defenceless, and its lords were greedy and isolated, resting on their legacy. For some seventy years the Lords of Medlan held <a href="Giselstatt" class="internal-link">Giselstatt</a> as Kings of Gisvale. Diedrik was followed in 622PA by his son Ringil I, who had married a Urien princess, and both are recorded among the <a href="Rulers of the Meadi" class="internal-link">Rulers of the Meadi</a>.</p> <p>It was the <a href="War of Trout and Drake" class="internal-link">War of Trout and Drake</a> that broke the house. When the Gismar loyalists crossed the Trinmo in 631PA the highland vassals of Medlan grew restless under Ringil, and the House von Praum and the House von Buhr stood with the loyalists and the House von Kor of Korsfall. In 655PA Wormill, the first seat of Diedrik, fell to Count Heinrich von Lot within days of the battle that cost the Gismar line its last two princes. Ringil died in 660PA without a credible successor, and the line of Diedrik was extinguished.</p> <h2>The End of Medlan</h2> <p>The lands of Medlan passed to the Crown with the union of Korsfall and Gisvale in 664PA, and in 682PA King Gildher II broke them up. The west went to the von Praum as the Duchy of Risfalt, and the east, with Wormill, to the von Buhr as the Duchy of Wargel, the two houses that had stood with the loyalists. The name of Medlan faded within a generation, and no arms of the Lords of Medlan are remembered. Wormill kept a quieter memory of the realm: the master mason <a href="Cearic" class="internal-link">Reynar Cearic</a> was born there in 577PA, a dozen years before Diedrik took Giselstatt.</p>
