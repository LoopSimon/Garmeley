<p>Wermia also known as the Kingdom of Wermia was an old realm that infamously fell during the Demonic <a href="The Incursion" class="internal-link">Incursions</a>  of the early 3rd century, its modern day its central territories have largely been deemed uninhabitable while its outskirts are ruled and under occupation by the <a href="Order of the Silver Wheel" class="internal-link">Order of the Silver Wheel</a>, the <a href="Nuv Werm" class="internal-link">Trytorium zi Werm</a> or the City of Jallbreg.</p>
<p>Wermia is dominated by the Weirwood, a now petrified large region of forest supposedly haunted by evil spirits, daemons and all sorts of horrors. South of the Woods lies the Pilgrims Path, guarded by the Knights of the Silver Wheel from their Fortress at Castle Nurk that has connected Urland to Vihr for centuries and protects those devout souls seeking the passage to holy <a href="Iztaw" class="internal-link">Iztaw</a>. North of the Woods lie the Blind Heights and the lone peak of Hagshorn on the peninsular of the same name, crowned by the trading city of Jallbreg that is striving for independence from its former overlords in Baeling in modern times.</p>
<p>In the times before the Incursion Wermia was one of, if not the most advanced realm this far north, dominated by an incredibly advanced magical elite for its time, much like the Augurs of Meadland, and ruled by a Ras from the walled city of Werm. For a detailed history of the Fall of Wermia and the Incursions see <a href="The Incursion" class="internal-link">here</a>.</p>
