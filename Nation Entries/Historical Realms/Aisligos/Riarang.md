<p>The Empire of Riarang emerged following the consolidation of the western hives, around two centuries after the end of the famines and droughts caused by the eruption of the Uraz had subsided. They manifested western Viktorai ideals within a strict hierarchy and are widely considered the first independent Pharang civilisation to create a great realm for themselves. Their Capital was build around the ancient <a href="Aldergrounds" class="internal-link">Aldergrounds</a> of Ri, whom the Pharang renamed to Riama. </p>
<p>The Empire fell in the mid 8th century, following nearly 150 years of decline as various philosophies had begin to sprout advocating for and against the usage and even worship of ancient <a href="Alder" class="internal-link">Alder</a> technologies. The warlike and traditionalist followers of Duria, the later first Bladequeen of the Path of Duria, emerged as the victorious faction in the ensuing struggle at the very end of the Empire of Riarang and would make the old capital of the great hives their new seat of power. </p>
