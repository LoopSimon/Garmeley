<p>The Great Empire of Tur Enzal is often cited as one of the earliest drow civilizations in history, together with the more southern Civilization of Anazirt that would eventually transition into the Kingdom of <a href="Murajk" class="internal-link">Murajk</a>. While its cities were formed much earlier around 3800BA, the Empire itself as a united entity is usually dated towards 2900BA and underwent drastic cultural, political and religious changes throughout its lifespan. The so called "Great Flood" that destroyed the ilrakhani proto Goblin civilizations would also spill the Doom for the Cities of Tur Enzal, that were all located within the "Womb of the World", also known as the Enzalad, from which the Empire and civilization derived its name. The Enzalad was essentially an enormous cave, stretching under modern day Urland, Vihr, Nostroya and Rakh up towards the Torn Sea and Meadland and is today known as the Obsidian Desert, the great underlands sea, only few settlements of the modern day underlands are still bordering the Enzalad, such as Mitimgar while the holds of Xer-Kol and Ogul-Vosko barely stretch deep enough to find themselves the outskirts of what is now an eternal watery grave. </p>
<p>The High-Civilization of the Enzali, later the Empire of Tur-Enzal, is only known within the records of those drow that survived its flood, those who fled north would eventually emerge in far Jeturlund and slowly adapt to the climate, becoming the <a href="Aelf" class="internal-link">Aelf</a>; Those who fled east, would join other drow habitations and settlements in the far east and eventually grow into the niryd underlands civilization of <a href="Tur Aman" class="internal-link">Tur Aman</a>; Those who fled west would join with the Drow of the Sundered Coast, the underlands below the Smoking Sea Coast, being in eager exchange with the precursors to the later established <a href="Rogyn" class="internal-link">Elfrealm of Rogyn</a>; Finally those who fled south joined their cousins in the southern Empire of <a href="Tur Ajk" class="internal-link">Tur Ajk</a>, and it is through their tales and records we know most about the enzali civilization, their culture, technology and history.</p>
<p>Over a thousands years before the Great Flood however, at the height of the Enzali civilization, a great conflict of faith was fought within the Empire. A group of religious zealots who had elevated one of the deities of the Enzal Pantheon, a figure known as <a href="Vuron" class="internal-link">Vuron</a> above all others and claimed to be their chosen ones. The bloody civil war, mostly retold in the mythological "Tale of Zarider" centuries later by enzali refugees in Tur Ajk, would end with the defeat and exodus of the Vuron Zealots setting up the Post Vuroni Imperial Age that would end in the Great Flood. The exiled Vuroni would, after years of conflict with their enzali cousins and the Goblins of the so called First Mitimgar, form their own cities in the tunnel systems connecting the Enzalad Cave with the far eastern stretches of the habitable underlands in what would become known as <a href="Tur Vuron" class="internal-link">Tur Vuron</a>. </p>
<p>The most significant and surviving fact of the Enzali drow culture was their arcane and technological expertise, being able to infuse stones and minerals with live and purpose and vice versa, and even able to create powerful and unheard of relicstones that would allow them to communicate via thought alone, sharing memories, dreams and emotions as other empires would words. As far as we know the process to create these relics, now called the Irenzali, is lost to time and only a few of these cognitively empowering amulets and rings have survived the Great Flood, although it is said that at the height of the ancient Civilization almost every citizen was able to mend their thoughts and dreams with each other, wearing the precious relics like mundane clothing.</p>
