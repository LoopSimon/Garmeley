<p>The Empire of Tur Ajk was the largest independent drow Empire of the east beheri underlands, following in the footsteps of the Civilization of Anazirt and the Kingdom of <a href="Murajk" class="internal-link">Murajk</a>. They stood in contact with the various elven dynasties, the hold of Fogor-Kol and many of the human Marivali of the time, and projected full autonomy over their undercities. It was in Tur Ajk that the <a href="Onosiam" class="internal-link">Doctrine of Onosiam</a> was first formulated and would still be practiced long after its fall before it was eventually subsumed and joined with the modern day <a href="Elven Paragonworship" class="internal-link">Elven Paragon Cults</a>. </p>
<p>Tur Ajk was in exchange with the Great central Drow Empire of <a href="Tur Enzal" class="internal-link">Tur Enzal</a> to their north and accepted thousands of refugees following its demise during the Great Flood. Through these refugees records and tales, found within modern day beheri undercities, is how scholars are able to reconstruct the little we know about Tur Enzal, its great civil war, the exodus of the <a href="Tur Vuron" class="internal-link">Tur Vuroni</a> and the technological achievements made by their civilization that in many parts would find their way to Tur Ajk as well.</p>
<p>During the rule of the Alpard Dynasty, specifically under Alparaz II and Alparaz III, the undercities would come more and more in conflict with the powerful surface dynasty, and eventually be conquered in a series of wars. The last free and great Empire of the Drow in Behera would fall and its citizens be enslaved for hundreds of years under the so called "high elven" rule, until the great Meter Liberation of 276BA, during which Meter "the Great" II, set free and elevated all drow citizens, creating the foundations for the modern day dual administrations above and below the surface of the world.</p>
