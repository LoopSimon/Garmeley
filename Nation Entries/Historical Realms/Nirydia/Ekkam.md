<p>The Omna of Ekkam, omna meaning realm in Lihbare, was a <a href="Bihra" class="internal-link">Bihra</a> realm on the shores of the Citrine Sea that rose in the lands left behind by the <a href="Vol" class="internal-link">Empire of Vol</a>. From its capital at modern day Relloril it ruled the islands of the Citrine Sea, among them Magola, Fenmyr and the Aurbones. Ekkam reconquered the Citrine Swamps and the Ganoa Valley from the <a href="Azuvke" class="internal-link">Khatrym of Azuvke</a> and founded the city of <a href="Jink" class="internal-link">Jink</a> on Lake Ganoa in 433PA. The realm was conquered and made subject by the <a href="Luchun Khaganate" class="internal-link">Luchun Khaganate</a> in 551PA, twenty-seven years before its longer lived neighbour, the <a href="Az" class="internal-link">Omna of Az</a>, met the same fate.</p>
