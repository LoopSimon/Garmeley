<p>Umo was a realm of eastern Nirydia that held the lands later divided between <a href="Uriston" class="internal-link">Uriston</a> and <a href="Nessos" class="internal-link">Nessos</a>. In its later years it fell under the rule of the witch king Zologos, who commanded hordes of the undead and crowned himself the reborn Pen Zuhur. His heresy was the cause of the Second and Third <a href="Zuhuri Holy Wars" class="internal-link">Zuhuri Holy Wars</a>, begun in 645PA and 701PA, and Umo fell with him at the end of the Third.</p>
