<p>The Kingdom of Arester was a urien nation located in central Ur during the Age of Silver that later was subsumed by Garmeley and Urland, modern day Eldmar and Urbridge have kept many of the Arester traditions alive since the Fall of Urland following the Hobgoblin Invasion. </p>
<p>The Kingdom goes back to the Province of Ares, named after the Ares Tribe of eastern Urland that was subjugated by the rakhi wars of conquest in 310BA, alongside the tribes of Garm. Almost nothing is known about the pre-rakhian history of the Ares Tribe and their sub cultures, who made up the population of the lands of Ur, as many records of their likely druidic and shamanistic societies were destroyed following the rakhi conquests.</p>
<p>The Arester Kingdom was based around the Eld Valley, the lands north of the Melm and the northern Lock, with its largest city being the capital of Whitelute. Although the Arester Kings were travelling their realm for most of the year in a large royal caravan holding court and administrating their subjects in all corners of the Realm. Arester upheld and practiced various knightly traditions, with many chivalric orders being more powerful than any local noble, putting pressure and exerting influence on the Nation. The largest of them, the Knights of the Gryphon, would later on reform themselves following the Splintering of Urland in the 8th century as the Order of Urbridge.</p>
