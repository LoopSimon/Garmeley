<p>The Auguri Dominion was a Union of tribes and settlements under the control of a shamanistic and magical elite of arcane scholars and warlords, it controlled vast stretches of <a href="Meadland" class="internal-link">Meadland</a> and parts of <a href="Othirdon" class="internal-link">Othirdon</a> and Urland between 295BA and 260PA.</p>
<p>The Auguri are notable for having resisted the excessive and unprecedented conquests of the <a href="Rakh" class="internal-link">rakhian Empire</a>, going so far as destroying the Army of Zunirel at the Battle of Gisfurt in 88BA. The Sages of Ralfantis, the religious and spiritual successors of the godlike figure, united a warring tribes of the various Meadi and Weadi peoples behind a single banner in an act of impressive diplomacy and cunning understanding in how to play the powerful Warlords to repeal the rakhi invaders and continue to exert their influence over the tribal hierarchies onwards. Slowly the carefully built up structures of reliance and their incredibly monopoly on magic allowed the Auguri Sages to construct a century long system of magocratic dominance.</p>
<p>Although under the control, at least to various and largely decentralized levels, of the Auguri Sages themselves, many of the tribes worshipped various constellations of natural deities and spirit gods, commonly referred to as the "Green Family" in western and northern Meadland or the <a href="Turvik Paganism" class="internal-link">turvik Gods</a> in the southern and eastern lands, the largely philosophical and intellectually advanced teachings of Augur Ralfantis were only practiced in a minority of communities around modern Day Praum and the Darkwater Falls from where the Sages ruled the Dominion.</p>
