<p>The Free City of Korwyn os found at the banks of the Gwar River north of the <a href="Rogyn" class="internal-link">Rogyn</a> Forest, it is an othiri nation controlled by ancient lisseani nobility, specifically ruled under the old line of Ab Korwyn. Following the peaceful dissolution of the old Kingdom of Lissean and the Union of its people under the Banner of the <a href="Lissvalley" class="internal-link">Lissvalley Federation</a>in 713PA, dissenting elements of the Lisseani establishment, primarily the powerful nobles of the largely autonomous south, rejected notions of a free Union of Othiri and declared themselves independent from the Federation, becoming the modern day state of Korwyn.</p>
<p>Korwyn is largely isolated and entertains moderate relationships with its former cousins to the north, while obviously steering largely clear of the elfs of Rogyn. In recent decades it has more and more aligned itself with the Lords of <a href="Erlmondy" class="internal-link">Erlmondy</a> to its west who similarly value strong noble traditions, although the othiri are wary to be subsumed by the significantly larger and richer neighbour. Wether or not the City can survive autonomously and prosper in its future remains to be seen, considering hostilities in Sertova and Othirdon are beginning to bubble to the surface once again.</p>
