<p>Cimfon is a Locni Maelzad that was declared as result of the Battle of Wightswound in the Alvenish Woods, based in western Ilrakhan within the region of <a href="Othirdon" class="internal-link">Othirdon</a> they have remained one of the stronger powers within the Region, working hard to restore the glory of the old Kingdom of <a href="Thirdonis" class="internal-link">Thirdonis</a> that ruled the lands in ages past. Cimfon has historically actively opposed Meadi influence and attempts of force across the Urdpeaks to take influence within Othirdon.</p>
<p>The Maelzad is a founding member of the so called "Visian League", aiming to protect its members from imperialist attacks by neighbouring countries, primarily Meadrath, together with Erlmondy and Lissvalley.</p>

<p>A large majority of Cimfons population is made up of locni humans, but substantial halfling and dwarven minorities can be found within its larger towns or the mountainous eastern parts of the Maelzad as well. Cimfons largest city is the harbour of Alvenish, with the historic Capital of Afgillion and the city of Ledfyr making up the other two greatest population centres. The country is defined by the contrast between Highlands, Lowlands and the Isles of the Hawks Bay. The central Retois Woods are the most prominent geographic mark and dominate the Cimfon Lowlands while the Highlands to the east make up the harder to farm and work lands, with mining being the dominant industry here. The Fortress of Cherelais, the headquarters of the <a href="Knights of the Green Tree" class="internal-link">Knights of the Green Tree</a> are situated in the south-east of the Realm, holding the Cherelais Pass.</p>
<p>The defining event of Cimfons modern history is the <a href="Meadi Othiri War" class="internal-link">Meadi-Othiri War</a> of 824-827PA, in which the Kingdom of Meadrath invaded through the Arvale Pass in an attempt to seize Ballina and establish a Visian Sea port. The opening of the war was a catalogue of failure as Skyroar Castle fell and Sir Berengar Harffan was taken prisoner by  <a href="Rivalt du Chian" class="internal-link">Rivalt du Chian</a> in the wars first engagement. Later the coalition of southern Locni lords under Herz Ascar of Irin was devastatingly beaten at the Battle of Arvale by a Meadi army that fielded five knights for every Locni they could assemble. That Cimfon did not simply collapse under these early blows owed everything to two things: First that the highland terrain west of the pass, which rendered Meadraths cavalry advantages largely irrelevant and allowed the Maelzads nobility to wage the grinding, attritional harassment campaign that slowed the Meadi advance to a crawl across nearly two years. And second the <a href="Knights of the Green Tree" class="internal-link">Knights of the Green Tree</a> under Lord Fionnor Ebonfoil, who held the Cherelais Pass throughout and prevented any flanking movement through the south.</p>
<p>When the <a href="Lissvalley" class="internal-link">Lissvalley Federation</a> entered the war in 825PA, the coalition that assembled before Ballina at the Battle of Red Oak represented the full extent of what Cimfon and its allies could put in the field. Mael Sidgwyn I "the Old Hawk" died in the melee at the hands of the Smaragd Knight, Count Adalbert von Lindsfall, whose own death followed minutes later at Ebonfoils hand in a duel that both armies watched in awe. Giselher I of Meadrath, donning armour late in the day and leading a personal charge that briefly threatened to reverse the engagement, was brought down by a Locni footman and carried from the field. Meadrath retreated through the Arvale. Sidgwyns daughter Vael Rhiannon I "the Tall" succeeded him immediately and oversaw both the peace negotiations and the diplomatic work that formalized the wartime  cooperation into the Visian League, the pact whose existence would become her fathers legacy.</p>
<p>The wars domestic fallout within Meadrath, the Fiseux Uprising, the fall of Morelle, and political instability, further secured Cimfons western frontier by consuming Meadi political attention for years afterward. Cimfon emerged from the war poorer, its army diminished and its ruler dead, but in possession of a formal multilateral guarantee of its independence backed by the two largest powers in Othirdon and Sertova combined.</p>
