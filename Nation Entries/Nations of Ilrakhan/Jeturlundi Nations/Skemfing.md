It lies amid the Frozen Sea and the glaciated mountain ranges of the Skemfing peninsula and has been ruled since 129PA by <a href="Tragna" class="internal-link">Queen Tragna Reasna</a>, who is said to have promised her heart to beings of Night and Ice to safeguard her people.</p> <h2>Origins</h2> <p>The Aelf descend from <a href="Drow" class="internal-link">Drow</a> refugees of <a href="Tur Enzal" class="internal-link">Tur Enzal</a>. When the Great Flood destroyed that empire in 1090BA, one group of survivors fled north, and in 1013BA, in an event the old records call the Promise of Resar, they emerged in the far northern lands of ice eternal. Over the following centuries they adapted to the cold and to the permanent seasonal darkness and light, in part by deliberate magical means, and by 245BA the refugees had fully assimilated and turned into the northern Aelf. For centuries they were organised in clans, and from 108BA the clans began to unify militarily. When a sickness killed most of the surface elves of Ilrakhan in 2PA, the Aelf of the far north were barely impacted.</p> <h2>Queen Tragna</h2> <p>In 123PA the Aelf Tragna rose as the prominent warlord of the north, and in 129PA she became Queen of Skemfing, the birth of the realm. She was born in 99PA and still reigns in 927PA, long past any elven lifespan. Aelf tradition holds this to be the result of her bargain with the beings of Night and Ice, a pact that outside scholars treat with considerably more scepticism, although they cannot easily explain her continued life. Her line is the Reasna, and over her long life she has taken many husbands and sired many princes, among them Laettr, Skemril and Ravel, and including short lived humans such as the hero Ralder, father of Skemril and Laettr.</p> <p>Both of those sons stand at the centre of the <a href="Fjorisjom Saga" class="internal-link">Fjorisjom Saga</a> of 201PA. <a href="Skemril" class="internal-link">Skemril</a>, Prince of the Aelf, joins three other heroes in an oath to rescue the stolen Gudrun from the giants of Jeturlund, and his elder brother <a href="Laettr" class="internal-link">Laettr</a> confronts the heroes on the island chain of Borsgad to keep Skemril from the quest in the name of their mother's line. The brothers fight for three days before Laettr breaks his staff and lets Skemril sail north. Both princes have long passed away.</p> <h2>The Land</h2> <p>Skemfing is a land of extraordinary harshness, of glaciers, extreme cold and the Frozen Sea. The Aelf are fully surface adapted and have been for centuries. The cave systems of Jeturlund are geologically unstable and host no large Aelf settlements, though small isolated communities persist in the deep places where the old Drow instinct for the subterranean has never entirely faded. Above ground the Aelf contend with the great arctic giants, whose territories overlap with and frequently contest their own.</p>  <p>The Aelf sail on icy northern voyages, and the Vannsi of the <a href="Zren" class="internal-link">Zren</a> to their south share with them the practical knowledge of arctic survival. Wandering Tideseers of the <a href="Vannsi" class="internal-link">Vannsi</a> are sought after on Aelf voyages into the frozen seas. The traders of <a href="Juelfluk" class="internal-link">Juelfluk</a> hold a monopoly on the little exchange that is conducted with the Aelf of Skemfing.</p>
