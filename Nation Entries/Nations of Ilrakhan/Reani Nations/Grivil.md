What started as a haven for bandits, mercenaries and outlaws from the surrounding realms has become a formidable city of independent and mostly free citizens, and along with <a href="Hainach" class="internal-link">Hainach</a> it is actively attempting to recolonise the wastes from the mainland.</p> <h2>Origins</h2> <p>The Windy Rocks Highlands were de jure the land of Hainach, successor of the <a href="Rean" class="internal-link">Kingdom of Rean</a>, which counted the mines and settlements on their southern, south-eastern and eastern sides among its fiefs. The hill folk and miners of the heights dreaded the rule and order of the lowlands. Once a year the Prince's men rode up into the hills to collect a heavy tax, for which the hill folk saw neither reason nor sympathy. Grivil grew as a town of such disgruntled communities. Outlaws, bandits and mercenaries found shelter there, and so did rural communities from the lands south of the Trinmo and refugees from the Kerlaen Wilderness who had no part in the robber barons of that country. In 847PA the Grivil Council formed, and the haven became a polity.</p> 
<p>Grivil is governed by the Syndicate Council, with a baron at its head, currently <a href="Polgo Fiago" class="internal-link">Polgo Fiago</a>, who made his name by quelling the racial unrest against the hobgoblins of the city and is greatly respected by them. No crown ever granted the title, which is why the barony calls itself free. The council seats the syndicates of the city, the companies of former bands and mercenaries that grew out of the haven, each of which pursues its own ventures across the region.</p> <h2>People of Grivil</h2> <p>Grivil is home to a surprisingly large number of <a href="Hobgoblin" class="internal-link">hobgoblins</a>, among the Reani, Urien and Garmish that make up most of its people. Many famous figures are based in the city, each called a syndic, a robber baron, a brigand captain or an entrepreneur, depending on who is speaking. Rezro Immar, actually a hobgoblin, is one of them. Thara the Blitz is a Garmish criminal and drug trader, and Yerdin Lorokop is a Reani smuggler and crimelord, originally from Hainach.</p>  <p>The people of Grivil keep both the <a href="Kindly Lords" class="internal-link">Kindly Lords</a>, the spirit gods of the Reani and Garmish, and <a href="Gethricism" class="internal-link">Gethricism</a>, the faiths of the realms that sent their outlaws. Their arms show a head under a fox mask on a shield divided in blue and orange. The fox is a classical image of Reani and Garmish folklore, associated with the way of the road, restlessness and safe journey, and it appears in many of the more entrenched cults of the Kindly Lords.</p> <h2>The Thieves War</h2> <p>By 890PA Hainach was receiving increasing reports of the upstart syndicates of Grivil claiming the Windy Rocks as their own territory. The Prince of Hainach set out to crush these thieves and vastly underestimated them. Among the soldiers of the Princely Army was <a href="Adral Fidrakis" class="internal-link">Adral Fidrakis</a>, later Sheriff of Kontini. The army was dragged into a brutal war in the mountain passes and crags of country it did not know, and in the end the Prince was captured. The Prince was released only after swearing a blood oath, known as the Stolen Oath, never again to send taxmen or soldiers into the hills. Since then the brigands of Grivil no longer raid the prosperous regions of the Vale of Rean, at least not when they can claim to come from elsewhere.</p> <h2>The Wastes</h2> <p>Hainach is more rival than partner, yet both are among the few settled nations that attempt to reclaim the land of the fallen Empire of <a href="Rakh" class="internal-link">Rakh</a>. Grivil is the starting point of the private settlement ventures of many syndicates and companies, and its mercenary bands provide safe travel through the Kerlaen Wilderness and the nearest stretches of the Wasteland. The Wasteland itself is deadly and its rivers carry a sickness, so the settlers keep to the more habitable stretches along its eastern margin and on the Adaccian Plateau, moving through landscapes thick with ancient remains.</p> <h2>The  Garmish Succession Crisis</h2> <p>Grivil mostly stays out of the <a href="Garmish Succession Crisis" class="internal-link">Garmish Succession Crisis</a> that divides its neighbour <a href="Garmeley" class="internal-link">Garmeley</a> to the north-east. It is culturally closer to the rich southerners and sympathises with them, yet they and their Hainach allies are also the people it stands in conflict with most. <a href="Hynek of Forrington" class="internal-link">Hynek of Forrington</a>, who as the protector of the south in Garmeley and <a href="Forrington" class="internal-link">Forrington</a> had met Baron Polgo Fiago several times, was assassinated in February 927PA, and rumour says his assassins found refuge in Grivil or were sent from it. That says more about a city that is home to many different factions and peoples than about the barony itself.</p>
