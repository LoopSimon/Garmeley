<p> Selbia is one of the major Vadcy within Vihr, it holds parts of the Expanse and is the southernmost Vadcy of the Realm, with a strong military culture. Its second largest city, Itarsky, is held by the Gri Michal of House Zrimiz.</p>
<h2>Geography</h2>
<p>Selbia lies in the south of the Vihri Expanse, south of <a href="Breza" class="internal-link">Breza</a>, with the capital Ibozka directly south of Trydlew. The Ibor River runs through the heart of the Vadcy. In the south it reaches the valley of the Pilv, the river valley of Fairriver and the lands upriver of it, which Vihr took from <a href="Rean" class="internal-link">Rean</a> at the end of the Rean war in 306PA, a country of hills, mines and good arable land. Its southern tip is the town of Fairriver. Beyond it lie the Grevy mountains and the dangerous ogre country around them. The Fairriver Valley is the western road from Vihr into <a href="Grevenna" class="internal-link">Grevenna</a>.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Itarsky is older than Ibozka, and in the legends of the Sarloni who live there it was the dogs of the river that guarded the first settlers from the wild ogres. Selbia was one of the eight lordships that marched on Zavlech in 377PA and became hereditary Vadcy in the settlement that followed. <a href="Darmil zi Perliav" class="internal-link">Darmil of Perliav</a>, the hero of the Zuzka wars, died of the Red Pestilence at Ibozka in 497PA. In 529PA the Sarloni Uprising broke out against the lords of Breza, Opuzna and Selbia, and Selbia was instrumental in putting it down.</p>
<p>The military tradition of the Vadcy is the work of House Sribo. In the <a href="Third War of Elbae Aggression" class="internal-link">Third War of Elbae Aggression</a> (805PA to 815PA) Var Rezvyr zi Sribo was the supreme commander of the coalition, and he proved the finest coalition commander of all three wars. He won the Battle of the Crossing, the first coalition victory on Elbae soil, and retook Danipol at the Battle under the Stars. He died at Danipol in 815PA when <a href="Jurahim al-Biad" class="internal-link">Jurahim al-Biad</a> sank the Horad Isthmus. His grandson Feliks held the Vadcy in the <a href="War of Horn and Fang" class="internal-link">War of Horn and Fang</a>. At the 2nd Battle of Tears in 866PA, when the Meadi army held the field after days of fighting, Feliks led the Selbia Brigade around the battle and fell on the Meadi supply convoys, destroying the knight escort and burning the wagons. Meadrath had won the field and could do nothing with it, and peace talks began before the winter.</p>
<p>Fairriver, the monastery town and the home of Saint <a href="Lyandra" class="internal-link">Lyandra</a>, was in danger in the winter of 922PA, when a necromantic curse threatened to take over the town. A party of adventurers known as <a href="Piero & Friends" class="internal-link">Piero & Friends</a> broke it.</p>
<h2>Ruling House and Politics</h2>
<p>The Vadcy is held by House Sribo, whose present head is Var Provim, the grandson of Feliks. As the lord of one of the eight ancient lordships, the Var sits in the <a href="Honoured Assembly" class="internal-link">Honoured Assembly</a>. Selbia is above all the Vadcy of the Selbia Brigade, a standing regiment which is among the most famous bodies of warriors on the continent and is feared far beyond Vihr. Its soldiers retire on generous pensions, and the traditional Selbian red may be worn by the Brigade alone. The second city of the Vadcy, Itarsky, is held by the Gri Michal of House Zrimiz. Fairriver is ruled by its abbey and is practically neutral, though it is technically the southern tip of Selbia.</p>
<h2>Society and Culture</h2>
<p>The people of Selbia are Tloky and Sarloni, and Gethric. The Tloky are the people of the north-west, and the Sarloni live in most of the rest of the Vadcy. The military culture is strong, and a veteran of the Brigade is a respected man in any village. Itarsky is a mostly Sarloni town, called the City of Dogs because of the great number of stone dogs that have been found in it. The arms of the Vadcy show a mounted knight on a red ground. The Bishop of Selbia does not sit in the capital but in the holy city of Mesziev, and the office, held at present by Bishop Urzmyr V, is among the most respected in the Church.</p>
<h2>Economy</h2>
<p>Selbia is farmland and mining country. The Ibor and the Pilv valleys are rich in grain, and the Pilv Valley has long had profitable mines. The Fairriver Valley is the western trade road into Grevenna, and the <a href="Trazlov Consortium" class="internal-link">Consortium</a> keeps an outpost at Fairriver. The pensions of the Brigade are a charge on the Vadcy, and it bears them gladly.</p>
<h2>Places of Note</h2>
<p>Ibozka, on both banks of the Ibor, is the capital. It is richer, more populous and more important than Itarsky, and its landmark is the Pearlywhite Palace, the residence of the Var, which stands on an island in the middle of the river. Itarsky is the older city and the second city of the Vadcy. Mesziev is the holy city and the seat of the Bishop. Fairriver is the abbey town on the Pilv. Edessa Tower, where <a href="Zovac Harlini" class="internal-link">Zovac Harlini</a> once taught, stands in the dangerous country beyond the south of the Vadcy and is claimed both by Vihr and by Grevenna, and neither of them wants the land badly enough to take it yet.</p>
