<p> Szievek is one of the major Vadcy within Vihr, controlling lands in the northern Expanse, it sits between Zavlech itself and the central Grachhyll. Its capital, the city of Szievek itself, is home to the largest University in all Vihr as well as the Eye of Kasimir, a massive astrolabe in the centre of the city.</p>
<h2>Geography</h2>
<p>Szievek lies in the northern Vihri Expanse, on the Lehva River, with the crownlands of Zavlech to the west and the foothills of the Grachhyll to the north-east. The Lehva comes up out of <a href="Levazci" class="internal-link">Levazci</a> to the south-east and turns north at Szievek on its way to the sea at <a href="Trazlova" class="internal-link">Trazlov</a>, so the Vadcy stands on the river road between the heart of the Expanse and the northern coast. Trazlova borders it on the north and <a href="Breza" class="internal-link">Breza</a> on the south. The plains are flat and temperate, and the farmland is rich.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Szievek was one of the cities that the early Zav kings founded or greatly enlarged, planted on the rivers of the Expanse as instruments of royal authority and of the Gethric faith. The ground had known the war before that. In the account of <a href="The Incursion" class="internal-link">the Incursion</a>, the host of the Abyss pushed as far east as the place where Szievek now stands.</p>
<p>Szievek was one of the eight lordships that marched on Zavlech in 377PA, and it took its place among the hereditary Vadcy in the settlement that followed. In those days it was the most powerful and the richest of all the Vadcy, with lands that took in large parts of what are now Trazlova, Levazci and Breza and of the crownlands around Zavlech. Over a century later it gave the realm a king. When the senior line of the House of Zav died out in 501PA, the crown passed to the Duke of Szievek, Kasimir of House Szinmo, a junior branch of the Zav. It was the first time that the lord of a Vadcy had taken the throne, and the Szinmo kept it for fifty-two years: Kasimir I "the Elder", Kasimir II "the Younger", the child Viago VI, called the Boy King, and last Katerina I, who reigned from 532PA until the dynasty ended in 553PA and the Vihri War of Succession began. That war ended in 562PA with the coronation of Zawis "Silverbeard" of House Worocek. The kings are listed in the <a href="Rulers of Vihr" class="internal-link">Rulers of Vihr</a>.</p>
<p>The war cost Szievek most of its greatness. In the war and the settlement that ended it, the Vadcy was cut down by two thirds, and the lands it lost now form parts of Trazlova, Levazci, Breza and the crownlands around Zavlech. House Szinmo kept the rest, and holds it still.</p>
<p>The University made Szievek famous beyond Vihr. By the 8th century it drew students from other lands. Farkiz II, the future Ras of Dalniz, studied there under <a href="Joakim zi Szarbel" class="internal-link">Joakim zi Szarbel</a>, and the Tloky prodigy <a href="Zovac Harlini" class="internal-link">Zovac Harlini</a> studied Natural Sciences at Szievek before he was called to the royal court at twenty-five. In the 9th century the Bishopric of Szievek, one of the most important seats of the Church in all of Ilrakhan, passed to <a href="Alfred the Younger" class="internal-link">Alfred the Younger</a>, who held it from 882PA until his death in 909PA and worked to connect the clerical scholars of Vihr with the arcane academies of the west.</p>
<p>The darkest chapter in the University's history is recent. <a href="Versy Barl" class="internal-link">Versy Barl</a>, a lecturer, carried out experiments in necromancy and biomancy that broke numerous laws and the <a href="Wermian Accords" class="internal-link">Wermian Accords</a>. He was discovered in 922PA, when the remains of a strange beast and the odd behaviour of rats in an abandoned part of the Szievek Sewers caught the attention of a servant. Professor Milsa, the Chief Librarian, was supposedly one of his victims. Barl was never caught, and he is said to have fled east to his homeland in Grachy. He is remembered as the Fleshmonger.</p>
<h2>Ruling House and Politics</h2>
<p>The Vadcy is held by House Szinmo, whose present head is Varina Katerina. She bears the name of the last Szinmo to rule from Zavlech. As the lord of one of the eight ancient lordships, the Varina sits in the <a href="Honoured Assembly" class="internal-link">Honoured Assembly</a>, and she is one of three women among the nine Var, together with the Varinas of Breza and Levazci. The throne's own seat in the Assembly belongs to the Sekretar of Vihr, <a href="Tadeuz Pilsud" class="internal-link">Tadeuz Pilsud</a>, the only member who is not of noble blood, who was born in the city of Szievek in 884PA.</p>
<h2>Society and Culture</h2>
<p>The people of Szievek are Low Vihri and Gethric. The city is the scholarly capital of the realm, and its University is the largest in Vihr. The head of the University, the First Seat, is one of the nine signatories of the <a href="Orenburg Pact" class="internal-link">Orenburg Pact</a>, together with the Rector of <a href="Korsfall College" class="internal-link">Korsfall College</a> and the Kar-Zavr of Vihr. The Chapter of <a href="Mørbrig" class="internal-link">Mørbrig</a> sets itself apart from the great houses of learning, Szievek University among them, by owing nothing to any worldly power. Alfred the Younger lectured at the University and taught the mage Olena Nargu, who was later tried as a criminal. The banner of the Vadcy shows a sheep carrying the banner of Vihr.</p>
<h2>Economy</h2>
<p>Szievek is farming country on a great river. The Lehva carries the produce of the northern Expanse down to the docks of Trazlov, and most of that trade passes through the hands of the <a href="Trazlov Consortium" class="internal-link">Consortium</a>, whose guilds dominate the inland commerce of Vihr. The sheep on the banner point to wool as one of the Vadcy's staples. The University and the Bishopric are the other great employers, and the city supports a crowd of scribes, booksellers and instrument makers besides.</p>
<h2>Places of Note</h2>
<p>Szievek, on the Lehva, is the capital and the one great city of the Vadcy. The University stands at its heart, together with the Eye of Kasimir, the massive astrolabe in the centre of the city, which is named for <a href="Kasimir" class="internal-link">Saint Kasimir the Martyr</a>. The city is the seat of the Bishop of Szievek, at present Mangol I. Beneath the streets run the Szievek Sewers, parts of which have been abandoned. Tienov is another town of the Vadcy, north of the capital toward Trazlov.</p>
