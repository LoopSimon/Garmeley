<p> Situated alongside the former wermian border, Torry is the newest of the major Vadcy within <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Vihr</a>. While geographically the smallest Vadcy of the Realm it holds a sizeable portion of the Vihri population in its coastal settlements. Its creation during the Council of 870 drew criticism from parts of the country's elite, though most of the great nobles welcomed it, as it was seen as a clear move against encroaching church power following sentiments that the clergy had "betrayed and sabotaged" the country during the preceding <a href="War of Horn and Fang" class="internal-link">Great War with Meadrath</a>.</p>
<h2>Geography</h2>
<p>Torry is a narrow Vadcy on the northern coast of Vihr, where the land meets the Torn Sea. It is the smallest of the nine, and the one with the least room to spare. To the west the Fera River and the Neverrest Heights mark the edge of the Wermian lands, which are governed from <a href="Nuv Werm" class="internal-link">Nuv Werm</a>, to the east lie the holdings of <a href="Iztaw" class="internal-link">Iztaw</a>, and to the south the hill country of <a href="Rovgi" class="internal-link">Rovgi</a> begins. The winters are mild and wet, as on all of the Vihri coast, and most of the Vadcy's people live close to the sea.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>For some six centuries the land that became Torry belonged to Iztaw, the holding of the Church, which had been formed in 240PA from Federation and Wermian ground and which was exempt from almost every tax and levy in the realm. That exemption was what turned the country against it. The <a href="War of Horn and Fang" class="internal-link">War of Horn and Fang</a> ended in 866PA with the <a href="Treaty of Uhl" class="internal-link">Treaty of Uhl</a> and the loss of the city, after fifteen years of bloodshed that Iztaw had been spared paying for, and in the bitter years that followed many in Vihr, in the court and the countryside alike, came to believe that the clergy had "betrayed and sabotaged" the war.</p>
<p>The Council of 870 answered that anger by taking land instead of lives. It cut a stretch of the northern coast and its hinterland out of the lands of Iztaw and made it a ninth Vadcy, the first created since the <a href="Honoured Assembly" class="internal-link">Lords' Crisis</a> of 377PA, and it was meant plainly as a check on the Church's growing power. The Church lost extensive holdings. Ras Orian II was on the throne, a secular ruler with little patience for the overreach of Iztaw. After the great war against Meadrath he had seen which of his subjects had stood by the crown and which had not, and he meant to show his power over a clergy and a nobility that were both weaker than they had been. The Council was criticised, but most of the great nobles agreed with it, and many were glad to see limits put on an Iztaw that had been untouchable. The Vadcy zi Torry has ever since been understood as the crown's answer to Iztaw..</p>
<h2>Politics</h2>
<p>The Vadcy is held by House Torry, whose current head is Var Volboris, and the house owes its rise to the Council of 870 in a way that none of the eight ancient lordships do. The Var sits as the ninth and youngest voice in the <a href="Honoured Assembly" class="internal-link">Honoured Assembly</a>, and is generally counted on the throne's side in the long quarrel between Zavlech and Iztaw. The Church has never forgiven the loss, and the boundary drawn in 870PA remains a sore point: tithes, tolls, mills and the rights to pasture along the old line are argued over in every generation, and neither side has ever fully admitted the other's claims.</p>
<h2>Society and Culture</h2>
<p>The people of Torry are mostly <a href="Vihri" class="internal-link">High Vihri</a> and devoutly Gethric. The Vadcy was made against the Church's power and not against its faith, and its lords have never been accused of impiety. Its inland parts share the old ways of the "Higher" Vihri of the hills, while the coast is more mixed, with Wermi families whose forebears came east over the Fera River in the centuries after the Incursions. Torry has also given the <a href="Order of the Silver Wheel" class="internal-link">Order of the Silver Wheel</a> a Paladin in Rekha of Torring, a quiet irony for a Vadcy created to curb the Church that the Order serves.</p>
<h2>Economy</h2>
<p>Torry lives by its coast. Its harbours send out fishing fleets and coastal traders, and like the rest of the northern coast most of the local shipping works within reach of the <a href="Trazlov Consortium" class="internal-link">Consortium</a>. Pilgrims bound for Iztaw, by sea or by the Pilgrims Path through Wermia, pass along its roads and through its ports, and the Vadcy has been happy to charge them for the privilege.</p>
<h2>Places of Note</h2>
<p>Torring is the capital and chief harbour, a seaport on the northern coast where the Var keeps his seat. Inland, the small towns of Udjem and Ilrid lie on the roads that run south toward Pjaz and Rovgi and east toward Iztaw.</p>
