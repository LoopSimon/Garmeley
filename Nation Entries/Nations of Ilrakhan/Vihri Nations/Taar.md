<p>The Gri zi Taar, or simply Taar, is a minor human realm in western Wermia situated between the Wermian Moor, the Taar delta, and Lake Ir. Various small settlements, villages and agricultural communities of the native Taarmen can be found, mostly on the coast of the Bay of Uhl, but the only two major towns in the area are the capital of Taarpel and the town of Irgshil. While officially recognised by the Throne of Vihr as part of its dominion, the Count, or Gri, of Taar acts largely autonomously, governing a culturally unique realm that often finds itself closer to its western neighbours in Uhl than to its eastern overlords in Vihr.</p>
<h2>Geography</h2>
<p>Taar lies on the eastern shore of the Bay of Uhl, in the west of old Wermia. The Wermian Moor stretches behind it, the Taar River runs through the realm to its delta on the Bay, and Lake Ir lies inland. Taarpel is the capital, and Irgshil the only other town of size. The rest of the land is villages, fishing harbours and the farms of the delta. East of the Moor and the Weirwood lies the March of <a href="Nuv Werm" class="internal-link">Nuv Werm</a>, which Taar has little to do with, while the free city of <a href="Uhl" class="internal-link">Uhl</a> lies across the water.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>The Taarmen are Wermi by descent, but they were never part of the organised administration that the Order of the Silver Wheel and Nuv Werm have kept in the east. After the fall of the Kingdom of Wermia they lived on the Moor and the coast, caught between larger powers and the raids of their neighbours. As Uhl grew rich in the centuries that followed, it drew the Taarmen into its trade, and the city's accepting of Vihr as its liege in 735PA brought them a little closer to the Throne.</p>
<p>The War of Horn and Fang changed their position. When Meadrath came for the Bay of Uhl, the Taarmen were on the front line, and when the Treaty of Uhl in 866PA made the city a free city under the joint guarantee of Vihr and Meadrath, the crown at Zavlech needed a piece of the Bay it could hold for itself. Money from Uhl helped the Taarmen to reorganise, and in 879PA Ras Orian II officially recognised the Gri zi Taar, under Gri Bedyrov, as a subject realm of Vihr, giving the crown a permanent and legitimate foothold on the Bay that no treaty could remove.</p>
<h2>Ruling House and Politics</h2>
<p>Taar is held by the Gri of Taar, of House Bedyrov, who rules a Wermian monarchy in all but the name of king. The Gri has no seat in the Honoured Assembly, as the Var of the eight ancient Vadcy have, and he answers to the Throne in name rather than in daily affairs. His real politics are those of the Bay: the favour of Uhl, whose investments have built much of the realm, and the good opinion of the crown, which values the land as a possession no treaty restricts. Taar is not Uhl, and it knows this, but it is the one part of the Bay of Uhl which is unambiguously Vihri.</p>
<h2>Society and Culture</h2>
<p>The Taarmen are a Wermian people who speak Wermian rather than High Vihri, and they are proudly different from their overlords. They are Gethric but not zealous, and the older tales of the Wermian ravens and the lost kingdom are told in every village. Culturally and commercially they look west across the Bay, and the markets of Taarpel trade in the Gryphon of Uhl.</p>
<h2>Economy</h2>
<p>Taar lives from the sea and the delta. The fishing harbours supply the Bay, the farms of the delta grow grain, and the golden mill on the arms of the Gri is a reminder that the milling of that grain is the realm's chief industry. Investments from Uhl have helped to pay for the mills and the harbours. The realm is small and not yet rich, but it grows.</p>
<h2>Places of Note</h2>
<p>Taarpel is the capital and the seat of the Gri. Irgshil is the second town. The Taar delta is the heart of the realm's farmland, and Lake Ir and the Wermian Moor mark its inland edges. The harbours of the Bay of Uhl are its life and its link to the world.</p>
