<p>The Gri zi Taar, or simply Taar, is a minor human realm in western Wermia situated between the Wermian Moor, the Taar delta, and Lake Ir. Various small settlements, villages and agricultural communities of the native Taarmen can be found, mostly on the coast of the Bay of Uhl but the only two major towns in the area are the Capital of Taarpel and the town of Irskel. While officially recognised by the Throne of Vihr as part of its dominion the Count, or Gri, of Taar acts largely autonomous governing a culturally unique realm that often finds itself closer to its western neighbours in Uhl than its eastern overlords in Vihr.</p>
