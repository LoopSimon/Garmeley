<p> The geographically largest Vadcy of <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Vihr</a>, Opuzna nominally controls the southern Expanse and the Ravenleaf Forest, which remains largely uninhabited. It is ruled by the House zi Terjan and vaguely related to the Worocek Dynasty that ruled the Rascy from 562 to 692PA, its capital Opuzna is one of the largest cities within the entire realm. The Vadcy is home to the Truzhi peoples of southern Vihr that make up one of the four largest cultural denominations within the land, and is proud of their regional traditions and history.</p>
<p>While the Ravenleaf Forest is practically part of Opuzna, it is only settled in various border areas, primarily around the River itself, and essentially uninhabitable the further one ventures inside due to the large presence of unbroken tribesmen and various wild monsters; the legendary  <a href="Zuzka" class="internal-link">Zuzka</a> Beasts of Yagund are said to have made their nests here.</p>
