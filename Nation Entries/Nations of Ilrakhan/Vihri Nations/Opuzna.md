<p> The geographically largest Vadcy of <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Vihr</a>, Opuzna nominally controls the southern Expanse and the Ravenleaf Forest, which remains largely uninhabited. It is ruled by the House zi Terjan and vaguely related to the Worocek Dynasty that ruled the Rascy from 562 to 692PA, its capital Opuzna is one of the largest cities within the entire realm. The Vadcy is home to the Wyt peoples of southern Vihr that make up one of the five main cultural groups within the land, and is proud of their regional traditions and history.</p>
<p>While the Ravenleaf Forest is practically part of Opuzna, it is only settled in various border areas, primarily around the River itself, and essentially uninhabitable the further one ventures inside due to the large presence of unbroken tribesmen and various wild monsters; the legendary  <a href="Zuzka" class="internal-link">Zuzka</a> Beasts of Yagund are said to have made their nests here.</p>
<h2>Geography</h2>
<p>Opuzna is the largest of the Vadcy by area, a broad country in the south of the Vihri Expanse. The Opuzna River, one of the three great rivers of the Expanse together with the Lehva and the Tryd, runs south-east from the capital, which stands on the north-western edge of the Ravenleaf Forest. The forest fills much of the south and east of the Vadcy, and north of it lie the Wytlands, the old country of the Wyt. To the east lies the Sarloni realm of <a href="Dalniz" class="internal-link">Dalniz</a>, and in the south the Vadcy reaches the border with <a href="Kherova" class="internal-link">Kherova</a>.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>The Wyt were here long before there was a kingdom. The forest was the seat of <a href="Kortyr" class="internal-link">Kortyr Paleblood</a>, the half-elven High Priest of the Ravenleaf, whose blood cult haunted the southern Expanse until he was buried alive, presumably in 190PA. When Viago of the Zav proclaimed the kingdom in 241PA, the last lords to hold out against him were the Sarloni and Wyt lords of the south, and they were broken at the Battle of Glancz in 251PA, fought near Opuzna. The Vadcy was one of the eight lordships that marched on Zavlech in 377PA.</p>
<p>In the 5th century the city gave its name to a quarrel of the Church. In 458PA Archbishop <a href="Adzul I" class="internal-link">Adzul I</a> of Kherova convened the First Council of Opuzna in the city, then as now one of the great cities of the south, on the road to Kherova. The council wrote the Doctrine of Dualism, which held that divine and arcane power differ in their origin. When the High-Bisop of Iztaw rejected the Doctrine, Adzul called the Second Council of Volazc in 471PA, in a small town of the Vadcy's southern marches on the Kherovan border, chosen on purpose for being far from the great cities. There the bishops issued the Kethric Declaration, which made the Patriarchate of Kherova independent. See <a href="Kethricism" class="internal-link">Kethricism</a> for the full account.</p>
<p>The forest has its own history. In 489PA the hero Darmil of Perliav broke the <a href="Zuzka" class="internal-link">Zuzka</a> and killed their maker, the Ogre Mage <a href="Yagund" class="internal-link">Yagund</a>, and the statue at the Crossing of Darmil stands near the forest in his memory. <a href="Fredric" class="internal-link">Fredric</a>, later a saint, is remembered as the slayer of the Hydra of Ravenleaf. Ogres still live in the forest.</p>
<p>Until the 680s the Vadcy was ruled by House zi Usily. In about 686PA the Usily were found to be plotting against Ras Zawis III, the last of the Worocek kings, and they meant to kill him with the help of mages, in what would have looked like an accident. The plot came to light and the Usily were ruined. The heroes of the affair were alchemists, who were raised higher and higher in standing, and that was the foundation of the <a href="The Alchemists Guild" class="internal-link">Alchemists Guild</a> in 691PA. House Terjan, which is vaguely related to the Worocek, was given Opuzna as the reign was ending, and has held it ever since.</p>
<h2>Ruling House and Politics</h2>
<p>The Vadcy is held by House Terjan, whose present head is Var Ilia. As the lord of one of the eight ancient lordships, the Var sits in the <a href="Honoured Assembly" class="internal-link">Honoured Assembly</a>. The Gris of the Vadcy hold the lands in the south, and two of them are known beyond Vihr. The first is the Gri of Treuka, a castle in the south which the crown granted in 925PA to <a href="Valenty Sykes" class="internal-link">Valenty Sykes</a>, the Grandmaster of the Alchemists Guild, who has never visited it. The second is the Gri of Eskerel, who hired the Velvet Tears mercenaries to pacify the lawless country south of Opuzna in the early 900s.</p>
<h2>Society and Culture</h2>
<p>The people of Opuzna are Wyt, and Gethric, and proud of their regional traditions. The Wyt are known for their love of dance and for the Bolov, a stringed instrument. Tribesmen who have never been broken to any lord live in the depths of the Ravenleaf. The Church has its see at Opuzna, where the Bishop of Opuzna, at present Erboris IV, holds court. The arms of the Vadcy show a red and white unicorn.</p>
<h2>Economy</h2>
<p>Opuzna is farmland and grazing country, and the unicorns of the southern grasslands are as much a part of its wealth as its grain. The river carries goods to the capital, which draws trade from Kherova in the south. The border with Dalniz is a smuggling frontier, and the Kirelli Cartel, which grew out of the Velvet Tears under <a href="Marosz Kirelli" class="internal-link">Marosz Kirelli</a>, is said to have long moved Zondust and Astafar across it. At Retsky, the Dalniz trade city on the border, the drug ring around Count <a href="Provost Mihun" class="internal-link">Provost zi Mihun</a> was broken up in 918PA.</p>
<h2>Places of Note</h2>
<p>Opuzna, on the river at the edge of the Ravenleaf, is the capital, one of the largest cities in the realm and the seat of the Bishop. Volazc is the town in the south where the Second Council met. Treuka is the castle in the south which is held by Sykes, and Eskerel is the rough country south of the capital.</p>
