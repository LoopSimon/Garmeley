<p> Named after the Tlok River, Tloky is a Vadcy of Vihr that lies between the crownlands of Zavlech and the southern Trollback Mountains.</p>
<h2>Geography</h2>
<p>The Tlok River comes down out of the Trollbacks and runs south-east through the valley that bears its name, past the town of Horsk and the capital Tlokyemy toward Perliav. West of the valley the mountains climb toward the dwarven hold of <a href="Xer-Kol" class="internal-link">Xer-Kol</a>, to the south the southern Trollbacks close in again, and to the east the Royal Woods divide the valley from the Tryd country of <a href="Breza" class="internal-link">Breza</a>. Most of the Vadcy's towns are strung along the river.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Tlokyemy is among the oldest cities of the Vihrmen. It grew from a seasonal gathering place into one of the three cities of the old <a href="Vihrmen Federation" class="internal-link">Federation</a>, with Trazlov and Trydlew, and around 130PA the Horn Lords began to assemble there in a formal council. The Federation had no capital by right, but in practice it was Tlokyemy. When Viago of the Zav proclaimed the Kingdom of Vihr in 241PA, the lords who had organised along the Tlok River line refused to submit. They were broken at the Battle of Tlok Valley in 246PA, and the Tloky and the allied communities of the hills came into the new realm.</p>
<p>Tloky was one of the eight lordships that marched on Zavlech in 377PA. Perliav, which grew from a small hamlet at the foot of the castle of its Gri into the prosperous town of today, gave the Vadcy a hero of its own. Darmil of Perliav was born in 443PA as the heir to the castle, and was a famed slayer of beasts. In 489PA he led the expedition of heroes that put an end to the menace of the <a href="Zuzka" class="internal-link">Zuzka</a>, and killed their creator, the Ogre Mage <a href="Yagund" class="internal-link">Yagund</a>, in his lair at Horomur. He was an outspoken supporter of the House of Zav, and died eight years after his victory, in 497PA, of the Red Pestilence. His line still rules Perliav under Gri Moreslav.</p>
<p>The darkest chapter in the history of the valley was the undead occupation. By 545PA the dwarven hold of Xer-Kol had fallen almost entirely under the dominion of Mendizaba, a dwarven necromancer who had achieved lichdom in her mountain stronghold of Xurugam and was raising the dead of the Tlok Valley in skeletal legions. <a href="Tytus" class="internal-link">Tytus</a> arrived in the Tlok settlements ahead of any Church authority and organised a combined force of local Vihri and free dwarves. He broke her host in three engagements in the valley in 545PA, then entered Xurugam alone and destroyed her phylactery. The place where he assembled his forces became the site of the Cathedral of Will in Tlokyemy, the chief place of worship of the saint. He was killed by his sister in 549PA and canonised in 555PA.</p>
<p>Tloky also gave Vihr scholars. <a href="Joakim zi Szarbel" class="internal-link">Joakim zi Szarbel</a>, Bishop of Tlokyemy and an honorary professor of Szievek University, applied arcane principles to divine casting and was an avid supporter of the Gethric Pogroms against the elves. <a href="Zovac Harlini" class="internal-link">Zovac Harlini</a>, born in 728PA as the heir of the Gri of Direszk, became Kar-Zavr of Vihr and the father of Realmatic Theory. In 763PA he disavowed the Pogroms, renounced his county and left court politics for the Tower of Edessa. In the 9th century Bishop Alfred Zoitok "the Elder" of Tlokyemy taught Alfred the Younger, who later became Bishop of Szievek.</p>
<h2>Ruling House and Politics</h2>
<p>The Vadcy is held by House Martej, whose head is Var Jan. As the lord of one of the eight ancient lordships, he sits in the <a href="Honoured Assembly" class="internal-link">Honoured Assembly</a>, as the lords of the Tlok River have since 377PA. Below him the Gris include the lords of Perliav, a line descended from Darmil, and of Direszk, the seat of the house that produced Harlini.</p>
<h2>Society and Culture</h2>
<p>The Tlok are the Vihri of the river valley under the mountains, one of the peoples of the <a href="Vihri" class="internal-link">Vihri</a> alongside the Higher and Lower Vihri, the Wyt and the Sarloni. They are Gethric, and the saints and heroes of their valley are warriors: Tytus, who is venerated by soldiers, seers and those who mourn lost things, and Darmil, the slayer of beasts. The settlements near the hold of Xer-Kol have a higher than usual population of dwarves. The arms of the Vadcy show a blue unicorn and a sheep on a white ground.</p>
<h2>Economy</h2>
<p>The Tlok Valley is farming and herding country, and the sheep on the arms suggest the flocks that graze it. Trade with the mountains matters at least as much. The Silverslach Trade carries the goods of the mountain cities and of the greater hold of Xer-Kol into Tloky and on into the rest of Vihr, and the town of Silverslach itself mines a great amount of silver. The <a href="Trazlov Consortium" class="internal-link">Consortium</a> keeps an outpost in Tlokyemy. Its great rival and occasional partner, the <a href="Xerental" class="internal-link">Xerental</a>, the trade arm of Xer-Kol, controls the Xer-Kol tunnel through the Trollbacks and handles the hold's export business with Vihr and Urland, above all its engineering.</p>
<h2>Places of Note</h2>
<p>Tlokyemy is the capital, the old meeting place of the Horn Lords, the site of the Cathedral of Will and the seat of the Bishop of Tlokyemy, at present Rasbor V. Perliav is a prosperous town that grew around the keep of its Gri, with statues of Darmil standing in it. Direszk lies between Tlokyemy and Horsk, near the mountains, and is the seat of the Gris of Direszk and the birthplace of Zovac Harlini. Horsk is a town in the upper valley, toward the mountains. Silverslach, north of the capital, mines a great amount of silver.</p>
