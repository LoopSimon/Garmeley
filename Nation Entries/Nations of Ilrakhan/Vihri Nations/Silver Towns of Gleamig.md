<p>The Silver Towns of Gleamig are four mining towns in the uplands of Gleamig, between the Tlok Valley and the Trollback Mountains, which hold their silver mines under a royal charter and answer to the crown rather than to the Var of <a href="Tloky" class="internal-link">Tloky</a>. Silverslach is the largest and most important of them. Their silver goes first to the Royal Mint at <a href="Szulbod" class="internal-link">Szulbod</a>, and it is the reason the crown has guarded their charter for more than three centuries.</p>  
<h2>Geography</h2>  
<p>Gleamig is the high, grassy country that rises west of the Tlok Valley toward the Trollbacks and the dwarven hold of <a href="Xer-Kol" class="internal-link">Xer-Kol</a>. Its valleys are cut by streams that run down to the Tlok, and the silver lies in the hills between them. Silverslach stands on the eastern edge of the uplands, north of Tlokyemy, where the road of the Silverslach Trade comes down out of the mountains. The three smaller towns, Rudno, Srebnik and Wysoka, lie higher up in the valleys of Gleamig, closer to the mountains and to the mouth of the Xer-Kol tunnel.</p>  
<h2>History</h2>  
<p>Silver was found in Gleamig in the days of the Federation, and the dwarves of Xer-Kol were mining it long before the Vihri learned how. The Silverslach Trade grew up around the metal and around the goods of the mountain holds, and when the Vadcy of Tloky was formed in 377PA the mining towns fell under its lords. The undead occupation of the Tlok Valley in the 540s emptied many of the upland mines, and it was only after <a href="Tytus" class="internal-link">Tytus</a> broke the host of Mendizaba in 545PA that they were worked again in earnest.</p>  
<p>In 591PA Ras Miskomir I of House Worocek took the towns out of the hands of the Var of Tloky and granted them the Silver Charter. Under it each town governs itself through its own council, the crown appoints a Warden of the Silver Towns who sits at Silverslach, and the silver of Gleamig must be offered first to the Royal Mint at a fixed price. The Var of Tloky kept his lands around the towns but lost the towns themselves, and his house has never accepted the loss. When Orian I moved the Mint from Zavlech to Szulbod in 781PA, the silver road from Gleamig moved with it. In 813PA Ras <a href="Miskomir IV" class="internal-link">Miskomir IV</a>, on his way home from a visit to <a href="Xer-Kol" class="internal-link">Xer-Kol</a>, toured the towns and valleys of Gleamig, and in a small wood in the uplands a flock of cockatrices fell upon his entourage. The beasts attacked the king's horse, and Miskomir was found beside it with his neck broken.</p>
<h2>Government and Politics</h2>  
<p>The Warden of the Silver Towns is named by the Ras and answers to the Mint Master of Vihr at Szulbod. He keeps the charter, collects the crown's share and commands the guards who escort the silver east. Each of the four towns has its own council of mine owners and guild masters, and the council of Silverslach is the most powerful of them. Many of the deepest shafts are owned in part or in whole by dwarves of Xer-Kol, who work them with their own crews.</p>  
<p>Three powers want more from the towns than they have. Var Jan zi Martej of Tloky wants them back, as every Var of Tloky has since 591PA. The <a href="Xerental" class="internal-link">Xerental</a>, the trade arm of Xer-Kol, wants a larger share of the mines. The <a href="Trazlov Consortium" class="internal-link">Consortium</a> wants the carting of the silver, which the crown has always kept in the hands of its own escorts.</p>  
<h2>Society and Culture</h2>  
<p>The people of the Silver Towns are Tloky, Gethric and hard, miners and smelters more than farmers, and the dwarves among them are more numerous than anywhere else in Vihr outside the settlements near Tizin-Kol. Dwarven and Vihri miners work the same shafts, and the towns have their own saints' days for Tytus, who freed the valley from the dead.</p>  
<h2>Economy</h2>  
<p>Silver is the wealth of the towns, together with lead and the other ores that come up with it. Silverslach also lives on the Silverslach Trade, the road that carries the goods of Xer-Kol and the mountain cities down into Tloky and on into the rest of Vihr, and its markets and warehouses are the busiest in the uplands.</p>  
<h2>Places of Note</h2>  
<p>Silverslach is the chief town, with the Warden's house, the great smelting works and the market of the Silverslach Trade. Rudno is the oldest of the upland mining towns. Srebnik holds the richest seams of Gleamig, most of them dwarf-owned. Wysoka, the highest of the four, stands nearest the mouth of the Xer-Kol tunnel.</p>
