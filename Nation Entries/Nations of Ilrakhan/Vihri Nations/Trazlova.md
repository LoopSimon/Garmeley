<p> Trazlova is one of the major Vadcy within Vihr and its most populated one. The Var of Trazlova has traditionally held the prestigious Office of Foreman of the <a href="Honoured Assembly" class="internal-link">Honoured Assembly</a>. A vast majority of the Vadcy's population resides within the city of Trazlov itself: sometimes referred to as "old Grach" due to its cultural ties with Grachy, at over 120000 inhabitants the port city is the largest city within the entire Country of Vihr, even exceeding the capital Zavlech. Trazlov is home to two major international organisations of commerce and importance: the <a href="Trazlov Consortium" class="internal-link">Consortium of Trazlov</a> as well as the <a href="The Alchemists Guild" class="internal-link">Men of Glass</a>.</p>

<p>Trazlova is under the rule of House Lawchlaw, a Cadet Branch and distant relative to the royal House of Ralpia, although especially in recent times this century long connection has not always held up on the chessboard of vihri regional politics.</p>
<h2>Geography</h2>
<p>Trazlova occupies the north-east of Vihr, on Trazlov Bay, where the Lehva River reaches the sea. To the east the Grachhyll rise toward the Iron Claw and the border with <a href="Grachy" class="internal-link">Grachy</a>, to the west lie the lands of <a href="Iztaw" class="internal-link">Iztaw</a>, and to the south the Lehva leads up through Szievek to the heart of the Vihri Expanse. Most of the Vadcy's people live in or around the port of Trazlov itself, which has become the largest city in the realm.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Trazlov was one of the first places where the roaming bands of the old <a href="Vihrmen Federation" class="internal-link">Vihrmen Federation</a> settled for good, and its harbour has looked outward since the beginning. In 124PA the sailor Fidor of Trazlov took his ship around the Iron Claw and landed in <a href="Nostroya" class="internal-link">Nostroya</a>, where the settlers who followed him became the first Grachi, which is why the city is still sometimes called "old Grach". The city took its part in the war against the Abyss that followed the Second <a href="The Incursion" class="internal-link">Incursion</a>: the fleet of the Urien made landfall at Trazlov, and the Lehva, which reaches the sea there, is the river on whose bank the Prophet Gethric gave one of the great speeches of the faith.</p>
<p>Trazlova was one of the eight ancient lordships that marched on Zavlech in 377PA and became hereditary Vadcy in the settlement that followed. Some of the ground it holds today then belonged to Szievek, the greatest of the Vadcy, and passed to Trazlova only after the Vihri War of Succession, when Zawis gave the lands of the beaten Szinmo to the lords who had backed him. In 639PA the merchants of the city founded the Trazlov Consortium, and in 691PA the Guild of Alchemy was formed in its walls, the beginning of the Men of Glass, by alchemists who had risen in the royal favour after they exposed the Usily Plot against Zawis III. Within a year Trazlova had given Vihr a king. In 692PA the Honoured Assembly and the Gethric Bishops chose Miskomir of House Ralpia, the Duke of Trazlov, to follow Zawis III, the last of the House of Worocek, on the throne, and the Ralpia have ruled from Zavlech ever since.</p>
<p>Around 710PA the Vadcy passed to a cadet line of the royal house, which became House Lawchlaw, and it has stayed with them. The Wolfsplague of the early 910s thinned the peasantry of Vihr, and the labour shortage that followed gave a new strength to the merchants and burghers of Trazlov, whose guilds had been growing since the Consortium and the Men of Glass were founded.</p>
<h2>Ruling House and Politics</h2>
<p>The Vadcy is ruled by Var Osran of House Lawchlaw, a distant relative of the royal House of Ralpia, and the Var of Trazlova has traditionally held the office of Foreman of the <a href="Honoured Assembly" class="internal-link">Honoured Assembly</a>. It is the most populous and wealthiest of the nine Vadcy, and its Var presides over the council that checks the throne, though the kinship between Lawchlaw and Ralpia no longer counts for as much as it once did. The real balance of power in Trazlova lies elsewhere. The Consortium and the Men of Glass answer to nothing in Zavlech and to little in the Var's hall, and the Var has long had to govern alongside them. <a href="Valenty Sykes" class="internal-link">Valenty Sykes</a>, now the Grandmaster of the Guild and one of the most influential men at the royal court, was once the adviser of the Var of Trazlova.</p>
<h2>Society and Culture</h2>
<p>Trazlova is the most urban and cosmopolitan of the nine Vadcy, though its people are Low Vihri by culture. They are traders, shipwrights, factors and guildsmen, and there are large Grachi communities in the city, drawn by the trade that connects it to the eastern coast. The burgher class has more weight here than anywhere in Vihr, and the nobility of the Vadcy has learned to live with it.</p>
<h2>Economy</h2>
<p>Trazlov is one of the "three great Ports" of the Torn Sea, alongside Sallesand and Uhl, and the largest port in Vihr. The <a href="Trazlov Consortium" class="internal-link">Trazlov Consortium for Free Trade</a> has its home here, and under its Headman Jan Pisudski, the owner of the Pisudski shipping company, it commands the mercantile trade of the eastern Torn Sea. It has tried hard to stay apolitical in conflicts like the <a href="Great Eastern War" class="internal-link">Great Eastern War</a> and protects its interests with agents called Fingers. The <a href="The Alchemists Guild" class="internal-link">Men of Glass</a> keep their headquarters in the Violet Hall within the city's walls, and train their members at the Guilds College there. The city depends on the produce of Grachy, above all on phosphorite, pitchblende and Graccum, which makes the war on its eastern border a constant concern for its merchants.</p>
<h2>Places of Note</h2>
<p>Trazlov, the capital, is a port of over 120,000 people, larger than the capital Zavlech itself. The Violet Hall and the Guilds College stand within its walls. The Lehva carries the trade of the Expanse down to its docks, and the Grachhyll and the Iron Claw to the east form the frontier with Grachy.</p>
