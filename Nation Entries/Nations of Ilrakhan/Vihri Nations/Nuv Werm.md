<p>The Trytorium zi Werm, or Nuv Werm, is the administration in charge of eastern Wermia's most populated territories and towns, primarily the coastal areas around Morael, and is an entity directly under the Throne of Vihr, designated by the Crown with the protection of lost Wermia and her people as well as the security of the Pilgrims Path. Especially in the latter task the Order of the Silver Wheel has historically been the more powerful and influential institution, protecting the Pilgrims Path from Castle Nurk, although in modern times the Order has fallen from grace. Unlike the many other lands under the Vihri crown, such as the powerful Vadcy, Nuv Werm is ruled by a Lord-Warden by the grace of the Crown, who is appointed directly instead of holding a de jure hereditary title.</p>
<h2>Geography</h2>
<p>Nuv Werm lies on the eastern side of the old kingdom, with its capital Morael on the north coast. To the south the land runs toward the country of the Order, where Castle Nurk stands on the Pilgrims Path, and in the west the great Weirwood, a petrified forest haunted by evil spirits, divides the March from the Wermian Moor and the Gri zi <a href="Taar" class="internal-link">Taar</a>. The Neverrest Heights rise inland. To the east the March borders Torry and Rovgi, the two Vadcy that watch it most closely. The heart of old Wermia, around the ruined capital of Werm, has been deemed uninhabitable since the Incursions.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>The Kingdom of Wermia ceased to exist in 219PA, at the end of the first Incursion, and the second in 226PA finished its ruin. The Order of the Silver Wheel was declared in 398PA to guard the pilgrims on the road to Iztaw. In 441PA Vihr declared the remnants of Wermia to be under its protection and established Nuv Werm. The Throne of Zavlech asserted its claim ever more strongly over the following centuries, and in 692PA Miskomir II was elected King of Vihr and Wermia, the first official claim to the lands of Wermia by a Ras.</p>
<p>The most important moment in the March's history came in 536PA, when the <a href="Wermian Accords" class="internal-link">Wermian Accords</a> were signed at Morael in Stagbone Hall, close to the remnants of Old Wermia. Rulers from across the continent gathered to ban the dark arts of necromancy, summoning and gate-making, with the paramilitary orders of the Church of Iztaw dictating much of what was written. The Order of the Silver Wheel lost much of its influence after its failure at the Conclave of Trinfurt in 764PA. In the War of Horn and Fang, the Royal Army's only road to the Bay of Uhl led through the Wermian lands by way of the Pilgrims Pass, and the supply difficulties of that march were among the defining troubles of the war.</p>
<h2>Government and Politics</h2>
<p>The Lord-Warden of Wermia, at present Valstaf, is appointed directly by the Ras. The greater title belongs to the crown: the Ras of Vihr is the Shield of Wermia, and the Lord-Warden governs in his name. The March has no seat in the <a href="Honoured Assembly" class="internal-link">Honoured Assembly</a>. A movement for a seat has been gathering strength in recent years, but the factions of Torry and Rovgi, its neighbours, oppose it. They fear that a March whose lands are potentially much larger than their own, and which would be granted a say and better rights of trade, would take power from them.</p>
<p>The Order of the Silver Wheel does not answer to the Lord-Warden. It is sponsored by the Church of Iztaw, under its Grand Master Fyor Ziminastrus at Castle Nurk, and the two powers share the Pilgrims Path in an uneasy balance.</p>
<h2>Society and Culture</h2>
<p>The people are the Old Wermi, heirs of one of the oldest feudal realms in Ilrakhan, who live around the Neverrest Heights and east of the Weirwood. They are Gethric now, and they keep the memory of the old kingdom with a stubborn, tragic pride. The Weirwood is a place of superstition, and travellers are warned off it. Pilgrims and knights of the Order are a common sight in the towns, and the people live with the road as other people live with a river.</p>
<h2>Economy</h2>
<p>Morael is a fishing and trading port, and the coast supplies most of the March's food. The pilgrim road brings in inns, guides, wagoners and the offerings of the faithful, and the ruins of Old Wermia provide a trade in salvage that the Lord-Warden tries to regulate. The Order keeps its own revenues from the Pilgrims Path.</p>
<h2>Places of Note</h2>
<p>Morael is the capital and the seat of the Lord-Warden. Stagbone Hall in the town is where the Wermian Accords were signed. Castle Nurk, in the south, is the high castle of the Order of the Silver Wheel. The Weirwood and the Neverrest Heights lie inland, and the ruins of Werm are the remnants of the old capital.</p>
