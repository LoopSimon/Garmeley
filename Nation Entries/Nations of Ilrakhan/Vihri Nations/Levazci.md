<p>Levazci is one of the major Vadcy within Vihr, situated on the southern slopes of the Grachhyll alongside the Lehva River, its name literally translating to "Land of the Lehva". It holds significance as the border region with <a href="Grachy" class="internal-link">Grachy</a>, as all wares and trade that leaves or enters Nostroya inlands is transported over the Hyllpass between Tuskwatch and Orzebachi.</p>
<h2>Geography</h2>
<p>Geographically, Levazci is limited by the Grachhyll and <a href="Mistspires" class="internal-link">Mistspires</a> mountains to the east and defined by the Lehva River that exits north towards Szievek before flowing into <a href="Trazlova" class="internal-link">Trazlov</a>. The Region is mostly hilly, with mountains in the east and plain areas in the west. The climate alongside the Lehva is transitional temperate continental, overall the main influence of the climate is dry continental, with frequent cold north winds in winter.</p>
<p>Mammals such as goats, boars, wolves, foxes, wildcats and hares are characteristic for Levazci, but the Vadcy is most famous for its local black quail.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Levazci stands where the Lehva comes down out of the mountains, and its history is the history of a road. Every caravan and every army that has gone between Vihr and the lands beyond the Grachhyll has had to cross the Hyllpass, and the lords of the Vadcy have lived with that for as long as there have been lords. Levazci was one of the eight lordships that marched on Zavlech in 377PA and became hereditary Vadcy in the settlement that followed. Some of the ground it holds today then belonged to <a href="Szievek" class="internal-link">Szievek</a>, the greatest of the Vadcy, and passed to Levazci only after the Vihri War of Succession.</p>
<p>The Vadcy was held by House zi Kerv until that house died out. In 802PA the crown raised House Tryn to the Vadcy in its place, rewarding its loyalty, though it was understood at the time that the favour of the throne of the day counted for as much as the service. Little of the Kerv survives apart from their cat, which still plays its trumpet on the arms of the Vadcy.</p>
<p>A generation after the rise of House Tryn the war came to the pass. The <a href="Brothers War" class="internal-link">Brothers War</a> of 828PA to 835PA, fought between Vihr and the Kingdom of <a href="Halgrach" class="internal-link">Halgrach</a>, was close to a disaster for Vihr, and the country around the Hyllpass saw some of its hardest fighting. Tuskwatch, the watch-town at the eastern end of the pass, fell almost at once and has been Grachi ever since. It was the only ground that Vihr conceded in the whole war. The Battle under the Red Cloud in 830PA and the Battle of White Bear Crag in 834PA, two of the great battles of the war, were fought in the lands near the pass. In the end Vihr was saved less by its own arms than by the collapse of Halgrach from within, whose king <a href="Oleksandr IV" class="internal-link">Oleksandr IV</a> died on the battlefield. Since the peace the pass has been the border, with Tuskwatch on the Grachi side and Orzebachi on the Vihri.</p>
<h2>Ruling House and Politics</h2>
<p>The Vadcy is held by House Tryn, whose present head is Varina Henrietta, one of the three women among the nine Var, together with the Varinas of Szievek and <a href="Breza" class="internal-link">Breza</a>. Tryn has held Levazci only since 802PA, and it has not forgotten whom it owes its place to. As the lord of one of the eight ancient lordships, the Varina sits in the <a href="Honoured Assembly" class="internal-link">Honoured Assembly</a>. Across the pass she deals with the Gram of the Hyllpass Region, at present Sonja Modriv, and the business between the two sides of the pass, in tolls, patrols and the passage of caravans, is much of what the government of the Vadcy does from day to day.</p>
<h2>Society and Culture</h2>
<p>The people of Levazci are Wyt and Sarloni, and Gethric. The Wyt keep the hills in the east, toward the Wytlands, and share the love of dance and of the Bolov, the stringed instrument, for which their kin in <a href="Opuzna" class="internal-link">Opuzna</a> are known. The Sarloni farm the plains in the west. Strangers are a common sight in the Vadcy: Grachi drivers and merchants from beyond the pass, and dwarves from <a href="Tizin-Kol" class="internal-link">Tizin-Kol</a>, whose tunnel network under the <a href="Mistspires" class="internal-link">Mistspires</a> is a vast one. The settlements near the mountains have a higher than usual population of dwarves. The Church has its see at Orzebachi, where the Bishop of Orzebachi, at present Marsko II, holds court. The arms of the Vadcy show a cat playing the trumpet, the old cat of the Kerv.</p>
<h2>Economy</h2>
<p>Levazci lives from the road. Nearly everything that goes between inland Nostroya and the Vihri heartland goes over the Hyllpass, and the Vadcy takes its share: the crown has its customs, and the Varina keeps the tolls and the market rights at Orzebachi. The goods then go down the Lehva by way of Szievek to the docks of Trazlov, and most of that traffic passes through the hands of the <a href="Trazlov Consortium" class="internal-link">Consortium</a>. The tunnels of Tizin-Kol are a second road into the Vadcy, and the trade with the hold is considerable. Besides the trade, the western plains grow grain, the hills carry goats, and the black quail for which Levazci is famous are bred and hunted in both.</p>
<h2>Places of Note</h2>
<p>Orzebachi, on the Lehva, is the capital, the seat of the Varina and of the Bishop, and a town of windmills. East of it the road climbs to the Hyllpass, the great road through the mountains for the trade of Nostroya, and at its far end stands Tuskwatch, which was Vihri once and is Grachi now. The fields of the Red Cloud and of White Bear Crag lie in the country around the pass.</p>
