<p>The Rascy zi Dalniz is a <a href="Vihri" class="internal-link">Sarloni Vihri</a> realm in eastern Ilrakhan, the only autonomous nation of the Sarloni peoples and a direct successor to the <a href="Leok" class="internal-link">Rascy zi Leok</a>. It emerged in the aftermath of the 2nd Vihri-Leoki War of the 620s PA. In the modern day it it closely allied with the Lordship of <a href="Grevenna" class="internal-link">Grevenna</a>, after a troubling history during the 8th century and its refusal to join any of the Reani Coalitions against the Sultanate during the <a href="Wars of Elbae Aggression" class="internal-link">Wars of Elbae Aggression</a>. Dalniz is fiercely independent and has come to blows with its various more powerful neighbours over its history that have attempted to conquer or reconquer its territories, to no avail yet. It has a good relation with the Dwarves of Tizin-Kol and various minor holds in the bordering Mistspire Mountains. The Rascy is currently under the rule of the somewhat controversial Drageri Dynasty.</p>
