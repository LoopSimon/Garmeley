<p>Ravland is a <a href="Sertova" class="internal-link">sertovan</a> Kingdom in the north of the peninsula and, together with <a href="Erlmondy" class="internal-link">Erlmondy</a> to its South, one of the two largest powers in the Region. It takes its name from the ancient Ravi Tribe that would fall under the conquest and rule of Aerlan in antiquity and was formed as a result of a great Civil war during the end times of the ancient Empire. Although, unlike its southern neighbour Erlmondy, Ravland sees itself as a distinct new nation with disdain and bitterness for Aerlan, its citizens still practice many of its traditions such as the old and truest form of Kalbinism. </p>
<p>Ravland is in an interesting position in modern times, as threats from their southern border following the <a href="War of Kin" class="internal-link">War of Kin</a> in the early 9th century have never fully wained they have looked elsewhere for allies. An old supporter of the people of <a href="Droil" class="internal-link">Droil</a> in the north, they are its closest friend now that the Island nation has finally gained freedom from the shackles of her skori and locni overlords. As richer and more populous Othiri powers have united against a perceived thread from Meadrath, Ravland has seen opportunity in both trade with <a href="Blijgrat" class="internal-link">weadi</a> and <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">meadi</a> merchants and military assurances with the mighty powers from across the mountains. Ravland in turn has essentially acted as a port and bridgehead for sailors and settlers from Meadland not only into Sertova but across the ocean towards the shores of far <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a>.</p>
