<p>Once a part of the Republic of Nahr, Revelle is now an independent realm ruled by and governed for the Halflings of Sertova, embodying an interpretation of Nyrkalbinist Tenents that would be called radical by most, the Dominion of Revelle has essentially become an ethnostate offering a haven and opportunity for any Halfling of pure blood that makes their pilgrimage to its towns. Revelle has broken off good relations with their former Overlord of Nahr, although their Secession was a bloodless affair now almost 150 years in the past and over and over again engaged in bloody conflict with the cevoli Grand Duchy of Martella on its eastern border, usually for vaguely religious and racist reasons. The once prosperous lands of Revelle have fallen on hard times in recent years and rumours of all out war coming in the vague of more and more foreign interest in the Region are growing.</p>
