<p>The Free Jarldoms are the petty jarldoms of north-western <a href="Trykkerog" class="internal-link">Trykkerog</a>, lying around the Gotjebay and in the cold hills and fjords between the bay and the Stormlands. They are not a realm but a patchwork of small lordships, each ruled from a hall or a keep by a Skori jarl who answers to no king. The strongest of them are held from the old keeps of Fjorik and Ingeborg. Once part of the Korringe of <a href="Vignasoya" class="internal-link">Vignasoya</a>, the jarldoms drifted out of the Korre's reach over the centuries and today no longer acknowledge him at all.</p>

<h2>Land and People</h2>
<p>The north-west is the poorest and harshest corner of the settled island. The Gotjebay cuts deep into the coast from the north and freezes in hard winters, and the land around it is a country of rocky hills, birch woods and narrow fields, with the wilds of the Stormlands to the east. Most people live in small farmsteads and fishing hamlets along the shore. The jarldoms are home to Skori and, further inland and higher up, to Hevri communities, and the two have mingled here more than anywhere else in the west.</p>

<h2>Government</h2>
<p>Each jarl rules his own lands with his household and his sworn men, and the number of jarldoms changes with every generation as lordships are divided between heirs, joined by marriage or taken by force. The jarls meet at a summer Thing on the shores of the Gotjebay to settle feuds, agree on the price of blood and deal with common threats, but the Thing has no power to command any of them. Their warriors often take service in the warbands of Vignasoya and <a href="Rensköldsvik" class="internal-link">Rensköldsvik</a> or sail abroad for pay, and the jarldoms are a common recruiting ground for anyone in need of hard men.</p>

<h2>Faith</h2>
<p>The Free Jarldoms are the stronghold of <a href="Forrvegur" class="internal-link">Forrvegur</a>, the old faith, which is kept here in its most traditional forms. There are few Gethric churches north of the Onje, and Gethric priests who travel into the jarldoms do so at the pleasure of the local jarl.</p>

<h2>History</h2>
<p>In the early history of Vignasoya the north-west was firmly part of the Korringe. When the <a href="Baeling Empire" class="internal-link">Baeling Empire</a> conquered the southern coast of Vignasoya in the 6th century, the Korres held out around the Onje River and the Gotjebay, and the jarls of the north-west were among their most loyal supporters. Their loyalty did not outlast the age of Skori power. After the Great Kingdom of Skorland broke apart in 667PA and the Skori lost their overseas holdings in the 8th century, the Korres had little to offer the northern jarls, and one after another they ceased to come to the Great Thing or to send their men to the Korre's wars. By the end of the 8th century the jarldoms were in practice independent, and the Korres, too weak to bring them back by force, have never formally given up their claim.</p>
