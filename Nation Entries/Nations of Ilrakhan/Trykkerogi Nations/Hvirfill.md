<p>Hvirfill is a high valley beneath the greatest peak of Fagnirs Rest in central <a href="Trykkerog" class="internal-link">Trykkerog</a>, and the home of the Nárauðna, an ancient tower that is held to be a work of the <a href="Giant" class="internal-link">Giants</a> of old. It is inhabited by Hevri and <a href="Firbolg" class="internal-link">Firbolg</a>, who worship the True Stones of the valley and guard the tower against all comers. Hvirfill has never been conquered. Few have tried to force its rough mountain passes, and none have succeeded.</p>

<h2>Land</h2>
<p>The valley lies high in Fagnirs Rest, above the great forest of the Uvarumr, which reaches down from its mouth towards the farmland of Briland and the shores of Melmers Bay. It can only be reached through a few narrow passes, which are closed by snow for much of the year. Its floor is a land of high pastures, birch groves, cold lakes and scattered farmsteads, and the peaks around it are home to <a href="Ogre" class="internal-link">ogres</a>, <a href="Troll" class="internal-link">trolls</a> and <a href="Trog" class="internal-link">trogs</a>. The Kingdom of <a href="Baeling" class="internal-link">Baeling</a> borders the valley to the south, but its rule ends where the passes begin.</p>

<h2>The Nárauðna</h2>
<p>The Nárauðna is a massive, tower-like structure that rises from the centre of the valley. According to the tradition of the Hevri and the Firbolg it was raised by the Giants in the age of the <a href="Giantsrul" class="internal-link">Giantsrul</a>, and it is said to have been made from the melted bones of the Raufjanda, a great red bringer of doom and death associated with the lyndwyrms, which the Giants ended in the distant past. Its material resembles no stone found elsewhere on the island, and its red surface is said to stay warm even in the depths of winter. No one in the valley claims to know what purpose the Giants built it for, and its guardians allow no outsider to enter it.</p>

<h2>People</h2>
<p>Most of the people of Hvirfill are Hevri, the smallest group of the <a href="Bykmåli" class="internal-link">Bykmåli</a>, who live here in larger numbers than anywhere else on the island. They speak the dialect of the surrounding lowlands and trade furs, wool and hides down the passes for metal, salt and grain. The Firbolg of the valley live in small family groups in the high pastures and woods around the Nárauðna, avoiding metal and the settled life of the Hevri, but the two peoples have lived side by side for as long as either can remember. The Firbolg tolerate the Bykmåli traditions and pantheons of their neighbours, and it is they above all who keep watch over the tower and the stones.</p>
<p>The valley has no lord. Matters that concern all of Hvirfill are settled by a council of the elders of the Hevri households and of the Firbolg families, which gathers at the True Stones and whose decisions are accepted by both peoples.</p>

<h2>Faith</h2>
<p>The people of Hvirfill worship the True Stones, great rune stones that stand around the valley and are said to have been carved by the Giants during their primordial war with the trolls. The runes on them are held to record the oaths, victories and laws of the Giants, and the elders swear their own oaths before them. Beside the stones the Hevri and Firbolg share the veneration of <a href="Otso" class="internal-link">Otso</a>, King of Bears and Giantkin, whose teaching of strength through community suits the life of the valley well. The Hevri also keep the gods of <a href="Forrvegur" class="internal-link">Forrvegur</a>, and the Gethric faith has never taken root in the valley.</p>

<h2>History</h2>
<p>Hvirfill is believed to have been one of the strongholds of the Giants who ruled Trykkerog and Nostroya in the age of the Giantsrul, before they withdrew beyond the Waelajokull and the Heavens Furrow in the 2nd century PA. The Firbolg, who count themselves among the descendants of the Giants, are said to have remained in the valley when their forebears departed, and the Hevri settled there in later centuries. Neither the Korres of <a href="Vignasoya" class="internal-link">Vignasoya</a> nor the Emperors of the <a href="Baeling Empire" class="internal-link">Baeling Empire</a> ever brought the valley under their rule, and the Gethric missionaries who followed Saint <a href="Melmer" class="internal-link">Melmer</a> found little welcome there. In recent years the ogres of Fagnirs Rest have begun to gather in greater numbers, and the valley, which lies closer to their mountains than any town of the south, has felt their pressure first.</p>
