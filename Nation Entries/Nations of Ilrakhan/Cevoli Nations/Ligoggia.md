<p>Ligoggia sits at the southern shore of the Bay of Butera and is, by the assessment of even its most generous admirers, a city of scoundrels. Its formal city council has long since been killed, bought out or replaced by figures of considerably less civic orientation. The trade in <a href="Battvian Flour" class="internal-link">Battvian Flour</a>, the stimulant drug derived from the rare Zegano plant, flows through Ligoggia more freely than anywhere in the known world, and the figure who controls this flow controls the city. That person, known only as <a href="The Baker" class="internal-link">"The Baker"</a>, is the closest thing Ligoggia has to a mayor, a judge and a city watch combined. Law in Ligoggia is whatever the Bakers people choose to enforce. What Ligoggia offers in exchange is a complete absence of questions about origin, occupation or past, the city attracts fortune-seekers, criminals, do-gooders, rogues in temporary difficulty and every variety of person who requires a port with no particular interest in their history. Naive visitors tend to discover the citys nature before they discover anything else. Those who survive this revelation of Ligoggias "free Spirit" more than often enough tend to find it useful.</p>
