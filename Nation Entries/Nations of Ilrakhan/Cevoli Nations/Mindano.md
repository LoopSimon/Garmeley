<p>Mindano is the oldest self-governing city-state in eastern Cevolia, its independence predating the <a href="Battvia" class="internal-link">Kingdom of Battvia</a> entirely and rooted in a period when the eastern civilized communities of the Terragna organized their own commercial and defensive affairs without reference to the disintegrating <a href="Cevolis" class="internal-link">Kingdom of Cevolis</a>. Unlike the <a href="Paper Cities" class="internal-link">Paper Cities</a>, whose independence was won through revolt against an existing authority, Mindano's autonomy was never seriously challenged and it simply filled the vacuum left by Cevolis' collapse around 590PA, having governed itself through its Council of Contesti ever since.</p> <p>The city is not a member of the Paper Cities League and has never sought to be: its independence is older and less contingent than theirs, and the particular identity of the league as it is defined by the rejection of Battvian authority does not map onto Mindanos history. What Mindano shares with the Paper Cities is geography, commercial interest and a preference for the continued existence of free cities on the Bay of Butera over Battvian dominance of the entire coast. It supported the Paper Cities militarily in both the Battvian Civil War and the Vorellan Wars for exactly these reasons, not out of solidarity with their founding principles, but because a Battvia that controlled Albesan, Butera and Ligoggia would eventually look south toward Mindano too.</p>
