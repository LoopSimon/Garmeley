<p>The largest and wealthiest of the three <a href="Paper Cities" class="internal-link">Paper Cities</a>, Albesan sits at the northern shore of the <strong>Bay of Butera</strong> and owes its pre-eminence entirely to paper and ink. The citys printing houses are the largest commercial printing operations in the known world , fed by the academic and cartographic demand generated by the <a href="Excavators Guild of Butera" class="internal-link">Excavators Guilds</a> rakhian expeditions, and by the broader appetite for literature, news and documentation that the <a href="Trinicism" class="internal-link">Trinicist</a> emphasis on personal scripture literacy created across western Ilrakhan. Albesan is governed by its Council of Printers, the heads of the citys major printing houses, who elect a Lord-Mayor from among their number.</p> <p>The current Lord-Mayor, Livius Grimo, holds both the mayoralty and the title of Master-Printer of Albesan, making him the most formally powerful individual in the city. In practice, the printing oligarchy governs through consensus, commercial leverage and the natural understanding that controlling what gets printed gives the council an influence over public discourse that no military force can easily replicate. People of almost all races and religions are welcome in Albesan, the city is by some measures the most cosmopolitan settlement in the western world, its prosperity insulating it from the worst of the region's religious and racial tensions. It has treaties with the dwarves of <a href="Ogul-Vosko" class="internal-link">Ogul-Vosko</a> and the petty kingdom of Niedmer across the mountains.</p>
