<p>Teggio is not a city-state in any commercial sense. Situated in eastern Cevolia against the foothills of the Kortopi Mountains, it grew around the Fortress Complex of Teggio, the headquarters of the <a href="Fellows of the Dancing Flame" class="internal-link">Fellows of the Dancing Flame</a>, and has been effectively governed by the Order since the early 5th century. What began as a hardline Gethric knightly order during the late Kingdom of Cevolis, purging clerical corruption and the old nobility's lingering attachment to the Cevolian Pantheon, became over the following centuries one of the most powerful institutional landholders in the region. Its vast estates, commercial businesses and military capacity making it a political power whose cooperation the Princes of Battvia, the Greater Merchants of the coast, and the Ducs of Jeledo have always found it prudent to cultivate.</p>

<p>The city's independence from Battvian authority is a matter of practical reality rather than formal treaty: no one has seriously attempted to subordinate the Fellows' fortress-city since the early years of the successor state period. In 927PA, under Great Master <a href="Giuseppe Dimandi" class="internal-link">Giuseppe Dimandi</a> "Capellianci", the Fellows are more politically active than they have been in generations, part of the Neo-Trinicist coalition with <a href="Stefano Maldini" class="internal-link">Bishop Maldini</a> and the <a href="Luminists" class="internal-link">Luminists</a>, which has renewed both their influence and the suspicion with which the Battvian court regards their ambitions. Notably, the Fellows absented themselves from both the Battvian Civil War and the Vorellan Wars, a studied neutrality whose reasons remain a matter of ongoing speculation in Cevolian political circles.</p>
