<p>Butera is an island-city sitting at the mouth of the Bay of Butera, its position making it the natural maritime gateway between the Smoking Sea's trade routes and the commercial hinterland of eastern Cevolia. The <a href="Excavators Guild of Butera" class="internal-link">Excavators Guild of Butera</a> controls virtually every aspect of the city's life like its massive port, its markets, its civic institutions and its considerable private military in the form of the merged Silver Company. The Guild was established here in the late 6th century following <a href="Mario Benvolo" class="internal-link">Mario Benvolo's</a> Great Expedition into the ruins of <a href="Rakh" class="internal-link">Rakh</a> in 580PA, and its monopoly over Rakhian archaeological expeditions has made it an institution of international significance, supplying artefacts to academic institutions from <a href="Korsfall College" class="internal-link">Korsfall College</a>in Meadrath to the <a href="Karajal School of Insight" class="internal-link">School of Karajal in Behera</a>.</p>
<p>Butera was almost entirely unharmed during the Battvian Civil War and the Vorellan Wars, its island position and naval superiority making direct assault prohibitively costly. In 927PA the Guild is expanding its operations into southern <a href="Itharia" class="internal-link">Itharia</a> and attempting to establish routes into far Cyraclion, hopeful adventurers who arrive expecting the romantic pioneering spirit of the Great Expedition will find instead that international interests and powerful institutions have fully colonized what was once a place of individual daring.</p>
