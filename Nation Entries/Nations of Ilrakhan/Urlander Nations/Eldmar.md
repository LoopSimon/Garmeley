It was formed in 703PA out of the Splintering of <a href="Urland" class="internal-link">Urland</a> that followed the <a href="Hobgoblin Invasion" class="internal-link">Hobgoblin Invasion</a>, when the lords of the valley refused the call of Jorwyle of Gelm and crowned the Countess of Eldshire, Theara of Hornsley, as a Counter Queen to the Lords of Gelm in <a href="Gelmsburg" class="internal-link">Gelmsburg</a>. The Domain of Eldmar stretches throughout the Eld Valley, between the <a href="Trollbacks" class="internal-link">Trollback Mountains</a>, the Ironwood and the western banks of the Eld River. It is small in area and is generally counted the weakest of the Urien minors, but it holds a great many people for its size, and its archers are known across Ur. Many of its ancestral traditions refer back to the old realm of <a href="Arester" class="internal-link">Arester</a>, including its more egalitarian culture and better treatment of women.</p> <h2>Geography</h2> <p>The kingdom lies in the Eld Valley, the long green valley that runs down from the foothills of the Trollbacks and takes its name from the Eld River. In the east and north-east the mountains rise toward Vihr, and ogres raid down out of them into the borderlands. To the north the Redwood stands between the valley and Gelmsburg, and the northern border is the most closely watched in the realm. To the south the dark Ironwood and the Ares Highlands lie between Eldmar and the Duchy of <a href="Garmeley" class="internal-link">Garmeley</a>, and to the west are the lands of the Order of <a href="Urbridge" class="internal-link">Urbridge</a>, the other heir of Arester.</p> <h2>Peoples</h2> <p>The people of Eldmar are Ares humans, the old people of the Eld Valley and the Ares Highlands, and the Urien of other realms have long thought of them as backwater folk. The valley is farmed in great steadings, large farmsteads that hold whole families and their labourers, and the land feeds far more people than its size would suggest. The bowmen of Eldmar are renowned, and the valley has never lacked archers to fill a levy. The old Arester customs of inheritance have never been dropped, and women hold land and title in their own right as freely as men.</p>  <p>The three coins on the arms of Eldmar recall a tale from the ancient traditions of Arester, in which a lone farmer bought the vale from a dragon for three golden coins. They are a popular symbol throughout the region.</p> <h2>Government and the Crown</h2> <p>Eldmar is a feudal monarchy whose rulers style themselves Queen or King in Eldmar. Its constitution is the Charter of the Vale, agreed by the lords of the valley before the crowning of Queen Theara I in 703PA. It grants the counts and barons of the valley rights that the crown of Urland never wrote down: trial by their peers, a voice in the raising of new levies through the Council of the Vale, and the protection of their titles and lands. The Council meets in the capital, and the Eldmari are proud that their crown rests on a charter. The crown passes to the eldest child, whatever the sex, as it did in Arester.</p> <p>House Hornsley held the crown for 119 years, from Theara I in 703PA to King Thormace in 822PA. Since the Lavellan Coup of 822PA the crown has been held by House Lavellan, whose line begins with <a href="Moira I" class="internal-link">Moira I</a>, Countess of Lavellan. The Lavellans are often called the House of Eldmar, but they are House Lavellan, and it is by that name that they rule.</p> <p>The strength of Eldmar is the levy of the valley, led by the knights of the counts and barons and made formidable by its archers. It has few heavy horse of the Gelmish kind and no gryphons, which are kept by the knights of Urbridge. The wealth of the kingdom is in grain, cattle, orchards and the timber of the Ironwood, and in the trade that goes along the Eld. Eldmar makes no claim to the crown of Urland, but it will not hear of the Gelmish one. It treats Urbridge as kin through Arester and Gelmsburg as the old enemy.</p> <h2>Religion</h2> <p>Eldmar is a <a href="Gethricism" class="internal-link">Gethric</a> kingdom. <a href="Andre" class="internal-link">Saint Andre</a> is much loved in the valley, as he is in Gelmsburg. The Queen of Eldmar signs the <a href="Wermian Accords" class="internal-link">Wermian Accords</a>, and signed the <a href="Treaty of Kulum" class="internal-link">Treaty of Kulum</a> in 908PA.</p> <h2>History</h2> <p>Eldmar lies in the old heartland of the Kingdom of Arester. After the Hobgoblin Invasion, the Fall of the West and the failed plot of Ricter of Gelm, most of the Urien nobility had lost faith in a united front behind yet another powerful leader. The Countess of Eldshire at the time, Theara of Hornsley, gathered a large host of the eastern nobles and landowners who refused the call of Ricter's brother Jorwyle to unite again behind the House of Gelm. Jorwyle's northern knights struck at the lords of the valley in a surprising move and were bested by an army that the valley raised quickly. Under an agreed Charter granting the counts and barons a variety of rights, Theara was created a Counter Queen to the Lords of Gelm, challenging the notion that only one king ruled in Urland. She was crowned Queen Theara I of Eldmar in the winter of 703PA, and a new Kingdom of the Vale was born.</p> <p>Gelmsburg never accepted the Counter Queen. In 713PA a Gelmish host came south in the <a href="90 Days War" class="internal-link">90 Days War</a>, which ended inconclusively after the Battle of Silverfields and the death of the mage knight George the Brave.</p> <p>In 822PA King Thormace was deposed in the Lavellan Coup by Moira, Countess of Lavellan, a powerful and ruthless subject who saw her chance. Thormace had grown reliant on a gnomish advisor, <a href="Hikon Zix" class="internal-link">Hikon Zix</a>, and the victors claimed that the gnome had held the king's mind with enchanted jewels. A great part of the court was put to death without trial, Zix was quartered, and Moira was crowned. Bad harvests followed, and the commons soon grumbled that this would not have happened under old King Thormace. Rumours that the Hornsleys had not all died never ceased, and around 830PA a boy known as Prince Brittlehorn was hailed as the true heir by some small communities, before Moira had him brought to court and put to a cruel death. The full story is told on the page of <a href="Moira I" class="internal-link">Moira I</a>.</p> <p>Moira's son Gadvin I took the throne in 838PA and became a much beloved monarch. In 841PA Eldmar joined Gelmsburg and Urbridge in the <a href="League of the Gryphon" class="internal-link">League of the Gryphon</a>, which Eldmar is sometimes counted the leader of, and it fought in both <a href="Wars of the Gryphon" class="internal-link">Wars of the Gryphon</a>. The rivalry with Gelmsburg lived on inside the League. The crown passed from Gadvin to his daughter Moira II, and in 899PA to Moira III, who rules today.</p> <h2>Eldmar in 927PA</h2> <p>Queen Moira III has ruled for nearly three decades. In early 926PA she and Lord Gorlan of Urbridge pledged themselves to the Green Banner of <a href="Coster of Melmbirth" class="internal-link">Coster of Melmbirth</a> in the <a href="Garmish Succession Crisis" class="internal-link">Garmish Succession Crisis</a>. The alliance was sealed by marriage, since Coster wed her youngest daughter, Princess Lena, in 919PA. Beyond the marriage, the Queen is moved by the shared Gethric cause of the north and by the hope of lands in northern Garmeley. The knights of Eldmar are led in the war by Redbor of Tanverley, the heir to the County of Tanverley, a renowned fighter and a proud son of Eldmar, and they ride with Prince <a href="Jakub Ralpia" class="internal-link">Jakub</a> and the <a href="Knights of the Unicorn" class="internal-link">Knights of the Unicorn</a> in the raids on eastern Garmeley.</p> <p>The war has left the valley thin. The Queen knows what the Gelmish host gathered on the Redwood border means, whatever the harvest levy is called, and she treats it as a bluff that she cannot afford to call. The throne itself is secure. The heir is the Queen's eldest son, Prince Gadlain, called the Easy, a man of extreme proportions and very obese, who already has children of his own by his wife, a daughter of the Count of Tanverley.</p>    <h2>People of Interest</h2> <p>Moira, third of her Name of House Lavellan<br> Queen in Eldmar</p> <p>Gadlain "the Easy" of House Lavellan<br> Prince of Eldmar, heir to the throne</p> <p>Lena of House Lavellan<br> Princess of Eldmar, Countess of Melmbirth by marriage</p> <p>Redbor of Tanverley<br> Leader of the knights of Eldmar, heir to the County of Tanverley</p> <p><a href="Moira I" class="internal-link">Moira of Lavellan</a><br> First Queen in Eldmar of House Lavellan</p> <p><a href="Hubert of Tanverley" class="internal-link">Hubert of Tanverley</a><br> Author and zoologist</p>
