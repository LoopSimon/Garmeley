<p> One of the petty Kingdoms claiming to be the one true successor of the historical great Realm of <a href="Urland" class="internal-link">Urland</a>, the Domain of Eldmar stretches throughout the Eld Valley, between the Trollback Mountains, the Ironwood and western banks of the Eld River. Historically ruled by the Queens of House Eldmar, they remain the weakest of the Urien minors. Many of its ancestral traditions actually back and refer to the old Realm of Arester, including its more egalitarian culture and treatement of women.</p>
<p>Following the Hobgoblin Invasion, the Fall of the West, Ricter of Gelms faltered ploy to unleash death upon the lands and a broken and  destroyed Kingdom, most of the urien nobility was disillusioned with the idea of a united front rallying behind yet another powerful leader against the hobgoblin menace. The Duchess of Eldmar at the time, Theara of Hornsley, gathered a large host of the eastern nobles and landowners behind her that flat out refused the call of Ricters brother Jorwyle to unite again behind the House of Gelm. This initially reactionary movement was tested when Jorwyle led his northern knights in a surprising move against the united lords of Eldmar and was only bested with a quickly raised army of the valley itself. A common threat and enemy bred the sentiment that, under an agreed upon Charter granting the Counts and Barons a variety of rights, the Duchess Theara would be created as a "Counter Queen" to the Lords of Gelm herself, challenging the notion that only one King ruled in Urland and was above the others. It was so that Duchess Theara of Hornsley was crowned as Queen Theara I of Eldmar  in the Winter of  703PA and a new Kingdom of the Vale was born.</p>
