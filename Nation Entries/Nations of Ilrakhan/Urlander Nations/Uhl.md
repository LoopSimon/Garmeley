<p> The Free City of Uhl, once part of the now fallen realm of <a href="Urland" class="internal-link">Urland</a>, is a Trading City on the Gullsong Sea and one of the Torn Seas "three great Ports" alongside Sallesand and Trazlov. Uhl is one of the largest cities in the entire known world with a population of around 130.000. The city itself administrates various territories surrounding it and leads the Pact of Cities, a loose alliance of various regional cities with economic alignment that seek the protection of Uhl, primarily the Counties of Talshol, Gaelsby, and Osbury. Following the <a href="War of Horn and Fang" class="internal-link">War of Horn and Fang</a>, Uhl is officially "free" of vihri or meadi influence and its independent status guaranteed by both superpowers in the <a href="Treaty of Uhl" class="internal-link">Treaty of Uhl</a>, which also protects its monopoly on the continents primary currency, the Gryphon.</p>

<p>While Uhl and its citizens love to style themselves as republic, the power within the city and its surroundings has always been held by the noble and patrician families as the disproportionate power of the merchant class derived from their wealth is unshakably anchored to the cities foundations at this point. The Count himself, while without a doubt powerful and influential, rules practically at the whim of the dominant members of the House of Seniority, an assembly of the most powerful families within Uhl that must agree on all major decisions of the Count and essentially dictate politics. </p> 
<p>The County of Uhl was nominally part of the Duchy of Turfield during the time of the Kingdom of Urland until the Fall of West Urland and the Splintering of the Realm at the beginning of the 8th century. Duke Oswald III of Turfield fell alongside the royal family of Urland and a majority of its entrenched nobility when the siege of the capital came to an end, and many local counts and barons of Turfield went on to gather under the Banner of Jorwyn of Gelm in 703PA instead when he proclaimed himself "King in <a href="Gelmsburg" class="internal-link">Gelmsburg</a>". The initial confusion, civil conflict with the nobility of the <a href="Eldmar" class="internal-link">Eldmari</a> and overall despair over not only the Hobgoblin Invasion itself but also the affair surrounding Jorwyns brother Ricter saw Count Foster of Uhl simply bide his time and neglect to swear allegiance to either party.</p> 
<p>When Duke Abelard von Kran, the ruler of <a href="Lesmere" class="internal-link">Lesmere</a> marched his armies at Hasterhall and across the Bow to "pacify and protect the poor souls of the land" in a blatantly opportunistic conquest in 719PA, and his son Kaspar von Kran assembled troops to march even further east in 735PA, the Count of Uhl accepted the offer of Ras Vormil I of <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Vihr</a> and the might of the eastern Kingdom projected itself onto the lands of the Bay. While Uhls safety from foreign spears was guaranteed by Vihr, its new overlord exacted heavy tolls and financial obligations on the City from hundreds of kilometres away and only almost half a century later, following the events of the War of Horn and Fang between Vihr and <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a> over control of the City and the Bay, would Uhl regain true independence.</p> 

<h2>Economy</h2>
<p>Uhl is especially associated with the Vrieman Banker Family and can be considered the capital of early capitalism. It has benefitted majorly from the establishment and expansion of the Urien Post, a postal service established in 913PA. The metal business of the Zelsner Family that owned the Uhl mint at Talshol is another example of an Uhlian burgher family holding a near monopoly on a certain industry, in this case the bank and metal business of the Vrieman and Zelsner has created a large economic boom within the city and attracted more and more people.</p>
