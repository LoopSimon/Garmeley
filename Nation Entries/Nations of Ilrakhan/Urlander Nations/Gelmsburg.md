It was formed in 703PA out of the Splintering of <a href="Urland" class="internal-link">Urland</a> that followed the <a href="Hobgoblin Invasion" class="internal-link">Hobgoblin Invasion</a>, when Jorwyle of Gelm was crowned King in Gelmsburg, the seat of the most powerful house to survive the fall of the west. The city was never the capital of Urland, which was Dursan, but it has been the capital of its own kingdom ever since, and it is the seat of the Archbishop of Ur. The realm runs from the Urhills in the north to the Redwood in the south and takes in the Gelmsgrove and most of the Field of Tears. Together with the knights of <a href="Urbridge" class="internal-link">Urbridge</a> in the south it forms the main bulwark against the hobgoblins of <a href="Ak-Saray" class="internal-link">Ak-Saray</a>, who hold the lands west of the <a href="Trinmo" class="internal-link">Trinmo</a>.</p> <h2>Geography</h2> <p>Gelmsburg lies east of the Trinmo and reaches back toward the <a href="Trollbacks" class="internal-link">Trollback Mountains</a>, beyond which lies the great kingdom of <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Vihr</a>. It is hill and woodland country, and the old Gim lands lie at its heart. In the north the Urhills rise toward the county of <a href="Uhl" class="internal-link">Uhl</a>, and below them lies the Field of Tears, the broad plain on which the armies of Vihr and Meadrath fought two of the great battles of the War of Horn and Fang. The Gelmsgrove lies around the capital, and in the south the Redwood stands between the realm and the lands of <a href="Eldmar" class="internal-link">Eldmar</a>. Gobsfall Keep holds the crossing of the Trinmo where the hobgoblin advance was stopped in 696PA.</p> <p>The old Duchy of Turfield, which once reached from the Bay of Uhl to the Bend of the Trinmo, is divided today. The Crown holds a large part of it, and the rest belongs to Uhl, to the Meadi duchy of <a href="Lesmere" class="internal-link">Lesmere</a> and to <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a>. The city of Turv on the Bend, which was Gelmish for half a century, is held by Gisvale and claimed by Gelmsburg. In the south lies the enclave of the <a href="Trifflands" class="internal-link">Free Lands of Triff</a>, once a Gelmish duchy and now beyond the reach of the Crown.</p>

The Gelmish are a Gethric people through and through, and the old Turvik practices survive only as rare rural customs.</p> 

<h2>Government and the Crown</h2> <p>Gelmsburg is a feudal monarchy. The Crown has been held by House Gelm since Jorwyle, who was crowned King in Gelmsburg in 703PA by the mage knight <a href="George the Brave" class="internal-link">George the Brave</a>. Jorwyle also claimed the title of King of Urland, and the Gelmish kings have never given it up. It stands in the full style used at a coronation and is dusted off whenever Eldmar gives offence, but no neighbour has ever acknowledged it in over two centuries, and in practice the kings are Kings in Gelmsburg.</p> <p>The King rules through the counts and barons of his lands, many of them the descendants of the lords of Turfield who gathered under Jorwyle's banner in 703PA, and his strength lies in the largest standing host of any Urien state. It is built on heavy Gim cavalry, the garrison of Gobsfall Keep and the chapter house of the <a href="Order of the Silver Wheel" class="internal-link">Order of the Silver Wheel</a>, which keeps a house in the capital. The kingdom is rich in grain from the Field of Tears and the Gelmsgrove, in iron from the Urhills and in timber from the Redwood, and the Crown takes tolls on the road to Uhl.</p> <p>Klovis I of House Gelm has ruled since 889PA. His eldest son and heir is Talion, the Green Prince, who takes his name from the green mantle he wears over his armour. Talion is a Paladin of the Silver Wheel and one of the most famous jousting champions in Ur. He rode in the tourney held at Niedrest in February 923PA, until the <a href="Niedrest Incident" class="internal-link">Niedrest Incident</a> brought it to an end. He is a friend of Prince <a href="Jakub Ralpia" class="internal-link">Jakub of Vihr</a> and would join him in the Garmish war if he could, but his father has forbidden it. It is widely believed that if Jakub were ever to make a play for the throne of Vihr against his elder brother after the death of Ras <a href="Vormil III" class="internal-link">Vormil III</a>, and if Talion were king by then, Gelmsburg would back him openly.</p> <h2>Religion</h2> <p>Gelmsburg is a <a href="Gethricism" class="internal-link">Gethric</a> kingdom and has stayed loyal to Iztaw through every quarrel of the church, the Schism of 764PA included. The Archbishop of Ur sits in Gelmsburg. When Dursan fell the old Archbishop died in the siege, and the seat was eventually relocated to the city, an early act of the Kings of Gelm that gave them a legitimacy their rivals could not match. The present Archbishop is Gadwin V, and it was an Archbishop of Ur, Iselher I, who called the faithful to the League of the Gryphon at Pearfawl in 841PA. Many hold, however, that the most respected bishop in all of Ur is not the Archbishop at all, but <a href="Nestor IX" class="internal-link">Nestor Liston</a>, the aged Bishop of Niedrest, who has kept clear of the politics of Iztaw all his life.</p> <p><a href="Andre" class="internal-link">Saint Andre</a>, the Unicorn Knight, is the most popular saint in Gelmsburg. The Cathedral of Saints Triumph, raised in his honour after his canonisation in 876PA, is in the final stages of its construction and will be one of the great churches of Ur.</p>  <p>The <a href="Gelmsburg Circle" class="internal-link">Gelmsburg Circle</a> is the old college of mages of the kingdom, always in the service and under the eye of the Dukes and Kings of Gelm, and it is a member of the <a href="Orenburg Pact" class="internal-link">Orenburg Pact</a>. The King of Gelmsburg signs the <a href="Wermian Accords" class="internal-link">Wermian Accords</a>. The <a href="Brass Spire" class="internal-link">Brass Spire</a> in the capital is one of the most infamous prisons in the western world, said to have held sorcerers such as <a href="Morfeld" class="internal-link">Morfeld</a> and Uthir in its time. No mage has ever broken out of it.</p> <p>Its best-known prisoner is Vinfried <a href="Kabernos" class="internal-link">Kabernos</a>, the former Courtmage of Gisvale, who was arrested in the city by knights of the Silver Wheel with the approval of Klovis I in 913PA and has been held there since. His trial, set for 915PA, was delayed indefinitely, and nothing is publicly known of his state. The Church of Iztaw has stood behind the Crown. Gisvale and the lords of western Meadrath have not let the matter rest. The toll houses of Gisvale at Erlmeet are slow for Gelmish merchants, the western lords petition the Royal Congress, and the court of Queen Gisela IV is cool toward Gelmsburg. The affair feeds the older rivalry between Gisvale and Gelmsburg, and it is whispered that Klovis sees the mage as a card worth more alive than dead, to be traded one day for Meadi silence on some other matter. The Silver Wheel wants a trial.</p> <h2>History</h2> <p>Gelmsburg was the ducal seat of the Dukes of Gelm under Urland, among the strongest of the Gim cities after Dursan. Its Circle was famous long before the kingdom, and it was at Gelmsburg that Tronos of the Veil put forward his thesis in 354PA. When the hobgoblins came out of the <a href="Kortopis" class="internal-link">Kortopis</a> in 690PA, Jorwyle of Gelm, Duke of Gelm, was among the lords who gathered at Gobsfall Keep, where the Tirbo horde was broken in 696PA. Urland fell to pieces afterwards. In 702PA Jorwyle's brother Ricter tried to use Morfeld's Key to destroy the invaders and crown himself King of all Urland, at the price of the city of Lindmol, and was killed by adventurers before he could. In 703PA Jorwyle was crowned King in Gelmsburg by George the Brave, who hoped for a quick reunion of all Urland, and claimed the title of King of Urland as well. The lords of the Eld Valley refused his call, his northern knights were beaten by a hastily raised army of the valley, and in the winter of 703PA those lords crowned Theara of Hornsley Queen of Eldmar. The claim to Urland found no takers.</p> <p>The Gelmish kings never accepted the Counter Queen of Eldmar. In 713PA Gelmsburg attacked Eldmar in the <a href="90 Days War" class="internal-link">90 Days War</a>, which ended inconclusively after the Battle of Silverfields and the death of George the Brave, and the hope of a quick reunion of Urland died with him. In 766PA Remillion declared the Free Lands of Triff, and in 767PA he slew the Duke of Triff and his host,, and the Crown lost a duchy that it has never retaken. In the decades after, King Gandry II of Gelmsburg courted the mathematician <a href="Jevana" class="internal-link">Jevana</a> of Leston, a member of the Circle, and her refusals are often held to be the reason for her parting from it.</p> <p>In 841PA Gelmsburg, Eldmar and Urbridge formed the <a href="League of the Gryphon" class="internal-link">League of the Gryphon</a>, and Gelmsburg is a member and sometimes counted the leader of it, although the League has never had a single commander. King Edwyn I raised the Gelmish host for the first war and never saw combat himself. It fought both <a href="Wars of the Gryphon" class="internal-link">Wars of the Gryphon</a>. The first ended in 843PA with the reconquest of Turv, which stayed Gelmish for half a century. In the second, King Folk II led the League, a warrior king said to move quicker than any man should and to grow twice his size in the heat of battle, so that he could wield a longsword in each hand, and the Gelmish called him Greatfolk. He took the Blight at Merbridge in 888PA and died of it in 889PA, and Klovis I took the crown. In 896PA Turv was lost in the <a href="War of 896" class="internal-link">War of 896</a>, when a coup of its citizens backed by Gisvale overthrew Gelmish rule and the Archduke's knights marched in to save the city. Gelmsburg has never accepted the loss, and relations between the two have been abysmal ever since.</p> <h2>Gelmsburg in 927PA</h2> <p>Gelmsburg has taken no side in the <a href="Garmish Succession Crisis" class="internal-link">Garmish Succession Crisis</a>, and the King's mind is not known. The royal host is kept under arms on the border toward the Eld Valley, officially for the harvest levy. Envoys sit at the court of Eldmar and with the Green Banner League, and Gelmish merchants and captains sell grain, horses and arms to both camps. With the knights of Queen Moira raiding eastern Garmeley the Eld Valley lies thin, and many at court see a chance to profit from it. Whether Klovis will wait for Eldmar to bleed or will take what he can has not been decided.</p> <p>What worries the King's counsellors more than Eldmar is Vihr. The Ras has officially condemned the intervention in Garmeley, but his son Jakub fights in it all the same at the head of a powerful order of knights, and Gelmsburg does not want to stand on the wrong side of a quarrel that may yet split the royal house of Vihr. At home, the matter of Kabernos and the old quarrel with Gisvale over Turv have cost the kingdom friends in the west, and the western lords are watching.</p>   <h2>People of Interest</h2> <p>Klovis, first of his name of House Gelm<br> King in Gelmsburg</p> <p>Talion "the Green Prince" of House Gelm<br> Prince of Gelmsburg, Paladin of the Silver Wheel</p> <p>Gadwin V<br> Archbishop of Ur</p> <p><a href="George the Brave" class="internal-link">George the Brave</a><br> Mage Knight, crowned the first King in Gelmsburg</p> <p>Jorwyle of Gelm<br> First King in Gelmsburg</p> <p><a href="Kabernos" class="internal-link">Vinfried Kabernos</a><br> Prisoner in the Brass Spire</p>
