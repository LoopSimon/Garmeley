The rich farmlands around the bends of the <a href="Trinmo" class="internal-link">Trinmo</a> have seen great historic wars, Kings and Emperors and multiple civil wars, but always <a href="Giselstatt" class="internal-link">Giselstatt</a> remained one of the most important cities on the subcontinent. Now the "Gate to <a href="Meadland" class="internal-link">Meadland</a>" is ruled as an Archduchy under Meadrath and afforded a great amount of freedom, much to the dismay of its northern rival <a href="Lesmere" class="internal-link">Lesmere</a>.</p> <h2>Land</h2> <p>Gisvale stretches from the waterfall at Erlmeet, where the Erl flows into the Trinmo, along the Bend of the river to the Bendburgs of Trinfurt, Barbridge and Turv and the Silentree Forest. At its heart lie the lands of the actual Gisvale, south of <a href="Giselstatt" class="internal-link">Giselstatt</a> and north of the <a href="Kortopis" class="internal-link">Kortopi</a> mountains, luscious and arable farmlands that have historically supported a larger population than the north, and the old moor of Gismoor along the river to the west. Druell, Musseling, Gerbrueck and Giswald are towns of these fields, the Tower of Alban stands in the hills of the west.</p> <p>The feet of the Kortopi hills used to be ruled by one of the fearsome Ogrelords of the past, and the higher hills and vales are still pestered by the brutish creatures. The kings of Gisvale fought them for centuries, and <a href="Ardol III" class="internal-link">Ardol III</a> is remembered as the Ogre Hammer, who took the eastern foothills near the Bend of the river from the ogres of the vale, although many of their hundreds of valleys were never truly bound by human hands. The border disputes with Lesmere mainly concern the Urien plains to the east on the other side of the Trinmo's Bend, although both Duchies tread carefully to avoid escalation and confrontation with the City of <a href="Uhl" class="internal-link">Uhl</a> itself. Turv, the last of the Bendburgs, was not always Gisvale's. It was won by conquest from <a href="Gelmsburg" class="internal-link">Gelmsburg</a> in the <a href="War of 896" class="internal-link">War of 896</a>, a loss the Gelmish crown has never accepted, and the Turvi of the lower Bend, called humourless by their neighbours, still live there beside the Gismer.</p> <h2>The Archduke and the Crown</h2> <p>The Archduchy was created at the Treaty of Leis in 786PA for Kilean von Kor, who had lost the Twins War against his brother, and he was permitted to rule his lands independently. The cadet house he founded took its name von Mart from Marten, his firstborn son, who died after the Battle of Leis. The present Archduke Karel I is the first Karel of the house, not the first Archduke. His son Frederick, Count of Banburg, married Queen Gisela IV in 919PA and is her consort.</p> <p>As an Archduchy, similarly to <a href="Dirneux" class="internal-link">Dirneux</a>, Gisvale enjoys an elevated standing within the Meadi nobility, and the Archduke once held a permanent seat at the High Council. Since the creation of the <a href="Royal Congress" class="internal-link">Royal Congress</a> the title has been largely one of prestige, although it still carries a permanent chair in the First Chamber. The Black Hound on red is the badge of the von Mart. The old Dragon Banner of <a href="Gismar" class="internal-link">Gismar</a>, raised in the victory over Medlan, is kept in <a href="Giselstatt" class="internal-link">Giselstatt</a> as a relic of the Crown and flown on feast days.</p> <h2>Faith and Folk</h2> <p>The Gismer take their name from Gismar, who slew the dragon Heximar in elder days, and the kings of old Gisvale ruled as his heirs. Even before the fall of the Auguri the people of the valley were set apart from their Meadi and Weadi cousins by their close ties to northern Urland and their worship of the Turvik gods, and the old shrines and the hill-burgs of the Auguri age still crown the hills. <a href="Turvik Paganism" class="internal-link">Turvik customs</a> live on as folk tradition, and Green Week is kept here with vigour.</p> <p>Yet the Bendburgs are especially heavy with Trinicism. In 764PA the Conclave of Trinfurt, supported by Queen <a href="Gisela II" class="internal-link">Gisela II</a>, led to the Gethric Schism, and the new church took its name from the city. When the Knights of the <a href="Order of the Silver Wheel" class="internal-link">Silver Wheel</a> tried to dissolve the Conclave by force, the militia of Trinfurt and the forces of the Meadi king held the Iron Tower against them. The <a href="Order of the Iron Tower" class="internal-link">Order of the Iron Tower</a> was founded in its memory and is strongest here, and the monks of the Abbey of the Even Touch north of the city are its patrons.</p> <h2>Giselstatt and Trade</h2> <p><a href="Giselstatt" class="internal-link">Giselstatt</a> is one of the great metropolises of all Meadland, the centre of the Kingdom's banking and international trade. Fairs are held six times a year, and alum, glass, spices and dyes from the south pass through its markets, where the merchants of the Gisvale Trade Company keep their houses. The city also has a famous brewing culture and a great festival of beer and ale, known as Goodmans March, held in the first week of March. The farmlands around it send their grain down the Trinmo, and at Druell, a major town of the Archduchy, the painter <a href="Migner" class="internal-link">Andreas Migner</a> was born to a grain merchant.</p> <h2>History</h2> <p>The Kingdom of Gisvale was founded in 273PA by Ardol I of the House of Gismar and ruled from <a href="Giselstatt" class="internal-link">Giselstatt</a> for almost four centuries, as the <a href="Rulers of the Meadi" class="internal-link">Rulers of the Meadi</a> record. Ardol V "the Blessed" became its first Gethric born king in 429PA. In 589PA Tibor II was betrayed and supplanted by Diedrik of <a href="Medlan" class="internal-link">Medlan</a>, and the long <a href="War of Trout and Drake" class="internal-link">War of Trout and Drake</a> of 631 to 664PA was fought largely across Gisvale. Tens of thousands died and the rich fields were devastated. In 659PA the Count of Mussling, Ringil's most formidable wizard, fell at Summerlake, and in the same years the necromancer <a href="Morfeld" class="internal-link">Morfeld</a> made himself a King of the Undead in the hills of the Bend and the Silentree Forest until his capture in 666PA. In 664PA <a href="Giselstatt" class="internal-link">Giselstatt</a> was conquered, the line of Diedrik extinguished and the Dragon Banner raised, and Liara I of Gismar was restored and wed Gunther III of Korsfall, founding the union of Meadrath.</p> <p>In 851PA, in the <a href="War of Horn and Fang" class="internal-link">War of Horn and Fang</a>, the Army of Gisvale marched east to reinforce the siege of Uhl, but the von Kran of Lesmere denied it their support, so great was the old rivalry, and it was beaten alone at the Battle of Dilfields. In 913PA the Archduke's court magus <a href="Kabernos" class="internal-link">Vinfried Kabernos</a>, born at Barbridge, was arrested at <a href="Gelmsburg" class="internal-link">Gelmsburg</a> by the Knights of the Silver Wheel, an affair that has deepened the old rivalry between the two realms. The Archduchy has also been home to the court magus <a href="Galiatar Inkfingers" class="internal-link">Galiatar Inkfingers</a>, born at Trinfurt, and to <a href="Pavo of Alban" class="internal-link">Pavo of Alban</a>, one of the greatest living magical authorities, who dwells in his tower in the south.</p>
