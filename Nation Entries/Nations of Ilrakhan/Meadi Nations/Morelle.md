<p>The Duchy of Morelle is a subject to the Kingdom of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a> and located between the Westerlands and the Whisperhills alongside the Mead River, it is ruled by the Dukes du Bregelle from its ancient Capital of Morelle. Morelle is the successor of the ancient petty Kingdom of Morelle that claimed dominance over north-wester Meadland; alongside Rulais & Korsfall, it was a successor of the fallen <a href="Auguri Dominion" class="internal-link">Auguri Dominion</a> that formed at the so called "Breaking of the Circle" around 260PA.</p>
<p>Morelle was once the premier vassal of Korsfall, and alongside <a href="Dirneux" class="internal-link">Dirneux</a> and later <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a> the only subject granted the title of Archduchy. However following the Meadi-Othiri War of the 820s a plot, now known as the Fiseux Uprising, by the Archduke to assassinate and usurp King Giselher I was unearthed. The now severely crippled King had Archduke Platis hanged alongside his two eldest sons, revoked the title of Archduchy of the du Bregelle line and granted northern Morelle and the important port of Meadhavn to the then Count von Firstberg, creating the new Duchy of <a href="Mear" class="internal-link">Mear</a>. The du Bregelle are now, over a century later, still hailed as Oathbreakers behind their backs and "Platis" has become synonymous with traitor in the meadi tongue.</p>
