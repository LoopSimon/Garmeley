Several princely rulers and kings have tried to subjugate them over the history of the Lowsteps, but no military invasion was ever truly successful. Their location between the two large regions of <a href="Othirdon" class="internal-link">Othirdon</a> and <a href="Meadland" class="internal-link">Meadland</a>, and a socio-economic order that rejects the dominant feudal culture of contemporary Inara, have made them a cultural melting pot between the Meadi and the Othiri.</p> <h2>Land and People</h2> <p>The Freelands fill the Lowsteps, the long chain of foothills that runs south from the Urdfall Peaks and shuts the valley of the Mead off from Othirdon. <a href="Cimfon" class="internal-link">Cimfon</a> lies beyond the hills in the west, and in the east the land falls to the Mead and the duchies of <a href="Mear" class="internal-link">Mear</a> and <a href="Morelle" class="internal-link">Morelle</a>. The land and its villages belong to the clans and villages themselves, the councils of the peasant republics choose their own speakers, and there are no hereditary lords. The head of the confederation is the Voxnos, a title that means "our Voice", chosen by the councils of the cities. At present the title is held by Lopera Hermetell, and the Queen of Meadrath, at present <a href="Gisela IV" class="internal-link">Gisela IV</a>, is the Lady Protector of the Freelands.</p> <p>Yeghall, the capital, is less a city than a vast gathering of merchants, much like a giant caravanserai. The best known fortune of the Freelands is that of the Gilderberg clan of Yeghall, whose company, Gilderberg Shipping and Logistics, is rich and flourishing and sends its caravans far beyond the Lowsteps. <a href="Renard Gilderberg" class="internal-link">Renard Gilderberg</a>, the third son of the Patriarch Arnold, was born in Yeghall.</p> <h2>The Charter of 821</h2> <p>In 821PA an effective civil war broke out within the Freelands. The communities of the pro Meadi bloc stood for a more centralised confederation, while a sizeable portion of the vales leaned towards Cimfon, and Queen Margaret II supported the centralising movement. She needed to take drastic action, or the pro Cimfon communities would have prevailed and the vales would likely have been occupied and fortified by the knights of the west. The Charter of 821, by which the Freelands were recognised at Korsfall, was that step, and three years later the <a href="Meadi Othiri War" class="internal-link">Meadi Othiri War</a> began. Today the Freelands are bound to the Crown by the protection it gives them, and the <a href="Knights of the Green Tree" class="internal-link">Knights of the Green Tree</a> of Cimfon, who have lately come under criticism for their conduct towards the halfling cities, still dispute land and sacred sites with them in the eastern highlands, as they have done for a long time.</p>
