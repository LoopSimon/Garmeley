<p>The Landgraviate of Weisch is a subject of the Kingdom of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a> located in the north of the realm bordering the Bloody Strait and the <a href="Blijgrat" class="internal-link">Blijgrat League</a>. During the reign of <a href="Gisela II" class="internal-link">Gisela II</a>, known as "the Good" to posterity, the <a href="Yahrwick" class="internal-link">Saint Yahrwick of Zeeport</a> freed the city and the northern bloody strait from baeling hands in 737PA and forever broke the hold of the northerners over the bloody strait and the coast of Meadland in what is now known as the Battle of Zeeport. Gisela acted quick and declared all the lands north of the Bela river rightful clay of the Crown, dispatching various armies and lords to fortify and defend the northern frontier not only against baeling resurgence but also the emerging and powerful weadi city states that had yet to unite as the League of Blijgrat. </p>
<p>Queen Gisela would create the Landgraviate of Weisch, named after the Weisch River, and grant it further lands unified under a single title, although nominally not on the same rank as a Duchy, the Landgraviate has historically held an extremely important role within Meadi politics and acted as an extension of the Crowns personal power and its most loyal subject ever since.</p>
