<p>The Duchy of Risfalt is a subject of the Kingdom of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a> and, alongside <a href="Wargel" class="internal-link">Wargel</a>, one of the two Duchies created in the decades following the Unification of Meadrath, breaking up the former Lands of Medlan and rewarding the von Praum as loyal allies to the von Kor dynasty.</p>
<p>Risfalt is home to Bellhome and with it not only the informal Archbishop, or due to schism related policies of the <a href="Church of Iztaw" class="internal-link">Church of Iztaw </a>"Primat", of all Meadland but also its largest house of Trinicist Worship and the countries centre of Faith, the Bellhome Cathedral, currently lead by Bishop Philip Dedrik Varascgone.</p>
