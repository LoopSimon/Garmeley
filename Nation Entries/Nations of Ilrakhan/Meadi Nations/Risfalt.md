Alongside <a href="Wargel" class="internal-link">Wargel</a> it is one of the two Duchies created in 682PA, when King Gildher II broke up the former Lands of <a href="Medlan" class="internal-link">Medlan</a> and rewarded the von Praum as loyal allies to the von Kor dynasty. It is ruled from Praum, and its Duke, Felix von Praum, is at present also Marshall of the Kingdom, an office that belongs to no duchy and that the Crown has given to many a high noble before him, and not only to dukes.</p> <h2>Land</h2> <p>Risfalt holds the Meadi Highlands, the hill country that rises towards the Kortopi Mountains, and reaches from the Crownlands in the north to the foot of the mountain wall in the south. In the west it borders <a href="Dirneux" class="internal-link">Dirneux</a> and in the east Wargel. Praum stands in the north of the duchy and Bellhome lies deeper in the hills to the south of it. Larbrick, Nebil, Gomspring and Sidge, the last on the border with Wargel, are the other towns of the duchy. The Lind rises from the lake of Drakesbleed in the north and runs west past the Lindfalls to Jera and the Mead, and the names of this country remember the dragon. The Drakesfall Hills lie just across the northern border, and the people of the Lind hold that the gorge in which <a href="Gismar" class="internal-link">Gismar</a> hid from the Black Dragon Heximar is the one at the Lindfalls, and that Drakesbleed took its name from the blood of the beast.</p> <h2>Praum and Bellhome</h2> <p>Praum is older than any kingdom of the Meadi. It was the capital of the <a href="Auguri Dominion" class="internal-link">Auguri Dominion</a>, and from it the Sages of Ralfantis ruled over the tribes of the land. Just outside the city, on Sages Hill, stood the Great Stones of the Auguri, which the Sons of the Flame, Gethric zealots, tore down in the 6th century, a deed later judged a travesty. The Sons of the Flame were a movement across the Meadi lands that sought to eradicate Turvik and Auguric memorials. Various bishoprics supported them, but when they threatened to arm themselves as a militia and contest the landed lords, the crown broke them up. Silk came to Praum and Bellhome in the late 9th century, together with Jera, and is made in both to this day, although Meadi silk is held to be of lesser quality than the silk of Cevolia.</p> <p>Bellhome is the centre of the Trinicist faith in Meadland. Its bishop is the informal Archbishop of all Meadland or, owing to policies of the <a href="Church of Iztaw" class="internal-link">Church of Iztaw</a> since the schism, the Primat, and the Bellhome Cathedral is the largest house of Trinicist worship in the country. The Trinicist church knows no hierarchy between its bishops, so the office carries honour and not command. The present Bishop of Bellhome is Philip Dedrik Varascgone, a conservative who looks with suspicion on the new teachings of <a href="Diarism" class="internal-link">Diarism</a>, born at the university of Jera in 894PA.</p> <h2>The House von Praum</h2> <p>The von Praum were counts and vassals of the Lords of Medlan when the <a href="War of Trout and Drake" class="internal-link">War of Trout and Drake</a> began and stood with the Gismar loyalists and the House von Kor. The Count von Praum took in the old court magus <a href="Wulfrick" class="internal-link">Wulfrick</a>, who left Korsfall rather than take part in the war and died at Praum in 661PA. After the union of the Meadi crowns the house was raised to the Dukes of Risfalt, and its snail on green and gold is the personal arms of the house and not a coat made for the duchy.</p> <p>Early in the reign of Queen <a href="Gisela II" class="internal-link">Gisela II</a> the house came close to ruin. The faction of nobles that held a second court at Dellweid and refused to acknowledge the young queen demanded that she marry Duke Otto von Praum or be deposed, and when the Praum Affair ended in the arrest and execution of the ringleaders, von Praum was among them. The duchy was not taken from the house. It passed to Otto's brother Thorsten, and Duke Felix descends from his line.</p> <p>Felix von Praum is known above all as a soldier. In the <a href="Vorellan War" class="internal-link">Vorellan Wars</a> of 910 to 919PA he led the armies of the Crown south through the Pass of <a href="Ott" class="internal-link">Ott</a>, with hundreds of Meadi knights at his side, and his coming tipped the balance of the war.</p>
