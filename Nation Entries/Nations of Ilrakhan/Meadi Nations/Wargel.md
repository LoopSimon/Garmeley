Alongside <a href="Risfalt" class="internal-link">Risfalt</a> it is one of the two Duchies created in 682PA, when King Gildher II broke up the former Lands of Medlan and rewarded the von Buhr as loyal allies to the von Kor dynasty. Wormill, once the seat of the Lords of Medlan, has been the seat of the von Buhr ever since.</p> <h2>Land</h2> <p>The heart of Wargel is the valley of the Trinmo around Wormill, where the river passes the town of Pelling. Upstream, the stretch between Rahn and Wormill is called Melaras Gift, after <a href="Melara" class="internal-link">Melara</a>, the goddess of the river. To the north and east the duchy takes in the southern Trinwood, the wide belt of forested hills that divides the Meadi heartland from the wetlands of the Weadi, and its neighbours are the Crownlands in the north, Risfalt in the west and <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a> in the south, where the border follows the moor of Gismoor. Below the moor a wedge of Wargel reaches down to the foot of the <a href="Kortopis" class="internal-link">Kortopi</a> Mountains. It holds the Allwund and the lands west of the river that runs south below it, as far as the mountains and the Ottian Pass, and the Tower of Alban stands just across that river in Gisvale.</p> <h2>Home of the Scarred</h2> <p>Wargel is often called the Home of the Scarred. Its duty is to guard the southern Highlands, the central Trinwood and the <a href="Ott" class="internal-link">Ottian</a> Pass, one of the great trade roads through the mountains between Meadland and Cevolia, not only from robber barons but also from the remnants of the Ogrelord bands and from more terrible monsters. The name comes from the veterans of the border guard, who carry the scars of ogres and robber barons home from their service. By the pass the armies of the Crown went south in the <a href="Vorellan War" class="internal-link">Vorellan Wars</a>.</p> <p>Within the duchy lies the Allwund, a massive crater where an object from beyond the world fell to earth thousands of years ago. It left behind the Wundore, a dark, heavy ore that is found only in its vicinity. It is hard to enchant, but smiths value it for hammers, and it is mined at the town of Wundplaz.</p> <h2>The House von Buhr</h2> <p>The von Buhr stood with the Gismar loyalists and the House von Kor in the <a href="War of Trout and Drake" class="internal-link">War of Trout and Drake</a>, a war that was fought at their door. Wormill had been the first seat of Diedrik of Medlan, Lord of Wormill, who supplanted King Tibor II of Gisvale in 589PA. In 655PA the loyalists drove on the city, and after a battle that cost the Gismar line its last two princes, Count Heinrich von Lot took it within days. When the Lands of Medlan were broken up in 682PA, Wormill went to the von Buhr with the new duchy, and the name of Medlan faded within a generation. The two golden gryphons holding a hammer are the personal arms of the house, as the snail is that of the von Praum of Risfalt, and the present Duke is Johann von Buhr. A second son of the house, who stood to inherit no title of his own, became the first Landgraf of <a href="Weisch" class="internal-link">Weisch</a>, and the von der Rend there are a cadet branch of the von Buhr.</p> <p>Wormill and the river towns have given the realm some well known names. The master mason Reynar <a href="Cearic" class="internal-link">Cearic</a> was born at Wormill, and so was the daemonologist <a href="Shymon Epios" class="internal-link">Shymon Epios</a>, who was expelled from Korsfall College after the incident of 916PA. <a href="Pavo of Alban" class="internal-link">Pavo of Alban</a> grew up at Pelling, a smaller town on the Trinmo near Wormill.</p>
