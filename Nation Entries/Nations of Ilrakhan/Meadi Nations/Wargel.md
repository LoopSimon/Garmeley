<p>The Duchy of Wargel is a subject of the Kingdom of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a> and, alongside <a href="Risfalt" class="internal-link">Risfalt</a>, one of the two Duchies created in the decades following the Unification of Meadrath, breaking up the former Lands of Medlan and rewarding the von Buhr as loyal allies to the von Kor dynasty.</p>
<p>Wargel is often called the "Home of the Scarred" due to its duties to both guard the southern Highlands and the <a href="Ott" class="internal-link">ottian</a> Pass as well as the central Trinwood from not only robber barons, but also remnants of the Ogrelord bands and more terrible monsters. Within its borders lies the Allwund, a massive crater where an extra-terrestrial object landed thousands of years ago and has left behind the unique Wundore that can only be found in its vicinity.</p>
