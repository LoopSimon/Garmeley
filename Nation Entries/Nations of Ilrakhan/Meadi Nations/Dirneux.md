<p>The Archuchy of Dirneux is one of the oldest persisting human realms in the world, having been ruled in an unbroken line by specifically the Dukes du Jera for over 500 years. During the Age of Silver, the Petty Kings of Rulais fell to the Kingdom of Korsfall and bend the knee to the Rhilmer, becoming part of the dominant Kingdom of Korsfall and later within it the great Kingdom of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a>. Rulais, alongside Morelle & Korsfall, were the successors of the fallen <a href="Auguri Dominion" class="internal-link">Auguri Dominion</a> that formed at the so called "Breaking of the Circle" around 260PA.</p>
<p>As an Archduchy, similarly to the Archduchy of <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a>, Dirneux is afforded an elevated standing within the Meadi nobility and the Archduke granted a permanent seat at the High Council. Since the creation of the Royal Congress under the late Queen Gisela III and the essential dissolution of the High Council, the title of Archduke has been largely a prestigious one although it is still afforded a permanent Chair within the Royal Congress first Chamber, as are all other premier nobles of the Realm.</p>
