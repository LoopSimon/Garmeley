<p>The Archduchy of Dirneux is one of the oldest persisting human realms in the world, ruled in an unbroken line by the Dukes du Jera for over five hundred years. It holds the upper valley of the Mead in the west of <a href="Meadland" class="internal-link">Meadland</a>, within the Kingdom of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a>, from the Kortopi Mountains in the south to the Meadi Lowlands in the north, and it is the homeland of the High Meadi, whose tongue became the language of the Crown, the courts and the Church. Its capital Jera is the seat of the oldest university in western Ilrakhan.</p>
<h2>Land</h2>
<p>The Mead gathers in the south of the archduchy from the Darkwaters, the many rivers that run down from the mountains through the great forests that share their name. It flows north through Sinkfield and past Jera, where the Lind joins it from the east, and on past Roussan to the border with <a href="Morelle" class="internal-link">Morelle</a>. West of the river lie the Westerlands, in their southern half a country of particularly good farmland below hills that hold tin and silver, rising towards the Arvale Pass and the stronghold of Arkeep, where the road runs west into <a href="Cimfon" class="internal-link">Cimfon</a> and the lands of Othirdon. In the east the valley of the Lind climbs towards Lindfalls and the heart of the Kingdom, and in the south the Darkwater forests stand against the <a href="Kortopis" class="internal-link">Kortopi Mountains</a>, beyond which lie the gnomes of <a href="Ott" class="internal-link">Ott</a>, whose lands Dirneux borders.</p>
<h2>The Archduke and the Crown</h2>
<p>Dirneux and Morelle were held in high regard from the day of their conquest, and their houses have always been counted closer to the royal house than to the rest of the nobility. As an Archduchy, like <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a>, Dirneux enjoys an elevated standing within the Meadi nobility, and the Archduke once held a permanent seat at the High Council. Since the creation of the <a href="Royal Congress" class="internal-link">Royal Congress</a> under Queen Gisela III and the dissolution of the High Council in all but name, the title is largely one of prestige, although it still carries a permanent chair in the First Chamber of the Congress, as do the seats of all premier nobles of the realm. The older style of Duke du Jera, borne by the house since its beginnings, remains in common use.</p>
<p>The house stands for the old guard of the nobility. Its present head, <a href="Jean du Jera" class="internal-link">Jean du Jera</a>, married Queen Gisela III, led the opposition to <a href="Odrick von Fulmer" class="internal-link">Odrick von Fulmer</a> and ruled as Lord-Regent for his daughter <a href="Gisela IV" class="internal-link">Gisela IV</a>. His heir is his son Renard, for under the right of Congenitura the queen, though his firstborn, will not inherit the du Jera title. Renard is at present in Ott on his father's errand.</p>
<h2>Society and Learning</h2>
<p>The people of Dirneux are the High Meadmer, and the great houses of the valley are styled "du" in the manner of the west. They are Trinicist, but the old ways are strong here. Meadoi in the south, below the Darkwaters, was a holy place of congregation for the Auguri, and the old reverence for scholars and mages has never left the valley.</p>
<p>The University of Jera is the oldest in western Ilrakhan and is renowned above all for its schools of law and history, whose graduates fill the courts and chancelleries of the Kingdom. In 894PA the theologian <a href="Perrin Diar" class="internal-link">Perrin Diar</a> spoke to its scholars and gave birth to <a href="Diarism" class="internal-link">Diarism</a>. The Archduke, a Trinicist with little love for the movement, has never moved against the university, and the Bishop of Jera, Berac Letrouse, an old friend of Perrin Diar and counted among the great living theologians of the Trinicist church, is surprisingly open to the ideals of Diarism.</p>
<h2>Economy</h2>
<p>The wealth of Dirneux rests on its soil and its roads. The farmland of the Westerlands feeds the valley, and its tin and silver supply the smiths and mints of the Kingdom. The Arvale Pass is the great trade road of the west, and the toll revenue of Arkeep made the du Jera one of the most powerful vassals of the Crown. Silk production came to Jera in the late 9th century, and Meadoi has grown a printing industry of its own, smaller than that of Albesan in southern Cevolia but large all the same.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Dirneux was once the Petty Kingdom of Rulais, the realm of the High Meadi and one of the three successors of the Auguri Dominion that rose at the Breaking of the Circle around 260PA, beside Korsfall and Morelle. In the late 3rd century PA the garrison of Arkeep threw back an attack of the Maelzad of Thirdonis in the Five Year War, and the western pass has been held from this valley ever since. In 375PA Korsfall declared war on the King of Rulais following the Feast of Dragons, and in 381PA Rulais fell. Its lands were known as Dirneux from then on, and Thierry du Jera was declared its Duke, the first of the line.</p>
<p>In the 7th century the house gave the Crown a queen, Margaret du Jera, wife of Gildher II and mother of <a href="Gisela II" class="internal-link">Gisela II</a>, whose steady hand as Queen-Mother helped her daughter break the revolt of the great vassals in the first years of her reign.</p>
<p>In 824PA Archduke Bernard du Jera rode into the Arvale beside Prince Giselher and Lord <a href="Rivalt du Chian" class="internal-link">Rivalt du Chian</a>, the Castellan of Arkeep, in the <a href="Meadi Othiri War" class="internal-link">Meadi Othiri War</a>, and after the defeat at Red Oak it was Bernard who brought the army home. He fell, loyal to the Crown, in the Fiseux Uprising of 832PA. One of the conspirators was a magus who channelled his spells through his sword, a blade that seemed to pulse with a life of its own, and Bernard slew him and took up the weapon, only to be consumed by the necrotic energies bound within it. The Meadi still say that a matter has "turned like a blade turned black" when it becomes dire and grave all at once. Bernard's son Jaques died soon after without children, and the archduchy passed to Jaques' brother Bernard the Younger, who led the armies of Dirneux in the War of Horn and Fang.</p>
<p>His son Jean, born in 875PA, became Archduke and the Prince-Consort of Gisela III. When the queen died in 910PA he headed the regency council, banished von Fulmer from court, purged his followers and tried in vain to disband the Royal Congress. In 913PA the young Gisela IV took the government into her own hands, restored von Fulmer and sent her father back to Jera. His follower <a href="Marrin Andil" class="internal-link">Marrin Andil</a>, a lord of Morelle, left for the new world and founded the free port of Ulbreek, and it is rumoured that part of its profits find their way back to Jera, as an insurance for the Archduke should the fortunes of the old guard turn.</p>
