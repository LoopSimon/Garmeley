<p>The Duchy of Mear is a subject of the Kingdom of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a> and located at the delta of the Mead River in the northernmost part of the Kingdom. It was created from the lands of the former Archduchy of <a href="Morelle" class="internal-link">Morelle</a> after the latter was disgraced during the Fiseux Uprising in 832PA and judged guilty by King Giselher I in 833PA. The Duchy is currently ruled by Albrecht von Firsberg, who is also High Mariner of the Kingdom, and has become instrumental in the operations of the <a href="Gesellschaft für Westmeadischen Handel" class="internal-link">K.G.W.</a> in <a href="Othirdon" class="internal-link">Othirdon</a>, <a href="Sertova" class="internal-link">Sertova</a> and most importantly the new world of far <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a>.</p>
<h2>Land</h2>
<p>Mear holds the north-western corner of <a href="Meadland" class="internal-link">Meadland</a>. Its coast runs from Dirdale by way of Loreux on the Maidensea to Meadhavn at the mouth of the Mead, where the river spreads into its delta. Inland the lower Mead winds through low, wet country, and the county of Gloss stands in the hills above it. Further inland the ground rises to the foothills of the Urdfall mountains, beyond which lies the dwarven Hold of <a href="Sulbra-Kol" class="internal-link">Sulbra-Kol</a>. The Silverwoods mark the border with Morelle in the south, and to the east the duchy meets <a href="Zmalig" class="internal-link">Zmalig</a>.</p>
<h2>The Dukes von Firsberg</h2>
<p>The people of Mear are the Low Meadmer of the west, styled "du" in the manner of the west, but their ruling houses were sent from the royalists of the Crownlands when the duchy was made. The Dukes von Firsberg have never forgotten that they owe the coast to one act of loyalty. They keep their estate not in Meadhavn, the capital, but further south at Castle Krenous, with only a small village beneath it.</p>

<p>The Golden Harp in their arms is the ancient symbol of House Firsberg. The inverse dragon is newer and proclaims their loyalty to the royal line von Kor specifically, and not merely to the institution of the Crown. The title of High Mariner of the Kingdom is not bound to a landed title, but by tradition it falls to the Duke of Mear.</p>
<h2>Trade and the Sea</h2>
<p>The Mead carries the grain of its valley and the gold and marble of Morelle to the sea, and all of it passes through the harbours of Mear. The royal navy has its largest base at <a href="Meadhavn" class="internal-link">Meadhavn</a>, but it also docks at Princes Watch and Visdack. Merchant houses such as Whisperhill Shipping have made Meadhavn one of the great ports of the Kingdom.</p>
<p>In 900PA the old guild offices of Zeeport, Meadhavn and Dirdale were united with the Département du Col as the Königliche Gesellschaft für Westmeadischen Handel, in the wake of the <a href="Treaty of Lotsprings" class="internal-link">Treaty of Lotsprings</a>. Its headquarters stand in Meadhavn, and the ships of the K.G.W. sail from the harbours of the duchy to Othirdon, Sertova and the new world in the west.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>The duchy owes its existence to the Fiseux Uprising. When the conspirators moved against the king in 832PA, the Count von Firsberg, a lesser noble of the Crownlands, was visiting the king with his eldest son. In the fighting that followed he took the Count of Fiseux hostage, and the guards of Fiseux seized his son in turn and threatened to kill the boy unless the count was freed. Father and son only nodded to each other, understanding that loyalty to the king came first, and the boy died. The plot was foiled, and Firsberg was rewarded. When Morelle was stripped of its rights in 833PA, its northern lands and the port city of <a href="Meadhavn" class="internal-link">Meadhavn</a> were granted to him, and the line survived through his other children to this day.</p>
<p>The navy of Mear proved its worth in the <a href="War of Horn and Fang" class="internal-link">War of Horn and Fang</a>. In 851PA Duke Thorsten von Firsberg led the Meadi fleet, reinforced by the combined fleets of the Blijgrat cities, against the Vihri and Uhlian fleets at the Battle of the Bay and broke the blockade of Fort Kranig. The Countess of Gloss, <a href="Muriel von Gloss" class="internal-link">Muriel von Gloss</a>, defeated the magus Viagoris at Felhome in 861PA and fell at Gadly Bridge in 865PA, and she is counted among the great names of the duchy.</p>
