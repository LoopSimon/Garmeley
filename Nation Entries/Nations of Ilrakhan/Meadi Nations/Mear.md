<p>The Duchy of Mear is a subject of the Kingdom of Meadrath and located at the delta of the Mead River in the northernmost part of the Kingdom. It was created from the lands of the former Archduchy of Morelle after the latter was disgraced during the Fiseux Uprising in 832PA and judged guilty by King Giselher I in 833PA. The Duchy is currently ruled by Albrecht von Firsberg and has become instrumental in the operations of the <a href="Gesellschaft für Westmeadischen Handel" class="internal-link">K.G.W.</a> in <a href="Othirdon" class="internal-link">Othirdon</a>, <a href="Sertova" class="internal-link">Sertova</a> and most importantly the new world of far <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a>.</p>
