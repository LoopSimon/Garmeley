<div class="wiki-infobox"> <h4 class="wiki-header">Free City of Zeeport</h4> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Country</strong> <span>Free City, tributary of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Government</strong> <span>Baron Cornelis van Zee,<br>the Burgherrad</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Population</strong> <span>ca. 46000</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Heraldic Symbol</strong> <span>White Gull on Blue<br>with a Leaf in its Claws</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Primary Religion</strong> <a href="Trinicism" class="internal-link">Trinicism</a> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Primary Culture</strong> <span><a href="Meadi" class="internal-link">Lotmer</a> <a href="Human" class="internal-link">Human</a></span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>Official Language</strong> <span>Weadi</span> </div> <div class="wiki-row"> <strong>First mentioned:</strong> <span>Early 4th century PA</span> </div> <hr class="wiki-hr"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Flags/Zeeport_Flag.png" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;"> </div> <p>Zeeport is a free city on the Bloody Strait, where the Bela and the Lot reach the sea, and with a little over 46000 people one of the largest cities on the northern coast. It is a Weadi city in culture and a Meadi one in politics. The Crown of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a> counts it as part of the Landgraviate of <a href="Weisch" class="internal-link">Weisch</a> and is paid tribute by it, but the city governs itself, and it holds a unique seat in the <a href="Blijgrat" class="internal-link">Blijgrat League</a>. Its privateers are famous, its merchants are rich, and its patron, Saint <a href="Yahrwick" class="internal-link">Yahrwick</a>, is a pirate who died to free it.</p> <h2>The City</h2> <p>Zeeport has grown up around its harbour at the mouths of the two rivers, and a great windmill stands above it. The Brod, the castle that the Skori built, still stands and belongs to the Baron, in a city that is ruled by the Weadi again. Statues of Saint Yahrwick stand all over Zeeport, and the largest church is Saint Yahrwick's Cathedral, run by Bishop van Fider. The city controls a small amount of land and a number of farming communities around it. Far out in the central Tirean Ocean, between the old world and the new, lies the island of Yahrland, which Zeeporter sailors claimed for the city. It is nothing more than a dozen houses.</p> <h2>Rule of the City</h2> <p>Zeeport has a dual government. At its head stands a noble, the Baron von Zee. The barony is a hereditary title, and it is held at present by Cornelis van Zee, who inherited it when his father Mattias died. Beside the Baron sits the Burgherrad, a council of guild and town leaders, largely merchants and other influential figures. The Burgherrad is the truly powerful body, and the Baron rules more or less at its behest, for his responsibilities are clearly defined. He is no mere figurehead, however. The Baron is expected to ensure the security of the city and its institutions and to protect the traditions of Zeeport, and as one of the richest men in the city he is heard with respect. Technically he even rents the land and the city to the Burgherrad. The arrangement works surprisingly well, largely because the Burgherrad is rarely united and acts like a council of individual patricians, which leaves the Baron a fixed point in the daily politics of the city.</p> <p>The Crown claims Zeeport as part of Weisch and is paid a tribute in the form of a share of the taxes levied on the citizens, in exchange for its protection. In the League the city holds a seat with as much voting power as the three founding cities, but it has no vote in any decision that touches the greater lands of the League or its other members. The seat belongs to the Baron officially, but in practice it is always held by an envoy of the Burgherrad, who introduces himself in a formality as the temporary representative of the Baron. Zeeport stayed out of the <a href="War of Horn and Fang" class="internal-link">War of Horn and Fang</a>.</p> <h2>Trade and the Free Port</h2> <p>Zeeport is invested in both of the great trading companies, the <a href="Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel" class="internal-link">G.U.V.</a> of the League and the <a href="Gesellschaft für Westmeadischen Handel" class="internal-link">K.G.W.</a> of the Crown, although largely not as a city but through its many rich merchant enterprises, and it is credited with using the trade routes and ports of both. Its old guild office was one of those united in the K.G.W. in 900PA. Zeeport is also one of the few cities that actively trade with the <a href="Halfling" class="internal-link">Halflings</a> of <a href="Revelle" class="internal-link">Revelle</a>, who are otherwise extremely hostile to outsiders.</p> <p>The city lives by a privateer structure of its own, in which the prey a captain may take depends on the flag it sails under. The ships of the K.G.W. and the G.U.V. are effectively off limits, and a captain who wants to avoid being hanged in Zeeport leaves them alone. In the 920s Stadtholder <a href="Jan van de Spiegel" class="internal-link">Jan van de Spiegel</a> of the Blijgrat League made deals with various of these privateers to attack Cevoli, Othiri and Sertovi traders coming back across the north Tirean Ocean from the new world.</p>

 <p>The people of Zeeport are Lotmer, the Weadi tribe of the Lot, and they are Trinicists. A number of <a href="Bykmåli" class="internal-link">Bykmåli</a> also live in the city, but mostly Olbellinge, who came with the old Baeling rule, while the Skori are still discriminated against. The city has a bishop of its own, who sits in Saint Yahrwick's Cathedral.</p> <h2>History</h2> <p>The first records of Zeeport go back to the early 4th century PA, when it was a fishing hamlet at the mouths of the Bela and the Lot. By the 6th century it had grown into a port that the <a href="Baeling Empire" class="internal-link">Baeling Empire</a> counted among its proudest conquests, and the <a href="Bykmåli" class="internal-link">Bykmåli</a> called it Brodgard. The Empire fell in 590PA, but the city did not regain its freedom with it. It was held by the Skori of <a href="Vignasoya" class="internal-link">Vignasoya</a>, who built the castle called the Brod and began to outlaw the worship of what they called the candlegods, the faith of <a href="Gethricism" class="internal-link">Gethricism</a>.</p> <p>In the end the city rose against them. According to the legend, Yahrwick, a pirate who had once attacked every ship that crossed his path, but who had come to fight more and more for the independence of the Weadi and against the heathen Skori, slipped through the blockade of Zeeport and brought food to the rebels. He then organised the resistance himself and led the fight against the foreign oppressors who had withdrawn into the Brod, and he died a martyr in the nights of the rebellion. The city was freed in 737PA in what is now known as the Battle of Zeeport. The Bishop of Korsfall canonised Yahrwick, and he did it alone, as a sign against Iztaw that the old church had no monopoly on canonisation. Yahrwick was loved by the Weadi and the northern Meadi, and the other denominations of the faith still reject him.</p> <p>Queen <a href="Gisela II" class="internal-link">Gisela II</a> used the victory to claim all the lands north of the Bela for the Crown, but in the decades after the liberation Zeeport was ruled by its Baron and not by the Crown, always uneasy at home and abroad. By 800PA the city was divided between a sizeable faction that wished to remain independent and others who wished to join the League, but only as an equal partner of the three founders. King Gildher III sent the Landgraf of Weisch with many knights and a sizeable force to bring peace to Zeeport, and the affair came close to catastrophe before all parties reached the Zeeport Agreement. Zeeport stayed independent and pays its tribute for the protection of the Crown, it was allowed to trade with the League and received its unique seat in the Council, and it governs itself as a Free City.</p> <p>In 900PA Baron Mattias van Zee, the father of the present Baron, was instrumental in arranging the <a href="Treaty of Lotsprings" class="internal-link">Treaty of Lotsprings</a> as a semi neutral party, and he signed it.</p> <h2>People of Interest</h2> <p>Cornelis van Zee<br>Baron von Zee and head of the city, son of Mattias and one of the richest men in Zeeport</p> <p>Mattias van Zee<br>Late Baron von Zee and father of Cornelis, who helped to arrange the Treaty of Lotsprings in 900PA and signed it</p> <p><a href="Yahrwick" class="internal-link">Yahrwick</a><br>Pirate saint of liberation and air, who died in the rebellion that freed the city</p> <p>Bishop van Fider<br>Bishop of Zeeport, who runs Saint Yahrwick's Cathedral</p> <p><a href="Kaspar Vasser" class="internal-link">Kaspar Vasser</a><br>Born in Zeeport in 890PA and now head of the Vasser Cartel in Behera, with a more than dubious reputation even by the standards of Zeeport</p>
