<p> Dominated by two mighty Rivers, the Mead in the west and the <a href="Trinmo" class="internal-link">Trinmo</a> to the east, the lands of the meadi are rich and prosperous. Second only to the Gethric Kingdom of <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Vihr</a> in the east, Meadrath remains one of the largest human realms in the known world as far as population is concerned, Meadraths largest cities are the Capital Korsfall, the population centres of Praum, Bellhome, Jera, Morelle and Wormill as well as its largest port Meadhavn. The Banner of the Kingdom shows the Black Dragon <a href="Dragon" class="internal-link">Heximar</a>, known as the Unlit, that was slain at what is now known as Drakesfall Hills by the legendary Hero <a href="Gismar" class="internal-link">Gismar</a>.</p>
<p>Sometimes referred to as the "Meadi Empire", Meadrath projects political authority over official and unofficial vassal states in Meadland but also across the seas, growing increasingly more interested in dominance over both the western half of Ilrakhan but also lands across the ocean in Aisligos. The Country is ruled by the Crown of Meadrath, currently held by Queen <a href="Gisela IV" class="internal-link">Gisela IV</a> as well as the <a href="Royal Congress" class="internal-link">Royal Congress</a>, an advisory board administrating the Kingdom and its dependencies. Meadrath has often been considered one of the "Three Great Powers" of contemporary politics alongside the <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Rascy zi Vihr</a> and the <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae Sultante</a>, as all three nations dominate their regions and keep each other in check, economically, militarily and scientifically.</p>

<h2>Geography</h2>
<p> Most of Meadrath lies in the North-Ilrakhani Plain of <a href="Meadland" class="internal-link">Meadland</a>, with flat terrain crossed by watercourses such as the <a href="Trinmo" class="internal-link">Trinmo</a> and Mead, wetlands and marshy conditions are found close to the <a href="Blijgrat" class="internal-link">Blijgrat border</a> and along the Bloody Coast. It is situated between various Mountain ranges offering natural resources and protection, most notably the <a href="Kortopis" class="internal-link">Kortopis</a> to the south and the Urdfall Peaks to the north-west as well as the Whisperhills and Trinwood elevations. The lands on the left side of the Mead below the Lowsteps are usually referred to as the Westerlands, the region north of the Kortopis is known as the Highlands and the areas north of the Whisperhills as the Drakstal. The Crown's own lands lie around Korsfall in the heart of the country, with the vassal realms ringing them: Dirneux and Morelle along the Mead in the west, Mear and Zmalig on the northern coast, Weisch in the north-east, Risfalt and Wargel in the Highlands, Gisvale on the Bend of the Trinmo, where Trinfurt, Barbridge and Turv are known together as the Bendburgs, Lesmere in the east and the Halfling Freelands in the Lowsteps. A fuller description of the land can be found under <a href="Meadland" class="internal-link">Meadland</a> and on the page of <a href="Trinmo" class="internal-link">the Trinmo</a>. </p>
<h2>Demographics</h2>
<p>The Majority population in Meadrath is made up of Meadi and Weadi human, with Urien Human, Half-elfs, Gnomes and Halflings making up the largest minorities. While some Trykkerogi have settled and assimilated in the northern coastal towns, they remain a small minority. <br> The traditionally lax and welcoming attitude of the Meadi Kings and Queens, in combination with the stellar reputation of the <a href="Korsfall College" class="internal-link">Korsfall College</a>, has lead to a larger than average percentage of Arcane practitioners in Meadrath, surpassed in numbers only by the <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae Sultanate.</a> </p>
<h2>Economy</h2>
<p>In the early 10th century, Meadrath alongside Blijgrat dominates Ilrakhani trade and manufacturing, merchants in Korsvale, Giselstatt and Meadhavn have developed large business organizations such as Whisperhill Shipping or the Gisvale Trade Company.  
The introduction of the high-temperature forge in the mountainous Meadrath has led to mineral mining, although the country is forced to import copper, bronze and tin from Battvia, Vihr and Urland. Mines and glasswork are benefitting greatly from royal tax exemptions since the early 10 century. Silk production, introduced in Jera, Bellhome and Praum in the late 9th century, enabled the Meadi to join a thriving market, but meadi products remain of lesser quality than Cevoli Silk. Wool production is widespread in the country, just like the production of linen and hemp; all major export production. </p>

<p>Giselstatt serves as the center of Meadrath`s banking and international trade markets, fairs occur six times a year and facilitate the export of meadi goods like the aforementioned fabrics and import of international goods as well as allowing the importation of more exotic goods from the south such as alum, glass, spices and dyes. Trade was roughly balanced with the Blijgrat League, but Meadrath ran a large trade deficit for a long time with Battvia due to the latter's silks and exotic goods, a fact that has changed in the last century due to the political control exerted on the south during the Cevolian Civil Wars.</p>
<p>In the 920s, owed to Meadraths demographic growth, its increased demand for consumer goods led to a fall in standard of living for rural peasants, who were forced to deal with bad harvests in the 910s and 20s, although many land-owning peasants and enterprising merchants had been able to grow rich during the boom. </p>
<p>Meadraths military ventures in Battvia and its civil wars demanded huge sums of cash, wich were largely raised through special taxes, financial crisis hit the royal household repeateldy in the late 9th century and one of the main tasks of the Royal Congress was to establish a government bond system on Korsfall, the "Meadi Town Hall Rent."</p> <p>

<h2>Religion</h2>
<small><i>see <a href="Trinicism" class="internal-link">Trinicism</a></i></small>
<p>The vast majority of the population follows the decentralized and diverse local Churches of the Trinicist Faith, strongest in the Lands around the Trinmo and Gisvale, for it was at Trinfurt on the eastern border of the Kingdom, in the Bend of the Trinmo, that the Conclave of 764PA and the Great Schism between the Gethric and Trinicist Church took place.<br> Further west, small communities and specific social demographics in the larger cities still practice <a href="Augurism" class="internal-link">Augurism</a> while almost no remnants of pre-Gethric belief such as <a href="Turvik Paganism" class="internal-link">Turvik Cults</a> can be found east of Korsfall.</p>
<h2>Politics</h2>
<p> The Kingdom of Meadrath is one of the most important political entities on Inara and the Nucleus of the "Meadi Empire" encompassing a sphere of influence stretching from Trinvale and Uhl, the Weadlands and central Meadrath all the way to the Halfling Freelands to the west. <br> The political nexus of all these nations inevitably is the Korsfall Court in the Kingdoms Capital that is dominated by the Royal Congress, a Council of dignitaries of the three estates that advises the Monarch on matters of economic, national and international policy. <br>
The so called "Meadi Empire" as it is referred to colloquially, describes the various minor or major nations and cities ruled directly or indirectly by the decisions of the Royal Court, this architecture of dependency and alliances is often referred to as the Fulmer-System, named after High-Chancellor <a href="Odrick von Fulmer" class="internal-link">Odrick von Fulmer</a> who was instrumental in the creation and organization of the modern day Royal Congress.</p>
<h3>The Realms of the Crown</h3>
<p>Besides the Crownlands around Korsfall, which the Crown holds directly, the Kingdom is ringed by ten great vassals, all of whom sit in the <a href="Royal Congress" class="internal-link">Royal Congress</a> and enjoy varying degrees of local autonomy. Two of them are Archduchies, five are Duchies and one is a Landgraviate, and two, Zmalig and the Freelands, stand apart in their form of rule.</p>
<small><i>see <a href="Dirneux" class="internal-link">Dirneux</a></i></small>
<p>The Archduchy of Dirneux, once the Petty Kingdom of Rulais, is one of the oldest realms in the world and the homeland of the High Meadi. The du Jera rule it from Jera, the seat of the oldest university in western Ilrakhan, and hold the Arvale Pass at Arkeep. Archduke <a href="Jean du Jera" class="internal-link">Jean du Jera</a>, the father of the Queen, leads the old guard of the nobility.</p>
<small><i>see <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a></i></small>
<p>The Archduchy of Gisvale holds the Trinmo from Erlmeet to the Bend and the Bendburgs of Trinfurt, Barbridge and Turv. It was a kingdom of its own, the realm of the House of Gismar, and its capital Giselstatt is still one of the great metropolises of Meadland and the centre of the Kingdom's banking and trade. Archduke Karel I von Mart rules it as head of the cadet house that Kilean von Kor founded at the Treaty of Leis, and his son Frederick is the consort of the Queen.</p>
<small><i>see <a href="Morelle" class="internal-link">Morelle</a></i></small>
<p>The Duchy of Morelle on the Mead was a kingdom, the premier vassal of Korsfall, and for more than four centuries an Archduchy. The du Bregelle lost the title and half their lands after the Fiseux Uprising of 832PA and are called Oathbreakers behind their backs to this day, but the gold and marble of the Whisperhills still make Morelle a rich duchy.</p>
<small><i>see <a href="Mear" class="internal-link">Mear</a></i></small>
<p>The Duchy of Mear holds the delta of the Mead and the north-western coast, with the great port of Meadhavn. It was cut from Morelle in 833PA for the von Firsberg, who had stood loyal to the king in the Fiseux Uprising, and Duke Albrecht von Firsberg is High Mariner of the Kingdom and a driving force behind the K.G.W.</p>
<small><i>see <a href="Lesmere" class="internal-link">Lesmere</a></i></small>
<p>The Duchy of Lesmere holds the Kranmoor and the Weadi Wetlands on the shore of the Bay of Uhl, and is the home of the Kranmer. The von Kran rule it from the rich port of Dellweid, with Fort Kranig as their ancestral seat, and their ancient rivalry with the Archdukes of Gisvale outweighed even the demands of a common war in the War of Horn and Fang. Duke Kasper von Kran is one of the premier vassals of the Crown.</p>
<small><i>see <a href="Risfalt" class="internal-link">Risfalt</a></i></small>
<p>The Duchy of Risfalt holds the south-western Highlands around Praum, the ancient capital of the Auguri Dominion, and Bellhome, whose bishop is the informal Archbishop of all Meadland. It was created in 682PA from the lands of <a href="Medlan" class="internal-link">Medlan</a> for the von Praum, and Duke Felix von Praum is at present Marshall of the Kingdom.</p>
<small><i>see <a href="Wargel" class="internal-link">Wargel</a></i></small>
<p>The Duchy of Wargel, the twin of Risfalt, holds the valley of the Trinmo around Wormill, the southern Trinwood and the road to the Ottian Pass. Its von Buhr dukes guard the border against robber barons and ogres, which has earned it the name Home of the Scarred, and the crater of the Allwund lies within its borders.</p>
<small><i>see <a href="Weisch" class="internal-link">Weisch</a></i></small>
<p>The Landgraviate of Weisch holds the northern coast on the Bloody Strait and was created by Gisela II in 739PA, after the Battle of Zeeport. The Landgraf ranks below the Dukes, yet Weisch is the military centre of the realm, home of the Drakenhelms and of the Academy of Visdack, and it is counted the most loyal subject of the Crown under the von der Rend, a cadet branch of the von Buhr.</p>
<small><i>see <a href="Zmalig" class="internal-link">Zmalig</a></i></small>
<p>Zmalig is a theocratic city-state on the Bloody Strait, founded in 710PA by the 4000 faithful and under the protection of the Crown. Its bishop, at present Ungerim Lerosch, rules it with far more worldly power than the clergy usually hold in the Trinicist church, and its citizens enjoy unusual freedoms.</p>
<small><i>see <a href="Freelands" class="internal-link">Freelands</a></i></small>
<p>The Meadi Freelands are a confederation of peasant republics in the Lowsteps, largely halfling, that reject the feudal order of their neighbours. They were recognised at Korsfall in the Charter of 821, the Queen is their Lady Protector, and their Voxnos, at present Lopera Hermetell, speaks for them from Yeghall.</p>

<p>The great Houses of the realm marry into each other often, and many of these marriages are matrilineal, the heirs of a great House carrying its name through the mother, as in the royal House itself. To keep the Houses from vanishing into each other, the Kingdom knows the legal institution of Congenitura: if the heir to the throne inherits the throne of all Meadrath, they relinquish the right to also inherit the primary title of the prince consort, the title of their other parent. In this way those Houses continue to exist and keep their own name and title, passing them to another child or, if there is only one child, back up the ladder of succession to a branch of the House, as is usual. The Crown has granted the right of Congenitura to various vassals as well, including all its Dukes. It is the reason that Queen <a href="Gisela IV" class="internal-link">Gisela IV</a>, the firstborn of <a href="Jean du Jera" class="internal-link">Jean du Jera</a>, will not inherit his title of Archduke of Dirneux, which is to pass to her brother Renard.</p>

<h2>Society and Law</h2>
<p>Meadi society is ordered into three estates, the Church, the nobility and the commons, who are all represented in the <a href="Royal Congress" class="internal-link">Royal Congress</a>. The second estate, the nobility, counts the learned among its own: the nobles of the quill, the universities and the greater academic establishment, held in the highest regard in these lands, and with them to a degree the wider magical establishment. The crown passes to the eldest child regardless of sex, which has given the realm queens as well as kings, and the great Houses are bound to the royal House by many marriages, with the right of Congenitura keeping their names alive.</p>
<p>The law of the realm is the work of the Giselan reforms: a royal judiciary with courts of appeal to the Crown, bailiffs to administer the country, no trials by ordeal and little judicial torture, and legal codes that were unified soon after the death of <a href="Gisela II" class="internal-link">Gisela II</a>. The Trinicist bishops of the realm raise no tithe and live from donations and worldly business.</p>
<h2>Culture</h2>
<p>The Meadi are not one culture but a gradient, described on the page of the <a href="Meadi" class="internal-link">Meadi</a>. In the east, from Korsfall to the Bend of the Trinmo, speech and names have a harder sound and the noble houses are styled "von", while in the west along the Mead they are softer and more courtly and the houses are styled "du". Beyond the Trinwood the Weadi, who style themselves "van", keep their own tongue and customs. Traditions from before Gethricism survive, among them the Green Week, a festival around midsummer, and the old ways are stronger in the west than in the centre or the east. Learning is held in high regard from the College at Korsfall to the university at Jera, and scholars, physicians and mages are respected as they were in the days of the Auguri.</p>
<h2>Military</h2>
<p>Meadrath commands the greatest military and administrative apparatus in western Ilrakhan, a professional army built around heavily armoured knights, a force made for open ground, as the harassment of the Locni highlanders and the longbows of Lissvalley showed in the <a href="Meadi Othiri War" class="internal-link">Meadi Othiri War</a>. The armies of the Crown are led by the Marshall of the Kingdom, at present Felix von Praum, Duke of Risfalt, and the fleet by the High Mariner, at present Albrecht von Firsberg, Duke of Mear. Such offices are not always tied to a landed title, although there are traditions, and the High Mariner is usually the Duke of Mear. The Noble Order of the Oak, a prestigious order of the Crown founded in the reign of Gisela III, honours those who have served the Kingdom well. The Rector of Korsfall College is a signatory of the <a href="Orenburg Pact" class="internal-link">Orenburg Pact</a>, which forbids the official circles and institutions of higher magic to take part in the destructive business of war, and the Meadi make war with men and steel.</p>
<h2>Foreign Relations</h2>
<p>Meadrath counts as one of the three Great Powers beside Vihr and the Elbae Sultanate. Its rivalry with Vihr, the other great power of the north, found its expression in the <a href="War of Horn and Fang" class="internal-link">War of Horn and Fang</a> and the <a href="Treaty of Uhl" class="internal-link">Treaty of Uhl</a>, and the two have kept each other in check since. In the south the Crown holds political influence in Battvia, which emerged from the <a href="Vorellan War" class="internal-link">Vorellan War</a> indebted to it, and has had a say in Cevolia since the Cevolian Civil Wars. In the west it is distrusted by the peoples of <a href="Othirdon" class="internal-link">Othirdon</a>, most of all by Cimfon and its partners of the Visian League, and Meadi monarchs have encouraged Trinicist missions there as a first step towards integration. With the cities and merchants of <a href="Blijgrat" class="internal-link">Blijgrat</a> the Crown trades and competes, it protects the Free City of <a href="Zeeport" class="internal-link">Zeeport</a> in return for tribute, and the <a href="Treaty of Lotsprings" class="internal-link">Treaty of Lotsprings</a> of 900PA regulates the western trade between them. Its eyes turn more and more across the ocean to the new world of <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a>, where the <a href="Gesellschaft für Westmeadischen Handel" class="internal-link">K.G.W.</a> is chartered to trade.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<small><i>see <a href="Rulers of the Meadi" class="internal-link">The Rulers of the Meadi</a></i></small>

<h3>The Breaking of the Circle</h3>

<p>The <a href="Auguri Dominion" class="internal-link">Auguri</a> never ruled the Meadi in the manner of kings. They ordained first tribal chiefs and later fortified warlords as their chosen rulers of men, and their way of asserting true dominance politically was to not rule at all. Over the centuries the world below the Sages changed. Larger sedentary cultures grew up, the men in charge of powerful warriors sought to rule more and more land, and the idea of a dynastic line emerged in Meadland, families accruing more and more power, often independent of Auguric blessing or council. These forces, and the arrival of Gethricism, did much to bring about the Breaking of the Circle in 260PA.</p>

<h3>Gisvale & Korsfall</h3>

<p>The History of the meadi after the fall of the <a href="Auguri Dominion" class="internal-link">Auguri Dominion</a> in 260PA is one of two kingdoms.
The Kingdom of <a href="Gisvale" class="internal-link">Gisvale</a> was formed in 273PA by Ardol I of House of Gismar, a descendant of the legendary hero <a href="Gismar" class="internal-link">Gismar</a> that was said to have slain the Black <a href="Dragon" class="internal-link">Dragon Heximar.</a> The Kings of the Gisvale ruled from Giselstatt along the mighty Trinmo and were known for their close cultural association to northern  Urland, most prominently shown in their worship of the <a href="Turvik Paganism" class="internal-link">Turvik Gods.</a> Even in the days before the Fall of the Auguri, this difference in faith clearly differentiated the people of the Gisvale from their meadi and weadi cousins.</p>
<p>Unlike Gisvale, the Realm of Korsfall was very much a direct continuation of the Auguri Dominion. The earliest known record of a "Lord of all Meadi in Korsfall" is the figure of <a href="Torvald I" class="internal-link">Torvald the Tall</a>, an Auguri general that likely took power soon after 260PA and most of this early history of Korsfall describes their repeated conflicts and wars with the western people of the Locni Valley. The people of Korsfall very much continued the Auguri traditions, such as their reverence for medicae, scholars and mages, but of course no longer practiced religious rites as the Arrival of Gethricism had played a large part in the fall of the Dominion. Torvald would found the House von Weide that would rule Korsfall until 469PA, after which the von Kor Dynasty succeeded the von Weide, whose descendants rule Meadrath to this day.</p>

<p>Later tradition calls Torvald an Auguri general, but the early accounts from around his time describe a warlord or Burgherr, a castellan in a long line of such men who protected the people around Weidhill as a travelling ruler. Weidhill was at first no more than a tower on a hill overseeing the <a href="Trinmo" class="internal-link">Trinmo</a>, but it grew into a keep with a village and a town around it and later merged with the neighbouring settlements of Kor and Hiln. Korsfall emerged as the name of the town around the late 2nd century PA. The House von Weide took its name from Weidhill, upon which many willows grew, and Torvald is remembered as the Tall, for his height and perhaps also for the way he ruled, from a hill overseeing the land.</p>

<h3>Wars of the Mead</h3>

<p>The three most dominant powers emerging from the ruins of the Auguri Dominion were the Kingdoms of Korsfall, Morelle and Rulais, leading the Rhilmer, Lower Meadi and High Meadi peoples respectively. In the 3rd and 4th centuries PA, the petty Kings of Morelle (285PA) and Rulais (381PA) would both fall to the conquest of Korsfall and the heirs of Torvald bending the knee and being raised as local warlords under the Banner of the von Weide Kings instead, essentially laying the foundation for the modern day hierarchies employed by the Kingdom of Meadrath. Both Rulais, henceforth known as the lands of Dirneux, and Morelle became Archduchies and premier subjects with unrivalled power compared to the lesser lords and ladies of Meadland, their ancestral history with Korsfall granting them a station of almost dignified brothers rather than conquered foes.</p>

<p>The war with Morelle began in 282PA and was decided in 285PA at the Battle of the Voiceless, which was fought in a mist so thick and still that the horns of Morelle could not be heard across the field, and the army of its king could neither be commanded nor rallied. The king of Morelle bent the knee, and Korsfall came to dominate northern Meadland.</p>

<p>The war with Rulais began in 375PA and was decided in 381PA at the Feast of Dragons, a battle fought in the gorge in the Drakesfall Hills where Gismar was said to have slain Heximar. Dagrin "Longhand", a hero of the Korsfall side, is said to have slain a mountain of men himself that day, among them the King of Rulais, Lerran du Argon, and to have proclaimed over the field "As brother has slain brother here, we have made a final feast for Dragons." Rulais did not survive its king, and Thierry du Jera, whose house had long held Jera Castle in the king's service, was declared the first Duke of Dirneux.</p>

<h3>The Gethric Age</h3>
<p><a href="Gethricism" class="internal-link">Gethric</a> teachings spread through Meadland from 263PA. Korsfall never officially converted, but by around 390PA the majority of its people were Gethric, as in Gisvale, and soon its kings took Gethric oaths following their coronations and cathedrals to the Candleflame were built in its towns. In Gisvale the line of Gismar ruled on, and Ardol V "the Blessed" became its first Gethric born king in 429PA. Not every old thing was welcome to the new faith. In the 6th century the Sons of the Flame, a movement that sought to eradicate Turvik and Auguric memorials across the Meadi lands, tore down the Great Stones at Praum. Various bishoprics supported them, but when the movement threatened to arm itself as a militia and contest the landed lords, the crown broke it up.</p>

<p>In Gisvale the kings of the House of Gismar fought the ogres of the Kortopi foothills for centuries. <a href="Ardol III" class="internal-link">Ardol III</a> "Ogre Hammer" took the eastern foothills near the Bend of the Trinmo from the ogres of the vale, although many of its hundreds of valleys were never truly bound by human hands. <a href="Marlina I" class="internal-link">Marlina I</a> "the White Lady" built Castle Gis at Giselstatt in 491PA and was beloved for her devotion to her people. She took no husband, for the Wizard Redemor, said to have been a scorned lover though that is likely fiction, had told her that her fruits would poison one in three and break the crown forever, and her younger sister's son succeeded her.</p>

<p>In Korsfall the House von Weide ended in 469PA. The von Kor were one of the emerging houses of the realm, castellans of the von Weide with records going back to the middle of the 4th century, and when Siegmar V died, his own sons having died before him, Gernot von Kor claimed that Siegmar had chosen him as his successor on his deathbed. The claim is heavily debated and largely unverifiable, and the little resistance to the new dynasty was crushed quickly and quietly. Siegmar is remembered as "the last Tree", a name given to him after his death. His successors included Kar II "the Seer King", who was rumoured to be an oracle able to foretell the future to a limited degree, and later scholars believe it, and <a href="Ulrike I" class="internal-link">Ulrike I</a> "the Hazel Queen", said to be the most beautiful woman in the lands of the Trinmo and the Mead. In 556PA, under Gunther I, the <a href="Korsfall College" class="internal-link">College at Korsfall</a> was founded.</p>

<h3>The Fall and Rise of the House of Gismar</h3>

<p><a href="Medlan" class="internal-link">Medlan</a>, the home of Diedrik, was a country not unlike Korsfall, Gisvale or Weadland. It was founded in 339PA by Toskar, a warlord of the Highlands who declared himself Lord of the Highlands, and from Wormill its lords quarrelled with the Kings of Gisvale over the valley of the Trinmo for some two and a half centuries. Its people had never been particularly Auguric and had been very early to convert. Diedrik is painted as an archvillain in many histories, for many reasons, and the victors write history, but he took Gisvale because it was rich and weak, defenceless under a king bound perpetually to his chambers, with greedy and isolated lords resting on their legacy.</p>

<p>At the dusk of the 6th century Tibor II, the then King of Gisvale, was betrayed and supplanted by Diedrik of Medlan, the Lord of Wormill, Tibors children would flee first to Weadland but be rejected in fear of war by the Warder of Schipwig and only found refuge at Korsfall Keep.
The mighty Diedrick would rule Gisvale until his death when his son Ringil, who had married an urien Princess would succeed him in 622PA. 
Tibors son, a boy called Giselher, now grown up and with children of his own, had spent the decades since rallying loyalists and allies to his banner and would now, with the Lord of Korsfall at his side, dare to make a bid for the Throne in Giselstatt.
The following conflict is known to history as <a href="War of Trout and Drake" class="internal-link">The War of Trout and Drake</a> and last for over three decades. At its end, Giselstatt was conquered, the line of Diedrik extinguished and the Dragon Banner of Gismar raised in supreme victory, but not without its costs. Tens of thousands had died and the vast majority of the rich farmlands and fields of the Gisvale been devastated, Giselher himself as well as his sons had died in battle and only his daughter Liara remained.</p>
<p>And so it was that on the even of Fall, in 664PA, Liara of the House of Gismar would marry Gunther of the House of Kor, the Lord of Korsfall and her fathers strongest supporter.
Liara would be declared Queen in Giselestatt like her Grandfather had been and the two ruled the lands of Korsfall and Gisvale together. The Union of the two realms was proclaimed in 664PA, and Gunther III and Liara ruled the united lands together until, in 674PA, their firstborn son Gildher was crowned as the first King of Meadrath, the unified Lands of his ancestors.</p>
<p>Under the rule of Gildher II, in 682PA the Crown would break apart the dominions of Diedrik, as they were before his usurpation of Gisvale, in the central meadi Highlands, creating the Duchies of Risfalt in the west and Wargel in the east instead and granting them to Houses loyal to Korsfall during the war that had refused to aid Diedrik during the War.</p>
<h3>Gisela II & the Rise of Trinicism</h3>
<p>In the year 704PA, Gisela of House Kor would be crowned Queen Gisela II of Meadrath, after the death of her father, Gildher II. Her reign lasted for over 68 years and usher in an age of prosperity and progress for the meadi people that would leave the Kingdom as one of the premier powers of the Inaran continent. During her formative years as a young monarch, Gisela succesfully confronted rebellious vassals and was guided by her mother, the late Queen-Mother Margaret of Jera.</p>
<p>Gisela instigated significant reforms in the meadi legal system, creating a royal judiciary and institutions of appeal to the court directly, while abolishing trials by ordeal, to institute her new legal reforms and administer the country more effectively, she established the office of the bailiff.
Giselas expanded upon the work of her father and grandfather and would further sponsor and expand the  <a href="Korsfall College" class="internal-link">College at Korsfall</a> and earn a reputation as a fair, judicious and scholarly Ruler across Ilrakhan.
</p>

<p>A few years after the outbreak of <a href="Wars of Elbae Aggression" class="internal-link">first Elbae War</a> the High-Bishop of <a href="Church of Iztaw" class="internal-link">Iztaw</a> woudl declare the Gethric Progroms, a series of mandates to banish and persecute elves and half-elves across all lands of the devout. This would cause great uproar within many regional Churches, especially around northern Urland and in the western regions of Meadrath and Cevolis where the Clergy of Iztaw had long been viewed as either corrupt politicians or petty servants of the Kings of <a href="Vihr" class="internal-link">Vihr</a>. In response to the Bull, many Bishops of the Church would gather at Trinfurt in 764 PA and debate for months the divine justice of not only such a progrom but also the role of the bishop, the view on the arcane and divine and the Hierarchy within the clergy.
When Knights of the <a href="Order of the Silver Wheel" class="internal-link">Order of the Silver Wheel</a> would attempts to forcefully disband the Gathering, Gisela supported local guards and adventurers that defended the priests successfully. The Great Schism between the east and the west that would occur and later lead to the Trinicist denomination of Gethric belief is a direct consequence of the Conclave.<p>


<h3>The Twins War & The Treaty of Leis</h3>
<p>Following the Death of Queen Margaret I, the daughter of Gisela II in 779A, her two twin sons, Gildher and Kilean both put forward claims to the Meadi Throne. While Gildher was the older of the two, Kilean had been a successful commandant of armies, well connected within Meadi Nobility and as a knight cut a popular and dashing figure with the peasantry. Days before the coronation of Gildher, the twins elderly midwife was presented by Kileans supporters and testified that it was Kilean after all who had been delivered first and he was the rightful heir to the Throne.</p>

<p>Although the claim was shady and weak, almost entirely dismissed by modern day historians, it was enough for Kilean to assemble a large faction of the country behind himself and declare that if his brother would not cede the throne to him he would take it by force, thus the later dubbed Twins war began in 780PA. The bloody conflict would last for 6 years until the death of Kileans firstborn son Marten following the battle of Leis, while Kileans faction was still belligerent and not broken, Gildher had unilaterally won the war and forced his brother to retreat beyond the Gisvale. If out of love for his twin and sorrow over the death of his nephew or simply to end the catastrophic war where meadi fought meadi, Gildher offered Kilean amicable terms at Leis and Peace was signed. Kilean would renounce his and his descendants claims on the throne of Meadrath in perpetuity and swear to defend Meadrath and her people and acknowledge the King of Meadrath as his superior. In return he would be made the Ruler of the newly established Archduchy of Gisvale and permitted to rule his lands independently. Thus Kilean became the ancestor of the von Kor Cadet House, the von Mart. </p>
<h3>The Meadi Othiri War & the Fiseux Uprising (824PA - 833PA)</h3> 

<p>The reign of Giselher I von Kor, who took the throne in 829PA following Margaret II, produced the most ambitious western campaign in Meadraths history and its most humiliating domestic crisis within a single decade. The <a href="Meadi Othiri War" class="internal-link">Meadi Othiri War</a> of 824-827PA was driven by a strategic calculation that Meadraths absence of any port on the Visian Sea could be resolved by military force through the Arvale Pass and the seizure of Ballina from the <a href="Cimfon" class="internal-link">Maelzad of Cimfon</a>. The campaign opened brilliantly as the professional Meadi army under the operational command of Rivalt du Chian, Castellan of Arkeep, with Prince Giselher, the Queen's younger brother, and Bernard du Jera, Duke of Dirneux, fighting alongside rather than commanding, swept through Skyroar Castle and shattered the Locni coalition at the Battle of Arvale, with Count Adalbert von Lindfalls "the Smaragd Knight" leading the van to considerable personal glory.</p> 

<p> For a year everything suggested the calculation had been correct. Then the Othiri refused to fight in the open, the <a href="Lissvalley" class="internal-link">Lissvalley Federation</a> entered the war with longbowmen that the highland terrain made devastating, and the <a href="Knights of the Green Tree" class="internal-link">Knights of the Green Tree</a> held the Cherelais Pass so thoroughly that no flanking movement was ever possible. The Battle of Red Oak, seven miles from Ballina, was among the bloodiest single engagements in recent Ilrakhani history, du Chian was captured, von Lindsfall was killed in a legendary duel with Grand Master Ebonfoil, and Prince Giselher personally led a late charge that broke through the Othiri line before a lowly footmans halberd found him and ended his ability to walk forever. Bernard du Jera brought the army home. The Visian Sea port remained out of reach.</p> <p>The wars domestic consequences proved more damaging than its military outcome. Prince Giselher returned to Korsfall paralyzed from the hips, a prince who, once crowned in 829PA, governed thereafter from a chair through proxies and closed-door audiences, with Bernard du Jera's loyal but politically unsophisticated voice carrying more weight at court than was wise. The Court Mage Gregor von Mur, who had withheld from the campaigns fighting due to the Orenburg Pact and fallen from court favour during the war, rehabilitated himself quietly by standing with the crown during what followed. Archduke Platis du Bregelle of Morelle, who had read a physically broken and increasingly reclusive king, guided by a well meaning man without political instincts, as a target of opportunity moved to seize the throne. The plot, known to history as the Fiseux Uprising after the Count of Fiseux who stood at its head, led to loyal Bernard du Jeras death in the process. A magus among the conspirators channelled his spells through a sword that always seemed to pulse as if alive, and Bernard slew him and took the blade in turn, only to be consumed by the necrotic energies bound within it, which is why the Meadi still say that a matter has turned like a blade turned black when it becomes dire all at once. The Count von Firsberg, a lesser noble of the Crownlands who was visiting the king with his eldest son, took the Count of Fiseux hostage and kept him while the guards of Fiseux killed his son, the two having only nodded to each other in understanding that loyalty to the king came first. The plot ultimately demonstrated that Platis had fundamentally misjudged both the kings mind and the loyalty of the court around him.</p>
<p> Giselher I, sharp as he had ever been despite the ruin of his body, had the Archduke arrested, tried, and hanged alongside two of his sons. The Archduchy of Morelle, which had held premier vassal status in Meadraths hierarchy since Korsfall first absorbed the petty kingdom in the 3rd century, was stripped of its title. Its northern territories including the vital port of Meadhavn were granted to the loyal Count von Firsberg, creating the new <a href="Mear" class="internal-link">Duchy of Mear</a>, while the remainder of Morelle continued under the thoroughly humiliated du Bregelle line, now merely ducal. The word "Platis" forever a synonym for traitor in the Meadi tongue.</p>
<p> Rivalt du Chian, exchanged for his Cimfoni prisoner Sir Berengar Harffan in 828PA, returned to Meadrath to a her's reception and spent the years that followed writing his memoirs "Of War and Shame", which remain the primary Meadi account of the western campaign and are notable, among military histories, for their refusal to blame anyone but their author for its outcome. Due Chien remained a Kings man in the face of du Bregelles treason.</p>
<h3>The War of Horn and Fang & The Treaty of Uhl (851PA - 866PA)</h3> <p>The reign of Arngil II von Kor saw Meadrath commit the full weight of its military and economic resources to the most ambitious campaign in the kingdoms history in an attempt to wrest the City of <a href="Uhl" class="internal-link">Uhl</a> and control of the Bay from Vihr's century old grasp.</p>
<p>The <a href="War of Horn and Fang" class="internal-link">War of Horn and Fang</a> that began in 851PA was one of the largest conflicts between the two great powers of Ilrakhan in recorded history, fought across fifteen years of attritional campaigning around Uhl, the Wermian Moor and the Bay itself. Arngil II, who had pressed for the war, spent his years of it at Fort Kranig, the old castle of Lesmere and ancestral home of the von Kran, whose port his own navy had unblocked at the war's opening, and the Meadi position was always strategically advantaged by geography: where Vihr' forces took months to reach the theatre through the Pilgrims Pass, Meadraths armies could march in weeks. The wars most celebrated Meadi figure was <a href="Muriel von Gloss" class="internal-link">Muriel von Gloss</a>, Countess of Gloss and former Rector of Korsfall College, whose open violation of the <a href="Orenburg Pact" class="internal-link">Orenburg Pact</a> to fight for the Meadi cause scandalized the magical establishment of both nations and whose defeat of the Vihri sorcerer Viagoris at the Clash at Felhome in 861PA was one of the wars defining moments. She was killed at the Battle of Gadly Bridge in 865PA by <a href="Andre" class="internal-link">Andre of Trydlew</a>. Arngil II died in 858PA having never seen a battlefield, and his son Gilmar I, guided closely by Count Warder von Inberg and Archduke Bernard "the Younger" du Jera of Dirneux, brought the war to its conclusion. The <a href="Treaty of Uhl" class="internal-link">Treaty of Uhl</a>, signed by Gilmar I and Orian II of Vihr in 866PA, declared Uhl a free city under joint guarantee thereby removing it from Vihri hands but denying Meadrath the annexation it had sought.</p> <p>The Treatys inconclusive outcome , albeit somewhat disappointing, accelerated a strategic reorientation that had been building throughout the conflict. The wealth of the Bay as it became clear, was not going to be won by conquest. Rather it would be competed for through trade, diplomacy and the slow construction of commercial dominance. Meadraths attention turned increasingly westward: toward the lands of <a href="Othirdon" class="internal-link">Othirdon</a> and Sertova beyond its western borders, toward the resolution of longstanding commercial rivalries with the merchants of <a href="Blijgrat" class="internal-link">Blijgrat</a>, and toward the extraordinary commercial prospects opening in the new world of <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a> across the ocean. A horizon that the <a href="Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel" class="internal-link">G.U.V.</a> and the <a href="Gesellschaft für Westmeadischen Handel" class="internal-link">K.G.W.</a> would eventually be chartered to exploit. The war had not made Meadrath weaker, its administrative and commercial institutions had absorbed the strain better than Vihr's had, but it had redirected Meadi ambition away from the east and toward a western and oceanic future that would define the kingdoms character for the century that followed.</p>
<h3>The Meadi Empire</h3>
<p>In the later years of the 9th century, Meadraths influence had only grown and it was now considered one of the "Great Three Powers" alongside the beheran Sultanate and the Vihri Kingdom. While Meadraths population is smaller than that of Vihr, although not by much, and its clerical institutions much weaker including the military aspects, as well as being economically outcompeted by the Behemoth that is the Elven Sphere of Prosperity, these are both issues Meadrath is only inching behind. The Kingdoms focus on diplomatic alliances and a strong block of vassals and dependencies with a careful respect for both local cultures and surprisingly also religious traditions haven given it the military capabilities to rival any other Alliance Block in the world. Its focus on new trade routes, applied engineering and practical approach to magic tell a story of exponential commercial growth in the last century, especially considering the westwards view the Realm has taken. Its true strength lies in its modern administration however that makes all of the rest of its values shine brightly: Centralization of power, codification of modern law, clear division of state & church, unification and chartering of merchant guild structures into large scale government owned enterprises as well as a focus on control and modern appliance and research of Magic have always been the brightest stars in the Meadi sky. </p>
<p>The late Queen Gisela III von Kor, had instituted the <a href="Royal Congress" class="internal-link">Royal Congress</a> under the leadership of the Count of Fulmer, now declared High-Chancellor, in the year 898PA.
While on paper a merely internal body of administration and efficiency, in essence this Congress enforced Meadi influence and power upon its neighbours through soft power and is largely responsible for the extensive centralization of political power in Korsfall and the "Meadi Empire", which refers to the control over Dirneux, Morelle, Mear, Risfalt, Wargel, Weisch, Lesmere, Gisvale, the Halfling Freelands and various other nominally fully independent nations such as Zmalig.</p>

<h3>The Universalists & the Old Guard (883PA - 913PA)</h3> <p>Gisela III von Kor, called the Kind, succeeded Gilmar I in 883PA and ruled for twenty-seven years. Hers was a gentle court and by many measures a successful reign, but it was a country governed by strong politicians, and none was stronger than <a href="Odrick von Fulmer" class="internal-link">Odrick von Fulmer</a>, the head of the Royal Diplomatic Corps of Korsfall, whom she named the first High-Chancellor of the Realm in 898PA. With him she founded the <a href="Royal Congress" class="internal-link">Royal Congress</a>, which took over the business of the old High Council of the great vassals, and with it the web of dependencies and alliances that became the Fulmer-System. The great houses kept their chairs, but the archdukes, who had once sat at the High Council, were left with a seat of prestige and little else.</p> <p>Von Fulmer and his followers, the so called universalists, were a new wave of realpoliticians who wished to concentrate power in Korsfall under the crown and make the realm as a whole more powerful. Against them stood the old guard, the nobility from across the realm who favoured decentralization and the old standing of the duchies, and whose voice was the Archduke of Dirneux, <a href="Jean du Jera" class="internal-link">Jean du Jera</a>, who as the queen's husband and Prince-Consort stood closer to the throne than any of them. The match had been arranged, but the queen is said to have truly loved him, and he her. The politics of the last decade of the reign were dominated by the quarrel of the two camps, and the two men themselves are said to have come to hate each other truly.</p> <p>Gisela III died in 910PA and her daughter, <a href="Gisela IV" class="internal-link">Gisela IV</a>, ascended the throne as a child, with a regency council headed by du Jera. The old guard had the upper hand at last: von Fulmer was banished from court in all but name, the duchies were strengthened, and du Jera tried, too late and in a doomed attempt to turn back the clock, to disband the Royal Congress. He had no wish to see Meadrath dragged into the <a href="Vorellan War" class="internal-link">Vorellan Wars</a> in <a href="Battvia" class="internal-link">Battvia</a>, but when the realm did intervene, under Marshall Felix von Praum, and Battvia emerged from the war indebted to the Crown, he admitted that even he could not have planned a better outcome, the only admiration for von Fulmer he is known to have voiced. In 913PA the young queen took the government into her own hands, dismissed much of the palace staff, recalled the exiled dignitaries and restored von Fulmer to his office, and du Jera was sent back to Jera. The old guard did not survive its leader's fall, and some of its men left the realm altogether, such as the Morelle lord <a href="Marrin Andil" class="internal-link">Marrin Andil</a>, who founded the free port of <a href="Ulbreek" class="internal-link">Ulbreek</a> across the ocean.</p>
