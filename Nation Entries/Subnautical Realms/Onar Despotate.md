<p>Ruled from the ancient city of Kaleidos, the Despotate is the largest realm of the Pry, the old and proud Krestasi of the southern Beheran Ocean.</p>
<p>The Despotate of Onar became the dominant successor of the <a href="Onari Empire" class="internal-link">Onari Empire</a> in the 5th century, previously simply known as the Province of Erezum it managed to unify the largest parts of the imperial core under its authority and save many imperial institutions and  cities during the collapse, although it is with no doubt a mere shadow of the lost Empire. The murder and death of <a href="Onar" class="internal-link">Onar</a>, the once living God of the Empire, has significantly changed leadership and clerical structures within the modern day Despotate compared to the later days of the Empire, but nevertheless the Pry view themselves as the true superlative of all Krestasi and consider the <a href="Golomid" class="internal-link">Golomid</a> a combination of traitors, heathens and upstarts.</p>
