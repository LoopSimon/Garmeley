<p> The Golomid Empire stretches out across the south eastern Niryd Sea and  the seafloor below. Its crustacean citizens are extremely isolationist and rarely seek out contact with other species of the surface, although they entertain a reasonably amicable relationship with the seafarers of the <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Sultanate</a> and the <a href="Qahori" class="internal-link">Qahori Federation</a> following the agreements made at the end of the <a href="Great Elbae Golomid War" class="internal-link">Elbae Golomid War</a>. Following the fall of the Onari Empire over 400 years ago, the Golomid Empire is usually considered the largest and most powerful of all the great Realms of the Krestasi. </p>
<p>The gigantic coral structures connected and ascended to the surface level of the ocean, known as the <a href="Risen Reef" class="internal-link">Risen Reef</a>, is the surface arm of the empire and, while technically a minor part of it in terms of its demographics, the Golomids entire exposure to all surface dwellers.</p>
<p>The Empire of the Golomid rose after the fall of the <a href="Onari Empire" class="internal-link">Onari Empire</a> in the 5th century, revolting against the southern despots ruling the Coral Cities of the Niryd Sea floor. Its mastery of the Coral arts is often ascribed to their ancient religious sects worshipping the so called "Crustlords" a faith that was developed as an underground expression of cultural rebellion by the Golomi Krestasi under the Onari and has become the Golomid Empires primary religion in the modern day. </p>
