<p>During the Elbae Sultanate's Conquests of Qa, the ancestors of the Qarzi tribe of today collaborated with the elven invaders and were thus granted autonomous rule after the end of the conquests. Hated and cursed by their Qahori cousins, they nevertheless enjoy a prosperous and safe existence as an Elayat of the mighty Sultanate and as its trade partners, being the world's largest producer of coffee, a good much desired in the western lands of Ilrakhan. The Qarzi Elayat is located in south-eastern <a href="Il Haena" class="internal-link">Il-Haena</a>.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>The ancestors of the Qarzi were among the Qahori tribes of southeastern Qa, holders of Benwaral, the second of the two holy cities of the <a href="Qalmyr" class="internal-link">Qalmyr Faith</a>, and of the coastal trade hub of Kahtis, whose abundant coffee plantations had made the region prosperous long before the elves arrived. When the <a href="Wars of Qahori Subjugation" class="internal-link">Wars of Qahori Subjugation</a> began in 413PA and the Alliance of the Old Moon organized the island's resistance under Kamer of the New Fire, the tribes of the southeast faced a choice that the Qalmyr Faith made agonizing and their geography made stark: fight alongside the Alliance on ground that offered no natural defensive advantage and against a military force that had already consumed the island's strongest cities, or negotiate a survival that preserved their people and their holy city at the cost of everything the Alliance was dying for. They negotiated.</p>
<p>The Qarzi provided intelligence, logistical access and auxiliary forces to the Sultanate's armies during the campaigns, and when the wars ended in 457PA and the Sultanate granted them autonomous rule over their southeastern territories, they emerged as the only Qahori community on Il-Haena that had not been destroyed, displaced or subjugated without compensation. Benwaral and Kahtis remained theirs. The hatred of every other Qahori alive also remained theirs, and has remained so across the five centuries since, a wound that the Qarzi's evident prosperity, their trade agreements with the Sultanate, and their coffee's presence on tables from Jiravid to Korsfall has done nothing to heal.</p>
<h2>The Keepers</h2>
<p>The Qarzi are orthodox. They follow the First Path of Qalmyr, the path of Qastor, and are known as its Keepers. The diaspora condemns them as collaborators and the harshest among them call their choice "Zolra's pragmatism", the survival of one people bought with the ruin of all the others. The Qarzi see it very differently. They hold that their decision was the only way the old ways of the holy land of Qa could have continued uninterrupted: Benwaral still stands, its shrine-keepers have never been driven out, and the rites of Qa have been kept there without a break for five centuries. The Black Flame of their banner is a fire banked but never put out, kept alive in the dark for the day it is needed. Seen from the diaspora, Benwaral remains a site simultaneously sacred and defiled, preserved intact because its guardians collaborated in the destruction of everything around it.</p>
<h2>The Elayat</h2>
<p>The Qarzi are a loyal subject of the Sultanate, and the Kingdom is rich and successful. The Qarzi are allowed to practice all their religious activities in peace, and there have been essentially no issues with uprisings. As the Elayat thrived together with the rest of the Elbae sphere of prosperity it was granted more and more autonomous privileges. The port of Kahtis, the coastal trade hub of the Elayat, is also one of the ports from which the <a href="Third Moon Fleet" class="internal-link">Third Moon Fleet</a> is to sail.</p>
<h2>Bone-Craft</h2>
<p>The Qarzi keep the bone-craft of Qa in its old forms. The shrines of Benwaral, preserved intact when so much else of Qa was lost, are the finest surviving example of the bone architecture of the old holy land, framed in the bone of the <a href="Igulan" class="internal-link">Igulan</a> and the <a href="Ossur" class="internal-link">Ossur</a>, and its carvers are among the most sought after of the Orcish world. Carved bone of Il-Haena is one of the goods the Qarzi send out together with their coffee.</p>
