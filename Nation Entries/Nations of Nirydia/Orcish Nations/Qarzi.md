<p> During the Elbae Sultanates Conquests of Qa, the ancestors of the Qarzi tribe of today collaborated with the elven invaders and were thus granted autonomous rule after the end of the conquests. Hated and cursed by their Qahori Cousins, they nevertheless enjoy a prosperous and safe existence as trade partners to the mighty Sultanate, being the worlds largest producer of coffee, a good much desired in the western lands of Ilrakhan. The Qarzi Elayat is located in south-eastern <a href="Il Haena" class="internal-link">Il-Haena</a>.</p>
<h2>History</h2> <p>The ancestors of the Qarzi were among the Qahori tribes of southeastern Qa, holders of Benwaral, the second of the two holy cities of the <a href="Qalmyr" class="internal-link">Qalmyr Faith</a>, and of the coastal trade hub of Kahtis, whose abundant coffee plantations had made the region prosperous long before the elves arrived. When the <a href="Wars of Qahori Subjugation" class="internal-link">Wars of Qahori Subjugation</a> began in 413PA and the Alliance of the Old Moon organized the islands resistance under Kamer of the New Fire, the tribes of the southeast faced a choice that the Qalmyr Faith made agonizing and their geography made stark: fight alongside the Alliance on ground that offered no natural defensive advantage and against a military force that had already consumed the islands strongest cities, or negotiate a survival that preserved their people and their holy city at the cost of everything the Alliance was dying for. They negotiated.</p>

 <p>The Qarzi provided intelligence, logistical access and auxiliary forces to the Sultanates armies during the campaigns, and when the wars ended in 457PA and the Sultanate granted them autonomous rule over their southeastern territories, they emerged as the only Qahori community on Il-Haena that had not been destroyed, displaced or subjugated without compensation. Benwaral and Kahtis remained theirs. The hatred of every other Qahori alive also remained theirs, and has remained so across the five centuries since, a wound that the Qarzis evident prosperity, their trade agreements with the Sultanate, and their coffees presence on tables from Jiravid to Korsfall has done nothing to heal. Within the <a href="Qalmyr" class="internal-link">Qalmyr Faith</a> the Qarzi occupy a position of permanent theological condemnation: they chose survival over sacred ground, Zolra's pragmatism over Qastor's demands, and the faiths communities in exile regard Benwaral, preserved intact because its guardians collaborated in the destruction of everything around it as a site simultaneously sacred and defiled.</p>
