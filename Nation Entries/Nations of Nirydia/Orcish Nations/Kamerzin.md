<p>Kamerzin, formally the Kamerzin Sandimok, is the Orcish kingdom of the island of Durennis in the far east of Nirydia, the "land of Dawn" of Qari tradition. It was founded by those among the diaspora who could not accept the <a href="Qahori" class="internal-link">Qahori Federation's</a> accommodation with survival and who swore to return to Qa one day in conquest. It takes its name from Kamer of the New Fire, the great military leader of the Alliance of the Old Moon, whose war the Kamerzin claim to continue.</p>
<h2>The Voyage East</h2>
<p>A generation after the Destruction of Qa, a militant faction of the diaspora gathered those who agreed with it and sailed east from the Qahori Archipelago into the Niryd Sea. They reached Durennis and built a civilization there that has regarded the <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae Sultanate</a> as an enemy across the centuries since. They are an industrious people, and a feudal kingdom under its Sandimar, Teremi. Their neighbours on Durennis are the successor states of Japhal: the traders of <a href="Zilkand" class="internal-link">Zilkand</a> keep good relations with the Kamerzin, and <a href="Raximan" class="internal-link">Raximan</a>, the southernmost port of the island, is home to many Orcs.</p>
<h2>The Returners</h2>
<p>The Kamerzin follow the First Path of <a href="Qalmyr" class="internal-link">Qalmyr</a>, the path of Qastor, and are known as the Returners. Their faith is the faith of the return: that the Orcs will go back to Qa, and that Qastor will give it back to them. Their emblem, the Sun and Moon, recalls the Old Moon of the Alliance that Kamer led.</p>
<p>Over the centuries the quest for a return home has become more and more of a farce. The Sultanate is half a world away and does not mind the Kamerzin, and the Kamerzin, who have everything but the island, no longer prepare for the day. The vow is still sworn at feasts and in the halls of the court, and the fleets that carry it are ceremonial. The Kamerzin also hate the <a href="Path of the Second Moon" class="internal-link">Path of the Second Moon</a>, as much as the Federation does, if not more.</p>
<h2>Bone-Craft</h2>
<p>The Kamerzin carried the bone-craft of Qa east with them. Every great house keeps a piece of old bone of the <a href="Igulan" class="internal-link">Igulan</a> or the <a href="Ossur" class="internal-link">Ossur</a>, carried out of Qa and meant to go home again, and the regalia of the Sandimar is made of it. The armour of the kingdom and the hall of the Sandimar are worked in the old way, in the bone of the beasts of Durennis.</p>
