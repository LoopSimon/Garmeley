<p> The Orcish Federation of Qahori is divided among several individual chiefdoms, often in conflict in the past, they were united by the House of Tihiok in the Qahori Federation under one leader. Excellent Seafairers, many of the orcs of the Qahori have given up the great dream of reconquering the Lost Lands of Qa and instead search for luck in the southern Oceans of Behera. The Qahori Federation is the only Nirydian country that enjoys mercantile relationships with the Ports of the Smaragd Sea. </p>
 <p>The Qahori Federation is the largest surviving community of the people who once called the island of Qa their home, a maritime federation whose extraordinary seafaring tradition is understood within the <a href="Qalmyr" class="internal-link">Qalmyr Faith</a> not as a cultural achievement but as a form of mourning, the wandering of a people driven from the land Qastor gave them and unable to return. The loss of Qa to the <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae Sultanate</a> across the <a href="Wars of Qahori Subjugation" class="internal-link">Wars of Qahori Subjugation</a> of 413 - 457PA, the event the Qahori call the Destruction of Qa, was experienced as simultaneously a military defeat and a divine verdict. The Qalmyr Faith had counselled against organized resistance; those who had argued loudest for war, most notably Kamer of the New Fire and the successive leaders of the Alliance of the Old Moon, had been proved unable to prevent the outcome their theology had predicted. That the elder Ranqar the Broken had begged them not to fight and had died watching the catastrophe unfold did not simplify the grief.</p>
 <p> The survivors who took to the sea carried both the loss of their homeland and the question of what their own choices had cost them. The community that eventually became the Federation settled on the Qahori Archipelago and rebuilt around the faiths pacific principles, excellent sailors and traders, maintaining mercantile relationships that the outside world found surprising given the hostility the Sultanate might have been expected to sustain, their dream of returning to thier home present in every prayer and absent from every practical calculation. A generation after the Destruction of Qa, the Federations commitment to Qalmyr pacifism was tested by the emergence of the <a href="Kamerzin" class="internal-link">Kamerzin</a>, a militant faction that had concluded that Qastors path had failed and Zolras offered the only answer, who gathered those among the diaspora who could not accept the Federation's accommodation with survival and sailed east into the Niryd Sea with promises to return one day in conquest. The Kamerzin reached the island of Durennis in the far east of Nirydia and built a civilization there that has regarded the Sultanate as an enemy across the centuries since. The Federation and the Kamerzin have not been the same people since the moment the fleet divided, and the Qahori do not speak of their eastern cousins with either approval or condemnation, only with the silence of a people that understands exactly why they left and cannot say they were wrong.</p>
