<p>The Divine Commune of Pervantes is the last remnant of the great Empire of Vanpur, following its destruction during the Keywars. The Xolor of Durennis have gathered here and are the largest community of chimera practicing Xolor remaining in the world in modern times, having married many core principles of the traditional Vas Ira with the practice of natural cannibalism inherent to the Xolor of Nirydia. They are currently in a tentative armistice with the mighty Omna of <a href="Lisava" class="internal-link">Lisava</a> on their western border, and diplomatically shunned by essentially all nations and communities of the island.</p>
