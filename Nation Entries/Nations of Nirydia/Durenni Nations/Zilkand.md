<p>In the Valley of Song and on the western coasts of the Bay of Zivac lies the Omna of Zilkand. Alongside Raximan & Lisava, Zilkand is one of three major successor states to the great Bihra realm of Japhal and on amicable terms with both of them. The traders of Zilkand practice good relations not only with its Bihra neighbours but also with the industrious Qalmyr of Kamerzin and even the humans of Gorat, they are known for their Giraffe riders and magical perfumes.</p>
