<p>The Omna of Tilduren is a Sunborn realm on the coasts of the Durenni Sands, having broken free during a religious revolt from the old Empire of Vanpur before its collapse. It is the only predominantly sunborn realm in the known world and lays claim to the entire Desert in the southern half of the great Island of Durennis. Following its breaking away from Vanpur, the Sunborn of Tilduren engaged in a great "cleansing" akin to the Xolori Pogroms of the northern continent and killed thousands of the remaining Xolori population that were unable to flee the coastal cities. </p>
