<p>Situated on the eastern side of the Blue Bay in southern Durennis is the City of Raximan, the southernmost port of the island and all of Nirydia, home to a cooperative population of native Sunborn and Bihra, as well as many Qalmyr. Raximan has launched various expeditions south, attempting to land in the new world of Sirenna and establish a colony on its unclaimed shores in the name of the Rayadan. In accordance with ancient law, established in the times of Japhal, the City as not attempted to settle further into the Durenni Sands, although welcomes any and all Sunborn thereof that seek a home in its walls. </p>
