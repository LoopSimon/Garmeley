<p>Sitting in the hidden Yorvale of the Limsul Peaks in southern Nirydia, the Bihra of Rha-Gis serve and worship their living god, the Dragon Noor-Rah, said to be the ordained child of the Heavens and the Earth and the supreme being of the cosmos itself. The High Temple can only be accessed via the two gigantic stone lifts, the lift of Telemon and the lift of Gis, and has never been conquered or assailed by foreign force for hundreds of years. The Rhagi are extremely isolationist and rarely treat with their neighbours, although various Opmani mountain communities in the southern Peaks are said to be in exchange with the Bihra. The High Temple withstood the attempts of various greater and lesser Khans of the steppes to conquer its Peaks and subsume the so Home of the Dragon God.</p>
