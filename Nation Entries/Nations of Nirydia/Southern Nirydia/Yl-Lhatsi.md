<p>The Temple of Yl-Lhatsi is the largest organized city of the Mogyri people of the Drizan Peninsula, sitting at the edge of Lake Tsolha. While nominally claiming authority over all Mogyri of the Region, in effect the Temples control is mostly restricted to its immediate surroundings and many rivalling noble bloodlines only swear allegiance to its authority in name. <br> The current Mycomother, the highest office of the Temple, is Suolang of the Lingpa, who succeeded the controversial Chorioactis Lingpa Arjanuva in 907PA. </p>
<p>Following the Golden Age of the Temple under the Great <a href="Gaden" class="internal-link">Hericidium Gaden</a>, the often more than less decentralized realm of the Mogyri of Drizan has slowly embraced its place in a world of more and more connective tissue between foreign powers and flowing trade. Its advantageous position between the Gorati and Durenni Power in the east and the busy merchant navies of the niryd Sea in the west have led to many attempts by powers foreign and domestic to open up the historically isolationist Temple Structure. The Treaty of Nzhun of 888PA is the greatest symbol of both hope for the future and the degenerate policies of a progressive corrupt, depending on the eye of the beholder, but has undeniably led to an increase in cultural, diplomatic and most of all economic exchange between the Temple and its western neighbours. </p>
<p>The lands of Drizan are, largely due to its climate, geography and unique biodiversity, almost exclusively, populated by the Mogyri people and have been for over a thousand years, resisting various attempts of conquest by the powerful Khaganates of the Steppes. Ever since Gaden, the process of Shroombonding has allowed select <a href="Bihra" class="internal-link">Bihra</a>, <a href="Human" class="internal-link">Humans</a> and <a href="Orc" class="internal-link">Orcs</a> to settle and live within the Peninsula as well.</p>
