<p>Beyond the Citadels and Cities of <a href="Kherova" class="internal-link">Kherova</a> are the Steppes of Nirydia, and within it the larger and lesser Khagantes and Tribes of the Steppe <a href="Bihra" class="internal-link">Bihra</a> prone to mutation, spiritualism and war.
While the Great Khans of the Steppe have risen and fallen quickly in history, the strongest ones have always left a mark behind on the history of the eastern lands.
</b>
The most influential and largest Khaganate of the current days is the Realm of Duol, whose administrative centre is the massive encampment of Duol Rabad, on the Cliffs of Rabad in the south western steppe. </p>
