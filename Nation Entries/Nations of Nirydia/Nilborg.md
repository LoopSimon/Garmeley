<p>Nilborg is a <a href="Juelfluk" class="internal-link">jueli</a> Colony that has been granted various rights and autonomies by the Crown of Juelfluk, sitting in the far northern lands of <a href="Nirydia" class="internal-link">Nirydia</a> near Faewish Bay. The first jueli settlers crossed Henmars Passage  centuries ago, but the town of Hremny was only founded in the late 8th century, as more merchants and settlers arrived and trade as well as diplomacy with locals became more important a new government became mandatory. King Munja II of House Udry granted the colony of Juelfluk its Charter of rights in 848PA and with it the first Baron of Hremny became its leader. To day Nilborg has grown into a sizeable enterprise and represents the growing hope of the jueli people in the east, its unique position as the only such ilrakhani colony in northern nirydia giving their merchants a monopoly on various luxury goods, although travel through Henmars Passage is only possible for around three months per year due to the weather conditions. </p>
