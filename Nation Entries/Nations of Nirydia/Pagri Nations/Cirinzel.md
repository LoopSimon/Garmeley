<p>Cirinzel is a Zuhuri realm of the <a href="Pagri" class="internal-link">Pagri</a> on the northern shore of the Gavivag Bay, with its capital at Trilom. It was one of the founding members of the <a href="League of Aviv" class="internal-link">League of Aviv</a> and fought in the <a href="Zuhuri Holy Wars" class="internal-link">Zuhuri Holy Wars</a> alongside the other Pagri realms.</p>
<h2>Trilom</h2>
<p>Trilom was not built by the Pagri but by the Gorati of the <a href="Zepria" class="internal-link">Zeprian Empire</a>, which at the height of its power held parts of the northern shore of the bay and of the Pagri lands beyond. The city is named after the three great gates built into its walls. It was at Trilom that the early and most powerful Zeprian emperors landed before travelling north to be crowned in Aviv, and in the early Holy Wars, in which the Empire was inarguably the main force of the faithful, it served as the staging ground for the Zeprian armies. In 667PA, a century before the fall of the Empire, Cirinzel took Trilom together with <a href="Gavivag Isle" class="internal-link">Gavivag Isle</a> and its <a href="Eye of Zuhur" class="internal-link">Eye of Zuhur</a>, and made the city its capital. From 872PA to 875PA Cirinzel lost the island and the Eye to <a href="Plašato" class="internal-link">Plašato</a> in the Gavivag War, and Trilom has since lived within sight of a Gorati weapon.</p>
