<p>The Great Gaal, sometimes called the Gaal Empire is one of the great powers of Nirydia and located north of the Terenni Sea on the island of Ynkal and across the strait in Ravaz. It is dominated by a <a href="Minotaur" class="internal-link">Minotaur</a> ruling class and primarily consists of a Bihra and Human population. The Gaal is currently embroiled in a war against the <a href="Igpuri Dominion" class="internal-link">Igpuri Dominion</a> and the sworn enemies of the <a href="Twintail Imperium" class="internal-link">Twintail Imperium</a> on the greater stage of eastern nirydian politics. The Realm practices a unique version of <a href="Iratism" class="internal-link">Iratism</a>  that adopts many elements centred around the the Cult of Volok of the ancient puthraci minotaur culture, although <a href="Zuhurism" class="internal-link">Zuhurism</a> is largely tolerated within its borders, especially in the western parts of Ravaz.</p>
<p>Gaal is famous for its warrior culture, Rasamar System of class and unique attitude to magic, it is the worlds largest if not only producer of the unique element <a href="Yeorite" class="internal-link">Yeorite</a> capable of disrupting and even fully supressing the channelling and steering of Planar Energy in the material realm. The Rasamar describes the "natural order of souls within the Tahan", mixing classical Principles of the Vas Ira with the worship of strength and blood found within the old Cult of Volok. In its current form it has taken on a very literal interpretation of many Yova writings, putting the perceived to be stronger and wiser Minotaurs above the Human and Bihra within society. Racial dominance and purity is important for the Gaal Lords, due to the unique biology of the Minotaur, allowing them to only sire a single child over the course of their life. As such within the Rasamar, Minotaurs can be viewed almost akin to the feudal nobility found within most of contemporary Ilrakhan.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>The Minotaurs of Puthrac cross the great eastern Sea, now known as the Gaalian Sea at an unknown date in old history through mythical means, a feat subject to many of the tales of the great Terenni mythos cycle. For hundreds of years they were only present on Ynkal, largely enslaved for their great physical strength by the Xolori <a href="Vanpur" class="internal-link">Empire of Vanpur</a> a period known as the Era of Shame in gaali history. In 279PA, the legendary Hero Hagrash of Ynkal leads thousands of Minotaurs into rebellion against their Vanpuri Slavers beginning a time of emancipation.</p>
<p>What followed was an age of strife and conflict, Vanpur remained in control over much of Ynkal for centuries until the outbreak of the Plague of Flesh in 401PA and the subsequent collapse and splintering of the Empire in 427PA. Various great Warlords rose in Ynkal, the island largely populated by Xolori, Bihra and Minotaur communities entered what is now known as the Era of warring hordes, seeing the rise and fall of many fierce leaders, warriors and heroes as no single power could unite the island or hold on to continuous power for centuries. It is during this time where we first see the appearance of the social hierarchy known as the Gaali Rasamar System that can be found within the Empire today, enshrining the Minotaur as a creature "above" the feeble races, referring to the Bihra, Xolori and humans of the region. </p>
<p>Gaal itself was established in the year 686PA, marking the end of the Era of warring Hordes of Ynkal and marking the beginning of Gaalian dominance over not only the island of Ynkal but later on Gilipor and the lands of Ravaz as well. In 699, the Invasion of Ravazid was launched, resulting in the destruction of the Kingdom and the first of the so called "Great Conquests". Gaali diplomatic policy has always been shifting in relation to the zuhuri of central nirydia and specifically the League of Aviv, with which it stood allied for much of the 8th century during the Fourth and Fifth Zuhuri Holy war. </p>
