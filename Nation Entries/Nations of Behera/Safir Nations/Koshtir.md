It is ruled by the Great Sage <a href="Hilcarax" class="internal-link">Hilcarax</a>, the Misteater, from the Temple of the Singer, and it is the only great city of <a href="Tal" class="internal-link">Tal</a> that does not follow <a href="Sochor" class="internal-link">Sochor</a>.</p> <p>The people of Koshtir are Mirani Safir, and their faith is the <a href="Singer Worship" class="internal-link">Faith of the Singer</a>, of which Koshtir has become a devout and zealous community, old Mirani is the language spoken within the city. Koshtir stands apart from the Yppori states of the mainland, and it is a great port of the Kaitan Gulf, where the ships of the <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae Sultanate</a> and its <a href="Grand Ziflis Company" class="internal-link">Grand Ziflis Company</a> come to trade.</p>  <p>Koshtir is ruled by its Great Sage, the High Priest of the Singer. The present Sage is Hilcarax, who in 624PA killed and absorbed the <a href="Yari" class="internal-link">Yari</a> of the Mists, <a href="Pnurzefec" class="internal-link">Pnurzefec</a>, and became a Yarimanji, and in 637PA was instituted as the Great Sage. He has ruled the city for almost three hundred years since.</p>  <p>The Empire of <a href="Naigan" class="internal-link">Naigan</a> attempted once to invade Koshtir, but the powers of the Sage over the mist were of such scale that an attack on the city from the sea or from the land was effectively impossible. Hilcarax agreed to hail Naigan as the rulers of the Purple Sea, and he is said to have foreseen that the great Purple Empire would fall in any case. Koshtir was never conquered.</p>  <p>In the last years of the <a href="Period of the Bleeding Heron" class="internal-link">Period of the Bleeding Heron</a> Koshtir fought <a href="Hanshi" class="internal-link">Hanshi</a> together with <a href="Chezin" class="internal-link">Chezin</a> and <a href="Rikarma" class="internal-link">Rikarma</a>. The war was fought largely over the Ariho and ended with the defeat of the royal army of Hanshi and the Peace of Chezin in 849PA.</p>
