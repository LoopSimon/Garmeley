<p> Once part of the <a href="Pravazik" class="internal-link">Pravazik</a> Empire , the great Tazamosch led his people to independence in the year 687PA. Almost exclusively populated by the green scaled Irud, they fervently worship the Yari Goddess Ihisa and believe she shall one day awaken and lift their holy city into the next world. Yokosch has been invaded multiple times by the <a href="Avdan-Kol" class="internal-link">Dwarves of Avdan-Kol</a> to its north but remains unbroken to this day.</p>
