<p>The Republic of Runsby, often referred to as Runland, is an island nation located on the Runsby & Lipley Isles, far off the Coast of western Behera. First a former colony of <a href="Spursley" class="internal-link">Kingdom of Spursley</a>, then a proper part of its Kingdom, the Isles have united under a single Banner ca a decade after the tragic fall of Spursley in 747PA. Unlike the <a href="Bilsworth" class="internal-link">Ministry of Bilsworth</a>, its citizens are not of the undead variety and both countries consider themselves the true successors of Spursley.</p>
<p>The Spurshey have adapted well to life as an island trading republic and are doing well for it. Runsby is governed by a Parliament in Hark, and being relatively secure at home it is able to commit vast resources overseas. The shadow of the fall of Spursley has not left it, for the curse that followed has taken root on the Dark Islands, and the mining of <a href="Brystone" class="internal-link">brystone</a> there, a volatile material that is dangerous to dig under any circumstances, is a risky undertaking that the Spurshey have long left to debt slaves and convicts. Parliament sits in two chambers, in which the old noble houses still hold great power, and custom gives the whole estate and title of a family to its eldest child, regardless of sex</p> 

<p>In the 870s the Spurshey began to cross the Tirean Ocean under the explorers Nale Dirrenger and Lady Eleanor Grig, and in 905PA Parliament chartered the colony of <a href="Dalesire" class="internal-link">Dalesire</a> on the Loreli Islands, in the south-east of Peadyr. Runsby has no part in the treaties of the Old World powers over the new one. As slavery is nothing new to the Spurshey, they have joined the <a href="Narzoi Slavetrade" class="internal-link">Narzoi Slavetrade</a>, and a small number of Narzoi slaves have already been shipped back to the core territories of Runsby, where Parliament means the trade to lead in the end.</p>
