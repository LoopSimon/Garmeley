<p>The City of Tah is a port town in the extremely inhospitable jungles of Vinjira along the southern coast of the Smaragd Sea. It is notably almost entirely home to the mysterious Wispmer, and has reached somewhat legendary status as a destination of daring adventurers and brave merchants. The White, almost milk like appearance of the Wispmer has given the City its nickname as the "City of Wax".</p>
