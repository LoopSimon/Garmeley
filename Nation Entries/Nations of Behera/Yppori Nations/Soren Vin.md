<p>The Kingdom of Soren Vin is an absolute monarchy of the Talang in the south east of <a href="Tal" class="internal-link">Tal</a>, ruled by King Molopgar V from its capital Natein. Its banner shows the Golden Elephant, and its crown goes back to the warlord <a href="Molopgar I" class="internal-link">Molopgar I</a>, the victor of the Pact of Water.</p>

<p>The kingdom holds the south eastern peninsula of the mainland, the country of the Talang. The Talang raised the earliest and greatest of the cities of Tal, among them ancient Char, and the country takes its name from their language. Natein is the capital and Bangoy is the other great city of the kingdom. Talang is the official language of the court, and the people follow <a href="Sochor" class="internal-link">Sochor</a>.</p>

<p>Soren Vin is an absolute monarchy. The present king, Molopgar V, bears the name of the founder of the kingdom. Some hold that the Golden Elephant on the banner is the Parakar, the elephant of the Sunrider Chenza, who carried the Sun across the sky (see <a href="Chenism" class="internal-link">Chenism</a>).</p>

<p>Molopgar I, who lived from 799 to 858PA, was a warlord of the south in the <a href="Period of the Bleeding Heron" class="internal-link">Period of the Bleeding Heron</a>. In league with the earlier rulers of <a href="Byamon" class="internal-link">Byamon</a> he formed the Pact of Water, the alliance that won the southern conflicts of the Period, and he later founded the Kingdom of Soren Vin and became its King. Soren Vin took no part in the war that ended with the Peace of Chezin in 849PA. Its old ally Byamon is its rival today.</p>

<p>Fiyang, the old capital of the <a href="Kingdom of Rin" class="internal-link">Kingdom of Rin</a> and of the <a href="Golden Empire" class="internal-link">Golden Empire</a>, lies on the river Fohang, which marks the border with Byamon, and it belongs to Soren Vin. The armies of <a href="Naigan" class="internal-link">Naigan</a> took it in 532PA, and it never again knew its former greatness. It is now a faded city of the south coast, living on its memory.</p>

<p>North of Bangoy lies the Ariho, the stretch of land between the kingdom and <a href="Chezin" class="internal-link">Chezin</a>, east of the swamplands. It was the cause of the war between <a href="Hanshi" class="internal-link">Hanshi</a> and the northern allies, and it was kept by Chezin at the peace.</p>
