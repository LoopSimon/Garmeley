<p>The Kingdom of Byamon is an absolute monarchy of the western jungles of <a href="Tal" class="internal-link">Tal</a>, ruled by King Thuriya Min from its capital Hytzen on the southern coast. It is the homeland of the Al Ypp, the people of the Yppo Riders, it is the land of the <a href="Great Kiln" class="internal-link">Great Kiln</a>, the oldest temple of <a href="Sochor" class="internal-link">Sochor</a>, and its rulers keep the Ivory Crown of the old <a href="Dynasties of Tal" class="internal-link">Dynasties of Tal</a>. Byamon is often considered the largest and most powerful of the contemporary realms of Tal.</p>

<p>Byamon is a country of jungle, rivers and warm rains, which reaches from the southern coast around Hytzen inland to the city of Jungun and toward the slopes of the Xermesis. Its people are the Al Ypp, the <a href="Yppori" class="internal-link">Yppori</a> of the western jungles, whose name and that of the <a href="Yppo" class="internal-link">Yppo</a> are one, and who are famed for their expert Yppo Riders. The great script of Talang is the language of the court and of law, while the Al Ypp keep their own speech beside it, and almost all of them follow Sochor. Many of their kin live beyond the Xermesis in the jungles of western <a href="Kaito" class="internal-link">Kaito</a>, under the rule of the Safiri states.</p>

<p>Byamon is ruled as an absolute monarchy. King Thuriya Min holds the whole authority of the realm from his court at Hytzen. The arms of the kingdom are the Red Sun in the Circle of Strength, a sun that recalls the Sun carried across the sky by Chenza in the old faith (see <a href="Chenism" class="internal-link">Chenism</a>).</p>

<p>The Ivory Crown of Tal was the symbol of the Dynasties of Tal. After the fall of the Muyra Dynasty in 229BA it was sealed in the mythical great vault, and later stolen by unknown forces. It resurfaced on the mainland in the later days of the Empire of <a href="Naigan" class="internal-link">Naigan</a>, and is held today by the rulers of Byamon, who count it the proof of their place among the heirs of the oldest realms of the Yppori.</p>

<p>The Great Kiln, the seat of the Osafar, stands in the jungle of Byamon, upstream of Fiyang on the strip of land where two smaller rivers run together to become the river Fohang. The Kiln has its own armed and sworn guard in the Brandgivers and the Osafar is no servant of the king, but pilgrims of the <a href="Ashcult" class="internal-link">Ashcult</a> come to it from all of Tal and the Purple Sea, making the standing of the kingdom as the land of the Kiln somewhat elevated.</p>

<p>InDuring the <a href="Period of the Bleeding Heron" class="internal-link">Period of the Bleeding Heron</a> the earlier rulers of Byamon joined the warlord <a href="Molopgar I" class="internal-link">Molopgar I</a> of the south in the Pact of Water, the alliance that won the southern conflicts of the Period. Byamon took no part in the war that ended with the Peace of Chezin in 849PA. The allies of the Pact are rivals today. <a href="Soren Vin" class="internal-link">Soren Vin</a> lies to the east, and between the two kingdoms, on the Fohang, lies Fiyang, once the capital of the ancient Kingdom of Rin and later the mighty <a href="Golden Empire" class="internal-link">Golden Empire</a>. The river is the border between them, and the faded city is now under the rule of Soren Vin.</p>
