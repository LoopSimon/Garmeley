<p>The Kingdom of Rikarma is a feudal kingdom of the Anpon in the north eastern highlands of <a href="Tal" class="internal-link">Tal</a>, ruled by King Quang Hùng from its capital Pienun. Its emblem is the Head of <a href="Sochor" class="internal-link">Sochor</a> and the realm is often known as the Burning Kingdom, practicing the rites of the Ashcult excessively. Rikarma is a country of highlands in the north east of the mainland and the home of the Anpon, the <a href="Yppori" class="internal-link">Yppori</a> of the north. Talang is the official language of the court, and the Anpon keep their own speech beside it. </p>

<p>Rikarma is a feudal kingdom. Its land is held by lords who owe the King service in war and in counsel, and who hold their estates from the crown. King Quang Hùng rules the realm from Pienun.</p>

<p>The Burning Kingdom was one of the three countries that fought <a href="Hanshi" class="internal-link">Hanshi</a> in the last years of the <a href="Period of the Bleeding Heron" class="internal-link">Period of the Bleeding Heron</a>, together with <a href="Chezin" class="internal-link">Chezin</a> and <a href="Koshtir" class="internal-link">Koshtir</a>, and it made peace with Hanshi in the Peace of Chezin of 849PA. The war was fought largely over the Ariho, the stretch of land south of Chezin, and it ended with the defeat of the royal army of Hanshi.</p>
