<p>The Kingdom of Rikarma is a feudal kingdom of the Anpon in the north eastern highlands of <a href="Tal" class="internal-link">Tal</a>, ruled by King Quang Hùng from its capital Pienun. Its emblem is the Head of <a href="Sochor" class="internal-link">Sochor</a>.</p>

<p>Rikarma is a country of highlands in the north east of the mainland and the home of the Anpon, the <a href="Yppori" class="internal-link">Yppori</a> of the north. Talang is the official language of the court, and the Anpon keep their own speech beside it. The people follow Sochor, and the Head of Sochor on the arms of the kingdom is the face of the god.</p>

<p>Rikarma is a feudal kingdom. Its land is held by lords who owe the King service in war and in counsel, and who hold their estates from the crown. King Quang Hùng rules the realm from Pienun.</p>

<p>Rikarma was one of the three countries that fought <a href="Hanshi" class="internal-link">Hanshi</a> in the last years of the <a href="Period of the Bleeding Heron" class="internal-link">Period of the Bleeding Heron</a>, together with <a href="Chezin" class="internal-link">Chezin</a> and <a href="Koshtir" class="internal-link">Koshtir</a>, and it made peace with Hanshi in the Peace of Chezin of 849PA. The war was fought largely over the Ariho, the stretch of land south of Chezin, and it ended with the defeat of the royal army of Hanshi.</p>

<p>Chezin lies on the coast to the east of the highlands, and the two countries are rivals over the tolls on the trade that passes between the highlands and the sea. They were once allies against Hanshi during the Period of the Bleeding Heron.</p>
