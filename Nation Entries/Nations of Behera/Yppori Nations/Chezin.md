<p>Chezin is a merchant republic of the Anpon on the north eastern coast of <a href="Tal" class="internal-link">Tal</a>, centred on the port city of the same name on Chezin Island. A council of the great trading houses governs it under the First Merchant, Datu Mallari, and its arms show the Boneshell. The Boneshell is a unique shellfish of the Purple Sea whose white shell gives the city its emblem. It yields a purple dye that is prized in Tal and far beyond it, the dye from which the sea takes its name, and the trade in it is the oldest source of the wealth of Chezin. The great palaces of the city are often called the Shell Towers of Chezin, in the honour of the humble thing that brought the city its wealth.</p>

<p>Chezin has no king. The great trading houses sit together in a council, and the First Merchant, chosen from among them, leads it and speaks for the city. Datu Mallari is the present First Merchant and the people follow <a href="Sochor" class="internal-link">Sochor</a>, with Talang as the official language of business.</p>
<h2>The Ariho</h2>
<p>The war between <a href="Hanshi" class="internal-link">Hanshi</a> and the northern allies of Chezin, <a href="Koshtir" class="internal-link">Koshtir</a> and <a href="Rikarma" class="internal-link">Rikarma</a>, was fought largely over the Ariho, the stretch of land north of Bangoy and south of Chezin, east of the swamplands. The royal army of Hanshi was beaten there and thrown into the sea, and the decision for peace was made. Chezin, which kept control of the Ariho, gave up in return all its claims to any island east of Chezin Island itself. Eight times since have warlords or even small groups of patriotic adventurers attempted to reclaim the borderlands from the grasp of the Merchants of Chezin, never with any lasting success.</p>

<p>The peace of 849PA bears the name of the city. It ended the war, and with it the <a href="Period of the Bleeding Heron" class="internal-link">Period of the Bleeding Heron</a> was largely held to be over.</p>

<p>Rikarma is the rival of Chezin over tolls, although the two were allies against Hanshi. The island of <a href="Naigan" class="internal-link">Naigan</a>, the seat of the old Empire, lies to the north east. Ever since it was taken over by the Sorcerer <a href="Jinùk" class="internal-link">Jinùk</a>, its has become a source of greater and greater worries for Chezins fleets, who previously considered the abandoned imperial Island an effective extension of their domain.</p>
