<p>The Divine Queendom of Hanshi is the leading power of the Purple Sea, a divine monarchy of the Rytoku on the great island of Ryto and the islands around it. Its capital is Gyedo, on the east coast of Ryto, its throne is the Pearl Throne, and its ruler is the Divine Queen Teruna.</p>

<p>Ryto is a large and mountainous island in the east of the Purple Sea, across the Ashen Strait from the mainland, with the capital Gyedo on its east coast and the city of Ipog on its west. Its people are the Rytoku, the island branch of the <a href="Yppori" class="internal-link">Yppori</a>, seafarers whose ships are the farthest travelled in Tal. Rytoku is the official language of the queendom. The Fitta Islands near Hanshi are the only place where the Asta poppies grow.</p>

<p>Hanshi is a divine monarchy, in the form of the old Empire of <a href="Naigan" class="internal-link">Naigan</a>, which the queendom claims as its predecessor. Its ruler, the Divine Queen, is held to be reborn through the Pearl Throne itself, so that whoever sits upon it is more than the heir of the queen before her. The present Queen is Teruna. Her arms are the Igali Fireflower.</p>

<p>The people of Hanshi follow <a href="Sochor" class="internal-link">Sochor</a>, but the <a href="Ashcult" class="internal-link">Ashcult</a> of the islands is noticeably different from that of the mainland. The Rytoku worship Sochor in many shrines across the isles of the queendom, though they too pay homage to the <a href="Great Kiln" class="internal-link">Great Kiln</a> and the Osafar.</p>

<p>Gyedo was one of the last holdouts against the Empire of Naigan, and in 681PA the Empire took the city and sacked it horribly, and the channeler Tihir Szuan died in the storming. After the fall of the Empire in 771PA Hanshi rose to the first rank of the powers of the Purple Sea. In the last years of the <a href="Period of the Bleeding Heron" class="internal-link">Period of the Bleeding Heron</a> it fought <a href="Koshtir" class="internal-link">Koshtir</a>, <a href="Chezin" class="internal-link">Chezin</a> and <a href="Rikarma" class="internal-link">Rikarma</a>, largely over the Ariho on the mainland coast. The royal army of Hanshi was beaten there and thrown into the sea, and it accepted peace. At the Peace of Chezin in 849PA, Chezin, which kept the Ariho, gave up all its claims to any island east of Chezin Island itself.</p>
<h2>The New World and Astafar</h2>
<p>The wealth of Hanshi rests on the trade in <a href="Astafar" class="internal-link">Astafar</a>, the drug made from the Asta poppies of the Fitta Islands. In 923PA Captain Armida Zheni sailed the Cylla from Gyedo and in 924PA she found the continent of <a href="Sirenna" class="internal-link">Sirenna</a>, which the Pearl Throne proclaimed royal territory (see <a href="The Discovery of Sirenna" class="internal-link">The Discovery of Sirenna</a>).</p>
