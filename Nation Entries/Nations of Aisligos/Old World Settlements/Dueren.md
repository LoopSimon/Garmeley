<p>Dueren, or Düren in the Meadi tongue, is the colonial holding of the <a href="Gesellschaft für Westmeadischen Handel" class="internal-link">Königliche Gesellschaft für Westmeadischen Handel</a> and the primary arm of the Meadi Empire in the new world, founded in 921PA on the island the Meadi explorers named Giselland in honour of Queen Gisela III of Meadrath. Unlike the settlements of Peadyr, Dueren has claimed an island largely untouched by prior colonial competition, positioning the K.G.W. as the dominant power in the northern reaches of the Aisligos sphere rather than as one contestant among many on a crowded shore. In its early years the colony had to fight off a swarm of <a href="Triarog" class="internal-link">Triarog</a> from the western part of the island.</p>


<p>Under the terms of the <a href="Treaty of Lotsprings" class="internal-link">Treaty of Lotsprings</a>, the lands north of the treaty line fall to Meadrath and the Gesellschaft, while Jurland and everything south of it belong to the League and the G.U.V. Dueren therefore stands clear of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a>, where the Meadi Crown has no claim, has no direct contact with its Narzoi and cooperates with <a href="Faarhovn" class="internal-link">Faarhovn</a> where the island is concerned, a division that makes the two settlements close partners despite the broader rivalry of their patron states, the two companies finding more common cause in regulating the ocean trade between them than in competing with each other. </p>

<p>As the most recently founded of the colonial settlements Dueren remains young, but the resources of the Meadi Empire behind it and the strategic clarity of its unchallenged island position give it a solid foundation from which to grow.</p>
