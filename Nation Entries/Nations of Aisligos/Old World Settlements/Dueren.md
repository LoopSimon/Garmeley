<p>Dueren, or Düren in the Meadi tongue, is the colonial holding of the Königliche Gesellschaft für Westmeadischen Handel and the primary arm of the Meadi Empire in the new world, founded in 921PA on the island the Meadi explorers named Giselland in honour of Queen Gisela III of Meadrath. Unlike the settlements of Peadyr, Dueren has claimed an island largely untouched by prior colonial competition, positioning the K.G.W. as the dominant power in the northern reaches of the Aisligos sphere rather than as one contestant among many on a crowded shore. Under the terms of the Treaty of Lotsprings, Meadrath and the Gesellschaft hold authority over the northern territories of the new world, a division that makes Dueren and Faarhoven close allies despite the broader rivalry of their patron states, the two companies finding more common cause in regulating the ocean trade between them than in competing with each other. As the most recently founded of the colonial settlements Dueren remains young, but the resources of the Meadi Empire behind it and the strategic clarity of its unchallenged island position give it a solid foundation from which to grow.</p>
