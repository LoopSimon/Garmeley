<p>Faarhovn is the principal settlement and commercial headquarters of the <a href="Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel" class="internal-link">Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel</a> in Aisligos, founded in 878PA on the island of Jurland, across the Eyks Channel from the northern coast of Peadyr. Of all the colonial settlements established by the powers of the old world, Faarhovn stands apart as the only one to have secured formal relations and official trading rights with the indigenous Narzoi, specifically the <a href="Kavi" class="internal-link">Kavi</a>, whose lands lie across the channel and whose cooperation has made Faarhovn the only colonial port permitted to conduct trade on mainland Peadyr itself. This unique arrangement, negotiated through sustained diplomacy rather than force, reflects both the commercial pragmatism of the G.U.V. and the calculated judgement of the Kavi, who have found in Faarhovn a more manageable partner than the more aggressive colonial ventures to their south and east.</p>
<p>Under the terms of the <a href="Treaty of Lotsprings" class="internal-link">Treaty of Lotsprings</a>, the lands west of the Tirean Ocean were divided between the Blijgrat League and the Meadi Crown along a diagonal line drawn across the old maps, running from south-west to north-east. Jurland and the lands south of the line, Peadyr among them, fall to the G.U.V. on behalf of the League. To the settlers, who know little of the lands beyond their own coasts, this leaves the Meadi with no claim to anything near Peadyr, and it makes Faarhovn not only the most diplomatically successful but also the most commercially significant Ilrakhan presence in Aisligos.</p>

<p>Among the members of the expedition that founded Faarhovn in 878PA was Gustav, the uncle of <a href="Jan van de Spiegel" class="internal-link">Jan van de Spiegel</a>, the present Stadtholder of the Blijgrat League.</p>
