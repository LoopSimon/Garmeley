<p>Faarhoven is the principal settlement and commercial headquarters of the <a href="Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel" class="internal-link">Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel</a> in Aisligos, founded in 878PA on the northern coast of Peadyr. Of all the colonial settlements established by the powers of the old world, Faarhoven stands apart as the only one to have secured formal relations and official trading rights with the indigenous Narzoi, specifically the <a href="Kavi" class="internal-link">Kavi</a>, whose lands adjoin the settlement and whose cooperation has made Faarhoven the only colonial port permitted to conduct trade on mainland Peadyr itself. This unique arrangement, negotiated through sustained diplomacy rather than force, reflects both the commercial pragmatism of the G.U.V. and the calculated judgement of the Kavi, who have found in Faarhoven a more manageable partner than the more aggressive colonial ventures to their south and east.</p>
<p> Under the terms of the Treaty of Lotsprings, Faarhoven and the broader G.U.V. hold jurisdiction over the southern reaches of the new world on behalf of the Blijgrat League, making it not only the most diplomatically successful but also the most commercially significant Ilrakhan presence in Aisligos.</p>
