<p>Almersport is a joint colonial venture of the Lissvalley Federation and the Kingdom of Erlmondy, founded in 895PA on the southern coast of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a> as a commercial port and colonial holding of the Visian League powers. Where other settlements have sought accommodation or at least the appearance of it with the indigenous <a href="Narzoi" class="internal-link">Narzoi</a>, Almersport has pursued expansion with open aggression, and by the modern day finds itself in a state of declared war with the <a href="Odise" class="internal-link">Odise</a> tribe whose ancestral lands the settlement has encroached upon. The colony reflects the combined ambitions of its two founding patrons, Lissvalley’s commercial appetite and Erlmondy’s proud martial tradition, and has become the most belligerent Ilrakhan presence on Peadyr, its conflicts with the Odise an ongoing source of tension not only with the native peoples but with the more diplomatically minded settlements to its north. Almersport’s aggression has in particular strained relations with Faarhoven, whose carefully maintained Kavi alliance is threatened by the wider resentment that Almersport’s conduct generates among the Narzoi tribes of Peadyr.</p>
