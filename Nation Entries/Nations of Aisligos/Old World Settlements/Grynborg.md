<p>Grynborg is the oldest human settlement in the continental sphere of Aisligos, founded in 719PA by Skori and Olbellinge sailors on the Gerjodi Islands, a chain of mountainous outcroppings in the cold northern waters of the Tirean Ocean. The islands were claimed by the Kingdom of <a href="Baeling" class="internal-link">Baeling</a> in the early years, which helped fund and build up the fledgling colony, but following the great plague quarantine of 820PA that cut Grynborg off from its old world patrons, Baeling and the other founding powers gradually withdrew their interest and left the settlement to its own devices.</p> 
<p>Grynborg recovered from the sickness slowly but completely, and in doing so became something its founders never intended, a fully independent realm, beholden to no baeling crown and answering to no distant lord. Its position on the Kelzen Route, the northern sea lane that connects the ports of the Torn Sea, most notably of Trykkerog, Blijgrat and Meadrath, to the shores of Aisligos via a favourable current, has made Grynborg one of the most reliably visited harbours in the known Tirean Ocean. With no territorial ambitions on the greater continent and little interest in the politics of Peadyr, Grynborg has instead perfected the art of the welcome port, a place of resupply, repair, trade and rest for every ship passing in either direction, its prosperity built not on conquest but on position.</p>
