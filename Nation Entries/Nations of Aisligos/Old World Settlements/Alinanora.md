<p>Alinanora is the crown colony of the Duchy of Turpador, founded in 900PA at the mouth of the Dilin River delta on the western coast of Peadyr, named in honour of Duccessa Alina I Sevir. The settlement takes its name and its founding spirit from Jaime di Dilin, the Turpadori explorer and diplomat who first negotiated with the Narzoi of Peadyr in 842PA and after whom the river itself is named. Like its fellow mainland settlements Alinanora has established itself against the formal objections of the indigenous Narzoi peoples, yet has maintained a markedly more measured and amicable relationship with the local communities than its southern rivals, pursuing influence through trade and presence rather than open confrontation. Backed by the Pastelliora and acting in close coordination with the Martellan port of Livoa nearby, Alinanora forms the western anchor of a growing Cevoli axis on and around Peadyr, with the Duchy of Turpador, the Grand Duchy of Martella and the Pastelliora financiers together working toward an exclusive Cevoli commercial presence in the new world, one that the settlement’s leadership hopes may eventually see its more aggressive rivals on Peadyr displaced without the need for open war.</p>
