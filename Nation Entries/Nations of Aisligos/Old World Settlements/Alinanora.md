<p>Alinanora is the crown colony of the Duchy of Turpador, founded in 900PA at the mouth of the Dilin River delta on the eastern coast of Peadyr, named in honour of Duccessa Alina I Sevir. The settlement takes its name and its founding spirit from <a href="Jaime di Dilin" class="internal-link">Jaime di Dilin</a>, the Turpadori explorer and diplomat who first negotiated with the Narzoi of Peadyr in 842PA and after whom the river itself is named. Like its fellow mainland settlements Alinanora has established itself against the formal objections of the indigenous Narzoi peoples, yet
has kept up a markedly more measured appearance towards the local communities
than its southern rivals, pursuing influence through trade and presence rather than open confrontation. Backed by the Pastelliora and acting in close coordination with the Martellan port of Livoa nearby, Alinanora forms the western anchor of a growing Cevoli axis on and around Peadyr, with the Duchy of Turpador, the Grand Duchy of Martella and the Pastelliora financiers together working toward an exclusive Cevoli commercial presence in the new world, one that the settlement’s leadership hopes may eventually see its more aggressive rivals on Peadyr displaced without the need for open war.</p>
<p>Behind that restraint lies the colony's part in the <a href="Narzoi Slavetrade" class="internal-link">Narzoi Slavetrade</a>. Since its founding the Vudi of the Dilin delta have been kidnapped and put to work for the settlement, while the leadership takes care never to be seen attacking them openly. Alinanora is, with Livoa, one of the two harbours where Narzoi captives are brought and sold on, and the Pastelliora who back the colony have grown rich on the trade. In 926PA a heavily guarded Cevoli vessel carrying Narzoi slaves was attacked by the pirate <a href="Kylian Juz" class="internal-link">Kylian Juz</a> half a day out of the colony.</p>
