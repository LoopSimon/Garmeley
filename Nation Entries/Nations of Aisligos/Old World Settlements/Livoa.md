<p>Livoa is the oldest of the Peadyr colonial settlements, founded in 858PA by the <a href="Martella" class="internal-link">Grand Duchy of Martella</a> on the small island of Pearsmount off the eastern coast of Peadyr. Born from the same Martellan expeditionary spirit that sent Marino Viamali across the Tirean Ocean in 840PA, Livoa carries the mixed Sertovi and Cevoli character of its founding duchy and has grown into one of the most important transit harbours of the new world, serving as the primary first port of call for ships arriving via the central Tirean Route. Unlike its fellow colonial settlements on Peadyr proper, Livoa has largely kept itself clear of the struggles between the colonizing powers and the indigenous <a href="Narzoi" class="internal-link">Narzoi</a>, its island position removing it from the immediate contest over Peadyr’s mainland. The city’s ambitions instead point further south and west, into the warmer waters of the Silken Sea, and Livoa has in recent years begun readying exploratory expeditions into those largely uncharted reaches, with the backing of the <a href="Pastelliora" class="internal-link">Pastelliora</a> and in close cooperation with its Turpadori allies at <a href="Alinanora" class="internal-link">Alinanora</a> a short sail to the west.</p>
