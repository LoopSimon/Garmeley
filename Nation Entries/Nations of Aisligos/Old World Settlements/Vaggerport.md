<p>Vaggerport is a free port and haven of pirates, smugglers and outlaws on a cluster of small islets east of <a href="Almersport" class="internal-link">Almersport</a>, and the most makeshift of all the settlements of the new world. The town is built into a great sea cavern, whose roof is pierced by three holes through which the sun shines in at different points of the day, an image that gave the city its heraldry, the United Arms under three Suns. Its people have no love for their old overlords or for the would-be rulers of the new world, and the city is known above all for its lawlessness.</p>
<p>Vaggerport began as a trading outpost of <a href="Nahr" class="internal-link">Nahr</a>, planted in the sea caves in 891PA, four years before Almersport and nine before the <a href="Treaty of Lotsprings" class="internal-link">Treaty of Lotsprings</a>, to which it was never a party. Nahr's harbour of Vissy is a popular starting point for voyages across the Tirean Ocean, and the outpost was meant as a foothold for its merchants. Within a few years its settlers, resenting the taxes and authority of a distant government, threw off the rule of Nahr in what is remembered as the Vaggerport uprising, and the outpost became a free city.</p>
<p>The people of Vaggerport are Sertovi, Othiri and Cevoli sailors, merchants and outlaws, among them many <a href="Halfling" class="internal-link">halflings</a> from Nahr and <a href="Revelle" class="internal-link">Revelle</a>, a rootless generation driven abroad by the economic plight of Revelle, of the same age as the pirate <a href="Kylian Juz" class="internal-link">Kylian Juz</a>. The city's formal government is the Traders Council, but the name is a formality for a truly informal group, made up of the richest and most powerful leaders of the city, and Vaggerport knows no law beyond what they and the harbour will bear.</p>
<p>Vaggerport will provision and repair any ship that pays, and pirates such as Kylian Juz, who is rumoured to make it his haven, are far from the only ones to dock there. The Visian powers behind Almersport despise the city on their doorstep but cannot spare the ships to clear it, and their own merchants quietly make use of it. It is not known to host any sale of captives of the <a href="Narzoi Slavetrade" class="internal-link">Narzoi Slavetrade</a>, though slavers too are served.</p>
