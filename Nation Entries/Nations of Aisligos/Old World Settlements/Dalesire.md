<p>Dalesire is the chartered colony of the <a href="Runsby" class="internal-link">Republic of Runsby</a> on the Loreli Islands in the south-east of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a>, and the foothold of the Spurshey in the new world. Its capital is Dalesire Port, at the southern end of the islands, and since its charter in 905PA it has been governed by a Lord-Governour appointed by Parliament in Hark. The Spurshey named the islands for Lorel, the goddess of their <a href="Doggerism" class="internal-link">Doggerist</a> pantheon who is said to rule the kraken of the Tirean Ocean, and hold the waters around them to be hers.</p>

<p>The Spurshey came late to the new world, but they came prepared. Their first ships arrived in the 870s and 880s under two famous explorers. Nale Dirrenger sailed in 874 and made four voyages until 878, charting the seas and setting up supply posts between the island that bears his name, Nalesland, and the Spurshey ports of the Dark Islands. In 882 Lady Eleanor Grig, a noble entrepreneur with a taste for adventure, led a larger expedition that mapped the tiny rocky islands of the Spring Sea, found the islands now called Grigs Landing, which were inhabited by hostile natives, and then the lush Loreli Islands. More supply posts and expeditions of unofficial explorers followed, and when Parliament in Hark granted the growing communities of the Loreli Islands a charter, they became an official colony in 905PA.</p>

<p>The first Lord-Governour was Archibald Grig, son of Eleanor, who held the office until his death of stomach complications in 914PA, and it is now held by Richard Teeming. Dalesire is among the best supplied and best run settlements of the new world. Runsby is relatively secure at home and able to commit vast resources to the colony, and its navy is easily the most powerful in these waters. The Spurshey have no part in the <a href="Treaty of Lotsprings" class="internal-link">Treaty of Lotsprings</a>, which they either never heard of or never cared about, and Dalesire is tentatively at peace with <a href="Almersport" class="internal-link">Almersport</a> and <a href="Alinanora" class="internal-link">Alinanora</a>, though it is a rather loose arrangement. The colony has also opened Spurshey trade with northern merchants and the goods of the Meadi and Weadi, where before the Spurshey knew only the Cevoli traders across the Smoking Sea.</p>

<p>Nalesland holds no major town, only supply hubs, but it is home to the colony's most important site, a large temple of Lorel where crews and settlers alike make their offerings to the Mother of Monsters. The people of Dalesire are overwhelmingly Spurshey, with a few Drow, old allies of the Spurshey, living among them.</p>
<p>As at home, much of the colony's labour is done by debt slaves and convicts sent from Runsby, and Narzoi slaves taken in the <a href="Narzoi Slavetrade" class="internal-link">Narzoi Slavetrade</a> already form a part of its workforce. A small number have been shipped back to the core territories of Runsby, which is where the Spurshey mean the trade to lead in the end, to dig the volatile <a href="Brystone" class="internal-link">brystone</a> of their mines.</p>
