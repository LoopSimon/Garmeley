<p>The Lordship of Ulbreek is a free port on the island of the same name, off the southern coast of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a>, founded in 913PA by the Meadi nobleman Marrin Andil. The island had been named decades before by a Weadi explorer of the same name, who found it unclaimed. Unlike the other Weadi and Meadi ventures in the new world, Ulbreek is not an endeavour of one of the two great companies, the <a href="Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel" class="internal-link">G.U.V.</a> or the <a href="Gesellschaft für Westmeadischen Handel" class="internal-link">K.G.W.</a>, or of the rich nations behind them, but the idea of a single rich Meadi noble.</p>
<p>Andil had been a follower of Jean du Jera, the Duke of Dirneux, who headed the regency council of his daughter, the young Queen <a href="Gisela IV" class="internal-link">Gisela IV</a> of <a href="Meadrath" class="internal-link">Meadrath</a>, after the death of her mother in 910PA. When the queen took the government into her own hands in 913PA, dismissed much of the palace staff, recalled the exiled dignitaries and restored Count <a href="Odrick von Fulmer" class="internal-link">Odrick von Fulmer</a> to office, the faction of the Duke fell out of favour, and Andil, fearing persecution, gathered his riches and his entire wealth and left for the new world. He sailed with an armada of bought and hired vessels, no fewer than fourteen ships, financed above all by the nobles who joined him, the Houses di Vadeux and von Larm. On the unclaimed island of Ulbreek he declared himself its Lord and raised a port town that serves any and all, not unlike <a href="Livoa" class="internal-link">Livoa</a>.</p>
<p>Andil still proclaims himself a servant of the Crown of Meadrath, at least in name, and the K.G.W. leaves him alone for it, though neither company stands behind him. Ulbreek lies on the League's side of the line drawn at the <a href="Treaty of Lotsprings" class="internal-link">Treaty of Lotsprings</a>, where the Meadi have no claim, yet it is ruled by a Meadi lord who answers to neither of the signatory companies. The lordship is an autocratic oligarchy in all but name. Andil rules, but the Houses that financed his armada, di Vadeux and von Larm above all, share in the government, for it is in the interest of all of them that the ruling class remains in power. Andil himself is a cunning and opportunistic man.</p>
<p>The people of Ulbreek are the Meadi of the lord's household and followers, together with Weadi, among them the hired crews and artisans of the armada, those who dislike the rule of the enterprise at <a href="Faarhovn" class="internal-link">Faarhovn</a>, and more likely those who were thrown out of it for various crimes and shortcomings. Most follow <a href="Diarism" class="internal-link">Diarism</a>, the faith born in Dirneux, the lands of Andil's former patron, and a very popular movement among the settlers and traders of the new world, where it is less regulated than in the old. The port will provision and repair any ship that pays, slaver or not, and Ulbreek is said to hold slave auctions openly, as part of the <a href="Narzoi Slavetrade" class="internal-link">Narzoi Slavetrade</a>.</p>
