The Thuni are led by a Council of Elders and speak their own Thuni Narzoi dialect. They keep their own religious practice, which centres on the worship of water as the source of all life.</p> <p>The Thuni are arguably the most prolific seafarers of all the Narzoi. They are simply more experienced at sea than their cousins, with a seafaring culture of their own, and their vessels may well be sturdier. From 497PA they began to cross the Heroes Grave Sea to Peadyr and made first contact with the <a href="Senso" class="internal-link">Senso</a> of the southern coast. They traded largely with the Senso and sometimes, marginally, with the <a href="Odise" class="internal-link">Odise</a>, though that trade has since dried up.</p>
