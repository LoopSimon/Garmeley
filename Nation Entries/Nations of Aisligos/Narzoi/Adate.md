<p>The Adate Firmament is the largest Narzoi society on the land of <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a> , originating from the Adate tribal Federation, the most numerous and powerful group of Narzoi in the Aisligos Sphere, and have fought against the Pharang of <a href="Duria" class="internal-link">Duria</a> for centuries, in their very own holy eternal war. They follow the <a href="Haozon" class="internal-link">New Haozon</a> and consider themselves the spiritual and material successors to the ancient Alder peoples.</p>
<p>Adate, as a sedentary civilisation with a continuous history and culture, developed out of the earlier Ai Caravan Tribes, nomads that travelled throughout the island of Aisligos itself on the backs of their domesticated <a href="Pangomor" class="internal-link">Pangomor</a>. Eventually Adate settlements grew around various ruins of the pre - <a href="Uraz" class="internal-link">Urazi</a> cultures, even some of the mythical <a href="Aldergrounds" class="internal-link">Aldergrounds</a> of the ancient Alder peoples, as well as on the more arable and farmable lands around the Komi River systems. The reason the Ai Narzoi progressed much further and created a settled and city based civilisation, in contrast to the Narzoi of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a> is without a doubt traced back to the Pangomor. These animals act in their various species adapted to different tasks, as beasts of burdens, sources of food, enable transport and exchange and are easily domesticated, not too dissimilar from the Inaran Horse or Cow. In fact the Adate culture in  particular is famous for their love and close bond with these creatures, the famous Adate Knights riding on  backs of the Elazi Pangomor is an iconic image within their society.</p>
<p>The Adate consider themselves better and "higher" than many of their fellow Narzoi, especially shown by the extremely limited encounters they have had with the Pea Narzoi, whom they think of as essentially primitives, almost animals, as well as their treatment of the Kardeni, Narzoi tribes from the far north of Aisligos, and the Verzi. Adate engage in actively in Slavery and the practice is largely responsible for their most ambitious own achievements, such as the great Aqueduct.</p>
