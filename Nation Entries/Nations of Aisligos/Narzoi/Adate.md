<p>The Adate Firmament is the largest Narzoi society on the land of Aisligos, originating from the Adate tribal Federation, the most numerous and powerful group of Narzoi in the Aisligos Sphere, and have fought against the Pharang of <a href="Duria" class="internal-link">Duria</a> for centuries, in their very own holy eternal war. They follow the <a href="Haozon" class="internal-link">New Haozon</a> and consider themselves the spiritual and material successors to the ancient Alder peoples.</p>
