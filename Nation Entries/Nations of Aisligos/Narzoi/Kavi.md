It is made up of four tribes, the Kavi, Insevi, Rele and Madi, and most Old World accounts name the Kavi as the first of the Narzoi to meet seafarers from Ilrakhan. Because they first met them as a single tribe, Old World writers long called the whole federation by that name, and many still do.</p> <p>The Kavi roam and cultivate their coast and highlands, and the crops of the island, among them wild rice, coca, sweet potato, the pea bean and squash, grow across their lands. They follow <a href="Chianism" class="internal-link">Chianism</a>, and they are especially known for their veneration of animals.</p> <p>The Kavi are the least organised of the federations. They have no clear leader and are ruled instead by a great council of the tribes known as the Ulechiam, an institution that exists in the other federations too but is there usually headed by a single figure, known among the <a href="Odise" class="internal-link">Odise</a> and <a href="Senso" class="internal-link">Senso</a> as the Ul. The federation is largely neutral in its dealings with the world beyond its borders.</p> <p>Contact with the Old World began early and has not always been peaceful. Most accounts name the Kavi as the people whom <a href="Marino Viamali" class="internal-link">Marino Viamali</a> met in 840PA, and in 847PA warriors of the Rele, a tribe known for hunting in the dark in imitation of the <a href="Triarog" class="internal-link">Triarog</a>, the monstrous bat-like creature of the new world, destroyed the Sertovi colony of New Rav on the north-eastern tip of the island. The federation has opposed <a href="New Fryd" class="internal-link">New Fryd</a>, raised on the same ground in 880PA, ever since. In 878PA, by contrast, the Kavi Shaman received the expedition of the <a href="Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel" class="internal-link">Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel</a> and agreed to welcome Weadi traders into their communities on condition of religious respect, an accord that made <a href="Faarhovn" class="internal-link">Faarhovn</a> the only colonial port permitted to trade on the mainland.</p> <p>The accord has endured for nearly half a century, though Almersport's conduct has strained it, and the federation remains neutral in the war between the Odise and <a href="Almersport" class="internal-link">Almersport</a>. Its most famous son is <a href="Tezmer" class="internal-link">Tezmer</a>, the greatest living shaman of Chianism, who has long urged peace and cooperation with the Dari, as the Pea call the newcomers from the east.</p>
