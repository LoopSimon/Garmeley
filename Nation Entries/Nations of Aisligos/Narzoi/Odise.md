It is made up of six tribes, the Odise, Flaia, Dila, Seme, Tari and Rana, and takes its name from the Odise tribe.</p> <p>The Odise live along their rivers, farming, fishing and travelling by canoe, and they follow <a href="Chianism" class="internal-link">Chianism</a>. The heart of their devotion is the <a href="Finger of Life" class="internal-link">Finger of Life</a>, a pilgrimage site in the interior of the island that is especially holy to the Odise, though it is also worshipped by the <a href="Kavi" class="internal-link">Kavi</a> and the <a href="Senso" class="internal-link">Senso</a>, the latter of whom rarely make the journey. The pilgrim road runs through the northern Riverlands. Like the other federations, the Odise are ruled by a council of the tribes, the Ulechiam, which is headed by a single figure known as the Ul.</p> <p>The Odise are today the federation most deeply at war with the Old World. The founding of <a href="Almersport" class="internal-link">Almersport</a> on the south-eastern coast in 895PA brought armed expeditions up the rivers and onto Odise land, and these soon began to disturb the pilgrims on their way to the Finger of Life. In 917PA the young stormcaller <a href="Rylla" class="internal-link">Rylla</a> of the Flaia rallied many warriors from across the tribes of the federation and raided the colony's camps. When she was captured in 925PA and burned alive in the Old World, the Odise took up arms as a whole, and the Flaia, who had no clear leader beyond their elders, have since joined the greater Odise tribe.</p> <p>The war has turned against them. Almersport, which was losing in the early years, is now being reinforced from the Old World and by private mercenary companies, a build-up that troubles part of the nobility at home, and it is winning relentlessly. Odise warfare is suited only to asymmetrical fighting, and in open battle the federation is badly outmatched. The Kavi shaman <a href="Tezmer" class="internal-link">Tezmer</a> has urged the Odise to stop, but the fighting continues.</p>
<p>The war has also made the Odise a source of slaves for the <a href="Narzoi Slavetrade" class="internal-link">Narzoi Slavetrade</a>. Captured warriors and increasingly ordinary members of the federation's larger tribes are enslaved by the colonists, particularly around the fighting at Fort Triq, and those who fall to Sertovi and Othiri masters are the worst treated of all.</p>
