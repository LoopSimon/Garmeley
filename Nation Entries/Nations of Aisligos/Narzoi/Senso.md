It is made up of four tribes, the Senso, Kange, Opacho and Drin, and its lands lie around the <a href="Ruins of Imala" class="internal-link">Ruins of Imala</a> and the Ghostlands. Of the three federations it lies furthest from the settled coasts of the Old World.</p> <p>The Senso were not always its dominant people. The Laso, a warlike tribe of western Peadyr related to them, destroyed the <a href="Aldergrounds" class="internal-link">Aldergrounds</a> of Imala in 379PA, and also attacked the Senso tribe itself. The Senso destroyed them in turn and essentially absorbed what remained, making the Senso the dominant tribe around Lake Senso long before the federation took shape. From 497PA t<a href="Thun" class="internal-link">Thuni</a> Narzoi of <a href="Bethun" class="internal-link">Bethun</a> began crossing the Heroes Grave Sea to the southern coast, and their contact with the Senso brought trade and some knowledge of seafaring, though not iron.</p> <p>The Senso are very wary of the Ghostlands, which they believe to be where evildoers wander after death, and they sometimes exile criminals into them, where the <a href="Triarog" class="internal-link">Triarog</a> hunt in great number, which amounts to a death sentence. They follow <a href="Chianism" class="internal-link">Chianism</a>, though their pilgrims rarely make their way to the <a href="Finger of Life" class="internal-link">Finger of Life</a>, and they are by far the most magically inclined of the three federations, the shamans of the Kange being able to weave magical powers through the power of blood. Like the Odise they are ruled by a council of the tribes, the Ulechiam, headed by a single figure known as the Ul.</p>
