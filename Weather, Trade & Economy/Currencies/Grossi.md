[[Rean]]
%%
<p>The Reani Grossi was a local Currency first minted by the monarchs of Rean, later adopted by the Lords of Hainach and Grevenna as well. In modern times, similar to its neighbours in Reanport, the Grossi is almost never used and largely a placeholder in significant domestic exchanges akin to a mark of trust, while almost all commercial activities are conducted with the Gryphon.</p>
