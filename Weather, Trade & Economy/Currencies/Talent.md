<p>The Sultans Talent, usually simply known as the Talent, is the official currency of the <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae Sultanate</a> and its dominions. It has been adopted by various realms in contact or economically dependant on the Sultanate, and it is one of the most important international currencies in the world.</p>  <p>The Talent trades at roughly one to two against the <a href="Urien Gryphon" class="internal-link">Urien Gryphon</a>, the other very popular and strong international currency, regulated by the <a href="Vrieman Bank of Uhl" class="internal-link">Vrieman Bank of Uhl</a>, so that one Talent is worth roughly two Gold Gryphons. The sums involved can be large: the bounty on the pirate captain <a href="Karlaz the Razor" class="internal-link">Karlaz the Razor</a> stands at 25000 Talents, roughly 50000 Gold Gryphons.</p>  <p>The Talent comes in three forms. The brass Dimtalent is worth roughly a twelfth of a Talent, the golden Fulltalent is the Talent proper, and the Liztalent, which gleams like a polished mirror, is worth around a hundred Talents. In Gold Gryphons that makes a Dimtalent about a sixth of one and a Liztalent about two hundred. Every coin shows a sea serpent, the heraldic symbol of the Sultanate, on one side and the face of the reigning Sultan, currently <a href="Fasmer II" class="internal-link">Fasmer II</a>, on the other.</p>  <p>The Talent is overseen and minted by the Asafi of the Treasury, the office that also manages the accumulated wealth of the Sultanate and maintains the financial instruments through which its commercial dominance is exercised.</p>  <p>In the Sultanate itself the Talent is the accepted currency, and the Gryphon is not common there, although the Vrieman Bank keeps an office in the capital, <a href="Jiravid" class="internal-link">Jiravid</a>. Both circulate in <a href="Hainach" class="internal-link">Hainach</a>, the subject princedom on the Sultanate's western border, and at the <a href="Mesovo Border" class="internal-link">Mesovo Border</a> an exchange building in the town of Gelreas lets visitors trade gryphons for talents.</p>
