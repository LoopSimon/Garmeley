<p>The Sater was an ancient coin used in Palmunia and later the Empire of Rakh, as the Empire did not enforce using its own currencies on its various tributaries the Sater was largely only used in the central Lameidus, Palmunia and Rakh. The dominance of the Empire however led to many other countries adopting their own "regional Sater" and societies like the urien and cevoli embracing minting of currency and and use of coinage in general</p>
