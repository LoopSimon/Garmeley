[[Garmeley]]
%%
<p>The Garmsmark, sometimes called the Silvermark or the Garmish Mark, has for centuries been the minted coin of Garmeley, the powerful realm in southern Ur. During the years following the Treaty of Uhl, it as become  less important and the Gryphon is starting to be the more popular currency in popular international ports such as Thoulmouth, although the Mark is still the regulated Currency of the Realm.</p>
