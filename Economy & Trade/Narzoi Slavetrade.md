<p>The Narzoi Slavetrade is the capture, sale and bondage of the <a href="Narzoi" class="internal-link">Narzoi</a> of Peadyr and the islands around it by the settlers of the <a href="The New World" class="internal-link">new world</a>, a practice that grew up in the shadow of the colonies and has grown with them. This article concerns itself with slavery in relation to the colonists from <a href="Inara" class="internal-link">Inara</a>. It should be noted that the <a href="Hiqae" class="internal-link">Hiqae</a> of <a href="Lazatec" class="internal-link">Lazatec</a> have for centuries held the Narzoi of <a href="Bulug" class="internal-link">Bulug</a> in a far more extreme bondage on the other side of the new world, altering them through breeding and biomancy into servants of their own design. Compared to that, the trade on Peadyr is young and still comparatively small, but it is no longer a rumour, and it is growing.</p>
<p>The trade began with the <a href="Vudi" class="internal-link">Vudi</a>. After the founding of <a href="Alinanora" class="internal-link">Alinanora</a> in 900PA the fisherfolk of the Dilin delta were kidnapped and put to work for the settlement, while its leadership took care never to be seen attacking them openly. As the colonies' hunger for labour grew, the raiders reached further. The smaller tribes scattered across the islands off the coast of Peadyr have been taken, among them the <a href="Thun" class="internal-link">Thuni</a> of <a href="Bethun" class="internal-link">Bethun</a>, and since the war between <a href="Almersport" class="internal-link">Almersport</a> and the <a href="Odise" class="internal-link">Odise</a> began, captured warriors and increasingly ordinary members of the federation's larger tribes are enslaved as well, particularly around the fighting at Kalbia and Fort Triq.</p>
<p>The greatest buyers are the Cevoli. They want hard labour and service, in the colonies and in their households at home, and the harbours of Alinanora and <a href="Livoa" class="internal-link">Livoa</a> are where most of the captives are brought and sold on. The Cevoli consider themselves a more noble and progressive people than their rivals and refuse to call the Narzoi beasts, for the Narzoi are far too close to men for that, but they have found other words. The <a href="Gethricism" class="internal-link">Gethric</a> Turpadori and Martellans call their slaves Wards of the Candle, and explain that by taking them into service they are saving their cursed souls and bringing them under the warding light of the faith.</p>
<p>The Sertovi and Othiri, who mostly follow <a href="Kalbinism" class="internal-link">Kalbinism</a>, trouble themselves with no such explanations. They are the stronger and more dominant peoples of the colonies, and to them it is simply natural that the Narzoi should serve. Narzoi held by Sertovi and Othiri masters are by far the worst treated of all.</p>

<p>The Spurshey of <a href="Runsby" class="internal-link">Runsby</a> come to the trade with a different need. Slavery is nothing new to them, for debt slaves and convicts have long laboured in their mines, and what they want are hands to dig <a href="Brystone" class="internal-link">brystone</a>, a volatile mineral that is dangerous to mine under any circumstances. The risk is greatest on the Dark Islands, where the curse that followed the fall of <a href="Spursley" class="internal-link">Spursley</a> has taken root, and few free Spurshey are willing to take it. In their colony of <a href="Dalesire" class="internal-link">Dalesire</a> Narzoi slaves already form a part of the workforce, and a small number have been shipped back to the core territories of Runsby, which is where the Spurshey mean the trade to lead in the end.</p>
<p>For every power that takes part the use is much the same. Whatever words are used to justify it, the great majority of Narzoi slaves never leave the new world, but are put to work in the colonies themselves, building and clearing, farming and serving, and the colonies that use them have profited greatly from it. Only a minority are shipped across the Tirean Ocean to the Cevoli homelands. One of the first such shipments, aboard a heavily guarded Cevoli vessel, was taken half a day out of Alinanora by the pirate <a href="Kylian Juz" class="internal-link">Kylian Juz</a> in 926PA, a loss that struck at the profiteers of the trade, the Pastelliora among them.</p>
<p>The ports that answer to no great power are caught in between. Neither <a href="Vaggerport" class="internal-link">Vaggerport</a> nor <a href="Ulbreek" class="internal-link">Ulbreek</a> was built for the trade, and both will provision and repair any ship that pays, slaver or not. Vaggerport, a haven of pirates and outlaws with no love for rulers of any kind, is not known to host any sale of captives. Ulbreek, the free port of the Meadi lord Marrin Andil, who serves any and all, is said to hold slave auctions openly.</p>
<p>The Weadi and Meadi have not taken part so far. <a href="Faarhovn" class="internal-link">Faarhovn</a> and <a href="Dueren" class="internal-link">Dueren</a>, where <a href="Trinicism" class="internal-link">Trinicism</a> is dominant, have kept clear of the trade, and the Trinicist chapters on both sides of the ocean disapprove of it, though with the two colonies uninvolved there is little they can do. Faarhovn in particular has every reason to keep its name among the Narzoi, for its accord with the <a href="Kavi" class="internal-link">Kavi</a> makes it the only port permitted to trade on the mainland. Yet within the <a href="Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel" class="internal-link">G.U.V.</a> itself a faction argues that this accord costs the Union a labour force that others are taking for themselves, and wants the good relations with the Kavi abandoned. It is a minority for now but growing stronger, and as the Union has never acted for any reason but financial calculation, it is not clear which way it will turn.</p>
<p>The Narzoi who know of the trade hate it, but many of the peoples of the interior, the Senso among them, are in little contact with the coast and know of it only by rumour. The settlers have meanwhile found the old prejudices that some tribes hold against others easy to exploit, and the trade has begun to divide the Narzoi as well as to bind them.</p>
