<p><a href="The New World" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a>, a continent across the North Tirean Ocean, was first discovered by skori sailors from <a href="Vignasoya" class="internal-link">Vignasoya</a> in the 7th century PA, although early settlements were quickly abandoned and the larger scale exploration and cross Tirean colonisation would only begin in the mid to late 9th century. In truth ilrakhani discoverers were not the first to find the islands of Aisligos, it was  the <a href="Hiqae" class="internal-link">Hiqae</a> of <a href="Pagia" class="internal-link">Pagia</a>, travelling in their so called Eternal Arks, who in 561PA landed on <a href="Bulug" class="internal-link">Bulug</a> over 100 years before ilrakhani found these lands in 688PA. Ilrakhani colonisation and in general relation to the greater New world mostly refers to the large island of a <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a> and its <a href="The Colonization of Peadyr" class="internal-link">Colonization.</a></p>
<p>The idea of riches in a "new world to the west" and soon after trade and opportunities with strange people from a strange land, quickly gave hope and interest to many adventuring spirits and enterprising merchants but more importantly woke the eye of not only powerful trading conglomerates but also the dominant rulers of western Ilrakhan. Many Kings and Queens to the far west of Ilrakhan had long been cut off from the incredibly lucrative nirydian and south beheran trade routes that all arrived in the stormy sea through elvish ports and saw a path west as a way to break the mercantile hegemony of the sultanate over eastern trade. The discovery of Aisligos and plans to settle and colonise it grew even more important during the "great turn west" of Meadi politics, that saw the ideas of a Meadi Empire of the west influencing and controlling the lands of not only Meadland but potentially also Othirdon grew more and more popular. The original Skori Seafairers of Vignasoya saw their interests quickly taken over and their means outspend, by the vastly richer realms of meadrath, Blijgrat, Lissvalley, the Cevoli powers and a multitude of Sertovi princes. </p>

<p>While Skori sailors, led by Lars Gerjodi, were the first to succesfully cross the north tirean ocean, they only made it to the group of mountainous islands now known as the Gerjodis or Gerjodi Islands. The first to set foot on Peadyr was the Ravlander explorer <a href="Balan Reyd" class="internal-link">Balan Reyd</a>, who in 785PA landed on its north-eastern shores and founded Camp Reyd, only for disease to kill him and many of his men within a week. The survivors carried home a partial map, but the true size of Peadyr remained unknown until the Weadi seafarer <a href="Jurim Eykszoon" class="internal-link">Jurim Eykszoon</a> charted much of its coast and the isles to its north in 834PA. He never landed, forbidden to do so by his sponsor, the mage <a href="Teriador" class="internal-link">Teriador</a>, who feared a plague. It was the Martellan expedition of <a href="Marino Viamali" class="internal-link">Marino Viamali</a>, inspired by Eykszoon's voyage, that in 840PA made the first sustained contact with the people of Peadyr: the Narzoi of the Kavi Federation. The Sertovi and Othiri still credit Reyd with the discovery of Peadyr, while the Cevoli credit Viamali, and the Weadi, along with the Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel founded in 861PA with the new world in mind, take particular pride in Eykszoon.</p>
