<p>The Period of the Bleeding Heron is the age of strife that followed the fall of the Empire of <a href="Naigan" class="internal-link">Naigan</a> in 771PA, when the realms of <a href="Tal" class="internal-link">Tal</a> and the Purple Sea were effectively fully splintered once again. It is named for the heron, the bird of the imperial house, which was carved above the gate of the Imperial Palace at Kiamar and was stained with blood in the sack. It lasted until the Peace of Chezin in 849PA.</p>
<h2>The Breaking of the Empire</h2>
<p><a href="Piri Cao" class="internal-link">Piri Cao</a>, the Hungering Wave, forced the last Emperor, <a href="Belen Yun" class="internal-link">Belen Yun</a>, to abdicate, and ruled as a warlord of the Sea lands for around eleven years, until he was murdered in 782PA, likely by one of his concubines on the order of one of his captains. Belen Yun, after proclaiming his abdication with his imperial seal, was sealed in his throne room, and he and his whole court died within it. The death of Piri Cao did not restore the Empire. The warlords whom the Emperors had raised as peacekeepers each held what they could, and over the following decades the lands of the Purple Sea settled into the states of today.</p>
<h2>The Pact of Water</h2>
<p>In the south the wars were won by the Pact of Water, an alliance between the warlord <a href="Molopgar I" class="internal-link">Molopgar I</a> of <a href="Soren Vin" class="internal-link">Soren Vin</a> and the earlier rulers of <a href="Byamon" class="internal-link">Byamon</a>. The Pact was the victor of the southern conflicts of the Period, and Molopgar later founded the Kingdom of Soren Vin and became its King. He died in 858PA.</p>
<h2>The Peace of Chezin</h2>
<p>The Period is largely declared ended by the Peace of Chezin in 849PA, in which the then warring countries of <a href="Koshtir" class="internal-link">Koshtir</a>, <a href="Chezin" class="internal-link">Chezin</a> and <a href="Rikarma" class="internal-link">Rikarma</a> made peace with their enemy, the great <a href="Hanshi" class="internal-link">Hanshi</a>. The war had been fought largely over the Ariho, the stretch of land north of Bangoy and south of Chezin, to the right of the swamplands. The royal army of Hanshi was beaten there and thrown into the sea, and the decision for peace was made. Chezin, which kept control of the Ariho, in return gave up all its claims to any island east of Chezin Island itself. Byamon and Soren Vin took no part in that conflict. Allies in the Pact of Water, they are rivals today.</p>
