<p>The Era of Thunders is the age of splintered rule in <a href="Tal" class="internal-link">Tal</a> that followed the fall of the Muyra Dynasty in 229BA and ended in 294PA, when the <a href="Golden Empire" class="internal-link">Golden Empire</a> defeated the army of the warlord Tien Jijichan. It is named for the monsoons that break over the Purple Sea, but also for the great instability and uncertainty of rule after the time of the powerful <a href="Dynasties of Tal" class="internal-link">dynasties</a>.</p>
<h2>A Splintered Tal</h2>
<p>For more than five centuries no house ruled Tal as a whole. Warlords and small kings held their lands sometimes for only a few years, and many of them ruled at the same time, so that the mainland and the islands of the Purple Sea alike lay in pieces. The lands of the old dynasties were fought over without ever being united again.</p>
<h2>Rin and the Rising Faith</h2>
<p>In the 2nd century PA <a href="Thandar" class="internal-link">Ung Thandar</a> set out the Circle of Thandar and founded the Arts of Change, in the middle of the age. One realm would stand above the rest in the southern lands, the Kingdom of Rin, a relatively strong power of the Era of Thunders. In its last years the faith of <a href="Sochor" class="internal-link">Sochor</a> was born: Sochor was born as a man in 253PA and ascended in 275PA, and in 281PA the <a href="Ashcult" class="internal-link">Ashcult</a> recognised Chusfun as his reincarnation. In 283PA <a href="Laizin" class="internal-link">Laizin the Strong</a>, King of the Rin, converted to the Ashcult and founded the Golden Empire, whose armies ended the Era of Thunders in 294PA.</p>
