<p>The Interregnum of the Runeblades is the name given in <a href="Baeling" class="internal-link">Baeling</a> to the period between the fall of the <a href="Baeling Empire" class="internal-link">Baeling Empire</a> in 590PA and the founding of the Kingdom of Baeling by Sivdil the Tall in 684PA. For almost a century no single ruler held Melmers Bay, and its towns were ruled by petty lords, by the warders the Empire had left behind and by the captains of mercenary companies, the most famous of which, the Runeblade Mercenary Company, gave the period its name.</p>

<h2>Background</h2>
<p>The direct line of Valund Baeling died out with the last Emperor, Erik II, in 590PA. The Empire had no institution that could outlive its ruling house, and its lands broke apart at once. The Skori of <a href="Vignasoya" class="internal-link">Vignasoya</a> had already won back their coast, the warders of <a href="Weadland" class="internal-link">Weadland</a> and the other overseas lands made themselves lords in their own right or were driven out, and on Trykkerog itself the great lords of the Empire turned on one another.</p>

<h2>Melmers Bay without a King</h2>
<p>The heartland of the old Empire suffered most. Alsebyn, Sallesand, Rillberg, Kastock and the other towns of the bay each fell under the rule of whoever could hold them, and many changed hands several times in a generation. Several lords claimed the inheritance of the House of Baeling, but none could make the claim good beyond his own walls. In the east, House Udry of Juelrup made itself master of the Malksbay and in 617PA founded the Kingdom of <a href="Juelfluk" class="internal-link">Juelfluk</a>, which kept out of the quarrels of the bay. In the west, the jarls of <a href="Rensköldsvik" class="internal-link">Rensköldsvik</a> ruled as independent lords, and across the Torn Sea the city of <a href="Jallbreg" class="internal-link">Jallbreg</a> governed itself as it had before the Baeling came.</p>

<h2>The Companies</h2>
<p>The wars of the lords of the bay were fought largely by hired companies, bands of warriors and sailors who sold their service to the highest bidder and who often became the masters of the towns that hired them. They were made up of the sons of ruined families, former imperial soldiers, Skori from the western fjords and adventurers from all around the Torn Sea. The Runeblade Mercenary Company, whose warriors carried blades engraved with runes of victory and warding, was the strongest of them by the second half of the 7th century. Under its captain Sivdil the Tall it took Alsebyn and Sallesand and brought most of the western bay under its control.</p>
<p>The fall of the Empire also brought back the old Bykmåli practice of raiding. Ships from Melmers Bay and the western fjords once more harried the coasts of Weadland and the southern Torn Sea, as they had before the Empire, and the raids did not cease until the new Kingdom of Baeling turned the strength of the bay to wars of its own.</p>

<h2>End</h2>
<p>In 684PA Sivdil the Tall claimed to be a direct descendant of Valund, took the name of Baeling and proclaimed the Kingdom of Baeling, with himself as its king and Alsebyn as its capital. His claim to the whole inheritance of the Empire ended the Interregnum on Melmers Bay and began the long <a href="Wars of Baeling Succession" class="internal-link">Wars of Baeling Succession</a> with Juelfluk. The kings of Baeling have since presented the Interregnum as an age of chaos from which Sivdil rescued the bay, while their enemies remember that the house that claims the Empire's name was founded by a captain of hired swords.</p>
