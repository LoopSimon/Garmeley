<p>The Green Plague is a mysterious sickness and disease that has begun its spread around the northern Zarsroad from the grachy city of <a href="Kharnov" class="internal-link">Kharnov</a>, and by now reached other major settlements such as the city of <a href="Izhevk" class="internal-link">Izhevk</a> upon the Jaerflow River. Both major cities have been quarantined and it is unclear to the public what may have caused this disease, but it seems to be spreading quickly.</p>
<p>The Disease has taken its name from the green-blue ish skin tone that seems to befall its victims, as of now various stages to the plague are unknown but apparently various members of the public have carried within themselves for days before visibly succumbing to the sickness, making it impossible to tell how far the Green Plague may have already spread.</p>
