<p>Vuron, also known as the Truth of the third Path, is an ancient and essentially dead religious belief system centred around the deity Vuron, who was originally merely a part of the greater <a href="Enzal Pantheon" class="internal-link">Enzal Pantheon</a>. Vuron Worship is almost exclusively a Drow belief system and rarely practiced in the modern day, with the wider Enzal Pantheon on its own already being practically extinct. In elder days it was the primary religion of the aptly named Undercity Empire of <a href="Tur Vuron" class="internal-link">Tur Vuron</a> and the splintered Undercities afterwards, before the Goblins of the 2nd Mitimgar brought vengeance and ruin upon the Vuron worshipping Drow.</p>
