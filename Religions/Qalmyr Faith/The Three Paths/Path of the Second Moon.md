<p>The Path of the Second Moon is the branch of <a href="Qalmyr" class="internal-link">Qalmyr</a> that worships <a href="Zolra" class="internal-link">Zolra</a>, the ruinous Luna Minor. Its followers call themselves the Zolraii. Every other name, among them the "Path of the Damned", is a name given by outsiders, above all by other Qari, to whom the Second Path is absolute heresy. It was founded in 788PA by the prophet <a href="Elabar" class="internal-link">Elabar</a>, and its only state is the kingdom of <a href="Abaleq" class="internal-link">Abaleq</a> on the Fazaar Isles.</p>  
<h2>Belief</h2>  
<p>The Zolraii hold that the struggle between Qastor and Zolra is over, and that Zolra won it. Since the <a href="Ikval" class="internal-link">Ikval</a> Qastor has been pure but stripped of the power of creation, and the world as it is, with its hunger, desire, ambition and storm, belongs to Zolra. She proved it when the elves took Qa and Qastor did nothing. To the Zolraii the First Path is a life of refusal, a people endlessly mourning what its god could not save, and its moral order a denial of what the living world is made of. They do not fear Zolra's whispers. They embrace them, and with them every tendency and emotion that Qastor cast away: appetite, greed, lust, pride, ambition, rage and joy. Hedonism is a central part of their practice, and where the First Path measures a life by what it resists, the Zolraii measure it by what it takes and enjoys.</p>  
<p>Though Zolra is also the Great Serpent of the depths and the storm, the Zolraii worship her above all as the Night. They worship the night more than the sea, and their feasts, songs and rites are held after dark, beneath the small moon that circles the pale one.</p>

<p>Their feasts keep time by her round, which takes a little over three days as seen from the world, so that the Zolraii count three days, three, three, three and four, and five rounds fill sixteen days. Twenty-five rounds, eighty days or two months of the Common Calendar, make a great round, which closes with a feast greater than the common ones. Once a year they also keep the night when the stars fall, which they hold to be the stars that chose Zolra and were taken into the sea, burning brightest in falling. It is a night of feasting and not of mourning (see <a href="Qinari" class="internal-link">Qinari</a>).</p>  
<h2>The Night of Zolrai</h2>  
<p>The Path began in 788PA on the Fazaar Isles, then part of the Qahori Federation. A man named Glarraq rejected his old name, took the name Elabar and announced his truth to his followers. That same night he and his followers hosted a great feast that overtook the isles in debauchery, and in the rush of the crowds various people were killed, most of them by accident. The Zolraii remember it as the Night of Zolrai, the night their faith was born, and keep its anniversary as their greatest feast. The other Qari remember it as a massacre.</p>  
<p>Elabar became the first Sandimar, and by the end of the century the Fazaar chiefdoms had broken with the Federation and entrenched themselves as a sedentary, proper orcish kingdom in the ancient style of Qa, as the Qarzi had done on Il-Haena.</p>  
<h2>Reputation</h2>  
<p>The orthodox tell of blood rites, serpent altars and killings in the dark, and the tales are repeated in harbours from Paqor to Sallesand, mostly by people who have never seen the Fazaar Isles. In truth the Zolraii are no bloodthirsty cult. They are sailors, farmers, merchants and courtiers who love their feasts and their night, and are no more murderous than any other Qari. "Damned" is the word of those who fear what they are not allowed to be.</p>  
<h2>The Zolraii and the Other Qari</h2>  
<p>Most devout Qari will neither trade with the followers of the Second Path nor set foot on the Fazaar Isles. Traders who wish to reach Abaleq dock instead at Yaran, the city of the Qahori Federation on the isle of Eheribi, which tolerates the traffic for the coin it brings, and sail on from there. The Kamerzin hate the Zolraii as bitterly as any. The people of Abaleq trade with the <a href="Krestasi" class="internal-link">Krestasi</a> of the Risen Reef, a contact the other Qari keep to a minimum, since they number the great beasts of the deep among the fallen Qinari. The <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae Sultanate</a>, which has never cared about the philosophies of the Qari and trades with anyone, deals with Abaleq like any other buyer.</p>
