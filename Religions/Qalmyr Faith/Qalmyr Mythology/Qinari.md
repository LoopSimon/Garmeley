<p>The Qinari are the star-beings of <a href="Qalmyr" class="internal-link">Qalmyr</a>, divine beings who share some of the traits of <a href="Qastor" class="internal-link">Qastor</a> and who appear in the heavens as stars and constellations. The Qari do not worship them as gods, for Qastor stands above them as the supreme being of the universe, but they are his own, and they shine because he does.</p>
<p>The same stars are worshipped as gods by the Cyclopi (see <a href="Starworship" class="internal-link">Starworship</a>). The Qari know them under their own names.</p>
<h2>The Qinari of the Sky</h2>
<h3>Teriq</h3>
<p>Teriq is <a href="The Sun" class="internal-link">the Sun</a>, the nearest and greatest of the Qinari.</p>
<h3>Qisvahl</h3>
<p>Qisvahl is <a href="Malham" class="internal-link">Malham</a>, the Child of Destiny, the North Star that never moves from its place. It is associated with hope and innocence.</p>
<h3>Hasteriq</h3>
<p>Hasteriq, "the One Before the Sun", is <a href="Sarhen" class="internal-link">Sarhen</a>. It walks ahead of Teriq, and its return at dawn marks the end of Zolra's winter.</p>
<h3>Mahzir</h3>
<p>Mahzir, "the Warden", is <a href="Tholmir" class="internal-link">Tholmir</a>. It stays low over the southern water and watches over the fallen Qinari of the depths.</p>
<h3>Zolqir</h3>
<p>Zolqir, "Zolra's Ear", is <a href="Karrod" class="internal-link">Karrod</a>. It is the Qinari that heard her whisper and did not fall, and it flares red when she whispers.</p>
<h3>Kasqari</h3>
<p>Kasqari, "the Sworn Two", is <a href="Dyrrim" class="internal-link">Dyrrim</a>, two Qinari born of one flame who refused to part.</p>
<h2>The Fallen</h2>
<p>At the <a href="Ikval" class="internal-link">Ikval</a>, when Qastor cast away his destructive tendency and it became <a href="Zolra" class="internal-link">Zolra</a>, she whispered to the Qinari. Most turned away. Some listened and fell into the sea, and they are the great beasts of the depths, the first victims of the split and the greatest insult to Qastor's greatness. Zolqir heard the whisper and did not fall, but was marked by it, and Mahzir stays low over the water to watch the ones who did.</p>
<h2>The Qinari and the Three Paths</h2>
<p>The First Path honours the Qinari as Qastor's witnesses, and the shrine-keepers and sailors keep their lore. The Zolraii of the <a href="Path of the Second Moon" class="internal-link">Path of the Second Moon</a> hold Zolqir holy as the Qinari closest to Zolra. The <a href="Path of the Third Moon" class="internal-link">Path of the Third Moon</a> holds that Qisvahl is the hope that is handed to you, and Mahzir the hope that you make yourself.</p>
