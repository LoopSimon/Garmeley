<p>Otso, King of both Bearkind and <a href="Giant" class="internal-link">Giantkin</a>. This mighty figure is often linked to physical might and endurance, although his true strength is said to lay in the bond that comes from community and the power of a focused leader or shepherd. Strength through community is often the theologically correct title that should be given to this Aeld deity. His devotees are mainly spread across the wild and untamed regions of <a href="Nostroya" class="internal-link">Nostroya</a> and <a href="Trykkerog" class="internal-link">Trykkerog</a> - seldom in bigger towns but more commonly found smaller communities held together by leaders rather then rulers. Although there is a darker side to Otso. Many of his followers often look past the messages in his stories pointing towards these cores messages about what one should fight for and why. Those who seek power to overwhelm a foe often do not concern themselves with The King of Giants more communal views but, instead they seek the strength and curiosity of of The Great Bear. This sort of power granted by the second-mentioned half of his discipline often leads to a wild, uncontrolled might that many lose themselves to if not kept in check.</p>
<p>Otso wouldn't ever lie, just as natural as how a desperate animal wouldn't fight if cornered.</p>
