<p>The Viktorai is a practice created and observed by the <a href="Pharang" class="internal-link">Pharang</a> of Aisligos that was developed in times of the Masters, the <a href="Heire" class="internal-link">Heire</a>, and served as a way for many Pharang to break the covenant that forced them into absolute servitude.</p>
<p>Originating from the teachings of Vikt, this tradition emphasizes social harmony, rigid conduct, generational piety, and respect for hierarchy. It served as the dominant political and cultural philosophy during the so called "Time of the raised Wing" and continues to influence Pharang values regarding family, education, and governance.</p>
