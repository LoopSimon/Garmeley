<p>The people of Palmunia worshipped the En-Yagal, also known as the Shapers, in great temples along their River cities. They are most famous for shaping massive figures of clay and stone inviting their masters to join them and take up form within the world itself, some of these relics have survived the millenia since Great Palmunis fell, some were brought as trophies to the triumphant Rakh, and others have been swallowed by the Desert, the Wind and time itself. The En-Yagal are associated with chaos and the pure expression of time and energy, sometimes associated with the  label of <a href="Protean" class="internal-link">"Protean"</a> by modern day scholars and archeologists.</p>
