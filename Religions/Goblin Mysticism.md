<p>Goblins believe in icons. Goblin Mysticism is the faith of the <a href="Goblinoid" class="internal-link">goblins</a> of the <a href="Underlands" class="internal-link">Underlands</a> and the primary religion of the Hive Cities of <a href="Pintrixaz" class="internal-link">Pintrixaz</a> and <a href="Mitimgar" class="internal-link">Mitimgar</a>. It is practised in many traditions that differ from city to city, but all of them share one conviction: that an image carries truths a word cannot.</p> <h2>Image as Language</h2> <p>To a faithful goblin an icon is not a likeness of the holy but a way of speaking it. What is shown is understood more deeply than what is told, and a truth that no sentence can hold may still be held by a picture. The faith values artistry, the hands that make the icons, and memory, the mind that keeps them, and it values vision itself, the act of seeing through which a truth arrives. Goblins see without trouble in the pitch dark of the Underlands, and many count this among the reasons sight is sacred to them.</p> <h2>The Blind</h2> <p>Where truth is shown rather than told, those who cannot see are shut out from the chief way of learning it, and several traditions treat the blind as a tragic caste. In Mitimgar they stand at the heart of its storyteller cultures: unable to behold the icons, they keep them in memory and speak them aloud, so that what was shown once can be told to all. In Pintrixaz the same sorrow produced an elaborate magical script, whose marks project images into the mind of the reader, so that even the blind may behold an icon. Not every tradition is so gentle. In Raggavil blindness is held to be a curse that only death can lift, and the afflicted are killed.</p> <h2>Other Faiths</h2> <p>Goblin Mysticism is not the only faith of the goblins. <a href="Fogorix" class="internal-link">Fogorix</a> follows the <a href="Cult of the Darksun" class="internal-link">Cult of the Darksun</a> instead, and the goblins' distant cousins, the <a href="Hobgoblin" class="internal-link">hobgoblins</a>, follow <a href="Kaladi Mysticism" class="internal-link">Kaladi Mysticism</a>, the veneration of deified heroes.</p>
