<p>The Great Pantheon of the Undercities and later Empire of Tur-Enzal is a regional evolution of the ancient drow practice of Onosiam. It knows six Deities and worships the same number, as well as the symbol of the Hexagon. One of the Enzali Gods, Vunru, later on would cause a massive schism and civil war leading to the Vuruni exodus and become the sole deity of the drow of Tur Vurun.</p>
