<p>The Onosiam is a broad generalization of various beliefs and cults of the ancient drow of the behier underlands that emerged with the Anazirt High Culture, was practiced during the days of the Kingdom of Murajk and even beyond the conquest of the Empire of Tur Ajk by the High elven Alparid Dynasty. In the modern day the Onosiam is essentially extinct and has been fully replaced with the rites of elven Paragonworship, although one could argue that various primary facets of the latter are indeed pastiches of old Onosiam rites.</p>
