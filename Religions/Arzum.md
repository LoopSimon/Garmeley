<p>Arzum is the divine mother in the many ancient traditions of the <a href="Incador" class="internal-link">Incador Civlization</a> of ancient Geol. Any practice of the Faith is outlawed in the lands now dominated by <a href="Doggerism" class="internal-link">the Teachings of Alfred Dogger</a>, and Arzum has largely fallen into obscurity on the mainland, although minor cults are still said to exist especially on the isolated Vansworth Islands.</p>
