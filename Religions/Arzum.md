<p>Arzum is the divine mother in the many ancient traditions of the <a href="Voktator" class="internal-link">Voktator Civlization</a> in ancient Geol. Any practice of the Faith is outlawed in the lands now dominated by <a href="Doggerism" class="internal-link">the Teachings of Alfred Dogger</a>, and Arzum has largely fallen into obscurity on the mainland, although minor cults are still said to exist especially on the isolated Vansworth Islands.</p>
<p>The Vokti Gnomes worshipped the divine female and Arzumi traditions are fundamentally tied to Vokti culture, although as Dandry and Geoli humans embraced the teachings of Arzum and the faith subsumed other local nature worship and spirit cults, combining their elements in the divine female image of Arzum, her image changed regionally and she was less and less depicted as a Gnome like figure herself.</p>
