<p>The Rayadan is a cyclical view of the world and part of the Vas Ira, practiced predominantly by the Sunborn Goblinoids, Durenni Bihra and Durenni Human of Durennis and the Pepper Islands. Within the Rayadan, the god Raym is the supreme being of the cosmos and the final inheritor of all collective experiences, to live and die in any way is to bring together the final Avatar of Raym that will be all that ever was. The Rayadan as a coherent religious practice was formed during the 2nd century BA by the followers and teachings of one of the Great Yova of the Vas Ira, the japhali scholar <a href="Ajaran Mor" class="internal-link">Ajaran Mor</a>.</p>
<p>The  1st - 2nd century PA work known as the Pragala is the great mythos of Gasch, the first Incarnation of Raym, and often considered one of the greatest surviving works of literature in the world.</p>
