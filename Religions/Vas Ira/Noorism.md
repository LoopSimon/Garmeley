<p>Noorism is an umbrella-term for a number of Vas Ira traditions which worship the divine Dragon Noor-Rah as the supreme being of the cosmos. The followers of Noorism are called Noorites and practice their faith almost exclusively within the valleys of the Limsul Peaks, the only place where Noor-Rah has ever been seen. Noorism originally likely developed as an amalgamation of various shamanistic and nature worshiping traditions within the Limsul mountains and became a religiously unified movement in the 2nd century PA.</p>  
<p>In the modern day, most Noorites live within the High Temple of <a href="Rha-Gis" class="internal-link">Rha-Gis</a> and the surrounding valleys of the Limsul Peaks. Various Gorati tribes have adopted Noorist traditions as well, especially the mountainous Opmani of <a href="Ruginë" class="internal-link">Ruginë</a>, although the northern Gorati Kingdoms have outlawed any practice of the Vas Ira. Beyond the mountains Noor-Rah is honoured as one of the Divine Beasts within the <a href="Karaluk" class="internal-link">Karaluk</a> of the coastal Bihra, but not as the supreme being of the cosmos.</p>
