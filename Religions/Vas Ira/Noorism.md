<p>Noorism is an umbrella-term for a number of Vas Ira traditions which worship the divine Dragon Noor-Rah as the supreme being of the cosmos. The followers of Noorism are called Noorites and predominantly practice their faith within the Azu Lowlands and the Mountains of Limsul. Noorism originally likely developed as an amalgamation of various shamanistic and nature worshiping traditions within the Limsul mountains and became a religiously unified movement in the 2nd century PA, rapidly spreading across various communities in south-western Nirydia. 
<p>In the modern day, most Noorists can be found within the Kareli Hierarchy or the Divine Temples of Rha Gis. Various Gorati tribes have adopted Noorist traditions as well, especially the mountainous Opmani of Ruginë, although the northern Gorati Kingdoms have outlawed any practice of the Vas Ira.</p>
