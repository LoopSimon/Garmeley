<p>Iratism is one of the great schools of the <a href="Vas Ira" class="internal-link">Vas Ira</a> , that is very popular in the far east of Nirydia. At its core it divides existence into the Heavens and the Earth and sees the journey of a lifeform as an eternal struggle to become free from the grip of the <a href="Konoii" class="internal-link">Konoii</a> , the Lords of Earth and join the <a href="Kava" class="internal-link">Kava</a>, the Lords of Heaven.</p>
<p>The Kava Lords of Heaven are organised in a personified pantheon:</p>
<h5>Maduga</h5>

One of the three principle powers within Iratism, Maduga is the great Protector of the Universe and a warring deity associated with Strength and Dance, she is the wife of Inpat and usually represented as action.

<h5>Inpat</h5>

Inpat, Husband to Maduga, is one of the three principle powers within Iratism representing consideration. Inpat is associated with Law and Hierarchies, both on a societal context of worldly rule but also in the greater question of the Tahan as an inward guiding principle.

<h5>Irat</h5>

Irat is the greatest of the three principle powers within Iratism, the child of Inpat & Maduga. He is widely revered, more specifically, as the remover of obstacles and bringer of good luck as well as the patron of arts and sciences, and the god of logic, intellect, and wisdom.
