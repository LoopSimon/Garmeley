The Kaladi are divine spirits that were once mortal and deified after death, usually heroes and leaders of the [[Hobgoblin]] people. They are honoured in festivals and associated with certain types of rhythms and sounds. Often Kaladi are associated with geographic locations, like caves, mountains, rivers or cities notably not as personifications of nature, like within the Faith of the [[Kindly Lords]] for examples, but as patrons and patronesses.

Popular Kaladi include:

1. Barxu, the kaladi associated with sexuality, fertiliy, water and love. It is said Barxu was a wise shaman in her life that led her people to the Green Grotto, named after her.
    
2. Tirbo the kaladi associated with order, divine rule, power and might. He is the patron Kaladi of Tirbo Lake, the large Lake in western urland, and the foremost Kaladi of the Tirbi of [[Ak-Saray]]. See [[Tirbo]].
    
3. Jibey of the Blue Fire the kaladi associated with the past and the dead. Jibey was a great leader and mage of the old cave clans, from the time before the escape from the caves, and was once the most worshipped of all Kaladi. Since the escape his role has changed: power and might have passed to Tirbo, and Jibey has become the Kaladi of the past and the dead.
