<p>Gianor, in old Safiri literally "Who Crafts the Light" is the Yari associated with Truth, Illumination and the Sun and one of the "Great Three" <a href="Yari" class="internal-link">Yari</a> that make up the <a href="Pravazik Pantheon" class="internal-link">Pantheon of Pravazik</a>.</p>
<p>Gianor resides within their hicui atop of Mount Gi near the city of Tzikun and has only communicated twice with mortals in the last millenia, both times as part of the Pantheon. Gianor has created a relatively normal amount of Ginru that are known as the Sar-Gi, and serve as his agents and champions. He takes a minimal role within the politics and ongoings of the Empire and is secondary to Arishi within the Cult itself, alongside Pra.</p>
