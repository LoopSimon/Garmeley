<p>Oleithec is a <a href="Yari" class="internal-link">Yari</a> worshipped within the Cult of the Hallowed and the patron of <a href="Yar-Oleia" class="internal-link">Yar-Oleia</a> where his hicui lies. His domain is usually associated with perfection, evolution and enlightenment and he is one of the "Great Enemies" of the <a href="Pravazik Pantheon" class="internal-link">Imperial Cult</a> of  the <a href="Pravazik" class="internal-link">Pravazik Empire</a>.</p>
<p>Oleitheic, known as "The Hallowed One", is known as the Creator of the Kash and sometimes also of the mysterious <a href="Wispmer" class="internal-link">Wispmer</a>, although the latter appear on many other parts of Inara as well their great City, <a href="Tasch" class="internal-link">Tasch</a>, practices the Cult of the Hallowed as well albeit in different rites, making Oleithec one of the few Yari religiously worshiped to a greater extend by a non Safir population.</p>
