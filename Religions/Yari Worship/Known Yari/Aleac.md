<p>Aleac, the Deep Hunter, is a <a href="Yari" class="internal-link">Yari</a> of Behera whose hicui lies within an unknown location, if it does even exist at all. He is one of the most mysterious Yari and resides within the Smaragd Sea. Aleac is often viewed as the patron deity of all Zoroid outside of the Empires <a href="Pravazik Pantheon" class="internal-link">Imperial Cult</a>, but aside from their association to water, little speaks to that fact in practice. </p>
<p>He sometimes associated with other sentient creatures of the seas, such as the <a href="Merfolk" class="internal-link">Merfolk</a> and <a href="Krestasi" class="internal-link">Krestasi</a> but never in the context of a creator, deity or even benevolent patron. Aleac is known and feared even amongst other Yari and has battled with <a href="Nictor" class="internal-link">Nictor</a> countless times in myth and legend.</p>
