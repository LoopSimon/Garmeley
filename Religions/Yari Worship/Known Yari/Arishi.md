<p>Arishi, the Queen of the Moon, is part of the "Great Three" <a href="Yari" class="internal-link">Yari</a> that make up the <a href="Pravazik Pantheon" class="internal-link">Pantheon of Pravazik</a>, and even amongst them the most superior being. She represents the Moons and is believed to be even more powerful than the Sun as the Moons were visible during Night and Day. Arishi is believed to control the weather and growth of crops and in elder days before the Imperial Cult,  devotees sacrifice animals, birds and sentient creatures to the Moon goddess, in extreme cases even their own children whom they believed would become deified.</p>
<p>As the prime deity of the Imperial Cult, she is the patron goddess of the Empire and her hicui is at the at centre of it, within the Capital of Yar-Arishi. Her Ginru are known as the "Children of the Moon" and serve her with absolute loyalty. She is one of the most active Yari and has communicated with the mortal members of the Cult numerous times over the last few centuries, even going so far as to issue the infamous "Decree of the Moon" that demanded the systemic eradication of the pale skinned Kash and their killing upon birth.</p>
