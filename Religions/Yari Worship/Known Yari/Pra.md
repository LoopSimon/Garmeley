<p>Pra, in old Safiri literally "Voice" is the Yari associated with Civilization, Culture and Art and one of the "Great Three" <a href="Yari" class="internal-link">Yari</a> that make up the <a href="Pravazik Pantheon" class="internal-link">Pantheon of Pravazik</a>.</p>
<p>While still one of the major deities within the Empire, the modern Imperial Cult is actually the offspring of the Great Rite of Pra, a religious institution that was largely subsumed during the rise of the Empire. Many relics of the Rite were incorporated and the very name of the Empire, Pravazik, is the living memory of his old influence. He is associated with the male and holds court once every five years in ritual, making him one of if not the most present Yari in mortal life. The Great Games of Pra are hosted in his honours with the champion being blessed as his Ginru, although often a singer, poet or sculptor is granted victory here rather than a martial champion.</p>
