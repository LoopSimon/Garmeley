<p>Atul, often known as "the Creator" or "the Eldest", is a mythological <a href="Yari" class="internal-link">Yari</a> whos existence is widely debated amongst Safiri scholars, he is supposed to have been the first of the Yari that emerged into the world of existence and the one to bless the primitive <a href="Astafir" class="internal-link">Astafir</a> with the gifts that would eventually lead to their evolution into the modern day <a href="Safir" class="internal-link">Safir</a>. In the many tales that describe him and his deeds, he is usually portrayed as a fatherly figure with male elements, almost always dying or fading away as a result for his actions and often put above or beyond even the other Yari in age and power.</p>
