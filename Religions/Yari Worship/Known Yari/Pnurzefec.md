<p>Pnurfezec was a <a href="Yari" class="internal-link">Yari</a> worshipped within the Cult of the Mist and the old Council of the Trees of central Kaito, where his hicui was located. He was infamously killed by <a href="Hilcarax" class="internal-link">Hilcarax of Koshtir</a> and his power absorbed in the latters ascension as a Yarimanji.</p>
<p>Little is known about the dead Yari of the Mists aside from his role in Hilcaraxs ascension to godhood, but his personal cult essentially fell apart with the exception of the few Ginru he created that survived his fall and are still out there, lone creatures of the Mists.</p>
