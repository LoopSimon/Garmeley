<p>Nictor is a <a href="Yari" class="internal-link">Yari</a> feared and worshipped by the Safiri of <a href="Behera" class="internal-link">Behera</a>, unlike many of his fellow Yari he does not seek to influence and control mortals or rule over others, nor does he hold court or plant his roots within a hicui, instead he is a wandering Yari, unknown to civilisation or institutions. His domain is usually associated with Strength, Fight and Freedom and he is most famously known for defeating the great Yari <a href="Zalo" class="internal-link">Zalo</a> who ruled over the <a href="Zalo Dominion" class="internal-link">Great Zalo Dominion</a> after which Nictor was only known as the Slayer. It is said that he is amongst the more powerful Yari of Behera and is one fo the few of his kind to have absorbed and retained the power of another Yari. Nictor has only taken a single Ginru, known as "The Barac" whom he set out on a journey to amass power and skill enough to one day challenge him and defeat him.</p>
