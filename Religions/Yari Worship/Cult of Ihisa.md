<p>The Cult of Ihisa is the oldest of all the personal Yari Cults within behera, and located around the northern jungles of Vinjira, where some souls have found entrance to the hicui of Ihisa herself. The City of Yokosch found rescue during the great independence wars with the Empire of Pravazik within the army of Ihisa, when the Paladins of the Oracle emerged and supported the 1st Tazamosh of Yokosch in exchange for their promise, to forever pledge the city in fealty to Ihisa.</p>
