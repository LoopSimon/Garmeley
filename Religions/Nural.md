<p>The Nural, also known as the "divine root" or the "divine tree" is a cult worshipping the so called grand Nural Tree that sustains all being, and is the origin of all life. There are a great amount of ancient belief systems and cults of worships from all across the known world, including many time periods of regions that were not then, and in many cases are not still, ever in contact with each other.</p>
<p>Scholars have compared rites and imagery from within the core tenets of the Nural with practices of central ilrakhani Spiritworship and the mogyri worship of the greater will, known as Tsurigism.</p>
