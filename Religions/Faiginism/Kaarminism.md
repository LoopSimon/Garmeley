<p>Within the greater context of the <a href="Faigin" class="internal-link">Faigin Faith</a> , but explicitly removed from the Faigin Church itself exists the practice of Kaarminism, named after the prophet Kaarmina, a legendary figure in nirydian history whose divinity is controversial and sometimes fundamentally refuted by members of the Church itself. Kaarmina was a Hobgoblin Shaman that saw the Light of the Mother in a Dream and went on to convert a great number of her people north of the Motherlands. The faith is considerably less organized and without a clear clergy but follows almost all essential tenents as the Faigin Church does too, it is predominantly practiced by the Human and Hobgoblins of Gellions Folly, <a href="Sebera" class="internal-link">Sebera</a> and <a href="Lugosch" class="internal-link">Lugosch</a> and has even become a minority group within the jueli settler communities of <a href="Nilborg" class="internal-link">Nilborg</a>.</p>
