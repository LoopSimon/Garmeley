<p>The Faigin Faith believes in the benevolent Mother Faigain who is believed to have created all life as she bled out during the birth of the world.
Her two children Gespar and Bilia, often referred to as the Star Children, wander the land and seas of the globe, protecting their mothers grand work.
The religion is organized within the Faigin Church.</p>
<p>The exact origins of the faith are unknown, but it likely springs forth from Muevi Shamanism and Fertility Cults from the Motherlands of central Nirydia, first mentioned of organized practice of the "Mothercult" and later documented worshippers of a deity named Faigin itself go back to the 5th century PA. The Faigin Church itself was ordained in modern day Girdineux by the then Realm of <a href="Levelix" class="internal-link">Levelix</a> and is currently practiced within <a href="Girdineux" class="internal-link">Girdineux</a>, <a href="Herat" class="internal-link">Herat</a>, <a href="Balerey" class="internal-link">Balerey</a> and <a href="Labella" class="internal-link">Labella</a> as the true Faith of the world and divine mandate of the Leveli peoples.</p>
<p>Notably the Faith puts emphasize on natural understanding and respect of the Flora and Fauna, with druidism and an elevated role of various animals such as the Fox and the Horse being popular and sometimes at the centre stage of Faigin rites. The Church itself is led by the Holy Grace, an exclusively female position acting as a spiritual leader for all her children, a visual representation of the mother on earth and the voice with which she speaks to all.</p>
The Followers of the prophet <a href="Kaarminism" class="internal-link">Kaarminism</a> are tolerated but often looked down upon within religious gatherings and the greater Faigin Church.
