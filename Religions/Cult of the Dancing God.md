<p>The Cult of the Dancing God is a faith popular amongst the Geipra of Aisligos, and revolves around the worship of a celestial body orbiting our world, wwhom the Geipra call the Dancing God. The god is <a href="Luna Minor" class="internal-link">Luna Minor</a>, the small bright moon that loops about the great moon through the night, which the Geipra take for a body circling the world itself.</p>
