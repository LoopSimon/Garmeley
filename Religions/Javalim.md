<p>The Javalim, sometimes referred as the Scriptures of Javal, is a system of religious beliefs almost entirely concentrated around the areas around the eponymous Mount Javal in the <a href="Roxeria" class="internal-link">Principality of Roxeria</a>. In the Vale of the One mind, at the top of Mount Javal, one can lay their eyes upon the "Pillar of the One" a shining spire in the centre of a lake from which life itself seems to burst, taking the shape of various animals. The Pillar of the One is protected by the Guardians of the Pillar and seen as the remnants of the central deific figure within the Javalim, the One. </p>
<p>It is common belief within the Javalim that the One was once a living and waking mind that birthed all life from itself, able to perceive the future and sacrificing itself in order for the multitudes of existence to come to be. The Pillar is commonly thought to be the centre of the entire cosmos of existence and the place where the One made his great sacrifice, while the so called "Divine Tears", found on the isle of Tazil, are usually believed to be his only remaining physical aspect on this world and the only way one could hear the wisdom of the One. The tears are well guarded by the Monks of <a href="Cinderstone Temple" class="internal-link">Cinderstone Temple</a>.</p>
<p>The modern day traditions of the Javalim are founded on the words of the Prophet Fenhir, the elder brother to the legendary revolutionary Rox that led the Revolution of Rox against the <a href="Azuvke" class="internal-link">Khatrym of Azuvke</a> during the 2nd half of the 6th century. However Fenhirs prophecies were embraced and build upon a much older tradition of shamanism and druidism centred around the One, who had already protected and worshipped the Tears and the Pillar from kheri settlers and bihra raiders for thousands of years.</p>
