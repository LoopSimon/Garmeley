<p>Chenism was the old faith of the <a href="Yppori" class="internal-link">Yppori</a>, older than the faith of <a href="Sochor" class="internal-link">Sochor</a>, and its god was Chenza, the Sunrider.</p> <h2>The Sunrider</h2> <p>The Yppori told that Chenza rode through the sky on the Parakar, a great elephant, and that he carried the <a href="The Sun" class="internal-link">Sun</a> with him on his ride, so that the day was his journey from one horizon to the other. Elephants still roam the jungles of <a href="Tal" class="internal-link">Tal</a>, and in the old faith they were held sacred for the sake of the Parakar.</p> <h2>The Herald of Sochor</h2> <p>As the faith of Sochor spread, its priests took Chenza into their own myth, and the old faith was largely absorbed by it. In the tradition of the Ashcult the old image of the Sunrider is changed only slightly: he is the father of Sochor, Doi-tor and Mezhan, and he was always a herald who foretold the coming of Sochor. He carried the Sun across the sky as a way to show his son the way in life, and the Parakar remains his mount.</p> <p>Some hold that the Golden Elephant on the banner of <a href="Soren Vin" class="internal-link">Soren Vin</a> is the Parakar of the old faith.</p>
