<p>Piril, or Pirilism, sometimes known as Pisil, is a philosophical and zumi tradition indigenous to <a href="Eressa" class="internal-link">Eressa</a>, emphasizing the values of harmony with oneself and the primordial essence of the world. The Piril includes a variety of schools, with some like the <a href="Ophaqim" class="internal-link">Ophaqim</a> or the <a href="Ixak" class="internal-link">Ixak</a> being considered "primary" schools.</p>
<p>A common goal of Pirili practice is the cultivation and evolution of oneself and ones soul, often accompanied by understanding through meditation and internal balance to achieve harmony and connection with the individual one the one hand and the primordial essence on the other.</p>
<p>The early Piril developed from a group of lineages of meditative sages and spiritual communities following them in ancient Eressa, ancient Pirilism the veneration of spirits and greater Gods was not uncommon, but has since been relegated to viewing them as a stagnated expression of the primordial essence, powerful foes to be overcome that have failed to become one with the primordial essence. </p>
