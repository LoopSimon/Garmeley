<p>Chianism is a religious faith prominent on the island of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a> in the continental sphere of <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a>, predominantly followed by the indigenous communities of the <a href="Odise" class="internal-link">Odise</a>, <a href="Kavi" class="internal-link">Kavi</a> and <a href="Senso" class="internal-link">Senso</a> Tribes. </p>
<p>Chianism revolves around nature worship, emphasizing the worship and prayer to Water, Land and Forest as well as the veneration of local guardian deities. At the core of the belief system is the Chian, the sacred grove of the narzoi communities, where the guardian deities known as the Har Chian resides and receives sacrificial offerings. </p>
