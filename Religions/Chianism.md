<p>Chianism is a religious faith prominent on the island of <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a> in the continental sphere of <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a>, predominantly followed by the indigenous communities of the <a href="Odise" class="internal-link">Odise</a>, <a href="Kavi" class="internal-link">Kavi</a> and <a href="Senso" class="internal-link">Senso</a> Federations. </p>
<p>Chianism revolves around nature worship, emphasizing the worship and prayer to Water, Land and Forest as well as the veneration of local guardian deities. At the core of the belief system is the Chian, the sacred grove of the narzoi communities, where the guardian deities known as the Har Chian resides and receives sacrificial offerings. </p>
<p>Chianism understands the living energy of the world as passing through a great cycle, in which it is returned to nature at the end of life, a cycle embodied in the <a href="Finger of Life" class="internal-link">Finger of Life</a>, a great wonder sitting above the Green Valley in the east of Peadyr.</p>
