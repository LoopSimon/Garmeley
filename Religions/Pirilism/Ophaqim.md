<p>The Ophaqim is the largest of the primary schools within the greater <a href="Piril" class="internal-link">Piril</a> religious belief system and practiced most notably within the <a href="Mazol" class="internal-link">Empire of Mazol</a> by the Iter Zumi of central Eressa.</p>
<p>Within the Ophaqim, a central aspect is that of the end and the beginning, the thought of an immortal existence and a quest for ever marching life, often associated with <a href="Ixak" class="internal-link">the Ixak</a>, is heretical and antithetical to the Ophaqim who instead seek to attain an nontangible state known as the Opha, "naturalness with the world". To achieve this they seek to free themselves from their ego and selfish desires, appreciating the flow of fate and the simplicity of the world. To find ones true Opha, ones determined place, is held in the highest regard within this School, the idea of trying to be something one is not, or overthinking ones place in the world is dangerous and tragic to the followers of the Ophaqim.</p>
