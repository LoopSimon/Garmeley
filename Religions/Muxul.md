<div class="wiki-infobox" style="overflow: hidden; display: flex; flex-direction: column;"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Muxul.png" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;"> <small><i>The Muxul, the first structure of Ott</i></small> </div> <p>Muxul, also called Muxulism or the Great Introspection, is the faith of the <a href="Gnome" class="internal-link">gnomes</a> of <a href="Ott" class="internal-link">Ott</a> and takes its name from the temple at its heart. There is no true god within the halls of the Muxul, but there is evidence of something that may as well have been one, and from this the gnomes took a very unique and odd view: a religion that is almost more a devotion to reality and the practice of understanding and self reflection than to any supernatural being. It mixes rationality, an acknowledgement of the supernatural and a reverence for natural patterns and mathematics.</p>  <p>A core tenet of the faith is that all can be explained, nothing ever will be. The strive for knowledge is always virtuous, but one must realize that any answer will only ever open more questions.</p>  <p>The Muxul was the first structure constructed in the crater of Al-Kortop, long before the cities of Ott were formed, and it lies on the far side of the crater from the first Ott. Even today space is afforded around it where no other construction is held.</p> <h2>The Schools</h2> <p>The halls of the Muxul hold schools of various principles. One became Prosum, the marriage of craft and magic, better known as the gnomish study of <a href="Artificery" class="internal-link">artificery</a>. Another is the Akkir, the study of music and mathematics, which led to the birth of the tonal Akosophosers, who are able to bend music to alter reality akin to magic.</p>  <p>Muxulism is practiced by the gnomes of Ott and by the Ottian diaspora, the gnomes who do not live only in Ott, mainly in <a href="Cevolia" class="internal-link">Cevolia</a>, <a href="Meadland" class="internal-link">Meadland</a> and <a href="Ur" class="internal-link">Ur</a>, as well as in other dwarven holds in <a href="Ilrakhan" class="internal-link">Ilrakhan</a>. Many other gnomes and dwarves of the Underlands worship the <a href="Terem-Zal" class="internal-link">Terem-Zal</a>.</p>
