<p>Ingele is, especially in Trykkerog, associated with various cults of Death, embracing omen and soothsayers who determine the time and manner of ones demise and do their best to bring it about, this ranges from children or elderly being expected to take their own lifes within isolated communities, to even martial Cults in eastern Trykkerog sacrificing souls to Ingele to bargain for their own life.</p>
