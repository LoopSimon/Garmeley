<p>The old Mother, Swarmkeeper Rynbrekka is one of the more important deities within the Aeld mythos and associated with Fertility and War. She is often seen in associated with Bees and Flowers. A Stone of worship to Rynbrekka is build in the stretches of the Grachwoods near the village of Varny.</p>
