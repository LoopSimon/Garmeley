<p>Bykmali Paganism, as most contemporary scholars describe it, is the collective name for the various cults worshipped by the people of <a href="Trykkerog" class="internal-link">Trykkerog</a> before the majority of the island's population converted to the Gethric faith. It was once the primary faith of the island but is now a minority religion, practised to a large part only in the western parts of the island in <a href="Vignasoya" class="internal-link">Vignasoya</a>.</p>
<p>In many respects it is hard to distinguish from the traditions of the <a href="Aeldcult" class="internal-link">Aeldcult</a>, and in large part it worships the same greater spirits and gods.</p>
<p>The greatest tale of the Bykmali faith is the <a href="Fjorisjom Saga" class="internal-link">Fjorisjom Saga</a>, the tale of four legendary heroes who venture through the lands of the north to hunt two giants and save the princess <a href="Gudrun" class="internal-link">Gudrun</a>.</p>
