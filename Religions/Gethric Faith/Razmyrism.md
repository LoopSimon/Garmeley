<p>The Razmyrist Sect is a vaguely heretical group of worshippers that view the Soul Shepard <a href="Razmyr" class="internal-link">Razmyr</a> as the one true Prophet and question <a href="Gethric" class="internal-link">Gethrics</a> role within the Incursion, they are a controversial group that largely stays isolated and has been repeatedly persecuted. The Razmyrists are found in parts of <a href="Grevenna" class="internal-link">Grevenna</a> and <a href="Dalniz" class="internal-link">Dalniz</a>, the gethric communities of <a href="Il Haena" class="internal-link">Il-Haena</a> as well as parts of <a href="Hainach" class="internal-link">Hainach</a> and southern <a href="Ur" class="internal-link">Ur</a>, although usually only in very isolated and small communities and sects. The most well known institution of this ideology are the stalwart monks of the <a href="Broken Fingers" class="internal-link">Broken Fingers</a>.</p>
