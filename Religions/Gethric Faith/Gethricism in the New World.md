<p>Some of the earliest ships following the adventurers and explorers that brought back news from the new land to the west, the continental Sphere of <a href="The New World" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a>, were trinic & gethric missionaries, seeking to claim the souls of the west for the prophet, to spread their word and not lose ground to heathens or even worse, rival heretics.</p>

<p>Due to the dominance of the cevoli, meadi and weadi sailors in this endeavour and the proximity of trinic centres of faith and trinic monarchs and rulers eager to claim this new world, Aisligos and its islands, predominantly <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a>, have become a stronghold for Trinicism , at least those few ports beginning to prosper. With many lost and hopeful sertovi, othiri and locni settlers setting out for the new world however, kalbinist communities remain strong as well, and the new school of Diarism, seeking a marriage between kalbinist Creator Worship and Gethric belief, has become more and more popular here too.</p>
<p>The faiths of the settlers have also shaped how the new world speaks of the <a href="Narzoi Slavetrade" class="internal-link">Narzoi Slavetrade</a>. Among the Gethric Turpadori and Martellans of the Cevoli colonies the Narzoi taken into service are called Wards of the Candle, and their masters claim to be saving their cursed souls, while the Kalbinist Sertovi and Othiri trouble themselves with no such explanations. Many Trinicist chapters on both sides of the ocean disapprove of the trade, but with Faarhovn and Dueren, where Trinicism is dominant, keeping clear of it, there is little they can do.</p>
