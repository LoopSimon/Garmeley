<p>Some of the earliest ships following the adventurers and explorers that brought back news from the new land to the west, the continental Sphere of Aisligos, were trinic & gethric missionaries, seeking to claim the souls of the west for the prophet, to spread their word and not lose ground to heathens or even worse, rival heretics.</p>

<p>Due to the dominance of the cevoli, meadi and weadi sailors in this endeavour and the proximity of trinic centres of faith and trinic monarchs and rulers eager to claim this new world, Aisligos and its islands, predominantly Peadyr, have become a stronghold for Trinicism , at least those few ports beginning to prosper. With many lost and hopeful sertovi, othiri and locni settlers setting out for the new world however, kalbinist communities remain strong as well, and the new school of Diarism, seeking a marriage between kalbinist Creator Worship and Gethric belief, has become more and more popular here too.</p>
