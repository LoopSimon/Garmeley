<p>Gethricism in the New World describes the spread of the <a href="Gethricism" class="internal-link">Gethric</a> faith and its denominations to the continental sphere of <a href="The New World" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a> and its islands. Gethric and Trinic missionaries were among the earliest ships to follow the explorers who brought back news of the new land to the west, and the colonies of the old world powers, above all those on <a href="Peadyr" class="internal-link">Peadyr</a>, have since become a stronghold of <a href="Trinicism" class="internal-link">Trinicism</a>. <a href="Kalbinism" class="internal-link">Kalbinist</a> communities remain strong among the settlers as well, and the young movement of <a href="Diarism" class="internal-link">Diarism</a> is growing in popularity.</p>
<h2>History</h2>
<p>The oldest human settlement in Aisligos, <a href="Grynborg" class="internal-link">Grynborg</a> on the Gerjodi Islands, was founded by Skori and Olbellinge sailors in 719PA, decades before the <a href="The Great Schism" class="internal-link">Great Schism</a>, and has followed High Gethricism ever since. Wider interest in the new world only came with the wave of discoveries and tales that began to spread through Othirdon, Sertova, Cevolia and Meadland in 835PA, and with the crossing of <a href="Marino Viamali" class="internal-link">Marino Viamali</a> in 840PA, as described in <a href="The Discovery of Aisligos" class="internal-link">The Discovery of Aisligos</a>.</p>
<p>Some of the earliest ships following the adventurers and explorers were those of Trinic and Gethric missionaries, who sought to claim the souls of the west for the Prophet, to spread his word and not to lose ground to heathens or, even worse, to rival heretics.</p>
<h2>Denominations</h2>
<p>Cevoli, Meadi and Weadi sailors dominated the venture, and the Trinic centres of faith and the Trinic monarchs and rulers eager to claim the new world lay close at hand. As a result Aisligos and its islands, predominantly Peadyr, have become a stronghold of Trinicism, at least in those few ports that have begun to prosper. Trinicism is the primary faith of the Martellan colony of <a href="Livoa" class="internal-link">Livoa</a>, the Turpadori crown colony of <a href="Alinanora" class="internal-link">Alinanora</a>, <a href="Faarhovn" class="internal-link">Faarhovn</a> of the <a href="Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel" class="internal-link">Geldfield Unie van Vrijhandel</a> and the Meadi colony of <a href="Dueren" class="internal-link">Dueren</a>. The colonies have a variety of small churches and abbeys, but the only seat of importance is in Livoa, where the Church of Saint <a href="Luca" class="internal-link">Luca</a> is commonly considered the centre of Trinicism in the new world.</p>
<p>Many lost and hopeful Sertovi, Othiri and Locni settlers have set out for the new world as well, and Kalbinist communities remain strong. Traditional Kalbinism is one of the primary faiths of the Ravlander colony of <a href="New Fryd" class="internal-link">New Fryd</a>, and <a href="Nyrkalbinism" class="internal-link">Nyrkalbinism</a> one of those of the free port of <a href="Vaggerport" class="internal-link">Vaggerport</a>. The new school of Diarism, which seeks a marriage between Kalbinist Creator worship and Gethric belief, is less regulated here than in the old world and has become more and more popular. It is the primary faith of the free port of <a href="Ulbreek" class="internal-link">Ulbreek</a> and is widely followed in New Fryd and Vaggerport.</p>
<p>The Gethric denominations remain largely the faiths of the settlements. The peoples of the interior of Peadyr hold to <a href="Chianism" class="internal-link">Chianism</a>, a faith of Water, Land and Forest, and the colony of <a href="Dalesire" class="internal-link">Dalesire</a> follows <a href="Doggerism" class="internal-link">Doggerism</a>.</p>
<h2>The Narzoi</h2>
<p>The missionaries have won some converts among the <a href="Narzoi" class="internal-link">Narzoi</a>. To many of them conversion is presented as a way out of a worse fate, or as the only thing left to hold on to when everything else has been taken from them. There are also small communities of Narzoi who have taken up the Gethric faith more willingly and live as free converts.</p>
<p>The faiths of the settlers have also shaped how the new world speaks of the <a href="Narzoi Slavetrade" class="internal-link">Narzoi Slavetrade</a>. Among the Gethric Turpadori and Martellans of the Cevoli colonies the Narzoi taken into service are called Wards of the Candle, and their masters claim to be saving their cursed souls, while the Kalbinist Sertovi and Othiri trouble themselves with no such explanations. In Ulbreek, where slave auctions are held openly, the Diarist settlers read the teachings of <a href="Perrin Diar" class="internal-link">Perrin Diar</a> on equality and worldly wealth as meant for themselves and their betters, and not for slaves. Many Trinicist chapters on both sides of the ocean disapprove of the trade, but as Faarhovn and Dueren, where Trinicism is dominant, keep clear of it, there is little they can do.</p>
