<p>Saint Lyandra of Fairriver represents Strength and Repose within the Gethric Canon, her favoured Weapon is the Greatsword. Lyandra was born a vihri human in 621PA and died at the age of 69 in 690PA, he was canonised at the Conclave of 691PA alongside <a href="Markur" class="internal-link">Saint Markur</a> under High-Bisop Taromir III, her remains were burned and her ashes are kept within a well guarded shrine in the mountains near Fairriver. </p>
<p>A Knight of the Silver Wheel, Lyandra was an advocate for Peace during the Garmeley-Vihri War of 648PA, also known as the Eight Years War, and founded the Abbeytown of Fairriver as a haven for those lost and displaced after times of War. She is worshipped and loved by Commonfolk and is one of the most popular Saint within all denominations. Lyandra is said to have possessed multiple artefacts that are now deemed holy relics in her life, chief among them the Shawl of Respite, the White Blade and the Cuirass of Mercy.</p>
<p>A key aspect Lyandra represents is Strength in mercy and salvation, and the power of staying ones hand when in a position of power. Her Fury is righteous because she offers repose and forgiveness when apt, drawing power from the innocent of past and future that she has sworn to protect. Lyandra welcomes men and women from all paths of life if they are willing to use their might for good, but she does not fret from burning away those who would cast the weak into darkness.</p>

<p>In the 660s, by then an already established hero and well renowned warrior for good in her time, Lyandra set out to hunt down the vile Necromancer Morfeld, who had terrorised towns and settlements in northern Ur. During her hunt, she met and briefly conversed with Dorian, later known as Saint Dorian the Kind, on whom the imposing living legend of Lyandra is said to have left a lasting impression. Lyandra captured Morfeld at Igsbury, a small village he had turned into a macabre theatre of the unliving, and refused to kill him there and then, following an excruciating battle. Instead bringing him to Uhl and overseeing his trial and later execution by fire in 666PA. Morfeld would later rise again in 787PA as a vengeful spirit, taking possession of the young sorceress Falia Venwick, who had acquired an artefact created by him and fell under the control of his shadow, yet clinging to the realm of the living. </p>
