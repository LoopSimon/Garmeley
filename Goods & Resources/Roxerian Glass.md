<p>Roxerian Glass is the glass made in the cities of <a href="Roxeria" class="internal-link">Roxeria</a>, whose reputation, like that of the glass of Jideux in far <a href="Cevolia" class="internal-link">Cevolia</a>, stands on its own. The highest of its Mirrormasters are almost never without a commission, and many elves of the <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae Sultanate</a> pay exorbitant sums for their work. The finest Roxerian mirrors are held to be more than glass, and the shields of polished mirror made for the Spellbreaker Guard of the <a href="Kherovan Inquisition" class="internal-link">Kherovan Inquisition</a> are among the most famous pieces of equipment in the south.</p>  
<h2>The Glasshouses</h2>  
<p>The glasshouses of Roxeria stand in most of its cities, the greatest of them in Chalgis, the commercial heart of the realm. They burn vast amounts of wood and peat, and the potash for the glass comes from the ash of the realm's forests. The best timber grows in the margins of the <a href="Blodrise Dark" class="internal-link">Blodrise Dark</a>, and some merchants still send crews of woodcutters and slaves into the forest for it, although the practice is unpopular and many who go in do not return. In the temples of the <a href="Javalim" class="internal-link">Javalim</a> the glass of the realm is set in great coloured windows, and the old Kheri reverence for lasting craft has found in glass its Roxerian form.</p>  
<h2>The Mirrormasters</h2>  
<p>The Mirrormasters are the highest masters of the glass trade, a closed guild whose secrets are guarded by the crown and the temples alike. A Mirrormaster may not leave the realm without leave, and to teach the craft to a foreigner is a crime, so that foreign buyers can only commission a mirror and wait for it, never hire its maker away. Runaway masters are hunted, and the few who have sold their knowledge abroad are spoken of in Roxeria as traitors to the One. The Mirrormasters themselves hold that their mirrors are blessed by the One, who saw all things, and many Roxanae homes keep a small mirror as a shrine.</p>  
<h2>Spellbreaker Shields</h2>  
<p>For generations the Spellbreaker Guard, the anti-magic force of the Kherovan Inquisition, has carried shields faced with polished Roxerian mirror, which are said to turn aside spells cast against their bearers. That the Inquisition of the <a href="Kethricism" class="internal-link">Kethric</a> faith should depend on the craft of the apostates of the eastern bay is a source of quiet embarrassment in Kher, and the Inquisition holds that the Mirrormasters' art, like the Runebinding of the Kheri dwarves, is a work of craft and conviction rather than of the arcane. The trade has made the Mirrormasters wealthy and given Roxeria a voice in Kherova that its faith alone would never have won it.</p>  
<p>Rumours also tell of mirrors made by the greatest of the Mirrormasters that allow those who look into them to see or speak across great distances, kept in pairs by the rulers and temples who can afford them. None has ever been shown openly, and the Mirrormasters neither confirm nor deny that such mirrors exist.</p>
