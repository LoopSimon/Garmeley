<p>Roxerian Glass is the glass made in the cities of <a href="Roxeria" class="internal-link">Roxeria</a>, whose reputation, like that of the glass of Jideux in far <a href="Cevolia" class="internal-link">Cevolia</a>, stands on its own. The highest of its Mirrormasters are almost never without a commission, and many elves of the <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae Sultanate</a> pay exorbitant sums for their work.</p>
