<p>Ganoan Dye is a dye made from the Ganoan Flower, which blooms only in the underwater harvest fields of Lake Ganoa in southern Nirydia, and is processed in the Parabol Domes of <a href="Majir" class="internal-link">Majir</a>. It is prized above all for its changing colour, which shifts over the course of a single day from a dark green in the morning to lighter oranges and reds around noon, into deep reds and blues in the evening, and finally to an onyx black at night. Folklore often attributes magical qualities to the dye, which is commonly called the "living colour", but fundamentally it is a luxury good of immense value that can only be produced in the Ganoa Valley.</p>  
<p>The making of the dye is dangerous at every stage. The Ganoan Flowers give off a hallucinogenic exhaust that makes their harvest perilous for the divers of the lake, and since the early 820s the processing in the Parabol Domes has released great amounts of toxic smoke that leave the lives of the dome workers short and rough. The Domes also leave behind a waxy residue known as <a href="Para" class="internal-link">Para</a>, which has become one of the most important drugs of the region. Much of the wealth the dye produces has long flowed to the guild elites of <a href="Jink" class="internal-link">Jink</a>, which became one of the causes of the war between the two cities in the 890s.</p>
