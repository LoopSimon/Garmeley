<p>The Deck of the Dragon is used in various games of cards amongst nobility and commoners alike across Ilrakhan and Inara, it is notable for having a core set of cards that is used in almost all games and various unique cards that can only be found in certain regions or as part of niche games.</p>
The Core set of the deck always includes:

The One, Two, Three and Four, Five, Father, Magician, Maiden & Knight of Hearts,
The One, Two, Three and Four, Five, Father, Magician, Maiden & Knight of Flames,
The One, Two, Three and Four,  Five, Father, Magician, Maiden & Knight of Swords.

The King, Queen, Beggar, Jester, Archer & Bard are cards that are not part of one of the colours and usually act as trump cards.

There are a few rare and and rather expensive cards, that can sometimes be played in certain games, giving various advantages in games to admittedly unfair advantages. Many of these stem from an older, turvik tradition of painting small symbols and tales onto pieces of cloth in religious veneration and are likely responsible for the more collection related aspect of the Deck.

Collectors of these rare and sometimes fully unique cards are traded in prices going as high as thousands of gold and some are even said to be infused with ancient magicks, although much of this can be chalked up to fairy tales spread by card players aiming to sneak in a "unique" Card into a game of Ante or Poker.
