<p>The Deck of the Dragon is used in various games of cards amongst nobility and commoners alike across Ilrakhan and Inara, it is notable for having a core set of cards that is used in almost all games and various unique cards that can only be found in certain regions or as part of niche games.</p>
The Core set of the deck always includes:

The One, Two, Three and Four, Five, Father, Magician, Maiden & Knight of Hearts,
The One, Two, Three and Four, Five, Father, Magician, Maiden & Knight of Flames,
The One, Two, Three and Four,  Five, Father, Magician, Maiden & Knight of Swords.

The King, Queen, Beggar, Jester, Archer & Bard are cards that are not part of one of the colours and usually act as trump cards.
