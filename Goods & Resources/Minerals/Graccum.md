<p>Graccum, also known as wealdstone or godsbone, is a silvery-white element, hard and corrosion-resistant, it was named by Voga Birena, a famous grachy alchemist of the 6th century after its alchemical and magical properties were first classified and discovered by her. Graccum had been used by the native people of <a href="Nostroya" class="internal-link">Nostroya</a> for likely over a thousand years prior to her discovery in religious architecture. It is extremly rare to be found and one of the most valuable precious metals. Graccum ores are a mixture of other metals such as iron and the separation of it from iron is a dangerous and usually toxic process conducted in degrees varying in efficiency and safety.</p>
<p>Graccum is desired by alchemists for its unique kynotic effect; when used the object of Infusion during classical <a href="Alchemy" class="internal-link">Kynosis</a>, it loses essentially zero planar energy resulting in an incredibly efficient Kynogma. Graccum based alchemical constructs are extremely coveted and well traded, they can act as a sort of "supercharger" for prisms in <a href="Artificery" class="internal-link">the practice of Prosum</a> and are sometimes said to even ease the access to planar energy for organic casters.</p>
<p>Weapons made of Graccum are rare and considered great treasures, often the basis for advanced magical artefacts and other objects. The Process of accessing Graccum, usually referred to as purifying the element is extremely dangerous and largely still researched with many unknown quantity, it can lead to the material reacting undesirably with oxygen and causing an explosive reaction. Natural purified Graccum is said to sometimes occur when lightning strikes the element or other mysterious environmental circumstances occur, however naturally purified Graccum is so rare it is almost impossible to find. As described by the necromancer <a href="Morfeld" class="internal-link">Morfeld</a> in his considerations on <a href="Brystone" class="internal-link">Brystone</a> and Graccum, Graccum even if purified is often in an unstable state, unable to retain its properties for prolonged times and slowly decaying to dust if not constantly resupplied, he calls this "blackened" or "unstable" Graccum, and further denotes that Graccum is much less reliable in the field of necromancy than the stable Brystone is, seeing its actual use case in the enchantment and manipulation of living and organic matter.</p>
<p>Godsbone, sometimes Wealdstone, are the terms the native pheki of Nostroya refer to the material to, often attributing divinity or spiritual power connecting to the Aeld, the Giants or the souls of their ancestor to, usually associated to the <a href="Aeldcult" class="internal-link">Aeldcult</a> traditions. Godsbone is said to sometimes whisper the voices of those who lived in the lands long ago, various sources have claimed to heard the ore speaking to them or conjuring whispers in the air. Such accounts are often attributed to the overall horrific standards within the deepest and most gruelling mines of the Ore, although one cannot rule anything out within the living lands of Nostroya.</p>
<p>The largest commercial Graccum Mine in the known world is near Solny in southern <a href="Grachy" class="internal-link">Grachy</a>.</p>
