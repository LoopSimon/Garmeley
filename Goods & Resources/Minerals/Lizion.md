<p>Lizion is a dark, glass-like stone found in the shallow caves beneath eastern Behera and nowhere else. It is warm to the touch, glows faintly in the dark, is partly radioactive, and gathers planar energy and gives it back. The bodies of the sphinxes are made of it throughout.</p>

<h2>The Stone</h2>
<p>Lizion is the colour of smoke, shading from violet to black, and it has no grain. Radiation has broken its crystal so thoroughly that it carves like glass in any direction and does not split along planes, which is why it can be worked into forms of great size. Polished, it shows tiny dark rings, each around a speck of radioactive dust, which those who work it call eyes. It is always warm, a few degrees above the cave around it, so that frost does not settle on it, and in total darkness it gives off a faint, cold, pale violet glow. Unlike the glow of <a href="Glintstone" class="internal-link">Glintstone</a>, it does not need to be still.</p>

<h2>Veins and Layers</h2>
<p>Inside the stone run branching veins of pale light, like lightning caught in glass. They brighten when the stone is struck, and in a Conflux they flare, so that a great block of Lizion seems to burn from within (see <a href="Conflux" class="internal-link">Conflux</a>). A mass of Lizion gathers planar energy and gives it up slowly, and a working done beside it is the stronger for it.</p>
<p>Lizion also grows in thin layers over thousands of years, and each layer keeps the planar and radiation level of the age in which it grew, in order, like the rings of a tree. The Turnometer can read them (see <a href="Talnod-Swarn Gradient" class="internal-link">Talnod-Swarn Gradient</a>), and it was by the layers of Lizion that the planar energies of the world were found to have stood raised for ages after the <a href="Nawiunic Event" class="internal-link">Nawiunic Event</a>.</p>

<h2>The Sickness</h2>
<p>The stone holds radon in its cavities and is itself partly radioactive. Humans who work it sicken, with a dry cough, loosening teeth and a wasting that no healer can cure, and few last more than some years at the face. Drow and elves bear it far better, which is why the seams have rested largely in their hands.</p>

<h2>The Seams</h2>
<p>Lizion is found only in the shallow caves beneath eastern Behera, in the region where the magnetic field of the world is weakest (see <a href="Globus" class="internal-link">Globus</a>), and nearly all known seams lie under the lands of the <a href="Elbae" class="internal-link">Elbae Sultanate</a>. The seams are few, and what has been taken from them is mostly carved. The greatest masses of Lizion in the world are the sphinxes themselves, and these, being revered, are never sampled.</p>

<h2>Liz</h2>
<p>The stone takes its name from Liz, whose children, in elven tradition, the sphinxes are (see <a href="Sphinx" class="internal-link">Sphinx</a>). The old tales do not say who or what Liz was.</p>
