<p>Brystone is a unique element native to western Behera that exhibits organic properties and is extremly reactive to planar energy, being popularly used as a conductor of it in various spells and invocations. However its unreliable nature has made it a less popular material in the realm of Prosum, and its most common application actually lies within the creation of Golems. The material has recently become the focal point of the so called "Talnod-Swarn Gradient" or rather its pre-eminent tool: the Turnometer.</p>
<p>The mages of Spursley infamous used an incredibly amount of Brystone concentrated around various Towers in Dandry to project their magic across the land from a single point, this would later be the catalyst for the Fall of Spursley and the Necrocurse affecting modern Day Bilsworth.</p>
