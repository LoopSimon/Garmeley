<p>Zonite or Zondogen, colloquially referred to as Zondust, is a stimulant with psychedelic and addictive properties, it is commonly consumed in crystalline form and stems from the central Nirydian continent where it is an extremely popular drug within the upper echelons of society. It was first discovered as a Drug by the <a href="Kobold" class="internal-link">Kobolds</a> of the Island of Zon, hence the name, as early as the 4th century BA and likely predominantly consumed during religious rites. To day the Zonite Trade is outlawed in most nations and the centre for hundreds of organised criminal institutions. The <a href="Shirin" class="internal-link">shirin</a> are especially prone to Zondust addiction and the trading in the drug is punishable by death in the <a href="Twintail Imperium" class="internal-link">Imperium</a>.</p>
