<p>Para is a hallucinogenic drug that forms as a residue in the Parabol Domes of <a href="Majir" class="internal-link">Majir</a> during the making of <a href="Ganoan Dye" class="internal-link">Ganoan Dye</a>. It is a wax-like substance that hardens within a few seconds of the process into a thin waxy layer. Para is most commonly consumed by letting it dissolve through body heat on the tongue or the skin, but it can also be heated in water or taken rectally. Its effects are extremely strong hallucinations, and the drug is addictive to some extent.</p>  
<p>Para is rumoured to open its users to the spiritual and the arcane, and it is highly coveted among the members of the <a href="Mystics Guild" class="internal-link">Mystics Guild</a>. Alongside <a href="Zondust" class="internal-link">Zondust</a>, which reaches the south from the Gorati lands beyond the Velvet Road, it is one of the two most important drugs of southern Nirydia.</p>
