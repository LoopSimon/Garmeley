<p>The Krili, sometimes mistakenly counted as Merfolk similar to the Mermaids or even towards the Krestasi, are actually evolutionary relatives to the Gnomes. Thousands of years ago they were severely mutated by the Palas-Coral a coral organism growing in the Geoli Sea. The Krili are now able to live and breath underwater and live within the south Geoli Reef and on the Tirean Archipelagos, as well as in smaller communities alongside the south beheri coast. They have developed rudimentary cultures and a form of writing and language, although seem to be restricted to tribal hunter-gatherer societies otherwise. </p>
