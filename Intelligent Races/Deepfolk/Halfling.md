<p>A distant descendent of those dwarves who, tens of thousands of years ago, left the mountainous realms and caverns and settled among the surface lands, the short statured halflings are primarily found in the western parts of the continent within Meadland, Cevolia, Othirdon and Sertova. Usually rising to a humble height of 3-3`6 feet, their ears are slightly pointed but proportionately not much larger than those of a human. Halflings are capable to reproduce with Dwarves, Gnomes, Krili, Svirfneblin and Wilgar as they are all related to the Trog as a common ancestor.</p>
