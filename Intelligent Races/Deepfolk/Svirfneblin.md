<p>The Svirfneblin are a race closely related to the common Gnome, living in the deep caverns of the underlands. Physically, they are small and wiry with rock coloured skin, (grays or browns) and large gray eyes; males are typically bald, while females have thin, stringy gray hair. Their society is organized around mining and gem-cutting, with a strong cultural emphasis on rubies and functional craftsmanship. They tend to have a strict division of labour, where men mine and women manage household and food resources.</p>
<p>While they evolved differently, the Svirfneblin are still close with their Gnomish cousins and dwarven relatives, and can often be found at the deepest levels of dwarven Holds. Interestingly they are the only racial group that was largely tolerated by the Drow during their millenia long eras of domination and still in their surviving undercities and newer realms.</p>
