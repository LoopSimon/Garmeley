<div class="wiki-infobox" style="overflow: hidden; display: flex; flex-direction: column;"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Gnome2.jpg" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;"> <small><i>An almadi male Gnome</i></small> </div> <p>The cousins and distant relatives of <a href="Dwarf" class="internal-link">Dwarves</a>, Gnomefolk dislike the underground and prefer to live under the sun and moon of the world instead. Like the dwarves they descend from the <a href="Trog" class="internal-link">Trog</a>, and the <a href="Svirfneblin" class="internal-link">Svirfneblin</a> of the deep Underlands are their close relatives. As opposed to their dwarven relatives they can barely grow a beard and are even smaller at an average 4ft, and they reach a lifespan of about 150 years. A love of repetition runs through gnomish speech and naming, which is why both cities of Ott are simply called Ott.</p> <h2>Origins</h2> <p>Gnomes are said to hail from the Mountains of Anvokt on the island of Vokt in the Bay of Geol of south-western Behera, where the <a href="Voktator" class="internal-link">Voktator Civilization</a> of the Vokti Gnomes, who worshipped <a href="Arzum" class="internal-link">Arzum</a>, would later rise around its capital Ximsalim-Vokt and then fall apart around 310BA. Three great tribes left the islands around Vokt in the Gnomish Period of Migration. The first, the forgotten tribe or Xan Daor, left about 8000BA and went south across the islands and seas to Sirenna. The second, Xan Ott, left some six thousand years later and went north. The third, Xan Il, went east to the Ilkiztis only a century or two before Vokt itself fell, and its descendants are the Ilkizti Gnomes, found today in <a href="Iz-Almadus" class="internal-link">Iz-Almadus</a>.</p> <h2>The Gnomes of Ott</h2> <p>The northern tribe, Xan Ott, later shortened to Ott, arrived in the Kortopis around 1900 to 1700BA and settled amongst the dwarven and goblin populations of the western Underlands. It lived for a long age amidst the dwarves of <a href="Sulbra-Kol" class="internal-link">Sulbra-Kol</a> and <a href="Ogul-Vosko" class="internal-link">Ogul-Vosko</a> and in 307BA left them behind to raise the two cities of <a href="Ott" class="internal-link">Ott</a>, which came to deal with outsiders as a unified entity in the Opening of Ott in 670PA. Its descendants, the Kortopi Gnomes, speak <a href="Languages of the world" class="internal-link">Ottian Gnomish</a>, the tongue of almost all gnomes of the world, and Old Gnomish Symbolspeak, a non-verbal language still used in gnomish craftsmanship but almost entirely lost outside the scholars of Ott. Outside Ott and its many smaller mountain communities there are almost no larger settlements of just gnomes.</p> <h2>Gnomes Abroad</h2> <p>Many gnomes live as the Ottian diaspora, mainly in <a href="Cevolia" class="internal-link">Cevolia</a>, <a href="Meadland" class="internal-link">Meadland</a> and <a href="Ur" class="internal-link">Ur</a> and in other dwarven holds in <a href="Ilrakhan" class="internal-link">Ilrakhan</a>. Instead of founding settlements of their own they prefer to found families in the communities of elves and humans, often seen in Gnomish Ghettos in the larger metropolises of the world, such as the Gnomish Quarter of <a href="Niedrest" class="internal-link">Niedrest</a>. Among humans they are often counted by the local culture, such as the Urien Gnome or the Sertovi Gnome. Many follow the <a href="Muxul" class="internal-link">Muxul</a>, a devotion to reality, understanding and self reflection born in Ott, while many other gnomes and dwarves of the Underlands worship the <a href="Terem-Zal" class="internal-link">Terem-Zal</a>.</p> <h2>Craft and Learning</h2> <p>It is said that while they are mostly spreading their knowledge through the commercial enterprise of institutions like <a href="The Ottian Syndicate" class="internal-link">The Ottian Syndicate</a>, the Gnomish Theoretical Metaphysicists and Arcanists are the most learned researchers in the realm of magical theory, especially when it comes to applied <a href="Artificery" class="internal-link">Artificery</a>, the study of Maintenance, Modulation and Creation of magical objects and artefacts. <a href="Larkomas" class="internal-link">Larkomas</a> of Ott laid the groundwork of modern planar theory, <a href="Charatov Podlim" class="internal-link">Charatov Podlim</a> of Almadus unveiled the first single core Automaton, and the mortars and rifles of a gnomish mercenary company changed the <a href="Vorellan War" class="internal-link">Vorellan War</a>.</p> <p>This deep understanding of such a useful aspect of the weave of the world, coupled with their politically speaking humble nature, has given the gnomes a great reputation in almost every corner of the world, although <a href="Doggerism" class="internal-link">Doggerism</a> depicts them as lesser and villainous creatures.</p>
