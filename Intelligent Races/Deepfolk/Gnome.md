The Cousins and distant relatives of Dwarves, Gnomefolk dislike the underground and prefer to live under the sun and moon of the world instead.  
As opposed to their [[Dwarf]] relatives, they can barely grow a beard and are even smaller at an average 4ft. While reaching the same lifespan of about 150 years.

Outside of the City of , there are almost no larger settlements of just gnomes, who instead prefer to found families in the communities of elves and humans, often seen in Gnomish Ghettos in the larger Metropolis of the world.

Gnomes are said to hail from the Mountains of Anvokt, located on the island of the same name in the Bay of Geol of south-western Behera, although the migratory period of the Gnome, roughly 9-7 thousand years ago originating at Anvokt, could also simply refer to their spread on the surface, and underlands migrations may have preceded it.

It is said that while they are mostly spreading their knowledge through the commercial enterprise of Institutions like the Ott Syndicate, the Gnomish Theoretical Metaphysicists and Arcanists are the most learned Researchers in the Realm of Magical Theory, especially when it comes to applied Artificery, the study of Maintenance, Manipulation and Creation of Magical Objects and Artefacts.

This deep understanding of such a useful aspect of the weave of the world, coupled with their politically speaking humble nature, has given the Gnomes a great reputation in almost every corner of the world.
