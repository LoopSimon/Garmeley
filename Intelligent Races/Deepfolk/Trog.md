<p>The Trog are a race of primitive humanoid mammals capable of sentient thought, and evolved from the mysterious Trolls over a houndred thousand years ago. They lived in the underlands following their migration out of the surface lands due to various geological events and the emergence of other, more populous cultures. Eventually the Trog would evolve into what are now known as the Dwarves and the Gnomes as well as those races offspring. Not many Trog remain in the living world of Ilrakhan, although they are still found in the most remote cavern and cave systems. Instead there are multitudes of Trog subraces, somewhat stunted compared to their evolutionary successors, that can be find in the Niryd Underlands. However due to the Great Sealing of Tur Aman little is known about those currently living Trog or their successors.</p>
