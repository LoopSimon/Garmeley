Even though their Urien Name alludes to it, these creatures have little in common with their Goblinoid "Cousins", and it is scientifically unconfirmed if the two are actually genetically closely related.  
  
Burly, lanky and muscled, Hobgoblins stand at around an average of 2 metres, towering over most fellow residents of the [[Underlands]], they tend to grow up to 75 years old and possess an average aptitude for magick.  
  
Hobgoblins, also known as Dirgae in their own language, tend to reside in large caverns near the surface, preferring wet and colder climates. They are avid hunters and usually organize in smaller communities instead of larger cities. They have been known to organize in large warbands under strong leaders but there are few examples of continuous nation states under hogboblin majority.  
  
The largest hobgoblin society on the surface is the loose federation of tribes called [[Ak-Saray]], in western Ur, where hundreds of thousands of Tirbi hobgoblins live in great tribes and clans that only rarely act as one. Since the [[Hobgoblin Invasion]] many hobgoblins, as small groups, as independent families or as whole clans, have left western Ur and Ur in general to serve as soldiers and mercenaries or to settle in surface realms elsewhere.  
  
The most distant of these communities are the hobgoblins of [[Tal]], whose ancestors came south as sellswords in the service of the [[Elbae]] Sultanate in the early 8th century PA and later fought for the Empire of [[Naigan]] and the warlords who succeeded it. They have since become a people of their own, speaking the Tal Gob dialect and following [[Takuzt]], a philosophy and faith that recognises only the four elements of Earth, Water, Air and Fire and rejects both the transitory elements of the Circle of [[Thandar]] and the [[Myan-Bond]]. They are divided into two branches. The Aït Xerra live in the highlands, on the slopes of the Xermesis around the Kaitan Gulf and in the high valleys of the Ilkiztis, and hold the hobgoblin state of [[Guadaxerra]] west of [[Koshtir]], one of the rare hobgoblin states of the world. The [[Mudaxari]] live as communities in the cities of the gulf, in [[Yorgos]] and in [[Iz-Almadus]] beside the Ilkizti gnomes.
