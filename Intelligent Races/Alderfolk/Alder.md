<p>The Alder are the mysterious precursors to the modern day Narzoi, often associated with the so called "Great Gift" that gave the primitive Purmen great abilities and elevated them to the race now known as the Narzoi. </p>
<p>The Alder build a great culture and civilisation known as <a href="Aldermon" class="internal-link">Aldermon</a> following the Age of Roil, the prehistoric times of elemental rule and primordial chaos of ancient <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a>. They constructed many wonders and grand cities, works of power and mystery whose true purpose is now lost to the souls wandering their lands in the modern day, but some of which, such as the famous Alderways, are still able to fulfil their original purpose thousands of years after the Alder fell. The ruins and sites of where great Alder once build their structures are commonly referred to as <a href="Aldergrounds" class="internal-link">Aldergrounds</a>, and much of what we know about this mysterious race is derived from discoveries, murals, and findings in these Aldergrounds.</p>
<p>We know that the Alder were assailed by an antagonistic force, one they called "The Great Enemy" and is usually associated with the <a href="Heire" class="internal-link">Heire</a>, the ancestors to the various insectoid modern day <a href="Pharang" class="internal-link">Pharang</a>. Their great war with the Enemy would result in an event known as the Catarmon, and old Aldish word whose exact translation is not known, but that would result in the end and destruction of the ancient High Civilization of the Alder, giving way for their, then almost pet-like successors to claim the lands of Aisligos, the Narzoi. In turn the only thing that remained of the Alder themselves are the <a href="Sarmi" class="internal-link">Sarmi</a>, vastly degenerated creatures that are likely the biological successors to the Alder, now merely wandering in the dredges and outskirts of the world.</p>
