<p>The Narzoi of are a race of blue-skinned, tall and slender humanoids native to the lands of <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a>, they embody many human features but lack external ears while their noses are broad and flat. The Narzoi of <a href="Bulug" class="internal-link">Bulug</a>, often simply called the Nuguli, are usually considered distinct from the "free" Narzoi of Peadyr, Verzi and Aisligos as they have lived for hundreds of years under the rule of the <a href="Hiqae" class="internal-link">Hiqae</a> of <a href="Lazatec" class="internal-link">Lazatec</a>, being genetically altered through their breeding and biomancy programs. </p>
<p>Commonly believed to have been "elevated and guarded" by the mysterious ancient race of the <a href="Alder" class="internal-link">Alder</a>, they are now their true successors in many ways and inhabit the remnants of this fallen high civilisation.</p>
<p>The Narzoi were the first sentient beings to encounter both the Hiqae and Humans of Eressa and Ilrakhan respectively when those species first set foot on the lands of Aisligos, in a period commonly referred to as the Death of the World in Narzoi culture.</p>
