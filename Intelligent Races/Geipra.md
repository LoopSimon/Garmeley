<div class="wiki-infobox" style="overflow: hidden; display: flex; flex-direction: column;"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Geipra.png" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;"> <small><i>A Geipra guide of the Red Scars</i></small> </div> <p>The Geipra are a sentient humanoid people native to the Green Mountains of eastern <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a>, distinguished from other peoples of the continent by their caprine features: broad flat horns, dense coarse fur across the face and neck, cloven feet and powerful hindlimbs, and by a capacity for vertical movement that has no equal among their rival sentient races. The Geipra can traverse near sheer rock faces and canyon walls with the ease of walking on flat ground, a physiological adaptation refined across generations of mountain habitation, and it is this ability more than any cultural or political factor that has shaped the trajectory of their history. They are of moderate stature, strongly built through the legs and lower body, and typically travel light, their material culture oriented toward portability and the demands of life in terrain that punishes excess weight.</p> 
<p>The Geipra migrations out of the Green Mountains took place across the so called Uraz Period, the centuries of seismic upheaval, ash cloud and drought that followed the eruption of <a href="Uraz" class="internal-link">Uraz</a> beginning in 283BA and did not fully subside until 122PA. Where most peoples of Aisligos found the period one of pure catastrophe, the Geipra discovered that the same tectonic violence that had devastated the continent's lowlands had also created something entirely new: the vast canyon network of the <a href="Red Scars" class="internal-link">Red Scars</a>, whose shattered walls and isolated spires were uninhabitable to virtually every other people and therefore unclaimed. Geipra communities crossed the Narzoi riverlands in successive waves throughout this period, moving westward to occupy the Scars while smaller populations remained behind in the Green Mountains, where ancient Geipra culture still persists to the present day. Whether the mountain communities and those of the Red Scars still regard themselves as a single people or as related but distinct branches is a question the Geipra themselves answer differently depending on whom one asks.</p>
 
<p>Within the Red Scars the Geipra have built a decentralised network of small settlements across the canyon walls and spires, resistant to census and to outside political pressure alike. Their most significant gathering is the "City of Winds", a trade hub of several thousand inhabitants suspended across canyon spires on a network of bridges and rope walkways. Politically the Geipra maintain a studied neutrality, a posture born as much from necessity as philosophy, serving as guides to the great powers adjacent to the Scars such as the <a href="Adate" class="internal-link">Adate Firmament</a>, <a href="Duria" class="internal-link">Duria</a> and the various smaller Narzoi and Pharang communities that press against the canyon margins, without formally aligning with any of them. Their religious life is organised around the <a href="Cult of the Dancing God" class="internal-link">Geipra Dancer Cults</a>, a system of worship centred on an asteroid body in orbit around the planet, whose significance to Geipra cosmology has produced one of the more distinctive spiritual traditions in the Aisligan world.</p>
