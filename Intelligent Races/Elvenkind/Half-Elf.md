<p>Classically described as the result of a Union between Elf and Man, dominant human features will usually quickly overtake the more discernible elven heritage. Half-Elfs are however also known to be the spawn of elven and orcish lineages, where orcish features will quickly come to dominate in turn. <p>

 <p>A relatively large number of ilrakhani Half-Elves exists within the Elbae Sultanate, as well as within the Southern Realms of Battvia, Hainach, Garmeley and the Paper Cities, although they are rarely seen in bigger numbers in Grevenna or Vihr. Many half-elves of the west surrounding the communities of Rogyn are the product of slave raids and kidnappings as the isolated minru elves of the forest strictly forbid and outlaw the intermixing between species. The northern elfs of the land of ice eternal, often referred to as Aelfs, are not known to intermingle much if all with outsiders, however reports of their offspring with bykmli sailors are not unheard of.</p>
