<p>The Jawahr is a small bean-shaped bulb that sits behind the pelvis, protected by its bone, and it is found in the elves, in the Drow and in the peoples that come from them. It appears to be central both to their aptitude for planar magic and to their long lives, but its exact function, and why it should exist at all, are unknown, and public research into it is essentially absent.</p>

<h2>Elves and Drow</h2>
<p>Both peoples carry the organ, but not alike. The Jawahr of an elf is the larger of the two and the more responsive to planar energy, and an elf may see up to 250 years. The Jawahr of a Drow is smaller and less responsive, and a Drow lives up to 180 years, though one with long exposure to planar energy may age as slowly as the oldest elven sages. The difference is seen only by those who have opened both, and very few have. Where the organ came from, and why it should be larger in one people than in the other, are questions asked rarely and never in public (see <a href="Nawiunic Event" class="internal-link">Nawiunic Event</a>).</p>

<h2>The Paragons</h2>
<p>The Jawahr takes a primary place, with the heart, liver and brain, in the mummification of a Paragon (see <a href="Elven Paragonworship" class="internal-link">Elven Paragonworship</a>). The Jawahrs of the Paragons are the oldest to survive intact. They are charged with planar energy far beyond any living elf, and in the Awakened they have made the whole body a Permanent Fluxbridge. The Paragons are revered as gods, and the Priesthood of the <a href="Golden Dusk" class="internal-link">Golden Dusk</a> keeps them. They are not to be studied.</p>

<h2>Study</h2>
<p>No one outside the elven academies has made a meaningful study of the Jawahr, and inside them the subject is so tied to elven culture and faith that it is a very hot one. At the <a href="Karajal School of Insight" class="internal-link">Karajal School of Insight</a> a number of scholars, led by Arouf Dimelis al-Dezer, the Ruby Eye and a radical, have asked leave to examine the Paragons under an academic lens. The request has been denied, as yet.</p>
