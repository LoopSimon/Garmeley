<p>The Nua, sometimes called the Nua-Elfs, were the mysterious predecessors to the elven cultures that would eventually divide between the underland Drow and the overland High Elfs. They were considerably smaller in size than their modern day successors and reportedly lacked various biological features, including the drow and elven prologued life spans, that mark them as unique amongst other intelligent humanoids. The Nua were a species residing in the central and southern Underlands and from what we know today, almost entirely subterranean, by the year 25000PA, almost all Nua had been replaced by the emerging Drow and later Elfs. Little of their lifes has survived the many civilisations that came after them but some of their art and culture has survived to this day such as the immaculate Spire of On, a giant underlands monolith painted and carved meticulously with strange markings that precedes the drow cultures of the area by thousands of years. </p>
