 <p>The Aelf, viewed by most surface civilizations as an entirely distinct elven species, though their origins tell a more specific story, are the descendants of <a href="Drow" class="internal-link">Drow</a> refugees who emerged from the far northern arctic lands of Jeturlund following the collapse of the great Drow civilization of <a href="Tur Enzal" class="internal-link">Tur Enzal</a> in approximately 1013BA, in an event the old records call the Promise of Resar.  What followed over the next millennium was a transformation wrought by both circumstance and deliberate magical adaptation: the cave-dwelling, light-sensitive Drow biology reshaping itself for a world of arctic surface life, extreme cold, permanent seasonal darkness & light, as well as the particular demands of surviving in a landscape dominated by some of the most dangerous predators and beings known to the world. The Aelf that exist in 927PA are the product of nearly two thousand years of that reshaping and recognizably elven in their fundamental biology, visibly distinct from both their Drow ancestors and the surface High Elves in almost every other particular.</p> 
 
 <p>Physically, the Aelf carry the Drow inheritance in altered form. The blue-grey skin tones of the Drow have deepened and shifted in the arctic, running to vivid blues and blue greens rather than the dark greys and obsidians of the Underlands. Eyes remain large and vivid, purples, silvers and pale blues are common, though the extreme light sensitivity that confines Drow to underground life has moderated sufficiently for surface existence, replaced by a cold tolerance that no other elven lineage shares. Hair has shifted considerably from the characteristic Drow whites and pale yellows, with warmer tones like auburn, gold, copper, appearing in many individuals alongside the pale shades that remain. The Aelf are fully surface adapted and have been for centuries: the northern cave systems of Jeturlund are geologically unstable and host no large Aelf settlements, though small isolated underground communities persist in the deep places where the old Drow instinct for the subterranean has never entirely faded. Above ground they contend with a landscape of extraordinary harshness with the Frozen Sea, the glaciated mountain ranges of the Skemfing peninsula, and the presence of the great arctic giants whose territories overlap with and frequently contest those of the Aelf.</p> 
 <p>The Aelf realm of <a href="Skemfing" class="internal-link">Skemfing</a> is governed by <a href="Tragna" class="internal-link">Queen Tragna Reasna</a>, born 99PA, aged six hundred and thirty years in 927PA, and alive. </p>
