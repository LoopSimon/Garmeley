<p>The Ginnil are the grastropod inhabitants of the Durfall, their forms combining iconic hardshell they use as housing and protection as well as their iconic mucus covered bodies themselves that allow them to traverse the treacherous Durfall as no other species can. The Ginnil are isolationist and display middling care for other lifeforms, but engage in symbiotic  relationships with various Narzoi communities near the Durfall, acting as guides and caravaners through the Clouds.</p>
<p>Ginnil can also be found outside of the Durfall, although their cousins, on other parts of Peadyr, Aisligos and in great number further south within Pagia and Eressa, are notable smaller in shape and size and in greater variance of colour and shell than the iconic Ginnil of the Durfall. While many Ginnil do prefer inhabiting wet and swampy regions, a common trait for all of them is their nomadic preference and lifestyle.  </p>
<p>The lifespan of the Ginnil can vary greatly based on their native regions, as they are relatively quick to adapt over only a few generations to new surroundings. The oldest known Ginnil are said to have reached a life of nearly 190 years while others end their natural lifespan at the age of 30.</p>
<p>Perhaps most unique to them across many sentient organisms in the world, they display a wide variety of intelligence between individuals, with some extraordinary individuals able to display such understanding and conceptualising they are able to innately develop magical abilities, without the support of a greater, communal and generational line of arcane tradition.</p>
<p>Most Ginnil despise metals and refuse to touch or wield them, some being even fully allergic to them while in turn producing slime so corrosive it can seriously destroy it. </p>
