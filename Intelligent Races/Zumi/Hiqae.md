<p>The Hiqae are a subgroup of Zumi, originally from the northern lands of <a href="Pagia" class="internal-link">Pagia</a> that engaged in generation spanning biomantic ritual and genetic modification of their populace, eventually leading to a society and group of people unable to reproduce or be identified with the larger <a href="Zumi" class="internal-link">Anzumi</a> populace of Pagia.</p>
<p>Similarly to the Grippli of Inara, the Hiqae are adapted to more amphibian lifestyles, with longer legs than their Anzumi cousins and noticeably webbed hands and feet, preferring watery or at least water adjacent habitats. Due to their somewhat artificial nature and unique evolutionary history, the Hiqae have developed refined systems or breeding compared to the Azumi and spawn millions of eggs in clusters or masses, as only a rare few of their tadpoles develop strong enough organs to survive.</p>
<p>Following a great shift within the broader zumi society, the Hiqae began being systematically persecuted and maligned, driven to near extinction. It was because of this that their leaders constructed the so called "Eternal Arks" and began their voyage north to the promised lands, finding themselves, after years of toil on the harsh and cruel waves of the Tempest, on the island of <a href="Bulug" class="internal-link">Bulug</a> in southern <a href="Aisligos" class="internal-link">Aisligos</a>, where they would later found their own Empire, the great <a href="Lazatec" class="internal-link">Lazatec</a>. It is unknown what happened to the surviving remnant population of Hiqae that remained in Pagia, although some are said to have found refugee in the <a href="Daigonis Xiguo" class="internal-link">Daigonis Xiguo</a>, the great Realm of the <a href="Yin" class="internal-link">Yin</a> in <a href="Eressa" class="internal-link">Eressa</a>.</p>
