<p>The Zumi, sometimes called the "Anzumi", literally meaning true Zumi, are the dominant populace of the continent of <a href="Eressa" class="internal-link">Eressa</a> and the, from an evolutionary standpoint, victorious species of the greater Zumi lineage. They are distantly related to the Grippli that can be found in <a href="Inara" class="internal-link">Inara</a> as well as cousins to the now exiled <a href="Hiqae" class="internal-link">Hiqae</a>.</p>
<p>With a noticeable resemblance to their smaller, more frog like cousins, the Anzum are larger and resemble the appearance of large Toads. Their skins are drier, and bumpier and their bodies heavier and more stout. The Anzum prefer life in drier regions compared to their more aquatic relatives and rarely display signs of amphibian mutations such as webbed hands or feet.</p>
