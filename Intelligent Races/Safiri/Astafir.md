<p>The Astafir are the immediate evolutionary predecessors of the <a href="Safir" class="internal-link">Safir</a>, fully serpentine sentient beings who inhabited the jungles of southern <a href="Behera" class="internal-link">Behera</a> long before the first Safir emerged, and who persist today in small, deeply reclusive communities in the most isolated reaches of those same jungles. Unlike the Safir, whose morphological range from fully bipedal to partially serpentine reflects thousands of generations of <a href="Yari" class="internal-link">Yari</a> Ichor influence, the Astafir received no such gift: they remained as they had always been as fully serpentine, and watched their descendants gradually replace them as the dominant genus of the continent over the span of millennia. They are genuine sentients, capable of basic community organization and the production of simple culture, but the long shadow of the Safir's dominance has not been kind to their development, the communities that survive are small, regressive compared to what fragmentary evidence suggests their ancestors achieved, and deeply hidden from a world that largely does not want to find them. The Safir relationship to the Astafir is one of the more uncomfortable aspects of their civilization to examine closely: the dominant culture describes them as lesser beings, monsters, or relics, language that many outsiders have noted says considerably more about the Safir's discomfort with their own origins than it does about the Astafir themselves, who are, in the most precise biological sense, simply what the Safir used to be.</p>
