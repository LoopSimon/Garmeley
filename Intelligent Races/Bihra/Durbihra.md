<p>The Durbihra are the <a href="Bihra" class="internal-link">Bihra</a> of the island of Durennis in the far east of <a href="Nirydia" class="internal-link">Nirydia</a> and of the neighbouring Pepper Islands. The smallest of the Bihra, they are known as traders, spice growers, perfumers and physicians, and their realms, the successors of the ancient Omna of <a href="Japhal" class="internal-link">Japhal</a>, have been the centre of the <a href="Rayadan" class="internal-link">Rayadan</a> for over two thousand years. Like the <a href="Azbihra" class="internal-link">Azbihra</a>, they are counted among the Zibihra by the <a href="Steppe Bihra" class="internal-link">Steppe Bihra</a>.</p>  
<h2>Physiology</h2>  
<p>The Durbihra rarely stand taller than 1.1 metres and are slight of build. Most live among the jungle rivers and seasonal floods of northern Durennis, and they are strong swimmers who move easily through undergrowth, mud and shallow bog. Like the Azbihra they are far less prone to mutation than the Bihra of the steppe.</p>  
<h2>Distribution</h2>  
<p>Most Durbihra live in the monsoon jungles and green river lands of northern Durennis, north of the Durenni Peaks and along the Bay of Kizac, and on the Pepper Islands. They are the majority of the Omnas of <a href="Lisava" class="internal-link">Lisava</a> and <a href="Zilkand" class="internal-link">Zilkand</a>, and live beside Sunborn and Orcs in the southern port of <a href="Raximan" class="internal-link">Raximan</a>. The south of the island is held by the Sunborn of <a href="Tilduren" class="internal-link">Tilduren</a> in the Durenni Sands, the Orcish kingdom of <a href="Kamerzin" class="internal-link">Kamerzin</a> holds the channel city of Kamerzin, and the Xolor of <a href="Pervantes" class="internal-link">Pervantes</a> hold the east.</p>  
<h2>History</h2>  
<p>The Durenni Age of Strife ended in 299BA with the founding of the Omna of Japhal, which ruled from Balramur and whose language survives as Old Durenni. In the early centuries of Japhal the Yova <a href="Ajaran Mor" class="internal-link">Ajaran Mor</a> wandered the island, and his followers formed the Rayadan as a coherent tradition in the 2nd century BA. The Pragala, the great mythos of Gasch and one of the greatest surviving works of literature in the world, was created by Durbihra in the 1st and 2nd centuries PA. Japhal came to an end in 510PA and was succeeded by the Omnas of Lisava, Zilkand and Raximan, of which Lisava, ruling from Prankabur, is today the largest and most powerful. Lisava fought the maritime Empire of <a href="Vanpur" class="internal-link">Vanpur</a> and its successors for almost a century before reaching a tentative armistice with Pervantes.</p>  
<h2>Society and economy</h2>  
<p>The islands and the northern coasts of Durennis are among the greatest sources of spice in the world, and the Durbihra who grow them sell to the ships of the <a href="Vanpur Pepper Trade" class="internal-link">Vanpur Pepper Trade</a> each year, with the ports of northern Durennis counted among the oldest friends of <a href="Dišela" class="internal-link">Dišela</a>. Zilkand is known for its giraffe riders and magical perfumes, and Prankabur is said to hold the most advanced school of medicine in the world, where the bodies of Bihra, Sunborn and humans are studied alike. The Durbihra speak Durenni and largely follow the Rayadan, while small Zuhuri communities live on the Pepper Islands.</p>
