 <p>The term Krestasi derives from the <a href="Orc" class="internal-link">orcish</a> word "Krest", meaning simply "crust," coined by Qari sailors in the decades following their first encounters with intelligent crustacean peoples in the waters south east of <a href="Il Haena" class="internal-link">Il-Haena</a>. It is a surface-dwellers generalization, applied to a family of distinct crustacean humanoid peoples whose actual relationships to one another, internal taxonomies and self designations are considerably more complex than the single term implies. Three major Krestasi civilizations are known to surface scholarship by name: the <a href="Golomid" class="internal-link">Golomid Empire</a> of the Niryd Sea, the <a href="Onar Despotate" class="internal-link">Onar Despotate</a> of the Tirean Ocean, and the <a href="Cities of Dreams" class="internal-link">Cities of Dreams</a> of the Beheran Ocean. Of these, only the Golomid has been encountered in any meaningful depth, and even that encounter is limited to the surface dominion the Golomid deliberately projected upward through the <a href="Risen Reef" class="internal-link">Risen Reef</a>, a fraction of an empire whose true scale on the ocean floor remains entirely unassessed by surface observers.</p> <h2>Biology</h2> <div class="wiki-infobox" style="overflow: hidden; display: flex; flex-direction: column;"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopSimon/Garmeley/refs/heads/main/Media/Golomid.jpg" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; display: block; flex-grow: 1;">

<p>The Krestasi are crustacean humanoids, bearing the hard chitinous exoskeleton, articulated claws, antennae and compound sensory organs characteristic of the crustacean family, organized into bodies capable of tool use, complex communication and the construction and maintenance of large-scale civilisation. Beyond these broad shared features, physical variation within and between Krestasi groups is extreme enough that surface observers encountering specimens from different castes or environmental breeding lines have historically assumed they were looking at entirely different species. The Krestasi practice of selective breeding, cultivated across what surface scholars believe to be thousands of years of intentional biological management, has produced physical differentiation so profound that the connection between a surface capable scout caste Krestasi and a deep trench habitation caste Krestasi of the same realm is easy to miss at first glance. The environment into which a lineage is bred shapes biology as significantly as caste function: communities bred for the crushing pressures of deep ocean trenches are unrecognisable beside those bred for near surface reef settlements, and volcanic vent communities develop capabilities that cold water populations do not possess. Surface capable castes represent only one narrow slice of Krestasi biological diversity.</p> <h2>The Caste System</h2> <p>Across every known Krestasi civilization, social organization is structured around a caste system that is not just social convention but biological reality. A Krestasis caste is determined before birth by the breeding decisions of their civilizations breeding masters: the lineage they are born from, the physical characteristics that lineage has been cultivated to produce, and the environmental conditions for which their body is optimized are all fixed before the egg is laid. There is no mechanism by which an individual moves between castes. The Krestasi do not experience this as injustice and the biological fixity of caste is as unremarkable to a Krestasi as the concept of having a body at all.</p> <p>The castes that surface researchers have been able to document  include what appear to be large ruling specimens responsible for governance, warrior castes bred for combat capability and varying degrees of surface operation, worker and artisan castes with fine manipulator claws suited to detailed craft, sensory enhanced priestly castes, and amphibious scout lineages capable of extended land survival. These should be understood as the castes that were bred to be accessible to surface observers, a sample selected by the Krestasi themselves. What the full caste structure of any Krestasi civilisation looks like in its entirety, including the deep castes that have never approached the waterline, remains unknown.</p> <h2>Coral Mastery</h2> <p>The primary expression of Krestasi technology is the manipulation of coral. The Krestasi do not build in stone or forge in metal. They cultivate. Through biochemical processes that surface scholars understand poorly and cannot replicate, Krestasi civilization shapes living coral into structures of extraordinary scale and complexity such as fortresses, habitation complexes, warships and entire cities grown over centuries of careful management into the forms their inhabitants require. The coral structures thus produced are not inert stone but living organisms, maintained and shaped by ongoing biochemical intervention, capable of continued growth in directions their cultivators direct and possessed of structural properties that purpose-grown coral achieves and quarried stone does not.</p>
<p> The drifting coral cities that Krestasi civilizations also employ, mobile settlements large enough to house significant populations, capable of directed movement through ocean currents that their coral architecture is grown to exploit, represent a further expression of the same mastery. A Krestasi warship is a living organism. A Krestasi city may not be in the same location next season. The strategic implications of this for surface civilizations accustomed to fixed fortifications and constructed vessels are significant and not yet fully understood. The most visible expression of this capability in the surface world is the <a href="Risen Reef" class="internal-link">Risen Reef</a>, but that structure, extraordinary as it appears to surface observers, was a centuries long project of a civilization that had been growing its cities for far longer than the surface world knew it existed.</p>
