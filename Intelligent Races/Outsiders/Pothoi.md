<p>The name Pothoi stems from the old palmunian underworld, where creatures of time and desire lived. On Tablets of the Aldercycle, they are associated with the value 7 and portrayed as entities opposite of those called the <a href="Deimoi" class="internal-link">Deimoi</a>. </p>
