<p>The academic classification for any outsider specifically aligned, usually suspected of origin of forces of Stagnancy, Order, Meaning and Persistence in various canons of cosmology, sometimes conflated with the <a href="Fractal" class="internal-link">Fractals</a>. On Tablets of the Aldercycle, they are associated with the value 1 and portrayed as entities opposite of those called the <a href="Protean" class="internal-link">Proteans</a> within the western canon.</p>
