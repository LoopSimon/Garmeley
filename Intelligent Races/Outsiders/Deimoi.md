%% [[Pothoi]] [[Palmunian Polytheism]] %%

<p>The name Deimoi stems from the old palmunian heavens, where creatures of space and harmony lived. On Tablets of the Aldercycle, they are associated with the value 8 and portrayed as entities opposite of those called the <a href="Pothoi" class="internal-link">Pothoi</a>. </p>
