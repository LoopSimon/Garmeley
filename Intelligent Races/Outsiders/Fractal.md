<p>The academic classification for any outsider specifically aligned, usually suspected of origin of forces of Stagnancy, Order, Meaning and Persistence in various canons of cosmology, sometimes conflated with the <a href="Aeon" class="internal-link">Aeons</a>. On Tablets of the Aldercycle, they are associated with the value 9 and portrayed as entities opposite of those the shirin call the <a href="Hinnuh" class="internal-link">Hinnuh</a>.</p>
