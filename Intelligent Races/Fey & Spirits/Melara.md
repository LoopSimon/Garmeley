<p> Melara, also known by her nicknames "The Trinmother", or "The Lady Immerquell", is a Greater Spirit of great histories and renown that is said to be the mother of the Great River, the Trinmo, that flows through Meadland and Ur into the Stormy Sea. She is said to reside in the Whisperhills and her hair be the flowing waters of the River itself, there are various cults of ancient meadi that still worship Melara as their living patron and goddess. In some tales she is the literal embodiment of the River, closely associated with the turvik deity Mera, while in others she is a motherly figure that represents the birth of the Trinmo as a separate entity.</p>
