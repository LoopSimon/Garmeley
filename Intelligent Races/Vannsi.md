<p>The Vannsi are an amphibious people descended from the great merfolk of the deep seas, their amphibian characteristics the product of tens of thousands of years of gradual evolutionary change. Though fully aquatic merfolk still exist in the far northern and southern waters, the Vannsi long ago developed the capacity to live and move on land, and it is in the cold coastal regions, wetlands and river systems of the northern world that their various cultures have made their homes. They are found across much of the Wyrmrest and Frozen Seas in smaller communities and clan settlements, with the largest and most structured of their cultures being the <a href="Zren" class="internal-link">Zreni Vannsi</a> of the eastern <a href="Trykkerog" class="internal-link">Trykkerog</a>, who represent the primary point of contact between the Vannsi and the human peoples of <a href="Ilrakhan" class="internal-link">Ilrakhan</a>.</p>

<p>In appearance the Vannsi are lean and powerfully built, with blue-grey skin marked by iridescent spot patterning that shifts subtly in different lights. Trailing fin extensions run along the limbs and spine, and the head carries prominent cresting, more dramatic in females whose crests grow in vivid colours of red and violet, while males present broader builds and heavier musculature. Their ears come to a point and their eyes are adapted for both air and water. Males are notably larger than females on average. Vannsi are mammals and give live birth; newborn children are physically capable but are kept predominantly in water for the first three years of life as a medical and cultural necessity, their bodies not yet sufficiently adapted to the demands of sustained land movement. Lifespan runs between fifty and seventy years.</p>

<p>Vannsi society at the local level is organised around clans, each occupying their own settlement and maintaining their own traditions. Spiritual and political life is guided by shamans known as Tideseers, practitioners especially attuned to the weather patterns, sea winds and storm systems of the northern coasts, disciplines that have proven essential to survival in these waters. Zenri Tideseers serve their clans but are also the primary candidates considered for elevation to the Inamid, the Council of Sages that governs the Zreni people. Not all Tideseers wish for such responsibility; many refuse the councils call in favour of the open sea, and these individuals are often sought by the <a href="Aelf" class="internal-link">Aelf</a> of <a href="Skemfing" class="internal-link">Skemfing</a> to join their icy northern voyages. The Vannsi cannot reproduce with humans, though relationships between the two peoples are not uncommon in communities where they live alongside one another. </p>
