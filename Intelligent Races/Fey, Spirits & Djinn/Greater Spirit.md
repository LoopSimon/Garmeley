A general term describing the various contemporary and / or historical named entities of the [[Spiritual Realm]] that have occasionally even taken form on the [[Material Realm]].  
Generally speaking these are found as embodiments of natural phenomena, often being objects of Worship in faiths such as the [[Kindly Lords]] or the [[Terem-Zal]] but the term is often used as a catch-all for the sentient or especially powerful creatures born within the Spiritual Realm.

In the southern canon of the arcane, such spirits are counted among the great wills of Tihir [[Szuan]]'s [[Myan-Bond]]. Szuan held that a great will may attach itself to an [[Elemental]] and live with it as one being, a Myan: the will gains a body in the Material Realm without leaving what it is bound to, and the elemental gains a temper it never had. Northern scholars long dismissed the idea as yppori faith, but accounts of elementals that guard shrines, keep bargains or mourn at graves have kept it alive.
