<p>Sphinxes, sometimes referred to as Sphinges or Synxi, and in elven tradition as the Children of Liz, are creatures of unknown origin with the head of a human, the body of a lion and the wings of an eagle, they are primarily found on the northern Beheran continent. Their bodies are <a href="Lizion" class="internal-link">Lizion</a> throughout, a dark glass-like stone that is warm to the touch, veined with a pale light and partly radioactive, and those who keep close to one for long are said to sicken. Sphinxes are intelligent creatures capable of channelling planar energies as magic, they are religiously revered within <a href="Elven Paragonworship" class="internal-link">elven Paragonworship</a> and sometimes guard ancient Tombs of Pharaos of the past.</p>
<p>How stone came to think and move, and why the sphinxes are bound to the elves and the Beheran desert, are not known. The name Children of Liz is as old as any elven tale, and the stone is called Lizion after the same Liz.</p>
