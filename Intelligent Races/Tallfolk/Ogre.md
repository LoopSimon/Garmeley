<p>The Ogre are the descendants of the <a href="Giant" class="internal-link">Giants</a>, cousins to the <a href="Firbolg" class="internal-link">Firbolg</a> and the precursors to the <a href="Cyclopi" class="internal-link">Cyclopi</a>, they are by far the most numerous of the Giantkin, having spread over all known Continents in Ages past and there is evidence for their evolution and migration periods predating even that of the purman, the ancestor to the modern human as they can be found on all Continents of the Globe.</p>

<p>During the Age of Stone and Bronze, the Ogres ruled much of Nirydia and vast stretches of Ilrakhan, dominating humans and other surface dwelling races. With the advent of advanced metallurgy and toolmaking, their race fell behind and was slowly but surely pressed away from the once dominating position they hold, in the modern day Ogres still roam the lands but their reliance on their natural physiology and might and inability to keep up with other races technological developments have made them a potent but largely minor threat compared to their once uncontested dominance over the surface world.</p>
<p>Mountains of monstrous muscle, ogres are terrifying in their appearance. Even the smallest of these hulking brutes stands at least eight feet tall, while some legends put the largest ogres at more than 14 feet tall, humanoid  in form, their bodies are, in the words of the famous Author <a href="Hubert of Tanverley" class="internal-link">Hubert of Tanverley</a>, "hideously distorted with faces naturally twisted and hateful". Ogres are infamously able to unhinge their jaws to eat beings as large as a halfling whole.</p>
<p>How the Ogres came to be is unknown but their main advantage, compared to their Giant ancestors, was their ability to reproduce, similarly to the Firbolg and Cyclopi as well, the natural adaptability, powerful physique and omnivorous appetite set the conditions for the Ogre as a race to spread over the entire world and dominate many lands for hundreds of years. While there seem to be various regional subtypes, most ogres are largely nocturnal creatures, and while the sun does not damage them or impede their vision, they still hate daylight.</p>
