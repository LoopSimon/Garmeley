<p>The Firbolg or Fir Bholg, are a species native to northern Nirydia and Trykkerog, who descended from the Giants of the North according to myth. They are larger humanoids, but significantly smaller than their Ogre cousins and spout a tough leathery skin and what many consider more primitive features, thick flat noses and large foreheads as well as colossal hands and a great amount of Fur. Many believe Firbolg to be envoys of changes, good tidings or omens of luck, but the strange creatures keep to themselves and avoid many products of civilisation such as metals like the plague, living in small family structure at the most and never in greater communities. The largest population of Firbolgs can be found within the nirydian Tundra known as Gellions Folly.</p>
