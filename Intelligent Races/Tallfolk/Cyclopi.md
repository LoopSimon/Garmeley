<p>An ancient people, the mutated <a href="Ogre" class="internal-link">Ogre</a> of the Kragi are referred to as Cyclopi: taller, smarter and one-eyed compared to their Tallfolk Cousins, but perhaps more importantly noticeably more keen on civilization building as seen in their ancient League of <a href="Cyraclion" class="internal-link">Cyraclion</a>. Many modern cyclopi contend themselves with observing the Stars from elevated constructions, but reports of wars with Satyr warbands speak to a not entirely pacifist nature.</p>
